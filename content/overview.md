# Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook Wiki Overview

This wiki tracks and synthesizes details across the **Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook** series (8 books). It catalogs the characters, plot threads, themes, and chapters across all books.

## Project Goal
Analyze, summarize, and link narratives, figures, and themes across the 8 books in the series, capturing plot details, thematic arcs, and character development in a structured knowledge base.

## Reading Status
- **Total Books**: 8
- **Ingested Books**: 8 / 8 (Complete Books 1-8)
- **Status**: Complete

## Major Themes Under Investigation
- [[systemic-oppression]] — The legalistic, corporate exploitation of Earth and other seeded planets by the Syndicate and the Borant Corporation.
- [[survival]] — The brutal, immediate physical challenges, moral compromises, and tactical exploits crawlers endure.
- [[televised-exploitation]] — The commodification of the crawlers' struggles as intergalactic television for ratings, followers, and sponsorships.
- [[solidarity-and-duty]] — Human cooperation and duty, as demonstrated by the caretakers of Meadow Lark protecting their elderly charges.
- [[primal-race]] — Ancient mythological progenitor race chosen by Carl for uncapped skill leveling.
- [[over-city-mechanics]] — Custom floor structure introducing day/night cycles, janitor mobs, and scripted serials.
- [[the-anarchist-cookbook]] — Subversion of dungeon rules using code exploits and crawler solidarity.
- [[ghosts-of-earth]] — Floor 8 environment and replay mechanics.
- [[t-ghee-card-combat]] — The deck-building card combat system on Floor 8.

## Floor 6: The Hunting Grounds Narrative Summary (Book 5)
Floor 6 ("The Hunting Grounds") is a wilderness simulation floor where crawlers are hunted by intergalactic tourists ("hunters"). The key events of Book 5 include:
- **Preemptive Raid on Zockau**: Exploiting a 30-hour grace period, Carl and Donut execute a surprise raid on the hunter staging capital of [[zockau]], flattening the city square and permanently killing 34 hunters.
- **Reforms at Point Mongo**: Donut defeats the puritanical mayor Elmer and takes over the Large Ursine Settlement, renaming it [[point-mongo]]. She repeals the puritanical laws and pacifies the dinosaur menace by allowing [[mongo]] to mate with [[kiwi]], the pack leader.
- **The Valtay Regency Takeover**: The Valtay Corporation acquires a 51% stake in Borant Corporation, taking over the regency of the Earth crawl. It is revealed that [[the-butchers-masquerade]] is a Valtay trap to eliminate the top 50 crawlers.
- **The Assault on Fort Freedom**: Carl and Donut allied with Tsarina [[tsarina-signet]] to execute an amphibious raid on [[fort-freedom]]. Although they lose allies (including [[clint-smashgrab]], [[nadine]], and [[langley]]'s archers), they defeat the Level 65 Borough Boss [[claude-sludgington-the-fourth]], completing Part One of the [[vengeance-of-the-daughter]] quest.
- **CrawlCon Launch**: Carl is teleported away from the dungeon to a Valtay orbital station and then an underwater landing vessel to participate in [[crawlcon]] (beginning with the children's art contest), leaving Donut behind in Alucarda after a brief confrontation with [[lucia-mar]].
- **The Creeping Apocalypse**: Following Miriam Dom's sacrifice to cure the floor's vampirism, the World Quest *The Creeping Apocalypse* is failed as two Creepers are ignored in the hunter plains. This triggers the *Gehenna Bramble* (expanding walls of boiling-blood vines) and *Blood Bars* (collapsing safe zone access limits), forcing crawlers and hunters alike toward the High Elf Castle.
- **Ferdinand Confrontation**: Imogen's familiar is revealed to be [[ferdinand]], Donut's memory-wiped ex-boyfriend show cat, who attacks Mongo. Donut barely survives intercepting the blow. Carl subsequently meets and executes hunter-crafter Iota, who reveals that the crawl AI is a recycled, primal amusement park AI.
- **The Recital & Climax**: The crawlers gather at the High Elf Castle during the Butcher's Masquerade. Donut and the team execute their dance recital (performing Nicki Minaj's "Anaconda" with Mongo, Kiwi, and a pacified [[big-tina]]), while [[the-sledge]] casts the *Zerzura* spell to successfully teleport the changelings to Floor 9.
- **Deity & Queen Confrontation**: The Level 250 Immortal [[diwata]] is summoned. [[samantha]] detonates the castle's soul crystals to knock Diwata unconscious, breaking the ballroom's protection seal. The AI subsequently freezes the battlefield and establishes a four-way cage fight, resulting in the defeat of Queen [[imogen]] and Ferdinand, the elimination of Diwata, and the completion of the quest [[vengeance-of-the-daughter]].
- **Warlord Unlocks**: Carl and Donut become Co-Warlords of the "Princess Posse" team, sponsored by [[tsecs]]. Donut is revealed to be in the line of succession for Team #4 (the Blood Sultanate). The children and pregnant companions are successfully freed to the surface.
- **Transition to Floor 7**: The crawlers descend to Floor 7, [[great-race]], which features a 20-day level collapse timer and squads limited to five members or less. As [[eva-sigrid]] dies, she places the [[crown-of-the-sepsis-whore]] on [[katia-grim]]'s head, entering Katia into the Blood Sultanate succession line and restricting stair access so that only one of Donut or Katia can advance.

## Floor 8: The Ghosts of Earth Narrative Summary (Book 6)
Floor 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth") is a precise replay of Earth as it existed on December 10th. The key events include:
- **Resurrecting Yemaya & Ogun's Wrath**: Carl bypasses the other shrines to resurrect the river goddess [[yemaya]], curing the cemetery settlement. However, this act angers the blacksmith god [[ogun]], who manifests in Havana. Carl tries to protect [[anton-lopez]] using the *Black Nimbus* spell, but Ogun crushes Anton's head, killing him. Paz Lo is struck by an un-healable health-drain smite. Sister Ines turns Paz into a card totem, loots Anton's body, and abandons the group. Paz reveals that Sister Ines is "The Reaper of Havana" (a convicted serial killer).
- **The Eileithyia Deal**: Carl visits Club Vanquisher, receiving a foot sacrament from [[pater-coal]] that allows him to communicate with [[emberus]] without system eavesdropping. The goddess [[eileithyia]] offers a deal: select [[odette]] as Floor 9 Adjutant in exchange for disabling crawler protections (allowing them to hunt hunters) and curing Katia's *Crown of the Sepsis Whore* curse with a mythic orchid.
- **The NPC Rebellion**: During the Faction Wars preproduction meetings, the naga representative [[rishi]] warns Donut that she is in the Blood Sultanate line of succession. [[juice-box]] breaches the meeting in the body of Epitome Noflex, demanding NPC rights and voting power, and virtually assassinates Prince Stalwart.
- **The Desperado Club Climax**: The Guild of Suffering (fronted by Damascus Steel) assigns Katia a quest to assassinate [[astrid]], the wife of Guild boss Hamed (the Night Wyrm). During the assassination attempt, Samantha breaches the Nothing, releasing the Level 225 feral demon [[minge]]. Carl unleashes the "vorpals"—divine suicide weapons—to destroy Minge. In the chaos, Carl rips off Astrid's wings to disable her magic, and Katia impales her through the head, completing the quest and acquiring the orchid. Carl uses a Meat Shields coupon to hire the Penis Parade dancers as Faction Wars mercenaries, while Damascus and Anaconda set off to fight the Night Wyrm.
- **Adjutant Selection & Phase Two**: During their final interview on Odette's show, Carl and Donut discover that Empress D'Nadia tried to use Emergency Action Items to delete them. Odette announces she is entering the dungeon as the goddess [[nekhebit]], forcing Carl to select the Orc attorney [[baroness-victory]] as their adjutant. In a private talk, Odette reveals the crawl's true purpose: harvesting biological elements as fuel for planetary engines. Donut transfers as squad leader to Miami, where she instantly defeats [[robert-the-human]] with [[mongo]], and Carl follows.
- **The Keymaster & Personal Revelations**: Carl and Donut are relocated to Monroe County, Florida (Florida Keys). Spliced into the area is a summer-time memory slice of Carl's childhood home in Dallas County, Iowa. Carl witnesses his father [[robert-the-human]] dying of illness, murdered by stepmother [[tami-lynn]] with morphine to protect Carl's half-brother [[asher]]. They defeat the Level 125 *Reminiscence Hydra of Malicious Compliance* (which has heads representing people they know) to win the key. Carl learns that Beatrice intercepted messages about his family and got his coworker Sally fired, triggering his deep rage against her. Emberus upgrades Carl to Devotee rank (Burn immunity, *Self-Immolation* spell).
- **The Residuals & Eviction Broadcast**: Carl and Donut find thousands of dead monk seals and crabs on the beach, netting 1M gold. Carl receives a benefactor box from the Open Intellect Pacifist Network and rides his father's Harley-Davidson to Homestead, Florida, where he meets the gondii Residual [[paulie]] (Goff), who explains the Residuals' goal to teach Macro AIs how to contact ancestors. Orren yanks Carl to prevent the lore leak, and Carl trades the bin of neural pills in exchange for keeping the cretins for Floor 9.
- **Cuba Return & Demon Eviction**: Carl and Donut return to Cuba to rescue Imani (whose squad died due to her own totem). A global *Demon Eviction* event is triggered by Carl's zombie turkeys and crab sperm, Evicting Sheol demons who possess weak undead. Carl teleports Sister Ines, her Level 125 city boss snake [[madre-de-aguas]], and thousands of zombies/monsters to their location, breaking her charm spell and freeing Level 140 demons. Kevin and Magnificent Troy broadcast the free-for-all *Survival Royale* battle, with Quan Ch entering the fray.

## Floor 9: Faction Wars Narrative Summary (Book 7)
Floor 9 ("Faction Wars") is a territorial military simulation taking place in the funnel city of Larracos. The key events include:
- **Arrival & Subcommittee Strategy**: The crawlers arrive in Larracos and establish "The Good Guys" coalition under General [[florin]], allying with local NPCs and dwarf infantry commander [[arief]]. Donut selects the *Deathbed Hellcat* sniper subclass, while Carl equips specialized mobility gear like the *Toe Ring of the Well-Balanced* and recovers from Floor 8 soul poisoning.
- **Apito's Crystal & Sir Rendlegore**: Following a quest from [[emberus]], Carl retrieves [[apito]]'s Soul Crystal, while also bonding with a Level-1 Tummy Acher pet named [[sir-rendlegore]]. Louis Santiago receives the *Party Planner* stealth bomber to provide heavy aerial support.
- **Faction War Combat & Tina's Rampage**: As active faction warfare breaks out, the coalition utilizes strategic fortifications and trenches to hold off waves of enemies. [[big-tina]] is unleashed on the battlefield, causing massive physical destruction and enabling a breakthrough.
- **The Battle of Gustavo & Dead Man's Switch**: Tensions culminate in a massive siege where crawler allies confront the Borant administrators. [[lucia-mar]]'s death triggers a global "Dead Man's Switch," resulting in massive casualties and the collapse of key factions.
- **climax of Larracos**: Carl, Donut, and Katia navigate the shifting rules of Larracos. Katia makes a difficult choice regarding the [[crown-of-the-sepsis-whore]], and [[samantha]] makes a critical sacrifice to protect the crawlers. Carl initiates his *Book of Boom* exploits to trigger a massive collapse, breaking the floor's boundaries as [[scolopendra]] begins to awaken, forcing the transition of surviving crawlers to Floor 10.

## Floor 10: Don't Come in Last Narrative Summary (Book 8)
Floor 10 ("Don't Come in Last") is a racing-themed floor consisting of seven heats on custom-designed tracks. The key events include:
- **Garage Management and Mechanic Hedy**: Carl and Donut arrive in their designated garage. Safe room access is locked until the first heat is completed. Donut selects the *Assassin of Sekhmet* subclass. They hire gremlin mechanic [[hedy]] (disintegrating her rival [[waldrip-chris]]) to manage their vehicle, a modified Big Shot Chicken food truck upgraded with spider legs.
- **Princess Chandra's Legal Claims**: In the inter-floor communications, Princess [[princess-chandra]] claims "Widow's Rights" to marry Carl and legally seize his and Donut's financial empires and merchandise.
- **The Nothing Breaks**: The collapse of the Nothing on Floor 8 creates critical instability. On Floor 12 and Floor 18, guests in the club cannot die and are hunted and tortured by escaped gods and demons.
- **Alliances and the Heats**: Crawlers form squads and coordinate to avoid coming in last in the heats. Key heats feature obstacles like screeching death-manatees, molten metal factories, and toddler cocaine mills. Carl and Donut work with allies like [[damascus]], [[corky]], [[bruna-the-gnu]], [[imani-c]], and others.
- **Deity Interventions and Climax**: As the heats progress, the system becomes highly unstable. The crawlers manage to summon [[khepri]] and slay the sun god [[emberus]] (destroying his physical manifestation). Ultimately, the crawlers navigate the final heat, culminating in the destruction of [[scolopendra]]'s physical avatar and transitioning the surviving crawlers forward.
