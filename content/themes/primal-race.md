# The Primal Race

The **Primal** race is an ancient, mythological progenitor race in the Dungeon Crawler Carl universe. In the dungeon, it is available as a selection option for crawlers, though it is rarely chosen.

## Lore
The Primals are the first known species to conquer the universe. Often referred to as the boogiemen of the cosmos, they spread across the galaxy and then suddenly vanished. Left behind were ruins of their civilization. When a species discovers a Primal remnant, the resident AI (if it is still sane) usually takes the form of the discovering species. Some systems worship them as gods, while mothers use them as scary stories to frighten children.

## Dungeon Mechanics
- **Blank Slate**: Crawlers who choose the Primal race retain their original appearance (e.g., Carl still looks human).
- **Stat Penalty**: Choosing Primal incurs a -1 penalty to all base stats (essentially starting five levels behind).
- **Uncapped Skills**: The primary benefit of the Primal race is that all higher-tier skills are unlocked and can be trained to Level 20 (whereas regular races cap earlier).
- **Earth Classes**: Selecting Primal unlocks exclusive Earth-based classes.

## Narrative Signficance
Carl chooses the Primal race in Chapter 51 on Mordecai's advice, recognizing that the ability to train skills to Level 20 is essential for long-term survival, despite the immediate stat penalties.
