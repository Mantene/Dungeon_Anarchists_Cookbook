# Solidarity and Duty

While the World Dungeon encourages individualistic survival and crawler-on-crawler violence for entertainment, a counter-theme of human solidarity and professional duty emerges, particularly through the Meadow Lark caretakers.

## Manifestations of the Theme

### Eldercare Protection
The night staff of the Meadow Lark retirement home—[[brandon-an]], [[chris-andrews-2]], [[yolanda-martinez-13]], and [[imani-c]]—refuse to abandon their 38 elderly residents. Despite the immense burden of moving wheel-chair and walker-bound patients through monster-infested corridors, the workers stay together, farm food for them, and escort them. Yolanda manages their daily needs, while Imani bears the massive trauma of mercy-killing residents who were horribly injured, gaining the **Crawler Killer x 12** tag.

### Safe Room Refuge
The caretakers assist the more mobile residents to take shelter in the Waffle House safe room. Brandon and Chris, originally maintenance workers, use their technical skills to build modular defenses, collaborating with [[carl]] to build "The Speedbump" redoubt to defeat the Borough Boss.

### Carl and Donut's Alliance
In [[027-book-1-chapter-27-second-floor]], Carl and Donut face a choice: leave the Meadow Lark group behind and progress to Floor 2, or stick with them to secure the staircase. Though Donut acts like she doesn't care, they both agree to help. Carl and Donut go down first to clear a safe path to the nearest safe room for the elderly residents, choosing solidarity over pure self-interest.
