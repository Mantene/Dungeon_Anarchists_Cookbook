# Over City Mechanics

The **Over City** (Floor 3) introduces complex urban rules, environments, and environmental cycles that differ from the first two floors of the World Dungeon.

## Floor Layout & Setting
- **Volcano Crater**: The floor spans approximately 300,000 square kilometers (about the size of Arizona) and represents a massive volcano crater containing medieval settlements surrounded by wide swaths of abandoned, decrepit ruins.
- **Concealed Map Legends**: In place of pathfinder skills, intersections feature hidden maps pointing toward exits, saferooms, and bosses.
- **Exits**: Staircases to Floor 4 are hidden underneath city bosses or unlocked upon completing specific quests.

## Day/Night Cycles
- **Daytime Rules**: Villages are safe zones. The ruins contain normal mobs (e.g., Circus Lemurs) that are relatively easy to handle. Village guards (Level 75) protect population centers.
- **Nighttime Rules**: The ruins become highly lethal. Only the main roads of villages are safe; alleys and ruins are swarmed by dangerous nocturnal mobs.
- **Janitor Mobs (Roomba Mobs)**: At night, "Street Urchins" (Level 8 spiky tumbleweed-like creatures) emerge to devour corpses, debris, and refuse to clean the streets. They are non-hostile unless blocked or attacked.

## NPCs & Serials
- **Biologically Printed NPCs**: The local populations are owned and engineered by Borant. Their memories are wiped and altered between seasons to match custom local storylines.
- **Elites**: Unique, named NPCs (like [[tsarina-signet]] or [[ringmaster-grimaldi]]) who have "plot armor." They act out scripted drama series for the viewers. If a crawler attacks them outside the narrative, they suffer severe penalties.
