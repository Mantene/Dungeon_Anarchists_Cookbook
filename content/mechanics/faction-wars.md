# Faction Wars

The Floor 9 mechanic. Ten teams fight for control of Larracos and its throne room. Winner gets survival guarantee and Ascendency access. The most politically consequential mechanic in the series.

---

## Basic Structure

**Floor:** 9 (Larracos, City of Dreams)  
**Timer:** 30 days, or 90 hours after a winner is declared  
**Stairwells:** Appear only after Faction Wars ends, or 30 hours before floor collapse  
**Teams:** 10 (9 attacking + 1 defending NPC team, as of Book 7)

**Win conditions:**
- **Attackers:** Hold the Larracos throne room for 6 consecutive hours
- **Defenders (NPCs):** Hold the castle for a full week without attackers holding the throne room

---

## Phases

| Phase | Duration | Rules |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **The Ceasefire** | 60 hours | Build army; recruit; no combat |
| **The Ramp-Up** | 180 hours | No offensive magic; enter enemy territory only through designated 1km-wide corridors |
| **Open Hostilities** | Until 4 teams remain | Full magic enabled |
| **The Peeling of Larracos** | Until win condition | Triggered when 4 teams remain; NPC/throne room contest |

---

## Teams

Faction spots can be **inherited** from defunct teams. Carl and Donut inherited spots from the Dreadnoughts and the Burrowers after Book 5.

Teams must have a **warlord** (crawlers only) and can have a **sponsor** (off-world entity bankrolling the operation). Sponsors were originally protected from harm — they could send mercenaries without personal risk.

### Key Rule Change — Book 6

Carl forced a public audience vote on pending action items. Result:

> **Remove Safety Protections for Faction Sponsors: PASSED 98%**

Sponsors can now be physically killed during Faction Wars. This single vote transformed the entire strategic landscape of Floor 9.

### NPC Home Team — Book 6

A second vote (52%) created the **NPC Home Team** (Team Retribution), with Juice Box and Ferdinand the cat as co-warlords. First NPC-led team in dungeon history.

---

## Mercenaries

Factions can hire mercenaries. Sources:
- Off-world contracted soldiers (standard)
- Former crawlers (the Homecoming Queen deployed 50,000 this way in Book 7, approved by the AI)
- Inherited mercenary slots from defunct factions

---

## Emergency Action Items

Each team has **3 emergency action items** per season. Carl used one to force the audience vote in Book 6.

---

## The Princess Posse Victory (Book 7)

Eight of the nine attacking teams were eliminated. Team Retribution (NPCs) forfeited when Ferdinand and Juice Box jumped into the Nothing per Justice Light's plan. The Princess Posse won by holding the throne room.

Full elimination order and methods: see [Princess Posse](../factions/princess-posse.md).
