# T'Ghee Card System

The core mechanic of Floor 8 (The Ghosts of Earth). A monster-summoning card game played during Phase 2 battles. Each squad builds a deck of totem cards and duels opposing squads for floor keys.

---

## Overview

Floor 8 splits into three phases. Phase 2 requires crawlers to defeat 10 card-game opponents to collect tokens for an exit key. The card system governs those battles.

The Squad Leader controls the deck. Bodyguards cannot cast spells during card battles (they can use physical abilities). The Midnight Express special card temporarily makes a bodyguard the deckmaster.

---

## Card Types

| Type | Function |
|------|----------|
| **Totem** | Summon a monster to fight |
| **Utility** | Buff an active totem |
| **Snare** | Debuff or control opponent's totems |
| **Mystic** | Permanent hand spells (non-consumable) |
| **Special** | Consumable one-time effects |

---

## Totem Rarities

Common → Uncommon → Rare → Very Rare → Legendary → Mythic → **Unique**

Utility card slots per totem:
- Uncommon: 2 slots
- Rare: 3 slots
- Unique: 7 slots

---

## Special Mechanics

| Mechanic | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| **Combo Card** | Fuse two totems into a single hybrid creature |
| **Glow-Up** | Permanently encode a totem's current buffed state onto the card |
| **Midnight Express** | Temporarily makes a bodyguard the deckmaster |

**Squad size:** Up to 6 active totems (8 with champion pack backup slots). One totem card can be kept when descending to the next floor.

---

## Ethical Dimension

Crawlers who die near the end of floor can be turned into T'Ghee cards by other crawlers — trapping them as totems. This is legal but ethically horrifying. A card can be **ripped** to free the person inside.

Sister Ines Quiteria did this to the dying Paz Lo (Book 6) — trapping him against his will. Donut later ripped the card at Paz's request, freeing him.
