# Race and Class System

Race and class selection happens on Floor 3 and is permanent for the rest of the crawl (except Donut's unique Character Actor class mechanic, which grants a new specialty each floor). The system is the most consequential decision any crawler makes.

---

## When It Happens

Race and class are chosen at the **Floor 3 tutorial guild**. Until then, stats cannot be freely distributed — they auto-allocate to race/class minimums once selected, with the remainder freely assignable.

**Order:** Race first. Then class. Race selection can lock in certain class options or unlock others.

---

## Race Selection

Races range from cosmetic (Carl's Primal looks identical to human) to transformative (Donut's Cat gives her quadruped movement, claws, 9 Lives). Some races impose gameplay restrictions; some offer hidden benefits.

Notable Earth-crawler races chosen in the series:

| Crawler | Race | Key Feature |
|---------|------|-------------|
| Carl | Primal | Blank slate; unlocks all higher-tier skills to Level 20; -1 to all base stats |
| Donut | Cat | No change in appearance; 9 Lives (halves damage from first 9 physical attacks/day); quadruped UI |
| Katia Grim | Doppelganger | Full shapeshifting into any observed form; equippable mass via backpack |
| Chris Andrews 2 | Igneous | Rock/lava humanoid; personality shift noted post-selection |
| Dmitri + Maxim Popov | Nodling | Two-headed ogre; special death mechanic — splits into two baby nodlings, who can be declared ineligible |
| Mrs. McGibbons | Frost Maiden | Cryomancy-focused; tiny blue-skinned form |
| Imani C. | Obsidian Butterfly | Insectoid; giant butterfly wings that buff allies when they pass through |
| Miriam Dom | Later: Vampire | Vampire race selected mid-series (after Floor 3); drank blood from Prepotente's kills |

**Primal (Carl's race):** The extinct cosmic progenitors. A blank slate that imposes -1 to all starting stats but unlocks all higher-tier skills to Level 20. Looks identical to human. No one else in the series chose this race.

---

## Class Selection

Class is chosen after race. Some classes have prerequisites (Carl's Compensated Anarchist required 500B+ views AND Level 5 Explosives Handling — both conditions met by the time he reached Floor 3).

"Earth-specific" classes are added each season, designed around the planet's culture.

Notable classes:

| Crawler | Class | Key Feature |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| Carl | Compensated Anarchist | Monk/Rogue hybrid; Escape Plan skill; trap/bomb focus; Charisma-based |
| Donut | Former Child Actor | Gains Manager Benefit — permanently binds Mordecai as her manager |
| Carl (subclass Floor 6) | Agent Provocateur | +1 INT/level; Advanced Bomb Maker's Workshop; explosives specialization |
| Donut (Floor 11) | Assassin of Sekhmet | Warrior-melee; Perpetual Tank; Cockroach to Level 15; Healer's Grasp aura |

---

## Donut's Character Actor Mechanic

Donut's **Former Child Actor** class includes a **Character Actor** specialty that changes each floor. This gives her a new batch of abilities every floor — but she loses the base class-granted skill levels when the specialty changes.

**Critical rule:** If Donut uses a class-given skill at least once before the floor ends, she keeps the levels she earned through use (not the base class grant). This means she can accumulate skills across floors by strategically using each new specialty's abilities immediately.

Her specialties by floor:

| Floor | Specialty | Key Gains |
|-------|-----------|-----------|
| 3 | (chose class) | Full Former Child Actor; Manager Benefit; binds Mordecai |
| 4 | Football Hooligan | Mascot skill; CON +10; Guinness skill (double strength when drunk) |
| 5 | Glass Cannon | Lowered spell costs; faster spell training; _Astral Paw_ |
| 6 | Legendary Diva | All spells cast through singing; _Laundry Day_; _Standing Ovation_; _Encore_; _Entourage_ |
| 7 | (skipped — Floor 7 destroyed) | — |
| 8 | Master Telephone Psychic | Retains Bard abilities + psionic spells (_Brain Freeze_, _Bad Attitude_, _Why Are You Hitting Yourself?_) |
| 9 | Deathbed Hellcat | Sniper/assassin; invisibility; 4× spell range; instakill chance; ghillie suit |
| 10 | (not specified) | — |
| 11 | Assassin of Sekhmet | Perpetual Tank; Cockroach L15; Healer's Grasp |

---

## Stat Distribution

After race/class selection, stat points distribute automatically to race/class minimums. The remainder is freely assignable.

Crawlers receive **3 stat points per level** but cannot use them until race/class selection on Floor 3. This means a crawler arriving at Floor 3 at Level 13 has 39 accumulated stat points to distribute.
