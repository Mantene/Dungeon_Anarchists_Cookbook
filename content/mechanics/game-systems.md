# Core Game Systems

The World Dungeon operates as both a resource reclamation mechanism and a for-profit intergalactic TV show. All mechanics are enforced by the Syndicate neutral observer AI.

---

## Stats

Five base stats visible to crawlers:

| Stat | Effect |
|------|--------|
| **Strength** | Melee damage, carrying capacity |
| **Intelligence** | Mana pool size, spell effectiveness, scroll duration |
| **Constitution** | Health pool, regeneration speed, potion cooldown timing |
| **Dexterity** | Speed, dodge, jumping ability |
| **Charisma** | Mob-charm effectiveness, negotiation, NPC reactions |

Average adult human: 3–5 in each stat. Hidden "Wisdom" stat exists but is not adjustable (changing it would alter personality).

**Stat distribution:** Crawlers receive 3 stat points per level but cannot distribute them until race/class selection (Floor 3). Points auto-distribute to race/class minimums; remainder freely assignable.

---

## Skills

Hundreds of skills that level through use. Visible in the Skills menu (filterable). Examples: Pugilism, Unarmed Combat, Explosives Handling, Magic Missile, Chopper Pilot, Slingshot, Aiming, Second Chance, Smush, Foot Soldier.

Skill descriptions are narrated by various AI "voices" — often themed to the skill type (the foot fetish voice for Smush, the game show voice for most loot).

---

## Inventory

- Unlimited dimensional storage; items time-locked (food never spoils)
- Must physically lift item for ~4 seconds to store it
- Cannot store living creatures (they die)
- Items retain their properties but not momentum when stored
- Quadrupeds (Donut) have a different UI; can pull non-slotted items into inventory without touching

---

## Party System

- Shared XP by participation percentage
- Party leader = member with highest combined stats
- Group chat (text-based, haptic buzz on receipt; Donut types in all caps)
- All party members visible as colored dots on minimap
- Sleeping crawlers go translucent and invisible on the map

---

## Rating System

Views → Followers → Favorites → Patrons

- **Views:** Any viewer watching for ~8 seconds counts as a View
- **Followers:** Bookmarked your crawler ID; get updates on your activity
- **Favorites:** Live stat/condition updates; limited number per viewer; a real endorsement
- **Patrons:** Sponsors (usually organizations) who buy loot boxes for you. More favorites = better patron bids.

**Key rule:** Loot box contents are NOT random. They are influenced by viewer engagement and sponsor interests. Staying entertaining = better loot. Complaining about missing items on camera = comedy for viewers = more loot of whatever you're missing.

**Patron auction system (starting Floor 4):** All 3 patron slots auctioned for one day per floor (Floors 4, 5, 6). Patrons who pay above the standard fee get loot box discounts; those who pay less pay more per box. Sponsors can transfer patronage to others starting Floor 7.

## Boss Types

In order of difficulty/tier:

1. **Neighborhood** — Bronze boss box, neighborhood map (populates ~1 sq mile of map)
2. **Borough** — Silver boss box, borough field guide (shows mob types in nearby quadrants)
3. **City** — Gold boss box
4. **Province** — Platinum boss box
5. **Country** — Legendary boss box
6. **Floor** — Celestial boss box

**Elites:** Special high-difficulty bosses not tied to the hierarchy.

All bosses drop persistent loot (maps, field guides) accessible to any crawler who finds the corpse. The killing party also gets a boss box and a bronze/silver/etc. star by their name.

Neighborhood and Borough bosses on Floors 1–3 can leave their rooms (learned in Floor 2: Krakaren Clone exits her chamber before the boss fight is triggered).

---

## Loot Boxes

Tiers: Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Legendary → Celestial

Types include: Adventurer, Weapon, Pet, Apparel, Shoe, Goblin, Brawler, Crowd Control, Survivor, Mechanic, Asshole (for morally questionable achievements), Benefactor (patron-sent, most valuable).

Contents are NOT random. They are algorithmically influenced by viewer behavior, sponsor interests, and the AI's entertainment calculus. Complaining about missing items on camera is essentially a request.

---

## Safe Rooms

Located throughout every floor. Appear on minimap even through fog of war. Rules:
- No combat (mobs teleport away if they attack; crawlers freeze for 100/3600 seconds on first/second violation; third violation = stripped and teleported to mob nest)
- **Close 1 hour before floor collapse** (policy change announced at start of Floor 2)
- Each room has a unique theme (fast food restaurant, bus station, storm shelter, Waffle House, Big Shot Chicken, etc.)
- Bopca Protector NPCs in some rooms (cook food, provide rooms, but won't discuss dungeon strategy)
- Free rental rooms on Floors 1–2; personal spaces purchasable Floor 4+

## Dungeon Codex

Crawlers who invent stackable weapons/devices/potions are entered in the Dungeon Codex. They earn 1 gold coin per kill when other crawlers use their recipe. Carl's Jug O' Boom is in the Codex. This income continues even after the season if the crawler survives.

---

## The Desperado Club

Access granted via Desperado Pass tattoo (automatic reward for killing 20+ non-combatants in one attack). Both Carl and Donut have this tattoo. Holding a Desperado Pass negates the ability to obtain a Vanquisher pass (whatever that is). The Club's nature and location are classified by Mordecai ("you'll learn about the third floors soon enough").
