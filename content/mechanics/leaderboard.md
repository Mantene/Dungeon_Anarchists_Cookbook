# Leaderboard and Bounty System

The public ranking of the dungeon's top crawlers. Being on the leaderboard means better loot, more viewers — and a price on your head.

---

## How the Leaderboard Works

Rankings are calculated from a combination of: levels, boss kills, social metrics (views/followers/favorites), and overall performance. The exact algorithm is not public. Leaderboard positions are announced publicly at each floor transition.

**The leaderboard populates** upon the collapse of Floor 3. Before that, only social metrics are visible.

**Suspension:** When fewer than 30 crawlers remain, the leaderboard is suspended. Carl and Donut's "final rankings" of #2 and #3 were set when the leaderboard froze.

---

## Bounties

The top 10 crawlers each carry a bounty — a gold reward for any crawler who kills them. Other crawlers can claim bounties.

**Bounty doubling rule:** All top-10 crawlers who survive a complete floor with a bounty have their bounties doubled going forward.

**Starting bounties (Book 2 leaderboard):**

| Rank | Bounty |
|------|--------|
| #1 | 1,000,000 gold |
| #2 | 500,000 gold |
| #3 | 400,000 gold |
| #4 | 300,000 gold |
| #5 | 200,000 gold |
| #6–10 | 100,000 gold each |

By Book 4, Carl's bounty had reached 1,000,000 (×2) as the #1 crawler. By Book 7, he had a 6,000,000 gold bounty.
