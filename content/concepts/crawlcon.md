# CrawlCon

**CrawlCon** is a massive, biannual fan convention held in Syndicate space, themed around *Dungeon Crawler World*.

## Details & Features
- **Attendance**: Attracts thousands of physical attendees and billions of virtual visitors from across the galaxy. 
- **Events**: Features panels, cosplay, vendors, art contests, and meet-and-greets.
- **Crawler Participation**: Active crawlers are rarely selected to participate during a live crawl due to safety and containment protocols. When they are featured (via virtual holographic presence), tickets and VIP lottery slots are sold for immense sums.
- **The 1938 Connection**: According to [[mordecai]], prep teams began landing on Earth in 1936 to harvest concepts. The first sci-fi convention on Earth (Philadelphia, 1938) was heavily influenced by Syndicate presence.

## Carl's Schedule
In [[166-book-5-chapter-21-crawlcon-invitation]] (Chapter 21), Carl receives a VIP badge (from his Platinum Fan Box) scheduling him for three virtual events at CrawlCon:
1. **Art Contest**: Judging juvenile quickdraw drawings alongside two other guests.
2. **Crawling Through the Ages Panel**: Leading a Q&A discussion regarding the crawl's history, moderated by Circe Took of the Hive.
3. **Autograph and Keepsake Session**: Signing merchandise and posing for pictures, including a duo likeness session with the Popov Brothers.
