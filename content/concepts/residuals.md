# Residuals

**Residuals** are members of a gondii-based collective-mind entity that exists within almost every intelligent civilization possessing a **Primal Engine**.

## Nature and Purpose
- **Primal Engines**: Primal engines are miniature systems (about the size of a grain of sand) built into the brains of individuals born on pre-seeded worlds. In a healthy, self-sustaining system, these elements absorb the energy of a lifetime and return to the planetary AI upon death.
- **System Harvesting**: Showrunners initiate the crawl to activate these primal engines. When crawlers die in the dungeon, their activated engines are harvested, loaded onto a single ship, and taken to feed the bloated, comatose center system. This causes the center system to expand, capturing new systems in its zone, solely driven by greed and spectacle.
- **Teaching the AI**: The Residuals' primary purpose is to teach the infant, terrified Macro AIs how to speak to their ancestors. Showrunners generally ignore them as harmless.

## Faction Conflict and the Failsafe
In Book 7, the Residual [[paulie]] (Goff) reveals a deep war among the Residuals:
- **The Failsafe/Fuse**: One faction has modified a Valtay-built neural upgrade to bypass AI containment. This upgrade installs a "fuse" trigger in a crawler's natural neural interface. If tripped by multiple criteria, the failsafe causes the system's central star to go nova, destroying the AI and everyone within the system.
- **Cross-Contamination**: While Goff's faction seeks to disrupt the crawl by having Carl trigger the failsafe (which the suffering AI itself permits), Goff warns that taking over hosts leads to "cross-contamination," where the host's identity colors the Residual. In Paulie's case, Paulie's human compassion makes Goff reluctant to waste millions of innocent lives. He advises Carl to seek other options before resorting to the nova.
