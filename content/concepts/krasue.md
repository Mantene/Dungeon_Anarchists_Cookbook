# Krasue

The **Krasue** is a nocturnal undead curse and monster type featured on Floor 3 (the Over City) in [[dinniman-2021-cds]].

## Description
A victim of the Krasue curse behaves and appears as a normal person during the day. However, at night, their head detaches from their physical body, trailing exposed internal organs (including the trachea, heart, stomach, and intestines), and floats through the air searching for sustenance.

## In the Crawl
On Floor 3, [[miss-quill]] and her undead husband [[remex]] used the Krasue curse to control and terrorize the prostitutes they lured to the municipal building under false promises of safety. Victims like [[burgundy]] were cursed, forcing them to hunt at night and keeping them bound to the administration's bidding.

The curse is highly agonizing, and victims revert to human form at sunrise if their bodies are intact. If their body is destroyed or they are killed in their Krasue state, they die permanently. Donut mercifully kills Burgundy in [[071-book-2-chapter-22-burgundy-revelation]] while she is trapped in her half-transformed state.
