# All-Tree

The **All-Tree** is a massive, magic-infused flora system that spans the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Mechanics & Properties
- **Floor-Spanning Roots**: While crawlers encounter separate outcroppings of white-barked trees, they are all part of the same colossal tree whose roots span the entire floor. The main body of the tree lies deep below, between the disputed lands and the celestial realm.
- **Magic Deflection/Protection**: Any forest-kin (npcs with forest heritage, like wood elves, bush elves, and dryads) within a few hundred meters of an All-Tree outcropping is immune to magic attacks. The tree absorbs the casted spell and turns it directly back on the caster, typically frying them.
- **Outcropping Destruction**: When the All-Tree deflects a spell, that specific outcropping is destroyed/burned, but it naturally grows back within a few months.
- **Neutrality**: The All-Tree is a mindless force of nature and does not distinguish between factions; it shields anyone with forest blood (including hostile High Elves and friendly Bush Elves alike). The High Elf Castle is built inside a large grove of All-Trees to exploit this protection.

## Key Events
- **Ambush Misfire**: In [[170-book-5-chapter-25-future-huntress]] (Chapter 25), the Skull Empire hunter [[the-talent]] attempts to ambush Signet's camp and stupidly casts a *Lightning* scroll near the All-Tree outcropping, causing the spell to reflect and vaporize him and his partner.
