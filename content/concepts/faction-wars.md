# Faction Wars

**Faction Wars** is the tactical team-based combat system active on Floor 9 of the World Dungeon.

## Mechanics
- **Warlords**: Selected crawlers (such as Carl and Donut) become team warlords who can vote on rules and direct forces.
- **Participation**: Teams can purchase entry or spots (the Princess Posse fan club successfully funds a spot at the end of Floor 6).
- **Lethality**: Rules govern whether crawlers can directly engage and kill each other, which Carl actively attempts to vote in to bypass Syndicate restrictions.
