# Small Bugbear Settlement

The **Small Bugbear Settlement** is a minor village on Floor 6 (The Hunting Grounds) located a few hours south of [[zockau]].

## Profile & Features
- **Inhabitants**: Populated primarily by bugbears, who are engineers and rivals of the Ursine.
- **Guards**: Guarded by Level 95 **Funeral Bells**—eight-foot-tall, neckless mushroom-men with painted faces and large spears. They have no sense of humor and patrol day and night.
- **Saferoom**: Includes a Whataburger fast-food restaurant that serves as a traditional saferoom.

## Events
- **Crawler Ingress**: This is the starting settlement where [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] enter the floor.
- **Night Weasel Ambush**: In [[136-book-4-chapter-26-mouthbrooding-mayhem]] (Chapter 2), invisible Night Weasel scouts sent by hunter [[zabit]] ambush the settlement gates, paralyzing the Funeral Bell guards and Carl. Carl is rescued and carried into the saferoom by Chris Andrews.
- **Defense Prep**: Carl warns council member [[goiter]] about the hunter release, urging the bugbears to fortify the settlement.
