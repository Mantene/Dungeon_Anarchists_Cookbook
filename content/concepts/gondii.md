# Gondii

**Gondii** are a species of parasitic brain worms associated with the Valtay Corporation.

## Characteristics & Mechanics
- **Body Hijacking**: Gondii take over the host's body by entering within a few hours of death.
- **Memory Retention**: They can read the host's memories and keep the body functioning for centuries.
- **Comparison to Other Parasites**:
  - Unlike **Intellect Hunters**, who also hijack bodies but cause them to rot rapidly, Gondii prevent host decay.
  - Unlike **Infiltrators**, they are less about shape-shifting and more about direct biological usurpation.
- **Dungeon Connection**: The Valtay use Gondii to control key figures and proxy representatives in the crawl (such as [[manasa]]).
