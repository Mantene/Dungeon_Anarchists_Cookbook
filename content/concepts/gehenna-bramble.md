# Gehenna Bramble

The Gehenna Bramble is an invincible, 10-meter-high wall of blood-boiling vines that spreads from Zockau at 4.5 km/h following the failure of the Creeping Apocalypse quest, forcing crawlers toward the High Elf Castle.
