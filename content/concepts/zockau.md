# Zockau

**Zockau** is the capital metropolis of Floor 6 (The Hunting Grounds), situated in the far northern region of the map.

## Profile & Features
- **Geography**: It overlooks a low-level monster valley designed to allow hunters to train before the crawlers arrive.
- **Hunter Staging Area**: During the 30-hour grace period at the start of the floor, all 1,002 hunters are sequestered within Zockau.
- **Aesthetic**: The city has a 1950s mid-century modern aesthetic, complete with rows of pubs and a concrete-arched Desperado Club.
- **Guards**: Guarded by bear-headed humanoids in heavy armor.
- **The Stairwell**: Contains the central stairwell and exit to Floor 7, surrounded by a manicured park.

## Preemptive Raid
In [[150-book-5-chapter-5-left-to-fester]] (Chapter 5), [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] execute a surprise raid on Zockau using *Teleport to Stairwell*. Carl detonates high-yield thermobaric barrel bombs that flatten a major portion of the city square, collapse the Desperado Club's facade (trapping the hunters within), and permanently kill 34 hunters (including [[xindy]] and [[chin-dua]]).
