# Fort Freedom

**Fort Freedom** is a heavily fortified high-elf stronghold and castle located on Floor 6 (The Hunting Grounds). It serves as the primary obstacle in the first phase of Tsarina [[tsarina-signet]]'s questline, [[vengeance-of-the-daughter]].

## Overview
- **Geography**: Positioned on a steep slope overlooking a river, with the southern bank left undefended.
- **Defenses**: Protected by magical wards, automated traps, and a garrison of high-elf soldiers, were-castors, were-beavers, and bush elves.
- **Command**: Commanded by [[claude-sludgington-the-fourth]], a Level 65 Borough Boss (Eryops Gigantis).

## Narrative Events
During the events of [[170-book-5-chapter-25-future-huntress]] through [[174-book-5-chapter-29-tattooing-nadine]], Tsarina Signet orchestrates an amphibious assault on the fortress.
- **Assault Strategy**: [[edgar]] the tortoise casts a massive stealth spell to hide their army as they approach the slope. [[carl]] utilizes [[samantha]] as a distraction by taping a loud alarm playing "Wonderwall" to her head and throwing her into the river.
- **The Trap**: The high-elf garrison sets a trap inside the castle, resulting in the deaths of several rebels and the wiping out of [[langley]]'s Finnish archer squad on the other shore.
- **Borough Boss Confrontation**: Carl inadvertently teleports the Borough Boss [[claude-sludgington-the-fourth]] onto the muddy shore, leading to a brutal combat where [[nadine]] is vaporized, and Claude is ultimately killed by Carl, Donut, and [[mongo]].
- **Aftermath**: Following the boss's defeat, the rebel forces capture the fortress, completing Part One of Signet's vengeance quest. The were-castors subsequently dam the entrance to the fortress to clean up.
