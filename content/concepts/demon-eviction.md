# Demon Eviction

A Demon Eviction is a system event triggered by excessive death and Sheol overflow. Evicted demons are ejected from Sheol and possess weak undead shells (zombies/skeletons) or dead bodies. If they grab a crawler, they drag them into Sheol, causing instant death. Cuba experiences a Demon Eviction event due to zombie farm animal containment failure in the global ocean.
