# The Nothing

**The Nothing** is the void space or deletion hazard existing behind the walls and portals of the World Dungeon.

## Characteristics
- **Hazard**: Falling or being pulled into the Nothing results in deletion or permanent exile from the crawl, unless protected by specialized spells or artifacts.
- **Floor 8 Collapse**: At the end of Floor 8, the Nothing is broken and drained into Sheol, the Ascendency, and Scolopendra's Lair, causing a massive structural breach and awakening the final boss.
