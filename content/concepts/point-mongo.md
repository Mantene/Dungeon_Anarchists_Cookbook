# Point Mongo

**Point Mongo** is the renamed designation of the [[large-ursine-settlement]] on Floor 6 (The Hunting Grounds).

## History & Takeover
- **Renaming**: Following the death of the puritanical mayor [[elmer]], [[princess-donut]] inherits the office of Grand (Mayor) of the settlement. In [[156-book-5-chapter-11-the-recital]], she officially renames the town "Point Mongo" in honor of her pet velociraptor, [[mongo]].
- **Reforms**: Donut uses the [[enchanted-collar-charm-of-the-effete-bourgeoisie]] to assume leadership, immediately dissolving all of Elmer's puritanical regulations. She legalizes swearing, dancing, and alcohol, and specifically permits dinosaurs and naiads within the city.
- **Administration**: Under Donut's new administration, several local guards are assigned names (e.g., [[miss-nance]], [[dinallo]], [[doctor-el]], and [[ice-man]]). The town becomes the staging ground for resolving [[the-recital]] questline.
