# Fantasy Hunter Camp

**Fantasy Hunter Camp** is a commercial training facility and simulation run in Syndicate space.

## Details & Function
- **Purpose**: It allows wealthy Syndicate citizens and tourists to pretend they are crawlers or hunters on the show.
- **Renting the Moon**: Faction leaders like [[epitome-tagg]] of [[the-dream]] rent out entire moons run by hunter companies (such as Vrah's family) to let their employees and family members play for a few days.
- **Faction Wars Pipeline**: The best-performing players at these fantasy camps are awarded positions in the official Faction Wars armies, which sometimes leads to them being recruited to enter the real World Dungeon as actual hunters.
- **Akland's Origin**: The hunter [[akland]] was a simple soy shipping accountant who won a spot in Faction Wars through fantasy camp, and was subsequently lured to the Hunting Grounds by showrunner money, illustrating how the Syndicate recruits civilian tourists into real-world combat.
