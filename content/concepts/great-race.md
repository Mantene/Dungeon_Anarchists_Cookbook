# The Great Race

**The Great Race** is the seventh floor of the World Dungeon, introduced at the end of Book 5.

## Overview & Design
- **Partnership**: The floor was designed and funded in partnership with the **Valtay Corporation** to secure capital and complete the project on time.
- **Structure**: The floor consists of several hundred kilometers of twisting, turning tubes and branches forming a massive maze. All paths lead to the exit.
- **Level Collapse**: The level collapse timer is set to **20 days**, a significantly shorter duration than previous floors, forcing crawlers to advance quickly.

## Rules & Mechanics
- **Starting Pods**: Crawlers enter in starting pods that slide into starting chutes in order, pulling them into the maze.
- **Tube Capacity & Closures**: Once a path is traversed by 10 crawlers or a single party, it closes for a duration based on difficulty.
- **Zero Tubes**: Most major nodes contain a "zero tube" (a path with no danger). Zero tubes can only be used once, creating a race to reach them first.
- **Squad Restrictions**: Parties with more than five members are split into squads of five or less. Squads cannot be broken on this floor. Carl and Donut form a squad of two, with Donut as the Squad Leader.
