# T'Ghee Card Combat

**T'Ghee Card Combat** is a card-deck based combat system introduced on Floor 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth") for squad battles.

## Mechanics and Deck Construction

### Capture and Squad Composition
- **Totem Capture**: Squad leaders receive 20 flags (such as Princess Donut's heart-covered boxers). Planting a flag in a local folklore mob or boss at less than 5% health converts them into a T'Ghee totem card.
- **Deck Setup**: A functioning deck must consist of exactly six active Totem cards. Excess cards can be stored, traded, or stolen, but only one card can be kept at the end of Floor 8.
- **Card Types**:
  1. **Totem Cards**: The summonable monsters (e.g., [[geraldo]], [[yago]], [[skylar-spinach]]).
  2. **Utility Cards**: Buffs played on active ally totems (remain active throughout combat).
  3. **Snare Cards**: Debuffs played on active enemy totems.
  4. **Mystic Cards**: Magical spells that target squad leaders or their decks.
  5. **Special Cards**: Unique, often one-time-use or consumable cards (e.g., *Temporarily Combine* cards).

### Combat Rules and Gameplay
- **Activation**: Deck combat is initiated when targeted by aggro from card-wielding mobs or when entering squad-versus-squad battles.
- **Hand and Draw**: Squad leaders start with a hand of 4 random cards. Playing a card triggers a replacement draw from the deck after a 10-second delay. Squad leaders can discard one card to the discard pile once every 30 seconds.
- **Squad Leader Restrictions**:
  - **Lockout**: Upon entering deck combat, the squad leader's normal spells, inventory access, and magical skills are locked.
  - **Permitted Actions**: They can still engage in physical melee combat and use items physically on their person (potions, wands, scrolls).
  - **Target Protection**: Summoned totems cannot directly attack an opposing squad leader if enemy totems are active on the field. The other squad members must defend their squad leader during this lockout.
  - **Exhaustion**: The lockout ends once all cards in the deck have been played or discarded.
- **Totem Performance**: Totems are summoned for a fixed duration. If killed, they are knocked out for the rest of combat. Injured totems do not heal between summons in the same combat.
- **Saferoom Practice Arena**: Saferooms provide a practice arena for mock card combat. Consumable cards used here are still permanently lost, and cooldowns do not reset.
