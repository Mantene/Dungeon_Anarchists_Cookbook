# Emergency Action Items

**Emergency Action Items** are special, high-priority rules and changes that can be proposed by Warlords during or prior to the Faction Wars phase of the World Dungeon.

## Mechanics
- **Proposals**: Each Faction Wars team is limited to proposing exactly three Emergency Action Items.
- **AI Decision**: Unlike regular action items, Emergency Action Items are not voted on by other teams; instead, they are decided immediately by the system AI.
- **Fairness & Showmanship**: Proposing items politely and providing options that maintain the fairness or entertainment value of the crawl makes the AI significantly more likely to decide in favor of the proposal.

## Carl's Emergency Action Item (Floor 8 Climax)
In Chapter 72, Carl uses his first Emergency Action Item to bypass the voting deadlock caused by corporate teams refusing to vote:
- **Proposal**: Since the Princess Posse must descend early and other teams are refusing to vote, Carl proposes that the AI decide all pending action items or let the viewers vote.
- **Result**: Passed in their favor. The AI presents the pending items to the viewers for rapid 30-second votes.
  - **Remove Safety Protections for Faction Sponsors**: Passed by 98%, allowing crawlers to kill sponsors on Floor 9.
  - **Tenth Team (Home Team)**: Passed by 52%, establishing a tenth team populated by NPCs based in Larracos, co-warlorded by [[juice-box]] and [[ferdinand]].
  - **Artillery Range & Field Size**: Physics limits on artillery range are removed and battlefield size is increased to accommodate the massive combatant numbers.
  - **Pet Leveling Accelerated**: Pet leveling is accelerated during Faction Wars (passed by 83%).
