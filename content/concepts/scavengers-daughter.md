# Scavenger's Daughter

The **Scavenger's Daughter** is a legendary utility patch/item wielder tool owned by [[carl]].

## Overview and Mechanics
- **Function**: It allows Carl to scan, analyze, and copy structure components, wiring, and machinery inside the dungeon.
- **Upgrades**: Over the floors, it is fed and upgraded using metals, Sheol bricks, and specialized tools, allowing it to interface with increasingly complex systems (such as the orc synergy towers and clocktowers on Floor 8).
- **Synergy with Card Combat**: Carl utilizes the Scavenger's Daughter to physically interface with the deck and towers during the Floor 8 card battles.
