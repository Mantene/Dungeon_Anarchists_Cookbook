# Ghosts of Earth (Floor 8)

**The Ghosts of Earth** is the eighth floor of the World Dungeon. Due to [[prepotente]]'s use of a level-skipping cheat on the sixth floor, crawlers bypassed the seventh floor entirely and generated directly onto Floor 8.

## Mechanics and Environment

### Phase Structure and Timing
- **Total Duration**: 21 days until level collapse.
- **Phase One**: Lasts 14 days. Crawlers are restricted to their starting regions, locked inside safe areas initially before being allowed to explore within a 99-kilometer radius (or 6 kilometers offshore for islands).
- **Phase Two (Assault Phase)**: Starts at Day 14. Squads are relocated to a different geographic region where they must fight to secure an exit key.

### Replay Environment
- **A Memory of Earth**: The floor is a precise physical replica of Earth as it was on December 10th, shortly before the system collapse.
- **Intangible Inhabitants**: Human and animal populations exist as non-corporeal, intangible ghosts. They are invisible on crawler maps and cannot perceive or interact with crawlers. Passing through them leaves them unaffected, though doing so can physically strip their clothes or items from their bodies.
- **Tangible Environment**: Non-biological, unattached items (vehicles, buildings, food, weapons, soil) maintain physical mass, momentum, and collision. Piling barriers in the street causes car crashes, but the ghostly drivers continue floating toward their destinations unbothered.
- **Loot Limitations**: Any Earth items or weapons looted from this floor turn to dust upon the level's collapse, preventing crawlers from stockpiling modern Earth weaponry.

### Folklore Mobs and Totem Captures
- **Regional Folklore**: The floor is seeded with mobs, bosses, and legendary creatures unique to the folklore of the region. (e.g., vampires and werewolves in Romania; sasquatches in the Pacific Northwest; Orishas and Monk Seals in Cuba).
- **Squad Configurations**: Crawlers are grouped into unbreakable squads of up to 5. Squad leaders receive 20 flags to capture folklore monsters at less than 5% health, converting them into [[t-ghee-card-combat]] totems. Squad capacity is expanded to accommodate up to 6 captured monsters (squad size of 7 for solo crawlers, 11 for a squad of 5).
