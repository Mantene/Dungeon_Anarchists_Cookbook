# Were-Castors

**Were-Castors** (were-beavers) are a sub-race of Were-Kin on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Origins & Lore
- **Chee Transformation**: The were-castors were originally children of the **Chee**, a peaceful carpenter race. When the High Elves invaded their lands, Princess [[imogen]] cast a transfiguration curse on the schoolboys, turning them into were-castors (while their schoolteacher [[nadine]] was turned into a giant caterpillar).
- **Survival**: Having spent their lives running and fighting, the surviving were-castors became an elite, battle-hardened fighting force aligned with [[tsarina-signet]].

## Forms & Traits
- **Chee Form**: In their humanoid form, they are three-foot-tall, stocky Rambo-like warriors with elf-like features.
- **Beaver Form**: They can transform into giant beavers covered in oily brown fur, with buck teeth, a lisp, and a flat black tail.
- **Abilities**: 
  - **Immunities**: Immune to the paralysis effect of night weasels.
  - **Sensory**: Highly sensitive hearing/smell, allowing them to track invisible enemies.
  - **Construction**: Possess expert carpenter and fortification skills, allowing them to rapidly construct wooden dams and picket defenses for towns (like Point Mongo) in their turned forms.
  - **Aggro Draw**: Possess high constitution and the ability to draw threat/aggro from hostile mobs.
