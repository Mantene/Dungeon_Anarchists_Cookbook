# Large Ursine Settlement

The **Large Ursine Settlement** is a major town on Floor 6 (The Hunting Grounds) located 40 kilometers south of the [[small-bugbear-settlement]]. It sits across a 250-foot covered wooden bridge.

## Profile & Features
- **Inhabitants**: Populated by Ursine (bear-like humanoids) who are extremely religious, moralistic, and hypocritical.
- **Aesthetic**: Very clean streets, manicured shrubs and flowers, and neatly arranged shops. It features a Club Vanquisher, a **Temple of Hellik**, and a saferoom called *The Galleon's Lap* (a Spanish restaurant).
- **Rules**: The town enforces a strict set of moral codes, including:
  - No fighting, swearing, alcohol, illicit drugs, or gambling.
  - No magic on the streets.
  - No dancing or singing outside temples.
  - No macaques, naiads, or mongrels.
  - **Absolutely no dinosaurs of any kind.**

## Events
- **Mayor's Death & Takeover**: In [[155-book-5-chapter-10-the-ursine-mayors-fate]] (Chapter 10), Mayor [[elmer]] tries to execute [[mongo]] and [[princess-donut]] for violating the town's rules. Donut pushes Elmer off the bridge slope with *Astral Paw*, resulting in his death. Donut gains credit for the kill and is named the Grand (Mayor) of the settlement.
- **Rules Revision**: Donut plans to use her authority as Mayor to overturn the anti-dinosaur rule and make dancing mandatory.
