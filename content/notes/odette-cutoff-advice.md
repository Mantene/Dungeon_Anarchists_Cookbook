# Odette's Cut-Off Advice (Book 1 Epilogue)

## The Passage

At the very end of Book 1, just before Carl and Donut descend to Floor 3, Odette gives one final piece of advice. The book ends before she finishes the sentence:

> *"It's just a suggestion. I don't know if, morally, this is a good idea or not, but this will greatly increase your chances of survival. It's something to look for during class selection. It's generally only offered to crawlers with a high charisma, so if it's available, it'll be hidden somewhere amongst Donut's choices. The problem is, if you pick it, it's going to make someone very angry."*

The book ends here. Donut asks if it's time to go. The series never revisits this exact moment.

## What We Know

The constraints Odette placed on this advice:
1. It improves survival odds significantly
2. It's available only to high-CHA crawlers (Donut qualifies — CHA 25 at time of this conversation)
3. It would be hidden among Donut's Floor 3 class options
4. It will anger someone specific ("someone," singular — not "everyone")

## Candidate Interpretations

**Character Actor / Former Child Actor subspecialty.** This is the most likely candidate. Donut's eventual path makes her the most versatile performer and infiltrator in the dungeon. The class is hidden, CHA-dependent, and its power to disguise and manipulate would anger sponsors and other crawlers with vested interests in Donut remaining a "pure" showman.

**The Crown of the Sepsis Whore mechanic.** Not a class, but Donut's crown was placed deliberately. It entangled her in the Blood Sultanate bloodline, forcing events on Floor 9. Odette wouldn't frame this as a class choice, so this is unlikely to be what she meant.

**A class that breaks from viewer expectations.** Donut's viewers fell in love with her as a cat. Odette told them not to change her race. A hidden class that granted power by *exploiting* the viewer relationship (rather than just benefiting from it) would anger the sponsor network and Borant.

## Why It Matters

This is the only piece of advice Odette gave that she explicitly did not complete. Every other recommendation she made in Book 1 has been resolved. This one hasn't — 8 books later, Odette's sentence remains unfinished. Either:
- The series treats Donut's choices as the implicit answer (she picked it, whatever it was)
- Or Odette never got the chance to deliver this advice on the record, and Donut found out some other way
- Or it's setup for a Book 9 revelation
