# Chapter 379: Conscripting Lucia Mar

## Summary

Carl enters the Nest tower to confront [[lucia-mar]], who has killed Toyotomi, [[bomo]], and the defending mages. [[li-na]] appears, having gouged out her own eye and replaced it with [[li-jun]]'s celestial-quality eye, which allows her to slow time and move with incredible speed. She gives Carl a Hell-Kissed Potion of Conscription (level 18), duplicated using Li Jun's scroll of potion duplication.

Carl ascends the ramp of the tower (now configured as a steep slope with Donkey Kong-style barrel defenses). [[princess-donut]] has been battling Lucia from above, throwing barrels at her while Lucia crawls upside down on the ceiling with her dog Gustavo on an invisible leash. Donut summons her card from the 8th floor—[[raul]] the crab, now upgraded to mythic rareness and level 110.

Raul and Lucia tumble down the ramp into Carl, and all three slide to the bottom. Raul catches Lucia, holding her up in the air. Lucia begs to stop playing, appearing defeated. Despite his reservations, Carl smashes the Hell-Kissed Conscription potion on Lucia's head, conscripting her into his army.

Li Na warns Carl about the level discrepancy danger but leaves to use the original potion on Harpocrates. Carl activates Sticky Feet to climb the ramp while Donut continues her barrel defense from above.

## Characters

- **[[carl]]** — Confronts Lucia, conscripts her using the potion despite moral reservations
- **[[lucia-mar]]** — Invades the tower, battles Donut and Raul, is conscripted
- **[[li-na]]** — Gives Carl the duplicated conscription potion, leaves to conscript Harpocrates
- **[[princess-donut]]** — Fights Lucia with barrel traps, summons Raul
- **[[raul]]** — Summoned at mythic rareness, captures Lucia with dramatic dialogue
- **[[li-jun]]** — Posthumously contributes his eye and scroll to the effort

## Themes & Plot Threads

- **Moral Compromise** — Carl conscripts Lucia despite his inner reluctance ("No. Not like this. Never like this.")
- **Li Na's Sacrifice** — Gouging out her own eye for Li Jun's celestial replacement shows her determination
- **Raul's Growth** — His upgrade to mythic rareness and level 110 makes him a significant combat asset
- **Donkey Kong Defense** — The tower's barrel-rolling defense system provides both tactical advantage and dark humor

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections

The conscription of Lucia is one of the chapter's most morally complex moments. Carl's internal monologue—"Sometimes, we do what we have to do to survive"—captures the ethical degradation war forces upon people. Raul's over-the-top dialogue about "Daddy Carl" provides absurdist contrast to the grim scene.
