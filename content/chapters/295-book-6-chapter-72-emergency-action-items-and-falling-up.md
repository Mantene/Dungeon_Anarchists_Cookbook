# Chapter 295: Emergency Action Items and Falling Up

## Summary
- **Regrouping**: Carl awakens during the escape. No crawlers died in the collapse, but Mongo's wing is burned off and Katia is injured (she grows her arm back using Carl's upgraded potions).
- **Samantha Departs**: Samantha leaves to return to her "king" (Amayon/her husband). Donut rips Shi Maria's card, but the quest remains active due to Carl's tattoo. Donut also rips [[paz-lo]]'s *God Predator* card to set his soul free.
- **Emergency Action Item**: Carl proposes his first Warlord Emergency Action Item: since they must descend early and other teams refuse to vote, he asks that the AI decide or let viewers vote. The AI decides in their favor, triggering rapid-fire audience votes on pending items:
  - *Sponsor Safety*: Audience votes 98% to **remove safety protections for Faction sponsors** on Floor 9.
  - *NPC Team*: Audience votes 52% to establish a **Tenth Team (Home Team)** for Floor 9 consisting of NPCs in Larracos, co-warlorded by [[juice-box]] and [[ferdinand]].
  - *Physics & Field size*: Longer artillery range and expanded battlefield size are passed.
- **Falling Up**: Carl, Donut, and Imani stand under a neon X under the church stairwell chamber. The portal activates, sucking Cuba's structures and crawlers upward. They successfully hit the stairwell.
- **Lingering Power**: Carl forgets to discharge the Scavenger's Daughter's soul essence. A voice in his head warns him this is a dangerous mistake.
- **Survivor Count**: 33,804 crawlers survive Floor 8, an unprecedented number heading into Floor 9.

Carl's brilliant use of the Emergency Action Item weaponizes the audience's bloodlust, stripping the corporate sponsors of their safety shields for Floor 9. Transitioning to Faction Wars with over 33,000 crawlers and an allied NPC team completely upends the Valtay's plans for a controlled slaughter.
