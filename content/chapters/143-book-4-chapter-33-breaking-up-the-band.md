# Book 4 Chapter 33: Breaking Up the Band

## Summary
Katia decides to lead her own group on the next floor, Carl and Donut make a dangerous journey across the lacuna, and the refugees are successfully extracted.
- **Priestly's Notes**: Carl reads Priestly's notes in the Cookbook, describing Larracos (Floor 9 Faction Wars capital) - an inverted funnel-shaped city of museums and colleges that gets razed in the wars.
- **Extractions Completed**: Phase two extractions save 1,500 crawlers from 15 bubbles. Prepotente kills the Level 150 feral turkey god, reaching Level 55 (highest in the dungeon).
- **Surface Journey**: Carl and Donut ride their chariot across the dark lacuna back to Bubble 543. Florin, a crocodilian PK crawler, thanks Carl and is inspired by the plan to toss a feral god into the Skull Empire's camp.
- **Katia's Departure**: Katia informs Carl and Donut that she wants to operate independently on Floor 6 to lead and protect the former Daughters of Hekla (with Louis, Firas, Daniel, Florin, and Gwen's team). She stays in their party to share personal space but will lead her own path. Carl gives her Eva's venomous saber *The Left Fang of the Green Sultan*.
- **Descent**: The biplane house *Twister* is sent through a portal to Floor 6 carrying the changeling children, piloted by Skarn. Louis, Firas, Li Jun, Li Na, and Level 12 Agatha descend the stairwell. Imani and Elle hand the Gate of the Feral Gods back to Carl and go down the stairs. Katia hugs Carl and Donut and descends.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- [[the-gate-of-the-feral-gods]]: The extractions are concluded, and the gate is recovered for the final phase.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Katia separating from Carl and Donut is a bittersweet but necessary development. Her growth from a dependent, self-doubting crawler to a confident leader willing to risk her life to protect others shows the positive, transformative impact Carl has had on her.
