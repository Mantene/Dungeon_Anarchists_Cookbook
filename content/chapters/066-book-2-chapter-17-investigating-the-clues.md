# Book 2 Chapter 17: Investigating the Clues

## Summary
[[carl]] loots [[vincente]]'s corpse, finding 1,500 gold, a *Meat Hooks* spell scroll (which Donut keeps), and a black military uniform featuring a crossed-lightning-and-wand shoulder patch that reads **201st Security Group – Magical Ops**. 

They examine [[gum-gum]]'s body, finding a blood-soaked **Mysterious Letter** written in squiggly necroscript, and a **Gate Pass** signed by *Magistrate Featherfall*. 

They return to the inn, where Carl brews *Hair of the Dog* potions under the direction of a drunken [[mordecai]]. Donut opens her Fan Box, which contains a framed Instagram photo of [[beatrice]] sitting on her ex-boyfriend Brad's lap. Carl is relieved to find the image no longer upsets him. 

Carl opens his Fan Box, receiving a **xistera** (a light-metal scoop used in the sport of jai-alai/Cesta Punta). Carl straps it to his arm and tests it by hurling oranges at the inn's door, realizing it provides him with a powerful, high-speed ranged option. 

Mordecai explains that the **201st Security Group** is a cult of banished city elves who worship skyfowl as angels of the tree goddess Apito (the Oak Mother). [[fitz]] reveals that GumGum had complained about the krasue, but the Magistrate only gave her permission to move the bodies.

Carl's emotional growth is highlighted here; he realizes he has fully moved past Beatrice's betrayal. The introduction of the xistera shows the system's focus on his memory-based hobby skills, replacing his crude slingshot with a devastating mechanical launcher.
