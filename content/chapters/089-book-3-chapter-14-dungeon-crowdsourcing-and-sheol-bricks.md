# Book 3 Chapter 14: Dungeon Crowdsourcing and Sheol Bricks

## Summary
At Station 283, the party waits for the *Nightmare Express* to make its second loop. Princess Donut trains her *Hole* spell to Level 3, figuring out how to combine it with *Clockwork Triplicate* so her velociraptor, Mongo, can manifest duplicates through the hole. 

During a brief return to the Desperado Club, the rock monster Sledge buys a magical protection spell with his own money to shield Princess Donut. Donut uses the expensive Dodge Guild training room (which grants a free level upgrade) while Carl, unable to enter, sits at the bar. Carl uses his new threshold-viewing ability to examine the club's backroom portal. Seeking a way to spread information, Carl asks the crawlers in the club to form chat bubbles and fist-bumps all of them, adding 22 new contacts to his roster. A Japanese crawler named [[koki]] shares a method to broadcast messages to everyone on one's contact list, leading to a flood of trade and help requests.

The *Nightmare Express* (a black steam engine numbered **666**) arrives. Its 40 cars are open-topped but covered in chain-link cages; 14 cars contain massive, hostile monsters. The front passenger door is exit-only, requiring passengers to enter via the rear caboose and crawl across the roofs in a narrow space under the tunnel ceiling. 

Instead, Carl executes a dangerous plan: he and Donut run into the dark tunnel. As the train barrels toward them, Donut casts *Puddle Jumper* to teleport them further down the track while Carl activates *Protective Shell*. The train passes through the invulnerability shield, crushing the monsters inside the cars. However, Carl is struck by the cowcatcher. High constitution, healing potions, and his invulnerable foot save him, leaving him prone on the cowcatcher.

Carl climbs the port-side gangway and smashes into the engine cab, finding the engineers dead. The train controls are heavily overpressurized, transforming the boiler into a **Shattering Train Bomb**. Carl opens the firebox door and encounters **[[fire-brandy]]**, a Level 75 Lesser Demon MILF (Sheol stoker NPC) living inside the fire. She guides Carl through venting the valves. Carl successfully stops and restarts the train, earning the *Kept A Rollin'* achievement, the *Three Cheers for Slaughter!* achievement (for killing three neighborhood bosses with the shield collision), a **Platinum Big Daddy Box**, and three boss loot boxes. He also loots the **Steam Engineer's Key** and a chest of **Sheol Bricks** (the dried, fuel-rich corpses of non-viable demon offspring). Carl levels up three times to Level 32 and directs Donut to ride Mongo along the tracks to search the boss corpses for maps.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's willingness to throw himself in front of a speeding train demonstrates his increasing desperation and reckless tactical style. The introduction of Fire Brandy highlights the dark, twisted lore undergirding the dungeon's infrastructure—running steam engines on the corpses of demon babies is a classic example of Matt Dinniman's grimly comedic world-building.
