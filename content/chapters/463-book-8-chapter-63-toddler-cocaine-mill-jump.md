# Chapter 463: Toddler Cocaine & The Mill Jump

## Summary
The race begins. [[carl]] uses small roof explosives to clear the truck's windshield of a tommy gun obstacle. They enter the "Gasworks Screw Factory, Steel Mill, and Doll Workshop," where containment fields turn invisible and vehicles must avoid merging. The factory is filled with tiny gnome-like NPCs called knockers. 

To bypass a dangerous steel-shaping room, the *Lady Dominators* make a 90-meter jump across a gap between conveyor belts over molten metal vats. [[prepotente]]'s mount Sweety follows, and Carl uses the truck's untested rocket boost, overshooting but correcting with the Bubble Buddy shield to land on the moving belt. 

Prepotente uses missiles to knock down the Lady Dominators' shield so his familiar, Bianca, can fly in to snatch Corky. They enter a room filled with mountains of "Toddler Cocaine" (Daddy's Little Snow Drops), manufactured by the knocker-owned Coblyn Corporation. The white powder is kicked up by the cars, covering everything in dust and obscuring vision.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **"I have dreamed thee too long"**: Executing the plan to rescue Corky from the Lady Dominators during the race.
- **The Absurdity of the Crawl**: The detailed history of the Coblyn Corporation and their illegal marketing of low-dose cocaine to toddlers.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The introduction of "Toddler Cocaine" is another classic piece of dark, satirical Dungeon Crawler Carl lore, highlighting the corporate corruption and drug trade within the dungeon system. Carl's risky rocket jump shows his split-second adaptability.
