# Book 6 Chapter 12: The Rosetta Interview

## Summary
Carl and Donut travel to the orbital production studio for their interview with former crawler Rosetta Thagra on *Shadow Boxer*.
- **The Staging Area**: Carl and Donut are transported to an underwater production trailer near the space station. Quasar and Harbinger are present in the wings.
- **Rosetta's Profile**: Zev profiles [[rosetta]] (Rosetta Thagra): she is a Crest, a former 9th-edition crawler who specialized in explosives, and a contributor to the [[the-anarchist-cookbook]]. She has recently finished her indentureship contract and hosts *Shadow Boxer*, sponsored by the Open Intellect Pacifist Network.
- **Donut's Segment**: Rosetta interviews Donut, discussing how Earth television shows shaped her sapient mind. Donut, initially hostile because Rosetta is a Crest, warms to the host.
- **Carl's Segment**: Rosetta changes outfits and hair color, revealing a leg tattoo. She turns to Carl and pivots the topic: "Let's talk about your dead mother." This triggers a sudden, painful memory for Carl of his 15th birthday, when his mother poisoned his father and hanged herself in their basement.

## Characters Introduced
- [[quasar]] — Carl and Donut's lawyer (previously introduced; first appearance in Book 6).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[systemic-oppression]]**: The dungeon media exploits the crawlers' worst personal traumas for public entertainment.
- **Cookbook Legacy**: Carl recognizes Rosetta as a legendary contributor to the Anarchist's Cookbook.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Rosetta's abrupt turn to Carl's maternal trauma is a calculated shock tactic, typical of the dungeon's media machine. However, Rosetta's background as a cookbook contributor hints that her interview has a hidden, cooperative agenda beyond simple exploitation.
