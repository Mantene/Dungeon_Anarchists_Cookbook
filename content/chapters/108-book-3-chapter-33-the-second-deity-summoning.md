# Book 3 Chapter 33: The Second Deity Summoning

## Summary
Carl's plan to throw the war god Grull at the Mimic Rex in Trainyard E goes awry when he is dragged through the portal. He lands in the trainyard next to the first mantaur. The system freezes the world to initiate a televised **Death Challenge** between the Level 90 **Mimic Rex** and the Level 250 **War God Grull** (revealed to be Prince Maestro in deity form).

Once the challenge starts, the Mimic’s tongue whips the forming Grull away. Carl drinks an Invisibility Potion and *Mordecai's Special Brew* (granting 30 seconds of rapid-healing invulnerability) to survive. Grull's massive axe strikes shatter the yard, repeatedly breaking Carl's bones, but Carl's injuries are instantly healed. Katia shape-shifts into a decoy Carl to lure Grull away, assisted by Elle.

Donut, Mongo, Li Jun, and Li Na jump through the portal cart into the yard. Li Na has prepared the second mantaur as a portable summoning vessel by amputating his lower body and arms, keeping him alive with healing potions. 

Carl punches the armless mantaur to trigger the summoning. The second summoning yanks Grull from his pursuit of Katia directly into the vessel. They throw the vessel into the portal, teleporting Grull to the bottom of the abyss. The music stops, the Mimic Rex is dead, and the *Nightmare* (driven by Tizquick) arrives as Carl collapses.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Li Na's modification of the second mantaur into a portable summoning vessel highlights the brutal pragmatism required to survive the crawl. The hack of the summoning system—stealing a deity from one summoning to another without a cooldown—proves that understanding the system's hidden rules is the only way for crawlers to overcome impossible odds. Grull's ejection to the abyss resolves the threat to the trainyard, leaving the mimic dead and the escape route clear.
