# Chapter 388: Chapter 80 — Death of Fang

## Summary
In Larracos, Kiwi the allosaurus goes into active labor at Bucket Boy's clinic, prompting Donut to urge a fast rescue. Sentry dogs (Tech-based security) emerge from the honeycombs to attack the group. Donut's Magic Missiles (operating at level 16 via *Brain Trust*) evaporate the dogs. Carl intercepts a nuke-carrying dog, pulling it into his inventory. 

Carl orders Juice Box to detonate Louis's *No Disassembles* (EMP bombs). The EMP fries the security system, but also triggers a mini tactical nuke, blowing up the Reaver castle. Warlord Fang's pet dragon Calliope crashes and dies. 

A system message announces that [[warlord-fang|Warlord Fang]] was hiding inside the dragon; his death defeats the Reavers, and Carl is credited with the kill. 

Juice Box teleports out with the unconscious Louis. She reveals a horrifying detail: the Reavers replaced Louis's heart and lungs with mechanical parts that contain biological material from [[epitome-tagg|Warlord Epitome Tagg]]. Samantha arrives, charred from the blast.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **The Parasite Tagg**: Tagg's attempt to survive Faction Wars by grafting his consciousness into Louis's new mechanical organs.
- **The Reaver Singularity**: Fang's survival mechanics (microscopic robot fly/dragon takeover) reflecting Reaver technology.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The destruction of the Reaver castle is a chaotic climax that resolves one of the remaining faction threats. Fang's desperate attempt to survive by hiding inside his pet dragon Calliope only to be accidentally blown up by Carl's EMP and nuke is a fittingly ironic end. The discovery that Tagg is literally Louis's heart and lungs adds a horrific medical dilemma to the next phase.
