# Chapter 486: The Big Six

## Summary
The casino furniture and NPCs transform into Shadow Mimics that ambush the crawlers. [[princess-donut]] uses *Clockwork Triplicate* on [[mongo]], while [[carl]] is frustrated by his stubby dachshund body. The AI (via a narrator/author lore dump) details the "Big Six" groups competing for control of the dungeon:
1. **The Crawlers** (including former crawlers like Herot and Forkith)
2. **Standard NPCs** (Juice Box, Hamed's faction)
3. **The Gods** (immature and unpredictable)
4. **Sapient Mobs** (Shadow Mimics, Ogre Imperium, Krakaren Prime, War Mages)
5. **The Demons in Sheol** (highly organized cyst system)
6. **Group Six** (located on the 11th floor)

The crawlers systematically destroy the mimics. [[lucia-mar]] uses a golden lasso portal weapon to bisect an amorphic mimic. Mitch Fiorelli is cornered and tries to bargain, mentioning that the mimics are vying with war mages, ogres, and "Cock-A-Doodle-Doo" for control of the "dungeon back door" (the Cabaret). Mitch lunges at Carl, but Carl opens the Eye of the Bedlam Bride on his dog forehead, freezing him in place. Donut finishes Mitch with a *Magic Missile*, and Mongo tea-bags his corpse.
