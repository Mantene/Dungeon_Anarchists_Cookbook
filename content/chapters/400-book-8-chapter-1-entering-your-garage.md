# Chapter 400: Chapter 1 — Entering Your Garage

## Summary
Carl and Donut arrive in their designated tenth-floor garage. Safe room access is blocked until the first "heat" is over, which starts in five minutes.

Carl checks his messages and learns that:
- 5,501 crawlers remain, indicating that about 75% of crawlers took exit deals at the end of the Faction Wars.
- [[justice-light]] sent a final message to the group chat before dying, with [[rosetta]] responding.
- [[quasar]] revealed that the Nothing is broken. On Floor 18, guests in the club cannot die but are being hunted and tortured by gods and demons released from the Nothing, while on Floor 12, the gods are behaving strangely.

Donut selects her new class: **Assassin of Sekhmet** (associated with her deity titles), which comes with pet accessories and a healing aura. She also receives a hostile message from [[khepri]] stating that a death cult is being formed in her name.

Two gremlin NPCs, [[hedy]] (a mechanical vehicle mechanic) and [[waldrip-chris]] (an animal trainer), enter and begin fighting each other to force Carl and Donut to choose between a mechanical or biological vehicle. Hedy warns them that special mounts cannot be targeted by pet spells like Twinkle Toes or Heal Critter. Donut selects mechanical to ensure the vehicle won't try to eat Mongo. As a result, Hedy is hired as their mechanic, and Waldrip Chris is instantly disintegrated by the system.

## Characters
- [[carl]] — Enters the tenth floor and checks messages about the broken Nothing and remaining crawlers.
- [[princess-donut]] — Selects the Assassin of Sekhmet class and chooses a mechanical vehicle.
- [[hedy]] — A Level 30 vehicle mechanic gremlin who wins the job after a scuffle.
- [[waldrip-chris]] — A Level 30 animal trainer gremlin who is disintegrated upon losing the job selection.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- [[the-collapse-of-rules]] — The breaking of the Nothing causing chaos on Floors 12 and 18 and the inability of guests to die.
- [[system-exploitation]] — The forced class choices and vehicle mechanics imposed by the tenth floor.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The high exit-rate (75%) among crawlers highlights the exhaustion and fear following the Faction Wars. The comical and brutal fight between Hedy and Waldrip Chris serves as a dark reminder of the system's cruelty, where NPC competition is literally a matter of life and death, culminating in Waldrip Chris's sudden vaporization.
