# Book 3 Chapter 2: Communal Saferoom

## Summary
The party disembarks at transfer station #83, which contains paths to the Red Line, Yellow Line, and the "Nightmare Express." The station features a general store run by [[limp-richard]], a Level 36 Mole Man NPC, and an Arby's-themed safe room run by [[wendita]], a Bopca Protector NPC. [[mordecai]] joins them, having transformed into a Level 50 Grulke Infantry (toad warrior) form. 

Crawlers remaining is down to 389,441 due to high casualties on Floor 3. Donut negotiates with Wendita to buy a personal space for 37,500 gold. Carl opens his achievements: "They like me! They really like me!" (500 trillion followers, rewards a Platinum Fan Box) and "Cuck Aquaman" (reversing the Celestial box veto, rewards a Platinum Box). 

Opening loot boxes: Carl gets the **Enchanted Necklace of the Haute Bourgeoisie** (+5 Dexterity, Talon Strike skill, and taxes from Poor Sapphire settlement), a *Bang Bro* spell tome, and a free Level 3 Personal Space upgrade coupon. Donut receives the **Enchanted Tiara of Mana Genita** (+3 Intel, mob detection) and a *Hole* spell tome. Katia gets a blue shield bracer with *Shatter*. They use their coupons to buy and conjoin a Level 5 personal space containing an Ultra-Stabilized Sleep Apparatus (2-hour rest, +10% stat/exp buff), an automatic shower (+10% stats, stealth), a training room, and an enhanced crafting studio.
