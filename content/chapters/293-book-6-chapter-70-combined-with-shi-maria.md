# Chapter 293: Combined with Shi Maria

## Summary
- **Collapsing FOCSA**: The team fights off swarming demon crabs on the upper levels of the FOCSA building while digging through rubble to reach the trapped Imani and Tran.
- **Totem Lost**: The *Lazarus* totem is dragged into a Sheol portal by a demon crab, permanently eliminating the card.
- **Golden Combo Attempt**: Donut attempts to discard or cycle her hand to get her staff totem. Carl tells her to summon Alpha Carl and Shi Maria, and then play *Golden Combo*.
- **Incapacitated Target**: The hostile spider totem [[shi-maria|Shi Maria]] immediately impales Alpha Carl through the head, killing him before the combination can be cast.
- **Fused with Carl**: With the primary target dead, the system automatically redirects the *Golden Combo* to the next available ally/minion target. Carl is combined with Shi Maria, creating a half-human, half-spider monstrosity.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The mechanical failure of the *Golden Combo* showcases the extreme danger of hostile, sapient totems like Shi Maria. By targeting Alpha Carl, she exploits the card combat mechanics to force a physical fusion with Carl, gaining direct access to his consciousness and inventory.
