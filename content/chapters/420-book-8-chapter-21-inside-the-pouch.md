# Chapter 420: Chapter 21 — Inside the Pouch

## Summary
- **Gremlin Distraction**: Carl uses an Emergency Gremlin scroll to summon eight gremlins with toolboxes, feeding them to Prison Pocket to distract him.
- **Engineer Death**: Genesis uses a grappling hook to pull the upgrade engineer, **Niels**, out of the pouch, killing him instantly in a bid to steal him.
- **Joey Costume**: Carl loots the *Enchanted Juvenile Macropus Dominus Skin* from Niels' corpse. Wearing it grants protection from seeping acid, a Jump skill, and Lotion Application, but negates his boxer and pedicure effects.
- **Defeating the Boss**: Carl, dressed in the joey suit, hops into Prison Pocket's pouch. He drops two hobgoblin big boom satchels at the bottom. Using *Gloom Wraith Phase*, he escapes the pouch and detonates the bombs, blowing the boss to pieces.
- **Race Results**: With the boss dead, the teams race across the finish line. The results for Heat Two are: Team Sparkles (1st), The Jugglers (2nd), Team Free Love (3rd), The Wild Hunt (4th), Lady Dominators (5th), One Fine Pig (6th), and Carl's team (7th). Girth the Trouble is eliminated.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's desperation leads him to wear a wet, smelly joey costume and crawl through rotting organic matter to defeat the Level 170 boss. The humor of the situation is juxtaposed with the visceral horror of being stuffed into a caustic marsupial pouch. Carl's last-place finish puts them in a precarious position, but they survive the heat.
