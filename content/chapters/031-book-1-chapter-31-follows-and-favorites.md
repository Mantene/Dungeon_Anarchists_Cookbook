# Book 1 Chapter 31: Follows and Favorites

## Summary
The Floor 1 collapse occurs as the timer runs out. The remaining crawler count drops instantly to **1,292,526** (meaning over 700,000 crawlers died in the collapse). In the Waffle House safe room on Floor 1, 24 Meadow Lark residents chose to stay behind to die on their own terms, while 6 joined [[brandon-an]]'s group. [[agatha]] remains missing.

[[carl]], [[princess-donut]], and the Meadow Lark survivors watch the premiere of *Dungeon Crawler After Hours* and the main program in their safe room. The recap show features Carl and Donut's fight against the tusklings for 15 seconds, showing Donut's magic missile attacks and [[imani-c]]'s disgust at dropping a strap-on dildo. Yolanda's mugshot is shown, but [[chris-andrews-2]] points out that Agatha was edited out of the scene entirely.

Brandon tells Carl that the Meadow Lark survivors plan to go down to the third floor early because they cannot train on Floor 2, and they want to avoid getting separated. Carl promises to scout ahead and clear the path.

The system announcement details the Floor 2 mechanics:
1. The floor timer is shortened to **six days** (accelerated due to lower-than-projected crawler numbers).
2. **Follows and Favorites** are enabled.
3. **Patronage Overhaul**: Crawlers are limited to three patrons. Patron spots are auctioned for one day on Floor 4, 5, and 6. The more a patron pays over the standard fee, the higher the quality of Benefactor Boxes the crawler receives.
4. **Bathroom Penalty**: Urinating or defecating outside a designated bathroom area will instantly summon a Level 93 Rage Elemental from Floor 13 to kill the offender and their entire party.

Donut gets her first followers and view metrics, trying to act casual for the camera.

## Characters Introduced
- **[[elle-mcgibbons]]**: Wheelchair-bound 99-year-old Meadow Lark resident.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival-of-the-collapse]]**: Over 700,000 crawlers fail to transition to Floor 2. 
- **[[televised-exploitation]]**: Follows, favorites, and the new patronage bid-matching auction tie a crawler's survival resources directly to their entertainment value.
- **[[escorting-meadow-lark-residents]]**: Brandon's team plans to drop the elderly residents down to Floor 3 early to avoid the Floor 2 danger.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The Floor 2 rules are immediately more punitive (timer shortened, death elemental for bathroom offenses). The corporate corruption is obvious: they are speeding up the crawl because of "lower projections," which Carl notes seems like an attempt to get rid of crawlers faster. Agatha's removal from the footage hints at her being an anomaly or a narrative complication the showrunners are hiding.
