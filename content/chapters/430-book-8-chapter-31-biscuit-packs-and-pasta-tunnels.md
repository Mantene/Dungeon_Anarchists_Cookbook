# Chapter 430: Chapter 31 — Biscuit Packs and Pasta Tunnels

## Summary
- **Cookbook Entry**: The chapter opens with a cookbook entry from Donut's *A Banquet Fit for a Princess*, written by **Luca Di** (Crawler #10,284,471, human Pastamancer, level 62). Luca details getting closure with his mother, **Giulia DiMarco** (who had dementia and took a Benevento witch race), before she died. The entry unlocks the spell *Temporary Macaroni* (Level 14).
- **The Race Begins**: Gluteus accelerates down the 30-degree steep decline. Carl deploys the two Jikininki ghouls in cages outside the truck using Hedy's pulley-and-lever system.
- **Missile Interdiction**: Carl fires eight missiles: 4 *Denial of Service* missiles at One Fine Pig's APV, and 4 at Team Sparkles' rolling bush. The missiles coat the targets in thick, green mucus, blinding them and slowing them to a crawl.
- **Sugar Hermits Swarm**: The starting invulnerability shields expire. Giant waves of Sugar Hermits emerge. The passenger-side ghoul throws *Biscuit Packs* (polyurethane-like expanding foam potion balls) to trap the skulls and create barriers.
- **Wall of Fire**: Donut casts her Level 15 *Wall of Fire* (boosted to 16), creating a mile-long, 100-foot-tall wall of flame parallel to the truck that incinerates thousands of Sugar Hermits.
