# Book 5 Chapter 20: Country Boss Imogen

## Summary
- **Lexis's Leak**: While in the green room during a brief Valtay system handover when no "sniffers" are online, [[lexis]] slips Carl and Donut crucial information.
- **The Country Boss Trap**: Lexis reveals that Queen [[imogen]] of the High Elves is a "country boss" and a repurposed human. 
- **The Masquerade Setup**: The Valtay plan to force the top 50 crawlers to attend a party at the High Elf Castle (The Butcher's Masquerade), which is a setup to "zero out" the top crawlers so they can be replaced by Valtay-favored choices. Lexis advises them to descend the stairs to Floor 7 early to avoid it.
- **Donut's Confession**: Donut admits she smuggled Valtay secret agents to Zev and the Borant Emancipation Front to help spark the corporate coup.
- **Guild Safehouse Yolanda**: Back in the safe room, Mordecai notes that the fantasy theme will remain. Katia's group has purchased a space and appointed [[imani-c]] as Guildmaster of the new guild **Safehome Yolanda** (named after their deceased friend Yolanda).
- **The Attack**: Clint and Holger (in their turned were-beaver forms) have been building Point Mongo's defenses. Clint bangs on the door, warning that elves are attacking the town with a catapult.

## Characters Introduced
- [[imogen]] — High-Elf Queen and Sorcerer General, Country Boss (mentioned).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[the-butchers-masquerade]]**: The masquerade is revealed as a Valtay-engineered death trap for the top crawlers.
- **[[were-castors]]**: The were-beavers assist in the defense of Point Mongo.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The revelation of the "Country Boss Trap" shifts the stakes of Floor 6. The Valtay are not saviors; they are control freaks who want to reset the crawl with their own selected crawlers. Donut's admission of smuggling agents shows she has been actively participating in the rebellion behind Carl's back. The name of the new guild, Safehome Yolanda, is a touching tribute to their fallen companion.
