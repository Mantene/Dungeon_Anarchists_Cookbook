# Chapter 484: Meeting at the Hairpin

## Summary
[[carl]], [[princess-donut]], and [[samantha]] back their vehicle into the Vendor Village, which is eerily empty. Carl's slugpox flares up, causing a Level 30 slug named "Lil' Mello Haze" (wearing a backward baseball cap and possessing a mace for a tail) to burst from his back. Carl leaves the slug behind and proceeds to the Hairpin bar (entrance to the Desperado Club) to meet up with the remaining crawlers. 

Chris is present in his werewolf-transformed human state, wearing his Meadow Lark work overalls. Elle is in goblin form, and Jacobus has become a starfish. The gathered crawlers burst into uncontrollable laughter at the sight of Carl as a dachshund. [[imani-c]] cures Carl's slugpox. Carl coordinates the team to enter the Desperado Club to use the casino's Wheel of Fortune "Nothing" portal to save their friends.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[comic-relief]]**: The crawlers laughing at Carl's ridiculous dachshund form, showcasing their resilience and bonding amidst absolute dread.
- **[[humanity-and-identity]]**: Chris's appearance in normal overalls representing the loss and preservation of their past lives.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The contrast between the looming threat of the crawl and the crawlers laughing until they cry at Carl's dachshund form shows the psychological coping mechanisms of these late-stage survivors. Carl's willingness to keep the slugpox active as a weapon reflects his desperate tactical pragmatism.
