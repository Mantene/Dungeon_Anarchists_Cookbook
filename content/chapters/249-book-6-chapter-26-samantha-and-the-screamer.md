# Book 6 Chapter 26: Samantha and the Screamer

## Summary
Carl and Donut rescue Samantha and negotiate a high-stakes deal to capture Shi Maria, who reveals she can speak and issue quests.
- **The Screamer's Appearance**: Carl and Donut locate [[samantha]] at the edge of the spider-web-choked nature preserve. The spider boss, **The Bedlam Bride** (or Shi Maria/The Screamer), is a Level 140 City Boss demi-god. She possesses the passive ability *Cockroach* (level 15) which fully heals her upon a fatal blow and grants a temporary shield.
- **Samantha's Delusions**: Samantha has formulated a detailed, delusional plan where Maria casts a reanimation spell to move her into the naiad golem body (*Princess Lunette*). Samantha then intends to have a torrid affair with Louis, go on a girls' night bachelorette party with the crawlers, marry her missing king, and kill her mother.
- **The Con Exposed**: Carl places the naiad body on the pavement. The spider is unable to reanimate it, claiming she needs specialized supplies. Samantha is furious and demands that Carl say something nice to her, warning that if she is offended, she will open her fifth eye (a marble-sized eye in the center of her head that casts permanent insanity and blindness).
- **The Deal**: Shi Maria speaks to Carl in a clear, sultry whisper. She wants to get to Larracos (Floor 9) to find her missing husband, **Hamed (The Night Wyrm)**, and prove her innocence. She proposes that Carl capture her as a T'Ghee card; once they reach the city, they will hire a master pulpmancer to set her free and reanimate Samantha. Carl accepts the deal despite knowing she is highly dangerous and manipulative.
- **The Threat**: Shi Maria warns Carl that his *Liquid Therapy* protection potion is only a temporary shield, stating: *"A broken window still lets the wind inside, Carl. We would both know... of what is growing inside of you."*
- **Capture**: Carl receives the quest **The Bedlam Bride** (escorting her to Larracos). Shi Maria rolls on her side, and Carl punches her abdomen and flags her, capturing her as their 6th T'Ghee card (bringing their active totems to 5 of 6).
- **The Chowder War Update**: Carl receives a quest update stating that [[sister-ines-quiteria]] has warned the Monk Seal Abbot of the crabs' sneak attack, setting up an imminent slaughter.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **System Lore & The Nothing**: The mystery of the demi-gods' background expands, linking Shi Maria to Hamed (The Night Wyrm).
- **Self-Aware NPCs**: Shi Maria and Samantha display high levels of systemic self-awareness, communicating with other crawlers (like [[elle-mcgibbons]]) about cards and game mechanics.
- **The Growth Inside Carl**: Shi Maria's warning about "what is growing inside of you" hints at a deeper, sanity-eroding force developing in Carl's mind, connected to the Primals or his exposure to divine influence.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The negotiation with Shi Maria is a chilling moment where the crawlers accept a highly dangerous asset into their deck. Shi Maria's ability to speak in a sultry whisper and manipulate Samantha shows she is far more intelligent than standard city bosses. Her warnings about the "broken window" in Carl's mind suggest that the *Primal* class or his mental strain is fracturing his sanity, exposing him to forces that even *Liquid Therapy* cannot block.
