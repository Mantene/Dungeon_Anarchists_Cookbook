# Chapter 321: Weapons Free

## Summary

The adjudication continues in Queen Imogen's kitchen. Panford replays the events of Carl’s possession: Mordecai, realizing they were being illegally observed by Sortion spies, had secretly brewed a potion to violently expel [[shi-maria]] rather than just wake Carl up. This caused a possessed Carl to manifest and slaughter the Sortion troops. Carl deduces that the Princess Posse did not have spies; rather, [[juice-box]]'s team of changelings had remained active in the Sortion courts and leaked the intelligence.

With Houston’s complicity ruled circumstantial, Opiee and [[baroness-victory]] vote to clear the Madness and Princess Posse of wrongdoing. Panford formally penalizes the Lemig Sortion with a 25% force reduction. 

Meanwhile, Sir [[ferdinand]] of Team Retribution passes an emergency action item, enforcing an immediate live-feed blackout for the entire dungeon, denying outside observers any view of the crawl.

Under the lingering influence of Shi Maria’s Insanity spell, Commander [[stockade]] experiences a severe psychotic break. He repeatedly smashes his face against the wooden table and floor, dying of self-inflicted wounds. 

Stockade's death eliminates the Sortion's leadership. The system awards all Lemig Sortion assets to the Madness. Because a faction was defeated, the system engages an acceleration scenario, shortening the ceasefire by 23.33 hours. 

The ceasefire ends immediately, initiating Phase One (Ramp-Up). Architect Houston instantly reveals his six-armed, bug-like body and fires a rifle at Carl, wounding him. 

Carl is teleported back to his empty field hospital. He is greeted by [[jason-manning-fucks]] and other sluggalos, who tell him to man the trenches as the Faction Wars officially begin and non-magical artillery fire strikes their shield.

- **[[carl]]** — Learns the full details of the possession battle; gets shot in the shoulder by Architect Houston before teleporting back to base.
- **[[stockade]]** — Commits suicide by smashing his own head against the floor due to the Insanity spell.
- **[[architect-houston]]** — Viceroy leader of Madness; reveals his bug-like body and shoots Carl with a rifle, inheriting all Sortion assets.
- **[[panford]]** — Dreadnaught Adjutant; renders the final ruling before the ceasefire abruptly collapses.
- **[[baroness-victory]]** — Princess Posse Adjutant; teleports Carl away just as Houston attacks.
- **[[opiee]]** — Bune Adjutant; comments on the incomplete severing of the spider possession.
- **[[jason-manning-fucks]]** — Level 55 Sluggalo combatant; tells the bleeding Carl to get out to the front lines.
- **[[ferdinand]]** — Proposes the live-feed blackout action item.
- **[[juice-box]]** — Messages Carl, confirming her team’s espionage efforts and the early activation of their defensive towers.

## Themes & Plot Threads

- **Psychosis & Insanity** — The horrific delayed effect of Shi Maria's Insanity spell driving Stockade to suicide.
- **Ceasefire Acceleration** — System mechanics automatically collapse the ceasefire early when a faction is defeated.
- **Live-Feed Blackout** — Ferdinand's action item blinds external showrunners, turning the crawl into a literal black box.
- **Start of the War** — The ceasefire ends early, and Phase One (Ramp-Up) begins with heavy artillery shelling.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections

This chapter is a masterclass in escalating tension. The sudden suicide of Stockade, the inheritance of his assets by Houston, the early termination of the ceasefire, and Houston's immediate physical attack on Carl all happen in rapid, breathless succession. The live-feed blackout is a massive shift in the dungeon's geopolitics, shutting out the galaxy's entertainment machine and making the crawl far more unpredictable.
