# Book 5 Chapter 64: Entering the Ballroom

## Summary
- **Ballroom Restrictions**: The crawlers enter Ballroom A of the High Elf Castle, where they are muted and peace-bonded (no inventory access). Violence is forbidden under threat of banishment to the Nothing.
- **Contingency Planning**: Carl coordinates with Imani and Katia to spread the word about Escape Plan C (Donut casting *Hole* to fall to the third level) and Escape Plan D (jumping with *Feather Fall*).
- **Looting Setup**: Carl signals [[bomo]] and [[the-sledge]] to begin looting the castle outside the party, while [[britney-proskurina]] prepares her own action.
- **Hunter Entry**: [[vrah]] and other hunters enter the ballroom, and Empress D'Nadia eyes them, anticipating a fight.
