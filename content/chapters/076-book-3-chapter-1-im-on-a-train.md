# Book 3 Chapter 1: I'm on a Train
![[Floor4AIGen3DMap.jpg]]
## Summary
[[carl]], [[princess-donut]], [[katia-grim]], and [[mongo]] appear in Cabin #20, a passenger car on the Red Line of Floor 4 ("The Iron Tangle"). The floor features a massive, color-coded subway and railway system. Due to her *Character Actor* skill, Donut is forced to select a new class within six minutes. Under [[mordecai]]'s guidance, she chooses the **Football Hooligan** class, which gives her +10 Constitution, a momentum bonus, and the *Mascot* skill (gives party dexterity/constitution buffs when Mongo deals damage or kills mobs) and *Guinness* (doubles strength when drunk).

The group explores the train cars. They discover Cabin #15 is a **Janitor's Lair** guarded by two Level 17 Jikininki ghouls in conductor uniforms. They defeat them, triggering Donut's *Mascot* buff. As they reach Sirin Station (#81), the platform is swarmed by Level 6 Drek babies. Carl places an Alarm Module (playing Fergie's "Fergalicious") and a Spike Module on the doors. At Mora Station (#82), Level 19 Psycho Stickers attack. Carl casts *Protective Shell*, which remains static in the tunnel coords while the train hurtles forward, bulldozing and paste-ifying all the encroaching monsters. The party runs through the blood-spattered cars, looting gold, toward the transfer station (#83).
