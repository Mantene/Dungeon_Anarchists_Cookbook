# Chapter 264: Climbing Iowa and Yule Cat

## Summary
- **Visitor Battle**: At the top of the mound, Carl and Donut face Opponent 7, **The Visitor** (Level 70 bat-like Nightgaunt demon). Carl's bomb triggers a major landslide.
- **Jola Summoned**: Donut plays **Jola** the Yule Cat (Level 110 fat Persian cat with a melted head candle), who devours the Nightgaunt and its Quench Moths. They collect Token 7 and *Lamp* snare cards.
- **Turkey Barn Crisis**: They enter a massive farm building filled with tens of thousands of Level 5 living turkeys. Mongo goes wild eating them, which activates the Level 75 Shaman turkey **Tom** and Level 80 Necromancer ogre **Sharp-Elbows**, initiating a double deckmaster battle.
