# Book 6 Chapter 16: The Reaper of Havana

## Summary
The Yoruban god [[ogun]] physically manifests, inflicting permanent punishment upon his former worshippers, while the dark past and racial abilities of [[sister-ines-quiteria]] are exposed.
- **Ogun's Manifestation**: Angry that his worshippers bypassed his shrine to resurrect [[yemaya]], the unsponsored deity Ogun (Level 250) manifests in the temple. 
- **Anton's Death**: Carl tries to protect [[anton-lopez]] by casting [[black-nimbus]] (negating his deity alignment) and spamming healing scrolls on him and [[paz-lo]]. However, Ogun clarifies that the spell only removes Anton from his mercy, not his wrath, and casually steps on Anton's head, crushing him to death.
- **Sister Ines' Backstory**: Paz, suffering from Ogun's permanent, un-healable "Inevitable" health-drain smite, reveals Sister Ines' true history to Carl and Donut. Ines possesses the passive racial trait *Toxoplasmosis* (which makes others want to protect her and forget her misdeeds). On Earth, Paz and Anton were prison guards in a women's prison where Sister Ines was incarcerated as "El Segador de La Habana" (The Reaper of Havana) for poisoning and murdering all the nuns in her convent.
- **Paz's Totemization**: Rather than let Paz die from Ogun's health drain, Sister Ines flags Paz at less than 5% health with a T'Ghee squad flag, converting him into a Common (green) card totem.
- **Deity Arrivals**: The siren goddess [[yemaya]] and the Valtay-sponsored goddess [[eileithyia]] manifest in the temple.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **Deity Tyranny**: Ogun's brutal execution of Anton and cursing of Paz highlights the cruelty of the dungeon's deities.
- **Trust and Deception**: The exposure of Sister Ines' past and her passive mind-altering *Toxoplasmosis* ability strains the alliance.
- **Card Mechanics**: Paz is turned into a T'Ghee card, raising moral questions about the nature of crawler totems.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's desperation to save Anton using the recently acquired *Black Nimbus* spell backfires, demonstrating that clever mechanical exploits have limits against raw divine power. The revelation of Sister Ines' prison past adds a layer of moral complexity; although she is a mass murderer, she was willing to shield her party members from a god, suggesting that the dungeon changes people in unpredictable ways.
