# Chapter 373: Infiltrating the Castle

## Summary
While the giant ball of flesh rolls toward the field of corpses, Carl, [[dong-quixote]] (on Gonk), [[jamal]], and [[kandy-newton]] (Bigs) use Invisibility potions to sneak toward the Madness castle. Carl stores Rend in his carrier. 

To distract [[harpocrates]], who is pursuing Donut's ball, [[princess-donut]] uses the PA system to blast Lil Wayne's "Lollipop" (reaching #1 on May 3, 2008), which shakes the ground with bass. Next, she plays King Missile's "Detachable Penis," requested by crawler [[choi-mi-seon-2]]. The songs successfully disorient the god. Britney reports that the Semeru dwarves' digger is approaching to help.

Dong triggers a *Summon Face-Melting Acid Rats* trap, which misfires due to the teleport ban. As they reach the back wall, the castle's protections deshroud them. Carl bypasses three door traps and pries open a garage-style door to gain entry. He summons Rend back out.

Carl casts *Ping*. The sonar scan reveals 20 soldiers: 18 in one room (including two conscripted off-worlders), and two in a room adjacent to the throne room (the operating theater), containing Viceroy [[architect-houston]] and a crawler. 

The system updates Carl's *Book of Voodoo*, listing three crawlers and a corpse in the castle. Carl identifies the corpse as **Zeynep** (level 58), whom he met in the Desperado Club. The two conscripted crawlers are Selvi and Tuba (from Emir/Zeynep's party). The third, non-conscripted crawler in the operating theater is [[burcu]], a badger-headed porsuk crawler of the swashbuckler class.

Carl attempts to message Burcu, but the system blocks it for "illegal corroboration." However, Burcu uses the *Book of Voodoo*'s loophole to quickly sketch a map of the surgical theater, marking fireball traps, before the book slams shut. 

The 18 guards and the two conscripted crawlers begin moving toward Carl's position.

## Characters
* [[carl]]: Leads the infiltration, prying open the door, scans the castle, and attempts to contact Burcu.
* [[dong-quixote]]: Rides Gonk, flails his sock, and triggers a misfiring rat trap.
* [[jamal]]: Bounces to the PA music and stands guard.
* [[kandy-newton]] (Bigs): Slimes along with her hatchet and is ordered to occupy the throne room.
* [[princess-donut]]: Distracts Harpocrates via the PA system using "Lollipop" and "Detachable Penis."
* [[burcu]]: A badger-headed porsuk crawler who uses the Book of Voodoo to feed Carl intelligence on Houston's traps.
* [[choi-mi-seon-2]]: A crawler who requested the song "Detachable Penis" in the chat.
* [[architect-houston]]: The prime of the Madness, located in the operating theater.

## Themes & Plot Threads
* **Voodoo Book Loophole**: Burcu's use of the Book of Voodoo to bypass messaging bans showcases how crawlers communicate when restricted.
* **The Cabin Strippers' Fate**: The presence of Selvi, Tuba, and Zeynep connects back to the assault on the Desperado Club on the eighth floor.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The choice of songs played over the PA system is both hilarious and highly tactical, specifically chosen to insult and disorient Harpocrates. Carl's discovery of the strippers' presence highlights the tragedy of the eighth-floor fallout, showing how survivors are conscripted and forced to fight for the enemy factions against their will.
