# Book 3 Chapter 12: Pooka Druggies and Surprise Three

## Summary
Donut reaches Level 28. Sponsored by Princess D'Nadia of the Prism Kingdom. Katia's sponsor is Princess Formidable of the Skull Empire. Carl loots Gore-Gore's body, obtaining 498 gold, the Ochre Line Engineer's Key, and the Ochre line route map. 

The party boards the **Dismemberment Limited** (maglev/monorail train). The operator is **[[levi-the-seventh]]**, a Level 7 Troll Flesher and Hobgoblin Skellie Symbiote. The train car is covered in blood and gore, triggering the quest: **Levi is On the Menu!** (Do not let Levi be devoured by Fleshers).

Level 10 Swamp Orc Fleshers (manta-ray-like flying flaps of caustic skin) attack the train, led by **[[dismember|Dismember the War Mage]]** (an elf-like boss). The Fleshers smother targets, melt them, and animate their skeletons as part of Dismember's resleeved army. 

Carl blocks the door with a gangway chock, and Katia shapes herself into a 6-legged brace to hold it. Donut and Mongo clear the edges. Dismember attempts to cast a spell through the door. Utilizing "Surprise Three," Donut casts *Hole* on the metal plate. Carl reaches through the hole, grabs Dismember by his silver hair, and pulls his head through. Donut cancels the *Hole* spell, instantly decapitating the War Mage.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The "Surprise Three" decapitation is a masterpiece of tactical synergy. By combining Donut's *Hole* magic with Carl's physical strength and timing, they bypass the boss's high magic resistance and armor, executing him instantly.
