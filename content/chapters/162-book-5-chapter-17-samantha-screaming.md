# Book 5 Chapter 17: Samantha Screaming

## Summary
- **Samantha's Tantrum**: Safe inside the Point Mongo safe room, Carl and Donut find [[samantha]] screaming in the training room. Donut had mentioned meeting Signet, causing Samantha to go wild.
- **Signet's Relation**: Samantha demands to be taken to Signet, claiming they are related (Signet is her third cousin thrice-removed by marriage, granddaughter of Princess Yungsten of the Naiad). Samantha claims Signet has the magic to return her to her body. Carl promises to bring her to the assault on Fort Freedom if she stops screaming.
- **Transfer to Security Checkpoint**: Zev transfers Carl and Donut to orbit for Odette's show. They enter a security checkpoint: a featureless glass chamber in low gravity aboard a spaceship. Carl views Madagascar and the Indian Ocean below.
- **Gnoll Riot Force**: Two Shade Gnoll Riot Force mercenaries pat Carl down and check Donut's collar charm.
- **Odette's Trailer**: They are teleported to Odette's trailer. Zev is physically present (very thin, wearing a rebreather). Odette appears as a hologram and reveals: [[beatrice]] is alive!

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[search-for-beatrice]]**: The shocking revelation that Beatrice survived and is off-planet.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The transition from the comedy of Samantha screaming to the cosmic perspective of orbiting Earth in a glass chamber is a striking reminder of the scale of the crawl. Carl's first view of Madagascar and the ocean below triggers a deep emotional reaction. Samantha's claim to be related to Signet by marriage introduces a bizarre lore connection that might be exploited, while Odette's bomb drop (Beatrice is alive) immediately upends the narrative tension.
