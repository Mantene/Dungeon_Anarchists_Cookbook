# Chapter 312: Reinforcements Arrive

## Summary

Carl, Donut, and Florin meet with [[tipid]] and [[rosetta]] at the Princess Posse's base camp. Tipid tells the story of his life after leaving the dungeon—the surgery he needed to survive, his debt, and his work with Doctor Porthus Hu of the Crawler Project, a non-profit dedicated to helping former crawlers. Dr. Hu also formed the Open Intellect Pacifist Network, aimed at ending the crawl entirely. Tipid explains that 50,000 former crawlers have arrived as mercenaries for the Princess Posse, all set to level 60 with standard merc packages. However, each of the eight enemy teams also received approximately 20,000 armed mercenaries, adding roughly 150,000 new enemies total.

[[mistress-tiatha]] drunkenly comments on the hollow promises of citizenship for former crawlers. The enemy mercenaries came in with their existing gear but got the Hired Mercenary class—no race change, limited spells. Tipid's class is Liquid Dervish, and he mentions his friend [[justice-light]], an eagle who is an expert on traps. Carl learns that former crawlers are experienced "tenners" who have survived Faction Wars at least once. The chapter ends with the team heading to the base to meet up with the broader group.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections

This chapter marks a turning point as the scope of Faction Wars becomes clear. The joy of reinforcements is tempered by the sobering math—they're still massively outnumbered. Tipid's character provides a window into what happens to crawlers after they leave, revealing the broader universe's exploitation and neglect. The former crawlers choosing to return despite the danger is deeply moving and speaks to the bonds forged through shared trauma.
