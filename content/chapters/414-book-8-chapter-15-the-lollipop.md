# Chapter 414: Chapter 15 — The Lollipop

## Summary
Carl and Donut enter **The Lollipop** bar, where safe room rules do not apply. Onstage, a Level 84 Seductress Kappa named [[dekoki]] (a member of **Team Yokai**, which also includes a white-robed ghost, a red ogre, and a floating paper lantern) performs an off-key rendition of Taylor Dayne's "Tell It to My Heart." To prevent her water-filled head bowl from spilling while she dances, she has covered it in plastic wrap secured with rubber bands. 

They push through the crowd of NPC racers to meet a large group of friendly crawlers at a back table:
- **[[imani-c]] & [[elle-mcgibbons]]**: Drive a Mowag Piranha V (an amphibious military troop carrier).
- **[[florin]] & Lucia**: Drive a tuk-tuk (auto rickshaw). They won first place in Heat 1 and spent their three upgrades on a tech shield, vertical climb/all-terrain tires, and a GPS upgrade. Florin is concerned because Lucia's personality is constantly flipping between herself (70% of the time) and the children trapped in her head (30% of the time).
- **[[louis-santiago-2]]**, [[britney-proskurina]], & [[daniel-bautista-2]]: Drive a neon-covered Chevrolet Tahoe lowrider with only two inches of ground clearance. They came in second-to-last place in Heat 1 after their opponents (the "tentacle guys") were eaten, receiving light-up wheel spinners as an audience-voted upgrade.
- **[[chris-and-rews-2]] & the Erins** (including new crawlers [[erin-stratos]], a Level 66 Moodright mage, and [[erin-gregory]], a Level 69 Swiftie knife-wielder): Drive a slow semitruck with a trailer.
- **[[zhang]] & [[li-na]]** (not present): Selected a biological octopus-like mount. Li Na is not speaking to Zhang and has slaughtered almost all their opponents, threatening to force crawler-on-crawler races.

Hiding under the table is **[[minus]]** (impersonating the deceased soother tourist [[linus]]), who is wearing a nude Elle T-shirt and has an Elle tattoo. Elle forces him to stay under the table, treating him as a nuisance. 

Prepotente returns to the table with a soda and a 1980s-themed karaoke list, announcing he has signed up to sing Baltimora's "Tarzan Boy."

The gathering in the Lollipop bar highlights the emotional strain on the remaining crawlers. While they try to relax with drinks and karaoke, the horror of their situation is ever-present, particularly in Florin's description of Lucia's splintered mind and the high probability that they will be forced to kill each other in the upcoming heats.
