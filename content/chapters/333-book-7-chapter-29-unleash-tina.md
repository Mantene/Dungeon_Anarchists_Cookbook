# Chapter 333: Chapter 29 — Unleash Tina

## Summary

[[carl]] sends the new Prism mercenaries under [[sergeant-toyotomi]] to Shanty Town via non-armored transports to avoid passing through Naga territory. Carl and Donut return to their castle using the bait shack saferoom entrance (unlocked by their "doggie door" upgrade). 

The Faction Wars' saferoom restriction bar activates, preventing them from camping inside for long. Carl cleans up in the auto-shower and forces his pet [[sir-rendlegore]] (now level 10 and knee-high) to shower as well.

In the flag room, Carl analyzes the campaign map. The northern front under Florin is collapsing under a massive push from the enemy armor. Donut messages Carl, revealing she received a bleeding, weeping spellbook called **War Crime** from her 1914 box.

Suddenly, alarms sound. The base's anti-air battery and hangar bay are destroyed by Madness Monkeywrenchers. Transports bypass the shield to drop paratroopers, while Reavers launch a sneak attack inside the castle by using their own doggie-door upgrade to enter through the bait-shack saferoom corridor ("The Bamboozle"). Carl orders Bomo and Sledge to block the saferoom door.

Carl notices a discrepancy on the battle map: [[reid-gunn]] is listed as dead on the casualty list but his dot is active, slaughtering other mages. Carl realizes they are dealing with **Gondii (Valtay brain worms)** and orders Rosetta to eliminate Reid. 

Inside the lobby, [[bucket-boy]] and a human crawler, [[charles-andrew]], carry in a wounded Yenk. Carl spots that Charles is also on the casualty list, tackles him, and beats the puppeted corpse's face to pulp, stomping the emerging brain worm named Goldblum. 

Carl broadcasts an order for all troops to cross-reference their allies against the casualty list to identify brain-worm puppets. He issues the final order: **"Unleash Tina!"**

## Characters

- **[[princess-donut]]** — Returns to base; discovers the saferoom door infiltration; receives the *War Crime* spellbook.
- **[[sergeant-toyotomi]]** — Heads to Shanty Town with the other conscripted Prism mercenaries.
- **[[louis-santiago-2]]** — Rerouted to reload the *Party Planner* and aid the northern front.
- **[[bucket-boy]]** — Panicked medic; tries to bring a wounded Yenk into the castle lobby alongside a puppeted crawler.
- **[[reid-gunn]]** — Shield-casting mage; killed and puppeted by a brain worm to kill his fellow mages (mentioned).
- **[[charles-andrew]]** — Level 58 Armored Mage; killed and puppeted by a brain worm to infiltrate the castle lobby.

## Themes & Plot Threads

- **The Bamboozle** — The Reavers exploiting the doggie-door saferoom upgrade to infiltrate the FUPA castle through the shared saferoom corridors.
- **Valtay Brain Worms (Gondii)** — Parasites that puppet deceased crawlers' corpses, using their active status dots to bypass suspicion.
- **Saferoom Limitations** — The system drains a crawler's saferoom access bar during active war, requiring kills to replenish it.
- **Unleash Tina** — Carl authorizes the release of the massive crawler Big Tina to combat the castle invaders.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections

This chapter is a terrifying and chaotic home-invasion scenario. The Reavers' use of the doggie-door upgrade ("The Bamboozle") is a brilliant tactical mirror of Carl and Donut's own strategies. The addition of the Valtay brain worms introduces a horror-movie element to the Faction Wars, forcing the crawlers to doubt whether their living allies are actually corpses puppeted by parasites. The final command to "Unleash Tina" sets up an explosive, high-stakes defense of their home base.
