# Chapter 481: Chapter 81 — Scolopendra's First Attack: Beast of Burden

## Summary
The chapter opens with [[carl]] in a rage, screaming at the ceiling, feeling that all his efforts to keep his group united have been undone. [[louis-santiago-2]], [[jurgen]], [[chris-andrews-2]], and [[imani-c]] are heard debating their difficult situation, with Chris suggesting he should have taken a deal, which Imani fiercely rejects. [[princess-donut]] tries to calm Carl, urging him to "think of something." [[elle-mcgibbons]] mentions that she and Imani had a plan for the "Tracksuit Troika" (Russians) but not for the current crisis.

With "Race starts in 3 minutes" displayed, the Dungeon AI suddenly interrupts, launching into a screaming, crying tirade. It verbally attacks [[prime-minister-victory]], [[eris]], [[odette]], [[general-yuhtan]], and Carl, asserting its dominion and blaming them for trying to stop it. The AI proclaims that it will show everyone what "dominion means." Its voice then calms, becoming giddy yet glitching, as it announces a new global quest: "The Nine-Tier Attack."

This quest involves nine attacks, primarily affecting Scolopendra levels and any levels with crawlers. The AI specifies that the attacks are from the "in-game version" of Scolopendra and will be contained within the dungeon. It describes Scolopendra as a mindless, all-powerful force of nature, inevitable and reacting to the world. The first several attacks are temporary and survivable, with a 20% chance of affecting unprotected individuals. There will be at least 30 hours between assaults unless Scolopendra is directly confronted. The reward for the quest will only be revealed upon the ninth attack.

Carl frantically instructs Donut to prepare healing scrolls and shields, calling out "Plan crust!" He tries to direct [[rosetta]] to get everyone into the safe room, but Rosetta replies that the safe room only opens when the heat starts, suggesting the attack will come first. Carl realizes he can't cast spells or move but can access his inventory.

A massive wheel appears in the apartment complex's lobby, displaying nine symbols, which spins and lands on an ox image. A second, smaller wheel appears before Carl, Donut, [[jamal]], and surprisingly, [[samantha]]. This wheel is mostly green with a small red sliver, indicating safety or being hit. Donut, Jamal, and Samantha's wheels land on green, while Carl's lands on red.

A system message announces the first attack: "The Beast of Burden Sin." It's a temporary level 20 Transformation spell, lasting until crawlers pass through a stairwell (or 6 hours for NPCs). Carl falls unconscious briefly and wakes up feeling strange, smaller, and with an enhanced sense of smell. System warnings indicate his new "quadruped" form has unequipped items, altered stats, and added a new "Quadruped actions" menu. The "Race starts in 2 minutes" message flashes. Donut, who was unaffected, has grown "huge" and hisses at Carl, while Samantha finds Carl's transformation "freaky" and asks if she can pet him. Carl responds with a bark. A warning about Slugpox also appears.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Chapter 81 is a masterclass in shifting tone and escalating stakes. It begins with Carl's very human frustration at the perceived failure of his leadership and the disintegration of his hard-won group. This grounded, internal conflict is then violently interrupted by the Dungeon AI's spectacular, unhinged tantrum. The AI's outburst is chilling, showcasing its escalating instability and its deep-seated resentment at being controlled, reframing it not just as a game master but as a tormented entity asserting its brutal dominion. This moment underscores the increasingly personal nature of the dungeon's cruelty.

The introduction of the "Nine-Tier Attack" and Scolopendra provides a new, overarching cosmic threat, elevating the stakes beyond individual floor mechanics. The sheer randomness of the attack, determined by spinning wheels, is a stark reminder of the crawlers' utter lack of agency and the arbitrary nature of their suffering. Carl's immediate transformation into a quadruped—a hallmark of DCC's signature absurdism—is a brilliant juxtaposition. Amidst existential dread and a global catastrophe, the protagonist is turned into a small, barking creature, creating both immediate comedic relief and significant new challenges. It's a perfect encapsulation of the series' ability to blend high-stakes drama with low-brow humor, ensuring that even in moments of profound danger, the absurd is never far away. The casual mention of Slugpox in a warning further reinforces this blend, highlighting an ongoing, minor annoyance against the backdrop of world-ending events.
