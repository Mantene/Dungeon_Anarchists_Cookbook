# Chapter 283: Reunion and Sea Snake Threat

## Summary
- **Reunion**: Carl and Donut safely reunite after the Capitol chaos.
- **Sister Ines's Ultimatum**: Influenced by her sponsor (the Nebulars), Sister Ines messages Carl. She has taken control of the giant sea snake boss [[madre-de-aguas]], who is possessed by the Sheol prince [[amayon]], along with three Level 140 pleasure demons.
- **The Threat**: Ines threatens to destroy the church stairwell exit unless Carl surrenders himself to her, using her massive boss resources to block their escape.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Sister Ines's transformation from a poetic nun to a highly dangerous agent of the Nebulars underscores how corporate sponsors manipulate crawlers behind the scenes. Her threat to block the exit forces Carl to rethink his strategy.
