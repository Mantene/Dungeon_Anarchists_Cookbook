# Book 1 Chapter 38: The Rage Elemental

## Summary
Jack's urine stain boils on the wall. [[yolanda-martinez-13]] instantly shoots Jack in the head to stop him, but the infraction has already occurred. Jack's body falls, and a **Level 93 Rage Elemental** coalesces from purple and black smoke. The monster is 15 feet tall, with six legs, obsidian claws, and a horned badger skull with eyes of red fire. Yolanda's arrows do no damage as the monster heals instantly. The description warns it only dissipates after claiming 666 souls.

Donut casts **Confusing Fog**. Randall falls off the train and breaks his hip. Yolanda refuses to leave him and stands her ground, firing arrows. The elemental rips through them, instantly disintegrating Yolanda into red ribbons of flesh and killing Randall. 

As the monster lunges at [[carl]], he casts **Protective Shell** (granted by the BigBoi Boxers). The elemental's claws rebound off the static barrier, halting its charge. 

The elemental casts **Reverse Gravity**. Carl and Donut are thrown to the ceiling, running upside-down. [[elle-mcgibbons]] is thrown to the ceiling, her wheelchair crashing on top of her. Carl carries her over his shoulder, runs to the edge of the spell, and drops back to the floor. He heals his broken bones with his spell.

They reach the safe room (styled as a **Big Shot Chicken** restaurant, run by a Bopca Protector named Qwist). Carl throws a *Jug O' Boom* at the elemental's back, plunging its health by 25% and temporarily slowing its self-healing, before slamming the door. The elemental pounds furiously on the door.

Only 36 Meadow Lark residents survive. Carl heals Mrs. McGibbons. Brandon rages at Jack's stupidity. Carl feels a wave of doubt, wondering if keeping these fragile people alive is just prolonging their torment.
