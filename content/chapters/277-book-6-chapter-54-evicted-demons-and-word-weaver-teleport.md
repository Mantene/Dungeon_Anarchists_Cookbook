# Chapter 277: Evicted Demons and Word-Weaver Teleport

## Summary
- **Recon Mission**: Five miles from the exit, they hear monk seals singing. Samantha is thrown on a recon mission and reports a giant sea snake washed up on shore, surrounded by zombie baby seals and crabs. Sister Ines is riding the snake's head, reciting poetry.
- **Demon Eviction event**: The *Chowder War* quest fails and *Hell Comes to Crawler Town* starts. A global *Demon Eviction* event initiates (due to zombie turkeys in the ocean and crab sperm). Evicted Sheol demons possess weak undead shells; if they grab a crawler, both are dragged to Sheol.
- **Retrieval**: Samantha chomps onto Sister Ines, and Carl uses the xistera's retrieval to reel her back to their location, but the teleport goes out of control.
