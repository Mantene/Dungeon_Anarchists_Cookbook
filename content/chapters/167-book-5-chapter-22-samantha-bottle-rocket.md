# Book 5 Chapter 22: Samantha the Bottle Rocket

## Summary
- **Weaponizing Samantha**: Carl designs a trap that attaches mini seeking missiles to [[samantha]]'s latex head. Since she has learned to open and close her mouth, she can pull the trigger string with her fangs. Because she is a withering spirit with only 1 HP but is indestructible, the explosions do not harm her.
- **The Brain Juice Potion**: To find the artillery hunters, Carl drinks *Major Charlie* (Brain Juice), which raises his Int by 10 (increasing his *Ping* range to 3 km) but will drop his Int by 10 for five minutes afterward. He also drinks a Levitation potion to get high-ground visibility.
- **The Artillery Hunters**: Carl casts *Ping* and locates two Moon Elf artillery hunters from [[the-dream]] 1.5 km away in a forest clearing.
- **Samantha's Flight**: Carl throws Samantha with his xistera. Samantha pulls the trigger in midair. One hunter is blown apart by the missiles, and the other, [[akland]] (Level 51 Moon Elf Artillery Specialist), is knocked unconscious. Samantha bites onto his ankle, and when Carl teleports her back, she drags Akland with her.
- **Marked for Death**: Carl takes Akland's +10 Con bracelet and has Donut heal him to wake him up for interrogation.

## Characters Introduced
- [[akland]] — Level 51 Moon Elf Artillery Specialist, hunter from The Dream.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[the-dream]]**: The hunter faction targeting Louis is confronted.
- **[[systemic-oppression]]**: The weaponization of a re-animated god (Samantha) as an explosive projectile to counter the hunters' siege weaponry.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's weaponization of Samantha is a brilliant, dark example of dungeon pragmatism. By utilizing her indestructible god-form as a rocket-propelled grenade, he solves the artillery threat without risking himself or the guards. The description of the *Brain Juice* potion's side effects shows the physical and mental tolls Carl is willing to accept to gain a temporary tactical advantage.
