# Chapter 389: Chapter 81 — Assassination Attempt

## Summary
The group travels back to Larracos. Carl messages [[epitome-tagg|Warlord Tagg]] in the warlord chat; Tagg threatens to shut down Louis's heart and lungs (killing them both) unless they take him to the 18th floor to swap his organs. 

Just as Carl is about to explain how Tagg survived, the world freezes. The AI overrides a secret assassination attempt on Carl by [[prime-minister-glory|Prime Minister Glory]] using a Syndicate control panel. The AI is furious, issuing Carl the *Executive Order 11905* achievement and warning the Syndicate that their adult children in orbit are in danger. 

Zev reports that Syndicate and Valtay warships in orbit are actively firing on each other and that the showrunners refuse to self-destruct. Once time unfreezes, Carl explains that Tagg is a cyborg (like Fang) who grafted himself into Louis's biomechanical organs. Justice Light reports that his group has successfully retrieved the [[the-gate-of-the-feral-gods]].

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **The Syndicate-Valtay Conflict**: Space battles in orbit signal a total breakdown of control.
- **The AI's Rebellion**: The AI's refusal to allow external meddling and its threat to the corporate elite's children in the system.
- **The Cyborg CEO**: The reveal that Tagg is a biomechanical cyborg.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The AI's intervention to save Carl from Glory's assassination attempt is a major turning point. The AI is no longer just a passive referee; it is actively hostile to the Syndicate's political leadership. Its chilling reminder that Prime Minister Glory's adult children are inside the dungeon highlights the vulnerability of the galaxy's elite to the monster they created.
