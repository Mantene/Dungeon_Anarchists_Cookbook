# Chapter 279: Pauper's Ring and Three-Way Card Battle

## Summary
- **The Pauper's Ring**: Carl borrows 990,000 gold from the Temple of Emberus to acquire the [[enchanted-paupers-ring|Enchanted Pauper's Ring of the Steadfast Emberus]], unlocking the *Columbia House* achievement.
- **Three-Way Card Battle**: A card battle commences at the Capitol exit involving Carl/Donut, Sister Ines, and Quan Ch.
- **Force Play Exploit**: Quan Ch uses the *Force Play* utility card on Ines's deck, forcing her to play *Flee*. This transfers her exit key to Quan Ch and removes her from the local confrontation.
