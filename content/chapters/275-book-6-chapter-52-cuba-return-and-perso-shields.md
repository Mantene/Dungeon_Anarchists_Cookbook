# Chapter 275: Cuba Return and Perso-Shields

## Summary
- **Stairwell Activation**: On December 26th, the stairwells open. In the Cuba zone, 2 squads have keys (Carl/Donut and Sister Ines). 3 do not.
- **Imani's Crisis**: Imani's squad failed their keymaster, wiped out by their own totem Amhuluk. She teleported to Cuba and ducked into the saferoom to escape Quan Ch (who is alone and keyless).
- **Mercenary Training**: Carl unlocks a Valtay Perso-Shield for himself and *The Bull Rush* for Donut. He trains with the mercenaries in the Mercenary Arcade, meeting Doctor Bones (Level 90 Skin Skellie Bard, whose former patron was Astrid). Carl plans to buy the 5M gold barracks upgrade.
- **Traps Tripped**: Carl's exit traps at El Capitolio in Havana are tripped by three crawlers.
