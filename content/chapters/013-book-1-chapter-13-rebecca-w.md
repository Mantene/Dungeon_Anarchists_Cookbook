# Book 1 Chapter 13: Rebecca W

## Summary
[[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] inspect the corpse of **[[rebecca-w]]**. They find she was completely stripped of gear, leaving only an apple core. The system tooltip reveals she was killed by crawler **[[frank-q]]**. Carl finds a single bullet wound in her chest, confirming a fellow human shot and looted her. 

This introduces the threat of crawler-on-crawler violence. The sight of the murdered woman triggers a memory of Carl's overbearing father, who once told Carl: *"I don’t need you to like me. But you will respect me."* Carl vows to hunt Frank Q down. Donut explains she learned about action stars like Charles Bronson because Carl left the TV on when he went to work. They decide to clear out the remaining red dots in the neighborhood before heading back to the safe room to watch the show's premiere.

## Characters Introduced
- [[rebecca-w]] (Deceased crawler, physical appearance)
- [[frank-q]] (Antagonistic crawler, mentioned)

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival]]**: The danger of other desperate, hostile crawlers who are willing to kill for loot.
- **[[threat-of-frank-q]]**: Carl commits to finding and neutralizing Frank Q.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Rebecca's death is a sobering reminder that humans are often their own worst enemies in crisis. Carl's flash of memory regarding his father hints at a history of dealing with bullies, explaining his intense, protective anger and determination to bring the killer to justice.
