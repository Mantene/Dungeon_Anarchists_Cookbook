# Book 6 Chapter 6: The Chowder War

## Summary
Carl, Donut, and Team Sister Ines engage the Monk Seal faction on the beach and activate their first T'Ghee totems.
- **The Beach Encounter**: The group heads to the northern beach of Havana and encounters a Level 70 Monk Seal Picket Sentry named [[yago]], trained in Caribbean Kung Fu.
- **The Chowder War Quest**: The system issues a compulsory regional quest, [[the-chowder-war]], explaining the conflict between the Monk Seals and the Red *Maníseros* Land Crabs. The seals want to prevent the crabs from polluting the ocean during mating season, while the crabs use *Juego de maní* martial arts to fight their way to the ocean to reproduce. Crawlers must resolve the war to complete the quest.
- **Yago's Capture**: During the fight, Yago casts a ranged-protection spell. Carl helps defeat him, and Team Sister Ines captures Yago using a squad flag, transforming him into an Uncommon T'Ghee card totem. Because they have open slots, Yago is automatically activated as a melee/tank minion in their deck.
- **Geraldo's Attack**: Immediately after Carl hands the card to Sister Ines, another Level 70 Monk Seal named [[geraldo]] attacks the party. Carl prepares to capture him for his own squad.

## Characters Introduced

## Themes & Plot Threads

- **[[t-ghee-card-combat]]**: First hands-on demonstration of capturing, activating, and summoning folklore totems in combat.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The description of the Chowder War highlights Matt Dinniman's signature style of vulgar, absurd, yet mechanically complex dungeon design. The automatic activation rule for totems restricts crawlers, forcing them to be strategic about which mobs they flag.
