# Book 5 Chapter 30: Valtay Landing Vessel

## Summary
- **Security Checkpoint**: Carl is teleported to a low-gravity space station in orbit, where he sees Madagascar and a massive fire in southern India.
- **The Bribe**: An older shade gnoll security officer pats Carl down. He requests that Carl "look approvingly" on his grandpup [[lix]]'s entry in the art contest. In return, he will overlook Carl stealing items from the production trailers.
- **The Valtay Submarine**: Carl is transferred to a massive underwater Valtay landing vessel parked at the bottom of Earth's ocean, pressurized to Earth standard.
- **Donut Safe**: Zev (wearing a rebreather) tells Carl that Donut is safe in the guildhall (*Safehome Yolanda*) after a fight with [[lucia-mar]].
- **Surveillance and Rest**: Zev notes that the Valtay have put the vessel under constant surveillance following the Loita incident and an escape attempt. She explains they decided to keep Carl in the trailer for both the Art Contest and the Panel (6 hours later) rather than transferring him back to the dungeon, which will also protect him from whatever is waiting for him at the Aloe Mana pub.
- **CrawlCon Prep**: Zev opens Carl's inventory so he can put on the CrawlCon badge. Carl's interface is blocked by the vessel's containment zone. The first event is judging the CrawlCon kids' art contest.

## Characters Introduced
- [[lix]] — Young shade gnoll, art contest participant (mentioned).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[crawlcon]]**: Carl prepares to participate in the first event.
- **[[the-butchers-masquerade]]**: The corporate surveillance and strictness of the Valtay are detailed.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The transition to the Valtay landing vessel is a stark shift in environment, showcasing the immense high-tech wealth of the new principal owners. Zev's intervention to keep Carl in the trailer is a double-edged sword—while it keeps him safe from a potential ambush by Lucia Mar at Alucarda's pub, it keeps him away from the dungeon and under constant surveillance. The gnoll's bribe attempt shows that corruption is rampant even within the security forces.
