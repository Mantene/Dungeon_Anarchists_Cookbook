# Book 4 Chapter 16: Disposal of the Goose

## Summary
Carl, Donut, and Mongo enter the floating home and face the bizarre, high-stakes Boss Battle against Denise the feral goose.
- **Entering the Home**: Inside, the home resembles a typical human suburban house, though disrupted by the *Wasteland's* crash. Carl loots the body of Commandant Kane on the kitchen table, finding 5,030 gold, a *Mysterious Watch* (the second watch piece), and a warning letter from the Mad Dune Mage Ghazi.
- **The Boss Battle Trigger**: Little Bonnie, who is under a mind-control spell, offers them "lemonade" (which she calls a Sausage Memorial Fund stand). As Carl tries to grab it, a Boss Battle is triggered, and a 3-minute timer to the house's collapse begins.
- **Fight Restrictions**: A magic dampening field activates, blocking all spells and scrolls (No Magic). A second restriction, "No Physical Damage," makes Carl and Mongo's direct attacks useless. The boss is **Denise the Feral Goose Mother** (Level 53 Borough Boss), a white goose wearing a baby blue bonnet and shawl who shoots razor-sharp teeth from her mouth like a shotgun.
- **Disposal of Denise**: Carl realizes the goose is only vulnerable to "environmental damage" from objects within the house (like mugs and pitchers). In a desperate final struggle with seconds remaining, Carl shoves the goose's head into the kitchen sink's garbage disposal, and Donut flips the switch. The disposal grinds the goose, defeating the boss with only 1 second left.
- **Liberation**: The Air Quadrant is officially liberated, allowing crawlers to travel between quadrants. The house landing safely bumps on the ground.

## Characters Introduced
- [[bonnie]] — Level 5 dirigible gnome child, daughter of Kane, who is under a spell.
- [[denise]] — Level 53 Feral Goose Mother (Borough Boss).
- [[commandant-kane]] — (Looted Corpse) Gnomish commander of the *Wasteland*.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- [[the-gate-of-the-feral-gods]]: Carl retrieves the second watch piece (*Mysterious Watch*) from Kane's body. Svern has the first, and Ghazi the Mage has the third.
- [[systemic-oppression]]: The boss fight restrictions and the mocking "Wild Goose Chase" achievement (revealing the potion was just regular lemonade) highlight the System AI's cruel design.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Using a garbage disposal to grind a Level 53 boss goose to death in a suburban kitchen is one of the most hilariously visceral and chaotic fights in the series. The detail that Denise was "plucked from the seventh floor" and driven mad by the Nothing suggests a tragic origin for many of the dungeon's monsters.
