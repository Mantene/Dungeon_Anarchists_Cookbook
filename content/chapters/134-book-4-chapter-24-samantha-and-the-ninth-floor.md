# Book 4 Chapter 24: Samantha and the Ninth Floor

## Summary
The decrystallization of the Sandcastle reverts Lika's sex doll head, which is possessed by the spirit of a lesser deity, and Carl gathers crucial intel on the Faction Wars.
- **The Decrystallization**: Because Gwen and Tran entered the Sandcastle's basement, the crystallization spell ends. Objects that were glass return to normal. Consequently, Lika's head turns back into latex and is ejected from Carl's inventory as a non-item entity.
- **Meeting Samantha**: The head is possessed by the *Withering Spirit of Psamathe* (whom Carl names **Samantha**). Samantha is a lesser deity of the Nothing, daughter of a Celestial Ascendency god, whose soul was split (half in the doll, half in the ghost of Quetzalcoatlus). She is unblinking, speaks through a posable jaw (which Carl tunes like a radio), and screams threats to "kill Carl's mother."
- **Hiring the Doll**: To keep her safe and prevent her presence from summoning an angry god outside, Mordecai suggests hiring her as a "trainer" NPC in their personal space. Donut hires her, and Carl locks the head in the training room.
- **The Submarine Plan**: With 4.5 days remaining, they plan to attack the Water Quadrant's submarine *Akula* to disable the pump. Donut refuses to swim again; Carl suggests she float in the decapitated house and drop depth charges.
- **Floor 9 Faction Wars Intel**: Carl asks Mordecai about the mechanics of Floor 9 (Larracos Faction Wars). Mordecai explains:
  - Larracos is an inverted cone-shaped underground city. It is surrounded by nine faction zones (depicted as flower petals).
  - Factions start with 15,000 troops, mostly NPCs, but they can pay for their own people (tourists/officers) to fill the ranks. Faction officers are protected from permanent death on Floor 9.
  - Faction players can send hunters to Floor 6 (The Hunting Grounds) to win gear to sell to the factions. However, hunters *can* die permanently on Floor 6.
  - Anyone can sign up as a hunter (starting at Level 30) until the opening of Floor 6.
- **Carl's Scheme**: Carl focuses on the map and the sign-up rules. Katia senses he is planning something incredibly stupid, which he does not deny.

## Characters Introduced

- [[valtay-skull-empire-conflict]]: The faction mechanics and hunter rules on Floor 6 and 9 are analyzed for potential exploits.
- [[systemic-oppression]]: The wealthy factions treating the Faction Wars like a "paintball weekend" while sacrificing 15,000 NPC troops highlights the Syndicate's total disregard for NPC lives.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The introduction of Samantha as a permanent, profane, screaming companion head locked in Carl's training room adds a brilliant layer of comedy and foreshadowing. Carl's interest in the sign-up rules for Floor 6 hunters suggests he intends to target wealthy tourists directly where they are vulnerable to permanent death.
