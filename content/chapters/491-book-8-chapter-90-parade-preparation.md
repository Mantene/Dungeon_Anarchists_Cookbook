# Chapter 491: Chapter 90 — Parade Preparation

## Summary
The float procession for Team 5 (Vengeance) is completed, consisting of five vehicles (including the chicken truck, an ice-cream truck, a stair truck, and a Toyota Tundra) and three giant animals (Onikuma the bear, Old Shuck, and Sweety the tapir). 

Tipid drives the chicken truck, Hedy drives the ice-cream truck, Jamal drives the Tundra, and Rosetta rides Onikuma. Prepotente and Jurgen ride Sweety. Donut uses her *Sprout Forest* ability to grow Requite Dahlias (black, gold, magenta, white) to cover the floats, matching the blankets and hats of her Gurkha class. 

The other teams are also preparing:
- **Float 1 (Resolution)**: Florin, Lucia, and Ajib's crew.
- **Float 2 (Decision)**: A group making a giant menu based on Donut's joke.
- **Float 3 (Betrayal)**: Elle, Imani, three Chinese crawlers, and Bodi.
- **Float 4 (Destruction)**: Makana, Sarah Hayse, and several Chinese crawlers with an Abrams tank and a Fleetwood RV.

Carl officially leaves the Royal Court of Princess Donut party and joins Safehome Yolanda, segregating his inventory and space. Bucket Boy is sent to wait in the guild commons to keep him safe from the combat. The countdown timer expires, and they are summoned to the staging area.
