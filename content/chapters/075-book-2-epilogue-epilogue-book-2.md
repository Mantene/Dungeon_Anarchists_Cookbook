# Book 2 Epilogue: Epilogue (Book 2)

## Summary
[[carl]], [[princess-donut]], [[mongo]], and [[katia-grim]] arrive in the Floor 4 green room. Carl builds a makeshift loincloth out of toilet paper because his pants and underwear are gone. 

Thirty minutes later, they sit for a live interview on *Dungeon Crawler After Hours with Odette*. Odette reveals that the dynamite detonator *did* fire, and the resulting soul release from the swordsmen guards tempered the wild magic blast, saving the town and the crawlers. 

For this, Carl, Donut, and Katia were awarded **Celestial Boxes**. However, because these boxes are incredibly tax-heavy for the showrunners and grant crawlers massive power, the Borant Corporation used their single seasonal host veto to cancel the rewards. 

Only [[quan-ch]] (Imperial Security Trooper) received his box because he opened it before the veto, getting a flying robe with magical wings. 

Because they caused the explosion, Carl's team received shared experience for all the killed mobs. Carl rises to Level 27, Donut rises to Level 26, and Katia rises to Level 21. 

Odette displays the tentative leaderboard:
1. [[lucia-mar]] (Level 29)
2. [[hekla]] (Level 28)
3. [[prepotente]] (Level 27)
4. Florin (Level 24)
5. [[miriam-dom]] (Level 27)
6. [[carl]] (Level 27)
7. [[princess-donut]] (Level 26)
8. Ifechi (Level 18)
9. [[li-jun]] (Level 25)
10. [[elle-mcgibbons]] (Level 13)

Odette warns Carl that King Rust has sponsored a deity (likely the war god **Grull**) to target him on Floor 4. She also warns him that Hekla is plotting to convince Donut to join *Brynhild's Daughters* to gain access to Mordecai as a permanent manager.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The host veto of the Celestial boxes is a classic example of corporate greed and systemic exploitation, cheating the crawlers out of their hard-earned rewards to protect profits. The reveal of Mrs. McGibbons on the leaderboard is a shocking development, showing that the Meadow Lark residents are somehow thriving on Floor 3.
