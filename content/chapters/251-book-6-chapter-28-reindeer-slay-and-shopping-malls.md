# Book 6 Chapter 28: Reindeer Slay and Shopping Malls

## Summary
Katia's addiction is detailed, and Carl and Donut test an 18-card deck in the mock arena, discovering the terrifying power and mental strain of Shi Maria's abilities.
- **Katia's Bliss Surfing**: Imani explains that Katia is comatose for ten hours at a "drug den" inside the Desperado Club after self-serving a bowl of drugs. The debuff ("Bliss Surfing") means she is reliving intense memories; she has a 50% chance of gaining strength but may lose intelligence. Elle is guarding her.
- **Mock Arena Setup**: Carl and Donut enter the Mock Battle Arena, choosing a 1980s-themed shopping mall simulation at Medium difficulty. They test an 18-card deck featuring their five active totems (Geraldo, Asojano, Skylar Spinach, Uzi Jesus, Raul, and Shi Maria) along with utility, snare, and mystic cards.
- **Totem Chaos**:
  - Donut summons [[heyzoos]] (Uzi Jesus), who lands in a flash of angelic music and immediately starts firing his Uzi with a Texan accent.
  - Donut then summons [[shi-maria]] (The Screamer). As soon as she appears, Uzi Jesus panics and fires on her; she retaliates by skewering him and swatting his Uzi away. He is killed and returns to dust.
- **The Slay-Deer Battle**: The arena spawns **Rude-Dolph the Blood-Nosed Slay-Deer** (Level 100 Puppet-Master, currently invulnerable) and his eight puppet reindeer. Rude-Dolph fires a continuous *Magic Missile* laser from his nose, shearing off one of Shi Maria's legs.
- **Splitting and Illusions**: Donut summons **Skylar Spinach**, who splits into a flying donkey head (*Skylar*) and a spiky snake (*Spinach*). Shi Maria utilizes *Greased Lightning* (applied by Donut) to cast rapid illusions, splitting into four copies.
- **The Fifth Eye & Mental Strain**: Shi Maria opens her closed fifth eye, driving the remaining puppet reindeer blind and insane, causing them to gore each other. Carl feels a sudden, painful mental pull in his head, linking the eye to a "river in my mind." He collapses to a knee, but hides his distress from Donut.
- **Geraldo's Shock**: With the puppets dead, Shi Maria pounces and rips Rude-Dolph in two, greedily devouring his entrails. Donut summons **Geraldo** the monk seal, who performs karate moves but stops dead in horror at the sight of the spider devouring the reindeer. The combat completes.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **Crawler Psychological Strain**: Carl's reaction to the fifth eye reveals that the Primal "river" in his mind is growing and remains vulnerable to cosmic/psionic magic.
- **Deity & Monster Interaction**: The chaotic interactions between Uzi Jesus, Shi Maria, and Geraldo show that managing high-level totems requires supreme coordination.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The shopping mall simulation is a brilliant piece of dark comedy, contrasting the nostalgia of a 1980s mall with the gore of Rude-Dolph and the horror of Shi Maria devouring a reindeer like spaghetti. Carl's physical reaction to Shi Maria's fifth eye—despite consuming *Liquid Therapy*—is a major warning sign. The "river in my mind" suggests that Carl is slowly losing his mental shielding, exposing a deeper vulnerability to cosmic madness that will inevitably complicate their descent to the lower floors.
