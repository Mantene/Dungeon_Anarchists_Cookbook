# Book 2 Chapter 23: Daniel Bautista

## Summary
[[carl]] climbs down a chimney to escape the collapsing municipal building. In the debris, he finds a charred glass case containing a Beanie Baby named "Kimaris" (an armored man on a black horse) and stores it. 

Outside, they meet the Tigran Swashbuckler [[daniel-bautista-2]] (Level 18, cousin/brother of Grace, Nica, and Lea Bautista). Daniel explains that he was supposed to kill [[miss-quill]] for a quest to get access to the Desperado Club. He was "cockblocked" by Carl's explosion, but is grateful that Carl avenged his family on Floor 2. 

Daniel shares that he is the sole survivor of his family of nineteen, expressing deep grief and identity confusion about his tiger body. He shares the location of another Floor 4 stairwell and establishes a chat connection with Carl before departing. 

Carl and Donut discuss their own dream jobs, and Carl admits he would have loved to be a simple forester watching for fires alone in the woods. They prepare to confront the remaining target, Remex the lich, in the guards' warehouse.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival]]**: The heavy psychological toll of the crawl and the loss of family.
- **[[solidarity-and-duty]]**: Daniel Bautista's gratitude for Carl's revenge on Floor 2.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Daniel's despair over his tiger body ("How can we make it to heaven if god doesn't recognize us?") highlights the psychological body horror of the crawl's race selections. Carl's quiet confession of wanting to be a forester reflects his deep exhaustion and desire for peace, contrasting with the chaotic brawler he is forced to become.
