# Book 5 Chapter 21: CrawlCon Invitation

## Summary
- **Recap Highlights**: The recap show features Vrah and her mantis team hunting crawlers, Lucia Mar defeating a stegosaurus boss with a damage-reflect spell, and Katia's team raiding a druid town to find Eva.
- **Hunter Communication Ban**: An Admin Announcement reveals that due to a new court injunction, **hunters can no longer communicate with the outside world**, leveling the playing field.
- **CrawlCon Roster**: Carl opens his Platinum Fan Box and receives a VIP badge for **CrawlCon**. He is virtually scheduled for three events: an Art Contest judging panel, a "Crawling Through the Ages" panel (moderated by Circe Took), and an Autograph/Keepsake session. Donut is extremely jealous because she cannot go.
- **Guild Merging**: Katia leaves the party to coordinate with the new guild, *Safehome Yolanda*. Donut's party *The Royal Court of Princess Donut* joins Safehome Yolanda. They unlock a Pet Stable and the *Instacot 60* bed upgrade (+15% stats).
- **Beaver Clint**: An explosion rocks the town. Carl and Donut rush outside and find [[clint-smashgrab]] transformed into a were-castor (a giant were-beaver with buck teeth, a lisp, and a flat tail). Elves are firing poison gas bombs at the town from a trebuchet in the woods. Carl has a ridiculous idea to stop it.

## Characters Introduced
No new characters are introduced (Clint's beaver/castor form is revealed).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[crawlcon]]**: Carl is selected to virtually attend the galactic fan convention.
- **[[were-castors]]**: Clint's were-beaver form is introduced.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The communication ban on hunters is a massive victory for the crawlers, stripping the hunters of real-time coaching from the outside world. The reveal of Clint's beaver form is humorous, but it immediately transitions into a crisis as Point Mongo is bombarded by elf artillery. Carl's "ridiculous idea" promises some creative improvisation using the available resources.
