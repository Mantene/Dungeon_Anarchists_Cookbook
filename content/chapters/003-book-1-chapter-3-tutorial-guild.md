# Book 1 Chapter 3: The Tutorial Guild

## Summary
Safe inside the Tutorial Guild, [[carl]] learns that [[mordecai]] is a Level 50 Rat Hooligan and a non-combatant NPC. Knowing the two remaining goblins outside will summon their clan if left alive, Carl temporarily leaves the guildhall to eliminate them. He boards the Goblin Murder Dozer, throws one goblin into the spinning front blades, and beats the Level 3 Goblin Engineer with his own cooking-pot helmet before crushing him by jumping on him from above. The dozer's boiler breaches, forcing Carl to scramble back into the guildhall before it explodes.

Back inside, Mordecai reveals he is a Changeling who was originally a "skyfowl" from a previous crawl. He survived to Floor 11 before choosing to become a guildmaster. He explains that the dungeon is customized to the planet it runs on, utilizing Earth's language and cultural concepts, and that humans are a "seeded" species.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival]]**: Carl's desperate fight on top of the moving bulldozer.
- **[[systemic-oppression]]**: The concept of "human-seeded worlds," showing that humanity's existence is controlled by the Syndicate.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's willingness to go back outside and fight the goblins shows his tactical survival instinct. We learn about Mordecai's tragic backstory—a former crawler who gave up on Floor 11 and was transformed into a guide. Donut's cat behavior (knocking over a vase of Mordecai's mother's ashes) provides comic relief.
