## Summary
- **Box Opening**: Carl opens his boxes: Gold Talk of the Town Box -> Medium upgrade patch with a burning book (+15% Intelligence, *Book Burner* spell-forget benefit); Wand Box -> *Wand of Nighty-Night* (10 charges, Level 10 sleep); Boss Box -> *Warrior's Gold Helmet of Resistance*; Benefactor Box -> a flyer for 'The Shepherd's House' in Homestead, Florida ('No pets allowed').
