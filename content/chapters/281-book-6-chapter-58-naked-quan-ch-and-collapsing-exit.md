# Chapter 281: Naked Quan Ch and Collapsing Exit

## Summary
- **Teleporting In**: Carl teleports into the thick of the Capitol stairwell battle to assist Ren's squad.
- **Totems Defeated**: The fused *Uzi Jesus* (combined with Skylar Spinach) and the snake totem *Asojano* are defeated, reverting into gold-edition totems.
- **Fashion Critic Spell**: The goblin bard [[jola]] casts *Fashion Critic* on Quan Ch, stripping him naked and removing all of his gear.
- **Quan Ch's Death**: Deprived of his gear and options, Quan Ch is killed in the combat. Princess Donut inherits both of his exit keys.
- **Collapsing Exit**: The combat has heavily damaged the stairwell doorway, triggering a 5-minute collapse timer that will permanently block the exit.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The comedic but lethal intervention of Jola's *Fashion Critic* contrasts sharply with the grim reality of the damaged stairwell. Even though Carl and Donut secured two keys, the structural damage to the stairwell exit introduces a ticking clock and a tragic dilemma.
