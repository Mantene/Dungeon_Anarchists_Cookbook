# Book 2 Chapter 5: Earth Hobby Potions

## Summary
While Mordecai sleeps, Carl and Donut open their Silver Earth Boxes. Donut receives Amazon packages containing a scented candle and a large cat tree, plus an **Earth Hobby Potion** that grants her Level 3 in **Scutelliphily** (the hobby of collecting shields/badges). She also retrieves her spellbook, obtaining the Level 1 **Clockwork Triplicate** spell (creates two clockwork clones of a pet/minion).

Carl opens his box, receiving a cactus plant, a box set of Louis L'Amour western novels, and a Hobby Potion that grants him Level 3 in **Cesta Punta** (Jai alai). 

Donut's high Charisma causes [[gordo]] to act subserviently, bringing her walnuts. Carl practices explosives-handling with his Bomb Surgeon skill and decides to go back into the ruins to hunt lemurs and locate the circus tents. 

In the ruins, Donut sneakily kills a sentinel lemur. Mongo eats its flesh, rising to Level 4. Donut spot the circus tents in the distance, hearing calliope music.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[carls-class-selection]]**: Carl practices explosives handling, using his Bomb Surgeon skill to sense the state of dynamite.
- **[[survival]]**: The crawlers acquire new equipment and spells to face the circus threat.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The Earth Hobby Potions (mandated by the Indigenous Species Protection Act) represent a bizarre corporate bureaucracy trying to "preserve" Earth culture while executing its population. Carl's emotional reaction to the Louis L'Amour books shows the lingering trauma of his childhood and the bittersweet connection to his past.
