# Chapter 348: Chapter 43 — Loophole Closed

## Summary
[[odette|Odette]] takes over as host in her floating wheelchair. She addresses [[cascadia|Cascadia]], who is drunk on tequila. [[princess-vinata|Vinata]] whispers to [[carl|Carl]] that Cascadia's home system has erupted in civil war, and Borant 3 was hit by a warship, killing millions. Vinata warns Carl they are ready for his attack and offers to resolve his "Katia problem" if he joins her father's court.

Carl is outraged that the off-world warlords are communicating with the outside by paying 30,000 credits to change the names of TV shows in the guide channel (e.g. Epitome Tagg's show "Yes. We have Deactivated the Infected Hardware"). Carl uses Team Retribution's second-to-last Emergency Action Item to close this loophole.

When Odette questions [[juice-box|Juice Box]], the changeling delivers a passionate speech condemning the galaxy's leaders and viewers for their cruelty to dungeon-born NPCs. Juice Box transforms into a terrifying, armless, midnight-black alien entity with waving tentacles and root-like scuttling legs. She warns, "It is coming. It is coming for you all," before scuttling through the wall. The Viceroy [[architect-houston|Architect Houston]] shakes with excitement at the form she took.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Juice Box's speech represents the voice of the millions of NPCs and dungeon-born who are created, tortured, and deleted for off-world entertainment. Her transformation into "The Hag/The Beautiful Place" (a legendary cosmic horror associated with the Nothing) terrifies the council and excites Houston. Carl's closure of the show-guide loophole cuts off the warlords' last connection to orbit, further isolating the factions in the dungeon.
