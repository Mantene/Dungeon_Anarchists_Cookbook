# Chapter 372: Rolling Battle Formation

## Summary
[[mordecai]] explains that Eris's rapture is part of the **Ceremony of Chaos**, a unique, once-per-crawl spell she usually casts on the 12th floor to summon mortal witnesses, rendering them invulnerable and keeping them in a frozen state. The witnesses are dropped in a massive pile. 

Donut reports that she can see the pile near the Dream castle, estimating it contains 80,000 people (including the entire Reaver army, which had been missing). She also notes that Eris accidentally made the Feral God Meatus invulnerable as well. 

[[rosetta]] warns that if Eris realizes her mistake and cancels the Ceremony, the invulnerability will drop early, but the witnesses will remain frozen, causing them to suffocate or be crushed under the weight of the pile. 

Carl prepares to abandon his Madness campaign to help free the pile, but Donut tells him to stay on mission. She has a two-part plan to roll the pile away from the god fight and toward the field of corpses. Donut casts **Rolling Battle Formation**, an Atrocity-tier spell she acquired from Vernon's stinky wizard hat, amplified by the Nest tower. 

The spell sweeps the entire pile—including Eris, Meatus (with his dink slopped out the back), and the stroboscopic rainbow beacon from Osvaldo's crystal—into a colossal, steering-controlled ball of flesh. Donut can drive it like a remote-controlled vehicle. Harpocrates pursues the ball, trying to reclaim his stolen appendage, but is blocked from approaching by the rainbow light.

Meanwhile, Louis's airship is ripped in half by the gravity magic of the bleached drake dragon. Louis drops his unarmed payloads, which fail to detonate, and feather-falls toward the Reaver castle, tracked by their security. Carl commands Samantha to go rescue him.

## Characters
* [[carl]]: Discusses Eris's spell with the team, coordinates with Louis, and decides to proceed with the Madness castle infiltration.
* [[princess-donut]]: Commits an Atrocity by casting *Rolling Battle Formation*, consolidating the 80,000 witnesses and two gods into a giant steering-controlled ball.
* [[mordecai]]: Explains the rules of the Ceremony of Chaos and expresses shock at Donut's spell.
* [[rosetta]]: Coordinates operations and reports on Eris's state inside the ball.
* [[louis-santiago-2]]: Survives the destruction of *Party Planner* and feather-falls into the Reaver stronghold.
* [[samantha]]: Abandons her pursuit by Yarilo and flies to rescue Louis.

## Themes & Plot Threads
* **Rolling Battle Formation**: Donut's katamari-like ball of flesh is a massive exploit of the Faction Wars rules, consolidating all troops and two gods into a single steered entity.
* **The Vulnerability of the Pile**: The consolidation of the floor's armies into a single, fragile mass introduces a high-stakes rescue mission.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's decision to cast *Rolling Battle Formation* shows her growth as a leader. Rather than panicking, she uses a comedic but devastatingly effective spell to relocate the entire army, saving them from the clash between Harpocrates and Meatus. The imagery of the giant people-ball rolling across the fields, with a god's appendage dangling out, captures the surreal tone of the series.
