# Chapter 423: Chapter 24 — Reuniting the Personality

## Summary
- **Dong's Injury**: Dong collapses on the Lady Dominators' driveway. Genesis and Rapture taunt them from inside. Carl carries him back to their garage.
- **New Quest: Half a prayer, half a song**: The AI triggers a quest revealing that Corcunda was split by a spell seasons ago into two halves: **Corky** (who danced at the Desperado Club and loves Dong) and **Porky** (who wanted to be a singer, dislikes Dong, and left). Corky is with the Lady Dominators, while Porky is with a singing guild. Combining the two via a fleshmancer will free Corcunda from his racing obligations. If Dong dies before completion, the quest fails.
- **Stand-in Reward**: If successful, the reward is summoning two stand-ins of Carl and Donut's choice to the arena on the 11th floor.
- **The Sapient Sock**: Imani examines Dong and discovers his health is being sustained by his proximity to his sapient weapon, the stinking "crust sock," which gets larger and smells worse daily. She warns that removing the sock might kill him.
- **Drunken Pets**: Bigs, Mongo, and Rend return from the bugbears completely drunk on beer. Samantha chats about her ex-boyfriend Charles who was a bartender.
