# Chapter 464: GTO Crash & Corky's Rescue

## Summary
As the truck rolls through the thick cloud of toddler cocaine, [[prepotente]] warns [[carl]] that Bianca has become "strung out" on the drugs and cannot rescue Corky. Carl's truck exits the cloud into a bright doll-packing room, finding that the *Lady Dominators*' GTO has fallen through a massive hole in the floor. The GTO is wrecked, its axle broken.

Carl backs the truck up to the hole. The area is swarmed by "Toot-Toot dolls" (Grape-flavored Cocaine Elementals) and "My First Sicarios" action figures, which possess a "pass-through" ability allowing them to bypass shields. 

[[princess-donut]] uses her level 17 *Puddle Jumper* spell to teleport down to the GTO and teleport herself and the unconscious Corky back up. Carl pulls Corky into the truck, but accidentally rips the mantaur's suit, exposing his guts. Donut heals him with a scroll. [[grigori]] cures Corky's "Strung Out" debuff, allowing him to wake up and reunite with Porky and [[dong-quixote]]. With 20 minutes left in the race, Carl shuts the back doors (severing a doll in half) and takes the wheel back from Dong to drive toward the next room.
