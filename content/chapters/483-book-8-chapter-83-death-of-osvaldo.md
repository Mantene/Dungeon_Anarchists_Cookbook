# Chapter 483: Death of Osvaldo

## Summary
The ceiling poleaxe explosions create a vertical exit shaft to the eleventh-floor portals. [[osvaldo]] flings himself upward to escape, leaving a grenade and traps behind to kill his mount, Bruna. [[princess-donut]] and the crawlers deploy protective shields and emergency gremlins to keep Bruna alive. Because Bruna is saved, the portal gate remains locked to Osvaldo. Stunned by the gate, Osvaldo is killed by [[britney-proskurina]], who claims the player-killer skull and the Ysalte crystal. 

The remaining teams return to their garages to escape using the plan to enter the Pineapple Cabaret holding area (the Nothing). They discuss the mechanics of stasis and how former crawler Herot can retrieve them. The Russian Cadillac team refuses the deal and attempts to run for the exit. In a joint effort, Prepotente, Chris, and the APV team disable their shields, and Donut crushes the Cadillac with *Astral Paw*, killing the three Russian crawlers and earning three player-killer skulls.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Osvaldo's selfish attempt to escape by killing his own mount backfires due to the crawlers' coordinated intervention to save the animal. Donut's rapid execution of the non-compliant Russian team highlights the brutal realities of the late-dungeon crawls where collateral damage becomes necessary to safeguard the collective group.
