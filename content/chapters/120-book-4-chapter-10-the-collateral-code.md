# Book 4 Chapter 10: The Collateral Code

## Summary
Carl learns the mechanics of the gnomish collateral watch, gathers Hump Town's children into their safe room, and receives a time-sensitive quest.
- **Water Quadrant Aftermath**: Carl messages [[imani-c]]; she shares that [[chris-andrews-2]] is ignoring her. The Water Quadrant conquest has triggered a trap: the Necropolis catacombs are filling with water, and the exits have sealed shut. Carl tells [[morris-sp]] to buy water-breathing scrolls and retreat to the Desperado Club.
- **Interrogating Henrik**: Donut uses her sunglasses' thermal overlay to identify **Henrik** as a changeling leader (his head glows hot). Henrik explains the daily proof of collateral:
  - Every morning, Henrik opens a pocket watch containing a magic mirror connecting to Commandant Kane on the *Wasteland*.
  - Kane makes a hand gesture; Henrik shows this to Wynne.
  - Wynne tells Henrik a time code; Henrik adjusts the clock hands, which mirrors to Kane's watch.
  - If the code isn't entered, the gnomes bomb the town.
  - Wynne resisted the code once moved to the basement, requiring the Dromedarians to drug him with Mairmei mushrooms.
  - The Bactrian town's collateral was a favorite pet pig of Kane's daughter, which they had to sun daily. Since the Subterranean crawlers killed it, the Bactrians were bombed.
- **Sheltering the Kids**: Carl returns to the Toe and ushers 40 children (Dromedarian and Changeling) into their personal space for shelter, accompanied by **Juice Box**. Three of the changeling children suffer from **Compression Sickness**, including **Ruby** (who has no arms and a deflated head).
- **The Changeling Plan**: Juice Box reveals that the changelings are trying to return to their ancient home, **The Hunting Grounds** (Floor 6). The path home was lost when City Hall collapsed. She promises to tell Carl the truth if he saves the city from the gnomes.
- **New Quest**: Henrik sends the time code message to the gnomes as Carl wrote it, but the gnomes cut communications. A timer of 1 hour and 15 minutes appears, triggering a new quest: **"Squeeze out the Juice Box"** (Save Hump Town from gnomish bombardment).

## Characters Introduced
- [[ruby]] — Minor Changeling child NPC suffering from Compression Sickness.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival-of-the-collapse]]**: The flooding of the Necropolis shows that taking castles out of order can trigger catastrophic environmental traps.
- **[[solidarity-and-duty]]**: Carl decides to use his personal space to shelter Hump Town's children, taking on their protection despite the logistical strain.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The collateral watch system is a classic hostage code, designed to ensure that the Dromedarians could not simply substitute a shapeshifter for the hostage gnome. By failing to save Wynne, the crawlers have broken the code, placing Hump Town in immediate danger. Juice Box's revelation about Floor 6 being the changelings' "ancient home" introduces an overarching narrative connection between the NPCs and the lower floors.
