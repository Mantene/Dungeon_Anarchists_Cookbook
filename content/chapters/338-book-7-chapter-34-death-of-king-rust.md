# Chapter 338: Death of King Rust

## Summary
[[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] meet with [[king-rust|King Rust]] in a registered parlay bubble. Contrary to expectations, the orc king is distraught and crying. He reveals that his youngest daughter, [[princess-formidable|Princess Formidable]], has seized a cruiser and is five hours away from reaching a monitor station orbiting Venus. Formidable intends to activate a planetary failsafe to blow up the star, vaporizing the solar system and killing everyone in it, to prevent the "insane" system AI from spreading to the galactic center. Rust asks Carl to send a message to his daughter on behalf of Earth's citizens to plea for her to stop, as she has a soft spot for "seeds."

Carl negotiates a deal: Rust's army will withdraw, and Carl's forces will not harass their retreat, giving everyone time to prepare. Privately, Carl messages Donut that they do not need to worry about the failsafe because it has already been deactivated; the AI's sphere of influence has expanded to the inner system, and Carl turned off the failsafe earlier. 

Just as the parlay concludes and the protection bubble pops, [[rosetta]] erupts from the ground wearing a tech invisibility cloak. She loops a whip around King Rust's neck, decapitating him instantly. The horde of tens of thousands of enemy troops roars in outrage and charges.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Rosetta's assassination of King Rust completely derails Carl's attempt to negotiate a peaceful retreat. While Carl wanted to exploit the failsafe threat to disperse the army, Rosetta's drive for personal vengeance forces an immediate battle. This highlights the chaos of Floor 9, where the personal agendas of individual crawlers and NPCs constantly disrupt grand strategies.
