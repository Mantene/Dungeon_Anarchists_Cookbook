# Book 1 Chapter 30: Dingoes and Grubs

## Summary
[[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] arrive on Floor 2. The layout is similar to Floor 1, but with white cinderblock walls and orange lichen. They see the **Stairwell to Floor 3** marked on the northeast quadrant map. 

They find the corpse of a **Brindle Grub** (Level 2) killed by [[agatha]]'s shopping cart. The description explains that Brindle Grubs act as dungeon janitors; they eat dead monsters, grow into pupae, and eventually hatch into highly dangerous monsters. Carl realizes they must completely destroy all monster corpses to deny the grubs food. 

They encounter **Danger Dingoes** (Level 5)—mastiff-sized dogs with black-metal corpse-paint faces. Donut, driven by a hatred for dogs, kills them with magic missiles, using almost all her mana, and then shreds their corpses. They also fight **Mind Horrors** (Level 4)—floating brain-like jellyfish that use psionic attacks to cause debilitating headaches in Carl. 

They find a large safe room styled as a French storm shelter, containing a locked mailbox. Carl opens his achievements:
- *Borough Boss!* (No reward)
- *Bully and a Thief!* (Bronze Asshole's Box: 5 gold pieces and Desperado Club drink tickets, awarded for stealing Agatha's shopping cart).
- *Battlefield Construction!* (Silver Mechanic's Box: **The Goo-Inator 3000** shaping tool).
- *This Little Piggy Went to Market!* (Platinum Shoe Box: **Enchanted Pedicure Kit of the Sylph**, awarded for killing 5+ bosses barefoot).
- *You found stairs!* (No reward)

Carl drinks a *Cheat Code Potion* which randomly boosts his **Pugilism** skill by 3, bringing it to Level 10. Donut receives a spellbook from the mailbox and learns **Second Chance**, a Necromancy spell that raises a dead monster of a lower level to fight for them for a minute. Donut is thrilled by the prospect of summoning zombie dogs.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival]]**: Managing Floor 2's ecology (destroying corpses to prevent Brindle Grubs from evolving) represents a new, active tactical survival requirement.
- **[[carls-class-selection]]**: Carl's Pugilism reaches Level 10, cementing his unarmed brawler path. The Pedicure Kit of the Sylph reinforces his barefoot status. Donut gains a necromancy spell, *Second Chance*, introducing minion-based magic to their arsenal.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The second floor introduces more complex environmental mechanics (the Brindle Grubs) and tactical challenges (Mind Horrors). The system's humor is on full display with the *This Little Piggy Went to Market!* achievement, which caters to the audience's foot-fetishism of Carl's barefoot run. Donut's excitement over the *Second Chance* spell shows her growing confidence as a magic-user.
