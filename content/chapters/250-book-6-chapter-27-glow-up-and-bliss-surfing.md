# Book 6 Chapter 27: Glow-Up and Bliss Surfing

## Summary
Carl and Donut grind for cards on their way back north, discover two powerful new cards, learn that Katia is in a coma, and receive the rules for Phase Two.
- **Grinding and Loot**: Moving back north, Carl, Donut, and Samantha kill a sleeping **La Ciguapa** (a Legendary female entity with backward feet, vulnerable to familiars). She drops two powerful cards:
  - **Damnation**: An Uncommon snare card that discards an opponent's totem card once it times out, whether it is dead or not.
  - **Glow-Up**: A Mythic consumable special card that permanently bakes a totem's current status and buffs (like duration extensions) into a new base state.
- **Daughter's Kiss Mechanics**: Carl tests the *Daughter's Kiss* melee attack, discovering it is twice as powerful and releases specialized residual effects (like sonic screams) if his back patch is filled purely with a single species of soul (e.g., Howling Gulls).
- **Update Broadcast**: In the daily show, Carl watches Osvaldo wearing the *Apito memorial crystal* around his neck in a city, and Florin using a 1940s tank to hunt a giant river serpent.
- **Katia's Coma**: Carl notices Katia hasn't responded to messages about the Astrid quest. He messages [[daniel-bautista-2]], who admits that Katia is "asleep" and "out of sorts" inside the Desperado Club after finding a secret merchant. Carl is highly alarmed.
- **Phase Two & Three Rules**: Cascadia broadcasts the transition rules:
  - At 12:01 AM on December 24th, squads teleport to a room to choose a Phase Two location (60-second limit).
  - Unused totems are destroyed; active decks must maintain exactly six totems.
  - Phase Two maps contain a key 10-15 km away. Squads have 48 hours to retrieve it.
  - At midnight on December 26th (Phase Three), squads teleport back to their original area. Those *without* a key will teleport to block the exit path of a keyholder, forcing crawler-on-crawler combat for the key.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **Systemic Murder Incentives**: The Phase Three rules deliberately incentivize crawler-on-crawler violence by forcing keyless squads to ambush keyholders at their exits, generating high-ratings drama.
- **Daughter's Kiss Upgrades**: The mechanical depth of the Scavenger's Daughter back patch grows as Carl learns to manipulate soul species purity for custom elemental attacks.
- **Secret Merchants & Addiction**: Katia's comatose state hints at a dangerous drug trade within the Desperado Club's secret passageways.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The Phase Three transition rules show the absolute sadism of the showrunners, who design a systemic bottleneck that guarantees desperate crawlers will try to slaughter each other for keys. Carl's realization that he might be forced to fight other crawlers leads him to consider using the *Mysterious Bone Key* benefit from his back patch, even though it will destroy his jacket and cost him a bone. Meanwhile, Katia's "out of sorts" state is an ominous development, suggesting she has succumbed to the Desperado Club's drug dens.
