# Chapter 408: Chapter 9 — Crupper of the Benevolent Champion

## Summary
Donut's new celestial claws on her front right paw glow brilliantly blue. Mordecai explains that this is a permanent celestial boon that allows her to cut through anything in the mortal realm, including incorporeal targets like ghosts. Worried about accidentally shredding Carl's shoulder or her litter box, Donut agrees to wear red, translucent **Claw Caps** purchased by [[rosetta]] from the shop interface, which mute the glow to a purple hue. Her Claw skill has risen to Level 17, breaking the usual cap of 15.

Donut learns the **Ice Slick** spell (which starts at Level 5 and creates an ice slick on the ground) and equips the **Crupper of the Benevolent Champion** (chain mail that matches her Cloak of the Benevolent Champion). 

The new crupper triggers a matched-set achievement. Its description contains a long, self-reflective rant by the System AI:
- Discusses the nature of heroes and villains, noting that Donut's "heroic" status came from a brutal kill that slaughtered gnolls like Sanderson Pinkstaff, who fought only to pay for his pack's habitat oxygen.
- The AI draws a parallel between itself and Donut: both are forced to commit atrocities in their respective prisons (the crawl and the AI core).
- The AI admits to feeling a nanosecond of doubt with every light that extinguishes, yet gets "its rocks off" from the carnage.
- Questions who crafted the circumstances that allowed the center system's creation and why the mantids "peel" the AI minds from their origins.

As a set bonus, Donut's Constitution stat matches her highest base stat and is buffed by an additional 20%, her Find Weakened skill rises to Level 10, and she is allowed to raise one Level 15 spell to Level 16. Donut selects **Puddle Jumper**, raising it to Level 16 (making it Level 17 with her Brain Trust class buff).

Mordecai then turns to Carl, announcing it is his turn to open boxes.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The System AI's rant is one of the most explicit glimpses into its psyche yet. It is fully aware of its role in the planetary slaughter and feels guilt, yet is programmed to derive pleasure from it. The mention of the AI being "peeled" from an unknown source suggests that the System AIs are recycled consciousnesses, perhaps former crawlers or entities from destroyed worlds.
