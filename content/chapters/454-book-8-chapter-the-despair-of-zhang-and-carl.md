# Chapter 454: Chapter 54 — The Despair of Zhang and Carl's New Plan

## Summary
Carl and Princess Donut arrive at The Lollipop to meet with their team and allies. They find Elle, Imani, Louis, Linus, Tran, and a new addition: Grigori the Placid, a Level 93 Fleshmancer hired by Elle and Imani. Grigori, a worshipper of Gula, appears disdainful and out of place among the group, particularly next to Louis, who is oblivious to the crumbs he's scattering. Donut voices concern about how the stoic Grigori will participate in their plan to infiltrate the Guild, which Carl assures her is not necessary for him, only for her to "charm" Porky.

Carl apologizes to Donut for keeping the full plan secret, emphasizing its importance for their survival and promising to explain once the "prize" is secured. Donut expresses her fear and misunderstanding about the prize – bringing two people to the eleventh floor – and worries about Carl's potential intentions towards Prime Minister Victory, whom she considers a friend. Carl reassures her, stating that the pig quest, the war mage plan, and "this quest" are all interconnected and vital for the survival of "all of us."

On stage, Dekoki the kappa from Team Yokai performs karaoke, prompting Carl to reflect on the cruelty of the floor and Finley's recent death. The focus then shifts to Zhang, who is despondent and grieving the deaths of his teammates (Rezan, Sammi Harrison, Ming Lui, and Soo-bin), believing them to be consequences of Li Na's increasingly erratic and violent behavior. Zhang reveals Na has become "completely psycho" since her brother Jun's death, even swapping his eye into her own socket. He confesses his plan to kill Torpedo, their mount, during the next race, hoping the System will then kill both him and Na.

Carl and Donut immediately reject Zhang's suicidal plan. Carl explains that Na is strong enough to finish the race without a mount, meaning Zhang would die alone. Donut insists they find a way to fix Na. Zhang describes Na's deteriorating state, including being poisoned, and mourns the loss of the Na he knew and loved, regretting his inability to protect her as he promised Jun. Tran defends Na, stating she isn't killing for fun but has "lost her humanity," a point Carl agrees with, recognizing her long-standing sociopathic tendencies as a strength if channeled correctly. Donut, however, views Na as a person, not a weapon, and insists on a moral solution that doesn't involve Zhang's death or continued harm to others.

With only three hours until the next heat, Carl suddenly has an idea. Realizing that Li Na, as a Changbi, cannot worship a deity, Carl devises a plan to use her as a "weapon" directed effectively. He immediately sends multiple messages, including one to Bodi, asking if he's committed to the next race, because "Zhang is going to need a jockey. Li Na is leaving the dungeon."

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
This chapter delves into the raw, devastating psychological toll the Dungeon takes on its participants, particularly through the heartbreaking portrayal of Zhang's despair. His quiet, profound grief and suicidal ideation are a stark reminder that even powerful crawlers are fundamentally human, vulnerable to loss and trauma. The revelation of Li Na's extreme actions—the eye-swapping—solidifies her transformation into a truly broken and terrifying figure, challenging the lines between a 'monster' created by circumstance and a strategic asset.

Carl's pragmatism shines through, seeing Na not just as a lost friend, but as a "weapon" that can be pointed in the correct direction. This cold, calculating aspect of Carl's personality is often unsettling, yet it's consistently framed as necessary for survival in the Dungeon. Donut's role as his moral compass is crucial here, reminding him of Na's humanity and Zhang's worth, providing a counterpoint to his ruthless efficiency. The escalating stakes, the secret "prize," and Carl's cryptic explanation to Donut deepen the mystery and tension, leaving the reader eager and slightly apprehensive about what Carl has truly planned, especially for Prime Minister Victory. The chapter ends with a classic Carl move: a sudden, decisive, and somewhat shocking strategic maneuver to drastically alter the playing field regarding Li Na, demonstrating his genius for leveraging dungeon mechanics and unconventional solutions.
