# Chapter 298: Prologue — Christmas Morning

## Summary
- **Primal Engines and Seeded Worlds**: In a frozen-time message, the Residual [[paulie]] (Goff) explains that living beings on pre-seeded worlds have internal "primal engines" (miniature systems) in their brains. When they die, the showrunners harvest these elements to feed the comatose center system, expanding its zone for profit.
- **Failsafe Upgrade**: Goff instructs Carl to drink his coffee, which contains an illegal, unregistered neural interface upgrade.
- **The Star Failsafe**: This upgrade gives Carl access to the AI containment "fuse." If the fuse is tripped by meeting five or six criteria, the system star goes nova, destroying the AI and everyone in the system.
- **AI's Death Wish**: Goff reveals that the mantid-made AI is in pain, wants to die, and is allowing this upgrade.
- **Compassion Warning**: Goff notes that his human host Paulie's compassion has "cross-contaminated" him. He urges Carl not to use the failsafe unless there is no other choice.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The revelation of the "primal engines" explains the true, horrifying resource-extraction purpose of the crawl. The introduction of the star failsafe changes Carl's role from a participant seeking survival to a potential system-destroyer. The thematic focus on "cross-contamination" and empathy highlights the tragic nature of the Residuals' possession.
