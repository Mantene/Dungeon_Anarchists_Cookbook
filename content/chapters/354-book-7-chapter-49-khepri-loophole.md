# Chapter 354: Chapter 49 — Khepri Loophole

## Summary
[[carl|Carl]] explains the game's deity mechanics: when someone worships a god, a link is formed in the form of an invisible light tendril. A non-corporeal fairy mob called a Bijou drags this tendril from the god to the strongest temple on the floor, which on Floor 9 is inside [[club-vanquisher]]. Certain classes can see these Bijou fairies; [[edgar|Edgar]] used his level 15 *Sense Movement* skill to detect their upward momentum, confirming someone had just initiated the escape ritual.

The escape method utilizes the god [[khepri|Khepri]], a god of light whose worshipers must swallow prospective followers (who have been transformed into beetles). The female guard who pledged to Khepri swallowed Warlord [[princess-vinata|Princess Vinata]] and her remaining court, then cast *Welcome Home* and *Travel the Path* (which turn the caster to light and follow the tendril) to teleport directly to the Temple of Khepri in Club Vanquisher. There, the guard will give birth to the beetles, restoring them to their naga forms.

Since [[theia|Theia]] (Vinata's usual escape route) is sponsored this season to protect Carl/[[samantha|Samantha]], Vinata was forced to use Khepri. Carl's team (Carl, [[katia-grim|Katia]], [[prepotente|Prepotente]], and Samantha) prepares to breach the Club Vanquisher entrance outside Larracos, locate Khepri's temple, and assassinate Vinata and her cousins before the one-hour lock expires and they can escape.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The details of the Khepri beetle-swallowing ritual highlight the bizarre and grotesque logic of the dungeon's high-level mechanics. The fact that the crawlers must rely on loopholes and technical analysis to intercept their targets shows that Faction Wars is as much a game of system exploitation as it is of martial strength. By utilizing the yam potions, the Cookbook data, and Edgar's skills, Carl is turning the system's own exploits against its developers.
