# Book 4 Chapter 22: Flushed into the Ocean

## Summary
Carl defeats the Sand Ooze, but the Sandcastle collapses, forcing a risky drainage release that flushes Carl and Donut into the deep, hostile waters of the Water Quadrant.
- **The Ceiling Walk**: Carl uses his *Sticky Feet* toe-ring ability to walk upside down along the ceiling to reach the Sand Ooze's core, which is hidden under the recliner. To distract the sound-hunting ooze, Katia throws a rigged banger sphere playing "Jump Around" down the stairs.
- **Burning the Core**: Carl applies a hot-burning *burn gel* (made from moonshine) to the recliner and ignites it. The ooze thrash and is defeated as the flames consume its core.
- **The Collapse**: The heat of the gel causes the glass ceiling to splinter, and the entire Sandcastle collapses. Carl and Donut are trapped in the basement, shored up only by a massive chunk of dwarven metal.
- **Ghazi's Death**: On the outside, Katia kills the frantic Ghazi (who is obsessed with saving his love doll). His body turns to sand. Katia loots a note detailing how to operate the *Mysterious Winding Box*.
- **The Flush**: With oxygen levels dropping and collapse imminent, Carl tells Gwen to have Tran open the drainage valve. A massive torrent of water from the Necropolis blasts through the Sandcastle, washing it away and flushing Carl and Donut into the Water Quadrant's ocean (sinking to 150 meters).
- **Underwater Recovery**: Donut is knocked unconscious and pierced by a shard, but Carl uses a water-breathing scroll and healing scrolls to revive her. As they swim to the surface, they detect a large, long aquatic monster circling below them, feeding on falling corpses. Donut wakes up and panics at being submerged in water.

## Characters Introduced
- None (Ghazi is killed).

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The juxtaposition of a high-tech/magic dungeon with Carl using a basic electrical grounding method and a House of Pain mixtape as a distraction is quintessential *Dungeon Crawler Carl*. Donut's visceral panic upon waking up underwater reinforces her core nature as a cat, reminding the reader of the immense psychological strain she endures.
