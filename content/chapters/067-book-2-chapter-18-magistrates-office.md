# Book 2 Chapter 18: The Magistrate's Office

## Summary
[[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] use Donut's *Puddle Jumper* (now Level 5, allowing vertical elevation jumps) to scale the town hall's third-floor landing. They bypass shopkeepers and assistants catering only to skyfowl. 

In the reception lobby, they meet [[miss-quill]], the elderly skyfowl assistant to the Magistrate who has a massive collection of Beanie Baby-style toys. Carl demands to see the Magistrate, but Quill refuses. 

Carl forces the issue by pulling the dead body of the prostitute from his inventory and dumping it on her desk, knock her Beanie Babies to the floor. The guards approach, but do nothing because Carl shows the Gate Pass. Miss Quill panics and leaves the room to find a cleaning towel. 

Carl inspects the door to the Magistrate's chambers, discovering it is magically locked. They leave, scaling down the building with a rope. 

Outside, they head west to grind. In the ruins of a gym pool, the sky suddenly turns pitch black as they are ambushed by a city elf casting anti-magic darkness and a swarm of Level 12 **Shambling Berserkers** (undead slug-like giants made of sewn-together body parts). Carl throws fused hob-lobbers using his xistera. He falls into the empty pool and lands on a bloated boss corpse (The Divider), which explodes. 

Donut uses her reanimated brain boilers and Mongo to clear the remaining mobs, raising Carl's health with a scroll. Carl reaches Level 19, Donut reaches Level 17, and Mongo reaches Level 12.

## Characters Introduced
- [[miss-quill]] — Elderly skyfowl assistant.
- [[burgundy]] — Young human/fathom dwarf assistant with Siberian-husky-colored eyes.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[over-city-mechanics]]**: Introduction of Shambling Berserkers and the day-to-night transitions of the Over City.
- **[[survival]]**: Tactical combat using Puddle Jumper, xistera, and reanimated minions.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's action of placing a dead hooker on Miss Quill's desk is a classic, crude brawler move that utilizes the legal boundaries of the system (the Gate Pass) to outsmart the NPC's programming. The subsequent dark ambush shows that the mastermind behind the krasue is actively attempting to assassinate them.
