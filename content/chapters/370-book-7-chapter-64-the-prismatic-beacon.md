# Chapter 370: The Prismatic Beacon

## Summary
[[boomer]] orders [[samantha]] to retreat. Samantha explains that **Yarilo** is chasing her because he blames her for getting him thrown into the Nothing. 

A mile away in the forest, a massive, prismatic funnel of light shoots straight into the sky, shaking and strobing. The system notes that the **Memorial Crystal: Apito** has shown itself, and issues a quest update for Carl to obtain it. Carl recognizes that the light is coming from [[osvaldo]], a neutral crawler who is hiding in the woods. Carl messages Osvaldo to put the crystal away, but Osvaldo replies in Portuguese that he is trying. The system warns Carl that messaging unaffiliated crawlers may be construed as illegal collaboration.

The prismatic beacon ruins their stealth, and the Madness observers deshroud Carl's team. [[prepotente]] reports that while the main army ([[bodi]]'s 103rd battalion) has successfully drawn the Madness defenders away from the castle, the other half of the Madness forces are now converging directly on Carl's position.

Carl is forced to pivot from his stealth infiltration plan. He decides to summon the remaining 18 members of his assault team and calls in [[jurgen]]'s 105th Scream Warriors to teleport to their location. Because they lack defensive mages, Carl orders Bodi's 103rd to retreat early. Carl requests Prepotente to withdraw from the 103rd's flank and teleport to the woods to shield Carl's team from the impending psionic onslaught. Donut is instructed to wait for Bodi's retreat, then detonate the field.

From Area 52, [[the-sledge]] launches their remaining missiles. The missiles pound the Madness castle, temporarily dropping its shields, but a massive force of Madness soldiers riding dune buggies is already speeding toward Carl's position in the woods.

## Characters
* [[carl]]: Abandons his stealth plan, redirects Prepotente, orders the 103rd's retreat, and prepares for a major confrontation.
* [[elle-mcgibbons]]: Observes the prismatic light and calls in shrapnel missile support.
* [[osvaldo]]: A neutral crawler who accidentally activates Apito's memorial crystal, producing the massive beacon.
* [[prepotente]]: Reports the Madness movements and agrees to teleport to Carl's side to provide mental shields.
* [[bodi]]: The leader of the 103rd battalion, who coordinates the retreat under Carl's command.
* [[jurgen]]: Prepares the 105th Scream Warriors to teleport in as reinforcements.
* [[princess-donut]]: Stands by to detonate the retreat field from the Nest.

## Themes & Plot Threads
* **The Memorial Crystal**: The activation of Apito's crystal connects back to the events of the sixth floor and represents a major sub-plot that Carl and Prepotente must resolve.
* **Tactical Pivots**: The failure of the stealth incursion showcases how quickly battlefield conditions can shift, forcing Carl to commit his reserve forces earlier than planned.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The system's strict warnings about "illegal collaboration" with Osvaldo illustrate the tightening administrative control over the crawl. The showrunners are actively trying to prevent the crawlers from organizing, forcing them to treat neutral crawlers as enemies.
