# Chapter 361: Justice Light Special — Deal with Juice Box

## Summary
[[justice-light]] oversees the final preparations of the trap tunnels. He orders [[sneaky-tyler-c]] and the other sluggalos to retreat to the surface to join the main army, promising they can return for a final defense if things go wrong.

Justice detains [[sick-ass-worgo]], a level 53 sluggalette sapper. He reveals he knows she is an imposter—a Class A/B changeling. The changeling sheds her slug form, shifting into a blank humanoid named [[jessica]], and projects the head of [[juice-box]] to speak. Jessica explains she is communicating on Juice Box's behalf, and that she has lived over 400 different lives. 

Justice convinces Juice Box to call off a subterranean digger that was poised to breach the tunnels. He shares a deeply personal memory: early in his crawl, he killed a lizard-like merchant in anger, causing many crawlers to die of undersupply. To atone, he became a merchant, running a trap store. Later, as Castle Guards, he and his friend [[dante]] were ordered to execute innocent ice elves. Dante refused and was instantly erased by the AI. Justice also refused once, losing his wing, but ultimately complied out of fear—a cowardice that haunts him.

Jessica briefly shifts into an ice elf, confirming she lived among them in a past life and knows their gentle nature. Justice explains that Dante proved there is no point of no return. 

Justice reveals he has deduced Juice Box's true plan: Team Retribution has been infiltrating various headquarters to touch as many gods as possible to gain their forms. Justice offers a deal: he will teach them how to touch gods and survive if they cooperate with "Operation Ruin" and stop their sabotage. Juice Box agrees, and the digger engine withdraws.

The chapter ends with Justice Light's character card, revealing he took a deal at the beginning of the 11th floor, worked as a merchant, and is the author of the eighth edition of the [[the-anarchist-cookbook]].

## Characters
* [[justice-light]]: A skyfowl Trap Master and author of the eighth edition of the Cookbook. He confronts the changeling, shares his tragic past, and makes a deal with Juice Box.
* [[sneaky-tyler-c]]: The lead sluggalo sapper who coordinates tripwire placements.
* [[sick-ass-worgo]]: A level 53 sluggalette sapper whose form is mimicked by the changeling Jessica.
* [[jessica]]: A powerful changeling who mimics Worgo and acts as a conduit for Juice Box. She has ice elves in her library.
* [[juice-box]]: Communicates with Justice Light via Jessica's projection, ultimately agreeing to the deal.
* [[dante]]: Justice's former crocodilian friend who was erased by the AI for refusing to massacre ice elves.
* [[lickity-spit]]: A sluggalo who was killed by a proximity scythe trap.
* [[worgo]]: The real sluggalette sapper who was mimicked by Jessica.

## Themes & Plot Threads
* **The Cost of Survival**: Justice Light's trauma stems from committing a massacre of innocent ice elves out of fear, contrasted against Dante's noble death.
* **The Changeling Agenda**: Juice Box's faction is systematically copying the forms of deities to build an arsenal of god-tier transformations.
* **The Cookbook Lineage**: Justice Light is identified as the author of the eighth edition of the [[the-anarchist-cookbook]].

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Justice Light's backstory provides a haunting explanation for his missing wing. His confession shows that the system intentionally breaks the moral codes of its inhabitants. By aiding the crawlers and partnering with Juice Box, he seeks redemption for the cowardice of his past.
