# Chapter 395: Chapter 87 — Scolopendra Awakens

## Summary
Samantha is returned via the xistera after stalling Emberus in the volcano. The floor is melting, and the team prepares to leave. Donut takes Katia's Annie card, and Elle takes Katia's funny Carl bra. Donut feels guilty about the cat-poop arrow. Bautista warns Carl that his Kimaris summon figure lasts the remainder of the floor. Samantha recognizes [[kimaris]] as her friend who gave her the pregnancy potion. 

Carl, Donut, and the other survivors enter the stairwells to the 10th floor. 

Justice Light remains behind on the floor. Rather than fleeing with Juice Box, he stays to activate his trans-dimensional trap. He snaps a twine, dropping a bijou fairy trap (and its connection threads) and a demon trap (with evicted demons) into the permanent portal to the Nothing (made from the broken box of the Gate of the Feral Gods). 

The Nothing breaks! The Nothing drains into: The Halls of the Ascendency, Sheol, and Scolopendra's Lair. Scolopendra awakens! 

A planetwide system message advises guests at the Scolopendra Club to evacuate immediately, warning that they must proceed through the 17th floor ("The Backstage Death Maze") to the 16th floor ("Agony of Mirrors") to find a single, non-magical Motel 6 mirror in Boise, Idaho. Justice Light dies as fire engulfs the floor.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **Breaking the Nothing**: Justice Light's legendary trap breaks the spatial barrier, flooding the Ascendency, Sheol, and Scolopendra's Lair with the Nothing.
- **Scolopendra's Awakening**: The primal world boss Scolopendra awakens, throwing the entire Syndicate and crawl into emergency panic.
- **The Backstage Death Maze**: The setup for future floors, forcing the Syndicate elite to proceed through Floor 17 to evacuate.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Justice Light's final act is a game-changing moment of revenge. By dropping the fairy and demon traps into the Nothing portal, he breaks the spatial veil, dragging the Nothing directly into the Halls of the Ascendency and Sheol, and awakening Scolopendra. His death is peaceful, spent envisioning the beautiful collapse of the system's geometry. The planetwide system message mocking the corporate elites trapped at the Scolopendra Club is a satisfying, dark payoff.
