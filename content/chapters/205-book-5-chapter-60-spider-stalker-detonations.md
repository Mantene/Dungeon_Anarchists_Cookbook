# Book 5 Chapter 60: Spider Stalker Detonations

## Summary
- **Creating Spider Stalkers**: Carl uses his Level 5 Automaton table to build "Spider Stalkers" (exploding robot rot stickers). He programs two of them to target [[samantha]] and explode five seconds after target acquisition.
- **Planning the Ambush**: The crawlers coordinate via chat to attack a group of hunters gathered at a non-saferoom pub (a Cold Stone Creamery) near the center of town. Donut begs them not to blow up the ice cream shop.
- **Initiating the Attack**: Carl casts *Tripper* and *Ping* to detonate the traps and send the exploding automatons in.
