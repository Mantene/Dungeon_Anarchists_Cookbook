# Book 1 Chapter 39: Platinum Scratchers

## Summary
The recap episode premieres, broadcasting Jack's offense and Yolanda's death. [[princess-donut]] makes an inappropriate comment about [[carl]]'s anatomy, drawing a death glare from [[imani-c]]. Donut is groomed by the Bopca Protector Qwist using the **Enchanted Fur Brush of the Ecclesiastic**, raising her Constitution to 4.

Carl and Donut open their rewards:
- **What goes up...** Achievement (surviving *Reverse Gravity*).
- **Like a Moth to the Flame** Achievement (damaging a mob 75+ levels higher than them).
  - Donut's reward: **Dungeon Gold Rush** (6-spot lottery scratcher ticket with positive/negative attack effects, 1-hour cooldown).
  - Carl's reward: **Fireball or Custard?** (5-spot lottery scratcher with a 50/50 chance to cast a Level 15 Fireball or summon healing vanilla/strawberry custard, 30-minute cooldown).

The recap show highlights:
- **Le Mouvement**: Their ammunition has run out. They encountered a giant Jell-O blob boss in a sewer that slaughtered the entire group except for one crying survivor.
- [[lucia-mar]]: Wields a magical shield and has 4 player-killer skulls.
- [[hekla]]: Leader of "Brynhild's Daughters," wearing a Norse breastplate.

The system announcement alerts crawlers to new rules:
1. Bathroom elementals are removed starting on Floor 3.
2. Satan's Lil' Hedgehogs are removed from Floor 2 (armor-piercing quills were deemed too overpowered).
3. **Safe room ejection**: All safe rooms will close exactly **one hour prior to floor collapse**, ejecting crawlers.

Outside, the Rage Elemental temporarily leaves to devour a swarm of grubs before returning. Carl notices 15 **Grub - Pupa Stage** dots on the map with a 10-hour timer. Recalling Rory's warning that monsters dissolve if they try to descend the stairs, Carl formulates a plan. He dumps his inventory weights and tells [[brandon-an]] they are going to build a launcher.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival]]**: The new safe room ejection rule prevents crawlers from hiding until the collapse.
- **[[televised-exploitation]]**: The recap episode turn the crawlers' desperate survival and tragic deaths into highly edited television for the intergalactic audience.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The addition of the safe room ejection rule is a direct response by the system to prevent crawlers from waiting out the collapse, forcing them to face the stairs early. Carl's realization about the stairwell dissolution rule—and his decision to build a launcher—marks a pivot from direct combat to mechanical trickery to survive a Level 93 opponent.
