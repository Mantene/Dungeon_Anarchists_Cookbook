# Book 2 Chapter 13: Desperado Rewards

## Summary
[[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] settle into [[mordecai]]'s guild room to rest and open their Floor 3 boss and quest rewards. Donut opens her Platinum Quest Box, receiving 5,000 gold, a Level 3 *Acute Ears* skill potion, and a pair of **Enchanted Fang Caps of the Expectorating Tizheruk** (which she gives to [[mongo]], increasing his bite damage by 50% with a chance of poison and paralysis). 

Carl receives multiple dynamites, a Lucky Bastard casino chip for the Desperado Club (worth 100,000 gold), and a **Scroll of Upgrade**. Carl uses the scroll, which randomly upgrades his *Enchanted BigBoi Boxers*. The boxers gain a massive stats boost (+5 Dex, +5 Int, +3 Con) and **The Freeballing Benefit** (grants +100% damage to all attacks originating below the waist, and below-the-waist skills train 20% faster, but limits his crotch area to a single layer of clothing—effectively banning pants).
