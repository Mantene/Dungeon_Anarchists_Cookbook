# Book 5 Chapter 75: Co-Warlord of the Princess Posse

## Summary
- **Children Freed**: [[zev]] explains that children and pregnant crawlers are forbidden, meaning [[bonnie]], the bear cubs, and Kiwi's unborn offspring have been freed to the surface.

Carl immediately adds a sponsor-voted action item: \"Remove Safety Protections For Faction Sponsors.\"\n- **Blood Sultanate Succession**: Donut is revealed to be in the succession line for Team #4 (the Blood Sultanate).\n- **Quest Update**: Carl receives the quest *Find Out Who Killed My Son* to retrieve the [[memorial-crystal-apito]] and investigate Geyrun's murder.
