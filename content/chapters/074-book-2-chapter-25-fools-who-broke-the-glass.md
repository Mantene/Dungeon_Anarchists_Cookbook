# Book 2 Chapter 25: The Fools Who Broke the Glass

## Summary
The Soul Crystal silently explodes, but the blast is successfully caged inside the Sheol Glass Reaper Case. The item transforms in Carl's inventory into **Carl's Doomsday Scenario** (an unstable custom explosive powerful enough to "rattle the teeth of a god," with an imminent explosion status). 

Remex convulsively triggers the first of four precursor wild magic bursts, which removes all magical properties from equipped gear. The floor of the depot collapses, deactivating the swordsmen guards. 

Carl, Donut, and [[katia-grim]] run east at full speed toward the stairwell (3 km away), urging other crawlers to unequip all magical items. [[conrad-e]] (Level 12 Ranger) fails to unequip his arrows, and they explode in his quiver, killing him. 

Donut's tiara (**Enchanted Crown of the Sepsis Whore**) crumbles into dust. The fourth pulse triggers, forcing the first two items in everyone's hotlists to activate, causing Katia to trigger her *Rush* and crash through a wall like the Kool-Aid Man. Carl and Donut are poisoned by potion sickness. 

They reach the stairwell with three minutes left. [[daniel-bautista-2]] and a group of crawlers descend to the safety of Floor 4. At 15 seconds, Katia presses the dynamite detonator to trigger the soul release, and they open the door to Floor 4.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The caging of the Soul Crystal gives Carl a literal "nuclear option" (Carl's Doomsday Scenario) in his inventory, a weapon of massive destruction that cannot be stabilized. The sprint to the stairs highlights the chaotic nature of wild magic, turning the crawlers' own inventory items against them.
