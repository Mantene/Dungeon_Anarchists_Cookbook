# Chapter 489: Chapter 88 — Parade Float Staging

## Summary
[[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] arrive at the Parade Float Staging Area on the eleventh floor. Only 23 crawlers remain. Carl is ranked 2nd and Donut is ranked 3rd, with the 1st position hidden. Carl reflects on his brief meeting with Quasar, who warned him that the exit deal at the start of the twelfth floor is untrustworthy. Donut reveals her manager tried to force her to take a deal, but something scared the manager away. 

[[mordecai]] checks in via chat, revealing he has been transformed into a non-corporeal "animistic spirit" that occupies physical objects. Donut is offered class upgrades and chooses the "Elite Gurkha Warrior" class (a warrior-melee/tank hybrid). 

Chaco (grand master of the parade) announces the floor rules: crawlers have ninety minutes to build themed floats for a 3 km parade route, ending in an arena where they must fight tenth-floor monsters and a floor boss. Prepotente and Jurgen's garage combines with Carl and Donut's. Their assigned theme is "Vengeance". 

[[zev]] and Rosetta contact Carl. Rosetta explains that Odette's husband, Harry Tisch (a Crest trillionaire who lives in the Skull Empire), is buying up stocks from the survivors, and her heist only enriched other elites. Prepotente tries to discuss combat strategy, but Donut is focused on decorating the floats with dahlia flowers.
