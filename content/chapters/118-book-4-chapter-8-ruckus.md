# Book 4 Chapter 8: Ruckus

## Summary
Carl opens his benefactor boxes and prepares to test his new anti-aircraft missiles against a flying boss.
- **Valtay Enhancement**: Carl pops the **Valtay Corporation Neural Enhancer #275 (Variant 35.j)**. It upgrades his interface to display **current elevation and airspeed**. 
- **Elevation Details**: Carl's elevation displays reveal they are currently at **8,932 meters Gravitational Zone Sea Level** (9 kilometers high, approximately the height of Mount Everest). This highlights the massive scale of the Necropolis of Anser.
- **Pacifist Box**: Carl opens the Silver Benefactor Box from his new pacifist sponsor, receiving a **Toraline Root Vegetable** (a very rare alchemy material that grows in lava-covered dirt, but is described by the system as tasting like dogshit).
- **Tomb Raiders Update**: Katia returns from the Desperado Club. She successfully transferred a general map of the upper half of the catacombs to Morris Sp and Bobby D.J. The Subterranean crawlers are highly stressed; they are forcing fodder crawlers to walk ahead of Bobby to set off traps, one of which recently killed a crawler by filling his sinuses with flesh-eating beetles.
- **Borough Boss Ruckus**: Carl learns about **Ruckus** (a Level ?? steampunk cyborg giant buzz-ard Borough Boss). Ruckus roosts near Hump Town every night and has been harassing Gwendolyn's group in the Land Quadrant, destroying two of their siege towers. 
- **Missile Test Plan**: Carl plans to test his custom-built sapper's missiles by shooting at Ruckus from a safe distance of one mile.
