# Book 4 Chapter 32: Divine Intervention Benefits

## Summary
Carl reviews his new religion under Emberus, loots Quan's rings, and initiates the massive crawler extractions using the Gate of the Feral Gods.
- **Worshipping Emberus Rules**: Carl reviews the rules of his new faith: don't harm fellow worshippers; offer blood and 5% of looted gold to a sun shrine once a day; no Hellik blessed gear; complete church quests. Benefits include map tracking of all Emberus/Hellik temples and worshippers, +100% XP for killing Hellik-worshippers, burn immunity, 10% burn chance on physical attacks, and free access to Club Vanquisher.
- **Patron Quests**: Emberus issues two quests to Carl:
  - *Find Out Who Killed My Son (Geyrun)*: Must investigate before Floor 18.
  - *Kill Hellik*: Slay his twin brother Hellik (God of Sun and Life) before Floor 12, rewarding a Celestial God Box.
- **Looting Quan's Rings**: Carl examines Quan's arm and loots two rings:
  - *Rockard's Ring of Sniping*: Imbues *Ripe* (marks targets below 50% HP) and *Loaded* (marks creatures with magical gear).
  - *Gold Band*: Engraved "For Daddy." Carl leaves it on the finger.
- **Loot Box Opening**: Carl opens his *Savage Box*, receiving fuzzy novelty *Enchanted Handcuffs* (requires 200 strength to break, locked/unlocked magically). His *Platinum Quest Box* gives the *Ping* spell (marks all non-crawlers/non-red-mobs in a 1km radius, including elites/hunters).
- **Crawler Extractions**: Carl, Katia, and Imani coordinate the extraction of 500+ crawlers from 8 bubbles using the Gate of the Feral Gods. The gatekeepers dial coordinates, let their groups through to Hump Town, and hand the gate to the next bubble, leaving a feral god behind in their popped bubbles.
