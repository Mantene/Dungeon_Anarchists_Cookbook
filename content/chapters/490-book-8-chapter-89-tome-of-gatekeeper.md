# Chapter 490: Chapter 89 — Tome of Gatekeeper

## Summary
In the safe room, the screens display a scrolling map of the Iron Tangle, zoom into the Pacific Ocean near the Juan de Fuca Ridge on Earth, and then display a yellow-brick road in a grassy plain with a countdown timer. 

[[princess-donut]] retrieves her mail, finding a wide-brimmed brown hat and a spell book. The book is an Advance Reader Copy of "Tome of Gatekeeper," a spell that temporarily strips targets—including deities and "OI entities"—of their class skills and worshipper spells, though it cannot be used until the twelfth floor. 

Carl asks [[mordecai]] (who is currently occupying the kitchen table) what "OI" means, but Mordecai does not know. Donut opens a Legendary Box, obtaining the "Legendary Steed" upgrade for Mongo's saddle. Carl uses a sewing kit to attach the "Lucky Shot" patch to his jacket, which will deflect a fatal blow to a party member once per floor. Jurgen notifies the team that the flowers are ready, and Donut calls everyone to begin decorating the float.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The "Lucky Shot" patch represents a massive gamble: Carl is willing to deflect a fatal strike to a party member, which fits his cold, survivalist math but will cause massive emotional distress if it hits Donut. The "Tome of Gatekeeper" spell will be crucial for the twelfth-floor Ascendency battles.
