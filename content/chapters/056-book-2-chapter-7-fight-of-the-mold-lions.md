# Book 2 Chapter 7: Fight of the Mold Lions

## Summary
[[carl]], [[princess-donut]], and [[tsarina-signet]] are ambushed by six Level 15 Mold Lions. Signet asks Carl to protect her while she prepares her summoning. Carl executes a tactical plan: he casts *Protective Shell* and has Donut cast *Confusing Fog*. One lion is trapped between the shell and the wall; Carl pummels it, and Mongo rips its belly, allowing Carl to pull its heart out.

Donut uses *Second Chance* to raise the dead lion as an undead minion, then casts *Clockwork Triplicate* on it. The zombie lions and clockwork clones clash with the remaining mobs, successfully defeating all six lions. Carl and Donut reach Level 14. 

Signet explains that her summon spells require a blood sacrifice, and she originally intended to sacrifice Carl. Since Carl survived without a scratch, she plans to use the blood of [[heather-the-bear]] instead. She schedules a meeting with Carl for the following night.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[over-city-mechanics]]**: Introduces the Mold Lions and the rules governing Signet's Blood Magic.
- **[[survival]]**: Carl and Donut coordinate their abilities (Protective Shell + Confusing Fog + Second Chance) to defeat high-level threats.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's realization that Signet was manipulating them to act as a blood sacrifice underscores the danger of trusting Elites. The fight shows Carl's visceral combat growth, culminating in him pulling a lion's heart out with his bare hands.
