# Book 1 Chapter 33: Plan Pied Piper

## Summary
[[princess-donut]] executes "Plan Pied Piper." She teleports via *Puddle Jumper* between the two Level 6 Laminak fairies, Caroline and Max. Because they are winged, Donut's *Talisman of the Slate Butterfly* turns them neutral. Donut charms the fairies and tricks them into leading all 40 Clurichaun workers into the adjacent foyer for a "break."

Once the Clurichauns crowd the room, [[carl]] throws two *Jugs O' Boom*, incinerating the entire group and leveling up to Level 11. Donut pounces and kills Caroline, but Max escapes to the ceiling, firing magic missiles that severely injure Carl (damage enhanced by his goblin pass). Carl mends his bones with a potion and uses his slingshot to clip Max's wing, allowing Donut to leap and crush her.

Carl loots the two Laminak Elite corpses, 50 gold, and MLM pamphlets. They loot the filling room, collecting 80 empty jugs, 60 full moonshine jugs, an Alchemy workstation, and an Engineer's table. 

They decide to bypass the boss door (marked with a wooden "Live, Laugh, Love" sign and a "pox party" schedule). Carl figures out the **Rev-Up Immunity Smoothie** recipe using the bicycle blender: 1/3 moonshine, 1 Laminak corpse, blend until pink (yields 10 doses). 

Before they can leave, the boss door blasts open. Two 15-foot pink tentacles covered in screaming, human-lipped mouths lunge out, and steel bars drop down, locking them in. A system achievement pops up: **Wait, Bosses Can Leave Their Rooms?** (introducing the Level 10 Neighborhood Boss, [[krakaren]] Clone).

## Characters Introduced
- **[[krakaren]]**: Level 10 Neighborhood Boss clone, an anti-vax/MLM octopus monster.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival]]**: Discovering that bosses can leave their lairs represents a terrifying shift in dungeon rules. 
- **[[carls-class-selection]]**: Carl reaches Level 11, and his *Aiming* skill hits Level 4. Donut reaches Level 11. Carl loots indestructible workstations (Alchemy and Engineer's tables).

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's discomfort with using Donut's charm ability to deceive and slaughter the monsters shows his clinging to human morality. Hearing Caroline express a desperate wish to "move down to the third floor" reminds Carl that the mobs are tragic pawns. The sudden rule-breaking appearance of the boss outside its room highlights that the Syndicate prioritizes dramatic television over fairness.
