# Chapter 267: Spliced Trailer and Harley Looting

## Summary
- **Junkyard Looting**: Carl and Donut fight zombie animals on their way to the single-wide trailer. Carl recognizes his father's Harley-Davidson 1965 FLH Electra Glide Panhead and loots it (stored as a permanent transportation item).
- **The Spliced Summer**: Carl realizes the trailer yard is spliced in from a summer month, featuring children's bikes (with Spider Gwen stickers) and flower pots with alliums, daffodils, asters, and hyacinths.
- **Vending Machine**: They deposit the 9 tokens into the soda machine featuring a silhouette of Carl's father. A 42-minute countdown starts before the fight begins, and Carl enters alone.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The spliced-in summer trailer yard is a deliberate setup by the showrunners, capturing the exact environment of Carl's childhood home shortly before the collapse to maximize emotional impact.
