# Chapter 314: Flag Room and Suicide Bomber

## Summary

Carl, Donut, Tipid, and Florin approach the flag room. Carl examines the stronghold map and sees Elite Herman the Fleet, an ogre strongman and legionnaire, the third brother of Areson and Apollon from Grimaldi's circus. Through chat, Florin explains scouting mechanics—only 18 scouts can cross borders during the ceasefire, and they've already teleported out a Dream scout and a Prism scout. Florin mentions [[justice-light]], the one-winged eagle trap expert, has gone subterranean.

Upon entering the antechamber, they find Herman writhing in pain at his desk. He's been fighting against something—he's covered in explosive satchels, a suicide bomber being controlled by Sensation Entertainment (now owned by the Dream). As multiple satchels destabilize simultaneously, Donut fires a Magic Missile, Tipid turns to smoke, and Florin shields himself. [[baroness-victory]], the Princess Posse's adjutant, calmly steps out of the flag room and buries her battle-axe in Herman's skull, killing him before the bombs detonate. Carl quickly inventories the corpse and its explosives.

Victory declares it an unlawful assassination attempt by the Dream corporation and files a grievance. She argues with the AI itself, demanding accountability, and accepts its apology. The team enters the flag room, awed and slightly terrified by their adjutant.

## Themes & Plot Threads

- **Corporate Manipulation** — Sensation Entertainment uses its elite to assassinate Carl
- **Adjutant Power** — Victory demonstrates both combat ability and political savvy
- **Scouting Mechanics** — The 18-scout limit and tower-based detection system
- **AI Accountability** — Victory holds the AI to its own rules, a rare act of authority

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections

Baroness Victory immediately establishes herself as one of the most competent and fearsome characters in the series. Her casual lethality combined with bureaucratic precision is both terrifying and reassuring. Herman's death is tragic—he was fighting the control and apologizing to his brother. The corporate exploitation of NPCs reaches a new low with this assassination attempt.
