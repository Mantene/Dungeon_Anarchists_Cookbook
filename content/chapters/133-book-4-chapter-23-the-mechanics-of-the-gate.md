# Book 4 Chapter 23: The Mechanics of the Gate

## Summary
Carl and Donut escape the deep ocean, settle in a new town, and analyze the Mad Dune Mage's notes on how to open the Gate of the Feral Gods.
- **Escape from the Sharks**: Submerged in the Water Quadrant, Donut panics, but Carl grabs her and swims to safety. They encounter a Level 41 *Concierge Shark* eating dead Glaber corpses. Donut resurrects a dead beluga corpse (*Bubble Beluga*) and triplicates it to create exploding decoys, attracting two more Concierge Sharks into a feeding frenzy. Carl uses a salvaged kayak to paddle to shore.
- **Vomiting Water**: When their water-breathing scrolls run out, Carl and Donut vomit dark, magical water from their lungs. Donut even coughs up a flopping fish.
- **Pandinus Town**: They arrive in Pandinus, a town populated by post-apocalyptic, Mad Max-style half-human, half-scorpion centaurs called **Pazuzu**.
- **Loot and Achievements**:
  - Carl receives the **"Milquetoast"** achievement (winning a boss battle without killing the boss), yielding a *Silver Pacifist's Box* containing two *Potions of Bloodlust* (doubles strength and speed but causes severe disorientation/berserking).
  - Donut receives the **"I was in the pool!"** achievement, yielding a *Bronze I'm Wet Box* with 10 water-breathing scrolls, a *Belt of Buoyancy* (keeps her afloat), and a skill potion (+1 Dodge, raising it to 10). Unlocking Dodge level 10 grants her a permanent *Deflection* buff (halving the accuracy of physical/magical projectiles shot at her).
- **The Gate Instructions**: Carl, Donut, and Katia study Ghazi's note. The Gate of the Feral Gods operates as follows:
  - Setting a destination time on one watch mirrors it on the other. Both watches are placed in the winding box, which winds the second watch until it syncs with the current time (arming the box). Removing the watches opens a one-way, 20-minute portal.
  - However, the portal remains a two-way gate to the Nothing, allowing a feral god to escape.
  - If one watch is placed on each side of the portal and the winding box is placed inside the portal, the gate closes safely without letting any monsters escape, though the box is lost.
  - The destination times in the note correspond to locations around the city of Larracos on Floor 9.
  - Mordecai warns that crawlers cannot use the gate to hop floors, and warns them never to wind it. Carl, however, spots a specific scenario in the drawings that catches his attention.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's Dodge level 10 unlock (*Deflection*) is a major defensive upgrade, though Carl notes it will divert missile fire toward him or Mongo. The mechanics of the Gate of the Feral Gods present a classic double-edged sword: a portal to escape the immediate level, but one that threatens to release an apocalyptic feral god. Carl's focus on the drawings suggests he is already scheming a way to exploit this risk.
