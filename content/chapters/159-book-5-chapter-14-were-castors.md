# Book 5 Chapter 14: Were-Castors

## Summary
- **Execution of Bravvo**: Carl executes Yenk hunter [[bravvo]], obtaining 1 point to Dexterity and his loot: Levitation potions, slow-refill mana potions, and a magical longsword.
- **Nadine the Caterpillar**: The were-castors ride on the back of [[nadine]] (Level 50 Bearded Sage Caterpillar), who was a Chee schoolteacher and actress transformed by the High Elves.
- **Wife/Mother & Circus Fates**: Signet explains that her father, King [[king-finian]] of the High Elves, is dead, and his pure-blooded daughter [[imogen]] now rules. Signet's mother was killed in a coup, and she was exiled. Grimaldi's circus performers have crumbled to freedom, but [[areson-the-wise]]'s brother [[herman-the-fleet]] is in Larracos.
- **Were-Castor History**: The six were-castors are **were-beavers** (were-castors). They were school children under Nadine's care when Imogen transformed them and stole their land.
- **The Rebellion Goal**: Signet explains she is gathering an elite force to seek revenge against the two cancers of the land: the Confederacy and the High Elves.

## Characters Introduced
- [[nadine]] — Level 50 Bearded Sage Caterpillar, former Chee schoolteacher/actress.
- [[imogen]] — High-Elf Queen and Sorcerer General, Signet's half-sister (mentioned).
- [[king-finian]] — Deceased High-Elf King, father of Signet and Imogen (mentioned).
- [[herman-the-fleet]] — Ogre Elite, brother of Areson and Apollon (mentioned).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[were-castors]]**: The were-beavers' origin and history as the transformed Chee carpenter race.
- **[[vengeance-of-the-daughter]]**: Signet's quest line is detailed as she seeks to destroy the High Elves and the Confederacy.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The revelation that the were-kin are "were-castors" (were-beavers) is a humorous but tragic detail. Imogen's transfiguration curse literally mocked the Chee's peaceful history as carpenters. Signet's motivations are purely personal and political—she wants to destroy the Confederacy that murdered her mother and the High Elves who exiled her, using the were-beavers as her core strike force.
