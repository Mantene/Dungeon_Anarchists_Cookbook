# Chapter 337: Parlay with King Rust

## Summary

Carl rides out on Simoom the rhino to meet King Rust for a registered parlay, with Donut hiding in the howdah. The parlay bubble provides protection for both sides. King Rust appears old, tired, and slumped—he's crying.

Rust reveals devastating news: his daughter Princess Formidable has announced the Skull Empire has backdoors in all military hardware they manufacture for others, and she's heading to a satellite in Venus orbit to trigger a failsafe that will blow up the entire solar system. This is an act of betrayal against the entire galaxy. The failsafe satellite was deliberately placed outside the AI's control zone.

Carl maintains composure, knowing the failsafe won't work because he already deactivated it via his illegal implant. Rust proposes a ceasefire—he wants Carl to plead with Formidable to stop, in exchange for the armies withdrawing. Carl negotiates the deal while secretly messaging Donut that the failsafe won't fire.

Just as the parlay concludes and Rust is walking away, Rosetta erupts from the ground wearing an invisibility cloak, whips King Rust's head off, and immediately begins transferring money using his credit chit device. The decapitated body falls, and after five seconds of stunned silence, the tens of thousands of enemy troops roar and charge.

## Themes & Plot Threads

- **Carl's secret** — The implant revelation: Carl deactivated the failsafe himself, meaning the solar system is safe
- **Rosetta's vengeance** — King Rust killed a friend of hers many cycles ago; this was premeditated revenge plus financial theft
- **Corporate warfare** — The Skull Empire's backdoors in military hardware mirror real-world supply chain security concerns
- **The AI's expanding control** — Its sphere has grown to capture Uranus and all inner planets

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections

This is one of the most consequential chapters in the series. Carl's reveal that he deactivated the failsafe—"I was the one who had turned it off"—is enormous, connecting back to his encounter with the residual. Rosetta's assassination is shocking in its timing and audacity, turning a moment of potential peace into total war. Her priorities (decapitate → steal money → cry) tell a complete story about who she is.
