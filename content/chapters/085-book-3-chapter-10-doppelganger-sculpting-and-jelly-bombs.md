# Book 3 Chapter 10: Doppelganger Sculpting and Jelly Bombs

## Summary
Katia Grim uses her new level 3 Makeup Table to sculpt herself to look 65% like Hekla, unlocking the *Walk in Their Shoes* ability. Donut reaches Level 30, and her Charisma hits 100, gaining the *Love Vampire* ability. Carl trains Powerful Strike to Level 9. Donut trains Dodge to 7. 

In the crafting studio, Carl deconstructs hob-lobbers using the Sapper table's Demolitions Workshop to study explosives yields. Donut practices riding Mongo, eventually sticking to the saddle, though Mongo is miserable and bucking.

The party travels to the Ochre Line platform to execute a train derailment. Carl tests the tracks with fuses and a clockwork duplicate of Mongo, confirming the third rail is electrified. Carl constructs two custom **Jelly Bombs** (metal tubes stuffed with hob-lobbers, contact plungers, and a stick of dynamite) and lowers them onto the tracks using a fishing pole. 

Carl receives a message from Elle: they explored a "five-station" containing three neighborhood bosses and a borough boss named Krakaren. The train rumbles, signaling the approach of their target train.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's *Love Vampire* skill—enabling her to bind an enemy's heart to hers so they absorb her damage—is a terrifyingly dark ability for a cat, illustrating the escalation of magic power on the deeper floors.
