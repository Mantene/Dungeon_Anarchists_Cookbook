# Book 4 Chapter 14: The Infiltrator in the Rock

## Summary
A tense standoff reveals that Chris Andrews is possessed by a deadly parasite controlled by Carl's nemesis, Maggie My.
- **The Suspicious Reunion**: Chris Andrews II describes escaping the Water Quadrant's flooding submarine *Akula* through a missile tube. However, he speaks coldly, claims that using basic interface features (like chat or inventory) damages his health, and expresses a desire to die in the dungeon.
- **The Reveal**: Carl realizes "Chris" is reciting words that match his enemy's motivations. Before she can act, Katia fires a *Petrify Rock Class* bolt (looted from an emergency box sent by Princess Formidable), freezing him. Carl confiscates the sphere in his hand.
- **The Celestial Grenade**: The sphere is a **Celestial Grenade** designed to summon a god. Chris's double sponsors, Prince Maestro and Crown Prince Stalwart of the Skull Empire, had provided it so Maggie could summon **Algos**, the god of pain, to wipe out the town and make Carl suffer.
- **The Infiltrator Parasite**: Mordecai explains that Maggie chose the **Infiltrator** (Scree) race, a brain parasite that takes complete control of a living host's body. The host (Chris) is locked inside his own mind, forced to watch helplessly. Maggie previously used Chris's body to assassinate her own husband, Frank Q.
- **The Dilemma**: Chris sends a message via Imani and Elle begging Carl to kill him to end his torment. Carl refuses to give up on him. Since Katia only has a few petrifaction bolts left, Donut uses *Hole* to drop Chris/Maggie through the street. They fall past the quadrant barrier and trap themselves in a sealed, half-flooded stone chamber within the Necropolis below.
- **Social Media Upgrade**: Carl instructs Mordecai to purchase the environmental upgrade for Donut's stream so they can monitor audience commentary directly.

## Characters Introduced
- None (Maggie My is confirmed as the parasite).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- [[valtay-skull-empire-conflict]]: Maggie's possession of Chris and possession of the Celestial Grenade is part of the Skull Empire's coordinated vendetta against Carl.
- [[solidarity-and-duty]]: Carl's refusal to kill Chris, despite Chris's own pleas, showcases his stubborn determination to protect other crawlers, even when faced with impossible choices.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's intervention to drop Chris into the hole saves Carl from the psychological trauma of killing his friend. Her protective instinct for Carl's mental state is a major step in her character growth. The revelation that people who die in the collapse are "in storage" and can be reused as monsters adds a horrifying new layer to the Syndicate's cosmic exploitation.
