# Chapter 424: Chapter 25 — Contingencies and the Deadly Gaze

## Summary
- **Mario Kart Party**: While Rosetta and Tipid head to the mercenary market, the NPCs and strippers play Wii Mario Kart in the safe room. Samantha dominates as Princess Peach, while Doctor Bones and Splash Zone struggle.
- **Fursona Level 8**: Carl's *Fursona* skill reaches Level 8, unlocking a temporary class named *Fursuiter* which grants buffs associated with his costume but increases the terrifying sensation that the suit is his real body.
- **Achievements & Loot**: Carl opens his boxes and receives several achievements: *Weaseler!*, *Slip-'n'-slide*, *Brotein!*, and *'76 Buccaneers!* (which rewards him a non-magical job application for Big Shot Chicken in Tucson).
- **Contingency Potion**: Carl drinks a *Platinum Hotlist Expansion* skill potion, expanding his hotlist to 25 spaces and unlocking the *Contingency* skill, which allows him to program automated item uses.
- **Rend's Death**: Mordecai enters the room in his Reaper form. His voice turns deep and ominous as he declares: "And now you perish once again. The next time will herald the beginning of the end." Mordecai kills Rend instantly.
