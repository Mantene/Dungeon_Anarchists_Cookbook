# Book 4 Chapter 19: The Liaison's Investigation

## Summary
Carl and Donut undergo a high-stakes interrogation by a neutral Syndicate investigator, revealing the mechanical truth of the assassination and returning to find five days have passed.
- **The Interrogation**: Carl and Donut are teleported to a back office in the Desperado Club. They face **Orren**, an independent Syndicate Liaison (revealed to be a Valtay worm in a swirling bowl of dark liquid under his hood). Orren is investigating Admin Loita's death due to conflicting reports between Borant, the Kua-tin, and the System AI.
- **The Chemical Theory**: Orren outlines his theory: the residue dust from Carl's *Hobgoblin Disco Ball* reacted with the moisture of Loita's rebreather to corrode a tamper-proof *Zentix* polymer panel on the toy cat's head. When the toy jumped down to follow her, the panel fell off, triggering the self-destruct. Orren offers Carl a Legendary Box and a safe passage to Floor 9 if he confesses and names his outside informant. Carl refuses.
- **The True Method**: In reality, the chemical corrosion was a happy coincidence. Carl had manually shimmed the toy's cheap battery cover using a strategically cut piece of his own left fingernail. The shim kept the cover barely attached, ensuring it would fly off during any sudden movement.
- **The Verdict and Punishment**: Because the AI and Kua-tin reports conflict and no consensus exists, Orren is forced to close the investigation without charging Carl. However, as a punitive measure, the Syndicate bans all third-party crawler teleportations for the rest of the floor (cancelling Katia's upcoming appearance and Donut's next interview with Odette).
- **The Five-Day Time Skip**: Carl and Donut are shocked to learn their interrogation took five days. During their absence, Katia reached Level 41 on the Land Quadrant, while Louis and Firas became skilled pilots of the floating house.
- **Looted Souvenir**: Carl reveals he successfully looted Loita's *kua-tin rebreather* device off her corpse, hiding it in his waistband during the interrogation.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's low-tech fingernail shim outsmarting the high-tech forensic analysts of the Syndicate is a brilliant, grounded display of his practical ingenuity. The revelation that the Kua-tin corporate bosses intervened to save Carl because he is "too valuable to throw away off-screen" highlights the ultimate protection afforded to high-performing crawlers by the media machine.
