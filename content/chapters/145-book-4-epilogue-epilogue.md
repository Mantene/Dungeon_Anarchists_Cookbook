# Book 4 Epilogue: Epilogue

## Summary
Sponsors discuss their support of Carl, while on the surface of Earth, Beatrice and the cat Gravy Boat are captured in Seattle during a black-ops operation by Odette's team.
- **Sponsor Backing**: Dr. P. Hu and Porthus discuss their massive spending on Carl's behalf. Porthus notes that Carl has shaken everything up, and his popularity on Odette's show is drawing more people to their cause.
- **Hunting Gravy Boat**: In the ruins of Seattle, walk-on bounty hunters Sadir (a Null) and Gennrik (Saccathian) track down the orange cat **Gravy Boat** (whom Beatrice calls Ferdinand). They breach Beatrice and Brad's tent in the New Queen Anne settlement.
- **The Ambush**: Lexis (Odette's associate producer, acting under Odette's orders) ambushes the hunters. She shoots and kills Gennrik. When Brad tries to grab Gennrik's dropped weapon, Lexis shoots Brad and leaves him to bleed out and die.
- **Beatrice Captured**: Lexis puts Beatrice in a bounty cube. She places Gravy Boat in a bounty cube to leave for Borant security to collect the Walk-On bounty.
- **Framing Sadir**: Lexis shoots Sadir in the leg to frame him as a rogue bounty hunter who turned on his partner and killed the humans.
- **Odette's Plan**: Odette instructs Lexis to bring Beatrice to her, stating she has a "much better use" for Beatrice than selling her to Borant, and that she will buy out Sadir's warrant to guarantee his cooperation.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The Epilogue reveals the dark, complex layers of the game show's background politics. Odette's capture of Beatrice suggests she is planning to use Beatrice as emotional leverage against Carl and Donut in a future broadcast, showing that even seemingly supportive figures like Odette are active manipulators of the crawlers' lives.
