# Chapter 364: Rishi the Usurper

## Summary
With forty minutes remaining before the Open Hostilities phase, [[carl]] visits [[rishi]] in the newly built brig complex. The naga sits inside a prismatic containment shield, stripped of his gear and with his four arms surgically removed as an extreme precaution by [[li-na]]'s White Dragons. Carl's viewer count spikes, showing a blue hue.

Rishi explains that he hid in the dirt to surrender rather than entering the Khepri temple because he knew [[princess-vinata]]'s plan to kill Carl with a god was a suicide mission. He reveals that the naga originally planned to steal the faction fleet and flee the Syndicate as part of "The Great Consensus"—a separatist philosophy.

Rishi has petitioned the Syndicate court on Carl's behalf, naming Carl the "de-facto leader of Earth" due to Carl's status as the number one crawler and warlord in the absence of a formal planetary government. Rishi presents a signed, AI-witnessed treaty: in exchange for allowing the naga to terraform and settle the other planets in Earth's solar system post-crawl, the naga will grant Earth survivors citizenship rights and five Naphil-class generation ships to ensure their survival.

Rishi reveals that he and his brothers have deposed the naga king (Vinata's father) and the ruling House of Greens. Rishi signs the contract as **Sultan Rishi the Usurper, First of his Name of the Naga Uprising**.

Carl receives the **Holistic!** achievement because over 95% of the viewers in the newly-coined Naga Protectorate of the New Reformer are watching his feed. He receives a Legendary Fan Box.

Declining the treaty, Carl tells Rishi he does not care about their political maneuvers. Instead, Carl uses the thirty-second contact to mark Rishi with the *Eye of the Bedlam Bride*, revealing Rishi's highest stat is Dexterity. Carl tells Rishi and the naga audience that the new Sultan is merely the "opening act" of the slaughter to come.

## Characters
* [[carl]]: Rejects Rishi's treaty, gains the "Holistic!" achievement, and marks Rishi for death.
* [[rishi]]: The newly crowned Sultan of the naga, who offers Carl five generation ships in exchange for planetary settling rights, only to be marked for death.

## Themes & Plot Threads
* **The Great Consensus**: The separatist movement within the Syndicate systems, illustrating the widespread desire to escape Syndicate control.
* **Planetary Ownership and Succession**: Carl is legally designated the de-facto leader of Earth under Syndicate law, allowing him to sign interstellar treaties.
* **The Spectacle of Violence**: Carl deliberately plays to the naga audience, choosing to execute their new leader on live television as a statement of defiance.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Rishi represents the political opportunism of the Syndicate's ruling classes. Even in the face of the system going primal, his primary concern is securing future territory and consolidating his own power via the Usurper title. Carl's disgust with this "Game of Thrones" posturing highlights his focus on direct, uncompromising resistance.
