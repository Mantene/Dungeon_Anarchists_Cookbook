# Book 5 Chapter 24: Obsidian Bracelet

## Summary
- **Cookbook Insights**: Carl reads a note by [[crawler-drakea]] in the 22nd Edition of the Cookbook, advising crawlers to set traps that target the sponsors' coins rather than fighting hunters directly, and to let hunters and elites clash.
- **Looting the Trebuchet**: Carl loots the first Moon Elf's corpse, finding another gonorrhea arrow and a photo of Louis (with a dog filter). Carl also claims the [[enchanted-venomous-elven-rock-chucker]] (a lightweight bamboo trebuchet that inflicts *Poison Cloud*), storing it in his *Siege Equipment* tab.
- **Tragic Ending Foreshadowing**: Carl worries that the *Vengeance of the Daughter* storyline is heading toward a tragic climax. If Signet attempts to kill her step-sister Queen Imogen (who is a protected Country Boss), the showrunners will likely zero out Signet and her companions.
- **Beaver Gland Lore**: Carl, Donut, and Holger grind on slickbacks. They discuss how beaver glands (castoreum) are used by humans for vanilla flavoring, which Holger finds disgusting.
- **Beaver Gods**: Samantha mentions a beaver god in the Nothing named [[capa]], who is used as a hat by the deity [[hehaka]].
- **Lightning Strike**: A massive blue bolt of lightning strikes the "all-tree" outcropping ahead. The group rushes forward. Donut's map shows a hunter surrounded by Signet's forces, with two other hunters dead. Samantha rolls ahead and gets stuck.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's realization that the *Vengeance of the Daughter* questline is designed to end tragically is a crucial bit of metagame analysis. Showrunners love tragic endings for secondary programs, and Imogen's status as a Country Boss makes her functionally invulnerable until the Masquerade, setup to wipe out the rebels. Samantha's random theology drop about Capa being Hehaka's hat adds to the surreal lore of the deities in the Nothing.
