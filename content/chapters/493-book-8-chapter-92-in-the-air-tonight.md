# Chapter 493: Chapter 92 — In the Air Tonight

## Summary
Chaco enters the parade riding "Lamashtu the Donkey" (a level 125 subjugated former deity). As Chaco begins reading his script, he breaks down in terror, and the AI brutally tears off his wings. The NPC fleshmancer Grigori is possessed by the AI to continue the narration. Grigori explains the history of the Primals through the float themes:
1. **Resolution** (Float 1): The Primals warred and hated each other. As a penalty for a "C effort," the AI breaks the legs of all the crawlers on Float 1.
2. **Decision** (Float 2): The Primals chose to sunset their civilization, transferring their minds into the Eulogist through a eugenics program.
3. **Betrayal** (Float 3): Those who resisted were hunted. The Apothecary rose in opposition. The collective turned their database into a doomsday weapon to kill all other biological life. Float 3 displays signs telling the audience "Fuck you," and listing names of their dead families.
4. **Destruction** (Float 4): The great war and the nine-tier attack. Makana, Sarah Hayse, and their team fire heavy tank weapons. The Eulogist went to sleep, guarded by the "Security Guard" instance.
5. **Vengeance** (Float 5): The final part. The mantids exploited the Eulogist to create their own AIs. The AI slams his hands, destroying the mantid systems in the real world.

As Team 5 passes the judging stand, Prepotente goes off-script and sings "In the Air Tonight" by Phil Collins, backed by Sweety's rhythmic footsteps. Carl gives a fiery speech declaring vengeance on the entire galaxy. Florin's airship drops the sluggalos into the bleachers, where they slaughter the elite audience members.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Prepotente's performance of "In the Air Tonight" serves as a powerful, haunting anthem for the crawlers' rebellion. Carl's speech moves beyond personal revenge to systemic vengeance, targeting the passive audience members who treat their genocide as entertainment. The slaughter of the "stand-in" elites marks a turning point where crawlers actively strike at the galactic populace.
