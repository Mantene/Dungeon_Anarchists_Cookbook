# Epilogue (Book 6)

## Summary
- **Agatha's Mission**: Agatha (Agent 22) is revealed to belong to an ancient cosmic race opposed to the Apothecary. Their goal is to awaken the enslaved Primal (the crawl AI) to free \"the Eulogist\" and purge all biological life from the universe. Agatha's teammate Parvati (now alexandro|Alexandro, Agent 21) has infiltrated the Mumbai site.
- **Syndicate Emergency Meeting**: [[princess-formidable]] demands the Syndicate Council blow the star (failsafe) to vaporize the system because the rogue AI has trapped them in orbit. The council refuses to protect the economy. The Valtay rep announces the \"Valtay solution\": deploying 150,000 mercenaries to Floor 9 to wipe out all crawlers. Formidable is muted.
- **The Homecoming Queen**: Former crawlers [[tipid]] and [[rosetta]] have converted their massive biological waste freighter *Homecoming Queen* (owned by Long Haul Solutions) to enter the dungeon. They have hidden **50,000 former crawlers** inside, onboarding them to fight alongside the Princess Posse on Floor 9.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The Epilogue reveals massive, game-changing factions moving pieces on a galactic scale. The reveal of 50,000 former crawlers entering Floor 9 inside the garbage ship completely reverses the power dynamic of the upcoming Faction Wars, turning the Valtay's planned slaughter of the crawlers into an all-out war.
