# Book 1 Chapter 35: Saved by Pedicure

## Summary
[[carl]] survives the explosion but is left deaf, blind, bleeding internally, and with broken legs, at 6% health. Because the Taint is still active, he cannot heal. [[princess-donut]] drags him out of the fire by his cloak. Once the Taint timer expires, Donut uses a healing scroll to save Carl's life. Carl realizes his feet were spared from amputation because of the "Unbreakable" buff from the **Enchanted Pedicure Kit of the Sylph**.

Returning to the boss room, they find the [[krakaren]] clone dead, smelling of microwaved Fancy Feast. The Alchemy Table is completely undamaged, confirming that personal space workbenches are indestructible—an exploit Carl plans to keep secret from the developers. Carl loots the **Floor 2 Neighborhood Map**, which immediately populates 50 red dots representing Brindle Grubs crawling toward the corpses.

They head to the Tutorial Guild. [[mordecai]] has shapeshifted into a **Bugaboo** (Level 50). When Carl delivers [[odette]]'s message, Mordecai reacts with absolute fury, stating he would rather devour his own children than see her again. He explains that "Damien," whom the Laminak fairy called for, is one of three location managers on Floor 2.

Carl opens his rewards:
- **Dungeonpreneur** Achievement: Awarded for registering **Carl's Jug O' Boom** in the *Dungeon Codex*. Carl will receive 1 gold coin for every kill made with this device by other crawlers.
- Boss Box: **Enchanted BigBoi Boxers** (+2 Constitution, grants the Level 15 **Protective Shell** spell, which creates a static 4.5m physical barrier for 20 seconds, no mana cost, 30-hour cooldown).

Suddenly, a system warning announces that weapons are prohibited around Admins. With a loud pop, a two-foot-tall Kua-tin Admin named [[zev]] appears in a puddle.

## Characters Introduced
- **[[zev]]**: Assistant Communications Representative for Borant Corporation, Carl and Donut's PR Agent.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival]]**: Discovering system exploits (indestructible workstations, stairs mechanics) becomes key to surviving high-level encounters.
- **[[carls-class-selection]]**: Carl obtains the *Protective Shell* defensive spell and the *Dungeonpreneur* codex weapon, locking in passive gold income.
- **[[search-for-beatrice]]**: Mordecai reveals Zev is arriving, and they must open boxes before she turns off the cameras.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The chapter marks the discovery of the workbench invulnerability exploit. Carl's physical survival due to the foot pedicure kit highlights the absurd humor of the dungeon—a foot fetish buff literally saved his life from amputating trauma. Mordecai's deep-seated hatred for Odette and fear of "Damien" hints at the massive, unseen intergalactic corporate structure running the crawl.
