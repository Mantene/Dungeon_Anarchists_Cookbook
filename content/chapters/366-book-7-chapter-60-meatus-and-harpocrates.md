# Chapter 366: Chapter 60 — Meatus and Harpocrates

## Summary
[[mordecai]] warns [[princess-donut]] not to use her PA speakers because [[harpocrates]] (the god of silence) hates sound and will target anyone making noise. Mordecai explains the bizarre lore behind **Meatus** and Harpocrates: Meatus is a gremlin-like creature who stole Harpocrates's "dink" (penis) to become a god. The sapient dink itself grows to god-size, likes music, and refuses to return to Harpocrates, who can hear a fly's heartbeat from half a planet away. A massive slurping sound and the stench of rotten soft cheese roll across the battlefield as Meatus is summoned, confirming [[samantha]]'s "cheese dick" comment.

On *Party Planner*, [[louis-santiago-2]] makes the bomb run over King's Point, the orc stronghold. Carl's presence overclocks the bombs. Louis drops the tech-busting *No Disassemble* EMP bombs and the high-yield *Waffle Makers*. The bombs shatter the orc castle's shields, though a long-range green death ray fired from an orc synergy tower narrowly misses the airship.

[[florin]]'s crocodiles engage the orc forces at the riverbank, where a 700-ton, five-meter-tall mechanical scorpion tank piloted by Warlord [[prince-stalwart|Stalwart]] attacks their flank. [[the-sledge]] and [[bomo]] provide missile support from Area 52 using modified controllers, hitting the tank with chaff and Mute missiles.

Following the bomb run, the Semeru dwarves agitate the lava tubes beneath the mountain of King's Point. The mountain erupts, destroying the orc castle and leaving the throne room exposed. 

Across the map, Meatus continues his loud, gargling struggle against Harpocrates, shouting that he is free and yearns to sing. Donut reports that Meatus used Harpocrates like a baseball bat, launching him directly into the Dream castle and disabling its shields. 

[[rosetta]] hands control of the southern campaign over to Donut. Britney confirms the southern digger is waiting below the surface for the gods to move away. As the dwarves report that the orcs have added a backup shield over the mountain, creating dangerous pressure, the volcano undergoes a massive secondary blast.

## Themes & Plot Threads
* **Dungeon Mythology**: The absurd origin of Meatus illustrates the twisted humor embedded in the system's deities.
* **Environmental Weapons**: The destruction of King's Point via a triggered volcanic eruption shows the crawlers' willingness to weaponize the environment itself.
* **Unintended Consequences**: The dwarves' failure to account for the orc backup shield causes the volcano to over-pressurize, resulting in a catastrophic blast that threatens the entire sector.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The comedic element of Meatus's stolen appendages contrasts sharply with the deadly volcanic eruption and the threat of the Basilica guards. Mordecai's deep knowledge of the game's lore remains a crucial asset, allowing the crawlers to navigate the gods' erratic behavior.
