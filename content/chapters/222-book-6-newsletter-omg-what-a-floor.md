# Book 6 Newsletter: OMG, What a Floor!

## Summary
This chapter is a newsletter written in the voice of [[princess-donut]] to her subscriber fan club, recapping the events of the sixth and seventh floors (from Books 5 and 6) as they prepare to enter the eighth floor. 

### Key Recap Points
- **Feral Gods Gate Deal**: On Floor 6, Carl temporarily surrenders the Gate of the Feral Gods to liaison [[orren]] to avoid administrative issues, securing four rock bodyguards including [[the-sledge]] and [[bomo]].
- **Donut's Legendary Diva Class**: Donut highlights her temporary class, allowing her to cast spells while singing (and plugs her upcoming album and Supreme Fan Tiers).
- **The Hunter Conflict**: Re-tells the conflict with the hunters, starting with the death of [[vrah]]'s sister, Carl's confrontation with Vrah's mother at CrawlCon, and the mother entering the crawl as the deity [[diwata]].
- **Katia's Separation**: Katia leaves the party to hunt down her player-killer ex-friend [[eva-sigrid]], successfully bringing Eva in but leaving the party (and falling in love with [[daniel-bautista-2]]).
- **Butcher's Masquerade Prep**: Introduces the upcoming pet show and talent show at the high elf castle, run by Queen Imogen.
- **Mongo's Traumatic Romance**: Mention of [[mongo]] meeting the female dinosaur [[kiwi]] (which Donut describes as highly traumatic for her "brave, sweet boy").
- **Signet's Quest**: Refers to the *Vengeance of the Daughter* storyline to help [[tsarina-signet]] reclaim her crown from Queen Imogen.
- **Prepotente's Loss**: [[prepotente]]'s handler Miriam Dom dies, and Prepotente is now accompanied by [[bianca]] (a demon goat dragon).
- **Lucia Mar Feud**: Donut kills one of [[lucia-mar]]'s dogs, initiating their feud.
- **Kiwi and Big Tina**: Explains that Kiwi is the mother of the giant allosaurus ballerina [[big-tina]], and both were formerly bears changed during Scolopendra's ancient attack.
- **Ferdinand's Return**: Donut's ex-boyfriend [[ferdinand]] returns as Queen Imogen's pet.
- **The Masquerade Climax**:
  - Signet gets revenge on Queen Imogen but dies in the process.
  - Mongo and Kiwi kill Vrah; Donut helps kill Vrah's mother with the *Laundry Day* spell.
  - Carl kills the final hunter.
  - The fan club successfully funds a spot on **Faction Wars**.
  - They teleport the elf castle to Floor 9 to serve as an army, and Carl and Donut become **Ninth Floor Warlords**.
- **Casualties**: Nice guy [[firas-m]] dies; [[gideon]] (whom Carl recruited) dies; Gwendolyn Duet dies; the Popov brothers die but are turned into babies and successfully exit the game.
- **The Pet Show Injustice**: Donut rants about [[tserendolgor]] altering the voting ballot box to steal Mongo's pet show crown for her pet meatball [[garret]], using the hashtag **#JusticeForMongo**.
- **The Shortcut**: Prepotente breaks the level transitions, sending them directly from the 6th to the 8th floor.
- **Viper Queen Class**: Donut briefly chooses the Viper Queen class before the transition.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's newsletter format is a brilliant structural technique to summarize complex, multi-book arcs with high comedy. Her self-absorbed diva persona shines through, but behind the vanity, her distress about Miriam's death, Katia's departure, Zev's brain-worm promotion, and Beatrice's return reveals the emotional weight of their situation. The inclusion of this newsletter restores a critical piece of narrative framing that is central to the transition into Book 6.
