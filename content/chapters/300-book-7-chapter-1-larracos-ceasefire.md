# Chapter 300: Chapter 1 — Larracos Ceasefire

## Summary
- **Arrival in Larracos**: Carl and Donut arrive on Floor 9 (Faction Wars) with 32,429 crawlers remaining. A 60-hour ceasefire is in effect.
- **Sushi Smoothie**: Carl cures his Soul Poisoning from keeping Scavenger's Daughter's soul power by vaporizing a dead shark using *Daughter's Kiss*, earning the *Sushi Smoothie* achievement.
- **Deathbed Hellcat**: Princess Donut selects the **Deathbed Hellcat** subclass, a sniper/assassin class that boosts range, targeting, and invisibility.
- **The Bloc vs. Team Retribution**: The corporate sponsors unify as "The Bloc" to attack Team Retribution (the NPC home team led by Juice Box and Ferdinand) and the Princess Posse.
- **The Truce**: Donut accepts a truce with Team Retribution, allying under "Ferdinand and his Bird," securing safe harbor in Larracos.
- **Cascadia's Broadcast**: Cascadia announces the rules of Floor 9: a team-based territorial war where safety protections for Faction sponsors have been removed.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's class selection of Deathbed Hellcat pivots her toward a stealthy, long-range sniper role, compensating for her fragile constitution. The removal of safety protections for corporate sponsors levels the playing field, making their enemies mortal in the dungeon.
