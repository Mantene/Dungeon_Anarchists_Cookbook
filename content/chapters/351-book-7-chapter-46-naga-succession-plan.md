# Chapter 351: Chapter 46 — Naga Succession Plan

## Summary
[[carl|Carl]] and [[princess-donut|Donut]] review the plan to save Donut and [[katia-grim|Katia]] from their contract restrictions. This requires killing the entire line of succession of the Blood Sultanate, which consists of four specific nagas: Warlord [[princess-vinata|Princess Vinata]] (the Sepsis Whore), her two "Ikbal" cousins, and the head of her castle guard (who is also her cousin/romantic partner). 

Traditionally, when a naga stronghold is besieged, the sultana and her elite guard disappear from the throne room undetected, even when teleportation is banned. [[rosetta|Rosetta]] explains her theory: Vinata escapes to [[club-vanquisher]] via a deity-based loophole. In previous seasons, Vinata used the goddess [[theia]]'s Prospect Congregation spell (which turns prospects into motes of light in the caster's pocket). However, since Theia is sponsored this season (to protect Carl/Samantha), Vinata must use Khepri, an obscure god of light. 

Under Khepri's ritual, the prospective worshipers turn into beetles, which a female guard swallows before using *Welcome Home* and *Travel the Path* spells to teleport directly to the Temple of Khepri in Club Vanquisher. Carl's team plans to breach Club Vanquisher's entrance outside Larracos, infiltrate Khepri's temple, and assassinate Vinata and her cousins.

Rosetta's analytical approach to the naga mystery is a testament to the intelligence gathered by former crawlers.
