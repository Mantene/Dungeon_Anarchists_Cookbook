# Book 5 Chapter 19: Reunion on Set

## Summary
- **On-Air Interview**: The segment begins without a studio audience. Zev notes that the censor blocks the introductory video about surface collections from Carl and Donut's view.
- **Beatrice's Appearance**: Beatrice appears as a hologram. She is thin, pale, wearing a glittering red gown and high heels, exposing a Princess Chonkalot tattoo on her lower back. She is heavily drugged, has memory gaps, and is shocked by Donut's talking and Carl's lack of pants.
- **The Off-Air Confrontation**: When the show goes off-air, Donut unleashes a furious tirade at Beatrice, calling her a "vile, disgusting bitch," and saying she betrayed Carl and was going to give Donut away. Carl says goodbye to Beatrice, and she is removed. Donut declares Carl is her person now.
- **The Valtay Coup**: An Admin Message announces the Valtay Corporation has been removed as Carl and Donut's sponsor due to a conflict of interest.
- **Corporate Regency Change**: A planet-wide message announces the Borant Corporation has been declared independent of the Borant System Government. The **Valtay Corporation has acquired 51% ownership of the Borant Corporation**, taking full control of the Earth crawl's regency. Zev remains their liaison. Donut whispers to Carl, "Viva la revolución," implying she had a hand in this coup.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[search-for-beatrice]]**: The final confrontation and relationship resolution with Beatrice.
- **[[systemic-oppression]]**: The corporate restructuring of Earth's crawl, shifting control to the Valtay Corporation.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's speech to Beatrice is the ultimate climax of their pre-collapse narrative. She defends Carl, exposes Beatrice's selfishness, and formally rejects her as her owner. This marks Donut's transition to a self-sovereign crawler whose primary bond is with Carl. The Valtay coup is a major political shift, and Donut's whisper indicates she smuggled assets to Zev to enable this corporate takeover, illustrating the crawlers' growing influence on galactic politics.
