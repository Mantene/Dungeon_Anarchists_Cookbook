# Chapter 405: Chapter 6 — Spider Legs Upgrade

## Summary
Carl and Donut are teleported back to their garage. The bugbear minivan is gone, though the flatbed trailer and tow chains remain. 

The Navitron GPS announces the details for Heat 2:
- **Distance**: 10 kilometers
- **Track**: Asphalt-paved road with rolling hills, divided into two paths assigned at random.
- **Goal**: Kill a gatekeeper monster on the assigned path to resume. Vehicles must stay on their assigned path.
- **Environment**: Torrential downpour and giant hail.
- **Hazards**: Hail, the gatekeeper monster, and the requirement that the vehicle must be driven by a cat (Donut) this time.
- **Time Limit**: 3 hours

Because they must alternate drivers, Donut has to drive this heat. Carl suggests building a custom driver rigging for Donut or hiring a mercenary from the local bar in Hungry Eyes. Donut adamantly refuses to bring [[samantha]] because of her chaotic nature.

Hedy recommends selecting a mobility upgrade for the truck before the tracks become unpaved. She presents three options: Hover Upgrade, All-Terrain Tires, and Retractable Spider Legs. Following her advice, Carl selects the **Retractable Spider Legs** upgrade, which allows the food truck to climb walls and bypass obstacles when activated. Hedy approves, teasing Carl as "sexy and smart."

[[mordecai]] messages them to put [[ren]] away or leave him in the garage before entering the safe room. Carl finds Rend and Mongo inside the food truck's kitchen, happily raiding the freezer for frozen French fries and chicken patties.
