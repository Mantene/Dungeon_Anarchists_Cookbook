# Book 1 Chapter 22: Meadow Lark

## Summary
[[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] discover the crawler encampment. It consists of 38 survivors, mostly elderly residents from the Meadow Lark eldercare facility in Wenatchee, Washington. Pushing a shopping cart is [[agatha]] (Level 2), the homeless woman who started the fire that evacuated the building. When the entrance opened, Agatha pushed her cart in, transforming the stairs into a wheelchair ramp. 

The care staff are the only combat-capable survivors: [[brandon-an]] (Level 6, wielding a massive light metal maul), [[chris-andrews-2]] (Level 6, his quiet brother), [[yolanda-martinez-13]] (Level 5 CNA, wielding a bow), and [[imani-c]] (Level 10 night staff nurse, wielding a longsword). Imani has a **Crawler Killer X 12** tag; Brandon explains she had to mercy-kill several residents who were burning or suffering horribly when the dungeon first collapsed. 

Brandon explains that 30 other residents are living in a Waffle House safe room, refusing to leave. The other active crawlers who left to seek alternative stairs were hunted down and killed. Brandon, Chris, Yolanda, and Imani refuse to abandon their patients. Carl decides to help them fight the Borough Boss rather than search for another exit. He plans to use the next 40 hours to grind for levels and build a modular defensive barricade (a "Speedbump" redoubt) to wedge the rolling boss.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[solidarity-and-duty]]**: The Meadow Lark caretakers refuse to abandon their elderly charges, demonstrating an unwavering moral code amid the dungeon's horrors. Carl also commits to helping them, refusing to leave the helpless behind.
- **[[survival-of-the-collapse]]**: Defeating the Borough Boss is the only viable path to Floor 2 for the 38 elderly crawlers.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The introduction of the Meadow Lark residents is one of the most tragic developments in Book 1. The dungeon does not spare the frail or the senile; it heals their fatal illnesses but leaves their physical limits and dementia intact. Imani's 12 crawler kills reflect the grim realities of mercy-killing and defense that the caretakers had to perform immediately upon entering.
