# Book 6 Chapter 29: Uzi Jesus and Waste Management

## Summary
Donut's sponsorship is sold to a waste management company, and she and Carl test their totems' personalities and suffer a quick defeat in a deck-to-deck simulation.
- **Sponsor Shift**: Donut is outraged to receive a notification that Empress D'Nadia sold her sponsorship. Her new sponsor is **Long Haul Biological Waste Management Solutions**. Carl points out that she has been proposing wild action items in the Warlord chat, making the shift unsurprising.
- **Totem Personality Conflicts**: Carl and Donut assess the dynamic of their totems:
  - **Asojano**: Powerful but aggressive; tried to cast a debuff on Donut before being blocked by mock arena safeties.
  - **Raul & Geraldo**: Constant conflict. Geraldo (monk seal) insults Raul (crab), who grovels before Donut while shouting about chi and "kowtowing." In one mock battle, Raul cowed to Shi Maria, who used him as a club to beat *Kappas* to death. Geraldo now calls Raul "the spider lady's jinetera."
  - **Skylar Spinach**: Uncommunicative and splits into a flying donkey head (*Skylar*, Dodge 15) and a noncorporeal spike-shooting snake (*Spinach*). The split halves become separate cards for the remainder of the battle.
  - **Shi Maria**: Their strongest card, but highly sadistic; she partially eats enemies (like a Medusa) and kisses them before they die.
  - **HeyZoos (Uzi Jesus)**: Childish and gaslighting. He only has a 50% chance of obeying orders. When asked to resurrect Geraldo, he sat idle and claimed Geraldo was "being a pansy" or "might be an atheist." In one match, he accidentally shot himself in the shoulder.
- **Deck-to-Deck Defeat**: Carl and Donut test the "Secret Challenge Mode" (deck-to-deck combat) against **Der Schachmeister** (Level 25 child deckmaster).
  - Der Schachmeister summons *Der Kornwolf* and *Die Kartoffelwölfin* (Level 100 wolves) and has an 8-second pull time (faster than Donut's 10-second pull).
  - The opponent casts a *Summon Lock* (locking Shi Maria) and a card-discard spell (*To Hell With Ya!*) that burns Shi Maria out of Donut's hand.
  - He casts *Peekaboo* to view Donut's hand.
  - Raul uses his *Bris* spell to decapitate one wolf, but is tackled by the second.
  - Samantha attempts a new spell, glowing blue and charging like a sun, but fizzles out.
  - The second wolf tackles Donut, resulting in a **Simulation Failed** screen.
- **Strategic Adjustments**: Carl realizes that in real deck-to-deck combat, he and Mongo must rush and physically attack the enemy deckmaster immediately to disrupt their spells, rather than relying solely on the totems.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **System Manipulation & Ratings**: The sponsor shift to a biological waste company shows the showrunners' passive-aggressive retaliation against Donut's antics.
- **NPC Self-Determination**: Samantha's sudden, uncontrolled blue-flaming magical discharge shows her powers are evolving independently of her doll body.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The deck-to-deck defeat is a necessary reality check for Carl and Donut, demonstrating that a deckmaster with a faster pull rate and debuff locks can easily shut down their heavy-hitter totems. The childish defiance of Uzi Jesus and the open hostility of Asojano show that their deck is a ticking time bomb of conflicting egos. Carl's tactical conclusion—that the best way to win a card game is to run across the field and punch the other player in the face—reflects his practical, rules-subverting combat philosophy.
