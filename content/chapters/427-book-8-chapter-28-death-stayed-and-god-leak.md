# Chapter 427: Chapter 28 — Death Stayed and the God Leak

## Summary
- **Plenty of Plenty Broadcast**: Carl and Donut enter the studio. The host, **Botis** (a Caprid), bounces around enthusiastically.
- **Death Sentence**: Botis suddenly screams, "YOU HAVE BEEN SENTENCED TO DEATH!" The other Caprids in shadow chant along. Donut appeals, and the death sentence is stayed so they can be judged.
- **The Interlocution**: A small red Caprid asks if their foliage input is sufficient. Donut complains about Carl's bathroom habits, and they are judged worthy.
- **Justice Light's Legacy**: Carl asks a Caprid named **Bathin** (a chief engineer of the Plenty Tunnel Project) what Justice Light did. Bathin explains that Justice Light's trap broke the in-game version of the Nothing, releasing its inhabitants (like the dread Kyryap and Krakaren Prime) onto the 12th floor and into Club Scolopendra on the 15th.
- **Emergency Chat**: Orren and Prime Minister Victory message Carl, begging him to ask the Plenty leaders about the integrity of the tunneling system. They explain that the Primal AI is spreading through the galaxy's tunnels and has destroyed a Valtay manufacturing facility. Furthermore, the AI has lost control of the unsponsored gods, who are roaming free near tunnel nodes.
- **The Declaration**: Carl ignores Orren's prepared question and instead asks: "Does the public know that the gods have escaped from the dungeon? And that the enhancement zones of the current AI have jumped into your tunneling system? Do the people know they're in danger? Do they know that they need to fucking run?"
- **The Fallout**: Prime Minister Victory is horrified that Carl said this on a live galactic broadcast. Orren notes, "And now the party really begins."

Carl's decision to bypass the Syndicate's agenda and directly warn the galaxy of the AI's spread is a massive turning point. He effectively breaks the information quarantine, exposing the outworlders to the same terror the crawlers experience inside the dungeon.
