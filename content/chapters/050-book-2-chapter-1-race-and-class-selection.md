# Book 2 Chapter 1: Race and Class Selection
![[Floor3AIGenMap.jpg]]
## Summary
[[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] teleport from the Floor 2 green room to the golden entrance hallway of the Over City (Floor 3). They are met by [[mordecai]], who has taken the form of a Level 50 Incubus. Donut is first to select her race and class. She refuses to change her race, staying as a **Cat**, which increases her Constitution slightly and grants her night vision, claw/slash, reflexes, and the *9 Lives* passive (halves damage from first nine physical hits daily).

For her class, Donut is presented with three AI recommendations: *Artist Alley Mogul*, *NecroBard*, and *Former Child Actor*. She chooses **Former Child Actor** (Earth class, Bard/Rogue hybrid), which grants her poison/disease immunity, Level 5 Cockroach skill, +10 Charisma, and **[[manager-benefit|the Manager Benefit]]**. 

Mordecai is forced to become her manager, meaning he will teleport to any safe room they enter. While he loses access to the codex and television, he can chat and brew potions for them. This choice was executed based on Odette's advice.

## Characters Introduced
- None (but Mordecai's Incubus form is revealed).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[manager-benefit|Manager Benefit]]**: Donut selects the class that binds Mordecai to them as their manager.
- **[[systemic-oppression]]**: The gamification of the crawl, forcing guides into subservient managerial roles.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's decision to act as if she picked *Artist Alley Mogul* before choosing *Former Child Actor* shows her growing cunning and acting skills. Mordecai's fury and subsequent despair at being tied to them highlights the heavy personal cost of crawler survival strategies.
