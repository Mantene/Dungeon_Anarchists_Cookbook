# Chapter 325: Planning the Assault

## Summary

In a fortified bunker at the southern front, [[carl]] and [[tran]] meet with Colonel [[boomer]] of the 106th Bloody Leeches. Boomer is a legendary, bulldog-like Level 62 Elven Legate who was once banned from Crawl Con for threatening a show runner. Donut is highly amused by him, commenting that he smells like cedar and acorns.

Tran outlines the enemy positions: the Dream's artillery trebuchets are positioned on the southern hills, constantly pounding their shield to exhaust their mages. Boomer identifies key enemy threats, including the cowardly "bombardier" elves of the Dream, the elite "Epitome household guard" led by [[epitome-tagg]] (who wears a ridiculous helmet), and the "Monkeywrenchers" (invisible Madness/Viceroy saboteurs). The term "Cheese Sticks" has spread as a derogatory slang name for the enemy troops.

The southern trenches are lightly defended by Prism and Madness NPCs/mercenaries, while the main forces in the north (fighting Florin and Katia) consist of Orcs, blobs, and Dream regulars. The whereabouts of the Naga and Reaver main forces remain unknown. 

Carl reveals that Louis's airship, the *Party Planner*, is hidden nearby and ready to strike. [[britney-proskurina]] has assumed the role of main NPC liaison, coordinating with changeling spies in Shanty Town. 

The primary target is the Naga castle, situated eight miles away on rocky ground. Historically, this castle falls first, but the Naga leader always mysteriously disappears and survives the conflict. Boomer lights his cigar, and Carl authorizes the counter-assault to begin.

## Characters

- **[[carl]]** — Leads the planning session; coordinates the airship strike and the ground counter-assault.
- **[[princess-donut]]** — Watches the briefing; chats with Carl about Boomer's bulldog-like appearance and cedar scent.
- **[[boomer]]** — Elven Legate and Colonel of the 106th; provides veteran tactical insights on enemy units and troop types.
- **[[tran]]** — Details the map logistics; his mechanical crab legs twitch with anxiety.
- **[[britney-proskurina]]** — Acts as the primary liaison coordinating with the NPC factions (mentioned).
- **[[epitome-tagg]]** — Leader of the elite Dream household guard, known for his ridiculous helmet (mentioned).

## Themes & Plot Threads

- **Cheese Sticks** — The crawlers' derogatory slang for the enemy faction mercenaries and soldiers.
- **Monkeywrenchers** — The invisible, elite infiltration agents of the Madness faction.
- **Sultana's Escape Secret** — The historical mystery of the Naga leader's consistent survival despite the quick fall of her castle.
- **Pre-emptive Strike** — Carl's decision to launch a pre-emptive strike to take out the Dream's artillery batteries before magic suppression ends.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections

This chapter is a classic tactical briefing that sets up the layout of the southern campaign. Boomer is a fantastic addition to the cast, representing the grizzled, cynical "tenners" who have survived the crawl's aftermath and hate the system with a passion. The slang term "Cheese Sticks" adds a realistic layer of military camaraderie to the crawler army, showing how they build shared culture under pressure.
