# Book 1 Chapter 2: Welcome to the First Floor
![[Floor1AIGenMap.jpg]]
## Summary
[[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] descend the ornate stairs into Floor 1 of the World Dungeon, where the temperature is a warm 80 degrees. At the bottom, they encounter giant double doors carved with a fish-demon (representing the Kua-Tin, the owners of the Borant Corporation). Upon entering the hallway, Carl receives his crawler designation (#4,122) and is flooded with achievements for entering the dungeon with a cat, without pants, without weapons, and alone.

They follow a neon sign for "Da Tutorial Guild" down a dark alley, only to be ambushed by a steam-powered Goblin Murder Dozer driven by a Level 3 Goblin Engineer and two Level 2 Goblins. Carl flees down a side hallway and finds the actual Tutorial Guild door. The guildmaster, a rat-like creature named [[mordecai]], refuses to open the door because there are monsters nearby. Carl is forced to fight, knocking out a Level 2 goblin with his bare hands. Donut slips inside through the cracked door, and because she is a crawler, Mordecai is forced to open the door to let Carl inside.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival]]**: Carl has to engage in hand-to-hand combat against a hostile goblin.
- **[[survival-of-the-collapse]]**: Carl and Donut make it to a safe zone (the Tutorial Guild).
- **[[carls-class-selection]]**: Carl receives stats based on his initial profile and learns he must wait until Floor 3 to choose a class.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The gamification is fully active here, with "achievements" that mock Carl for his lack of clothing and weapons. This chapter highlights how the system plays with Earth expectations—goblins are real, and they operate under video game conventions (health bars, levels).
