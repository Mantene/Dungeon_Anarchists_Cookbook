# Chapter 310: Chapter 10 — Mayorship of Shanty Town

## Summary
- **Clarabelle and Hamed**: The party meets [[clarabelle]] at the Desperado Club. She reveals that crawlers are temporarily banned by order of the new manager, **Hamed** (the Night Wyrm, former Guild of Suffering leader and father of Damascus Steel and Anaconda). Clarabelle notes that Hamed is filling the club with non-NPC, non-crawler guards.
- **Ferdinand's Toilet Paper Turban**: [[drick]] enters with the co-warlord [[ferdinand]] (Cat, Level 100) sitting on his head, wearing toilet paper to simulate a turban. Ferdinand demands the return of his rhino mount (Simoom) and his magical turban.
- **The Trade**: Donut reveals she found Ferdinand's turban after the *Butcher's Masquerade*. Carl trades Ferdinand [[quan-ch]]'s boomerang bowler hat and gold in exchange for the **mayorship of Shanty Town**.
- **Alliance Renamed**: The alliance between Team Retribution and the Princess Posse is renamed to **\"The Good Guys\"**.
- **Small Wedding Ceremony**: Juice Box and Louis hold a brief "small wedding" in the club entrance bar. The wedding is legally binding but includes a one-month trial period during which either party can file divorce papers without penalty before the "Confirmation" ceremony.
- **Urgent Call to HQ**: Carl's interface is flooded with recruit notifications. [[florin]] messages Carl, urging him to travel to the headquarters via the doggie door to meet a large group of people.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Acquiring the mayorship of Shanty Town gives Carl's faction direct control over the suburban ring surrounding Larracos, expanding their strategic footprint. The introduction of Hamed as the Desperado Club's new manager, along with his mysterious guards, sets up a confrontation within the club.
