# Book 4 Chapter 13: Frenzied Gerbil Retribution

## Summary
The party faces a bizarre punishment from the System AI before returning to Hump Town to open their loot and meet an old ally.
- **The Gerbil Horde**: While looting falling gnomish bodies, Donut finds "Live Ammo Balls" containing Level 11 Frenzied Gerbils. Carl realizes the System AI is furious that he avoided getting hit by them earlier (appeasing the AI's barefoot-smushing fetish). The AI sends a massive horde of thousands of Frenzied Gerbils to attack Hump Town as punishment.
- **The Smush**: The defenders are overwhelmed at the gates of Hump Town. To end the attack, Carl is forced to step on and smush fifty of the gerbils with his bare feet. The AI immediately halts the onslaught and awards Carl the **"You're the reason why daddy drinks!"** achievement and a *Gold Makeup Sex is the Best Sex* box.
- **Loot Box Openings**:
  - **Carl**: Receives *Potions of Half Splat* (prevents fall death, leaves user at 5% health), a *Platinum Sharpening Stone* (buffs gauntlet damage and stats), and a *Coffee Shop Author Kit* (adds a third tab to his scratchpad interface, allowing scroll creation).
  - **Katia**: Receives a special armor-piercing, level-scaling crossbow bolt.
  - **Donut**: Opens her Veriluxx Benefactor Box, containing a prototype toy cat that speaks in Garfield quotes ("I sure do like lasagna. I hate Mondays, Carl"). Mongo bites its head off, causing it to catch fire and explode. Donut also gets fang caps for Mongo (+15% speed and strength) and an Animal Wrangling skill potion.
- **Chris Andrews' Return**: After the town's children leave the safe room, Carl is approached by **Chris Andrews II**, who has undergone a dramatic transformation into a Level 35 Igneous Zulu Warrior.
