# Chapter 383: Chapter 75 — Stayfast Ink

## Summary
Edgar finishes the line art dog tattoo on Donut, unlocking the *Bijanbi* spell (associated with the deity Wepwawet). Milk uses specialized Words of Power ink to temporarily boost the spell's level to 15. Before leaving the tower, Milk gives Carl a wineskin of *Stayfast Ink* (permanent ink that never fades) and an *Adept's Fountain Pen*, hinting at how Carl can write permanent messages in his Book of Voodoo / Cookbook. 

Harpocrates throws the massive battle ball into the hole of Larracos. The defensive towers fire at it, draining its health. However, Carl and Donut discover they are trapped inside the Nest by a secondary power-siphoning net trap left by Lucia Mar. Carl commands Dong Quixote to retrieve the marble-sized trigger to disable the net. With the battle ball breaking and time running out, Li Na orders Donut to cast *War Crime*. Because they are trapped and cannot jump, Carl tells Donut to choose a non-fire spell instead of *Wall of Fire* to prevent the Nest tower from burning down.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **The Cookbook & Permanent Ink**: The acquisition of *Stayfast Ink* represents a major step forward for the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook, allowing Carl to write entries that time and distance cannot fade.
- **Lucia's Traps**: Lucia's tactical genius in using power-siphon networks to keep opponents trapped.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The interaction between Milk and Carl is highly symbolic. Her quote, "No single recipe is a feast in itself," emphasizes that there are multiple ways to fight the system. The gift of permanent ink gives Carl the tools needed to leave a lasting legacy in the Cookbook, ensuring that subsequent generations of crawlers will benefit from his knowledge without it fading.
