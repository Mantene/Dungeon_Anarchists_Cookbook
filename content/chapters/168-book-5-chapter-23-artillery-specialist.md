# Book 5 Chapter 23: The Artillery Specialist

## Summary
- **Interrogation**: [[akland]], handcuffed and terrified, pleads for his life. He explains that he is actually just a soy shipping accountant in the real world who did well at a "fantasy hunter camp" on a moon and won a spot in Faction Wars.
- **Dream's Vendetta**: Akland reveals that [[the-dream]] faction doesn't care about Carl or Donut; their sole target is [[louis-santiago-2]] because he besmirched the honor of their leader [[epitome-tagg]]'s mother.
- **The Execution**: Carl does a dramatic monologue for the camera, highlighting the cosmic tragedy of a corporate accountant playing "hunter" with real lives. He marks Akland with the Ring of Divine Suffering and beats him to death with his bare hands.
- **The Ring Levels Up**: The execution levels up the Ring of Divine Suffering (now giving +2 stats per kill). Carl reaches Level 55\. 
- **The Obsidian Bracelet**: Carl loots Akland's body, obtaining the [[enchanted-obsidian-bracelet-of-the-raggle-rouser]] (+10 Con, +5 Dex, +15% Fire Resistance, casts level-10 Fireball). He gives it to Donut.
- **Samantha's Evolution**: Donut compares Samantha's rapid rolling and biting behavior to the movie *Critters*. Carl plans to use her to take Fort Freedom in the morning.

## Characters Introduced
- [[epitome-tagg]] — Leader of The Dream hunter faction (mentioned).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[the-dream]]**: The Moon Elf faction's focus on executing Louis.
- **[[systemic-oppression]]**: The realization that hunters are ordinary citizens (like accountants) playing games with crawler lives.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Akland's confession highlights the sickening banality of the crawl's corporate structure. He is not a warrior; he is a soy crop shipping accountant who won a vacation package to murder humans. Carl's speech is a deliberate attempt to shame the Syndicate audience, while his brutal execution of Akland shows that he will not allow the tourists to hide behind their corporate titles. The leveling of the Ring of Divine Suffering and Donut's acquisition of the fireball bracelet significantly increase their combat power.
