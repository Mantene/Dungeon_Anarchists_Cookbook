# Book 6 Chapter 3: Scavenger's Daughter Upgrades

## Summary
Carl opens his Celestial Predator Box, equipping a game-changing divine patch, and receives his squad flags.
- **Celestial Predator Loot**: Carl opens the huge, rotating skull box:
  - He receives the [[scavengers-daughter-patch]] (divine back patch). Upon equipping it to his leather jacket, he gains massive stat increases (+25 Dexterity, +25 Strength), deity detection, and the *Soul Reaper* and *Mysterious Bone Key* benefits.
  - He receives the *Enchanted Prison Tattoo Kit*, *Doctor Ratchet's Automaton Build-It Kit*, the *Enchanted Right Back Atcha Personal Shield*, and the *Enchanted Nipple Ring of the Defiler* (specifically for crawlers marked for death by a god, granting the *Black Nimbus* spell, +25% cleric damage, and shrine-destruction abilities).
- **Britney's Trauma**: Katia chats that Britney Proskurina is severely traumatized, weeping and clutching Louis, because a facial burn from Floor 6 isn't healing. Donut warns that this is a classic "villain origin story."
- **Squad Flags**: Carl receives his 20 squad flags. The flags are embroidered heart-covered boxers with "The Royal Court of Princess Donut" motorcycle club-style patches and a bomb patch, matching Carl's jacket.

## Characters Introduced
- [[britney-proskurina]] — Human crawler, Louis's companion (previously introduced; first appearance in Book 6).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **Crawler Scaling**: Carl gains divine-tier items and customizable upgrades (tattoo kits, automaton kits) to build his personal combat arsenal.
- **Britney's Descent**: The psychological toll of the crawl begins to break Britney, foreshadowing her eventual development.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The Scavenger's Daughter patch is a double-edged sword: it offers Carl immense scaling power (*Soul Reaper*) and a trump card (*Mysterious Bone Key*), but at the potential cost of his own bones. The design of Carl's squad flags — heart boxers with a bomb — is a perfect representation of the comedic but explosive synergy between Carl and Donut.
