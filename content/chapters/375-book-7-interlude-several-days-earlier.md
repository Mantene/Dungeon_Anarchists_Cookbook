# Chapter 375: Interlude — Several Days Earlier

## Summary
Several days before the start of the final phase, [[carl]] and [[katia-grim]] consume mind-altering potions in the saferoom. In their inebriated state, they touch hands, creating a psychic connection that links Carl directly to Katia's dream. 

Before merging with Katia's mind, Carl briefly experiences a psychic overlap with those nearby:
* [[mordecai]]: Senses his deep, bottomless well of sadness.
* [[rosetta]]: Senses her tired rage.
* [[edgar]]: Senses his fear and wonder at remembering hundreds of past lives.
* [[bucket-boy]]: Senses his shame at not remembering his past lives like other NPCs.
* [[imani-c]]: Senses the heavy guilt she carries for fleeing her family in Detroit to seek peace as a nurse. He also learns that she is secretly in love with [[chris-andrews-2]].

Carl's consciousness enters Katia's dream, which reconstructs the second-worst moment of her life. They stand at a children's hospital in Iceland. Katia's ex-husband, a professor named [[fannar]], confronts Katia at the entrance. Fannar had reported Katia to block her adoption application because of her own history of cancer, which she had tried to hide.

The dream shifts to the hospital bathroom, where Katia locks herself in a stall and silently screams in despair. She then walks to the ward to say a final goodbye to the baby she hoped to adopt: a little girl named [[annie-baby|Annie]] who has aggressive brain cancer. The baby has a splint-device on her arm to protect her IV, and her eyes struggle to focus, but she happily babbles and bounces in her crib. 

Katia holds and sings to baby Annie. She explains that Annie died alone in that ward a few weeks later. The bird totem on Katia's shoulder (Annie) is named after this baby.

Katia expresses her deep love for Carl, Donut, and [[daniel-bautista-2]] (who loves her). She tells Carl she loves them as much as she loved the baby. Carl reassures her that they are family now.

The dream is suddenly shattered when [[shi-maria]]'s consciousness, confused and screaming, bursts into the room. She yells that she did not kill him. A male voice replies: *"Bitch, you swallowed me whole."*

## Characters
* [[carl]]: Connects psychically to the minds of his team before joining Katia's dream, offering comfort.
* [[katia-grim]]: Shares her painful memory of baby Annie and her ex-husband Fannar, expressing her love for her dungeon family.
* [[imani-c]]: Her private guilt and secret love for Chris are briefly perceived by Carl.
* [[fannar]]: Katia's ex-husband who blocked her adoption application.
* [[annie-baby]]: The terminally ill baby Katia tried to adopt, who died alone in the hospital.
* [[shi-maria]]: Bursts into the dream screaming that she didn't kill her husband.

## Themes & Plot Threads
* **Psychic Merges**: The potions allow a rare, unmediated mental connection between Carl and his allies, exposing their deepest vulnerabilities.
* **The Origin of Annie**: Katia's bird totem, Annie, is revealed to be named after the dying baby she was forbidden to adopt, representing her maternal grief.
* **Shi Maria's Secret**: The disruption of the dream by Shi Maria and her husband's voice hints at the dark truth of her past before she became a crown.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
This interlude provides crucial psychological context for Katia's desperation to leave the dungeon. Her history of cancer, her ex-husband's betrayal, and the death of baby Annie explain why she carries so much maternal guilt. Carl's reassurance that they are "family" highlights the genuine bonds formed among the crawlers, which stand in stark contrast to the superficial alliances of the Faction Wars.
