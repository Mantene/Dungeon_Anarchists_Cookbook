# Book 5 Chapter 29: Tattooing Nadine

## Summary
- **Tattooing Process**: Edgar the tortoise uses a silver stick to ink [[nadine]]'s ashes onto Signet's thigh. The other tattoos swirl around to leave bare skin.
- **Nodling Splitting**: Signet explains that her three-headed ogre tattoo is [[di-we]], her deceased protector. When a nodling dies, they split into new individuals based on the number of heads. Di-we split into [[areson-the-wise]], Apollon, and [[herman-the-fleet]].
- **The Finished Tattoo**: Nadine's completed tattoo shows her as a young female Chee with a child (representing [[clint-smashgrab]]) hiding behind her skirt, preserving their memories. The were-castors bow to Signet's skin.
- **Wonderwall Loop**: The were-castors build a dam around Fort Freedom's entrance. The loop of "Wonderwall" is still playing in the vent system.
- **Moving South**: Carl, Donut, and Samantha head south to cross the river and head toward a small dryad settlement. They loot the dead Ursine on the south shore.
- **Alucarda**: They arrive at a small village named **Alucarda**. Zev pings them to get to a safe room immediately. The view counter spikes to the right. The dryad out front reveals the mayor of Alucarda is **Lucia Mar**. Carl is transferred away, leaving Donut behind.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The nodling splitting mechanic is a fascinating bit of lore, explaining the origin of Areson, Apollon, and Herman. The tattoo memorial process is deeply moving and spiritual, showing how Signet's body serves as a walking graveyard of her companions. The cliffhanger at Alucarda is tense—Carl is teleported away for CrawlCon, leaving Donut alone in a town governed by the psychotic crawler Lucia Mar.
