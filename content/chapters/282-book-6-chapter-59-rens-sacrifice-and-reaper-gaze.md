# Chapter 282: Ren's Sacrifice and Reaper Gaze

## Summary
- **Key Transfer Dilemma**: The party realizes that they cannot transfer a key to Ren's squad without a high risk of random card loss.
- **Ren and Khulan's Sacrifice**: With Chuluuna dead and their chances slim, Ren decides to sacrifice herself and the grieving Khulan. She triggers a directional self-destruct bracer.
- **Inherited Loot**: Ren leaves all of her gear, cards, and pet carrier to Carl. Her pet [[garret|Garret the Tummy Acher]] is acquired by Carl, reset to Level 1, and given a new *Reaper Gaze* weakness alongside its four existing sylvan crystal, hobgoblin sweat, rivenwing bite, and undead flesh weaknesses.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Ren's choice is a heartbreaking display of loyalty and practical despair. Rather than risking a fumbled card transfer or dying uselessly, she chooses to go out on her own terms, leaving her remaining assets to Carl in the hope that he can avenge them.
