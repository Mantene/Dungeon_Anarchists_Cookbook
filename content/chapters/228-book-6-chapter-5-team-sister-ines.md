# Book 6 Chapter 5: Team Sister Ines

## Summary
Carl and Donut meet Team Sister Ines, a trio of fellow crawlers who spawn in the Havana region.
- **Meeting the Crawlers**: Carl and Donut encounter three human crawlers near a park:
  - **[[paz-lo]]**: A crawler who worships the god [[ogun]]. He possesses a directional self-destruct spell.
  - **[[anton-lopez]]**: Paz's cousin; a Fugitive class crawler who is highly skilled with a crossbow.
  - **[[sister-ines-quiteria]]**: A former nun with the class Poet Laureate.
- **Intel from Ren**: [[ren]] (the Valtay-aligned crawler) messages Carl with background on the trio: they are loners who had a brutal sixth floor. Anton Lopez killed two members of their own party early in the crawl after they attempted to kill Sister Ines. Paz's self-destruct spell is extremely dangerous.
- **Friction and Shared Enemies**: Donut instantly dislikes Sister Ines due to her feline physical adaptations, calling her a "fake cat" and suspecting her because she "doesn't use a litterbox." Imani mentions the trio was in the same bubble as [[quan-ch]] on Floor 5 and hated him, giving them a common enemy with Carl.
- **The Target Mobs**: Sister Ines reveals they are tracking local folklore monsters: "monk seals."

## Characters Introduced
- [[paz-lo]] — Human crawler worshipping Ogun.
- [[anton-lopez]] — Fugitive class human crawler.
- [[sister-ines-quiteria]] — Poet Laureate human crawler and former nun.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **Crawler Camaraderie**: Trust is difficult to establish on higher floors, but shared survival trauma and common enemies (like Quan Ch) help bridge the gap.
- **[[ghosts-of-earth]]**: Introduction of the regional Cuban folklore factions.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The introduction of Team Sister Ines shows how diverse the surviving crawlers are. Anton's lethal protection of Sister Ines highlights the dark, protective bonds formed in the dungeon. Donut's elitism regarding feline biology provides comic relief in the face of grim survival realities.
