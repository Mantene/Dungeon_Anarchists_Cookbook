# Chapter 340: Chapter 35 — Dwarven Tunnels

## Summary
In the immediate chaos following [[king-rust|King Rust's]] death, [[carl]] runs forward to loot Rust's corpse and severed head. [[rosetta]] uses a handheld device to scan Rust's retina and family ring, initiating a massive bank transfer. As the enemy forces charge, an enemy mage casts Dispel on Carl's smoke, which accidentally cancels the ceiling fortifications of a hidden dwarven transit tunnel below. Carl, [[princess-donut]], Rosetta, Simoom the rhino, and Gonk the swamp yak fall twelve feet into the tunnel.

Semuru dwarves defend the tunnel from enemy invaders and restore the ceiling, cutting off the combat. In the dark tunnel, Rosetta's transfer is approved by Colloid Bank, and she breaks down crying in relief, having successfully stolen billions of credits from the orc king in revenge for a friend killed cycles ago. 

System messages announce the death of [[warlord-hortense|Warlord Hortense]] in a "fragging incident" and the defeat of [[the-operatic-collective|The Operatic Collective]], with their assets awarded to the Bone Clan. [[imani-c]] reports that the enemy army is retreating due to the massive blast. Before Carl can explain the failsafe threat to Rosetta, he feels the familiar tingles of teleportation and is pulled into the Desperado Club.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
This chapter showcases Rosetta's tactical preparation. Her heist represents a major blow to the Skull Empire's resources, and her emotional breakdown shows the psychological toll of her long-awaited vengeance. The sudden fall into the dwarven tunnels highlights the multi-layered design of Floor 9, where the underground structures built by the NPCs provide tactical cover for the crawlers.
