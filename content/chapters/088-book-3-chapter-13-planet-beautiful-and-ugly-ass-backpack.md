# Book 3 Chapter 13: Planet Beautiful and Ugly-Ass Backpack

## Summary
At Mauve Line Station 281, the party retreats to a J.CO Donuts and Coffee safe room run by [[nodd]], a Bopca NPC. Zev messages Carl and Donut, booking them for a segment of the popular show *Planet Beautiful* in two days. They will narrate a segment on Earth's beauty pageants and pet shows.

Carl buys an **Armorer's Table** and upgrades his **Sapper's Table** to Level 3. Carl reads the Cookbook: Rosetta, Allister, and Forkith discuss using backpacks to stabilize volatile explosives, and Drakea outlines pure hatred for the Naga.

Carl crafts the **Ugly-Ass Backpack** from dingo hides. He loads it with 1.25 tons of metal scrap and metal rods. Katia transforms into a 6-legged slug to support the massive weight, then equips the backpack. The backpack's mass transfers to her body, enabling her to grow into an 11-foot-tall, metal-sheathed Hekla-form with giant spikes for legs and gorilla arms. She can unequip the backpack to her inventory to instantly return to normal size. This "backpack tank" build grants her a huge strength bonus.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The backpack exploit is a brilliant synthesis of game mechanics and physical properties. It allows Katia to bypass the normal restrictions on doppelganger mass, turning her into a devastating physical shield at the cost of intense physical pain during transformations.
