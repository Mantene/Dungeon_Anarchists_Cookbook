# Book 2 Chapter 16: Alley Ambush

## Summary
[[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] visit the Silk Road market inside the club. They meet [[pustule]], an eccentric Level-30 hobgoblin explosives merchant who runs *Hobgobs and Boom Sticks*. Donut negotiates the price of dynamite and fused **Hob-lobber** bombs down to 1,000 gold. Carl also checks the value of his custom *Boom Jugs*, which Pustule offers to buy for 3,750 gold each. 

They leave the club after dark. On their way back to the safe room, [[mongo]] suddenly screeches and sprints into a dark side alley. Following him, Carl and Donut discover the corpse of [[gum-gum]] the orc, whose chest has been torn open and blood drained. 

They are confronted by a group of three **Krasue** (vampiric, flying female heads trailing bloody organs) and a tall city elf named [[vincente]]. Vincente claims he has trained his whole life to destroy them and begins casting a spell. Carl kicks him in the groin while Donut fires a *Magic Missile* at his neck, blowing his head off instantly. The krasue heads flee from Donut's light.

## Characters Introduced
- [[pustule]] — Hobgoblin explosives merchant.
- [[vincente]] — Level-16 City Elf magic user (killed).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival]]**: Immediate physical hazards in the dark streets and custom combat tactics.
- **[[over-city-mechanics]]**: Introduction of the Krasue (undead vampire mobs that only take damage from magic and must have their physical bodies destroyed to die).

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The sudden escalation of the quest is a stark reminder of the dungeon's cruelty. The contrast between Pustule's comical, gross merchant antics and the horrific mutilation of GumGum showcases the series' signature shift from humor to horror.
