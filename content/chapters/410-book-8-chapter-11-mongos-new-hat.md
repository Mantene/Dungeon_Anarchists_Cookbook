# Chapter 410: Chapter 11 — Mongo's New Hat

## Summary
Donut continues to complain about the bootleg, unlicensed makeup palettes recovered from Rishi's trash pile, specifically the color "Neon Blue." [[samantha]] emerges from the garbage wearing a bright, glowing red lipstick called "Pussy Lips" and keeps it.

Carl hands the backup white cleaner bot (looted from Architect Houston's operating theater) to [[mordecai]], who resets it to help clear out the mountain of Rishi's possessions. The safe room's main cleaner bot is highly suspicious of the new bot. Carl tells Donut they need to clean up and prepare for the next race.

Later, Carl and Donut return to the garage. They find [[splash-zone]], [[dong-quixote]], Mongo, and [[ren]] sitting in a circle watching [[hedy]] perform balance tricks with a massive wrench. A crew of strange, mute helper-gremlins are crawling over the food truck performing repairs; Hedy explains they aren't real gremlins and only appear briefly after races.

Dong Quixote helps place the Egyptian pharaoh hat (Nemes of Pestilence) on Mongo's head, which makes him look ridiculous. Donut prepares to give Rend his Rambo-style headband. Hedy advises Carl to learn about the other racing teams to know who they can trust and who will try to blow them off the road. Carl flatly replies that they do not trust anyone.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The scene in the garage offers a warm, comically domestic moment before the next race. Mongo wearing a cheap costume pharaoh hat and Rend wearing a Rambo headband provides levity, contrasting with the dark reality of the upcoming heats and Carl's grim resolution not to trust anyone.
