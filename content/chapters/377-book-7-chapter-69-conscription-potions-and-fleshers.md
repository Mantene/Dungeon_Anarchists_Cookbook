# Chapter 377: Conscription Potions and Fleshers

## Summary

Carl observes the massive battle formation ball from outside with [[dong-quixote]] and [[ren]]. A dark shape—a flesher—drops from the ball as a woman is ejected. [[baroness-victory]] identifies her as Nami, the influencer who had been driving the goddess [[eris]]. Someone has removed Eris's soul armor, ejecting the driver. Carl realizes war mages have arrived via meteor-like long-range jump spells with the purpose of conscripting gods into their army using Hell-Kissed Conscription Potions.

[[samantha]] reports that a war mage successfully used a conscription potion on the god Yarilo, though the war mage died in the process. The war mages traded the Gate of the Feral Gods with a minion at the Midnight Market in exchange for five of these potions.

Carl orders [[princess-donut]] to stop breaking the ball apart, fearing that a conscripted god could destroy them all. He heals the ball instead and starts retreating. [[li-na]] reports she installed her brother [[li-jun]]'s eye into herself and stopped a mage from reaching Harpocrates.

[[fopsy]] theorizes that the residual controlling the war mage team is trying to stop the ascendency game as soon as possible, believing the local AI is a threat to the Eulogist. The ball begins moving toward Larracos as dozens of fleshers start scuttling toward Carl's position, forcing a hasty retreat.

- **Conscription of Gods** — War mages trade the Gate of the Feral Gods for potions that can conscript deities, a major escalation
- **The AI Threat** — Fopsy reveals the residual may view the spreading AI as a threat to the Eulogist, motivating the war mages' actions
- **Sacrifice and Escalation** — War mages die casting the conscription potions, showing the desperation of the conflict

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections

This chapter marks a pivotal shift in the Faction Wars as the war mages reveal their true strategy—conscripting gods rather than simply fighting them. The revelation that the residual is trying to shut down the ascendency game because of the AI's spread adds a new layer of complexity to the political machinations surrounding the crawl. Carl's decision to heal the ball rather than break it shows his strategic thinking under pressure.
