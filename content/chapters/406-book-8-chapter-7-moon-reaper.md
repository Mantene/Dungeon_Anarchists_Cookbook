# Chapter 406: Chapter 7 — Moon Reaper

## Summary
Carl and Donut enter the safe room, where the **Blood Bar** is in effect, limiting safe room time to 10 hours unless refilled by killing mobs. 

[[mordecai]] is currently in the form of a Level 50 **Moon Reaper** (a terrifying, spirit-based janitor mob from Floor 15). When different races approach him for the first time, Mordecai glitches, his hood falls back to reveal a giant flaming eyeball, and he delivers eerie, apocalyptic prophecies:
- **To Carl**: Warns that death awaits him and that he is being pulled by both "the tree" and "the abyss."
- **To Donut**: Predicts a dark decision involving a sacrifice of many lives/souls, addressing her as the "Oak Fell Champion of Nekhebit."
- **To others**: He previously told [[bigs]] she would "drown in fire," made [[samantha]] cry, and told Splash Zone he would "die screaming while his family watched."

After snapping out of the trance, Mordecai glitches through several shapes (his Canadian guide form, a tuxedo cat, an otter, and a giant slug) before stabilizing back into the reaper. He explains that his true race is a **shadow mimic** (highly intelligent mimics that eat changelings), and the combination of shadow mimic traits and the Moon Reaper form is causing the glitches and prophecies, which he dismisses as non-literal system programming.

The safe room mailbox delivers a slimy, writhing spell book for **Oozy Form** (a defensive/camouflage slime-shifting spell). Donut refuses to learn it, preferring her new **Smoke Form** spell (acquired from her Assassin of Sekhmet class). Carl learns the **Oozy Form** spell instead. Donut also notes she has acquired a healing aura called **Healer's Grasp**.

## Characters
- [[carl]] — Learns the Oozy Form spell and receives a cryptic prophecy from Mordecai.
- [[princess-donut]] — Reluctant to learn the slime spell and shows off her new class spells and aura.
- [[mordecai]] — Glitches in his Moon Reaper form, delivering unsettling prophecies to the safe room occupants.
- [[bigs]] — A sluggalette NPC who complains about Mordecai's fire prophecy.
- [[rosetta]] — Warns that Mordecai's glitching triggers whenever a new race gets close to him.

## Themes & Plot Threads

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The Moon Reaper's prophecies, though dismissed by Mordecai as system-generated flavor text, align closely with major thematic conflicts, specifically the struggle between "the tree" (the World Tree/Apotheosis) and "the abyss" (Scolopendra/Nothing). Donut's classification as the "Oak Fell Champion" further ties her to these primordial forces.
