# Book 5 Chapter 1: Agent Provocateur

## Summary
Carl faces Syndicate Liaison Orren to deal with the Gate of the Feral Gods and selects his Compensated Anarchist class specialty before entering Floor 6.
- **The Liaison Meeting**: Carl and Katia meet with [[orren]] in his office. Orren notes that Carl's flooding of Floor 9 has caused major Syndicate financial distress but also profit. Since the System AI forbids confiscating the Gate of the Feral Gods, Orren presents three options: 1) block portal access across Floor 6 (killing thousands of crawlers); 2) accelerate Carl to Floor 6 start (killing Carl and those nearby); or 3) Carl voluntarily surrenders the Gate winding box in exchange for a receipt, to be returned on Floor 9. Carl demands a lawyer, halting the coercion.
- **Entering Floor 6**: The system announces the start of Floor 6 ("The Hunting Grounds"), featuring 360 sequestered hunters boosted to Level 50. Carl meets [[ian]] (a Bush Elf arena attendant and former crawler) in the staging area. Ian warns Carl to stay on the trails, avoid Zockau, and beware the mantis hunter [[vrah]].
- **Class Specialty Selection**: Carl chooses the **Agent Provocateur** specialty for his Compensated Anarchist class, prioritizing explosives, bomb-making, and an advanced Bomb Maker's Workshop over direct melee/strength upgrades. Katia chooses the HAZMAT endorsement, and Donut chooses the Legendary Diva bard subclass.

## Characters Introduced
- [[orren]] — Independent Syndicate Liaison (Valtay gondii parasite).
- [[ian]] — Level 30 Bush Elf Registration Attendant and former crawler.
- [[vrah]] — Level 70 Mantis hunter and leader of the Dark Hive (mentioned).
- [[ajib]] — Level 29 Prodromoi human crawler missing half of his left leg (minor).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[systemic-oppression]]**: The Syndicate attempts to bypass system rules to delete Carl's gate artifact, threatening crawler lives if Carl refuses to cooperate.
- **[[carls-class-selection]]**: Carl specializes as an Agent Provocateur, focusing on massive-casualty explosives and saboteur mechanics.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's choice of Agent Provocateur over the direct strength/melee option recommended by Mordecai signals his intent to fight the dungeon on a systemic scale. He values the capacity to build massive, customized explosives over immediate individual combat superiority, setting up a war of attrition against the hunters.
