# Chapter 413: Chapter 14 — Jurgen's Mystery Meat

## Summary
Carl and Donut enter the **Hungry Eyes Vendor Village**, where safe room rules do not apply, and ingestion/poisoning rules are suspended. 

Carl ponders the mechanics of the 11th-floor Arena. By choosing the audience vote for a killed mob's fate, crawlers can send the mob or a "stand-in" (including people outside the dungeon ecosystem, like residents of the 18th-floor resort) to the Arena. Carl considers which high-profile Syndicate officials (like Harbinger or Prime Minister Victory) they might be able to drag down to the Arena to cause maximum chaos.

Three Bactrian camel racers push past them into the town, which features a 1980s neon-infused cyberpunk aesthetic. Carl notes the local Club Vanquisher (which he is permanently banned from) and spots a blank storefront with a notification: **C&W&U Location. Illegal Modifications and More. Grand Opening Soon.**

They head down a street of bars, passing **The Hairpin** (which houses a Desperado Club entrance in the back) to go to **The Lollipop**, which has a karaoke sign. 

Outside the Lollipop, they meet [[prepotente]] and [[jurgen]], who are now racing partners. Prepotente is angry because their biological mount (**Sweety**, a slow, tapir-like mammal called a **Perriso**) is slow, and Jurgen has decided to buy street-vendor mystery meat on a stick from a Draconian, believing his intuition that it will buff the mount's speed. Jurgen boasts about his iron stomach, only to immediately begin violently vomiting the mystery meat onto the sidewalk.

## Characters
- [[carl]] — Enters the town and contemplates using the Arena rules to target high-ranking Syndicate figures.
- [[princess-donut]] — Purchases a gaudy yellow-and-green trucker hat that says "I'd Hang a Little with Chad" for her mood wall.
- [[prepotente]] — Upset about their slow Perriso mount and Jurgen's reckless eating.
- [[jurgen]] — Asserts his intuition and eats street meat, instantly getting sick.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- [[the-collapse-of-rules]] — The lack of safe room protections in the town square and the suspension of food safety rules emphasize the raw danger of the Vendor Village.
- [[crawler-resistance]] — Carl's plan to drag Syndicate elites like Harbinger into the 11th-floor Arena represents a new avenue of asymmetrical warfare.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Jurgen's instant sickness serves as a humorous warning about the rules of the Vendor Village. Carl's strategic thinking about the Arena shows that he is always looking for ways to drag the people running the crawl down into the mud with them, transforming a game mechanic into a targeted execution tool.
