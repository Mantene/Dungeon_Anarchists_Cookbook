# Book 1 Chapter 37: The Parade Float

## Summary
[[mordecai]] clears his room of the grubs. He explains that the dungeon's waste disposal system spawns 1 to 15 Brindle Grubs whenever a corpse is created to prevent camping. [[carl]] maps the area and realizes they have reached the quadrant limit of **5,000 grubs**.

Carl coordinates with [[brandon-an]] via chat to rendezvous. Carl and Donut carve a path through the harmless but thick level 2 grubs, trying not to step on them (which causes their peers to cannibalize them). 

At the rendezvous, they find the Meadow Lark caretakers have built a massive, wheelchair-based "parade float" train (10 cars long, carrying 39 people) to transport the residents. [[imani-c]] and [[chris-andrews-2]] wear pig harnesses to pull the train, connected by a 300-foot magical, light, and unbreakable chain (**Yog's Special Chain**). Brandon reveals they trained by defeating a giant blimp boss in a warehouse, reaching Level 9 (with Yolanda also reaching Level 9).

As they escort the train, Carl walks beside [[elle-mcgibbons]] (ID #12,330,800), who is 99 years old. Elle suffers from confusion, mistaking Carl for her late husband Barry and making suggestive jokes about touching her boobs. Donut sits on her lap. Jack (a crude Bengals fan) makes a vulgar joke.

Suddenly, [[zev]] pings Carl, warning him that the "save-the-elderly" storyline is trending well, but 20% of focus groups view them as "dead weight." Her message is cut short as she screams, "Run to the safe room now!"

Carl turns to see Jack standing off the train, pants at his ankles, urinating directly onto the dungeon wall. The urine stain instantly begins to sizzle and boil.
