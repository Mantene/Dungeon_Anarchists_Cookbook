# Book 5 Chapter 26: Goddess of Unrequited Love

## Summary
- **Goddess of Unrequited Love**: Donut reveals [[samantha]] is the Goddess of Unrequited Love (or crazy ex-girlfriends) in the Nothing. Samantha wants to recover her body to locate her sand-ooze child on Floor 9 and then find her former lover, a king.
- **The Castle Assault**: Signet's team moves into the water toward the castle. Edgar, the beavers, and paper monsters go under. Areson, Nadine, and a few elves stay on the shore with Carl and Donut.
- **Cursed Background**: Areson explains that High Elves assisted the usurpers in killing the tsar, and now the naiads are basically slaves to the High Elves. He notes Signet's curse (water shying away from her) was cast by a High Elf.
- **Underwater Battle**: A lightning bolt shoots out of the river from the submerged battle. Dead bodies float to the surface (headless naiads, paper cutouts, and a mangled bush elf).
- **The Trap**: Samantha reports in chat that she is inside the castle, but it was a trap. Carl equips his water-breathing ring (+gills on neck, -1 Cha).
- **Explosion & Pursuit**: An explosion blows out the stained-glass window. A short, hairy body floats up (one of the were-beavers). Nadine (the caterpillar) loses her mind and charges down the hill to the water. Carl pulls his xistera extension into his inventory, preparing to dive in to rescue them.

## Characters Introduced
No new characters are introduced (Samantha's deity portfolio is revealed).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[vengeance-of-the-daughter]]**: The assault on Fort Freedom runs into a trap.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Samantha's identity as the "Goddess of Unrequited Love" (or crazy ex-girlfriends) perfectly explains her obsessive, unhinged behavior and her drive to find her "king." The detail that the High Elves orchestrated the coup that ruined the Naiad Confederacy reinforces the theme of high-elf manipulation on this floor. Carl's decision to dive into the water with gills shows his willingness to abandon his safe shore post to protect his companions.
