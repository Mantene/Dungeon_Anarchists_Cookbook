# Book 5 Chapter 74: The Final Cage Match

## Summary
- **Four-Way Battle**: The AI initiates a \"Cage Fight Extreme\" between the crawlers, the remaining hunters, Queen [[imogen]], and the weakened [[diwata]].

- **Imogen's Defeat**: Queen Imogen and [[ferdinand]] are defeated, completing the quest *Vengeance of the Daughter*.
