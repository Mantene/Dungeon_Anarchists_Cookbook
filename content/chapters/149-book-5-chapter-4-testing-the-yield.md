# Book 5 Chapter 4: Testing the Yield

## Summary
Carl tests the capabilities of his new Advanced Bomber's Studio, Donut struggles with her tone-deaf singing requirements, and they launch a surprise offensive.
- **Departures & Shopping**: Katia and her group leave for the south. Donut and Mordecai go shopping but fail to find a *Shield* spellbook or a *Golden Throat* autotune item. Donut's Legendary Diva spells require her to sing in key to be effective; without the Golden Throat, her spells remain weak. She practices support spells (*Standing Ovation*, *Entourage*, *Encore*) and learns the scroll *Laundry Day*.
- **Bomber's Studio**: Carl explores his new workshop. He uses the virtual testing environment to copy and simulate the *Carl's Doomsday Scenario* bomb. He slices hobgoblin dynamite into twenty pieces to engineer new IEDs.
- **Preemptive Ambush Plan**: Carl decides to strike the sequestered hunters early before they are released in 21 hours. He has Bomo cast *Teleport to Stairwell* to send them to the capital city of Zockau.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[the-anarchist-cookbook]]**: Carl's use of the Advanced Bomber's Studio to simulate the Doomsday bomb represents his preparation for using massive, rule-breaking explosive weapons.
- **[[survival-of-the-collapse]]**: Donut's class progression and the need for the "Golden Throat" highlight the tactical reliance on specific magical gear to make crawler classes viable.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The Advanced Bomber's Studio is a major mechanical upgrade. By providing a sandbox to test explosive yields and environmental dynamics, it allows Carl to minimize the risk of his customized creations failing or causing self-harm, turning him into a highly methodical, scientific saboteur.
