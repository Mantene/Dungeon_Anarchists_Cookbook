# Chapter 376: Rosetta Special — The Ninth Edition

## Summary
Adjutant [[baroness-victory]] confirms that the stolen funds have reached their destination, allowing Porthus's people to purchase two Naphil-class generation ships. In exchange, the Open Intellect Pacifist Network's ship, *Homecoming Queen*, will shoot down the yacht containing the noble orc children, leaving [[princess-formidable]] or [[the-maestro]] in charge of the Skull Empire. 

Rosetta reflects on Porthus's plan: the families of the crawlers will board the ships and escape to the Plenty system, trading one ship to the "goats" for passage through the forbidden gate at Impetus Point, reaching the furthest edge of the galaxy until the wars settle.

Rosetta recalls Porthus's interactions with the Apothecary (the collective mind). Rosetta distrusts her, noting that the dungeon portrays her as a mind-controlling tentacle monster, but Porthus insisted she has a good reason for helping and prefers to be called "her." Porthus had kept her motives secret from Rosetta so the local AI—and thus the Eulogist—would not learn them.

Rosetta remembers cycles ago when she and her comrade [[brooks]] were Castle Guards. After their crawl, the orcs of the Skull Empire mocked the crawlers, and Brooks tried to stop them by casting spells at the portal screen. Warlord Rust (later King Rust) lassoed Brooks and pulled him into the portal glass, cutting him in half. Rosetta avenged Brooks by decapitating King Rust with his own whip.

In the chat, [[tipid]] and [[justice-light]] discuss their next move. Justice Light urges them to retrieve the Gate and fight the war mages. Rosetta is ready to accept death, believing they have already sent their message to the universe. 

Rosetta wonders why [[agatha]] is leading the war mages. She realizes Agatha is a "residual" of the more rare kind, aligned with the Nebulars who worshiped the Eulogist, making her an enemy of the Apothecary. 

Rosetta chooses to continue fighting to support her comrades. The chapter ends with Rosetta's character card, identifying her as a Crest Gazetteer, author of the ninth edition of the Cookbook, and host of the documentary series *Shadow Boxer*.

## Characters
* [[rosetta]]: Theater Commander and author of the ninth edition of the Cookbook. She reflects on Porthus's plan, her revenge against King Rust, and her distrust of the Apothecary.
* [[baroness-victory]]: Confirms the shipyard transaction and the upcoming strike on the orc yacht.
* [[porthus]]: Rosetta's close companion and mastermind of the shipyard heist and escape plan.
* [[brooks]]: Rosetta's deceased former comrade, cut in half by King Rust.
* [[menerva]]: A missing crawler from Porthus's crawl whom Porthus is obsessed with finding.
* [[agatha]]: Warlord of the War Mage Rebellion, identified as a residual aligned with the Nebulars/Eulogist.
* [[justice-light]]: Urges the team to reclaim the Gate and fight.
* [[tipid]]: Coordinates with the group from the FUPA.

## Themes & Plot Threads
* **The Apothecary vs. The Residuals**: The ideological split between the collective mind (the Apothecary, who wants to collapse the Syndicate) and the Nebular-aligned residuals (like Agatha, who worship the Eulogist).
* **The Cookbook Lineage**: Rosetta is identified as the author of the ninth edition of the [[the-anarchist-cookbook]].
* **The Ultimate Revenge**: Rosetta's execution of King Rust is revealed as a long-awaited act of vengeance for the murder of Brooks.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Rosetta's reflections expand the scope of the novel beyond the dungeon walls. The heist of the Syndicate's funds to buy generation ships shows that the rebellion is happening both inside the crawl and in the wider galaxy. Rosetta's cynical love for her comrades shows that despite the system's attempts to harden them, the crawlers retain their humanity by choosing to fight for one another.
