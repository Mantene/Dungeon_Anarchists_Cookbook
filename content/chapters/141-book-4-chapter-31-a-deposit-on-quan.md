# Book 4 Chapter 31: A Deposit on Quan

## Summary
Carl and Donut defeat Quan Ch, summon Emberus to save Orthrus, and Carl rips Quan's left arm off when he tries to escape.
- **Biplane Combat**: Quan Ch refuses to back off. Carl and Donut attack him: Carl throws a hobgoblin disco ball to stick to his wisp-shield, and Donut fires the machine gun and magic missiles, followed by Carl remote-detonating a hob-lobber to knock him out of the sky.
- **Worshipping Emberus**: Orthrus is rendered unconscious at 2% health. With other crawlers trying to kill the puppy and Quan returning, Carl chooses to worship Emberus to invoke *Divine Intervention* (invulnerability for 60s) and throws the celestial grenade at Quan Ch, summoning Emberus. The biplane *Nightmare II* crashes into Emberus's face and explodes, but the invulnerability saves Carl. Emberus finds Orthrus and returns to the Celestial Halls, completing the quest.
- **Ripping Quan's Arm Off**: Carl and Donut land in the land quadrant of the Soulless Prophet. Quan Ch attacks Carl. Carl, still invulnerable, tackles him and punches his face. When the buff expires, Quan Ch tries to escape using a rocket-flight ability, but Carl grabs his glowing left arm and rips it entirely off at the shoulder. Quan Ch flees to the stairwell missing his arm and three rings (which Carl loots).
- **Sun Tattoos**: Carl receives two sun tattoos, one on each hand, reflecting his worship of Emberus. Donut calls Carl "crazy, crazy, straitjacket crazy."

## Themes & Plot Threads
- [[the-gate-of-the-feral-gods]]: The puppy quest is completed, and Carl is marked as an adherent of Emberus.
- [[systemic-oppression]]: Carl's rage against Quan Ch reflects his hatred for bullies who act selfishly in a system designed to force crawlers into compliance.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's violent disarming of Quan Ch is a major milestone: he directly defeats one of the top, most heavily geared crawlers in the dungeon through sheer grit and tactical exploitation of invulnerability. It sends a message to the entire system that Carl is not to be trifled with.
