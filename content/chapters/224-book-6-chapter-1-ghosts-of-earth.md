# Book 6 Chapter 1: Ghosts of Earth

## Summary
Crawlers arrive on Floor 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth") and receive the floor rules and mechanical constraints.
- **Floor Inception**: The eighth floor begins with 38,532 remaining crawlers. Carl receives a Platinum Venison Box for his actions on Floor 6.
- **Donut's Class Selection**: Donut selects the **Master Telephone Psychic** class (rejecting the Agony Urchin class). She retains her bard skills and Venom Spitter level 3, and obtains new spells: *Brain Freeze*, *Bad Attitude*, and *Why Are You Hitting Yourself?*, alongside a major mapping upgrade.
- **Crawler Check-In**: Crawlers verify their starting locations via chat. Carl, Donut, and manager Mordecai are in El Capitolio in Havana, Cuba. Imani and Elle are at Great Wolf Lodge in the western US; Katia is in Iceland; Li Na and Chris are in Beijing; Florin is in Ecuador. Prepotente, who triggered the floor skip, is missing and suspected to be in the Bahamas.
- **Floor 8 Rules**: The system details Floor 8 mechanics:
  - Phase 1 lasts 14 days, trapping crawlers in their starting region.
  - The floor replicates Earth on December 10th. Biological entities are intangible ghosts, but physical items have mass and momentum.
  - Looted Earth items turn to dust upon level collapse.
  - Crawlers are locked into squads of up to 5, with empty slots to be filled by capturing regional folklore mobs at <5% health using 20 provided flags.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[ghosts-of-earth]]**: Introduction of Floor 8 environment and rules.
- **[[systemic-oppression]]**: The AI turns the crawlers' home planet into a "playground" memory where they fight folklore monsters.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The concept of fighting within a memory of their home planet is deeply psychological. Seeing the ghostly avatars of people going about their lives right before the collapse serves as a haunting reminder of what the crawlers have lost.
