# Book 4 Chapter 29: Where the Red God Glows

## Summary
The crawlers activate a Group Quest to protect the fire god's puppy Orthrus, and the remaining Subterranean Quadrant crawlers sacrifice themselves to pop their bubble.
- **Orthrus & The Quest**: Carl, Katia, Gwen, and Tran go outside and spot the Level 10 two-headed hell-hound puppy **Orthrus**, who is massive but fragile. A Group Quest **Where the Red God Glows** begins: they must protect the puppy from lightning and concierge sharks. If he dies, Emberus will melt bubble 543.
- **Quest Chat & Muting**: The Quest Chat room reveals the status of the remaining Subterranean crawlers: Low Thi, Morris Sp, and Bobby (all possessed), Maggie My (enslaved), and Chris Andrews 2 (enslaved). Donut berates Maggie in chat. Chris warns that Maggie has outside help and is cheating, but he is immediately muted by the system.
- **Subterranean Deaths**: Bobby is crushed. Low Thi acts as a decoy and is killed by Quetzalcoatlus. Morris Sp reaches the submerged electrical panel, connects it to the lightning tower, and is killed immediately after.
- **Popping the Bubble**: The lightning tower is struck, electrifying the water and vaporizing Quetzalcoatlus. The Subterranean Quadrant is liberated, popping Bubble 543 and opening all stairwells.
- **Saving Orthrus**: The lightning stops, but sharks are still biting Orthrus. Donut heals the puppy with *Heal Critter*. Carl asks Donut if she still has the *Meat Hooks* scroll from Floor 3.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The total destruction of the Subterranean Quadrant crawlers is a stark reminder of the dungeon's cruelty. Their coordinated sacrifice, guided by Carl's instructions, allows the rest of the crawlers to survive and progress, representing the core value of crawler solidarity.
