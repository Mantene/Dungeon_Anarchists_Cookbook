# Chapter 273: Residual Paulie and Lightning Lou

## Summary
- **Lightning Lou**: On his way north, Carl stops at a gas station and finds a sleeping ghost bloodhound named Lightning Lou, accidentally dislodging its collar.
- **The Message**: At 'The Shepherd's House' shelter in Homestead, Carl finds a Syndicate Standard spray-painted message: '*Look for me at breakfast every morning. I wear the red hat with the flaps. Coffee then speech*.'
- **Paulie the Residual**: Carl waits until 7 AM and approaches Paulie, an elderly Asian man in a red trapper hat. The man looks directly at Carl and says: 'Nothing like coffee to wake your ass up. Hello, crawler.'
