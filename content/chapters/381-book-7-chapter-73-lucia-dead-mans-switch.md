# Chapter 381: Chapter 73 — Lucia's Dead Man's Switch

## Summary
Carl reflects on the definition of an "Atrocity" and Donut's *War Crime* spell (looted from the Legendary 1914 box). Zev contacts Carl to explain the glitch surrounding [[lucia-mar]]. The AI will not allow her to be administratively extracted, and her mind is linked to approximately 120,000 human children trapped on the surface in the kinder facility. If Lucia dies, they all die, effectively making her a psychotic dead man's switch. 

Meanwhile, Harpocrates beats the god Yarilo to death with a mace-like ball, which angers the gods and strips the Scavenger's Daughter's benefit from Yarilo. Carl runs up the Nest tower to reunite with Donut, who has reached level 74 and level 15 Magic Missile by sniping Larracos's defense towers. Rosetta, Milk, and [[edgar]] arrive at the top of the tower; Edgar has brought his tattoo kit to tattoo a new level 8+ spell on Donut so she can combine it with *War Crime*.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **The Surface Connection**: The reveal that children who failed to enter the dungeon are trapped in a surface facility and linked to Lucia's survival.
- **The AI's Intentions**: Zev mentions the "Eulogist" is the AI in the center system, hinting at the AI's core nature.
- **Moral Cost of War**: Carl's horror over the *War Crime* spell and the necessity of atrocities for survival.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The chapter heightens the stakes by showing that survival in Faction Wars requires committing horrific acts (Donut's *War Crime*). The revelation that Lucia is a dead man's switch for 120,000 children adds a tragic layer of complexity, transforming her from a simple antagonist into a hostage of the system's design.
