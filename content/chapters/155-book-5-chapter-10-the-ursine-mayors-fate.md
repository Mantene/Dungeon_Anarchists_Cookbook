# Book 5 Chapter 10: The Ursine Mayor's Fate

## Summary
Carl and Donut reach the Large Ursine Settlement, accidentally execute the hypocritical Mayor Elmer, and receive a direct warning from the mantis leader Vrah.
- **Entering the Ursine settlement**: Carl and Donut travel south, meeting the bugbear Goiter briefly before reaching the covered bridge to the [[large-ursine-settlement]]. The town has strict moral rules, including a ban on all dancing, magic, and dinosaurs.
- **Elmer's Execution Offer**: Mayor [[elmer]] (a Level 40 Ursine) refuses to let Mongo enter and demands a 150 gold fee to execute Donut and Mongo. When Elmer steps forward, Donut casts *Astral Paw* to push him down the steep river slope. Elmer tumbles, hits his head on the rocks, and dies. A Level 80+ Naiad drags his corpse under the water.
- **The Takeover**: Since Donut's spell caused his death, she is named the Grand (Mayor) of the settlement. The guards turn hostile, forcing Carl and Donut to flee into the saferoom *The Galleon's Lap*.
- **Vrah's Warning**: Preparing to launch the Zockau bomb, Carl finds a note pinned to their personal space door by [[vrah]]. Vrah warns that all hunters are now protected from explosions, and she ordered the Zockau guards to worship [[emberus]] (Carl's god) so Carl will be smote if he bombs them. Vrah vows to capture, torture, and decapitate Carl.
- **The Hunt Begins**: The system announces the gates are down and the hunters are loose.

## Characters Introduced
- [[elmer]] — Level 40 Ursine and Mayor of the Large Ursine Settlement, deceased.
- [[goiter]] — Level 42 Bugbear council member.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[the-butchers-masquerade]]**: The official release of the hunters (the countdown hits zero) initiates the hunting season on Floor 6.
- **[[systemic-oppression]]**: The Ursine's moralistic, repressed society and Elmer's attempt to execute Mongo for profit show the corruption of the NPC authorities who cooperate with the system.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The sudden death of Elmer and Donut's ascension to Mayor of the Large Ursine Settlement is a brilliant, accidental twist. By taking control of the city, Donut can rewrite its moralistic rules, converting a highly repressed zone into a strategic, crawler-friendly fortress. Vrah's warning note shows the hunters are highly intelligent and capable of utilizing game exploits defensively, using Carl's own religious adherence to Emberus as leverage to block his bomb deployment in Zockau.
