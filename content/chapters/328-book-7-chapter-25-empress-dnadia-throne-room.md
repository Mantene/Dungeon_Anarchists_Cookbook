# Chapter 328: Empress D'Nadia's Throne Room

## Summary

[[carl]] awakens in the trench after a brief 25-second knockout caused by a Soul Poisoning debuff named **Post Nut Clarity**, triggered by his use of Daughter’s Kiss. 

He learns that the ground assault was highly successful. The NPC team (Team Retribution) pulled off a devastating exploit: they filled the middle fallback trench with water using a scroll and had a porsuk bartender NPC use a chemical-alteration spell to turn the water into acid, hiding it under an illusion. The fleeing enemy forces jumped blindly into the acid and dissolved, leveling up the bartender NPC twenty times.

Boomer takes command of the ground stalemate. Carl, Donut, and Mongo jog to the rendezvous and board the *Party Planner* airship. Carl is displeased to find that Donut has allowed children into battle—[[bonnie]] is operating a door ballista, and [[skarn]] is using his shapeshifting to disguise himself as a thin adult named Gangue to act as a scout.

The airship flies 30 miles low and fast to the Prism faction headquarters (a small warehouse). The castle is virtually unguarded. Carl and Donut kill the three external sentries, bypass their traps, and enter the headquarters. 

Baroness Victory teleports in, looking shaken by the horrors of the acid trench massacre. Carl detects three guards and the Prism faction leader, Empress [[princess-dnadia]] (a female Saccathian), in the throne room. Carl puts on the **Ring of Divine Suffering** and steps forward to confront her.

## Characters

- **[[carl]]** — Recovers from the Post Nut Clarity debuff; boards the airship and infiltrates the Prism castle, equipping the Ring of Divine Suffering.
- **[[princess-donut]]** — Sneaks into the castle with Carl; defended her decision to let Bonnie and Skarn onto the airship.
- **[[boomer]]** — Assumes command of the ground trenches to hold the enemy in a stalemate.
- **[[bonnie]]** — Operates a door ballista on the *Party Planner* wearing an oversized Dallas Cowboys jersey.
- **[[skarn]]** — Shapeshifts into a thin adult named Gangue to serve as the airship's scout.
- **[[princess-dnadia]]** — Saccathian Empress of the Prism; waits in the throne room (mentioned).
- **[[baroness-victory]]** — Teleports in; expresses disgust and shock at the brutal acid trench exploit.

## Themes & Plot Threads

- **Trench Exploits** — Chemical alteration spells combined with illusion spells turn standard utility water into a devastating defensive trap.
- **Children in Combat** — Carl struggles with the reality that children like Bonnie and Skarn are actively fighting on the front lines.
- **Post Nut Clarity Debuff** — A dangerous side effect of Soul Poisoning that knocks Carl unconscious for 25 seconds after using the Daughter’s Kiss skill.
- **Ring of Divine Suffering** — Carl equips a powerful accessory to prepare for a confrontation with the Saccathian Empress.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections

This chapter highlights the absolute brutality of the Faction Wars, where even the NPC factions use horrific chemical exploits (acid traps) that shock the seasoned Adjutants. The revelation of the "Post Nut Clarity" debuff is a classic piece of Dinniman's absurd and vulgar game-system humor, but it carries lethal tactical stakes. Carl's confrontation with Empress D'Nadia represents a decisive strike to take down one of the ten active faction leaders early in the conflict.
