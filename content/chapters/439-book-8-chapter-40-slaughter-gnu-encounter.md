# Chapter 439: Chapter 40 — The Slaughter Gnu Encounter

## Summary
- **Throne Grievances**: Carl sits in Dorota (Donut's upgrade throne) in his underwear because his costume flayed off. Dorota complains that she has no purpose without Donut and worries Carl will give Donut "pink eye."
- **Upgrades Selected**: Carl selects the vehicle upgrades:
  1. Gyro stabilizer (prevents flipping).
  2. Bubble Buddy electrified shield (can be upgraded to float).
  3. Resizable all-terrain tires.
- **Race Four Rules**: Zev announces the "survival containment" bubble rules. The bubble is indestructible and protects vehicles/mounts from toxic air. If the vehicle is destroyed or the mount dies, the team loses the bubble but doesn't automatically lose; they can still survive if they cross the finish line before another team. There is a mandatory 2-hour pit stop at the lost jungle city of Upano. The winner gets a key to open a defeated team's garage.
- **One Fine Pig's Wrap**: One Fine Pig APV receives an audience-voted wrap featuring anime Tigrans kissing a fat pig's backside.
- **Team Flamengo**: The new team is Team Flamengo, consisting of **Osvaldo** (Level 72 Curupira, small frame, red hair, backward feet) and **Filipe L** (Level 63 human Strongman). They ride **Bruna**, a six-legged armored beast called a **Slaughter Gnu**, carrying a dwarf mercenary with a crossbow.
- **Coordinate Rejection**: Carl messages Osvaldo, warning him about the other teams and begging him to coordinate. Osvaldo rejects Carl's help, saying he won't attack them unless it's them or him. Donut insults Osvaldo, calling him a "straight-to-video, low-budget leprechaun," and privately tells Carl it is easier if they stay enemies because it is a death match.
