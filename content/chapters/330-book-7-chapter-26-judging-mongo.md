# Chapter 330: Chapter 26 — Judging Mongo

## Summary

[[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] infiltrate the throne room of the Prism faction headquarters. The three external guards have been neutralized; Carl duct-tapes the unconscious samurai rhino NPC and sets [[sir-rendlegore]] to watch him.

Inside, Empress [[princess-dnadia]] (a Saccathian) attempts to defend herself with a magical crossbow. Carl uses non-magical banger spheres to deplete her shields, and Donut summons two clockwork duplicates of [[mongo]] to corner her. 

D'Nadia’s Saccathian Adjutant is [[nihit]] (the reporter Carl throat-stabbed with a pen on early floors). He stands next to [[baroness-victory]] as they watch the confrontation.

D’Nadia deliberately uses a knife to keep her health slightly below 100%, exploiting a mechanic that prevents Carl from using the **Ring of Divine Suffering**. Donut's clockwork Mongos rip off her gown, exposing her green tentacles, and bite off the tentacle holding the knife. Carl immediately casts a Heal scroll on her. Once she is at 100% health, Carl successfully applies the Ring's mark to her highest stat (Intelligence). 

As they wait 30 seconds for the mark to solidify, the clockwork Mongos beat D’Nadia into the red. Carl experiences an intense, blinding rage toward D’Nadia for her hypocrisy: she pretended to be empathetic on television, yet spent fortunes to enter the dungeon and murder crawlers. 

Carl considers using his ultimate powers to execute her, but Donut begs him to calm down and step back. Carl relents. Once the mark forms, Donut steps forward and tells D'Nadia that Mongo is disappointed in how she judged him during the pet show.

## Characters

- **[[carl]]** — Neutralizes the guards; heals and tags D’Nadia with the Ring of Divine Suffering; struggles with extreme, blinding rage.
- **[[princess-donut]]** — Summons clockwork Mongos; calms Carl down and takes the lead to execute their plan.
- **[[princess-dnadia]]** — Saccathian Empress of the Prism; tagged by the Ring of Divine Suffering.
- **[[nihit]]** — Saccathian Adjutant for the Prism; observes the confrontation in silence.
- **[[baroness-victory]]** — Princess Posse Adjutant; stands alongside Nihit.
- **[[mongo]]** — Present with his clockwork triplicates, growling at the Empress.

## Themes & Plot Threads

- **The Ring of Divine Suffering** — Marking a target's highest stat (in this case, Intelligence) to cripple their capabilities.
- **Stat Marking Mechanics** — The target must be at 100% health to be marked, leading to tactical healing.
- **Signet's Legacy** — Carl and Donut confront D’Nadia over her past mockery of the late Tsarina Signet.
- **Emotional Bleeding** — Carl's burning rage highlights his deteriorating mental stability and the heavy toll of the Soul Poisoning.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections

This chapter is a chilling demonstration of the crawlers' cold tactical execution. Healing an enemy just to mark them with a crippling debuff is a terrifyingly pragmatic use of game mechanics. The return of Nihit provides a great link to earlier floors, showing how the lives of the characters remain interconnected. Donut's intervention to pull Carl back from the brink of using his doomsday skills shows that she is increasingly acting as his emotional anchor.
