# Chapter 365: Chapter 59 — Launch of Operation Ruin

## Summary
[[carl]] explains the layout of **Operation Ruin**, which consists of four simultaneous offensives designed to finish Faction Wars within seven hours:
1. [[florin]] attacks the orcs.
2. [[boomer]] attacks the Reavers.
3. [[princess-donut]] defends against the Dream.
4. Carl leads the operation to eliminate the Madness (specifically targeting [[architect-houston]]).

[[britney-proskurina]] acts as the communication dispatcher between the crawlers and [[juice-box]]'s Team Retribution. [[rosetta]] serves as Theater Commander. 

Carl boards the stealth airship *Party Planner*, piloted by [[louis-santiago-2]], to amplify the yield of the bombs during the initial run over orc territory. From there, Louis will drop Carl off in Reaver territory to meet [[elle-mcgibbons]], while [[samantha]] stays behind to support the Reavers' offensive. 

Samantha joins them in the cabin, sporting "victory rolls" styled by [[kiwi]] (the pregnant former dinosaur crawler). Samantha's forehead writing has changed to "Fuck the Cheese Dicks" (which Carl corrects to "cheese sticks"). Donut messages that she has installed a deluxe wireless PA speaker system on the Shanty Town towers, which she intends to use for bard spells and taunts.

Just before launch, Carl discovers that [[bonnie]] (wearing a custom gnomish farseer helmet and a Scream Warriors shirt) and [[skarn]] (copying [[holger]]) have snuck onto the airship's weapon bay. 

The Open Hostilities phase begins with an aggressive announcement from [[cascadia]]. Immediately, seeker missiles fired from Reaver territory target *Party Planner*. Louis dives the ship, and Bonnie uses the upgraded ballista guns to successfully shoot down all three incoming missiles.

Simultaneously, the Dream's heavy magical artillery targets [[tran]]'s Bulwark and Shanty Town. Dream troops mass-teleport directly outside the FUPA, rushing [[tipid]]'s defenses.

In response, Elle opens the first gate, summoning the Feral God **Meatus** near the Dream castle. The feral god instantly summons the deity **Harpocrates** (the god of silence), creating a massive clash of forces. Rosetta orders Britney to abort using the diggers near the silent god.

## Characters
* [[carl]]: Boards the *Party Planner*, discovers the stowaway kids, coordinates defenses, and preps for his jump.
* [[louis-santiago-2]]: Pilots *Party Planner* through seeker missile locks and drops the initial payloads.
* [[samantha]]: Floats in victory rolls, jokes about "cheese dicks," and prepares to assist Boomer.
* [[bonnie]]: Snuck on board to operate the door guns, successfully shooting down three Reaver missiles.
* [[skarn]]: Changeling child who snuck on board alongside Bonnie, mimicking Holger.
* [[princess-donut]]: Messages Carl about the PA system and her flak spells.
* [[kiwi]]: Mentioned as having styled Samantha's hair.
* [[tipid]]: Defends the FUPA against the mass-teleported Dreamers.
* [[rosetta]]: Directs the theatre operations and orders the Scream Warriors' rapid deployment.
* [[britney-proskurina]]: Acts as dispatcher for the various battalions.
* [[cascadia]]: Issues a brief, hostile announcement to kick off the phase.

## Themes & Plot Threads
* **Coordination and Chaos**: Despite meticulous planning, the launch of Operation Ruin is immediately complicated by stowaways, unexpected seeker missiles, and the chaotic summons of gods.
* **The Silence of Harpocrates**: The summoning of Harpocrates introduces a dangerous wildcard that punishes sound, directly threatening the dwarves' noisy digger plans.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The contrast between the child crawlers' eagerness to fight (evidenced by Bonnie's custom shirt and farseer helmet) and Carl's strict "no child soldiers" rule highlights the tragic reality of the crawl. Even when forbidden, the children are forced to defend themselves, showing a chilling proficiency in operating anti-air artillery.
