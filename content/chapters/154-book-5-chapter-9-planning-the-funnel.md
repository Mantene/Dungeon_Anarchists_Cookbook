# Book 5 Chapter 9: Planning the Funnel

## Summary
Carl maps the massive Floor 6 geography, coordinates bridge demolitions to funnel the hunters, and designs a massive thermobaric bomb with Donut's help.
- **The Floor 6 Geography**: The Hunting Grounds covers 100,000 square kilometers (resembling Iceland). A river branch bisects the map from northeast to southwest. Carl has Katia coordinate the crawlers to destroy all bridges except one to funnel the hunters.
- **The Ursine Settlement**: Miriam messages Carl that a large Ursine settlement is located 40 km south, across the river. The main roads are clear of traps due to caravan anti-trap magic.
- **The Bomo Bomb Scheme**: Carl designs a five-foot-tall, high-yield thermobaric bomb using an egg-shaped metal casing and *Nitro Sludge* (an oxygen-amplifier chemical). The system names it **"Just Wait Until Your Daddy Gets Home" - Wheeled Bomb**.
- **Donut's Clockwork Trick**: Donut suggest making Bomo a minion of her royal court, which will allow her to cast *Clockwork Triplicate* on Bomo. A clockwork copy of Bomo will pull the wheeled bomb out of the Zockau saferoom and blow it up, keeping the real Bomo safe inside. They prepare to travel south in the Royal Chariot dune buggy.

- **[[the-butchers-masquerade]]**: The upcoming hunter release (3.5 hours) drives the crawlers' efforts to funnel and ambush the hunters at strategic bottlenecks.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's proposal to use *Clockwork Triplicate* on Bomo to deploy the wheeled bomb is a brilliant tactical integration of her minion-raising abilities. It allows them to execute a devastating suicide-bomb attack in Zockau while protecting their collateral mercenary Bomo from permanent death, showing she is becoming a highly capable strategist in her own right.
