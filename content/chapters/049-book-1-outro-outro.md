# Book 1 Outro: Outro (Book 1)

## Summary
The book concludes with a humorous meta-achievement:
- **New Achievement! You read a book!** (Awarded for completing the first book without throwing it against the wall).
- **Reward**: Wait until January of 2021 before reading the next book, *Carl's Doomsday Scenario*.

The author Matt Dinniman appeals to readers to leave a review on Amazon to help support the series.
