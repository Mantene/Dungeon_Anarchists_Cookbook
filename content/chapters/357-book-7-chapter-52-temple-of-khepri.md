# Chapter 357: Temple of Khepri

## Summary

Carl, Katia, and Prepotente navigate to the Temple of Khepri using the multi-destination portal system. Prepotente helps tune the portal dial, and Rosetta provides the correct address (453). Carl's portal analysis reveals Vinata and several nagas inside, along with what he initially assumes is a Khepri cleric—but is actually the god Khepri himself.

Samantha, who has the "Damned" status from surviving the Nothing, opens the door with her mouth, and all three rush inside. They receive devastating notifications: they've been denied entry, marked for death by Khepri, and—most critically—Vinata has the yellow Immortal tag.

Carl earns the "Charge of the Light Brigade" achievement for walking into an elaborate trap. The AI commentary is unusually sympathetic, even declining to give a reward unless Carl survives. However, a genuine temporary effect from being in Khepri's presence grants Carl 90 seconds of immortality.

A boss battle sequence begins with portraits of both teams. Zhang gets a flashing "2X Jackpot if Zhang Lives!" sign. The AI sets up betting and a 30-second timer. Admiral Corinne Whimpleshin, the Blood Sultanate adjutant, appears and calls foul, but Victory dismisses her—temple rules apply.

Khepri is described as a level 250 god with a bug for a head, a retainer of Adrasteia. His temple is "endless"—a room with one long table covered with 433 kinds of bugs. The chapter ends with the timer hitting zero and battle beginning.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections

The AI's unusual moment of near-sympathy—"Even I'm not a big enough of a prick to give you a reward for this one"—is remarkable. The chapter perfectly escalates tension as each revelation (Vinata's immortality, Khepri's presence, the trap) compounds the danger. Prepotente's quiet litany of names before battle is a touching character moment.
