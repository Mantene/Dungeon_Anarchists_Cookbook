# Chapter 352: Chapter 47 — Mantaur Transformation

## Summary
[[louis-santiago-2|Louis]] reports that the sluggalos are doing donuts in the grass north of the castle, and there is fighting in the south. [[elle-mcgibbons|Elle]], [[imani-c|Imani]], and their team enter the naga castle. Imani has a barbed-wire tattoo on her ankle containing [[carl|Carl's]] level 15 Tripper spell. When she uses it to detonate traps, Carl feels a painful shock in his chest. Donut complains about the "Pamela Anderson" style tattoo, expressing her guilt and anxiety through complaints.

[[katia-grim|Katia]] transforms into a mantaur form resembling the late Eva. [[daniel-bautista-2|Bautista]] is terrified for her and wants her to eat the flower that allows her to leave the floor. Katia hugs Carl and whispers in private that if things go wrong, she will sacrifice herself to give the flower to Donut so Donut can go to the 12th floor. Carl insists Donut would never allow that.

[[samantha|Samantha]] hops around, whispering in Bautista's ear and suggesting they have a go at it to drive their exes mad. Sergeant Toyotomi directs the clocktower construction, while Jun and Zhang trail wires. Prepotente's goat-dragon Bianca is seen landing in the distance.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Katia's willingness to sacrifice herself for Donut highlights her selflessness and the tragic bind the crawlers find themselves in. Bautista's fear for her is tragic, reflecting the trauma of losing loved ones in the dungeon. The physical pain Carl experiences when Imani uses his Tripper spell shows that the toraline yam exploits come with a literal, biological cost, emphasizing that there are no free shortcuts in the dungeon.
