# Book 4 Chapter 7: Total, Utter Failure

## Summary
Carl's group escapes the collapsing City Hall and coordinates with the Subterranean crawlers.
- **Escape from City Hall**: A camel blasts a rocket at the blocked doorway. Carl counterattacks with an eighth-strength hob-lobber, detonating the camels' external explosives and killing them. Donut uses *Hole* on the alley wall, and Carl uses a Grulke Infantry figure to summon a Level 15 frog to distract the guards. Donut puddle jumps the group down the street, and Carl uses an empty whiskey bottle to fake intoxication and blend in.
- **The Collapsed City Hall**: City Hall collapses. Emerald, a Level 30 camel NPC, explains that they must provide the gnomes proof of collateral every day after sunrise, otherwise the gnomes will bomb the town.
- **Sponsorship Updates**: The crawlers receive their second sponsors:
  - Carl: **The Open Intellect Pacifist Action Network, Intergalactic NFC** (tax-free non-profit, pacifist, doesn't support the crawl).
  - Donut: **Veriluxx RealPet Companions** (brings the highest sponsorship bid in series history; receives a product sample she must feature, followed by an infomercial).
  - Katia: **Squim Conglomerate** (mining company, will appear on *Dungeon Sidekicks*).
- **Subterranean Strategy**: At the Desperado Club, protected by a new cretin bodyguard, **Very Sullen**, Carl meets **Morris Sp** (spider-centaur Freelance Psychiatrist) and **Bobby D.J.** (human Spy). They reveal:
  - The Subterranean Quadrant is a trap-heavy maze populated by **Nude Glabers** (undead mole rat creatures) leading to the *Crypt of Anser*.
  - The Subterranean crawlers accidentally killed their collateral NPC (a pig) by feeding it banana seeds. A Bactrian camel knocked their map into the air quadrant before the room blew up.
  - **Quetzalcoatlus**: Emperor Anser's wife, sealed alive in the tomb, now an undead bird-screaming ghost. Henrik and Svern were trying to drug Wynne to force him to cast a temporary physical resurrection spell on her so they could touch her and steal her powers.
  - Katia begins transcribing the Necropolis map for the Subterranean crawlers.
- **Benefactor Boxes**: Carl receives a Bronze Benefactor Box from the Valtay and a Silver Benefactor Box from the Pacifists.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The Dromedarians' desperate reliance on daily "proof of collateral" shows the fragility of their peace with the gnomes. With the collateral (Wynne) now dead, a timer has started on Hump Town's survival. Carl's new sponsor, a pacifist network, highlights the ideological factions watching the crawl from the galaxy, though their non-combat focus may limit their practical aid.
