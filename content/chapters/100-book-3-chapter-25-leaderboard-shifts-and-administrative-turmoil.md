# Book 3 Chapter 25: Leaderboard Shifts and Administrative Turmoil

## Summary
At Station 75, Carl, Donut, and Katia disengage the front engine car. Li Jun takes the main train of 1,000+ survivors back to Station 36 to join Imani's team. The remaining members of Brynhild’s Daughters disperse into the crowd. Carl gathers over 700 conductor hats and 15 colored-line keys from the departing crawlers, planning to drive the engine car to the abyss at the end of the line to distribute them to the 500+ stranded crawlers. Carl receives a humorous achievement, **Mentally Unstable Clothing Hoarder!**, for holding so many identical hats.

The three crawlers explore Station 75, which contains hobgoblin workshops, gnoll armories, and a bar called "The Downward Dog." In the safe room, Katia opens her ten loot boxes:
- She gains the **Club Vanquisher** membership, officially joining Carl and Donut's social lounge party.
- She receives the **Find Crawler** skill (Level 3) from a Gold Savage Box.
- She claims her 500,000 gold bounty and a **Legendary Bounty Box** for killing Hekla.
- She opens a Platinum Slam Master Box, yielding the **Enchanted Wrestling Belt of the Great Gorgo** (+5% Str, +5% Con, and the *Avalanche* benefit, which doubles collision impact force).

They watch the daily recap show, which honors Hekla's history (including how she killed a rat with her husband's rib bone to get her legendary crossbow). The show exposes the truth of Katia and Eva's past: on Floor 3, Eva abandoned Katia during character creation to join Hekla, later lying about why they separated. The recap also portrays Hekla's death as a deliberate act of rage by Katia.

The updated leaderboard is revealed:
1. **Lucia Mar** (Level 35) — 1,000,000 points
2. **Prepotente** (Level 34) — 500,000 points
3. **Carl** (Level 34) — 400,000 points
4. **Donut** (Level 32) — 300,000 points
5. **Quan Ch** (Level 38) — 200,000 points
8. **Katia Grim** (Level 37) — 100,000 points

Zev teleports in, still wearing her deep-diving helmet due to Hinter company core core-rental issues preventing the pocket system transition. She congratulates Katia on the top ten and warns that a hostile Bloom party representative, **Loita** (former representative of Hekla), is trying to take over their accounts. Zev also reveals a massive privacy violation: starting tomorrow, the dungeon will make crawler private chats public to premium intergalactic viewers.

- **Tizquick** — (Already introduced)

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[the-iron-tangle-escape-plan]]**: Carl plans to use the single engine car to transport 700+ hats to the stranded crawlers at the abyss.
- **[[solidarity-and-duty]]**: The crawlers cooperate to pool their hats and keys, demonstrating collective hope and mutual trust.
- **[[televised-exploitation]]**: The system continues to rewrite crawler histories for drama (portraying Hekla's death as intentional) and violates basic privacy by selling crawler chats to premium subscribers.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The revelation that crawler chats will become public is a devastating blow, stripping the crawlers of their final sanctuary of private communication. It forces Carl to be even more careful with his plans, especially regarding the Cookbook. The dissolution of Brynhild's Daughters and the shifting of the leaderboard shows how volatile the crawl's power structures are. Zev's vulnerability and exhaustion humanize the low-level administrative staff, who are themselves oppressed by Kua-tin political factions like the Bloom party.
