# Book 2 Chapter 24: Remex the Grand

## Summary
[[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] prepare to confront the lich in the guards' depot. Outside, a crowd of town NPCs (raised by [[fitz]]) gathers to defend the town. Carl rings the exterior of the warehouse with ten sticks of dynamite. He hands the detonator to [[katia-grim]], instructing her to detonate the building if they die or if he calls it, establishing a mutually assured destruction gamble. 

Carl and Donut climb a ladder and enter the second floor. They find the target, [[remex]] (Level 1 Soul Leech Capacitor), who is not a boss or a lich, but a zombified skyfowl minion of Miss Quill. He is tethered to a floating C-Grade Soul Crystal. 

Remex tells his story: he was a former legendary crawler who retired. His wife [[miss-quill]] brought him back as a capacitor to siphon mana from sacrificed prostitutes, and used his avatars to drop the bodies in the alleys. 

The quest **The Sex Workers Who Fell from the Heavens** is completed. Remex warns that with the sunrise, he will be gone, but the failed ritual will trigger a massive explosion. 

The system issues a group quest, **The Fools Who Broke the Glass**, warning that the failed spell has poisoned all skyfowl, and a massive magical blast will flatten a 45 km radius in seven minutes. Safe rooms are closed off. Carl decides to attempt a desperate plan to save Katia and the other 80 crawlers in the area. 

Carl uses the empty **Sheol Glass Reaper Case** (from the Beanie Baby "Kimaris") to cage the floating Soul Crystal. The crystal begins to crack.

## Characters Introduced

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's choice to risk his life to save Katia and the other crawlers, rather than running to the safe room, defines his character. It shows that he refuses to accept the game's rules of "every crawler for themselves," choosing to fight for the survival of the collective.
