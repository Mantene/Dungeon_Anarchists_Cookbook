# Book 2 Chapter 21: Katia Grim

## Summary
[[carl]], [[princess-donut]], and [[ricky-joe]] wait out the sunrise on the rooftop. Once the temple is destroyed and the guards depart, they drop down. Ricky Joe returns to his home to assist with his mother's labor. 

They go to the Narwhal safe room and meet [[katia-grim]], Hekla's friend. Katia is a **Doppelganger** crawler with the **Monster Truck Driver** (Juggernaut) class. She is Level 9, with very high Con (51) but low Strength. Mordecai helps her sculpt her face using a mirror piece Carl gives her. Katia officially joins *The Royal Court of Princess Donut* party to share experience. 

During the recap show, the media airs a segment setting up a future duel between Donut and [[lucia-mar]]'s dogs, Cici and Gustavo 3. Lucia Mar makes a threat to hunt down and execute Donut and Carl. 

Mordecai explains that the system AI is compensating for shorter floors by throwing high-end loot boxes at high performers, explaining the sudden surge in crawler power. Carl and Donut prepare to break into the magistrate's home after dark. In the alley, they grind against Level-14 Shadow Leaks, raising Mongo to Level 13.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Katia Grim's character is a tragic example of a crawler who has been set up for failure by her guide (who coached her to choose a highly difficult race and class). The system's attempt to force a feud between Donut and Lucia Mar shows the television network's priority on ratings, turning child crawlers against each other.
