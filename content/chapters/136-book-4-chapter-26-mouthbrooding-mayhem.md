# Book 4 Chapter 26: Mouthbrooding Mayhem

## Summary
Carl loots the final pocket watch, but they are cornered inside the submarine's bridge when a Level 82 City Boss swallows the vessel, triggering a televised Special Event boss fight.
- **Inside the Bridge**: Carl, Tran, and Vadim enter the *Akula's* bridge. Carl loots the body of the changeling principal Henrik, finding the first *Mysterious Watch* (giving Carl both watches and the winding box) and a *Torn Book Page* describing the mechanics of *Wailing Shrieker* ghosts (defeated by exorcism, destroying the lover's corpse, banishment, or electric shock).
- **The Court Lock**: The System attempts to place an "administrative lock" on the watches/box due to a court appeal by Syndicate plaintiffs. However, the appeal is immediately dismissed for "Lack of Standing," and the lock is removed.
- **Lusca Attacks**: The sub is dragged down to the bubble's floor (1,500 meters deep) by a gigantic mountain-sized creature—a "sharktopus" with massive tentacles. Katia is forced to retreat inside the sub.
- **Special Event Broadcast**: The world freezes as the fight is turned into a televised "Special Event" broadcast live to subscribers. The announcers are Kevin (the lizard recap host) and **Magnificent Troy** (an orc sports commentator).
- **The City Boss**: The boss is **Lusca** (Level 82 City Boss, Octo-Shark Brood Mother Queen). She is a mouthbrooder who has snapped the *Akula* in half, swallowing the front half (containing the bridge) whole.
- **The Rules**: A 10-minute timer begins. Lusca's hungry babies will swarm the interior of her mouth to feed on the sub's occupants. Anything left after 10 minutes will be swallowed into Mama's digestive system.
- **Carl's Command**: Carl messages Donut to immediately drop all depth charges set to maximum depth and orders all crawlers on the surface to flee to saferooms.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The legal battle in the Syndicate court happening in a literal flash of text shows the background war between factions trying to manipulate the game and the automated AI enforcement of rules. Carl's immediate order to drop depth charges onto his own location shows he is willing to risk self-injury to break the boss's containment.
