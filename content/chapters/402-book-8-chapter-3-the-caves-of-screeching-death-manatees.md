# Chapter 402: Chapter 3 — The Caves of the Screeching Death Manatees

## Summary
The race begins with a loud horn. The Navitron 1000 system on the dashboard announces the details of Heat 1:
- **Driver**: Carl
- **Navigator**: Princess Donut (with her extensive deity-based titles, including "Assassin of the Great and Feral Sekhmet")
- **Distance**: 53 kilometers
- **Track**: Mostly flat, asphalt-paved road
- **Environment**: The Caves of the Screeching Death Manatees
- **Time Limit**: 6 hours

The truck's headlights do not work, so Donut uses her [[torch-spell|Torch]] spell to illuminate the dark road. Carl identifies several opponents heading out: a roofless muscle car, a fast bear-like creature, a skittering bug creature, a minivan, a dog-like shape, and a bizarre giant tumbleweed with a brushed unicorn head sticking out of the top, ridden by a pinkish-red rodent.

Donut summons Mongo to serve as her seat, but the terrified dinosaur quickly pukes on the dashboard. 

In the group chat, [[prepotente]] posts an urgent warning: **do not kill or prevent opponents from finishing.** If more than one opponent is eliminated in a heat, the system will mix and match the surviving teams in future heats, which will inevitably force crawler-versus-crawler races. [[zhang]] chimed in, warning that [[li-na]] has already violated this warning by killing an opponent.

As Carl approaches the massive cave entrance, he witnesses the wreckage: the minivan is overturned and flaming, a bugbear is crawling away, and a house-sized Screeching Death Manatee is dragging a bus-sized ladybug vehicle off the road. The manatee crunches the ladybug to death, and its burning rider is instantly disintegrated by the system.

- [[crawler-cooperation]] — The crawlers realize they must cooperate and keep their NPC opponents alive to prevent being pitted against each other.
- [[the-collapse-of-rules]] — The deadly, chaotic nature of the "Don't come in last" race structure instantly claims lives at the cave entrance.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The dynamic of the race shifts instantly when Prepotente realizes the mathematical trap of the floor. The crawlers are forced into the bizarre position of having to protect and shepherd their NPC opponents to avoid having to fight each other later, turning a death race into a defense mission.
