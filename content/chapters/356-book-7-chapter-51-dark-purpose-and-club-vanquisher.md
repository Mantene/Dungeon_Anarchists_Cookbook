# Chapter 356: Dark Purpose and Club Vanquisher

## Summary

Carl, Katia, Prepotente, and Li Jun infiltrate Club Vanquisher to reach the Temple of Khepri and kill Vinata. Their plan uses [[li-na]]'s dreads—amplified through wires—to disable the guards. Li Na activates her Dark Purpose dread, which is far more powerful than expected.

The dread doesn't just disorient enemies—it causes the trophy heads on the walls to come alive, the rugs to stir, and the NPC guards to mutilate themselves in horror. Mork, the ram blasphemer, rips out his own eyes and tears at his chest before dying, completing the Kill the Blasphemer quest. Carl receives a Celestial Boon: a Level 15 Heathen Scald that adds damage to all punch attacks.

After Mork dies, ghostly chains appear and drag the spirits of all the dead—including the trophy heads—into the floor. The area becomes "defiled," frost forms everywhere, and the fireplace dies. The club sounds an emergency shelter-in-place alarm for demonic activity.

Li Na reveals she is cycling through her dreads to rapidly power-level them, reaching level 15 on Dark Purpose, then switching to Defile Soul, Blood Horror, and finally Succulent Decay. [[samantha]] rolls into the massacre scene late, covered in stew.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections

This chapter marks a turning point in understanding Li Na's true power. Her dreads aren't simply disabling spells—they cause existential horror that makes victims destroy themselves. The trophy heads coming alive and the chains dragging spirits away suggest the dreads tap into something deeply connected to the dungeon's metaphysics. Li Na's calculated decision to power-level during this chaos reveals her strategic ruthlessness.
