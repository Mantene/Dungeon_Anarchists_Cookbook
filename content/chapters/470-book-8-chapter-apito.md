# Chapter 470: Chapter 70 — Apito's Crystal and the Tangled Threads of Destiny

## Summary
In the stables, [[prepotente]] reluctantly shows [[carl]] the "Memorial Crystal. Apito.", which he wears like a necklace. The crystal is 0% charged, warm, and heavier than it looks. Prepotente explains that it's supposed to charge by killing, allowing access to hidden spells and knowledge, but it hasn't worked for him or for [[osvaldo]], who originally possessed it. The crystal did activate once, shining brightly, when [[yarilo]] emerged from the Nothing during Faction Wars, an event Carl remembers. Carl speculates this might have been when [[apito]] (Yarilo's grandmother/stepmother, who banished him) should have appeared. Osvaldo has a quest to discover why these crystals aren't charging and apparently has a second one.

[[princess-donut]] chastises Prepotente for his "mean" reaction to Osvaldo, reminding him of his mother's advice to "be a good boy." Prepotente's god, "The Midnight Epicure," gave him a quest during the Butcher's Masquerade to obtain and hold onto the crystal, awaiting a sign to use it. Prepotente describes the Epicure as an apocalyptic god who protects prey animals by transforming them into devourers.

Carl discusses his own quest to uncover who murdered [[emberus]]'s son, [[geyrun]], believing it's crucial to unraveling the larger mysteries of the dungeon, including [[rosetta]]'s "Scavenger" explanation and even the [[ai]]'s potential blind spots. He notes the connection between [[scolopendra]] and Apito as possible siblings or aspects of the same entity. Both Carl and Prepotente confirm that they received no god updates while holding the crystal within the stable, reinforcing Rosetta's theory that this specific location shields them from direct System observation.

The conversation is interrupted by [[samantha]]'s chaotic arrival, without [[grigori]], whom she dislikes. She claims to have a "secret plan" mirroring Carl's. Donut, frustrated, openly reveals Carl's "stupid plan": to leave the party, gather mutually hostile immortals on a floor where they can't die, and trick them into killing each other, which Donut dismisses as an elaborate suicide attempt that endangers everyone. Samantha, unfazed, declares her plan is "just like" Carl's.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- [[mystery-and-conspiracy]] — The central plot thread of Geyrun's murder is deepened, connecting to the Memorial Crystal, Apito, Scolopendra, and the Scavenger. Carl's growing theory that even the AI doesn't know everything highlights a deeper conspiracy.
- [[divine-intervention-and-quests]] — Multiple characters (Carl, Prepotente, Osvaldo) are driven by god-given quests related to the same mysterious artifact, intertwining their destinies.
- [[over-city-mechanics]] — The unique properties of the Memorial Crystal, its non-charging, its activation, and the confirmation that god updates are suppressed in specific locations, further detail the dungeon's underlying mechanics.
- [[character-development]] — Prepotente's interaction with Donut about his mother reveals a softer, more vulnerable side beneath his gruff exterior.
- [[solidarity-and-duty]] — Donut's exasperation and concern for Carl's "suicide plan" underscore her loyalty and protective instincts despite her criticisms.
- [[televised-exploitation]] — The mention of Prepotente discovering his god through "Plenty of Plenty" reinforces the idea of contestants being influenced and even spiritually guided by broadcast content and sponsors.
- [[survival]] — Carl's plan, while framed as suicidal by Donut, is his attempt to leverage the unique properties of Floor 10 (immortality for certain contestants) for a high-stakes, high-reward strategy.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
This chapter serves as a crucial lynchpin, drawing together several seemingly disparate plot threads and mythological references that have been building since earlier books. The Memorial Crystal, initially a minor artifact, is revealed as a central piece of a larger, ancient puzzle involving Apito, Yarilo, Emberus, and Scolopendra. Carl's growing intuition that the AI itself might not possess all the answers is a profound development, shifting the scope of the conspiracy from purely Systemic manipulation to something far older and more fundamental to the universe.

The comedic elements, particularly Donut's blunt assessment of Carl's "elaborate suicide" plan and Samantha's chaotic enthusiasm, provide a necessary counterpoint to the weighty revelations. Donut's genuine fear for Carl, despite her sarcastic delivery, highlights the deep bond between them, showcasing the personal stakes intertwined with the cosmic ones. The confirmation that certain areas can block god updates adds another layer to the dungeon's mechanics, suggesting hidden pockets of agency or information beyond the System's direct, constant oversight, which could be a critical exploit for Carl's party moving forward.
