# Book 6 Chapter 34: Hiring Strippers and Jola Combo

## Summary
As the Desperado Club prepares to close, Carl uses a Meat Shields coupon to rescue the Penis Parade dancers, while Damascus and Anaconda set out to avenge their mother's murder.
- **Evacuation Timer**: A warning system gives Carl, Katia, and Samantha fifteen minutes to exit the Desperado Club before they are forcibly teleported out.
- **Damascus's Plea**: Damascus Steel asks Carl to hire the dancers of the Penis Parade as mercenaries. He explains that after Astrid's death, the club will undergo a restructuring under new management, and they will be reassigned to the Larracos level Meat Shields where Carl would eventually have to fight against them.
- **The Family Backstory**: Damascus reveals that Astrid was their mother and Hamed (the Night Wyrm) is their father. He explains that they are "naturals"—people who signed away their rights or were forced into the dungeon. They retain their original family bonds despite playing different races in the crawl.
- **Confronting the Wyrm**: Damascus and Anaconda refuse to leave as mercenaries. Instead, they head to the back cubicle to confront and avenge their mother by fighting their father, the Night Wyrm.
- **Hiring the Dancers**: Rosemarie (the elderly dwarf manager of the hidden Meat Shields office in the Penis Parade, who has a mole creature named Bernie) processes the contract. Carl uses his 100,000 gold Meat Shields coupon to hire the 13 male strippers, the skeleton DJ Doctor Bones, and the crocodilian Bucket Boy. They immediately transfer to the Princess Posse's guild barracks.
- **Jola and the Card Combo**: Donut messages Carl, thrilled with the new additions. She reveals that Katia gave her a mythic Icelandic card named [[jola]] (the Yule Cat). To fit it into their deck, Donut used a consumable *Combo* card to merge two of their problem cards, using a *Glow-Up* card to make the change permanent.
- **Jola's Warning**: Donut warns Carl that their new totem Jola is excellent, but adds a cryptic warning: "Jola is great as long as there are no children around."
- **Exit and Grin**: Carl and Katia exit the club. Carl realizes that the flooding of the lower levels of the Desperado has left several of their Faction Wars rivals trapped at the bottom, which makes him grin.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **The "Naturals" in the System**: The revelation of "naturals" who sign away their rights and are forced into the dungeon highlights the corporate enslavement of sentient beings in the Syndicate primary zones.
- **Mercenary Rescue**: Carl uses the game's mechanics (Meat Shields) to rescue NPCs from being reassigned to hostile combat roles on the lower floors.
- **Factions and Preparation**: Donut's deck optimization (the Jola card and card combo) prepares the party for the brutal combat of Floor 9.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The concept of "naturals" who are forced to live out lives as generated NPCs while retaining their original identities is one of the most tragic details of the dungeon's lore. Astrid used her position to protect her sons, Damascus and Anaconda, by keeping them employed under her watch. With her gone, they choose a suicide mission against their father Hamed rather than fleeing as mercenaries. Donut's combination of card totems shows her growing tactical independence, even if her choice to keep the dangerous Shi Maria card represents a calculated risk.
