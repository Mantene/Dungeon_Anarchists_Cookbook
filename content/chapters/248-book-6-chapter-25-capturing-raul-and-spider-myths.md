# Book 6 Chapter 25: Capturing Raul and Spider Myths

## Summary
Carl and Donut ambush and capture Raul the cultivator crab, and prepare to confront Shi Maria (The Screamer), the spider demi-god who swallowed Samantha.
- **The Ambush of Raul**: Carl uses a *Phase Through Wall* potion to phase inside and out of the U-Haul truck where [[raul]] (the cultivator crab) is meditating. Donut casts *Hole* to drop him inside, *Mute* to silence him, and *Brain Freeze* to weaken his resistances. Raul, terrified and shouting self-help mantras, is beaten into the red and flagged, becoming their 5th T'Ghee card (a Common green card).
- **Samantha's Status**: Carl unmutes [[samantha]], who reveals she is stuck in the web of her "new best friend" Maria. She claims Maria (a demi-god spider) can reanimate the flesh golem body in Carl's inventory to free her, and is excited to meet Louis because he "sounds tasty."
- **The Screamer Lore**: Carl researches the spider in the Bahamas book. Known as **The Screamer** (or Shi Maria), she is a translucent, VW-bug-sized spider with long hairy legs and a human head with a hinged jaw and four black eyes. She is a former celestial demi-god who was banished for allegedly eating her husband (**Hamed the Night Wyrm**).
- **Prepping for Battle**: The Screamer specializes in illusion and fear-based spells, including *Unending Insanity* (level 15) and *Lights Out* (blindness, level 15). To counter them, Carl and Donut consume **Liquid Therapy** (an expensive alchemy potion brewed by Mordecai that protects against fear and insanity).
- **The Chowder War Update**: A system message announces the re-ascension of the Goddess Semiramis (another crawler-awoken deity). A quest update reveals that [[sister-ines-quiteria]] has sided with the Monk Seals, warning them of the crabs' sneak attack, setting up a massive impending battle.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **The Chowder War**: The local conflict expands as Sister Ines aligns with the seals, threatening to turn the crabs' migration into a slaughter.
- **Corporate Deity Manipulation**: The re-ascension of Semiramis confirms that other crawlers are being used as pawns in the Floor 12 Ascendency games.
- **Survival Mechanics**: The use of custom alchemy (Liquid Therapy) and phase potions highlights Carl's growing reliance on advanced consumables to survive unique boss mechanics.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The capture of Raul shows Carl's practical tactical execution, bypassing the crab's high intelligence and cultivation spells by utilizing phase potions to strike from unexpected angles. The description of Shi Maria/The Screamer reveals a tragic, delusional entity, mourning the husband she may have eaten. This mirrors Samantha's own state as a disembodied, sanity-draining head, highlighting the recurring theme of female entities driven mad and warped by the system's cruelty.
