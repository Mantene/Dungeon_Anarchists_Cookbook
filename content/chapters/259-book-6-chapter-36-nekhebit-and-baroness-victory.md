# Book 6 Chapter 36: Nekhebit and Baroness Victory

## Summary
Odette announces her shocking entry into the dungeon as the goddess Nekhebit, forcing Carl to choose the Orc attorney Baroness Victory as the team's Faction Wars adjutant.
- **Odette's Ascension**: Odette reveals she cannot serve as their adjutant because she has sponsored a deity and is entering the Celestial Ascendency game herself. She will play the ancient vulture goddess **[[nekhebit]]**.
- **Nekhebit's Appearance**: The goddess is depicted as a slender human-like figure in a tattered, threadbare red dress with the head of an Egyptian vulture. Her skin is covered in heavy scars, her yellow beak is cracked and chipped, and she carries an oak bow.
- **The Adjutant Candidates**: Odette presents three available candidates:
  1. **[[ripper-wonton]]**: Quokka host of *Danger Zone*, experienced but rigid and physically slow.
  2. **[[baroness-victory]]**: Orc attorney and judge, half-sister of the late Queen Consort Ugloo.
  3. **[[drick]]**: Bald Dream elf containing a Valtay worm in his brain, former crawler.
- **Quasar's Recommendation**: While Donut wants the cute quokka Ripper Wonton, Quasar (Carl's Nullian attorney) strongly gestures for Carl to reject him. Quasar enthusiastically advocates for Baroness Victory.
- **Harbinger's Departure**: During the selection, Harbinger (the goat liaison) abruptly blinks and disappears from the studio, distracted by an external event.
- **The Orc's Impartiality**: Carl questions Baroness Victory regarding the relatives of hers he has killed. She impassively confirms his kills but asserts her absolute impartiality, noting she recently sentenced her own aunt to indentureship for tax evasion. She also points out that she is the only candidate the other teams sued to keep out.
- **The Selection**: Carl chooses Baroness Victory as their Faction Wars adjutant, rejecting Drick and Ripper Wonton.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **The Celestial Ascendency**: Odette's transformation into Nekhebit shows that the dungeon's elite are not safe from the game; they are drawn in as active combatants, setting up a clash of deities on Floor 9.
- **Legal Exploitation**: Baroness Victory's legal background and Quasar's recommendation suggest that Floor 9 will be heavily contested on legal rules and loopholes.
- **Valtay Infiltration**: The presence of Drick (a worm-head Valtay) as a candidate highlights the Valtay's efforts to position their agents in critical referee roles.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Odette's decision to enter the crawl as Nekhebit is a massive pivot, showing she has been planning an escape or confrontation of her own. Her cracked, chipped beak and scarred form represent the tattered history of the goddess she sponsored. Carl's choice of Baroness Victory is a pragmatic one, guided by Quasar's legal instinct. The fact that the other teams actively tried to sue to block her from participating suggests she is a powerful legal weapon that Carl can use to exploit the dungeon's rules.
