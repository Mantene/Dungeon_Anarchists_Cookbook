# Chapter 380: The Battle of Gustavo and the Eulogist's Warning

## Summary

Carl learns that [[lucia-mar]]'s conscription didn't work as expected—her personalities take turns controlling the body, and the conscription only bound the "Lucia" personality, not the others. She doesn't know how to play the game or use magical items.

The god Yarilo arrives nearby, granting Carl a temporary comedic effect: "Protection from all venereal diseases." [[mongo]] escapes down the tower chute, and Donut tries to follow but is trapped by Lucia's net.

Outside the tower, Carl, [[raul]], [[mongo]], and Gustavo 3 (Lucia's rottweiler) collide. Harpocrates—now conscripted by [[li-na]] and ranked as Legionnaire in the White Dragons—fights Yarilo using the ball as a flail. Gustavo impales Mongo with Porcupine Defense quills, nearly killing him. Raul is destroyed by Gustavo's lightning. Carl is battered by the gale tower.

[[ren]] arrives, now much bigger, and uses Wrecking Ball to destroy the back half of Gustavo, saving Mongo's life. Lucia reappears in hag form, healing Mongo with a pet potion. But her personality shifts, and she delivers a chilling warning: the Eulogist now has another ally in the dungeon, and Carl can't kill Lucia without killing all the children of Earth.

## Characters

- **[[carl]]** — Fights Gustavo, gets battered by the gale tower, watches helplessly as Mongo nearly dies
- **[[lucia-mar]]** — Reveals her split personalities, heals Mongo, delivers the Eulogist warning
- **[[mongo]]** — Nearly killed by Gustavo's quills and lightning, saved by Rend
- **[[ren]]** — Saves Mongo's life with Wrecking Ball, has grown significantly larger
- **[[raul]]** — Destroyed by Gustavo's lightning attack
- **[[princess-donut]]** — Trapped in tower, unable to help

## Themes & Plot Threads

- **Lucia's Complexity** — Her multiple personalities make the conscription incomplete and dangerous
- **The Eulogist's Reach** — Lucia's warning that killing her would kill Earth's children reveals a horrifying connection
- **Rend's Growth** — His increased size and Wrecking Ball skill make him a key defender
- **Mongo's Near-Death** — One of the most emotionally intense moments, with Carl thinking "This is what will break her"

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections

The fight with Gustavo is visceral and desperate. Carl's thought that Mongo's death would be what finally breaks Donut shows how well he understands her. Lucia's warning about the Eulogist adds a terrifying new dimension—the enemy has leveraged the children as hostages. Rend's save is one of the most satisfying moments in the battle.
