# Chapter 437: Chapter 38 — Ghosts in the System

## Summary
- **The Worn Path Method**: Akuma explains that because there are too many surviving crawlers, the AI had to pull old NPCs/mobs from emergency backups to populate the races. A former crawler named **Herot** (the skink, author of the 16th cookbook) discovered that imperfect recycling causes mobs to retain memories, calling this the "Worn Path Method."
- **The Pineapple Cabaret**: Herot and another former crawler, **Menerva**, exploited this by building the 17th floor (a decoy, horrible maze) and attaching a pocket dimension to it: the **Pineapple Cabaret**. This dimension acts as an oasis where NPCs and mobs can escape the dungeon's cycles.
- **The Infestation**: Akuma and the mages left the Cabaret to get the Scavenger's Daughter (Samantha's daughter) during Faction Wars, but in their absence, the Cabaret became infested with shadow mimics, ogres, and other monsters. They need Carl's team to clear it.
- **The Portal Exploits**: The Cabaret can be reached via the casinos at the Desperado Club or Carl's shop interface. However, anyone who worships a god (like Carl, who is marked by Emberus, and Osvaldo) cannot enter, as their presence would trigger a smite.
- **Akuma's Trap**: Akuma admits he wanted Carl to kill two teams so the system would force Carl into a heat against his friends, leaving him with no choice but to work with the war mages. Infuriated, Carl punches Akuma in the face and breaks his nose.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The revelation of the Pineapple Cabaret adds a layer of hope and desperation. Herot's selflessness in building a permanent haven for discarded NPCs demonstrates the deep humanity of the cookbook authors. However, the requirement that entrants cannot worship a god blocks Carl from escaping there, trapping him in the Ascendency conflict.
