# Chapter 432: Chapter 33 — The Single Mandatory Pit Stop

## Summary
- **Driving Restrictions**: Because both Carl and Donut touched the pedals/wheel in the last race, they are banned from driving in the next heat. They must hire new mercenaries to drive.
- **Mount Survival**: Carl sees Onikuma (the razor foxes' bear mount) and Old Shuck (the Juggler dog) cross the finish line and disappear, confirming that mounts are saved if their riders die.
- **Heat Four Details**:
  - Distance: ~99 kilometers.
  - Track: "The You're Gonna Die If You Go in Here Jungle Trail of Tremendous Cannibalistic Slaughter" (unpaved, jungle, acid rain, horrifying traps).
  - Special Rules: Vehicles will have a "special containment unit" and share the track with 3 other heats.
  - Mandatory Refueling Pit Stop: Within the lost jungle city of Upano (2-hour stop, allows repairs in garage).
  - Time Limit: 20 hours. Starts in 7 hours.
- **Galactic Status Update**: Carl checks in on the other crawlers:
  - Imani and Elle: 2nd place, lost another 2 teams. They will be on the same track as Carl next race.
  - Florin and Lucia: 1st place, lost 3 teams.
  - Prepotente and Jurgen: won, lost no teams.
  - Chris (big rig): 3rd place.
  - Louis, Britney, and Bautista: survived, last place, getting another audience-vote upgrade.
  - Zhang and Li Na: won, but only crawlers left, including Sammi Harrison (isopod summoner driving a fire truck).
  - Prepotente and Louis's teams are on the same track next race ("Satan's Water Park").
- **Mercenary Search**: Carl tasks Rosetta and Tipid with hiring two mercenaries who can drive and defend, with fast reflexes, at the town market.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The driving ban forces Carl and Donut to rely entirely on external help for the next race, increasing their vulnerability. The massive scale of Heat Four, combining four separate heats onto a single 99 km jungle track with acid rain, sets up a high-stakes convergence of major crawler groups.
