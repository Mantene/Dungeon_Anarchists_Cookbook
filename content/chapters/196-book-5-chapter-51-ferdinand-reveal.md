# Book 5 Chapter 51: Ferdinand Reveal

## Summary
- **Siege Tactics**: Carl uses the trebuchet *Elven Rock Chucker* with weedkiller to kill a creeper from a mile away, gaining the *B'sieging!* achievement.
- **Ferdinand's Attack**: The elven procession led by Ferdinand (Queen Imogen's familiar) on the rhino Simoom arrives. Ferdinand attacks Mongo with lightning; Donut blocks the bolt with her chest and nearly dies.
