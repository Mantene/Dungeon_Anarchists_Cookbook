# Chapter 444: Chapter 45 — Carl's Furious Confrontation

## Summary
The chapter opens with Carl in a blind rage, storming the garage of "One Fine Pig" to confront the Tigran drivers, Dario and Nico, who had run over his mercenaries. Their gremlin, Lipstick, cowers as Carl enters. The Tigrans' APV retaliates with an ethereal gun, blasting through the garage wall and flipping a nearby double-decker bus, causing it to ignite. Three dromedarian mercenaries from the bus retaliate by attacking the Tigrans' APV with spiked baseball bats. Elle quickly extinguishes the fire by encasing the bus in ice. Carl continues his assault on the APV, his gauntlet denting the armored door, but the vehicle's electric shield activates, throwing him back and violently killing one of the dromedarian mercenaries by snapping his neck. The shield also crushes and explodes Lipstick the gremlin against the garage wall.

Imani and Princess Donut intervene, pulling Carl away and calming his uncontrolled fury, reminding him he can't fight a vehicle and might harm Penelope, the pig inside. Carl reflects on the brutal, sudden deaths of his mercenaries (Quemada, Finley, Olga) and the dromedarian, feeling overwhelmed by the unfairness and grief. He realizes his team is now without a driver (Imani cannot drive the APV) and cannot hire new mercenaries or even use Jamal. Donut encourages Carl, reminding him of their contingency plans, and Carl, regaining composure, instructs her to consume the bodies of Finley, Olga, and the fallen dromedarian to prevent them from being used by other crawlers.

Carl then overhears Dario and Nico discussing their strategy: prioritizing Penelope's safety within the truck, finding a new gremlin on the track, and Nico's confidence in taking Carl, despite Dario's warning about Donut. Donut's growl signals their presence, and the Tigrans re-engage their shield. The chapter concludes with a message from Louis, updating Carl and Donut on his team's race, detailing their current first-place standing, Bodi's buffs making Eileen fast, the weird waterslide tubes, and a "Satan guy" making strange comments, along with a warning about acid rain protection. Carl replies that they are on their way.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
This chapter is a powerful display of Carl's raw emotional core, as his grief and rage over the brutal, casual deaths of his mercenaries boil over into an uncontrollable outburst. It highlights the deeply personal stakes for Carl, contrasting his almost berserker fury with Donut's calm, pragmatic influence. The scene is a microcosm of the Dungeon's violence: quick, unexpected deaths, property destruction, and the immediate need to adapt to escalating threats. Carl's reflection on the "unfairness" of it all underscores the psychological toll of constant exposure to death, even for someone as hardened as him.

The chapter also cleverly advances multiple plot threads. The Tigrans' motivations around Penelope the pig are further elucidated, setting up a clear antagonist and a high-stakes objective beyond merely winning the race. Louis's message provides essential world-building, reminding the reader of the broader, chaotic reality of Floor 10 and its diverse inhabitants, while also offering crucial environmental information (acid rain protection). This juxtaposition of Carl's intensely personal conflict with the wider dungeon narrative reinforces the scale and complexity of "Don't Come in Last." The chapter ends on a note of grim determination, as Carl's anger transforms into a cold resolve, signaling that he is prepared to take drastic measures for his team's survival.
