# Chapter 303: Chapter 4 — Gloom Wraith Phase

## Summary
- **Carl's Loot Boxes**: Carl opens his Floor 8 loot. Notable items include:
  - **Enchanted Toe Ring of the Well-Balanced**: Slipped onto his left pinky toe. Grants *Heart Balance* (auto-triggers a health potion at 2% health), *Mind Balance* (protects from enslavement/conscription), and *Physical Balance* (Steady Balance and the *Walk on Air* skill).
  - **Walk on Air Guilt**: The *Walk on Air* skill is revealed to have been absorbed from [[li-jun]] after Carl ate his eye, triggering severe guilt in Carl.
  - **Midnight Market Patch**: A patch depicting a Reaper Spider Minion merchant. Grants +5% Dexterity and access to the Midnight Market (located in the Desperado Club).
  - **Gloom Wraith Phase**: Carl drinks the *Hell-Kissed Celestial Skill Potion of the Prince of Woe*, gaining the level-13 skill **Gloom Wraith Phase**. It allows non-corporeal bursts of speed that pass through obstacles and scramble unarmored biological targets, at the cost of halving his current mana points.
- **Hair Restoration Debacle**: Carl attempts to use the *Rev-up Magic Hair Restoration Tonic*. Katia applies it, but the stripper [[splash-zone]]'s attempt to power wash it off fails due to saferoom spell restrictions. Carl is left with waist-length, romance-novel-style wavy hair for two weeks.
- **Army Hierarchy**: Carl and Donut assign officer ranks:
  - [[florin]] becomes General to lead the defenses.
  - The strippers are named Sergeant First Class / Adjutants to command the Legionnaires (former Sluggalos).
  - [[louis-santiago-2]] is named Army Air General.
- **Samantha's Rampage**: Samantha rolls out of the room to kill her "arch-enemy" [[tish]] (Ghazi's ex-girlfriend at Larracos College) but is caught and brought back by Katia.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's acquisition of *Walk on Air* serves as a visceral, unwanted reminder of his cannibalistic action to survive the card battle. Meanwhile, the comedic hair restoration incident breaks the tension, even as Carl and Donut's power levels reach a threshold that Mordecai notes is "seriously scary" for their level.
