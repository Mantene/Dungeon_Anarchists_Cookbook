# Chapter 394: Chapter 86 — Katia's Choice

## Summary
With 8 minutes left before the floor burns, Carl throws Samantha into the volcano to stall Emberus. 

Katia wakes up, and the goddess Eileithyia appears. CEO [[huanxin-jinx|Huanxin Jinx]] (sponsoring the goddess) demands Katia make her choice. 

Donut uses her upgraded *Laundry Day* (level 16) to eject Huanxin Jinx from the goddess's soul armor. Huanxin lands in a magic-neutralizing circle drawn on the floor. 

Donut monologues, activates her quadruped weapon mount, and has Mordecai load a non-magical bolt crafted with his mother's ashes. Donut shoots and kills Huanxin Jinx. 

Victory explains that the goddess is frozen until Katia chooses. Victory also reveals she is now the acting Syndicate Prime Minister, and that the newest mantis system went nova. 

Katia makes her choice: "I wish to be made pregnant," which makes her ineligible for the crawl. She chooses the human Louis as the father. Eileithyia converts Katia back to a human, revealing she is missing her right arm, left leg, right leg up to the knee, and an ear. They attach cybernetics Katia built and Bautista finished. 

Eileithyia confirms the baby will be a boy, and Katia disappears to the surface kinder facility.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **The Pregnancy Exploit**: Faction Wars' ultimate loophole, allowing pregnant crawlers to bypass the crawl and be sent to the surface.
- **The True Cost of Crawling**: The shocking reveal of Katia's hidden amputations, hidden behind system appearances.
- **Vengeance for Mordecai**: The execution of Huanxin Jinx, avenging Mordecai's brother.
- **The Sepsis Whore Crown**: The magic requirements binding Katia to the 12th floor until she escapes.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Katia's departure is one of the series' most emotional moments. The reveal of her true physical state—having lost three limbs and an ear during her crawl, hidden behind magical aesthetics—is a devastating reminder of the physical toll the dungeon takes on its survivors. Her choice to become pregnant, choosing Louis as the father, is a brave sacrifice to escape the system and protect the surface children, turning a mechanical loophole into a mother's heroic path of rescue.
