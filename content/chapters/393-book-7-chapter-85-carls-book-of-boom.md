# Chapter 393: Chapter 85 — Carl's Book of Boom

## Summary
The Sledge casts *Zerzura*, initiating a 5-minute transfer of Faction Wars' combined cities (Larracos and Shanty Town) to the 12th floor. Hired mercenaries and cretins will return to storage, while local NPCs, Semeru dwarves, and surviving outworlders will be evacuated. Out of 50,000 former crawlers who returned, only 28,000 survived; out of 32,500 crawlers, only 20,221 survived to the 10th floor. 

Carl refuses to use the Ring of Divine Suffering to slaughter the defeated enemy mercenaries for stat boosts. Donut swallows the ring to dispose of it forever. 

Ferdinand and Juice Box say goodbye before leaving. Milk presents Carl and Donut with a parting gift: the Book of Voodoo has split into *A Banquet Fit for a Princess* (for Donut) and *Carl’s Book of Boom* (for Carl), containing temporary skills and personal thank-you notes from the 28,000 departing crawlers. 

As Larracos vanishes, Carl spots a rebuilt statue of the goddess Ysalte with a giant pickaxe. Ferdinand and Juice Box forfeit, and the Princess Posse wins Faction Wars.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **The Book of Boom**: The creation of Carl's new volume of Voodoo, offering massive utility from departing crawlers.
- **The Rejecting of Power**: Carl's moral decision to throw away the Ring of Divine Suffering rather than sacrifice others for stats.
- **The Zerzura Evacuation**: Faction Wars' resolution, saving thousands of NPCs and transferring the city.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's refusal to use the Ring of Divine Suffering is a critical character choice. While it would have maxed his stats like Li Na, it would have turned him into the very monster the show's viewers want. Swallowing the ring symbolizes Carl and Donut's defiance of the system's corrupting mechanics. The Book of Boom represents the collective gratitude of the crawl, cementing Carl's role as a leader of the resistance.
