# Chapter 280: Teleport Traps and Stairwell Ambush

## Summary
- **Teleport Setup**: Carl sets up a two-part teleport trap system utilizing his robotic spider automatons to move combatants or bosses.

- **Capitol Ambush**: Ren's squad—consisting of Ren, her stone-skin tank [[chuluuna]], and Chuluuna's wife [[khulan]]—is ambushed by Quan Ch at the Capitol stairwell. Carl prepares to teleport into the stairwell area to assist them.
