# Book 6 Chapter 10: Pueblo de los Olvidados

## Summary
The crawlers enter a cemetery in Havana, triggering a compulsory regional quest within a corrupted Ghommid settlement.
- **Practice Success**: Paz discusses Team Sister Ines's practice runs: they defeated a Level 65 Chinese chimpanzee monster (*Qa*) using Yago on medium difficulty.
- **Quest Activation**: The combined teams enter a local Havana cemetery, triggering a regional lock-in and a compulsory quest: [[pueblo-de-los-olvidados]]. The system explains that a "Santero" has aggravated the dead, corrupting the Ghommid (other-realm spirit) settlement and turning the inhabitants into ravenous undead monsters.
- **Ghommid Attack**: The team is attacked by non-corporeal Ghommids, including [[kipper]] (Level 30), a former local dishwasher. Kipper uses frost attacks that freeze-root Carl's arm, putting it at risk of shattering before they successfully dispatch the mobs.
- **Saferoom Intruder**: The crawlers retreat to their saferoom to heal. As they settle in, a red-tagged NPC boss unexpectedly opens the door and walks straight into their saferoom.

## Characters Introduced
- [[kipper]] — Level 30 Unsettled Ghommid spirit.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[pueblo-de-los-olvidados]]**: Initiation of the corrupted cemetery quest.
- **[[ghosts-of-earth]]**: Introduction of Ghommids (spirit entities) and the environmental constraints of the cemetery settlement.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The Pueblo de los Olvidados quest introduces a distinct cultural layer to Floor 8, blending Cuban Santería folklore with dungeon crawler mechanics. The cliffhanger of an NPC boss invading the sacred space of a saferoom violates one of the crawl's most fundamental rules, signaling the breakdown of system boundaries.
