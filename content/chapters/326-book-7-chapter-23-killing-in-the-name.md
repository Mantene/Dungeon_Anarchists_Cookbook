# Chapter 326: Killing in the Name

## Summary

The chapter opens with a cookbook entry by Crawler [[ossie]] (18th Edition) about using confusion to terrify stalwart enemies. 

In the southern trenches, the Princess Posse prepares to launch their counter-offensive. Carl has stacked numerous protective shields, and [[imani-c]] arrives to cast wispy calm buffs on the troops, as well as **Total Ear Protection** on Donut and [[mongo]]. 

Adjutant [[baroness-victory]] and the Dream elf Adjutant for the NPC team, [[drick]], teleport into the trenches to observe the assault. 

The offensive begins on Carl's signal. Louis pilots the *Party Planner* and drops a line of heavy bombs directly in front of the enemy trenches to clear traps and strip their protection buffs. Elle targets the enemy’s anti-air batteries with a missile launcher, disabling the first gun. 

Simultaneously, their lone trebuchet fires salvos of 50 upgraded **Dazzlers** (alarm and strobe-light traps). The traps flash blinding multi-colored lights in the defenders' faces and blast Rage Against the Machine's "Killing in the Name" at deafening, ground-shaking volumes. 

Warlord Donut orders a full-scale charge, promising a prize for the soldier who kills the most "Cheese Sticks." Under the cover of invisibility potions, illusionary doubles, and deafening music, Carl, Donut, and over two thousand Princess Posse soldiers charge over the top into No Man's Land.

## Characters

- **[[carl]]** — Leads the charge into No Man's Land; coordinates the multi-pronged assault.
- **[[princess-donut]]** — Issues the battle orders, offering prizes to encourage positive reinforcement; rides Mongo into battle.
- **[[imani-c]]** — Casts wing/wisp buffs and Total Ear Protection on Donut and Mongo.
- **[[drick]]** — NPC team Adjutant; teleports into the trenches to watch the battle out of curiosity.
- **[[louis-santiago-2]]** — Successfully executes the bombing run using the *Party Planner* airship.
- **[[elle-mcgibbons]]** — Destroys the enemy's first anti-air battery with a missile launcher.
- **[[britney-proskurina]]** — Signals the NPC team to join the charge.
- **[[ossie]]** — Cookbook author whose quote about using confusion guides the strategy (mentioned).

## Themes & Plot Threads

- **Trench Assault & Psychological Warfare** — Using loud, aggressive rock music ("Killing in the Name") and strobe lights to disorient and terrify the enemy.
- **Warlord Leadership Styles** — Donut uses positive reinforcement (prizes) rather than threats to motivate her army.
- **Adjutant Immunity** — Adjutants can turn invisible and observe active battle zones without penalty.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections

This chapter is a high-octane battle opener that showcases Carl's tactical creativity. Freeing the AI and using the Cookbook guidelines allows the crawlers to execute wild, outside-the-box strategies. Using Rage Against the Machine's "Killing in the Name" as a sonic weapon is classic Dungeon Crawler Carl—gritty, chaotic, politically resonant, and highly effective. The emotional exchange between Carl and Donut before they charge shows that beneath the cat-girl armor and royalty roleplay, the trauma of the dungeon is making them numb.
