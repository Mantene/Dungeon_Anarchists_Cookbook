# Chapter 344: Chapter 39 — Alliance Fracture

## Summary
[[orren|Orren]] implores [[carl|Carl]] and [[princess-donut|Donut]] not to reveal the details of the AI's containment breach to avoid galactic panic, though Carl knows the entire Syndicate council witnessed it. Orren teleports them back to their Shanty Town base.

System updates reveal the Bloc alliance has completely fractured: the Bone Clan (orcs) and the Dream have left the Bloc, which now consists only of the Blood Sultanate, the Madness, and the Reavers. The Good Guys Alliance remains Team Retribution and the Princess Posse.

Donut engages in a savage private chat with the newly crowned King [[prince-stalwart|Stalwart]], mocking his crown, his empty bank account (due to [[rosetta|Rosetta's]] heist), and his father's disappointment. She claims she turned [[king-rust|King Rust's]] skin into a bathroom rug for Mongo. Stalwart is enraged and threatens to boil her fur off.

Back at the base, [[boomer|Boomer]] briefs Carl on the battlefield. The Operatics were eliminated, and their castle/assets went to the Bone Clan. Boomer suggests it is time to move on the Operatic castle—which is a coded signal to initiate **Operation: Snake Pit**. The goal is to wipe out the nagas to save Donut and Katia. Carl prepares to confront the nagas.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The political fragmentation of the Bloc alliance presents the crawlers with a major tactical opportunity. Donut's psychological warfare against Stalwart is brilliant, exploiting his insecurity over his father's death and his sudden poverty to keep him off-balance. The transition to Operation: Snake Pit shows that despite the cosmic drama, the crawlers must remain focused on the immediate threat: the naga stronghold and the contracts holding Katia and Donut.
