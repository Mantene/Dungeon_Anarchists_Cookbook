# Chapter 261: Monroe County and Father's Death

## Summary
- **Florida Keys Relocation**: Carl and Donut appear in a glitched house in Monroe County, Florida (Florida Keys). Robert the Human (Carl's father) is already dead, having been instantly chomped by Mongo before the fight started.
- **Emberus Quest**: Carl receives the quest *Find out Who Killed my Son* from Emberus, instructing him to locate a temple of Nekhebit (who holds a grudge against Apito) and question the priestess.
- **Spiders Quest Complete**: Carl's *Spiders* quest is completed, and he is directed to a temple of Eileithyia to collect his boon. Eileithyia warns him not to warn Odette.
- **Token Fight**: Carl defeats a Level 45 Babababoon in a parking lot, collecting Token 2.
