# Chapter 398: Recap — Samantha's Floor 7 Report

## Summary
Samantha is in [[carl]]'s personal room redecorating it, complete with new wallpaper being installed by sluggalos, a carpet she "borrowed" from the Larracos College of Magic, and a swing she insists is a chandelier. She calls over [[bucket-boy]] and [[tipid]], a former crawler who recently had his memory wiped and restored, to explain the events of the ninth floor (Faction Wars).

In her characteristic chaotic and rambling style, Samantha recaps the key events of the Ninth Floor Faction Wars:
- The faction wars turned into a massive conflict where [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] led their own army alongside an NPC army.
- Old crawlers returned to the dungeon to fight.
- The NPC team and the city of Larracos were transferred to the Ascendency floor (Floor 12) via a spell, meaning they won't be seen on the tenth floor.
- The War Mage Rebellion, led by [[akuma]], killed Prince Stalwart, stole the Gate of the Feral Gods from Elle, and worked with [[agatha]], who has since disappeared.
- A conflict involving [[meatus]], Yarilo, and other gods occurred. yarilo died, and Meatus was defeated and passed through a Li Na chain, which prevented [[li-na]] from healing and drove her mad.
- [[li-jun]] died.
- Lucia's remaining dog died, and it is revealed that she has many children's minds trapped in her head.
- [[katia-grim]] and Donut wore matching hats that forced them to hunt a snake lady.
- Samantha tried to find her mother, [[theia]], but found a skyfowl (Justice Light) instead.
- Samantha merged her body with [[louis-santiago-2]] to save his life, giving him gills and making him her "cousin."
- [[katia-grim]] became pregnant (humorously attributed by Samantha to Louis's virility) and left the dungeon for a "nunnery" (sanctuary).
- Justice Light broke the Nothing, releasing the demon ladies who hate Samantha.
- [[scolopendra]], the great centipede at the bottom of the dungeon, has begun to wake up.

[[bigs]] the sluggalette joins and listens, approving of Samantha's explanation, while Tipid accepts the chaotic explanation and decides he has heard enough.

## Characters
- [[samantha]] — Recounts the chaotic events of the Faction Wars while taking over Carl's personal space.
- [[bucket-boy]] — A young Crocodilian healer who helps Samantha and tries to keep Tipid informed.
- [[tipid]] — A former crawler who has a gross scar on his head. He listens to Samantha's recap.
- [[bigs]] — A sluggalette NPC who supports Samantha's redecorating and listens to her story.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- [[the-collapse-of-rules]] — The breaking of the Nothing and the awakening of [[scolopendra]] signal structural failure in the crawl.
- [[crawler-resistance]] — The actions of the War Mage Rebellion and the displacement of the NPCs represent systemic disruption.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Samantha's narrative is unreliable but touches on the critical turning points of the previous book. Her casual treatment of monumental events like the death of Li Jun, the pregnancy of Katia, and the awakening of Scolopendra highlights her deteriorating sanity and alien perspective, contrasting with Tipid's serious demeanor.
