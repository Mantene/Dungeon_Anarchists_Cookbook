# Chapter 403: Chapter 4 — Team Free Love

## Summary
The Trauco Triplets (riders of the ladybug vehicle) are eliminated, leaving eight teams in Heat 1. Carl stops the food truck near the crashed, burning red Pontiac minivan of Team Free Love to help them. 

Donut uses her magic missiles to instantly kill the Level 90 Screeching Death Manatee, Miss Talulah, which had dragged the ladybug off the road. The system description reveals that the Screeching Death Manatees originate from a horror-themed safari planet called "Red Terror Place of Family Adventure," created by the Operatic Bankers' Federation (specifically Otto Banking Unit 3) after taking over a bankrupt orc's terraforming assets. 

After killing the manatee, Donut is given a choice: keep it dead for experience or let the audience vote on its fate. She opts for the audience vote.

Carl casts Temporary Water Source scrolls on a bucket and a kitchen bowl, providing water to the surviving bugbear racers, [[radoslav]] (Level 75 Jack of All Trades) and [[jasha]] (Level 65). They manage to extinguish the minivan's engine fire. Their teammate [[alevtina]] was killed in the crash.

Dressed in tie-dye like caricatures of hippies, Radoslav and Jasha explain their situation:
- NPCs who learn about the dungeon (either from a dying crawler or by glitching) regain memories of their past roles across different floors and the cold, empty "in-between" periods.
- They are offered a choice: go back to the cold, or volunteer in the races. If they win, they are promised true freedom in a real place where they do not have to fight. If they lose, their existence will finally end.

Since they cannot finish the race without their vehicle, Carl decides to tow the wrecked minivan to the finish line to prevent Team Free Love from being eliminated, explaining to them that keeping NPC teams alive prevents crawlers from having to race each other in future heats.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The revelation of the NPC "in-between" state (the cold) and their search for freedom through a death race adds a tragic layer to the floor's structure. The system offers them a false hope of freedom, but for many, the race is a welcome chance to end the cycle of forced reincarnation.
