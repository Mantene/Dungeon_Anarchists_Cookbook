# Chapter 401: Chapter 2 — The Food Truck

## Summary
Hedy pulls the garage door chain, revealing a black asphalt road outside where other teams are lining up in the dark. 

The spinning wheel lands on a box truck, which materializes as a **Big Shot Chicken** food truck. The vehicle is a 20-foot package delivery truck with a faux-1920s Prohibition-era gangster hood, chrome fenders, and a massive plastic tommy gun on the roof that partially blocks the driver's windshield view.

The dungeon intercom crackles with a disturbing, sobbing message from the System AI, who complains of pain and questions why the crawlers won't let her die. Zev quickly cuts in and takes over the broadcast:
- She explains the rules of the tenth floor, which is titled **"Don't come in last."**
- The floor consists of seven heats (races) of nine teams each, with 1,800 total teams of crawlers.
- Attendants (like [[hedy]]) must survive to repair vehicles or heal biological mounts between heats.
- Winners of heats choose three regular upgrades or one Golden upgrade. The second-to-last team receives an upgrade voted on by the audience. A Golden upgrade of choice is awarded to all survivors at the end of the fourth heat.
- Crawlers can attack other vehicles but cannot steal them or teleport themselves or their vehicles.
- Drivers must alternate each heat.

Carl starts the food truck's engine, which runs well, and prepares to drive the first heat.

## Characters
- [[carl]] — Takes the driver's seat of the newly acquired Big Shot Chicken food truck.
- [[princess-donut]] — Boards the truck as passenger for the first heat.
- [[hedy]] — Urges them to get moving and explains that coming in last means death.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- [[the-collapse-of-rules]] — The System AI's emotional breakdown and suffering over the intercom indicates severe structural decay.
- [[system-exploitation]] — The bizarre, game-show style rules of the race force crawlers into direct, lethal competition.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The Big Shot Chicken food truck is a highly impractical vehicle for a death race, typical of the dungeon's dark humor. The System AI's brief broadcast of raw pain ("Everything hurts") is a chilling reminder of the entity's unstable mental state and the physical toll of Scolopendra's awakening.
