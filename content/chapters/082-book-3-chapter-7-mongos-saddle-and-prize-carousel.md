# Book 3 Chapter 7: Mongo's Saddle and Prize Carousel

## Summary
At Station 131, Mongo reaches Level 15 ( pony-sized, 13 feet long) and gains the **Earthquake** skill. They enter the safe room (Arby's) and personal space. [[mordecai]] has upgraded his Alchemy Table to Level 3. He brews **Superb Constitution Buff Potions** (Donut drinks them, increasing her base Con by 4) and **Mordecai's Special Brew** (immortality/healing for 30s, but causes 10h potion sickness). 

Donut opens her Platinum Fan Box, receiving the **Enchanted Mongoliensis Saddle** (+15% spell damage for rider, anti-piercing; +20% Con and +20% melee damage to Mongo). 

Carl opens his Platinum Fan Box, which triggers the "Prize Carousel" segment hosted by [[chaco]], a Level 66 Pterolykos Song Bard NPC. Recognizing Chaco from a past tragedy involving his former manager and Odette, Mordecai throws a metal chair at Chaco's head. The system freezes the chair and banishes Mordecai to a 7-day "Naughty" stasis timeout. Carl is forced to choose one of nine blind prizes on a ticking timer, including his father's 1965 Harley Davidson motorcycle (which triggers a traumatic childhood memory of his father beating him). Carl notices a golden glint off a leather book decorated with the anarchy symbol. Guided by this hint, Carl selects Prize 6: *The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook* by Anonymous.

## Characters Introduced
- [[chaco]] — Song Bard Pterolykos game show host NPC

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[manager-benefit]]**: Mordecai's banishment leaves the party without their guide for almost the entire floor.
- **[[televised-exploitation]]**: The game show segment utilizing personal trauma as entertainment.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Mordecai's sudden, blind rage at Chaco reveals a deep, unhealed wound from his past crawl. The show's cruelty is on full display here, dangling Carl's father's motorcycle as a psychological trigger to manipulate his choice.
