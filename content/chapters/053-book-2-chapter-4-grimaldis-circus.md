# Book 2 Chapter 4: Grimaldi's Circus

## Summary
[[carl]], [[princess-donut]], and Mongo trek through the ruins. They chat with Zev, who reports that intergalactic viewers approve of their class/race selections. Suddenly, they are ambushed by Level 8 Circus Lemurs throwing knives from window ledges. The system announces a new Quest tab and offers the quest **The Show Must Go On**: discover why Grimaldi's Traveling Circus still exists and end its reign of terror.

They run from the lemurs and encounter a Level 10 stilt clown, **Terror the Clown**. Donut's magic missile breaks its knee, and Carl pummels its head in. Carl loots butcher knives, gold, and a Big Top Ticket. 

They take refuge at a tavern safe room named "The Belly-Rubbed Pug," run by a Bopca named [[gordo]]. Mordecai teleports into the tavern heavily drunk on mead and immediately passes out in his own vomit.

## Characters Introduced
- [[gordo]] (Bopca safe-room protector)

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[televised-exploitation]]**: The quest is presented with game-show fanfare, highlighting the commodification of the apocalypse.
- **[[survival]]**: Carl and Donut must fight and navigate nocturnal traps in the ruins.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The introduction of "Terror the Clown" highlights the horror elements of Floor 3, transforming childhood circus figures into grotesque, bloodthirsty monsters. Mordecai's drunken teleportation serves as comic relief but also underscores the stress and coping mechanisms of the guides.
