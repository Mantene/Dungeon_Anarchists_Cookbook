# Chapter 360: Meeting Milk

## Summary
Immediately following the collapse of the Blood Sultanate, Warlord [[carl]] uses the remaining seconds of his temporary immortality to continuously cast his Heal spell and reconstruct his body. [[kandy-newton]] (referred to as "Bigs"), a sluggalette crawler, is credited with killing [[princess-vinata]] (the Sepsis Whore), resulting in the Sultanate's defeat and the forfeiture of its assets to the [[the-princess-posse]].

[[samantha]] arrives in a charred state, furiously complaining that her mother, the goddess [[theia]], appeared in her temple not as her mother but as a male skyfowl sponsored by the Open Intellect Pacifist Network. [[baroness-victory]] remarks that such sponsor interference violates Faction Wars rules but is common. 

With the Khepri temple lobby sealed and the hallway blocked by mantaurs, ogres, and the clashing gods [[diwata]] and [[vertumnus]], Carl initiates "Operation Head Banger." He throws a Black Nimbus potion at [[brother-widespread-tempest]], a level 125 Ogre Basilica Cleric Guard. Carl then uses Megadeth's "Holy Wars... The Punishment Due" via alarm traps to drive the surrounding mantaurs berserk, forcing the now-vulnerable ogre to flee. Carl activates [[li-na]]'s borrowed skill, **Profane Iron**, sprouting prehensile chains from his flesh to dispatch the remaining mantaurs.

Carl and the retreating lobby team—[[katia-grim]], [[prepotente]], [[li-jun]], and [[zhang]]—escape into the hidden Calligrapher's Guild. There, Carl meets the guildmaster, [[milk]], who is a Xenopus (half-frog, half-bat) creature. Carl recognizes her as the author of the sixth edition of the [[the-anarchist-cookbook]] and his sister. Overwhelmed by physical pain and emotional realization, Carl collapses into her embrace and weeps.

## Characters
* [[carl]]: Heals his shattered body, executes a tactical retreat using Megadeth's "Holy Wars" to berserk the mantaurs, and uses "Profane Iron" chains before discovering and embracing his sister, [[milk]].
* [[kandy-newton]] (Bigs): A level 29 sluggalette crawler who killed Vinata. She has pink-dyed hair, facial piercings, and a hatchet embedded in her head. She assists Carl as a sapper.
* [[samantha]]: Charred from her encounter in the temple, she complains about her mother's sponsor appearing as a male skyfowl.
* [[baroness-victory]]: Observes Carl and discusses faction rules regarding sponsored deities.
* [[brother-widespread-tempest]]: A level 125 Ogre Basilica Cleric Guard who worships [[taranis]]. Hit by Carl's Black Nimbus potion, losing his "Untouchable" status, and flees.
* [[milk]]: Guildmaster of the Calligrapher's Guild and author of the sixth edition of the Cookbook; revealed to be Carl's sister.
* [[katia-grim]]: Contacts Carl about the lockdown and meets him at the guild.
* [[prepotente]]: Guides the group to the hidden guild entrance.
* [[li-jun]]: Escapes the lobby and joins the group in the guild.
* [[zhang]]: Expresses relief at surviving the lockdown.
* [[princess-donut]]: Messages Carl from safety, confirming they did not need to use the diggers.

## Themes & Plot Threads
* **The Cookbook Lineage**: Carl's discovery of [[milk]] as his sister and the author of the sixth edition of the [[the-anarchist-cookbook]] deepens the personal stakes of the crawl.
* **Administrative Interference**: The Open Intellect Pacifist Network's sponsor bypasses normal deity appearance rules, displaying the tension between sponsors and system rules.
* **Physical and Mental Degradation**: Carl's description of his body reconstructing and the mental strain of the crawl highlights his growing trauma.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's emotional breakdown in Milk's arms is a rare moment of vulnerability where his "mask" slips. The physical agony of reconstruction combined with the revelation of his sister's presence in the dungeon breaks through his stoic exterior.
