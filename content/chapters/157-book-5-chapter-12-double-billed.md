# Book 5 Chapter 12: Double-Billed

## Summary
- **Eegees Safe Zone**: Carl pulls Donut into the pub *Eegees*, run by the Bopca [[marta]], to calm her down from a severe panic attack.
- **Donut's Breakdown**: Donut is terrified of being abandoned, expressing anxiety over Katia leaving, Carl's dangerous plans, Mongo leaving her, and the Floor 9 death trap (Crown of the Sepsis Whore). Carl cups her face and promises he will never leave her behind on any floor.
- **Dino Pacification**: Mongo stumbles back into town exhausted and collapses (health bar full). Kiwi's pack's tags turn white (friendly/neutral) for Donut, but remain red for Carl.
- **Double-Billing Achievement**: Carl receives the *Double-Billed!* achievement for being featured on two separate feeds simultaneously, rewarding him with a Platinum Fan Box. A guard's missile detonates in the distance.

## Characters Introduced
- [[marta]] — Bopca proprietor of the Eegees pub in Point Mongo.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[search-for-beatrice]]**: Donut's fear of abandonment is laid bare, forcing Carl to commit to a solid, unbreakable pact of mutual survival.
- **[[the-recital]]**: Mongo's safe return and Kiwi's pacification shift the dinosaur threat to a neutral status for Donut.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's panic attack is a rare and moving moment of vulnerability. It highlights the immense psychological pressure she faces, carrying the burden of her ex-owner's betrayal and the looming threat of the Floor 9 naga death trap. Carl's promise to never leave her reinforces their bond as a team. The "Double-Billed" achievement highlights how showrunners are manipulating their feeds for higher viewership.
