# Book 4 Chapter 27: Confronting Lusca

## Summary
The crawlers defeat the Level 82 City Boss Lusca, but Vadim dies during his escape attempt, and Carl uses the Gate of the Feral Gods to teleport the survivors.
- **The Feeding Frenzy**: Under a 10-minute timer inside the submarine *Akula*'s bridge, Carl mixes a Potion of Bloodlust with explosives and duct-tapes it inside a bolted minifridge. The resulting frenzy drives the baby octo-sharks to cannibalize each other, clearing the room.
- **Vadim's Death**: Vadim seals himself in the escape pod room and launches the last pod. The pod is intercepted by the system's pain-amplifying jellyfish, and Vadim dies before reaching the surface.
- **Defeating Lusca**: Carl, Katia, and Tran exit into the open mouth. Carl casts *Protective Shell* (static 12m sphere). As Lusca swims at 35 km/h, she hits the sphere like a wall, peeling her top jaw back and exposing her to 1,500m depth pressure. Katia uses a pressurized steam propeller and a 25-foot buzzsaw ("Leatherface") to slice Lusca's brain, killing her.
- **The Teleportation**: Suffocating under deep-sea pressure (2% HP drain/sec), Carl uses the Gate of the Feral Gods (winding box and two watches) to teleport them to the beach of Pandinus. The portal's exit water dumps on the beach, toppling a metal wall and a guard tower. They run to the inn, enter the personal space, and the world rumbles as the feral god is summoned.
