# Chapter 291: Raul's Ascension and the Nothing Teleport

## Summary
- **Leap of Faith**: Carl catches Samantha in midair to slow his descent as he drops to the beach.
- **Midnight Express**: Carl uses the *Midnight Express* card to temporarily assume the Deckmaster role, enabling Donut to act freely. He summons the crab [[raul|Raul]] (Level 55) and [[alpha-male-carl|Alpha Male Carl]].
- **Refilling the Souls**: Raul cannot perform his reproductive duties to spawn the necessary souls without seeing baby seals. Alpha Carl berates and threatens Raul, providing the pressure he needs to fertilize the ocean. This generates millions of crab sperm (which the system registers as mobs/souls).
- **Nothing Teleport**: Carl triggers his teleport trap to teleport himself directly into the trap inside Samantha's head (which links to the Nothing/Sheol) to lower his health to critical levels.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The absurd comedy of Alpha Carl berating Raul to help him perform on a dead beach highlights the wild, black-comedy tone of the series. Carl's calculated decision to teleport into the Nothing to induce critical health is a terrifying tactical exploit, relying on low-health buffs to save the day.
