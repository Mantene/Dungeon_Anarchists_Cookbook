# Chapter 384: Chapter 76 — The Oak Fell

## Summary
Donut commits her third Atrocity by casting *War Crime*, gaining the druid class "The Oak Fell" and the title "Champion of Nekhebit." The battle ball breaks, releasing thousands of enemies. The chaos goddess [[eris]] briefly freezes time. She spins a wheel that spares Carl from Meatus's castration curse, making his Level-15 *Pied Piper* skill from Harpocrates permanent instead. Eris warns Carl that the War Mages traded the [[the-gate-of-the-feral-gods]] to [[paca]] at the Midnight Market, that [[britney-proskurina]] is possessed by an axe, and offers to help him kill Taranis, Emberus, and Hellik. 

Once time unfreezes, Donut combines *Bijanbi* and *Astral Paw* to pull all enemies, including the god Meatus, into a giant spectral cat's paw. Donut swipes her paw down repeatedly, slamming the enemies through a massive lattice of chains created by Li Na. The enemies and Meatus are shredded to pieces. The music "Break Stuff" by Limp Bizkit plays as a system track. Carl vomits over the side of the Nest tower in horror.

## Characters
- [[carl]] — Meets Eris, gains permanent *Pied Piper* skill, and witnesses the gruesome finisher.
- [[princess-donut]] — Casts *War Crime*, becomes "The Oak Fell" and "Champion of Nekhebit", and executes the enemies.
- [[eris]] — Goddess of Chaos who freezes time, grants Carl a permanent skill, and shares crucial secrets.
- [[meatus]] — Feral god who is shredded through the chain lattice by Donut.
- [[li-na]] — Deploys the massive lattice of chains to slice the enemies.
- [[dong-quixote]] — Instructed to run from the falling carnage.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **Atrocities and Classes**: The structural reward system of Faction Wars that grants powerful classes to those who commit horrific deeds.
- **Divine Intervention**: Eris's chaotic interference and her warning about the Gate of the Feral Gods.
- **The Physical Toll of War**: Carl's visceral reaction to the slaughter of thousands of enemies.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
This chapter features one of the most violent and memorable sequences of Faction Wars. The juxtaposition of a giant, adorable cat's paw slicing thousands of soldiers through a chain-link fence like "cherry pies" to the tune of Limp Bizkit illustrates Matt Dinniman's signature blend of absurdity and horror. Donut's transformation into "The Oak Fell" signals her growing power—and the system's attempts to lock her into the roles designed for gods.
