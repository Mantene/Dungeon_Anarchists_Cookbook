# Chapter 429: Chapter 30 — Sugar Hermits and the Princess Throne

## Summary
- **Prep for Heat Three**: Carl and Donut return to the garage. Because the kangaroo suit makes it hard for Carl to use the pedals, they select **Gluteus Maxx** (the hairy stripper) to drive, as he is the best at Mario Kart.
- **Dorota the Throne**: Donut sits in her new upgrade chair, the **Smart Companion Royal Pet Throne (Ultimate Diamond Pampered Pet Princess edition)**, which she names **Dorota**. The chair talks with a Polish accent, agrees with Donut, and constantly bickers with Dr. Metcalf.
- **Starting Shield**: Zev announces the race rules: each team has a starting shield providing invulnerability for the first 90 seconds. The track is a steep 30-degree decline asphalt road.
- **Drug Overdose**: Carl discovers that Gluteus Maxx took some outworlder uppers that Samantha found. Gluteus is high, foaming at the mouth, and raving about "tasting time." Carl realized he has no choice but to have Donut prepare her *Laundry Day* spell to strip the kangaroo suit off him, despite the risk of flaying.
- **Sugar Hermits Wave**: The track is flooded with migrating **Sugar Hermits** (Level 11 skull-like mobs that wear Day of the Dead painted skulls as shells and use purple tentacles to extract brains and bones). They are migrating toward a giant kaiju beetle skull at the finish line.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's new upgrade chair, Dorota, is a hilarious commentary on luxury pet items in the galaxy, providing a foil to the cynical Dr. Metcalf. The humor of Gluteus' drug-induced raving about high-elf sweat is balanced by the immediate threat of the Sugar Hermits plowing into the windshield.
