# Book 1 Chapter 17: Hobgoblin Pus

## Summary
[[carl]] loots the remaining engineering supplies from the goblin workshop, including tools, alchemical liquids, and tons of black powder. He places the black powder barrels on a cart, topping them with metal scraps (screws, bolts) to act as shrapnel. To detonate it, Carl uses a valuable C4-like explosive called "Hobgoblin Pus" paired with magical single-use detonators. 

Their plan is to push the cart into the boss room, shut the door to avoid being locked inside, run for cover, and detonate it. Carl positions the cart, and [[princess-donut]] opens the door. Carl heaves the cart into the room, and Donut slams the door shut. As they run to a safe distance, Carl prepares to press the trigger. Just before he fires the detonator, Donut casually mentions that she saw a lot of babies, pregnant goblins, and elderly goblins in the room.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival]]**: Carl employs a "chickenshit" but highly practical method to defeat the boss without risking a direct locked-room confrontation.
- **[[televised-exploitation]]**: Donut "directs" the action like a movie scene, treating the life-or-death scenario as a performance.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's desire to survive leads him to ignore the traditional rules of the dungeon's boss battles. However, the dark twist at the end of the chapter—where Donut reveals the presence of non-combatants and infants in the room right before the detonation—highlights the moral horror of their situation. Carl is forced to choose between his survival and his humanity, with the system actively setting up situations that strip away the latter.
