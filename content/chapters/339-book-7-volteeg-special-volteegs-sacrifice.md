# Chapter 339: Volteeg Special — Volteeg's Sacrifice

## Summary
This chapter is told from the perspective of [[volteeg]], a gargoyle crawler and author of the seventh edition of the [[the-anarchist-cookbook|Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook]]. 

Volteeg reflects on his past as a pet starling belonging to **Mistress Henspar**, who loved him. To gain intelligence, he turned into a gargoyle on Floor 3, a decision he later regretted because it permanent changed his body and took away his ability to sing. After Henspar died on Floor 7, Volteeg was consumed by rage and shame, leaving only a single entry in the Cookbook at the end of Floor 9 before taking an NPC guard deal. During Faction Wars, he was redeployed to Earth as Weeper Commander Volteeg for [[the-operatic-collective|The Operatic Collective]], his true crawler identity masked by a Pacifist Network mole.

Following the death of [[king-rust]], the Bloc alliance begins fracturing as [[warlord-hortense|Warlord Hortense]] claims leadership, disputed by [[prince-stalwart]]. Hortense orders the Operatic forces to attack the crawlers. Volteeg, prompted by [[rosetta]] and [[porthus]] to finally take action against the system, decides to sacrifice himself. He manually bypasses the fire control system of his Weeper tank, filling it with volatile energy balls. Playing Mozart's classical music, he drives his tank directly into the center of the Bloc army next to Warlord Hortense's caravan and detonates it. 

The suicide blast kills Volteeg and 10,000 Bloc soldiers, including Warlord Hortense, instantly eliminating the Operatic Collective from Faction Wars.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Volteeg's sacrifice is a moving story of a former pet who suffered immense grief and shame inside the dungeon. His decision to finally act on the Cookbook's teachings highlights the legacy of the Cookbook authors. By eliminating the Operatic Collective, Volteeg significantly shifts the power balance on Floor 9, showing how individual acts of rebellion can disrupt the Syndicate's entire operation.
