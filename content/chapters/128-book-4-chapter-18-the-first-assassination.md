# Book 4 Chapter 18: The First Assassination

## Summary
Carl executes his first planned assassination of a Syndicate administrator, utilizing a historic exploit from the Cookbook.
- **Preparations in the Crafting Room**: Carl infuses a *Hobgoblin Disco Ball* (unstable deflagrating tossable made of a smoke curtain and invisibility potion). Robot Donut accidentally knocks it off the table, setting it off. It coats Carl and the robot in technicolor rainbow dust.
- **Entering the Submarine**: Carl, Donut, and the dusty robot teleport to the talk show trailer (an underwater security submarine). Admin **Loita** is present on the couch, wearing her water-spraying rebreather. Carl tests the room's security, and Loita boasts that the trailer is shielded during taping; no one can enter or leave.
- **The Argument**: Donut and Carl confront Loita about Zev's demotion. Loita admits they performed "unspeakable things" to Zev's mother and aunts to force Zev back into compliance, calling human culture a "rot" and "dry vermin contagion."
- **Rigging the Toy**: Before entering the studio, Carl places the robot Donut on the couch next to Loita. He opens the toy's battery compartment tab to trigger the 5-second self-destruct countdown, then leaves.
- **The Explosion and Dilemma**: During the commercial taping with soother hosts Gravo and Liddi, the trailer shakes as the toy detonates. The explosion obliterates the couch and severely wounds Loita (missing legs and an arm). The blast triggers a security lockdown shield.
- **Loita's Death**: The picture window is cracked, held only by a forcefield. Because of the security shield, Loita cannot teleport to receive medical aid. Borant faces a choice: drop the shield to save Loita (which would flood the sub, killing their top-grossing stars Carl and Donut) or let her die. They choose to let Loita die. Carl watches her succumb to her injuries.
- **Cookbook Entry**: Carl writes his first entry in the *Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook* under the tag "One", thanking the author of the 19th edition (Crawler Coolie) whose notes on talk show trailer shields inspired the assassination.
