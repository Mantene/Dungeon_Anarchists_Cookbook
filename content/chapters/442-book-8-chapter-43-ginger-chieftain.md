# Chapter 442: Chapter 43 — Ginger Chieftain

## Summary
As they enter the outskirts of cannibal territory, [[olga]] enlarges the truck's tires to handle the bumpy road. [[imani-c]] and [[elle-mcgibbons]]'s APV follows closely. The team is attacked by pale, wood-masked cannibals dropping from the trees, who explode like tomato soup against the vehicle's shield. 

[[princess-donut]] casts her *Torch* spell at full blast to illuminate the dark jungle. Ahead, a natural bridge decorated with skeletons is defended by a hundred cannibals. Donut uses her newly empowered *Astral Paw* spell to sweep the bridge clear and snap the tree structure, though the spell triggers a long cooldown.

The road is blocked by [[great-rusty]], a Level 99 neighborhood boss and Chieftain of the southern ginger cannibal settlement. The dungeon description parodies redheads as genetically engineered perverts immune to acid and electricity, capable of sucking souls. Great Rusty casts *Soul Suck*. Carl is immune due to his *Mind Balance* skill, and Donut is immune due to her stats, but Olga is hit with the "Gingered" debuff, knocking her into an unconscious, draining state.

Carl pulls Olga from the driver's seat, and [[finley]] takes the wheel. Donut fires a magic missile, exploding Great Rusty instantly. Olga revives but remains in a mindless fugue state. [[mordecai]] messages Carl, explaining Olga needs a cleric's healing.

A mandatory quest, **"Pasty Inferno,"** is triggered: Carl's team must locate and kill [[maurice]], chieftain of the northern ginger cannibal tribe, or they will be unable to finish the race. Carl gets into a brief argument with his GPS AI, Dr. Metcalf, over the unexpected quest.
