# Book 1 Chapter 46: The Menagerie Key

## Summary
The dungeon rumbles as the boss fight ends. [[carl]] loots the **Floor 2 Neighborhood Map** and a **Master Pen Key** (which only works once for non-Kobolds). 

They enter the main arena room and trigger two achievements:
- **I’ll take the ceramic dalmatian, Pat!** (Discovering a reward room).
- **Menagerie!** (Discovering a pet reward room; the key opens only one cage).

They find ten cages containing potential pets, including a green slime, a rat, a **Tummy Acher** (a two-legged meatball with a mohawk), and a Brindle Grub. [[princess-donut]] insists on the last cage:
**Mongoliensis** (A baby dinosaur resembling a raw piece of chicken with pink downy feathers, a serpentine tail, tiny arms, and a toothy beak).

With a 30-second countdown ticking, Carl unlocks the cage. The creature attacks, and Carl holds it down while Donut feeds it pet biscuits. The creature turns out to be male, and Donut names him **Mongo**. 

Mongo is a Level 1 pet-class mob. He remains red-tagged (hostile) and snaps at Donut's nose, biting her. The description warns that Mongoliensis are stubborn, will attack any mob to the death, and are fully grown at Level 15. Carl realizes they must get Mongo to bond with Donut before the floor collapse, or he will teleport away.

## Characters Introduced
- **[[mongo]]**: Donut's newly acquired velociraptor pet.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[carls-class-selection]]**: Carl and Donut gain a pet-class companion, adding a melee summon to their team dynamics.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The pet reward room is a classic RPG trope. While Carl pragmatically favors the Brindle Grub (hoping for a flying Vespa mount), Donut's aesthetic preference wins out. Mongo's design (a raw pink chicken velociraptor) is hilariously ugly, and his instant hostility towards Donut sets up a comedic training arc.
