# Book 1 Chapter 15: The Gilmore Goblins

## Summary
With 3 days and 2 hours left before Floor 1 collapses, [[carl]] analyzes the dungeon layout, realizing it is structured as a grid of squares corresponding to the Seattle streets above. While traveling, they are attacked by a Level 2 green slime. Carl's physical attacks burn him, melting his right glove and jacket sleeve, though his Damage Reflect kills the slime. 

They enter the goblin neighborhood, using Carl's Goblin Pass brand and [[princess-donut]]'s high Charisma (37) to charm three Level 2 goblins (whom Donut names [[ba]], [[face]], and [[murdock]]). They are led to the Goblin Workshop, which houses engineers, steam machinery, and two Level 7 Goblin Shamankas. Donut charms the shamankas (naming them [[rory]] and [[lorelai]]), who break down crying about having to eat their fathers raw to graduate Shamanka University. 

Carl trades the two baggies of Bad Llama methamphetamine for a steam-powered chopper with a sidecar, coal, and grenades. Rory explains that goblins cannot descend the stairs without dissolving. Instead, they sleep during floor collapses and wake up for the next season. Rory hopes that by killing crawlers, her clan will be sent deeper into the dungeon where they can live in peace.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival]]**: Carl suffers chemical burns from a slime, prompting them to seek a vehicle to cover ground faster.
- **[[systemic-oppression]]**: The tragic cycle of the mobs, who are trapped on their floors and forced to fight crawlers in the hope of someday finding peace.
- **[[survival-of-the-collapse]]**: Carl and Donut acquire a steam-powered chopper to aid in their search for a staircase.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's Gilmore Girls references (naming the shamankas Rory and Lorelai) and A-Team references contrast with the tragic realization of the mobs' plight. Rory's explanation of how the mobs experience the collapse exposes another layer of the Syndicate's cruelty, showing that the monsters are just as trapped as the human crawlers.
