# Book 2 Chapter 14: The One-Eyed Narwhal

## Summary
[[carl]], [[princess-donut]], and [[mordecai]] locate a safe room pub called **The One-Eyed Narwhal**, operated by a Level-30 human NPC named [[fitz]]. After installing the electric fang caps on [[mongo]], Carl assigns 10 stat points to Strength and 2 to Constitution. 

During the nightly recap show, they watch a montage of Mongo defending Donut, and see updates on other crawlers, including a Tigran crawler and a goat lady ([[miriam-dom]] and [[prepotente]]) who enhanced her pets with biscuits. An older, blood-stained Orc NPC named [[gum-gum]] approaches their table begging for help, but Mordecai rudely dismisses her. 

The next day, Carl and Donut sell their low-level loot at various skyfowl-run general stores. They spend 15,000 gold on a magical pet carrier cube to allow Mongo to rest properly at night. Later, they grind slowly against slow-moving, psionic brain-boiler mobs. Carl levels his *Iron Punch* to 10 (unlocking the *Breadbasket* stun effect), while Donut reaches Level 16. 

At the edge of town, they find GumGum piling up a mountain of twisted, bloodless female corpses. She explains that someone is dumping dead prostitutes in the alleys every morning. Carl accepts her quest, triggering **The Sex Workers Who Fell from the Heavens**.

## Characters Introduced
- [[fitz]] — Human Safe Room pub proprietor.
- [[gum-gum]] — Older Orc street sweeper NPC.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival]]**: Grinding mobs, managing pet fatigue, and negotiating with merchants.
- **[[over-city-mechanics]]**: Introduction of safe room owners who are not Bopca, and the presence of municipal cleaning quests.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's soft spot for the helpless NPCs shines through here. Even though Mordecai warns them that the system is baiting them with garbage quests, Carl's basic humanity won't allow him to ignore a crying woman, setting them up for further complications.
