# Chapter 349: Chapter 44 — Blood Hunter Reveal

## Summary
The warlords discuss the creature [[juice-box|Juice Box]] became. [[architect-houston|Architect Houston]] explains it is a trans-dimensional entity called The Hag, The Unwashed, The Stalker, The Last Light, The Thirst, The Fell, The Inexorable, or "The Beautiful Place," which people see at the moment of death. Cascadia reveals the showrunners are banned from using it as a mob.

As [[princess-donut|Donut]] stalls for time by proposing "Princess TV", she notes that the Sultanate has its own TV station. [[odette|Odette]] reveals that the Sultanate owns the "Blood Hunter Holdings" studio, which produced the failed drama *Blood Hunter*. 

The name *Blood Hunter* strikes [[carl|Carl]] like a meteor. He remembers that *Blood Hunter* was the show where the host and hunter killed Miriam Dom, the owner of [[prepotente|Prepotente]]. Carl puts the pieces together: Prepotente must have tracked the naga connection to Miriam's death. Carl realizes that Vinata's standing army is already dead, slaughtered by Prepotente before Faction Wars even truly began. Vinata has been bluffing this entire time. Carl laughs hysterically at the realization, knowing that **Operation: Snake Pit** is compromised. Donut mocks Vinata, telling her, "You done stuck your pecker in the wrong beehive."

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's realization is both comical and terrifying. The Blood Sultanate, which seemed like an imposing force under Vinata's electric charisma, is actually a paper tiger. Prepotente's silent, devastating rampage against the naga forces before the battle even began highlights his immense power and single-minded focus on avenging his owner. This completely alters the dynamics of the raid.
