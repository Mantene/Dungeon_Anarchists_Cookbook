# Chapter 358: Boss Battle Khepri

## Summary

The boss battle in the Temple of Khepri begins. The AI provides extensive lore: Vinata's secret husband is dead, and her kingdom's plans to seize shipbuilding factories are being leaked. Her team includes herself, three military veterans, and two cousins (a graphic designer and PR agent).

The fight teams are Carl, Katia, Prepotente vs. Vinata and six nagas, plus Bautista, Li Jun, Li Na, and Zhang on comms. The AI opens betting and sets a 30-second countdown. Zhang gets a flashing "2X Jackpot if Zhang Lives!" sign, and the AI openly speculates about his death.

Admiral Corinne Whimpleshin, the Blood Sultanate's camel adjutant, calls foul over the gods, but Victory dismisses her—temple rules apply. Khepri, confused by the frozen scene, chitters angrily. The countdown ends and chaos erupts.

The chapter provides rich lore on Khepri: a level 250 god of Rebirth and Sunrise whose head is literally a bug, retainer of Adrasteia after Khnum's mysterious death. His "endless" temple is a long table with 433 kinds of bugs. The AI's commentary reveals unprecedented frustration at having to generate an "endless" temple.

The boss battle setup is simultaneously absurd and terrifying. The information about Vinata's kingdom falling apart adds unexpected depth to the antagonist.
