# Chapter 290: Leveled-Up Frank and Refill the World with Souls

## Summary
- **Summoning Wave**: The card battle progresses as Li Na summons Xing Tian (Level 80) and Katia summons the skull-headed Útburður raven Annie (Level 80).
- **Stealing Banjo**: Donut uses *The Thief* card to steal a card from Amayon, summoning [[banjo|Banjo the Level 120 Hell Scrub]].
- **Naamah's Attack**: Amayon summons the Level 120 succubus Naamah, who pins Carl and shatters her teeth against his technology-based shield before Banjo rips her apart.
- **Raid Stash and Frank Q**: Ysalte plays *Raid Stash* to steal cards from the crawlers, summoning **Leveled-Up Frank (Sober Edition)**, a Level 100 Night Elf Blood Assassin created from the deceased crawler [[frank-q]].
- **Quest Update**: All of Amayon's cards are discarded or destroyed (the last one by Tran's exploding wheelchair). The quest pauses and updates: Amayon lacks the souls required to cast his return portal because the world is mostly empty. The crawlers must \"refill the world with souls\" of the same type that triggered the eviction event (seals, crabs, slugs).
- **Carl's Leap**: As the match freezes for 30 seconds, Carl decides to leap off the roof of the skyscraper toward Samantha, intending to take control of the deck.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The resurrection of Frank Q as a Level 100 assassin totem serves as a chilling reminder of how the dungeon reuses deceased crawlers as mechanical tools. Carl's literal leap of faith off the skyscraper roof shows his willingness to engage in high-risk physical stunts to circumvent the card battle's limits.
