# Book 5 Chapter 18: Beatrice is Alive

## Summary
- **Rescue Explanation**: Odette explains she had Beatrice rescued from surface bounty hunters who wanted to sell her to Borant to make her a country boss. 
- **The Blackmail**: Odette is using Beatrice off-world to blackmail Borant, securing a higher revenue share contract.
- **The Ambush Warning**: Zev warns Carl that he won a CrawlCon spot in his Platinum Fan Box. He will be transferred to a fan interaction event alone (without Donut), where another show planned to ambush him with the news of Beatrice. Odette's interview is designed to preempt that story.
- **Reunion Decision**: Odette requests permission to bring Beatrice on the show. Carl is furious, but Donut decides they will do the reunion to face the information on their own terms.
- **Zev's Warning**: Zev warns Carl to shut up and focus on supporting Donut, indicating deeper corporate/political changes are in play. Lexis calls them to the set.

## Characters Introduced
- [[lexis]] — Odette's producer and personal assistant.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[search-for-beatrice]]**: The confrontation decision is made to preempt the showrunner ambush.
- **[[systemic-oppression]]**: The exploitation of Beatrice as a country boss candidate and Odette's corporate blackmail show the disregard for human lives.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's decision to go on with the reunion shows a massive leap in maturity. Rather than breaking down or allowing Carl to shield her, she chooses to face Beatrice on camera to preempt being blindsided later. Carl's threat to kill Odette if this is a game shows his protective rage, while Zev's warnings suggest that the corporate struggles behind the crawl are reaching a boiling point.
