# Book 4 Chapter 6: The Collateral Secret

## Summary
Carl's group infiltrates City Hall to uncover the Dromedarians' leverage over the gnomes.
- **Infiltration**: During the hot midday hours when the town is asleep, Firas M uses **Puddle Jumper** to teleport Carl, Donut, and Louis Santiago 2 to the second-floor balcony. Donut uses **Hole** to bypass the metal security door.
- **The Guards**: They reach the guardroom. Carl uses a low-damage sparkler to turn the guards' dots red, allowing Louis to knock them out with **Cloud of Exhaust**. Donut uses *Hole* to remove the door bolts, though she leaves the door frame structurally broken.
- **Changeling Principals**: The guards revert to their true forms: **Changeling Principals** (Svern, Level 49; and another), a power-hungry shapeshifter sect. Carl kills Svern and loots his sapphire head for Mordecai's alchemy, while Louis and Firas kill the other, leveling up to 24.
- **The Necropolis Map**: Carl descends into the low-ceiling stone tomb room and loots the **Map of the Necropolis of Anser**, adding a massive catacomb maze to his system. He also loots three shriveled black mushrooms.
- **The Hostage Gnome**: Carl finds **Wynne** (Level 50 Gnome Flesh Mechanic, uncle of Commandant Kane) chained past the quadrant boundary forcefield. Wynne was held hostage by Dromedarians to prevent Commandant Kane from bombing the town. He was being drugged by **Henrik** to force him to resurrect the beast "Quetzalcoatlus."
- **Quest Failure**: A group of Subterranean crawlers—**Low Thi** (Level 25 human D-Bag Geek), **Tyler Storm** (Level 26 human Weather Engineer), **Mike Barnes 3** (Level 23 human Banana Farmer), and **Morris Sp** (Level 23 spider-centaur Freelance Psychiatrist)—arrive from the tomb side. Low Thi kills Wynne with a spear, hoping to loot a map, which fails Carl's quest and awards Low Thi the *Cockblock* achievement.
- **Coordination**: Carl tells Morris Sp (who has Desperado Club access) to meet him at the club in an hour to transcribe the Necropolis map.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Low Thi's impulsive execution of Wynne showcases the self-sabotaging behavior of lower-level crawlers, who act out of short-sighted greed rather than cooperative planning. This failed quest destroys a major diplomatic asset—the uncle of the gnome leader—which will likely escalate the conflict between the dirigible gnomes and Hump Town.
