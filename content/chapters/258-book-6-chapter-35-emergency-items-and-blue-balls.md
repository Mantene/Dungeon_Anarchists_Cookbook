# Book 6 Chapter 35: Emergency Items and Blue Balls

## Summary
Carl and Donut appear on Odette's show, where they review the fallout of the Desperado Club flood and discover the hostile Emergency Action Items filed against them by Empress D'Nadia.
- **Desperado Fallout**: Odette reveals that Carl's actions at the Desperado Club forced a mass exit of 35 trapped faction officials, who were promptly eaten by concierge sharks in the flooded city or forced to use their fail-safes. Donut jokingly calls it a "happy little accident."
- **Crawl Talk**: Donut embarrasses Carl by mentioning "blue balls" on air, linking it to Carl's complaints about his ex-girlfriend Beatrice.
- **Preproduction Scrutiny**: With Harbinger (the goat liaison) watching from the shadows, Carl and Donut are careful not to discuss the suspended face-to-face meetings or the NPC rebellion.
- **Rejected Action Items**: Odette displays a rejected proposal by Donut to rename Crown Prince Stalwart to "Captain Enormous" (which Donut hints was due to him boasting about his anatomy).
- **D'Nadia's Betrayal**: Odette shows three rejected *Emergency Action Items* proposed by Warlord Empress D'Nadia of the Prism:
  1. Delete all existing NPCs on the Larracos level.
  2. Disallow crawlers from participating in Faction Wars.
  3. Remove the Princess Posse and delete Carl and Donut.
- **System Rejection**: All three of D'Nadia's emergency items were rejected by the system. Donut is furious, realizing D'Nadia tried to delete them and likely voted against Mongo in the previous floor's pet show.
- **The Adjutant Role**: Odette explains that they need to pick an adjutant immediately. She describes the role as a third-party referee and consultant who accompanies the warlord onto the battlefield, remains protected from damage, calls fouls, and provides daily recaps.
- **Odette's Withdrawal**: Odette announces that she cannot serve as their adjutant this season, displaying three other potential candidates instead.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **Faction Wars Sabotage**: Empress D'Nadia's attempt to delete Carl, Donut, and all Larracos NPCs highlights the cutthroat nature of the Faction Wars politics.
- **Televised Exploitation**: The studio interview serves as a battlefield of information, where Carl and Donut must navigate strict censorship from Harbinger while appealing to their galactic fans.
- **The Adjutant Mechanic**: The introduction of the adjutant role sets up a critical choice that will shape the team's legal protections and strategic options on Floor 9.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's outrage at D'Nadia is a mix of tactical realization and childish betrayal (especially regarding the pet show vote). The revelation of D'Nadia's emergency items shows that the factions are terrified of the Princess Posse and the wildcard element Carl represents. Carl's grin during the discussion of the flooded Desperado Club shows his satisfaction in disrupting the cozy, stagnant arrangement the faction officials had made at the expense of the crawlers.
