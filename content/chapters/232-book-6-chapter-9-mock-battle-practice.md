# Book 6 Chapter 9: Mock Battle Practice

## Summary
Carl tests the new card combat mechanics in the saferoom's Mock Battle Arena.
- **Entering the Arena**: On December 12 (twelve days before the Assault phase), Carl enters the saferoom's Mock Battle Arena.
- **Practice Deck Settings**: Carl's deck is incomplete, containing only one active totem card ([[geraldo]]) out of the required six, alongside four utility/special cards. The system issues warnings about the incomplete deck and notes that the arena environment and his own explosive attacks can still harm him.
- **The Squonk Combat**: Carl initiates a low-difficulty practice match. The system summons a Level 35 **Squonk** (an ugly, depressed, constantly weeping creature from Eastern US folklore that has no attacks). Carl plays Geraldo to defeat it.
- **Practice Assessment**: After completing the mock fight and resetting his deck, Carl assesses that they must acquire more card totems before attempting medium-difficulty practice.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[t-ghee-card-combat]]**: Practical testing of the card deck mechanics, showing the limitations of a near-empty deck.
- **[[ghosts-of-earth]]**: Introduction of folklore mobs from other regions (the Squonk) in the mock training environment.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The inclusion of the Squonk, a cryptid from Pennsylvania folklore defined by its ugliness and self-loathing, provides a moment of dark, melancholic humor. Carl's caution regarding the consumable cards highlights his pragmatic approach to managing his resources in the dungeon.
