# Book 5 Epilogue: Welcome to the Great Race

## Summary
- **Floor 7 Start**: The crawlers enter Floor 7, [[great-race]], which has a 20-day collapse timer. Leaders are ranked (Carl is Rank 3 with a 1,600,000 gold bounty).\n- **Squad Limits**: Parties are split into squads of 5 or less; Carl and Donut form a squad of 2. [[prepotente]] leaves the party, and Donut becomes squad leader.\n- **The Crown's Curse**: As [[eva-sigrid]] dies, she places the [[crown-of-the-sepsis-whore]] on [[katia-grim]]'s head, where it melds into her form. This enters Katia into the Blood Sultanate line of succession, restricting stair access to Floor 10 such that only one of Donut or Katia can advance.
