# Book 5 Chapter 16: Were-Beavers

## Summary
- **The Mullet Debate**: [[clint-smashgrab]] and [[holger]] accompany Carl and Donut back to Point Mongo. Holger boasts that his mullet is a "sign of dominance," leading to bickering and a fistfight between the two were-castors in the mud.
- **Chee History**: Holger and Clint detail their history with Signet and Imogen. They survived Imogen's transfiguration curse because the spell acted as a reflect/immunity. They also reveal the High Elves stole soul crystals and a protective item from Scolopendra's resting place to shield themselves.
- **Night Weasel Ambush**: A pack of invisible night weasels ambushes the group. Mongo uses his *Earthquake* attack to reveal them, and Holger and Clint fight with extreme speed and high constitution, showing an innate ability to draw mob aggro.
- **Point Mongo Defenses**: Mordecai sets up guard posts and alarm traps on the bridge and riverbanks.
- **The Apothecary Sponsor**: Carl and Donut receive their third and final sponsor: **The Apothecary**. The Apothecary has sponsored most of the top 10 crawlers, indicating a major political move backed by Plenty/Krakaren wealth. Zev pings them: they have 15 minutes to get to a safe room for Odette's show.

## Characters Introduced
- [[holger]] — Level 50 Were-Kin (were-castor) warrior, Elite.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[were-castors]]**: Clint and Holger display their beaver forms and combat/defense abilities.
- **[[the-apothecary]]**: The Plenty-affiliated sponsor sweeps the top 10 crawlers' sponsor slots.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Holger's bickering with Clint shows their distinct, kit-like sibling dynamic, which contrasts with their lightning-fast coordination in combat. The night weasels fight highlights the combat utility of the were-castors—they act as high-Con tanks that draw aggro, while Mongo's Earthquake acts as a vital utility to expose invisible foes. The Apothecary's sweeping sponsorship signals corporate maneuvers behind the scenes.
