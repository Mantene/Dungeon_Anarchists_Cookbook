# Chapter 435: Chapter 36 — The Reverse Tooth Fairy

## Summary
- **Eris Departs**: The world unfreezes. The meat skewer in Uzi Jesus' hand turns to dust. Carl quickly writes down the details of Eris' four coin options in his mental pad.
- **Jacobus Summoned**: Because of Eris' presence triggering Carl's *Scavenger's Daughter* chaos patch, Imani's Legendary card, **Jacobus the Reverse Tooth Fairy** (Level 90), is permanently summoned and can no longer be returned to card form.
- **The Tandfeen**: Jacobus is a tiny, buff, blond humanoid in a white loincloth with buzzing hummingbird wings. He speaks Danish, demanding money and teeth ("Vis mig dine tænder" — show me your teeth, "Betal mig trehundrede og tyve guld" — pay me 320 gold). Imani catches him and tells him not to attack their friends.
- **Chat Discussion**: Carl explains his encounter with Eris and shows the group his palm tattoo.
  - Elle expresses deep distrust of Quemada and fire fairies.
  - Prepotente compares the coin choices to the Donut/Katia conundrum.
  - Louis reports that Britney has just been grumpy, not acting differently.
  - Florin asks if Eris knows about Lucia.
  - Carl urges the team to sit down and make a plan, suggesting they talk to the war mages (Akuma) first.
