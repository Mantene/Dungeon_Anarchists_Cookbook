# Book 5 Chapter 56: The Zerzura Spell

## Summary
- **Zerzura Mechanics**: [[quasar]] explains the *Zerzura* spell, which can transfer a full town and its NPCs one floor down, but skips floors on Scolopendra levels (sending the changelings straight to Floor 9). The spell has severe side effects: it marks the town and the caster, who are then hunted by powerful undead "Children of Inpewt" resurrected from anything the landing town smushes.
- **Masquerade Announcements**: A crawler broadcast outlines the rules of [[the-butchers-masquerade]]. The top 50 crawlers receive a plus-one token, and weapons, magic, and inventory access will be locked inside the Ballroom. Safe passage is guaranteed by Queen [[imogen]].
- **Floor 7 Preview**: The broadcast reveals that the next floor (originally Floor 7, now Floor 8) has been swapped. The new Floor 7 is [[great-race]], designed in partnership with the Valtay Corporation to finish it on time.
- **Prepotente's Delivery**: Donut is contacted by [[prepotente]], who wants to meet and give her a present, and is already on his way.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The Zerzura spell presents a high-risk compromise to save the changelings, but foreshadows immediate danger on Floor 9. The upcoming Masquerade is clearly structured as a death trap under the guise of an elite party.
