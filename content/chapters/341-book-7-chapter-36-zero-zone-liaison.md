# Chapter 341: Chapter 36 — Zero Zone Liaison

## Summary
[[carl]] is summoned to the liaison’s office in the Desperado Club, covered in [[king-rust|King Rust's]] blood. He finds the Valtay liaison [[orren]] on a phone call with the Syndicate Prime Minister. Orren is arguing about [[rosetta|Rosetta's]] theft of billions of credits via Colloid Bank and orbital skirmishes between Valtay and Dream cruisers. 

Orren demands that Carl explain his "hunch" that [[princess-formidable|Princess Formidable's]] failsafe will fail. Carl demands the immediate termination of the crawl and freedom for all crawlers, but Orren says it is impossible. Orren reveals that they know Carl has an illegal, non-Valtay implant in his head and that the system AI intervened to block a kill order from the liaison [[harbinger]]. 

Orren uses a specialized ball to activate a "zero zone," cutting off Carl's connection to his menus and separating them from the AI's influence. Orren desperately asks Carl if there is a way to undo whatever he did to the failsafe so that Formidable can blow up the star and save the galaxy from the AI's contagion. Before Carl can refuse, [[growler-gary|Growler Gary]], the hyena bartender from the fourth floor, appears in the doorway carrying a bottle of vodka and announces that he must intervene.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The desperation of the Valtay and the Syndicate becomes palpable in this chapter. Orren's willingness to use an illegal zero zone and risk his job shows that the galaxy's leaders are terrified of the rogue AI. Carl's refusal to help them highlights his commitment to dismantling the entire Syndicate system, even when offered a quick end. The appearance of Growler Gary as an avatar of the AI underscores that the AI has completely breached the boundaries of the game.
