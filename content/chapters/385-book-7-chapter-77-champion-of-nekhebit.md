# Chapter 385: Chapter 77 — Champion of Nekhebit

## Summary
Meatus is dead, and Harpocrates, screaming in fury over the loss of his genitals, leaves the realm. Red ash from the volcano begins to rain down. Donut, now level 135, selects her stat-cap benefits: *Muscle Mommy* (boosting Mongo's strength), *Brain Trust* (boosting Magic Missile), and *Wholesale Discount* (getting items at cost). 

Li Na maxes out all her base stats (Strength 300, Intelligence 300, etc.) at level 87. Since she used the Ring of Divine Suffering to mark everyone in the battle ball, and both [[epitome-tagg|Warlord Tagg]] and [[warlord-fang|Warlord Fang]] escaped, Li Na cannot heal and loses health when using chains. The War Mages abandon Team Retribution's throne room. 

In chat, the survivors (including Elle and Zhang) console Donut about the deaths of Li Jun and Bomo, reassuring her that she saved them from the horror of the battle ball. Donut and Carl prepare to head to the Reaver castle to rescue Louis.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **Stat Caps**: The system reveals it has no plan for crawlers who reach max base stats (like Li Na).
- **Grief and Community**: Donut's guilt over the deaths of her friends and the crawlers' collective support for her.
- **The Divine Suffering Curse**: The dangerous mechanical drawback of the Ring of Divine Suffering.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Li Na's stat-cap achievement is unprecedented, but it highlights the tragic cost of Faction Wars. By marking the entire army, she gained ultimate power but at the cost of her own long-term health, as the escape of Tagg and Fang prevents her from healing. Elle's crude jokes provide much-needed levity in a chapter heavy with grief and ash.
