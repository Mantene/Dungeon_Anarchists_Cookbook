# Chapter 425: Chapter 26 — Mercenary Ghouls and Potatoes

## Summary
- **Rend's Resurrection**: Rend immediately resurrects in the safe room. Because this is his sixth death, he retains his memories and identity, and becomes 25% stronger, but gains a new random weakness: **Potatoes**.
- **Weakness List**: Rend's complete list of weaknesses now includes: Sylvan crystal, Consuming hobgoblin sweat, The bite of a rivenwing, Consuming undead flesh, Reaper Gaze, and Potatoes.
- **Pet Safety**: Carl decides to keep Rend inside his pet carrier/inventory to prevent him from eating undead flesh and dying again.
- **Fleshmancer Location**: Rosetta and Tipid return from town. Rosetta reports they found a fleshmancer. Tipid reveals the performance guild is hidden inside the Lollipop karaoke bar, though Carl won't like the entrance requirements.
- **Mercenary Hire**: Rosetta presents the only available mercenaries left at the market: two Level 25 **Jikininki ghouls** (the same janitor mobs encountered on the Iron Tangle). Because they are undead, Rosetta warns Carl to keep Rend far away from them.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Rend's growing list of weaknesses highlights the game's arbitrary rules, adding "potatoes" to a list that includes cosmic forces like Reaper Gaze. The hiring of the Jikininki ghouls shows how depleted the dungeon's resources are, forcing the team to rely on basic cleaning mobs as defensive mercenaries.
