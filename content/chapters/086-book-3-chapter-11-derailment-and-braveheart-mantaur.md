# Book 3 Chapter 11: Derailment and Braveheart Mantaur

## Summary
The Jelly Bombs detonate, derailing the Ochre Line steam train. Carl levels up to 29 and receives a Gold Engineering Box. The front engine car falls on its side, mostly intact. The Level 40 Ochre Line Train Engineer, **[[gore-gore]]** (a ManTauR, half-human/half-human hybrid covered in blue Braveheart paint) crawls out of the engine.

Believing the party to be passengers, Gore-Gore explains that employees disembark at Station 435, where the Kravyad magic portal resets their memories. The engine car is driven through a massive portal just after 435, which teleports only the engine back to the yard, while discarding the remaining cars. 

Gore-Gore states that only engineers are allowed in the first car, and he is honor-bound to kill anyone who enters. A ram-headed cleric from Club Vanquisher alerts Gore-Gore that Carl and Donut caused the derailment. Enraged, Gore-Gore attacks, initiating a Boss Battle.

Carl drops a smoke curtain and casts *Fear* and *Bang Bro*. Donut's magic missiles are ineffective. Gore-Gore beats Mongo aside, leaving him near death. Katia blocks a blade attack with her metal arm, losing some metal. Carl's punches increment his gauntlet's "summon Grull" percentage to 10.5%. To avoid summoning the god, Carl casts *Protective Shell*, which ejects Gore-Gore. The mantaur pulls Carl along. Carl drop-kicks Gore-Gore onto the tracks, where his body short-circuits the third rail, electrocuting him. Carl is sponsored by the **Valtay Corporation**.
