# Chapter 307: Chapter 7 — Larracos College of Magic

## Summary
- **Donut Holes Merch**: Donut is outraged to find Louis wearing an enchanted "Donut Holes" fan club t-shirt depicting a caricature of her backside. Zev confirms in chat that the fan group is a licensed brand, though the royalty threshold for crawlers is set impossibly high.
- **Entering Larracos**: The party exits the college saferoom into the beautiful, Rapunzel-tower-filled city of Larracos. Carl is struck by the tragedy that this rich, living city will be destroyed during the faction war.
- **War Leader Arief**: They meet **War Leader Arief**, a Level 80 Semeru Dwarf infantry commander. He is *Despondent* (-15% stats, +10% damage taken from worshippers of other deities) because his goddess Ysalte is dead.
- **Semeru History**: Arief shares the tragic history of the Semeru dwarves: they dug seeking the gods, discovered the roots of the *All Tree*, built their castle as an apology, and were devastated by Scolopendra's nine-tier attack, reducing their population from millions to under 10,000.
- **Marriage Proposal**: Arief informs them that co-warlord [[juice-box]] wants to see Carl privately, and wishes to marry Louis to solidify their truce.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The comedy of the "Donut Holes" merchandise contrasts sharply with the looming tragedy of Larracos's destruction. The lore of the Semeru dwarves digging to find the roots of the All Tree adds a mythological layer to the dungeon's setting, suggesting the game's designers built the simulation around deep cosmic mysteries.
