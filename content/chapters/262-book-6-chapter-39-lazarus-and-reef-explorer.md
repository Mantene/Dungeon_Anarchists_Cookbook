# Chapter 262: Lazarus and Reef Explorer

## Summary
- **Card Battle Progress**: Carl and Donut progress through the token battles (one-shotting opponents 3, 4, and 5). Carl reaches Level 68, Donut Level 59, and Mongo Level 40.
- **Reef Explorer Battle**: They face Opponent 6, a Level 65 Reef Explorer with 4 Level 50 Swordfish Interloper bodyguards. A swordfish blocks Carl's bomb and dive-bombs him, getting stuck head-first in the dirt.
- **Lazarus Summoned**: Donut plays her Level 140 Unique combo totem **Lazarus-A-Bang-Bang** (combined Uzi Jesus and Asojano), who fires a bazooka that shoots metallic flesh-devouring beetles, obliterating the swordfish. They collect Token 6 and 30 stacking *Precision* cards.
