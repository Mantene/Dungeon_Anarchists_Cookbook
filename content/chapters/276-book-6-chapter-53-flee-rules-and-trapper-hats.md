# Chapter 276: Flee Rules and Trapper Hats

## Summary
- **Motorcycle Ride**: Carl retrieves Samantha and lashes her to the Harley-Davidson. Donut rides Mongo as they head east toward Guanajay to save Imani.
- **Cascadia's Flee Rule**: Cascadia outlines new Flee rules: if a keyholder uses a Flee card, their key is automatically transferred to the opponent.
- **The Plan**: Carl plans to have Donut initiate a card battle with Imani, cast Flee (giving Imani the key), and then Carl will use the *Mysterious Bone Key* patch benefit to unlock the exit door (which destroys his jacket and uses one of his bones).
- **Eye in the Sky**: They receive a notification that their location has been pinpointed by a Level 10 *Eye in the Sky* search spell.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The Flee card rule change turns keys into directly stealable loot, making card battles extremely dangerous. Carl's willingness to sacrifice his jacket and a bone to save Imani demonstrates his deep loyalty.
