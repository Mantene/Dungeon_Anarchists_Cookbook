# Book 6 Chapter 22: NPCs' Rebellion and Compromises

## Summary
Juice Box's rebellion escalates at the preproduction meeting, and Carl and Donut reunite with the top crawlers at the Desperado Club, acquiring a bizarre new card.
- **NPC Rebellion Demands**: Continuing the preproduction meeting, [[juice-box]] demands the creation of a 10th Faction Wars team for unaffiliated NPCs, granting them regeneration and full voting rights. Carl adds the action item to the system queue, but Epitome Tagg and Cascadia state that the system cannot support such a fundamental shift.
- **Meeting Disrupted**: Juice Box detonates a hobgoblin pus explosive on the green blob representative (Admin Cell Mass Number Three). Tagg, D'Nadia, and Luke flee the virtual space. Suddenly, Crown Prince Stalwart is struck in the eye and killed virtually by a mysterious blue magical arrow.
- **Naga Compromise**: Left alone at the table, [[rishi]] (the Naga representative) proposes a compromise: the Blood Sultanate will support giving unaffiliated NPCs invulnerability and a safe zone this season if Juice Box stops her terror attacks. He warns Carl that the Sultanate is prepared to react harshly if she refuses.
- **Reunion at the Desperado Club**: Carl and Donut travel to the suburban Havana Desperado Club, meeting the rest of the crawlers (Katia, Bautista, Louis, Tran, Britney, Li Jun, Zhang, Li Na, Chris, and Florin).
- **Assassination Logistics**: Carl and Katia secretly message each other about how to target Astrid. Because Carl is a member of the *Naughty Boys Employment Agency* (and Astrid is the manager), she might hang out there, but adding it to their saferoom costs 500,000 gold. They decide to cause a distraction in the club to lure her into the secret passage in the women's bathroom.
- **HeyZoos Acquisition**: [[florin]] reveals he has a Unique (orange) card he cannot control because of his low charisma; the summoned totem actually shot him in the stomach. He gives it to Donut. The card is **HeyZoos (Uzi Jesus)** (Level 140 Unique Totem, an undead, ranged healer who wields an Uzi). This brings Carl and Donut's deck to 4 of 6 active totems.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **NPC Self-Awareness**: Juice Box's demands for NPC rights and regeneration represent a major narrative shift, threatening to turn the entire background population of the crawl into a sovereign faction.
- **Tactical Deck Building**: The acquisition of HeyZoos provides a powerful but highly volatile ranged healer to their deck, highlighting the high risk/reward nature of T'Ghee combat.
- **Physical Toll**: The appearance of Britney (severely burned), Tran (floating in a magical wheelchair), and Louis highlights the brutal, permanent physical damage suffered by their closest allies.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Juice Box's demand for NPC rights shows that she is thinking beyond simple survival; she is trying to create a systemic escape for all generated entities. The fact that Rishi the naga is willing to negotiate suggests that the showrunners and factions are genuinely terrified of what the NPCs might do if they align with the crawlers. Meanwhile, the introduction of "HeyZoos" is a prime example of the AI's dark, blasphemous humor, turning a religious icon into a gun-toting undead minion who is just as likely to shoot his summoner as heal them.
