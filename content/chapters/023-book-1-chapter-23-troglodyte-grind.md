# Book 1 Chapter 23: Troglodyte Grind

## Summary
[[carl]] defaces or alters the spray-painted signs leading to the Meadow Lark camp to protect the elderly residents from hostile crawlers. Carl and [[princess-donut]] set off to grind for experience and locate a Taco Bell safe room. 

They enter a new quadrant populated by **Troglodyte Pygmies** (Level 2), which are fast, venomous lizard-like humanoids. Donut's *Magic Missile* is now Level 5, allowing her to scale its mana usage (from 3 to 6 mana). They also fight **Troglodyte Bashers** (Level 3) and **Troglodyte Virtuosos** (Level 4), which use a 15-foot venomous whip-tongue attack. Carl's nightgaunt cloak nullifies the poison damage. 

Carl and Donut develop seamless combat coordination, with Donut using Carl's shoulders as a firing platform and hiding under his cloak. Carl points out that Donut's Strength is 18—twice his own—and urges her to practice using her claws, which she dismisses as "inelegant" and "not ladylike." They find the quadrant's neighborhood boss room: a long rectangular gym with a glass automatic sliding door that reads *"Open 24 Hours"* and *"Fitness, Weights, Gains."* Carl lights one of his last cigarettes and they step inside.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival]]**: Grinding is a tactical necessity to boost skills and level stats before facing the Borough Boss.
- **[[carls-class-selection]]**: Carl's bare-knuckle skills rise through combat, and he observes that Donut's strength (18) is disproportionately high due to her feline stat multipliers.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's character quirks—her refusal to use her claws because it's "inelegant" and her reference to "Ferdinand" (a show cat from her past)—provide humor that offsets the grind. Carl's decision to hide the signs to the Meadow Lark encampment shows his growing distrust of other crawlers.
