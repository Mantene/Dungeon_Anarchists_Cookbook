# Chapter 397: Interlude — Author Afterword

## Summary
In the author's afterword, Matt Dinniman announces that Faction Wars (Book 7) is complete. He shares updates regarding the Dungeon Crawler Carl franchise:
- Hardcover editions of Books 1-3 have been published by Ace (a division of Penguin Random House).
- Hardcover editions of Books 4-6 are scheduled for release in 2025.
- The Webtoon comic adaptation and Season 2 of the full-cast audio version by Soundbooth Theater are in development.
- He mentions a potential screen adaptation and invites fans to join his Patreon and Reddit communities.

Dinniman dedicates a moving tribute to his dog, Freddie the Labrador (2013-2024), who passed away during the final days of writing this book. Freddie was Dinniman's constant companion, sitting at his feet during the writing of the vast majority of the series.

## Characters
- Matt Dinniman — The author.
- Freddie — Matt Dinniman's beloved Labrador who passed away in 2024.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **Real-World Franchise Growth**: The expansion of Dungeon Crawler Carl into traditional publishing, webcomics, and audio adaptations.
- **In Memoriam**: The personal loss of Dinniman's writing companion, Freddie.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The afterword provides a touching glimpse into the author's personal life. The dedication of the book to Freddie highlights the emotional connection between writers and their companion animals. The massive growth of the franchise—moving from self-published web serial to mainstream hardcover releases—is a testament to the passionate fanbase that Dinniman's writing has cultivated.
