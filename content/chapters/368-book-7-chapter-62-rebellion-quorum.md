# Chapter 368: Chapter 62 — Rebellion Quorum

## Summary
[[elle-mcgibbons]] regains consciousness, complaining about the impact. The system awards Team Retribution all of the Bone Clan's assets, though the King's Point throne room remains a capturable asset in open air. 

Carl collects the four dead war mages' bodies into his inventory to prevent their heads from exploding. 

In the distance, Eris (chaos) is heard laughing hysterically and screaming, "I'm not the one laughing. Stop!" [[mordecai]] and [[rosetta]] explain that Eris is piloted by [[nami]], a model/influencer who underwent surgery to look and sound like [[hekla]]. Under the new mandatory pilot rule, Nami has been forcibly pulled into the goddess's body.

[[florin]] reports heavy fighting against orc forces who are now under NPC command. He notes that demons are appearing to claim corpses, and that lava from the erupted volcano is flowing into the river bed toward Larracos. 

[[justice-light]] panics over the loss of the Gate, insisting they need it back. Britney reports that Juice Box has teleported away to attend an emergency adjudication hearing with Sir Ferdinand.

Baroness Victory explains that the war mages broke their conscription allegiance by using a clever combination of Pull spells to kill Stalwart, and that they immediately teleported to Juice Box and Ferdinand. Adjutant Drick has called a foul, claiming illegal interference. Victory teleports away to attend the hearing.

[[edgar]] warns Donut that [[epitome-tagg]] is fleeing the Dream castle on a zebra, using a decoy to trick them. Donut asks Sledge to fire missiles and a chain ripper at Tagg. The targeted missiles miss, and Tagg escapes.

Carl and Elle prepare to step through the portal to the woods above the Madness castle. Carl commands Samantha to stay behind and paint the target on the bleached drake circling the Reaver castle. Samantha agrees to help, but is distracted by the sight of a dragon.

## Themes & Plot Threads
* **The Legal Quorum**: The adjudication of Team Retribution's alliance with the War Mage Rebellion shows the administrative battles occurring behind the scenes.
* **Residual Influence**: Rosetta's commentary about the "Apothecary" and the "Eulogist" hints at the deeper ideological war regarding the dungeon's future.
* **The Peeling of Larracos**: The flowing lava threatens Larracos, foreshadowing the literal peeling of the floor.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Rosetta's thoughts reveal that the Open Intellect Pacifist Network's goal is to show the universe the cruelty of the crawl, rather than just survival. She accepts her likely death as a price for sending a message, showing the ideological conviction that separates the older Cookbook authors from the current crawlers.
