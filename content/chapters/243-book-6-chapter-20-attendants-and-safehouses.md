# Book 6 Chapter 20: Attendants and Safehouses

## Summary
Carl and Donut begin moving south through Havana to retrieve Samantha, Donut blocks Sister Ines on chat, and Carl discovers that Mordecai is embezzling gold to save changeling children.
- **Heading South**: As December 15th begins, the ghommid village is peaceful and fog-free. Carl and Donut make plans to head south through Havana to retrieve [[samantha]], who is trapped in a spider web demi-god's nest on the southern coast.
- **Sister Ines Tension**: Donut reveals that she sent a message warning [[sister-ines-quiteria]] that they would track her down, take her cards, and rip [[paz-lo]]'s card to punish her for her past actions. In response, Sister Ines blocked Donut on chat. Carl is frustrated, knowing this will make it harder to locate her.
- **Safehouse Stop**: They stop at a safehouse located in a suburban Havana home run by a SURly bopca named Vitasy. Vitasy notes that a Desperado Club is located further south.
- **Mordecai's Secret Embezzlement**: Back in the saferoom, Carl and Donut notice makeup smudges and a pink bow on [[mordecai]]'s head (which he couldn't see because cyclopes have no reflection). They realize he has been embezzling gold (about 1,000 gold a day) to hire six changeling children—including the armless girl [[ruby]]—as safehouse attendants to protect them from the Floor 7 transition battle. Carl reassures Mordecai that he would have approved the expenditure anyway.
- **Out of Time**: Zev notifies Carl and Donut that they are being zapped to a preproduction meeting in Earth's orbit.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **Crawler and NPC Solidarity**: Mordecai's secret action to save the changeling children at financial cost highlights the deep bond between the crawlers and their NPC manager.
- **The Toll of the Dungeon**: Surly NPCs and Surly bopcas demonstrate that the dungeon's high-level zones wear down even the friendly entities.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's direct confrontation of Sister Ines shows her black-and-white moral compass, which contrasts with Carl's more tactical, compromise-driven mindset. Meanwhile, Mordecai's choice to embezzle funds to protect the changeling children shows his emotional vulnerability and desperation; despite his gruff manager exterior, he will break rules to act as a father figure for these orphans.
