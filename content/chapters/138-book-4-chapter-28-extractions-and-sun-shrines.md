# Book 4 Chapter 28: Extractions and Sun Shrines

## Summary
While safe in their personal space, Carl opens his boss loot, receives intel on the Faction Wars, and learns that the blind fire god Emberus is rampaging outside.
- **Achievements & Loot**: Carl opens his Floor 5 boss and achievement boxes:
  - *Rock Bottom*: Gold I'm Wet Box containing the *Ring of Water Breathing* (which grows gills and slimy skin).
  - *Summary Judgement* (x7): Seven stock certificates (Sigmund Textiles, etc.) and seven photos of the plaintiffs' mothers (Ugloo, Epitome Noflex, etc.), revealing the identities of the factions suing him.
  - *Janet Jackson's Nipple*: Platinum Fan Box (locked).
  - *Soft Vore*: Platinum Spicy Box containing the *Enchanted Anarchist's Battle Rattle* patch jacket (which grants stat increases per patch and explosive throwing bonuses) and a small Earth Upgrade Patch.
  - *Gold Boss Box*: *Drakea's Enchanted Kerchief of Disorder* bandana (+5 Detect Traps, *Tripper* spell to detonate nearby traps, and the *Remote Detonator* benefit).
- **Samantha's Guidance**: Samantha, the hired sex doll head, explains that the rampaging god is her great-uncle/cousin **Emberus**, a blind fire deity looking for his lost hell-hound puppy [[orthrus]].
- **Crawlers in Danger**: Tserendolgor messages that Emberus is pounding on her bubble because she is a Dog Soldier crawler, mistaking her for Orthrus. Carl realizes they must pop the bubble and rescue the real puppy.
