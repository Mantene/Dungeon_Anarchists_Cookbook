# Book 1 Chapter 18: Collateral Damage

## Summary
The explosion is larger than expected, knocking [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] over but leaving them unhurt. A deluge of notifications confirms that the goblin chieftain boss is dead. Donut reveals that she saw baby goblins wearing tiny, oversized jackets (resembling Baby Yoda) and that Carl's cart ran over one of them. Both Carl and Donut reach Level 8. 

Carl is deeply sickened and feels like an "asshole" for killing non-combatants, while Donut rationalizes that they had no choice. Returning to the workshop, they find it completely destroyed. The coal pile has ignited, blocking the entrance to the boss room and preventing them from retrieving the neighborhood map. Spotting a line of white dots on their minimap (goblins returning from the meth war), they flee north into an unexplored quadrant to avoid a confrontation.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival]]**: Fleeing the goblin sector is essential to avoid the wrath of the returning goblin horde.
- **[[systemic-oppression]]**: The dungeon designer's placing of non-combatant families in a boss room represents systemic cruelty, forcing crawlers to commit atrocities to progress.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's visceral guilt over killing the goblin family marks a crucial point of psychological tension. Unlike Donut, who separates the "goblins" as video game monsters, Carl struggles with the realization that the dungeon is turning him into a monster. The fact that the fire blocks their access to the boss's map serves as direct narrative punishment for their shortcut, forcing them to navigate blind.
