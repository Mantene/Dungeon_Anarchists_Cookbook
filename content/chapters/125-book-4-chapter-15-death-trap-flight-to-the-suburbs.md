# Book 4 Chapter 15: Death Trap Flight to the Suburbs

## Summary
Carl and Juice Box discuss the changelings' past, and Carl flies a salvaged gnomish plane to the floating house of Commandant Kane.
- **Juice Box's Revelations**: Juice Box explains that changelings are suffering from **Compression Sickness** (1 in 4 births are sterile and deformed, like Ruby). Henrik wanted to resurrect the pterodactyl ghost **Quetzalcoatlus** because she has a spell to alter plants, which could create a food source to cure the defect.
- **The Feral Gods Quest**: Juice Box explains that the **Gate of the Feral Gods** is a real artifact consisting of three parts: two pocket watches and a winding box (held by Henrik, Kane, and the Mad Dune Mage). Combining them opens a gate but tears a hole to the Nothing, letting a feral god escape into the opening world. Carl receives a new quest: **"The Gate of the Feral Gods"**.
- **Katia's Past**: During the sandstorm, Katia opens up to Carl about her past in Iceland. She had been pre-approved to adopt a child, but was disqualified due to an undisclosed event. She notes that Maggie's descent into madness was driven by maternal grief, and she is glad she never had a child.
- **Zev and the Toy**: In a chat with Admin Loita and Zev, Donut realizes Zev is acting stiffly. Zev suggests naming the Veriluxx toy cat "Ivy", a code that Donut recognizes, causing her to gasp.
- **Ascending to the Sky Castle**: Carl and Donut salvage a gnomish Drop Bear biplane, renaming it *Nightmare II* (originally *Death Trap*). They deploy the emergency recovery balloons to float 5 kilometers up to the ceiling of the bubble.
- **The Floating House**: Carl uses the plane's engines to taxi along the ceiling to the floating castle. The castle is a suburban beige house with a double garage, porch, and a Dallas Cowboys jersey welcome sign.
- **A Creepy Welcome**: Carl, Donut, and Mongo climb down the balloon net to the house. The front door opens, and Commandant Kane's young daughter, wearing a blood-stained Dallas Cowboys jersey, invites them in for lemonade, announcing that her father is already dead.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Zev's suggestion of the name "Ivy" is clearly a hidden message to Donut, hinting at a secret communication or plan. The description of Commandant Kane's castle as a blood-stained, suburban house complete with Christmas lights and a Dallas Cowboys jersey creates a deeply unsettling suburban-horror atmosphere.
