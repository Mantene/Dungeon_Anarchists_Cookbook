# Chapter 297: Tempest's Floor 8 Recap — School Report

## Summary
This chapter is framed as a conversation between [[quasar]] and his niece [[tempest]], who is recording him for a school report about the events of Floor 8. While fixing Quasar's sink (which doesn't actually work — "it's more an art piece"), Tempest extracts a profanity-laced, irreverent recap of the eighth floor.

### Key Recap Points
- **Floor 8 Structure**: A monster card collection/battle floor. Crawlers had to collect cards and use them to fight card-wielding monsters. Quasar describes the fights as chaotic — "like a Saccathian orgy."
- **Carl and Donut's Team**: Got stuck in a place called Cuba. Collected cards including a seal, a crab named Raul, a giant cat, a donkey-snake, [[uzi-jesus]] and [[asojano]] (combined into [[lazarus]]), and Alpha Carl ("really just Carl but with way better hair and a much cooler voice").
- **Quan's Betrayal**: A crawler named [[quan-ch]] stole Carl's key, forcing Carl to kill him (though he didn't receive a player killer skull).
- **Tsren's Death**: A dog-girl crawler with a flamethrower and a pet meatball died. Carl now has both the flamethrower and the meatball. "It was sad as shit."
- **Sister Ines**: A cat-girl nun who was secretly a serial killer. [[princess-donut]] did not like her. Ines went insane and started working with the [[sheol]] demon [[amayon]] during a demon eviction event. [[louis-santiago-2]] put her down but was marked for death by the god [[ysalte]], who was later killed by [[paz]] (who had been turned into a card).
- **Hidden Event**: Something happened after Ysalte's death that only viewers saw — involving "one of the ladies in the party" — and will be a big deal later.
- **Tiara Constraint**: On Floor 9, neither [[princess-donut]] nor [[katia-grim]] can leave until all the [[naga]] are dead, and even then only one of them can leave. This is because they both put on a tiara.
- **Katia's Deal**: [[katia-grim]] has a deal with [[huanxin-jinx]] sponsoring the goddess [[eileithyia]] to go to the 12th floor as a celestial attendant. She must eat a flower that gives three choices, one being the 12th-floor attendant option.
- **Huanxin Jinx**: The CEO sponsor who cloned herself and was once banned for cheating. When she was banned, it was [[mordecai]] who "got railgunned by it."
- **Faction Wars**: Ten teams now active. The Princess Posse is one. NPCs are led by [[juice-box]], a psychotic changeling in love with [[louis-santiago-2]], who kept making her morph into historical Earth figures.
- **The Rich Crawlers**: [[prince-stalwart]], the banker [[operatic]], and [[epitome-tagg]] can now "really die." Floor 9 is going to be "a shitshow."
- **Eye of the Bedlam Bride**: The spider is now inside Carl's chest. Nobody knows how that will turn out.

## Characters Introduced or Developed
- **[[tempest]]** — Quasar's niece. Smart, foul-mouthed, and not intimidated by her uncle. Writing a school report for teacher "Miss Guss." This is her first appearance.
- **[[quasar]]** — Carl's alien agent/manager. Shown here in a domestic setting, revealing he's divorced (Aunt Nova left him), lives "far out in the middle of nowhere," and genuinely cares about Tempest despite his abrasive exterior. His teaching philosophy: "Never give shit up for free." Admits he and everyone around him may be in danger for representing Carl.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
This is one of the most character-rich recap chapters in the series. While it functions as a "Previously on..." summary, it does far more: it establishes Tempest as a new character, deepens Quasar's characterization from comic-relief alien agent to a three-dimensional figure with family ties, fears, and genuine affection. His admission at the end — "I think we all are [in danger], kiddo" — is one of the rare moments of unguarded vulnerability from a character who typically hides behind vulgarity and bravado. The recap format (school report for "Miss Guss / AnalBeads") is pure Dinniman — weaving exposition through character voice so effectively that the reader barely notices they're being caught up.
