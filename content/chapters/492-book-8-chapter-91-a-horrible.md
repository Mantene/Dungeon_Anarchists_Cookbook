# Chapter 492: Chapter 91 — A Horrible

## Summary
The parade begins. The route is a long yellow-brick road obscured by a low, knee-high fog. Standing on either side of the road are thousands of silent, unmoving people wearing earthy brown rags and oversized, colorful caricature masks of goblins, trolls, and demons. 

Carl examines a child-sized spectator and receives a description labeling her "A Horrible. Level X. This is a non-combatant. She's not really here because she no longer exists. You take that mask off, and underneath it, you will find what some people call a Primal." 

The AI's voice (speaking as a residual consciousness/Eulogist memory) explains that these people are memories of what the Primals once had before they ascended to post-physical form. The narrator reflects on the tragedy of their lost individuality and the weaponization of their collective mind. Carl tells Donut he understands what the system is explaining. Chaco's voice blares, announcing the start of the parade.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The AI's monologue serves as a profound explanation of the dungeon's lore. The "Horribles" are the remnants of the original Primal species that initiated the collapse of their own civilization through collective eugenics. Carl's ability to empathize with the AI's tragic past shows his deepening connection to the system.
