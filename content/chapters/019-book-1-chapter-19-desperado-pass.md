# Book 1 Chapter 19: Desperado Pass

## Summary
With two days and 18 hours left before the floor collapse, [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] fight through a quadrant of "Rot Stickers"—small, suicide-bombing ravioli-like creatures that inflict Take Down. Carl's trollskin shirt protects him from the knockdowns, while Donut shoots them down with magic missiles. They find a safe room designed like a dingy bus station waiting area. The human survivor counter stands at **3,594,517**. 

They eat the daily "experience cookies" (+9.8 EXP) and open their accumulated loot boxes. For the goblin massacre, Carl receives two mocking achievements: *You Monster!* (awarding a Bronze Asshole's Box) and *War Criminal* (awarding a Gold Asshole's Box). He also gains the **IED** skill (Level 3) and unlocks the *Demolitions Workshop* menu. 

Donut opens her boxes and receives the **Enchanted Fae Scale Quadruped Crupper of the Fleet** (silver-scale armor) and a *Minion Army* spell tome (which she cannot learn yet due to insufficient mana). Both Carl and Donut are branded with a **Desperado Pass** tattoo (Donut on her shoulder blade, Carl on his neck). The pass allows entry to the Desperado Club but prevents them from ever getting a Vanquisher Pass. From his boxes, Carl receives a +2 Ring of Constitution and a Skill Potion that grants the **Determine Value** skill. He uses it to sort his inventory by value, finding that his *Wisp Armor* tome and the Chopper are his most valuable items. They rest on the cots to prepare for the next broadcast.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival]]**: The rapidly dropping global survivor count underscores the urgency of finding a staircase.
- **[[systemic-oppression]]**: The system gamifies the slaughter of non-combatants with achievements like *You Monster!* and *War Criminal*, revealing the AI's sick sense of humor.
- **[[carls-class-selection]]**: Carl continues to acquire utility and combat stats, notably gaining the *Determine Value* skill and the *IED* skill.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The system's mocking tone in the achievements shows that the showrunners want to keep the crawlers off-balance, rewarding them for atrocities while calling them monsters. The Desperado Pass introduces an mysterious political layer to the crawl, establishing a dividing line between different "classes" of crawlers (Desperados vs Vanquishers).
