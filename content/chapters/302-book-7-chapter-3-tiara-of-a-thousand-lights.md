# Chapter 302: Chapter 3 — Tiara of a Thousand Lights

## Summary
- **Loot Box Opening**: Donut opens her abundant card-based loot boxes.
- **You're Not Done Yet**: Donut learns a black, screeching spellbook called *You're Not Done Yet* (a dark resurrection spell similar to *Second Chance*).
- **Tiara of a Thousand Lights**: Donut receives a Celestial Benefactor box from her sponsor, *Long Haul Biological Waste Management Solutions*. It contains the **Tiara of a Thousand Lights and Accompanying Charge Pillow**. The tiara can absorb up to 99 buffs from hats placed on the pillow for 10 hours (ruining the original hat).
- **Universal Charger Pillow**: She also receives a Platinum Benefactor box from the *Apothecary* containing a purple charging pillow that works for any item type, transferring buffs to a like item. Donut starts draining her new boonie hat and her *Tiara of the Inebriated Dragonfly*.
- **Mongo Healed**: Carl heals Mongo's missing wing by smashing a high-tier healing potion vial on his tail.
- **Cloak of the Benevolent Champion**: Donut equips [[quan-ch]]'s celestial cloak. To optimize the stat-equalizing benefit, she drinks a *MySpace Photo* potion (+30 Charisma), tricking the cloak into boosting her base Constitution from 4 to 193.
- **Cloak Spell Buffs**: The cloak immediately boosts four of her spells toward level 15: *Bad Attitude*, *Wall of Fire*, *Heal Critter*, and *Puddle Jumper*. It also grants the level-5 *Find Weakened* skill.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's power spike is staggering. By using the Charisma-boosting potion right before equipping the Cloak of the Benevolent Champion, she permanently resolves her primary weakness: her extremely low Constitution. The Tiara of a Thousand Lights provides an open-ended mechanism to consolidate her vanity hat collection into a single, god-tier item.
