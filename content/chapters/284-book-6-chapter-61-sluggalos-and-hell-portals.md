# Chapter 284: Sluggalos and Hell Portals

## Summary
- **Stairwell Collapses**: Carl and Donut make their way toward the church while hearing reports that other crawler teams across the world are dealing with collapsing stairwell access structures.
- **Sheol Portal Threat**: The Level 140 pleasure demons open black-hole portals to Sheol. Crawlers pulled into these portals instantly explode because they are mechanically barred from descending directly to Floor 15. The demons require two souls each before they will return to Sheol.
- **The Sluggalos**: Carl instructs [[imani-c|Imani]] to release their conscripted, punk-rock strip-club slugs (the "Sluggalos") from the barracks to distract the demons.
- **Bahamas Update**: Carl messages [[prepotente|Prepotente]] (Pony) in the Bahamas, asking if he feels the environment getting hotter, realizing that the Sheol eviction event is expanding.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The introduction of the "Sluggalos" as a suicidal distraction force shows Carl's readiness to exploit ridiculous dungeon loopholes to buy time. The instant-explosion mechanic of the Sheol portals highlights the strict systemic restrictions governing crawler floor transitions.
