# Book 4 Chapter 4: The Royal Chariot

## Summary
Carl and Katia build a custom vehicle and witness an aerial assault on the neighboring village.
- **The Royal Chariot**: Using their engineering and metalworking tables (along with Katia's *Earth Hobby* engine knowledge), Carl and Katia construct a custom, lightweight ATV with a rear treadmill track and front rubber steering wheels. The vehicle, named *The Royal Chariot*, can be disassembled into two pieces and stored in Carl's inventory.
- **Aerial Bombardment**: During a field test in the dunes, the *Wasteland Castle* unexpectedly flies over the distant Bactrian village and begins dropping bombs. Hump Town sound a wailing siren and deploys anti-aircraft guns on the walls. Fearing for their safety, Carl and Katia disassemble the Chariot and return to town.
- **Saferoom Procedures**: Mordecai clarifies that saferooms only protect Borant-owned NPCs if a crawler is present inside them, meaning the Bactrian town NPCs have likely been wiped out.
- **Recap Episode & Leaderboard**:
  - The recap show highlights the end of Floor 4: Miriam Dom and Prepotente stacking debuffs to render a boss unconscious for five hours, only for Quan Ch to wake it up using a *Disrupter* bolt and steal the kill.
  - **Ifechi's Death**: The show broadcasts the tragic death of the healer [[ifechi]]. At the exit stairwell, [[lucia-mar]] cast her **Rubber** damage-reflect spell on Ifechi's head. When Ifechi's partner [[florin]] shot Lucia and her dogs, the damage reflected, instantly killing Ifechi. Lucia then reflected damage onto Florin, but he survived due to a protection spell Ifechi had placed on him.
  - Florin is shown devastated, holding Ifechi's leech staff and refusing to spin the wheel or progress, earning a player-killer skull.
  - **Floor 5 Leaderboard**:

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival-of-the-collapse]]**: The crawlers build vehicles and compile anti-aircraft plans to deal with the Wasteland Castle's patrols.
- **[[televised-exploitation]]**: The recap show highlights Ifechi's horrific death as entertainment, prompting Carl's resolution to execute Lucia Mar.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Lucia Mar's *Rubber* spell represents a terrifying defensive mechanic that turns the party's own strength against them, making her extremely dangerous. The tragic loss of Ifechi shatters the stability of one of the top crawler pairs, leaving Florin broken and suicidal. Carl's placement at #2 on the leaderboard, with a x2 bounty modifier, ensures he is the prime target for every bounty hunter on the floor.
