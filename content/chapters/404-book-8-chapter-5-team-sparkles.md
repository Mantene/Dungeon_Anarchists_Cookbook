# Chapter 404: Chapter 5 — Team Sparkles

## Summary
Carl constructs a flatbed trailer to tow the bugbear minivan, using wood planks, spare wheels, and the weight of his meatball summon, [[ren]], to stabilize it. Meanwhile, Mongo discovers the gangster-themed horn in the Big Shot Chicken food truck, repeatedly blasting its noisy sound effects.

Team Sparkles catches up to them:
- **Mount**: **Avernus Creeper** (a biological mount composed of a tangle of walking vines related to Gehenna Brambles).
- **Riders**: [[dwight]] (a Level 87 Enchanter Sparkling Unicorn with a gruff Boston accent who is a notorious prick) and [[lucienne]] (a Level 81 Fink Mandagot chinchilla-rat creature with a French accent).

Dwight and Lucienne immediately insult Donut, calling her "fatty" and a "corpulent swine." Lucienne is hostile because she worships the goddess [[diwata]], who hates Carl. When Mongo blasts the food truck's horn, the Avernus Creeper panics and stampedes directly over Carl's trailer and the bugbear minivan, leaving behind invasive **Avernus Creeper Thorns** which Carl collects for Mordecai's alchemy table botanist upgrade.

Carl, Jasha, and Radoslav lift the minivan onto the trailer, chaining it to the food truck's bumper. Jasha remains in the van while Radoslav rides in the back of the food truck. Alevtina's corpse is left in the water per bugbear mourning customs.

Driving through the cave, they spot the eviscerated corpse of another manatee (Brianna Kim), killed by the Jugglers' mount, Old Shuck. Radoslav mentions that their Team Free Love is supposed to be a band of musicians. 

Donut receives a notification that the audience voted to transfer **Otto** (from the Operatic Bankers' Federation) to the arena as a manatee stand-in. In the group chat, Elle complains about a forced "fan meet-and-greet" in a safe room where she has to sign merchandise for her sole remaining superfan, a creepy soother who bypassed the Earth-system quarantine.

Carl and Donut reach the finish line at **Hungry Eyes Village**. Radoslav thanks them for their help and joins Jasha in the minivan so they can cross the finish line in their vehicle. The bleachers at the finish line are empty. Donut laments that they will eventually have to kill the friendly bugbears.
