# Book 2 Chapter 2: Compensated Anarchist

## Summary
[[carl]] goes through his race and class selection. He is presented with race choices including Hobgoblin, Human, and Sasquatch. On Mordecai's advice, Carl chooses **[[primal-race|Primal]]**—a blank slate race that inflicts -1 to all base stats but unlocks all higher-tier skills to be trained up to Level 20.

For his class, the AI recommends *Bomb Squad Tech*, *Prizefighter*, and *Compensated Anarchist*. Carl selects **Compensated Anarchist** (Earth class, Monk/Rogue hybrid), which boosts trap-engineering, unarmed combat, IED handling, hides in shadows, grants the *Fear* spell, and unlocks the *Naughty Boys Employment Agency*. However, it imposes a -25% damage penalty with bladed weapons and a +25% mana cost penalty for damage spells.

Carl distributes his 36 stat points. To meet the class minimum, he dumps 17 points into Charisma (raising it to 25) and 6 into Dexterity (raising it to 11).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[primal-race]]**: Carl chooses the blank slate progenitor race to unlock Level 20 skill training.
- **[[carls-class-selection]]**: Carl selects the Monk/Rogue hybrid brawler class.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's choice to dump points into Charisma highlights the system's manipulation, forcing brawler crawlers to pay a steep tax to obtain versatile skills. The choice of the Primal race reflects Carl's forward-looking strategy: he is sacrificing short-term power for long-term versatility, betting that he can survive long enough to train his skills beyond the standard caps.
