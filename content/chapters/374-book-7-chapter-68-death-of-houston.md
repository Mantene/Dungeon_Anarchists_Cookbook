# Chapter 374: Death of Houston

## Summary
[[dong-quixote]] and Warlord [[carl]] defeat the defending guards. Dong brains the last gnoll mercenary, dropping gold coins that he feeds to his crust sock flail. [[kandy-newton]] (Bigs) ties up two conscripted crawlers, [[selvi]] (a level-57 elf Valide healer) and [[tuba]] (a level-56 human Boring Ol' Fighter). Both suffer a severe, no-healing bleeding debuff for refusing to fight. Carl commands Bigs to secure the front entrance and occupy the throne room, which begins the Princess Posse's six-hour capture timer.

Carl and Dong enter the surgical theater. The room contains **The Grease Monkey**, a chrome Viceroy Portable Surgical and Scientific Exploration Unit, which is operating on [[architect-houston]]. Nearby are the tables of the desecrated corpse of Zeynep and the bound, conscious [[burcu]]. 

Houston is being merged with a dead changeling named [[namico]]. The room is ringed by eight *Containment Breach Trap Mark IVs* which are Tripper-proof, chained, and wired to a dead man's switch on Houston. If anyone unaffiliated steps on the floor, the traps will flood the room with Sheol fire. 

In the chat, Eris wakes and cancels the Ceremony of Chaos, removing everyone's invulnerability. The witnesses are still safe inside Donut's ball, but Carl loses his invulnerability. 

To buy time, [[li-jun]] plays a song via speaker 11 to distract Harpocrates. The god kills Li Jun, who sends a final, loving message to his sister [[li-na]] and [[zhang]]. 

Carl uses *Walk on Air* to leap onto the empty table, prying away and pocketing a white Viceroy cleaner bot. He leaps to Zeynep's table, uses the *Wand of Nighty-Night* to sleep Burcu, and releases her bonds. However, Houston's health plummets, and the dead changeling begins growing roots. Carl throws a healing potion at Houston to keep him alive, but Burcu's table tips over. She falls to the floor, and Carl lands on his feet, triggering the Sheol fire traps.

Carl casts **Gloom Wraith Phase** to escape. As he phases through Houston and the changeling, he experiences a strange slow-motion moment where the root-wrapped entity takes on Li Jun's face, thanking Carl for giving him more time with his sister and friend. Carl phases out of the room as it incinerates, killing Houston and Burcu. Carl is knocked unconscious by the magical blast.

The Madness is defeated, and Larracos is opened, entering the **Peeling Phase** (first team to hold Larracos Castle wins). 

Agatha, Warlord of the War Mage Rebellion, proposes three approved emergency action items:
1. War Mages get full warlord status and can claim thrones.
2. War Mages get the Conscription spell.
3. Affiliated members of a defeated team will drop dead immediately.

The War Mage Rebellion occupies the throne room of Team Retribution in Larracos. They now control the city defenses, with a 30-minute grace period before the towers activate. If they hold it for six hours, the War Mage Rebellion wins.

## Themes & Plot Threads

* **The Sacrifice of Li Jun**: Li Jun's heroic death highlights the personal costs of the operation.
* **The Viceroy's Discovery**: Houston's dying words suggest that the changelings and the dungeon itself are connected to a deeper, cosmic truth ("the Beautiful Place").
