# Book 3 Chapter 3: Well-Rested

## Summary
Carl, Donut, and Mongo sleep in the conjoined space, gaining the "Well-Rested" buff (+10% stats, +10% skill/exp training for 30 hours). The party watches the Daily Recap Show, which details the history of Earth's subways and railway systems, with the explanation of the Iron Tangle's layout REDACTED. Katia reports that Brynhild's Daughters are at a transfer station #83 at the intersection of the Orange and Indigo lines.

The daily leaderboard and bounties populate: Lucia Mar is #1, Hekla #2, Prepotente #3, Carl #6, Donut #7, and Elle McGibbons #10 (now a Frost Maiden Blizzardmancer). Bounties are placed on the top 10 crawlers. The daily announcement reveals that stairwells will only open six hours before level collapse, preventing early descent.

Carl opens a message in a "Messages from deceased Crawlers" folder. [[brandon-an]] is dead. He died holding off Shade Gremlins on Floor 3 so [[imani-c]], [[elle-mcgibbons]], and others could escape. Brandon asks Carl to find his brother [[chris-andrews-2]], who left the party after a fight, to tell him he loves him and is sorry. Devastated and enraged, Carl goes to the training room and spends an hour training his *Bare Knuckles* skill against wooden dummies.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Brandon's message is a sobering reminder of the absolute fragility of relationships in the dungeon. Carl's response—channeling his raw grief and anger into violent physical training—underscores his refusal to let the dungeon break him.
