# Chapter 457: Chapter 57 — Barroom Blitz Karaoke

## Summary
The chapter opens with the bar erupting into a chaotic brawl while music continues to play. [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] realize the dead DJ means they must perform the song themselves. A drunken [[dwight]] attempts to stab Carl and Donut, but his aim is off, and the corpse of [[lucienne]] (a gremlin) slams into [[yuto]], a red demon from Team Yokai, escalating the fight. The brawl quickly involves Yokai, scarecrows, and the Minister of Blood-Letting's skeletons.

Amidst the chaos, Carl and Donut begin to sing "Take On Me." Carl ducks bottles and kicks combatants away from Donut, all while maintaining his performance. [[dong-quixote]] appears, using his giant nickel sock to clear the stage. Donut hits her high chorus note, augmented by Auto-Tune, and uses a magic missile to disarm a Draconian. Carl delivers his extra-high note, causing his performance gauge to shower digital sparkles and flame, momentarily halting half the fighting crowd in awe.

As the song continues, [[mongo]] joins the fray, clearing space with his tail. [[imani-c]] knocks Dwight unconscious with a potion to protect him, while [[louis-santiago-2]], [[britney]], and [[bautista]] guard him. Donut casts Ice Slick on the dance floor, sending NPCs crashing into the bar where [[prepotente]] and a rummaging goat are present. A fairy creature sets the stage curtains on fire, forcing Carl to move, but Mongo bites the fairy out of the air. Dong Quixote's sock is torn, spilling nickels (instead of the gold pieces he'd been inserting), drawing even more combatants into a scramble for the coins.

Carl hits his final high note as digital fireworks mix with the spreading real fire. However, at Donut's last line, a paralyzed [[dekoki]] the kappa falls backward onto her, spilling the water from his head and causing her to cry out, failing her final note. Louis and Britney quickly extinguish the fire. The DJ booth explodes, silencing the music.

Carl is then congratulated by the System for being granted permanent access to the Stage Performance Guild and earning the "Sing for your supper!" achievement. The reward is that hidden guilds with entrance requirements will now appear on his map if he is in close proximity. Donut, however, has a "FAILED" sign over her head, huffing indignantly. A secret door in the bar wall begins to glow, indicating Carl's earned entry. Donut admits she still loves the song despite her failure.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- [[survival]] — Carl and Donut actively protect other crawlers (like Dwight) from being killed in the bar brawl to prevent further crawler-on-crawler matchups in the racing heats.
- [[over-city-mechanics]] — The revelation of the Stage Performance Guild, the "Sing for your supper!" achievement, and the reward of hidden guilds appearing on the map demonstrate new layers of the dungeon's system. The performance gauges also tie into this.
- [[televised-exploitation]] — The entire dungeon is a spectacle, and even a chaotic bar fight with karaoke is framed as a performance judged by the System for entertainment and rewards.
- [[solidarity-and-duty]] — Despite the competitive nature of the dungeon, Carl and Donut feel a duty to prevent other crawlers from dying to avoid detrimental System penalties in future heats.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
This chapter is a masterclass in chaotic humor, perfectly blending high-stakes dungeon survival with an utterly absurd karaoke performance. The juxtaposition of a deadly bar brawl with Carl and Donut passionately belting out "Take On Me" is inherently comical, highlighting the surreal nature of the Dungeon. Carl's unexpected vocal talent and ability to maintain his performance amidst literal flying bottles and combatants underscores his adaptability and sheer grit, contrasting sharply with Donut's unfortunate, system-induced failure. The System's recognition of Carl's performance, rewarding him with a guild access, further emphasizes the Dungeon's arbitrary and theatrical criteria for success, even in the most unconventional scenarios. It's a moment of both triumph and tragedy, wrapped in musical pandemonium, that showcases the series' unique blend of action, character development, and laugh-out-loud absurdity.
