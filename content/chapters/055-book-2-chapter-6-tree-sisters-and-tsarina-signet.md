# Book 2 Chapter 6: Tree Sisters and Tsarina Signet

## Summary
[[carl]], [[princess-donut]], and Mongo discover the corpses of three Level 8 Filipino human crawler sisters: [[grace-bautista]], [[nica-bautista]], and [[lea-bautista]] (who had transformed into a tree-like Ent race). They were killed by Circus Lemurs. Carl loots two +2 Strength rings from Nica and an Enchanted Anklet of the Fallen Oak (+1 Dex, +1 Con, +3 Double Tap) from Lea, which Donut puts on.

They scout the circus encampment and see tents, cages, and defenses being built by clowns. A poster of [[tsarina-signet]], a horned elf woman, is crossed out and labeled "Traitor." 

As night falls, they retreat, killing a few lemurs. Carl is hit by a magical mortar fired from the circus cauldrons. The building they are in collapses, and they fall into a hidden basement. 

Using Donut's *Puddle Jumper*, they return to the surface and encounter Tsarina Signet (Level 60 Elite Summoner). Carl is hit by her passive charm aura and is forced to snap his own left pinky finger to break the spell. They realize they are surrounded by circus monsters.

- **[[over-city-mechanics]]**: Introduces the concept of Elites, serial dramas, and the scripted nature of Floor 3.
- **[[survival]]**: Carl's extreme measure of breaking his own finger to resist a charm spell.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The discovery of the Bautista sisters highlights the brutal reality that many human groups are being wiped out off-camera. The contrast between the sisters' tragic end and the loot Carl extracts from their bodies shows the grim moral compromises required for survival.
