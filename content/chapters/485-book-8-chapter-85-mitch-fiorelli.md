# Chapter 485: Mitch Fiorelli

## Summary
The group enters the Desperado Club. Clarabelle lets them pass. Inside, a monster jazz band is playing, featuring early-floor acquaintance [[clay-ton]] on bass. Clay-ton recognizes [[chris-andrews-2]] and [[princess-donut]] and orders the guards to let them pass. 

In the remodeled casino, they meet the new croupier, [[mitch-fiorelli]], who is a Level 120 Enforcer for a redacted organization. Mitch agrees to set the Wheel of Fortune to the "Nothing" spot for a price of 2,000 gold per person plus a tip. Quasar pays the 5,000 gold test fee. Carl scans the thrumming portal and confirms it leads to a stasis chamber. Chris says a tearful goodbye to [[imani-c]] and jumps through. Suddenly, [[britney-proskurina]] also jumps in, leaving the party. Mitch gets angry that they cheated, demanding 10,000 gold per crawler. While Carl negotiates, Donut activates her magical glasses, noticing the wheel has no magic flow, indicating a trap. Mitch calls his guards, and the table attempts to eat Donut.
