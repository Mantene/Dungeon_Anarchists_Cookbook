# Book 4 Chapter 34: A Clash of Demons

## Summary
Carl and Donut defeat Maggie My's parasite, save Chris, flood the Faction Wars market in Larracos, and prepare to descend to Floor 6.
- **Porthus's Log**: Carl reads Porthus's log in the Cookbook: he accepted a deal of 100 seasons of servitude after Floor 11/12 in order to survive and seek revenge.
- **Demon Attack**: The Level 200 Feral Minor Demon **Slit** (who hates Samantha) attacks the bubble with an electric chain whip. Dr. P. Hu's group sends Carl an Emergency Platinum Benefactor Box containing a custom Xistera extension slot (decapitated head-tosser attachment). Carl throws Samantha's head 50 kilometers away. Slit flies after her, but a giant multi-armed goddess (Samantha's mother god) appears, rips Slit in two, and vanishes.
- **Defeating the Scree Worm**: Under camouflage, Maggie My tries to move the winding box. Carl drinks the Phase Lava Rock potion, reaches into the rocky head of Chris Andrews, and pulls out the Scree worm. The worm bites Carl and burrows into his arm. Carl drinks a double-healing potion and vomits out the worm, which Mongo eats, ending Maggie My.
- **Juice Box & Louis**: Carl discovers that Juice Box and Louis are in love and plan to get married. Juice Box carries the facsimile watch and flies down into Larracos to spread the truth.
- **Flooding Larracos**: The gate opens inside Larracos's faction market. The Water Quadrant's ocean (along with bombs, sharks, and pain jellies) dumps directly into Larracos, flooding the market, killing NPC shopkeepers, and destroying the factions' ability to buy gear.
- **Descent**: Carl reaches Level 47 (ranking #1 crawler in the dungeon) and Donut is Level 39. They enter the stairwell to Floor 6.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's decision to flood the faction market is a brilliant, systemic attack. By targeting the NPC shopkeepers, he destroys the economic loop that allows wealthy tourists to purchase gear on Floor 9 using Floor 6 hunter spoils. Carl is taking the war directly to the Syndicate's wallets.
