# Chapter 391: Chapter 83 — Samantha's Sacrifice

## Summary
Victory and Fopsy explain the political chaos in orbit: a No Confidence vote has been called on Glory, space battles are raging, and Fopsy might become Syndicate Prime Minister. 

In the warlord chat, Carl tries to negotiate with Tagg. Tagg demands they take him to the 18th floor to swap his organs, unaware that the Reaver castle has been destroyed and Larracos cannot go to the 12th floor. If they capture the Dream headquarters, both Tagg and Louis will die. 

Grieving for her sand-ooze daughter, Samantha proposes a plan to save Louis: swap his mechanical heart and lungs with the healthy ones from Princess Lunette's incomplete flesh golem body in Carl's inventory. Samantha agrees to give up using Lunette's body for herself to save Louis. Carl tells Samantha he is proud of her. 

As they prepare for the surgery, Samantha casually remarks she had a hand in the death of Geyrun. Immediately, a system message announces that Emberus has entered the realm.

- **Political Breakdown**: The disintegration of Syndicate leadership in orbit.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Samantha's character development shines in this chapter. Known primarily as a crude, chaotic, and self-centered entity, she makes a genuine sacrifice to save Louis. However, her impulsive mouth immediately ruins the moment when she admits to Geyrun's murder, dragging the wrath of Emberus down upon the entire floor.
