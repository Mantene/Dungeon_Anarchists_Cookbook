# Chapter 496: Chapter 95 — Summoning Khepri and Abandoning Emberus

## Summary
The chapter begins with [[carl]] and [[penelope]] activating Gloom Wraith Phase to infiltrate the colossal centipede, Scolopendra. System messages confirm the entry of deities [[taranis]] and [[emberus]] into the realm, with Taranis appearing outside and Emberus inside. Carl immediately receives a temporary buff from Taranis, empowering lightning attacks but increasing vulnerability to them. Inside Scolopendra's cavernous interior, Penny begins to suffocate despite her water-breathing abilities. In a desperate move, Carl pierces Penny's nipple with "Petey Motteux’s Little Secret," a magical nipple ring from [[mistress-tiatha]]'s collection, to grant her the Oxygenated benefit. [[mordecai]] clarifies that splooge, while potion-like, is treated as a food-based buff, bypassing potion cooldowns.

Carl uses Oozy Form to navigate Scolopendra's pulsating internal organs, aiming for Club Scolopendra. He monitors a timer, anticipating Scolopendra's next attack, while his allies (including [[princess-donut]], [[imani-c]], [[florin]], and [[elle-mcgibbons]]) hunker down in Team Makana's RV, protected by [[prepotente]]'s Apito memorial crystal. Upon entering Club Scolopendra, Carl loses Taranis's buff and gains Emberus's temporary effect, granting immunity to Sheol-based attacks. The club, however, proves not to be entirely safe as Scolopendra unleashes its second "Sin of Consumption" attack. Carl and Penny narrowly avoid being hit, but a horrifically tortured alien tourist named [[cerni-pinta]] (a bristle and medical debt collector) is devoured by worms after landing in the "red" on the attack's mini-wheel. Donut reports that her mounts, [[gonk]] and [[sweety]], were affected, with Sweety dying.

Scolopendra begins to tumble and crash, creating chaos within the club, despite an automated voice assuring guests of safety. Carl proceeds to the large lobby where Emberus awaits in a human-like form, radiating heat and anger. Carl puts his audacious plan into motion, claiming he knows who killed Emberus's son, [[geyrin]]. He first names [[khepri]], the "fake sun god," and uses his prize from a previous race to summon Khepri to the arena, where he appears in giant form outside. Carl then explicitly removes his Emberus ring, abandons his faith in the god, and boldly claims *he* killed Geyrun, adding a crude insult about Emberus. The chapter ends as Carl's hand tattoos burn, Emberus glows with confused rage, and several things happen at once.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
This chapter is a masterclass in the blend of gross-out humor, desperate improvisation, and high-stakes planning that defines *Dungeon Crawler Carl*. The moment Carl pierces Penny's nipple with a magical ring to save her from suffocation perfectly encapsulates his chaotic resourcefulness—it's absurd, painful, and utterly effective. The dark humor is further amplified by Mordecai's delayed clarification about splooge, leading to Carl's deadpan response about the pig's nipple.

Carl's audacious plan to confront Emberus is a brilliant piece of narrative tension. His calculated lies, the summoning of Khepri, and especially his theatrical abandonment of Emberus and direct provocation are classic Carl. It's a high-risk, high-reward maneuver that demonstrates his absolute commitment to his elaborate contingencies, even when it means facing the direct wrath of a god. The tragic interlude with Cerni Pinta, a corporate alien debt collector brutally devoured by worms, serves as a poignant reminder of the dungeon's indiscriminate cruelty, offering a fleeting moment of cosmic irony within the chaos.
