# Chapter 367: Chapter 61 — War Mage Rebellion

## Summary
The volcanic explosion sends *Party Planner* cartwheeling. [[louis-santiago-2]] manages to stabilize the airship despite losing a propeller. [[skarn]] transforms into an octopus-like shape to save [[bonnie]], whose broken arm instantly heals. 

With the ship damaged, Carl, [[samantha]], and the stowaway kids prepare to jump. Carl orders Skarn and Bonnie to return to Area 52 and retreat to the safety of Larracos rather than Shanty Town. Carl uses a *Controlled Feather Fall* scroll to jump, landing in Reaver territory near [[elle-mcgibbons]]'s portal beacon.

As they land, the goddess [[eris]] (chaos) is spontaneously summoned due to the battlefield conditions. Simultaneously, Team Retribution occupies the throne rooms of both the Bone Clan and the Dream. 

An emergency action item proposed by Sir Ferdinand is approved: Effective immediately, Ascendency players piloting deities must appear in their designed forms and cannot choose to stay back. Any deities currently active are instantly populated by their pilots. 

A group of enemies teleports directly to Elle's portal. Carl rushes forward to find Elle thrown by a spell and knocked unconscious. Warlord [[prince-stalwart|Stalwart]] is on his knees, surrounded by four dead war mages and four living ones. 

The living war mages are led by [[akuma]], a level 100 high-elf symbiote and leader of the **War Mage Rebellion**. The system notes that war mages are dungeon-born symbiotes created when a flesher reanimates a skeleton (a symbiote) and is re-sleeved 12 to 16 times until the magic itself becomes sapient. They are typically mind-wiped and used as mercenaries, but they have retained their memories and rebelled. Furthermore, their heads explode (creating a *Sapient Moment of Destruction*) roughly ten minutes after death.

The three war mages chant, causing Warlord Stalwart to explode. Before dying, Stalwart says: "My sister was right." Akuma, who has snatched the **Gate of the Feral Gods** from Elle, stares at Carl's bare feet, sneers, "Nice shoes, asshole," and teleports away with his squad.

## Themes & Plot Threads
* **Dungeon-Born Sentience**: The war mages represent a class of entities created by combat mechanics who have achieved sapience and rebelled against their roles.
* **Mandatory Deity Piloting**: The approval of Ferdinand's emergency action item forces Ascendency sponsors to inhabit the physical forms of their deities.
* **The War Mage Rebellion**: The emergence of this new "non-team entity" disrupts the faction balance, turning a scripted war into a genuine rebellion.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Akuma's insult regarding Carl's bare feet shows that the war mages retain the typical arrogance of high-elf spellcasters. The revelation of the war mages' origin—being recycled and mind-wiped repeatedly—provides a clear motive for their rebellion: they are fighting against their slavery.
