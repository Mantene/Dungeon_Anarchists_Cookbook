# Chapter 482: Plan Mach Schnell

## Summary
In the final seconds before the starting light of the last heat turns green, the crawlers check in via chat. Several of them have been transformed by the floor's environmental effect: [[elle-mcgibbons]] is now a goblin, Jacobus is a starfish, Chris is a werewolf in human form, and [[carl]] is transformed into a long-haired dachshund. Carl's speech is altered to include a high-pitched German accent. 

Because Carl cannot drive in his dog form, [[princess-donut]] adds [[samantha]] as a driver and takes control of the steering mechanism. Carl accesses the group messaging to set up "Plan Mach Schnell" (Contingency Three). Carl verifies with [[mordecai]] that the guild garden is still intact. He attempts to reason with [[osvaldo]], but Osvaldo refuses to back down, stating it is too late. The light turns green. Donut immediately attacks the vine-unicorn [[dwight]], removing its magical horn and killing it to eliminate Team Sparkles. Carl triggers the detonator for the ceiling poleaxe charges, preparing to drop the roof on the starting area to create an exit path.

This chapter showcases how far the remaining crawlers have come in terms of combat efficiency, dismantling Dwight's team in under a second.
