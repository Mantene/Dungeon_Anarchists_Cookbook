# Book 4 Chapter 1: Welcome to the Bubbles
![[Floor5AIGenOverview.jpg]]
## Summary
Carl, Donut, and Katia arrive on Floor 5, "The Bubbles" (specifically Bubble #543, in the Air Quadrant). 
- **Bounty Double Modifier**: Carl receives a Gold Venison Box for entering the stairwell while in the top 10, but the reward notes that all bounties against him are now doubled.
- **Donut's Class Selection**: Donut selects the **Glass Cannon** class, gaining lower spell costs, higher training speed, and stronger magic missiles, but she fails to receive the +15 Constitution base due to her Character Actor roll.
- **Hump Town Entrance**: The party walks through a sandstorm and discovers a subterranean forcefield-blocked tomb (the Necropolis of Anser). They arrive at Hump Town, a brothel town populated by bipod camels called Dromedarians. They meet **Clay** (a Dromedarian guard) and pay a toll with a blitz stick to enter. Clay tells them to stay out of City Hall, triggering a quest: **"Stay out of city hall."**
- **The Bubble Mechanics**: The sandstorm ends, and the camels roll up the fabric awnings covering the town. Carl looks up and sees that the entire quadrant is covered by a high-altitude dome ("bubble") about 4-5 kilometers high. The air castle is visible as a tiny speck near the top of the dome.
- **Mordecai's New Form**: **Mordecai** joins them at a tavern called The Toe, having been transformed into a Rock Edge Skyfowl (cleric build, clipped flight, no potion brewing). They watch a system broadcast explaining the floor rules:
  - The floor has 1,172 bubbles. Each bubble contains 150+ crawlers and is divided into four quadrants (Land, Sea, Air, Subterranean).
  - Each quadrant contains a single castle and stairwell.
  - To pooped/exit the bubble and proceed to Floor 6, **all four castles** in the bubble must be conquered.

## Characters Introduced
- [[clay]] — Level 30 Dromedarian guard at Hump Town.
- [[juice-box]] — Changeling prostitute at The Toe.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival-of-the-collapse]]**: The crawlers are split into groups of 150 per bubble, and all four castles must be taken to pop the bubble and progress.
- **[[systemic-oppression]]**: The introduction of the double bounty modifier penalizes high-performing crawlers.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Floor 5 introduces a new level of cooperative pressure. By tying progress to the fall of all four castles in a bubble, the system forces crawlers to coordinate, meaning that stronger crawlers cannot simply abandon weaker ones. This design increases the likelihood of friction and failure. Donut's Glass Cannon class makes her incredibly powerful offensively but extremely fragile, highlighting the escalating risk.
