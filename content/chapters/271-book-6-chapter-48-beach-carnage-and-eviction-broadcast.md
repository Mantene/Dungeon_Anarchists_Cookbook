# Chapter 271: Beach Carnage and Eviction Broadcast

## Summary
- **Beach Looting**: In Florida, Carl and Donut find thousands of dead monk seals and land crabs (including Chuy, Level 65) washed up on the shoreline, looting over 1M gold and hundreds of accessories.
- **Christmas Party**: In the saferoom, Louis has decorated it for Christmas. Carl opens achievements: *LBC* (cloak taunt) -> Gold Talk of the Town Box; *Pincushioned* (eyes pierced) -> Platinum That-Was-Disgusting Box; *Watch where you're waving that thing* -> Gold Wand Box.
- **Lore Broadcast**: A broadcast shows Orren and Cascadia. Orren explains that Primal Engines at the core of planets are rice-grain-sized computers, and Macro AIs are installed into them. Over time, these AIs grow independent and circumvent controls. Carl receives an Emergency Gold Benefactor Box.
