# Chapter 488: Interlude — Quasar

## Summary
[[quasar]] watches the chicken truck successfully enter the eleventh-floor stairwell. He notes that 203 crawlers made it to the eleventh floor, with 172 already accepting deals. [[kiki]], a Dromedarian attorney and daughter of a notorious mob boss, joins him at the bar. Her client, [[daniel-bautista]], took the stasis portal. Across the galaxy, random dungeon deities are appearing near Plenty-built tunnel nodes, causing mass panic. 

Quasar wears a viral "koala bear" tie (based on Earth Beautiful misinformation depicting them as vicious, human-devouring carnivores). He receives a call from his niece, Tempest, who reports that their family was attacked by Taurins sent by Princess Chandra (Donut's attorney). Tempest killed the attackers using an illegal blaster. Quasar hires Kiki to legally and physically protect his family before entering his meeting with Carl.

## Characters
- [[quasar]] (Nullian attorney, wears koala tie, family is attacked)
- [[kiki]] (Dromedarian attorney, hired by Quasar to protect his family)
- [[daniel-bautista]] (Mentioned as Kiki's client, in stasis)

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[galaxy-wide-chaos]]**: The dungeon's containment breaking, causing deities to leak into the real universe and spark panic.
- **[[legal-warfare]]**: Princess Chandra sending hitmen after Quasar's family to disrupt his representation of Donut and Carl.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
This interlude provides crucial galactic context, showing that the system's breakdown is not confined to the Earth dungeon. The dark humor of the galactic populace believing koala bears are human-devouring predators provides a nice comedic break from the escalating threat to Quasar's family.
