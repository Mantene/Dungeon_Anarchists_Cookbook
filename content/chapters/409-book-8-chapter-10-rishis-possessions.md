# Chapter 409: Chapter 10 — Rishi's Possessions

## Summary
The safe room cleaner bot disintegrates the remains of the sliced kitchen table. Carl goes over his newly acquired achievements and boxes:
- **Bombs Away!** (for dropping high-yield explosives while falling from Larracos): Yields a **Gold Payload Box** containing a **Hotlist Expansion Potion** that doubles his hotlist capacity from 10 to 20 slots.
- **Default Winner** (for winning the Faction Wars by disqualification/default): Yields a **Bronze Vanessa Williams Box** containing an unenchanted can of white spray paint.
- **Wastrel** (for letting Donut eat the Ring of Divine Suffering): Yields a reward of "Fuck off."
- **Come to Daddy** (for rejecting the exit deal): Yields a **Bronze Daddy's Little Secret Box** containing five **Bomb Blanket** potions (which prevent small explosives from detonating when thrown over them).
- **Gold Adventurer Box**: Yields a quadruped baby carrier backpack that allows Donut to phase or shift forms along with Carl when worn.
- Carl's explosive stock is replenished with Hob-lobbers, dynamite, and upgraded **Trollish Boom Boxes**.

Finally, Carl opens the **Legendary Fan Box** shaped like a cobra's head. Because Chandra asserted Widow's Rights to claim Carl as her husband, the system has awarded Carl all the holdings and personal possessions of her late husband, [[rishi]] (the Blood Sultanate warlord). 

The box dumps a massive, ceiling-high mountain of wet, scorched, sewage-smelling garbage into the safe room, blocking the private rooms and bathroom. The pile contains melted trophies (including a damaged GreaserBall participation trophy), ruined furniture, broken electronics, and wet clothes. 

[[samantha]] and [[bigs]] dive into the trash pile. Samantha retrieves an unlicensed, bootleg makeup palette featuring Donut's face and colors like "A Night Out with Mongo" and "The Butcher's Mascara," which outrages Donut.

## Characters
- [[carl]] — Opens his boxes and receives the massive pile of Rishi's household garbage.
- [[princess-donut]] — Disgusted by the trash and furious at the bootleg makeup palette.
- [[bigs]] — Finds the trash pile cozy and compares it to her barracks.
- [[samantha]] — Spelunks in the trash pile and retrieves the unlicensed makeup.
- [[mordecai]] — Questions how the system transported so much junk into the safe room so quickly.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- [[syndicate-corruption]] — The literal dumping of a deceased NPC's household possessions onto a crawler as a "marriage gift" highlights the system's absolute lack of boundaries.
- [[the-collapse-of-rules]] — The cleaner bot using disintegration rays and the safe room being physically blocked by massive external items represents systemic chaos.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The contrast between Donut's sleek celestial upgrades and Carl's junk-filled rewards is highly symbolic of their respective roles in the crawl. The mountain of stinky Naga trash represents the literal weight of Carl's unwanted marriage to Chandra and the physical clutter of the Syndicate's corrupt bureaucracy.
