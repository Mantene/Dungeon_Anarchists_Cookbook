# Chapter 306: Chapter 6 — Casket in the Sky

## Summary
- **Airship Portal**: Louis explains his celestial box guild upgrade: a roof-based dimensional hangar portal. The hangar allows airships to be stored, upgraded, and repaired, but its external portal door is vulnerable to destruction. No airships are allowed on the 10th floor.
- **The Party Planner**: They view Louis's stealth tilt-rotor light bomber, named **Party Planner** (in honor of Louis's deceased partner Firas's suggestion).
- **Casket in the Sky Details**: The vehicle is fifty feet long, oval, glass-nosed, B-17 hybrid. It counts as Faction Wars "Armor" (limit of 10 at the start of fighting, though more can be stolen). It has forward/aft guns, stealth, and a multi-use bomb bay that gives a 50% increase to bomb yield.
- **Reality Dissociation**: Carl spots real-world tinsel on the floor, triggering a brief moment of dissociation regarding the simulated nature of the dungeon. He recalls the warnings about the starfailsafe and Goff's admonition to be sure it is their only option.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The B-17 bomber-like "Casket in the Sky" offers a potent offensive option, though its description warns that only a pilot with "gonads the size of bowling balls" would fly it into fortified territory. Carl's reaction to the real-world tinsel shows his increasing mental strain and dread regarding the failsafe upgrade.
