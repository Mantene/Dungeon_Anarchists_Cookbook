# Book 3 Chapter 6: Queasy Resistance and Dwarven Robots

## Summary
At Banshee Station (#116), the party retreats into a cave network. Carl explains to Katia that Vernon's wife and memories were artificial. Kua-tin Admin [[zev]] messages Carl and Donut, warning them that the audience finds Katia "boring." She urges them to make her more vibrant or ditch her to avoid losing views and sponsorships. Desperate to stay, Katia lets Donut design a new look for her: a black bodysuit, boots, and a purple mohawk.

They fight Level 21 Red Cornets (skinless rabbit monsters) whose echolocation sound attacks inflict a **Queasy** debuff (severe vomiting). After multiple fights, the party gains the **Queasy Resistance** skill. Mongo reaches Level 14. 

They explore the warren's suspected boss room, finding a blue-pulsing control sigil under Carl's feet (visible only to him via *Escape Plan*). The room is a storage bay containing a wire cage cart (which Carl inventories) and two deactivated Dwarven Industrial Light-Duty Automatons (robots). Carl blows up the robots with hobgoblin dynamite to harvest gears, damaged batteries, and 15 tons of scrap metal. [[daniel-bautista-2]] messages Carl that [[imani-c]] is alive and wants to meet at the Desperado Club. He also shares that stations ending in 1 have a Desperado Club, while those ending in 9 have a Club Vanquisher.

## Characters Introduced
- None (First appearance of Red Cornets and Dwarven Automatons).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[televised-exploitation]]**: The viewers' rating demands forcing Katia to alter her appearance.
- **[[survival-of-the-collapse]]**: Gathering information on the floor layout and monster behavior.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Katia's desperate compliance with Donut's styling shows the intense psychological pressure on crawlers to conform to the intergalactic viewers' desires. To be "boring" is a death sentence, forcing crawlers to trade away their dignity for ratings.
