# Book 6 Chapter 8: The Bedlam Bride

## Summary
Carl and Donut watch the floor's first recap show, receive detailed card combat rules, and receive a distress call from Samantha.
- **The Redacted Recap**: The crawlers watch a highly redacted recap episode:
  - Crawlers are slaughtered by an undead Easter Bunny and a sentient umbrella.
  - Elle captures a Sasquatch wearing Bermuda shorts.
  - Prepotente captures a giant bat.
  - Mordecai notices a brief appearance of **[[prime-minister-glory]]** (the Gleener boss of the entire Syndicate) on screen next to Kevin, noting this is highly unusual.
- **Card Combat Rules**: The system delivers the comprehensive mechanical rules of [[t-ghee-card-combat]] (including the 4-card starting hand, 10-second draw cycles, 30-second discard cooldown, and squad leader lockout). It also announces the addition of Mock Battle Arenas to saferooms.
- **Samantha's Distress Call**: Samantha messages Carl via chat: she scouted the southern coast and was eaten by a spider-like demigoddess named **[[shi-maria]]** (known by aliases such as the **Bedlam Bride**). Samantha is alive inside her stomach and claims Shi Maria is a rare pulpmancer mage who has agreed to put Samantha into a physical body if Carl retrieves her.

## Characters Introduced
- [[prime-minister-glory]] — Gleener Syndicate Prime Minister (mentioned).
- [[shi-maria]] — Spider demigoddess and pulpmancer (mentioned).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[t-ghee-card-combat]]**: Full rules drop and mechanical details of the card game.
- **Syndicate Politics**: Prime Minister Glory's appearance signals mounting tension in the Syndicate hierarchy.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Samantha getting eaten by a spider demigoddess because she "hitched a ride" is classic comedic timing. The name "The Bedlam Bride" immediately appeals to Donut, setting up their next long-term travel goal. The card game mechanics are highly restrictive, particularly the lockout of the squad leader's normal spells, making physical martial capabilities and squad cooperation vital.
