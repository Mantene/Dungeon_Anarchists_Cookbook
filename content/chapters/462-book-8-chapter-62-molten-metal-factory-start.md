# Chapter 462: Molten Metal Factory Start

## Summary
The chapter begins with an excerpt from *A Banquet Fit for a Princess* by Mees Koning, a deceased Half-Orc mage, who thanks [[princess-donut]] for a brief, kind conversation before Faction Wars and leaves her a drawing of a level 14 "Big-Ass Magnet" spell. 

Before the race, [[carl]] visits the pet stables to feed Rend and Penelope. He finds [[dong-quixote]] patting Gonk the swamp yak, wishing he could charge into battle one last time. In the truck at the starting line, Carl and Donut are joined by Dong, Porky (in a white gimp suit), and [[grigori]] (who is carsick). They receive updates: [[zhang]] has a solo driver penalty, and [[mordecai]] confirms the status cactus is holding steady. Dr. Metcalf, their GPS, maps the Gasworks Screw Factory, Steel Mill, and Doll Workshop, warning of molten metal floors that require wall- or ceiling-driving. The starting lights turn green, and they take off.
