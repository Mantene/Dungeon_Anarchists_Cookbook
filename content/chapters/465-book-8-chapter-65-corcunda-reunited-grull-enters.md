# Chapter 465: Corcunda Reunited & Grull Enters

## Summary
[[carl]] drives the truck upside down on the ceiling tracks of the massive, molten-steel-filled mill room using the Roller Limbo upgrade. In the back of the inverted truck, [[grigori]] and [[dong-quixote]] work frantically to fuse the two halves of [[corcunda]] back together. They seal the brains and heads using plastic wrap. [[princess-donut]] sits on the oven burner, expressing vocal disgust at the exposed organs and confusion at the mantaur's anatomy.

The room is populated by "Slag Elementals" (Level 75 Neighborhood Bosses, infused and regenerating). [[prepotente]] messages that hitting them with non-molten metal staggers them. Carl uses the trans-dimensional upgrade to phase through a slower ferret mount. A glob of molten metal hits the truck's hood, exposing the engine.

Grigori casts the final part of the fusion spell. The two halves of Corcunda fuse, and their health bars join into one under the name **Corcunda**. Dong holds the fused mantaur with all his strength, though Dong's own health is dangerously low. Immediately after, a Marked-for-Death symbol appears over Corcunda's head, and a system message announces: **"Grull has entered the realm."**

## Characters

- [[dong-quixote]] — Present, holds the two halves of Corcunda together, health in the red.
- [[corcunda]] — Present (reunited Corky and Porky).
- [[grigori]] — Present, casts the fusion spell.
- [[prepotente]] — Mentioned via chat, gives tactical advice about the elementals.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **"I have dreamed thee too long"**: The quest to reunite Corky and Porky is completed, but it triggers immediate divine intervention.
- **Deity Hostility**: The entrance of the war god [[grull]] to punish Corcunda for failing his faith.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The visual of the crawlers performing complex flesh-sculpting surgery while standing on the ceiling of a speeding truck over a river of molten metal is a highlight of the book's chaotic style. The immediate arrival of Grull underscores that resolving quests in the dungeon often leads to even greater threats.
