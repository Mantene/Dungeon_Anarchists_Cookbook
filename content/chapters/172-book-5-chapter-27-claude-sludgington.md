# Book 5 Chapter 27: Claude Sludgington

## Summary
- **Accidental Teleportation**: Carl pulls his xistera extension into his inventory, recalling Samantha. However, Samantha was biting onto the borough boss inside the castle, causing the Level 65 Borough Boss [[claude-sludgington-the-fourth]] (Eryops Gigantis) to teleport directly onto the muddy shore.
- **Nadine's Death**: Claude immediately attacks. He shoots a multi-branch lightning bolt that hits [[nadine]], vaporizing the giant caterpillar.
- **The Battle**: 
  - Carl casts *Protective Shell*, which launches the massive amphibian straight up into the air. 
  - Donut casts a level-10 *Fireball* (from the new bracelet) directly into Claude's unarmored stomach as he falls.
  - Claude splats on the dome and slides off, getting stuck face-down in the trees.
  - Carl and Donut target his open stomach wound with a hob-lobber and magic missiles.
  - Mongo (with Donut on his back) charges and dives directly into the stomach wound, eating his way out the back of the boss.
- **Boss Explodes**: The shell spell wears off, and the trees spring-launch the dead Claude onto Carl, crushing him. Carl takes massive damage but heals. Claude's body explodes in gore. Samantha lands in Carl's lap.

## Characters Introduced
- [[claude-sludgington-the-fourth]] — Level 65 Borough Boss (Eryops Gigantis), deceased.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[vengeance-of-the-daughter]]**: The battle at Fort Freedom reaches a bloody climax on the shore.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The accidental teleportation of Claude Sludgington is a chaotic and terrifying twist. By pulling his xistera extension, Carl inadvertently brings the boss directly to their position. The death of Nadine is a tragic loss, but it triggers a furious, coordinated counter-assault. The combination of Carl's Protective Shell, Donut's Level-10 Fireball, and Mongo's savage internal devouring is a spectacular display of crawler teamwork.
