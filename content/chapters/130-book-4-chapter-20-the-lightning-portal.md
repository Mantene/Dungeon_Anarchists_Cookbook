# Book 4 Chapter 20: The Lightning Portal

## Summary
Carl and Donut travel to the Land Quadrant, meet the battle-hardened Gwendolyn Duet, and prepare to storm the Sandcastle of the Mad Dune Mage.
- **Decapitated House**: Louis and Firas fly the floating house to Hump Town to pick up Carl and Donut. To allow the house to cross quadrant borders without the stairwell, they had to decapitate the home, removing the entire second floor and master closet (leaving the first floor open to the elements). The stairwell remains guarded in the desert.
- **The Land Quadrant**: The house lands on the Land Quadrant's beach. Carl meets **Gwendolyn Duet** (Level 34 Human Fighter), a rough, no-nonsense Canadian crawler with First Nations tattoos.
- **The Sandcastle Dilemma**: The "castle" is a medieval-style fortress made of sand, guarding the main entrance to the Necropolis. There are two ways to conquer it:
  - **The Easy Way (Gwen's Plan)**: Turn a drainage wheel to release the Necropolis's water. This would wash away the sandcastle like a hose, but also dissolve the Mad Dune Mage Ghazi, losing the third piece of the watch-and-box artifact (*The Winding Box*).
  - **The Hard Way (Katia/Zev's Plan)**: Electrify the doorway using two lightning rod towers during a sandstorm. This opens a temporary glass hallway into the castle, allowing a small party to enter and confront the mage.
- **Entering the Portal**: During a sandstorm, Carl notices a loose electrical cable on the south tower. He instructs **Tran** (Level 28 Human Swashbuckler) to drag the wire to the drainage wheel to ground it. A lightning bolt strikes the towers, electrifying the doorway. The magical door vanishes, opening a glass hallway. Katia, Carl, Donut, and Mongo rush inside.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's shipyard experience allows him to spot the electrical redundancy and loose wire immediately. The dynamic between Katia and Gwendolyn highlights the friction between survival pragmatism (Gwen's easy way) and the broader rebellion goals (Katia/Zev's hard way).
