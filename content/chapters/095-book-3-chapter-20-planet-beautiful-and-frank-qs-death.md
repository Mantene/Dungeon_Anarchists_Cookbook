# Book 3 Chapter 20: Planet Beautiful and Frank Q's Death

## Summary
Carl and Donut are teleported to Senegal Production Systems' deluxe submarine trailer for their appearance on the *Planet Beautiful* show. Because the trailer is below sea-level (within the main zone's five-kilometer depth limit), Carl has full access to his interface and inventory. The trailer's robot assistant, Mexx-6000, humorously notes that the Borant Corporation built this season's crawl with "spit and duct tape" and swap-meet technology, leaving major security vulnerabilities.

At the physical soundbooth studio, Carl objects to his script about human beauty pageants. The script, written by a cheap, rushed AI consultant, is filled with internet subculture terminology (such as "roasties" and "beta males") and Fabio photos. Carl refuses to read it, forcing the irritated gray alien producer, Bin, to swap it for a script about video arcade history. Donut, meanwhile, enthusiastically narrates a segment about pet shows, fabricating a dramatic tale of intrigue surrounding cocker spaniels.

After the recording, they return to the trailer's green room. Donut receives a message from Sledgie at the Desperado Club: **Chris Andrews** (now a rock monster of a different kind) entered the club, fought and killed **Frank Q**, and walked out. Chris is now banned from the club. Carl is shocked, remembering Brandon An's final words about loving his quiet brother.

Before exiting the submarine, Carl pilfers several self-powered refrigerating shelves, a makeup table, and a couch, storing them in his inventory for future bomb/trap crafting.

Meanwhile, Daniel Bautista II messages with a major crisis: they accidentally killed the Kravyad at Station 435 while trying to paralyze her. The employee portal snapped shut, leaving over 150 crawlers (including Bautista, who sold his conductor hat) stranded. Carl warns them to hike 50 kilometers on foot to the abyss portal, find the catwalk, and attempt to hijack a named train at Station 436.

As Carl prepares to step through the portal back to their personal space, he pulls a screenshot and discovers that their room is suddenly packed with around thirty female crawlers, including Hekla and her daughters, alongside Katia.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[televised-exploitation]]**: The *Planet Beautiful* segment shows how the Syndicate commercializes human culture, utilizing inaccurate and offensive AI-generated scripts to feed the galaxy's curiosity.
- **[[threat-of-frank-q]]**: The thread is fully resolved with Frank Q's death at the hands of Chris Andrews.
- **[[survival-of-the-collapse]]**: Bautista's group faces an existential crisis after the closure of the employee portal, forcing a desperate trek to the abyss.
- **[[escorting-meadow-lark-residents]]**: Chris Andrews' radical shift to a stone monster and his sudden execution of Frank Q marks a dark development for the Meadow Lark survivors.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Chris Andrews' killing of Frank Q highlights the psychological toll of the crawl. The quiet, passive caretaker has transformed into a stone monster who takes justice into his own hands. The dual-layer zone explanation by Mexx-6000 exposes the slapdash, cost-cutting nature of the Borant Corporation's construction of the dungeon, which Carl immediately exploits by stealing furniture. The final cliffhanger of Hekla's daughters occupying Carl's personal space suggests Katia has made a major, potentially dangerous alliance behind Carl's back.
