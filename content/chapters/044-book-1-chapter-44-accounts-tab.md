# Book 1 Chapter 44: The Accounts Tab

## Summary
Back in the safe room, [[princess-donut]] opens her gold sapper boxes, obtaining a **Spike Module** and **Alarm Module**. She admits to [[carl]] that she overheard [[mordecai]] advising him to leave her before the ninth floor Faction Wars. Carl tells her not to worry.

The recap episode reveals that the remaining crawler count has dropped below one million to **990,303**. The system announcement details modifications to Floor 2:
1. Non-sapient mobs will no longer disintegrate when descending stairs (patching the exploit Carl used).
2. The number of Brindled Vespas is halved, and their acid damage is slightly reduced.
3. Brindle Grubs now have only a 50% chance to proceed to the pupa stage.

Carl unlocks a new HUD tab: **ACCOUNTS**. It tracks his *Dungeon Codex* royalty fees for the *Carl's Jug O' Boom* (currently at 4 kills/4 gold). Cashing out incurs a 25% deposit fee (rounded up).

[[zev]] returns, explaining she was cleared on appeal. She reveals that Borant is delighted with Carl and Donut because the "Pork Boy Snick" viral video humiliated a prince of the **Skull Empire**, their political rivals. Carl's appearance fee has doubled, making it equal to [[lucia-mar]]'s. 

Zev confirms that their next interview is on [[odette]]'s show and that the Kua-tin Bloom party is pleased with their performance. Donut teases Carl about the viral video as they head out.
