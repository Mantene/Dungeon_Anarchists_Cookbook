# Chapter 299: Porthus

## Summary
- **11th Floor Outreach Guild**: Porthus, a High Elf Rogue covered in blood, meets with his virtual soother attorney. The 10th floor is described as a nightmare where non-natural objects had sapience and consumed each other.
- **Menerva's Sacrifice**: Porthus regrets panicking and taking the exit portal, leaving his friend Menerva behind on the 10th floor with her foot stuck.
- **Game Guide Agreement**: Porthus rejects low-floor undead and city attendant deals, and chooses the **game guide** option (100 seasons indentureship, starting at half his level). This choice allows him to keep some inventory trinkets and sentiment items, and gives him a chance to help future crawlers via the Cookbook.
- **Cookbook Author**: Porthus is confirmed as the author of the second edition of the *Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook*.
- **Current Status**: Porthus is missing and wanted by Syndicate Security.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Porthus's backstory is a poignant study of survivor's guilt and panic. His decision to become a game guide reflects a desire to redeem himself by helping future crawlers, aligning with the Cookbook's legacy.
