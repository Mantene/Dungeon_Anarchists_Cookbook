# Book 1 Chapter 26: The Ball of Swine

## Summary
With 6 hours and 30 minutes left before Floor 1 collapses, [[carl]], [[princess-donut]], and the Meadow Lark crew ([[brandon-an]], [[chris-andrews-2]], [[yolanda-martinez-13]], [[imani-c]]) gather at the entrance of the Borough Boss chamber. Despite their protests, [[agatha]] wheels her shopping cart in. The gate slams shut, and the courtyard walls sink into the ground, revealing a circular subway-like track. The arena transforms into a rave-like scene with neon lasers, flashing blacklights, and pounding EDM music designed to mask the sound of the approaching boss.

The Borough Boss is revealed: **The Ball of Swine** (Level 15), a massive rolling sphere of flesh composed of 30 Tuskling aristocrats (knights and courtesans) merged together in a battle formation. The party quickly builds "The Speedbump" redoubt—a modular, angled fortress constructed from looted goblin tables, weight benches, and iron bars. Carl, Brandon, and Chris had embedded 50 *Enchanted Shuriken of Bloodlust* into the front ramp to inflict compounding damage on the rolling boss. 

Agatha sits in the center of the redoubt. The swine ball hits the structure at high speed, getting wedged between the ramp and the ceiling. The pressure causes the ceiling to start collapsing, and bolts shoot off like shrapnel. Working from inside, Carl, Imani, and the others stab the flesh of the ball with shuriken-tipped spears. 

Carl pops the ball. It bursts, and the 30 Tusklings fall out, disoriented and unarmed. They are Level 4 (Knights) and Level 5 (Courtesans) wearing tuxedos and glittering evening gowns. Having no memory of their time in the ball, they are easily slaughtered by the party. Carl uses his war gauntlet to kill the Tusklings, and the red forcefield around the central stairwell drops.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival-of-the-collapse]]**: Popping the Ball of Swine is a massive victory, unlocking the stairwell to Floor 2 for the Meadow Lark residents.
- **[[televised-exploitation]]**: The rave theme of the fight, complete with neon lasers and EDM music, emphasizes the show's focus on spectacle and ratings over crawler safety.
- **[[solidarity-and-duty]]**: The group's successful defense of Agatha and their coordinated construction of the redoubt highlight their teamwork and mutual protection.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The design of the Ball of Swine is a brilliant and grotesque narrative setup. The Tusklings are presented as literal "fat cats" (aristocrats in formal wear) who form a rolling ball of destruction to oppress the weak, which Carl and the caretakers literalize by using physical engineering to pop them. The fight shows the power of the *Shuriken of Bloodlust* when combined with physical traps, bypassing a direct battle through mechanical exploit.
