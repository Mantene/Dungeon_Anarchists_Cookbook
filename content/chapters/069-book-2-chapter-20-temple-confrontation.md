# Book 2 Chapter 20: Temple Confrontation

## Summary
[[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] return to the safe room. [[zev]] visits them, wearing her spacesuit, expressing relief at their survival. Carl and Donut discuss the conspiracy: Prince Stalwart was set up to attack them, and Borant benefits from the resulting Valtay-Skull war. 

They exit the safe room to confront the city elves of the 201st Security Group at the **Apito Education Center** (labeled *Corrupt Temple* on the minimap). They meet [[salvatore]] and [[carmine]] out front. Donut's oakwood anklet triggers the elves' religious rage, as they associate it with the *Oak Fell* (antichrist). 

An NPC dwarf mother, [[eunice]], hits Salvatore on the head with a rolling pin in retaliation for the elves throwing rocks at her children. An Icer elf freezes Eunice. The town guards arrive and try to attack the frozen dwarf. Carl initiates a fight by punching a level-75 guard, branding him a "troublemaker" in the town. 

Eunice's son, [[ricky-joe]], punches Mongo for eating his pet salamander Benjamin. Mongo bites Ricky Joe's arm off. Carl grabs the bleeding boy, tosses a custom *Boom Jug*, and Puddle Jumps the party to a rooftop. The explosion kills the eight remaining elves and burns the temple down. Carl stabilizes Ricky Joe with a potion. Mongo regurgitates the severed arm.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The scene where Eunice beats Salvatore with a rolling pin followed by Mongo eating Ricky Joe's arm is a peak example of the dark, chaotic comedy of the dungeon. Carl's decision to punch the invincible guard to save Eunice shows his unyielding moral compass, even at the cost of turning the town guards hostile.
