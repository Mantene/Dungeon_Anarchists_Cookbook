# Book 5 Chapter 47: Reaching Miriam Dom

## Summary
- **Vampire Progenitor Crisis**: Carl and Donut reach the clearing. Miriam explains that Viscount Fog died at sunset and that Prepotente's Ring of Divine Suffering has marked her, preventing healing.
- **Miriam's Death**: To cure the floor's vampirism and save Pony, Miriam must die. Carl marks her with his own Ring of Divine Suffering right before dawn. She crumbles to dust at sunrise, curing the vampires. Carl gains +3 Intelligence.
