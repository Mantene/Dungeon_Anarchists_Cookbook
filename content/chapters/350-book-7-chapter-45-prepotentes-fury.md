# Chapter 350: Chapter 45 — Prepotente's Fury

## Summary
[[carl|Carl]] and [[princess-donut|Donut]] return from the Warlord Council. Carl checks the officer list and finds everyone safe, but the plan has gone sideways. 

[[elle-mcgibbons|Elle]] reports that most of the naga army was already found dead, rotting in their barracks. [[prepotente|Prepotente]] (now level 99) went apeshit, slaughtered the naga army, but was locked up in a time-based penalty stasis by [[baroness-victory|Baroness Victory]] and the other judges for threatening them during a hearing. The nagas had claimed Prepotente was working for Carl. Released from stasis, Prepotente is furious that Carl's army is in the naga territory, accusing Carl of ruining his plans for vengeance against the Sepsis Whore (the owners of the *Blood Hunter* show that killed Miriam Dom).

To make matters worse, Prepotente insulted [[imani-c|Imani]], causing the protective sluggalos to go crazy and chase him across naga territory, leaving rivers of orange goo. Carl adjusts the plan: they will assault the castle without the sluggalos. [[mordecai|Mordecai]] hands Carl potion balls designed to provoke specific deities and warns him not to use too many on the same religion. Carl gathers the potions and [[samantha|Samantha]] and prepares to move out.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Prepotente's fury at Carl highlights the tragedy of his isolation. His level 99 status makes him the strongest crawler in the dungeon, yet he is emotionally volatile and deeply lonely. His anger at Carl is fueled by his grief over Miriam Dom, which mirrors Carl's own anger. The conflict between their parallel plans shows that cooperation is difficult but necessary if either is to survive this floor.
