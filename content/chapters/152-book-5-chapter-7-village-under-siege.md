# Book 5 Chapter 7: Village under Siege

## Summary
Carl and Donut separate from Miriam and Prepotente, entering a Whataburger saferoom in a Bugbear Settlement, where they prepare for the hunter release.
- **Bugbear Settlement**: Carl arrives at the [[small-bugbear-settlement]]. Like other towns near Zockau, it has already been looted and trapped by the hunters. Carl separates from Miriam and Prepotente, promising to stay in touch.
- **Saferoom Whataburger**: Carl and Donut enter a pub saferoom that houses a **Whataburger** fast-food restaurant. Carl grabs a soda, and Donut releases Mongo.
- **Personal Space Meeting**: In the personal space, they meet Katia, Louis, Firas, and Britney. Chris is outside waiting for Team Meadow Lark. Donut preemptively cuts off Mordecai's lecture, stating she won't abandon Carl but demands he share his plans.
- **The Next Plan**: Mordecai advises them to open their boxes, reset buffs, watch the recap, and put distance between themselves and Zockau before the hunters are released in 13 hours. Carl hints at a much larger explosion he is planning.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival-of-the-collapse]]**: The crawlers rely on familiar Earth saferoom environments (Whataburger) to recuperate and prepare for the upcoming hunter onslaught.
- **[[the-butchers-masquerade]]**: The countdown to the hunter release (13 hours) drives the immediate need for tactical relocation.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's direct interception of Mordecai's warnings shows her growth in maturity and resolve. She recognizes that she cannot stop Carl's high-risk plans, so she chooses to participate and manage the risks together as a unified front, rather than fracturing their partnership.
