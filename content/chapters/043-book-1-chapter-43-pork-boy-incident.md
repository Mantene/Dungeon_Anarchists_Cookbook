# Book 1 Chapter 43: The Pork Boy Incident

## Summary
During a commercial break, [[the-maestro]] threatens Carl, boasting that his family—the **Skull Empire** (Skull Clan)—is a major Syndicate power and has won six of the last ten Faction Wars on the ninth floor. He scoffs at [[princess-donut]]'s tiara, revealing she is locked into the **Blood Sultanate** line of succession on Floor 9, a faction that is almost always eliminated first. He states he will be the Skull Clan's War Leader on Floor 9.

The Maestro brings out surprise guests: [[frank-q]] and [[maggie-my]]. A recap video reveals their true backstory:
- They traveled with Maggie's teenage daughter, Yvette. Their tutorial guide (a Mind Horror) told them they had to kill other crawlers to survive.
- They ambushed [[rebecca-w]]'s group, murdering them. Yvette refused to participate.
- Later, Yvette was injured by a Bad Llama's lava. Believing she was dying, Maggie choked her own daughter to death to claim her experience points.
- The video reveals that Yvette's initial severe injuries were caused not by a llama, but by the dynamite trap Carl left in the rat corpse. Frank lost his right hand in the blast.

Maggie and Frank appear on set. Frank is silent, missing his right hand (cut cleanly like a mannequin). Maggie is furious. Carl confronts Maggie for murdering her own daughter. Maggie lunges at Carl with a glowing purple dagger, but it passes harmlessly through the hologram. 

The Maestro presents parting gifts:
1. Carl receives a pair of **Bates zip-up tactical boots and socks**—a troll gift designed to ruin his barefoot buffs. Carl plays it cool, pretending to love them.
2. Frank and Maggie receive a **Legendary Skill Potion** that upgrades Frank's *Find Crawler* skill to Level 15, allowing them to track Carl and Donut by name anywhere in the dungeon.

Carl challenges the Maestro to single combat on the ninth floor, earning a massive reaction from the crowd. The show ends, and Carl's views explode due to a viral, explicit fan-edit video called the "Pork Boy Snick," which humiliates the Maestro.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[threat-of-frank-q]]**: The PK duo's history is exposed. Equipped with the tracking potion, they pose a constant, active threat to Carl and Donut.
- **[[blood-sultanate-succession]]**: The Maestro confirms that Donut's tiara makes her a target on the ninth floor, locked into a Faction War against his Skull Clan.
- **[[televised-exploitation]]**: The release of the "Pork Boy Snick" viral video demonstrates how intergalactic audiences manipulate and consume the crawlers' broadcast feeds.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Maggie's actions are horrifying; she choked her own daughter for experience points, yet blames Carl for leaving the trap. The revelation that Carl's trap indirectly led to Yvette's death adds a layer of moral complexity to Carl's actions. The Maestro's gift of the tracking potion elevates the PK duo from local floor killers to a persistent, inescapable threat.
