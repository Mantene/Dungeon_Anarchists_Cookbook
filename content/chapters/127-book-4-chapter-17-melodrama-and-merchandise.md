# Book 4 Chapter 17: Melodrama and Merchandise

## Summary
Carl loots the suburban home, bids a forced farewell to Zev, and makes a crucial decision about his approach to the producers.
- **Managing the House**: Carl learns that the house's floating balloon is powered by an F-Quality *Soul Crystal* housed in a clamshell device. He adjusts it to keep the house anchored. Carl systematically loots the suburban home for a massive haul of earth items (tools, laptops, TVs, decorations, and even the electrical panel).
- **Decoy Plane**: Louis and Firas retrieve the salvaged Drop Bear biplane *Nightmare II* and park it in the house's garage.
- **Farewell to Zev**: Admin Loita and Zev message the party. Because Carl bought the stream comment board upgrade, Zev is officially removed as their social media manager and restricted to behind-the-scenes work. Loita threatens to feed Mongo to a mob if Donut talks back again. Donut is heartbroken she couldn't say a proper goodbye.
- **Veriluxx Insights**: Mordecai reveals that the toy company Veriluxx is connected to Veritan Linkage, a Syndicate Soother lending fund that advocates for the enslavement of debtors. The toy's back panel has a self-destruct warning, and it moans sexually when Carl pats its head, highlighting the shoddy and corrupt corporate design.
- **Watch Signaling**: Carl tests Kane's *Mysterious Pocket Watch*. Setting its alarm tab causes it to vibrate, signaling the first watch. In the watch's mirror, Carl sees Henrik, who has transformed into a fish-like creature to survive the flooded Necropolis.
- **Bonnie's Grief**: The calming potion Bonnie was given wears off, and she begins to weep for her father. Mongo returns her stuffed pink rabbit to comfort her, and Juice Box promises that Hump Town will care for her. Loita interrupts, mocking the "melodrama" and announcing their show starts in 20 minutes. Carl resolves to execute a highly risky, secret plan.

## Characters Introduced
- None (Bonnie's backstory is expanded).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- [[the-gate-of-the-feral-gods]]: Carl uses the *Mysterious Pocket Watch* to confirm Henrik is still alive in the flooded Necropolis.
- [[systemic-oppression]]: The corporate background of Veriluxx and the cruel treatment of Zev and the crawlers by Loita deepen the theme of systemic exploitation.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The contrast between Mongo's fierce combat role and his gentle comfort of Bonnie shows the deep emotional intelligence of the pet dinosaur. Zev's forced departure is a emotional blow to Donut, but Carl's response shows he is no longer just playing to survive—he is planning a direct rebellion.
