# Chapter 438: Chapter 39 — The Green Needle Karaoke

## Summary
- **Muting Akuma**: The group re-mutes Akuma and leaves him with 2% health in the C&W&U upgrades room after an imp walks in. Carl fist-bumps Akuma to get him into their chat, which works as long as they are on the same floor.
- **Sodas and Shakes**: Louis drinks his strawberry milkshake, which causes his gills to blow out red, strawberry-scented water. Prepotente brings his 200 sodas to the Lollipop karaoke bar but is kicked out by the bartender for drinking outside beverages, but only after screaming at the bartender.
- **Secret Guild Entrance**: Tipid explains that the stage performance guild is hidden in the Lollipop karaoke bar. The song selection binder has secret marks on specific songs. If a crawler sings one of these songs and keeps the magical gauge in the green (like Guitar Hero), they gain entrance to the guild. Only those who achieve the green needle are allowed in.
- **Tipid's Song**: Tipid sings "Love Shack" by the B-52s to demonstrate, but he cannot sing, and his needle stays in the red.
- **Celine Dion Debates**: Prepotente displays his intelligence by reciting detailed Celine Dion trivia, noting her first English album *Unison* debuted in 1990, meaning she isn't in the 80s-themed binder.
- **Fleshmancer Hired**: Imani, Chris, and Elle return to the bar with a newly hired mercenary fleshmancer. They keep him hidden in their guildhall. Carl asks Mordecai to brew bard voice-control potions to help them sing.
