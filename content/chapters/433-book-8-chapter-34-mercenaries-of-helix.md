# Chapter 433: Chapter 34 — The Mercenaries of Helix

## Summary
- **Complexions and Buffs**: Carl's kangaroo costume has timed out, leaving his skin exfoliated. Jamal brushes Carl and Donut to reset their daily buffs.
- **Dressing Room Renovation**: Samantha reveals she has redecorated Carl's room in the safe room with "badass wallpaper." A sluggalo notes it's pretty sick.
- **Team Helix**: Rosetta and Tipid present three new mercenaries: **Team Helix**, a veteran three-for-one squad who raced in two heats:
  - **Olga**: Level 75 female Grulke toad/frog (can drive).
  - **Finley**: Level 81 Porsuk badger, whose claws glow green with poison enchantments (can drive).
  - **Quemada**: Level 96 flaming fire fairy (provides protection).
- **Samantha's Seduction**: Samantha rolls up to Finley, wetly sucking on his ankle and inviting him to "show her magical things with his claws" in her room, leaving Finley thoroughly bewildered.
- **Triple Price Demand**: Quemada spots the Emberus shrine in Carl's room and reveals she is a fellow worshipper. She states that Emberus told her in a vision to help Carl slay his brother Hellik. Knowing this is a martyrdom mission, she demands triple the negotiated price so Olga and Finley will be financially secure if she dies. Carl agrees to the price.
