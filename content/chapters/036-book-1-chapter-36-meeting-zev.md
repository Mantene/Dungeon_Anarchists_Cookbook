# Book 1 Chapter 36: Meeting Zev

## Summary
[[zev]] stands about two feet tall, wearing a water-filled glass bubble helmet and a bulky space suit. As a Borant Assistant Communications Representative, she acts as Carl and Donut's newly assigned PR Agent under the **Crawler Assisted Outreach Program**. She manages their interview requests and schedules a round-table appearance in 40 hours.

[[carl]] loses his temper, shouting that they are being treated as ratings playthings while fighting for survival. Zev reacts with surprising honesty: she explains that she is a low-level pawn, and if she fails her duties, her entire family will be wiped out by her superiors. 

[[princess-donut]] breaks the tension by comparing the argument to a scene from *Gossip Girl*. Zev and Donut immediately bond over their mutual love for Earth shows like *Gossip Girl* and *Riverdale*.

While Zev and Donut are talking, Carl whispers to [[mordecai]] to ask about "the Party" Zev mentioned. Mordecai explains that the ruling Kua-tin party is **The Bloom** (an ultra-nationalist, fascist-like government). The Bloom has bankrupt the Kua-tin system and is forcing Borant to accelerate the crawl to cash out early, resulting in lethal bugs and rule changes designed to kill crawlers.

Zev vanishes with a splash of water. When Carl opens the guildhall door to return to the dungeon, a waist-high pile of Brindle Grubs spills into the room.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Zev's character adds depth to the corporate antagonists. She is not a malicious executioner, but a terrified low-level employee under a brutal fascist regime (the Bloom). The contrast between the horrific intergalactic stakes and Donut discussing *Gossip Girl* fan-fiction with an alien fish highlights the absurd humor that keeps the crawlers sane.
