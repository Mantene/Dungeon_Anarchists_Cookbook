# Chapter 467: The Garden & The AI's Gift

## Summary
[[carl]]'s truck limps into the garage as the crawlers mourn the death of [[dong-quixote]]. Chat updates reveal that [[louis-santiago-2]]'s team finished last in their heat but survived, hiring an "engineer lady" mercenary who is an upgrade thief. [[britney-proskurina]] ripped the head off a skeleton-astronaut with her bare hands. [[florin]]'s team came in first. Out of 60 teams in Carl's heat, only 18 survived. Dwight the unicorn won the heat by driving over the outside of the factory building.

Dr. Metcalf provides details on Heats 6 & 7, which will run consecutively in the shrunken interior of the 7-floor L'Engle Building. Racers will be shrunk to Lego-size.

Carl receives the quest completion for "Half a prayer, half a song." The AI (glitching and lamenting about losing control) grants him a reward: he can choose two creatures or people within the AI's sphere of influence to bring to the 11th floor arena, to be selected inside the arena itself. The AI warns Carl about Eris, the war mages, and warns: *"Beware exits that seem too good to be true."*

[[mordecai]] messages that a single flower has bloomed on [[li-na]]'s cactus, signaling she arrived safely in the [[pineapple-cabaret|Pineapple Cabaret]] and the escape route is safe.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The AI's message is particularly revealing: it admits to "losing control" of the dungeon and warns Carl about the war mages and Eris. The warning "Beware exits that seem too good to be true" casts a dark shadow over the Pineapple Cabaret plan, suggesting it may not be the clean escape the crawlers hope for.
