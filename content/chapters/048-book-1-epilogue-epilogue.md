# Book 1 Epilogue: Epilogue (Book 1)

## Summary
Between floors, [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] appear on [[odette]]'s show. [[mongo]] behaves mostly by perching on Donut's back. Odette mentions that Carl should buy a **magical pet carrier** (the dungeon's equivalent of a pokeball) to store Mongo safely in inventory.

Odette reveals that the show *Death Watch Extreme* has gone on hiatus due to the "Pork Boy Snick" controversy. King Rust of the **Skull Empire** has demanded people stop calling [[the-maestro]] "Carl's Naughty Little Piggie," but the public is ignoring the demand. Carl repeats his challenge to single combat with the prince on air.

After the segment, Odette talks privately with them. Carl delivers Mordecai's hostile message. Odette sadly admits she pushed Mordecai too hard in his own crawl, costing him his brother.

Odette gives Carl advice for the upcoming Floor 3 class selection:
1. Let Donut choose her class first so Carl can tailor his selection to match hers.
2. Ignore the AI's race recommendations, but pay close attention to its class recommendations.
3. Look for a pathfinder or mapping class to help navigate future floors.

Carl asks about the **Valtay Corporation**. Odette warns him to be extremely cautious. She explains that the Valtay is a parasitic, technologically advanced corporate system government poised to take over the crawl from the bankrupt Kua-tin. The next season of the crawl will be run by them, featuring androids and pulse rifles instead of fantasy monsters.

Finally, Odette translates Mongo's breed name, *Mongoliensis*, revealing that Donut's pet is a **velociraptor**. She leaves Donut with a cryptic recommendation: look for a hidden class choice available only to high-charisma crawlers, warning that picking it "will make someone very angry."

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The revelation that Mongo is a velociraptor is a brilliant comedic and tactical shift—Donut's cute pink "chicken" is actually a prehistoric apex predator. Odette's warning about the Valtay Corporation confirms that the dungeon's setting and aesthetic are completely subject to the corporate sponsor running it, moving from fantasy to technological sci-fi in the next season.
