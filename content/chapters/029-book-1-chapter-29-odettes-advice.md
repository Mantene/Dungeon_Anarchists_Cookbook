# Book 1 Chapter 29: Odette's Advice

## Summary
The cameras stop, the audience disappears, and the set reverts to the boat. [[odette]] removes her mantis helmet, revealing a human-like woman around 60 years old. She detaches her torso from the crab body, revealing that she is legless and rides in a hovering disc wheelchair. Odette complains about [[lucia-mar]]'s dogs mauling her producer and talks off-camera with [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]]. 

She asks Carl to tell [[mordecai]] to seek her out when the crawl ends, revealing that she was Mordecai's trainer during his own crawl. She warns them never to say "mud skippers" (a derogatory term for the Kua-tin/Borant corporation) in the dungeon. 

Odette gives crucial advice for the crawl:
- **Donut must stay a cat**: The audience loves Donut's cat form. If she changes her race on Floor 3, her ratings and potential sponsorships will collapse.
- **Loot boxes are not random**: The system AI tailors loot boxes to force crawlers into entertaining situations or save them when their death would hurt ratings. (For example, Odette was given her legendary attachment belt when she lost her legs on Floor 11 so she would return to the fight).
- **Mordecai's contract**: Mordecai's indentureship contract only counts this season if his crawlers reach Floor 4. If they fail, he remains trapped for another 7 or 8 seasons.

Odette admits she spiked Donut's treats with a temporary wisdom and confidence boost to make her a better interviewee, and states that her own motivation for helping them is purely financial.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[systemic-oppression]]**: Odette's explanations expose the corporate reality of the crawl: loot boxes are rigged for ratings, trainers have high-stakes indentureship contracts, and the system uses drugs to manipulate crawlers for interviews.
- **[[survival-of-the-collapse]]**: Odette's advice to keep Donut a cat and to trust Mordecai's training is vital for their survival past Floor 3.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Odette's off-camera persona is pragmatic and transactional, contrasting with her bizarre on-camera appearance. Her revelation that the loot boxes are rigged by the AI to maximize entertainment and survival ratings confirms Carl's suspicions. The detail that Mordecai needs them to reach Floor 4 to free himself adds a personal stake to their partnership, explaining his dedication.
