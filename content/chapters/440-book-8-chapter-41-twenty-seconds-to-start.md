# Chapter 440: Chapter 41 — Twenty Seconds to Start

## Summary
As the race to the Upano pit stop approaches, Dr. Metcalf (the truck's GPS system) outlines various routes. [[carl]] selects a route that bypasses boss monsters: starting on the direct path, crossing the flight-capable piranha river, looping around the southern cannibal village, and taking a dirt road into Upano. 

Outside, acid rain begins to fall, prompting [[olga]] to activate the vehicle's new shield. Carl worries about [[corcunda]] (Corky) on Team Lady Dominators, recognizing him as a key figure in his plans. 

Carl sends a message to [[osvaldo]] to ask about the memorial crystals. Osvaldo reveals he had two crystals: one for Tupa and one for [[ysalte]] (the Vinegar Bitch), but both quests were corrupted. Osvaldo gave one to [[prepotente]] but was yelled at. Osvaldo expresses exhaustion and despair, noting that everyone associated with Carl (like Paz, Anton, and Sister Ines) dies. 

Meanwhile, the fire fairy [[quemada]] suffers a severe panic attack in the cab, sobbing and questioning what is real. The starting light turns yellow, with twenty seconds to the start of the race.
