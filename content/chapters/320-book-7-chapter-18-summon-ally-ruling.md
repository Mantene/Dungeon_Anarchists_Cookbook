# Chapter 320: Summon Ally Ruling

## Summary

The three faction warlords—[[carl]], [[stockade]] of the Sortion, and [[architect-houston]] of the Madness—gather with their respective adjutants ([[baroness-victory]], [[panford]], and [[opiee]]) in Queen Imogen’s private kitchen. They convene for an official field ruling adjudication regarding the incident in the ballroom. 

Panford projects screens displaying the mechanics of the failed sabotage. Stockade had offered peace terms and issued a **Summon Ally** (Level 15) teleportation request to Carl right before Carl drank the sleep potion. When Imani removed Carl's toe ring, the sleeping Carl's interface accepted the parlay. The system, compensating for Carl's unconscious state, partially teleported him as a translucent, non-corporeal avatar. 

However, because Carl and Katia touched hands, their consciousnesses merged. When [[shi-maria]] was pulled into Katia’s dreamscape, the entities [[hapanzi]] and Shi Maria clashed, allowing Hapanzi to trap her. When Stockade attempted to fully teleport Carl, the AI ruled the backfired possession as an AI action, and the temporarily possessed Carl slaughtered fifty goblins. During the hearing, Stockade inadvertently admits his guilt, leading Panford to formally sanction the Lemig Sortion and the Madness.

The adjudication is cut short by a series of urgent chat messages. [[prepotente]] warns that the elves' trebuchets and 100 invisible Madness mercenaries are preparing to attack the southern border. [[elle-mcgibbons]] and [[florin]] report massive enemy movements and tanks shedding camouflage netting on the northern/Naga fronts. Meanwhile, [[lucia-mar]] is spotted drinking at the "Possum Envy" pub near the Reavers' border. Carl issues a "Code Red" and orders his forces to their lines. Open hostilities are about to begin 20 hours ahead of schedule.

## Themes & Plot Threads

- **Summon Ally Exploit** — The technical loophole of utilizing persistent warlord parlay prompts to teleport unconscious targets.
- **Dream Surveillance** — Adjutants and system managers can monitor and record crawler dreams and memories.
- **Early Escalation** — The ceasefire is effectively broken 20 hours early as enemy factions prepare to launch surprise pre-emptive strikes.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections

This chapter is a masterclass in dungeon mechanics. The details of the *Summon Ally* exploit show how deeply the factions study the interface rules to look for legal loopholes. The sudden transition from bureaucratic adjudication to a desperate scramble for battle lines heightens the tension. The presence of the "Meow Time" Seattle cat food bag adds a surreal, haunting reminder of the outside world, contrasting the corporate warfare with domestic mundanity.
