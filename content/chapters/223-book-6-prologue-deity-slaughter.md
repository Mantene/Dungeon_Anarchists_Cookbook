# Book 6 Prologue: Deity Slaughter

## Summary
The prologue details the tragic tenth-floor climax of [[mordecai]]'s original crawler party hundreds of cycles ago, revealing the backstory of [[uzzi]]'s sacrifice and the origin of Mordecai's hatred for [[odette]] and [[chaco]].
- **The Deity Summons**: Imminent level collapse threatens the party. Odette summons two storm deities: the AI-controlled [[adad]] and the sponsored [[dodola]], who is controlled by Grixist heiress [[huanxin-jinx]]. The exit puzzle disc is ready but must be struck by storm magic within the stairwell circle, requiring [[hold-steady]] to keep it primed.
- **Huanxin's Extortion**: Huanxin demands a 50% cut of Odette's tenth-floor bonus under threat of zeroing the party. Odette refuses. Meanwhile, Mordecai, grounded far from the party in ratkin form, prepares a suicidal attempt to target the puzzle using his *Final Blow* skill.
- **Uzzi's Sacrifice**: To save Mordecai (and her own citizen bonus), Odette instructs Chaco to use the [[dart-of-ophiotaurus]]. Chaco complies, worshipping Dodola and throwing the dart into Uzzi's breast. This forced-summons Dodola inside a *Circle of Containment* shield, instantly killing Uzzi.
- **Dodola's Defeat**: Dodola materializes in the containment shell, teleports Adad away, and attempts to cast a level-20 lightning spell. Because deity immunity is delayed for five units after summoning, Chaco's containment causes the lightning spell to reflect and explode, killing Dodola. The key mote opens the eleventh-floor stairwell. Chaco jumps ten levels, but Mordecai is devastated by his brother's murder and vows to hunt down and kill Odette. Armita is left terrified of Huanxin's retaliation.

## Characters Introduced
- [[uzzi]] — Skyfowl warrior and Storm Commander crawler; Mordecai's brother (deceased).
- [[hold-steady]] — Skyfowl crawler who primed the exit puzzle.
- [[armita]] — Former crawler, Odette's friend, and demigod attendant who acted as the go-between.
- [[huanxin-jinx]] — Wealthy Grixist heiress and Celestial Ascendency sponsor.
- [[dodola]] — Sponsored storm goddess driven by Huanxin Jinx (destroyed).
- [[adad]] — AI-controlled storm deity (teleported away).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[systemic-oppression]]**: The Ascendency sponsors treat crawler lives as currency, extorting them during critical moments.
- **Mordecai's Vengeance**: The catalyst for Mordecai's deep hatred of Odette and Chaco is established.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Odette's choice to sacrifice Uzzi rather than accept Huanxin's terms or let Mordecai die highlights her desperation to escape indentureship. She prioritized her own freedom and citizenship over Uzzi's life, showing the moral degradation forced on managers and crawlers by the system.
