# Book 5 Chapter 28: Big Tina

## Summary
- **Premature Questjaculation**: Carl receives an achievement of the same name (no reward) because he killed Claude before the system could assign the quest.
- **Clint and Nadine Dead**: Holger mourns the deaths of [[clint-smashgrab]] (who was killed on the other shore) and [[nadine]]. 
- **Big Tina footprint**: Samantha reports that the Ursine guards on the other shore were wiped out by a giant three-toed dinosaur footprint with pink feathers around it.
- **Big Tina identified**: Areson identifies the dinosaur as [[big-tina]] (a young dinosaur with a pink feather boa, an empty wand, and an anger problem who hates Ursine). She is Kiwi's pack member who ran away.
- **Quest Update**: [[the-recital]] quest is updated: Carl must kill Big Tina or resolve her issues.
- **Vengeance Part One Complete**: Signet returns, noting they easily took the castle and the Confederacy is defeated. **Part One** of the Vengeance quest completes. (Langley's team died because they left the southern shore unprotected).
- **Vengeance Part Two**: **Part Two of Two** is triggered: Carl and Donut must help Signet assassinate Queen [[imogen]] of the High Elves at the yearly High Elf party ([[the-butchers-masquerade]]). Reward: Looting the High Elf Castle and an item protecting against Scolopendra.
- **Tortoise Ritual**: Edgar the tortoise prepares a ritual under the moonlight to memorialize the dead.

## Characters Introduced
- [[big-tina]] — Young dinosaur with a pink feather boa, focus of The Recital quest.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[the-recital]]**: The quest is updated to deal with Big Tina.
- **[[vengeance-of-the-daughter]]**: Part One completes, and Part Two is triggered to assassinate Queen Imogen.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The completion of Part One comes at a high cost: Clint, Nadine, and Langley's team are dead, and the southern shore guards were slaughtered by Big Tina. This highlights that "ignoring the other shore" has lethal consequences in the simulation. Part Two ties directly into the main plot thread, setting Queen Imogen as the ultimate target at the Butcher's Masquerade, which is a setup to "zero out" the top crawlers.
