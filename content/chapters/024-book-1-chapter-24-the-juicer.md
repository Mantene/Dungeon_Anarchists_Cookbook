# Book 1 Chapter 24: The Juicer

## Summary
[[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] enter the gym and are locked in for the boss fight against **[[the-juicer]]**, a Level 9 Neighborhood Boss. The Juicer is a troglodyte bodybuilder caricature who throws exploding, flaming weight plates. Carl reads a scroll of *Confusing Fog* to blind the enemies. However, because of Carl's low Intelligence (3), the fog only lasts 15 seconds. 

While the fog is active, Carl eliminates the six Level 3 Bashers. He is wounded by shrapnel from a flaming plate, which he heals. Donut fights the Juicer and the three Virtuosos, dodging their venomous tongue attacks. Carl kills the Virtuosos, using one of their heads as a makeshift glove after it bites down on his wrist. 

Carl attacks the Juicer with a 250-pound barbell and a 100-pound weight plate. The Juicer survives and grabs Carl by the throat, choking him to near-death. Donut pounces onto the Juicer's neck, tearing open a high-pressure vein with her teeth and claws. The Juicer slaps Donut away, sending her crashing into the wall. Carl delivers the killing blow to the Juicer's head. He rushes to Donut, who has a broken leg and a tiny sliver of health left. Donut dramatically acts out her "death scene," naming her past cat-love [[ferdinand]], but recovers after taking a healing potion.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival]]**: Carl and Donut rely on desperate teamwork and immediate healing to survive a near-fatal boss encounter.
- **[[carls-class-selection]]**: Carl utilizes the environment and heavy weights, showing how his physical Strength can be used creatively. Donut's melee bite/claw attack is devastating but exposes her low constitution and physical fragility.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The Juicer is another grotesque representation of human culture (bodybuilding, steroids, Axe body spray) twisted into a monster. The combat highlights a major mechanical rule: scroll duration scales with the reader's Intelligence. Donut's dramatic death scene provides a lighthearted ending to a fight that came within seconds of killing both of them.
