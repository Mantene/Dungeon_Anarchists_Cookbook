# Book 1 Chapter 20: Naughty List

## Summary
[[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] wake up to the recap show. Donut discovers [[frank-q]] hiding in the women's bathroom. Carl confronts Frank, who claims to be a federal agent (ICE) who killed [[rebecca-w]] and her associates in self-defense. However, Carl notices details that expose Frank's lies: Rebecca had a tutorial guild item in her inventory (meaning she was killed later, not immediately during the collapse), and Frank's daily cookie experience shows he is partied with a living crawler. 

Using party chat, Carl warns Donut not to fight back when the attack comes. Frank and his stealth partner, [[maggie-my]], ambush them. The moment Frank shoots at Donut and Maggie fires at Carl, the safe room's anti-violence rules trigger. Both attackers are frozen in place with a blazing **Naughty** tag over their heads (Strike 1: 100 seconds freeze). 

Carl and Donut quickly examine Maggie My (Level 9, with 5 crawler kills). Carl decides to set a trap: he kills a hallway rat, places a ticking goblin dynamite stick inside its corpse inventory, and leaves it. If Frank or Maggie attempt to loot the rat while pursuing them, it will explode in their hands. They start the Copper Chopper and drive away as the daily system announcement plays, detailing fixes to exploding toilets, the removal of the overpowered "Blender Fiend" mobs, and the warning that crawlers who descend to Floor 2 early will miss television coverage.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[threat-of-frank-q]]**: Carl and Donut encounter and survive an ambush by Frank Q and Maggie My, learning that they are systemic crawler killers.
- **[[survival]]**: Carl turns the safe room's strict anti-violence rules against his attackers, escaping a fatal ambush.
- **[[systemic-oppression]]**: The Borant system announcements show the corporate nature of the crawl, adjusting mob difficulty (like the Blender Fiends who blended 15,000 crawlers in a single day) to maintain viewer entertainment.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's analytical mind shines here: he spots the inconsistencies in Frank's story and uses the safe zone's strict game mechanics to neutralize a superior, armed force. Donut's emotional turnaround from thinking "Frank is an American hero" to screaming in panic shows her vulnerability and trust in Carl's strategic thinking.
