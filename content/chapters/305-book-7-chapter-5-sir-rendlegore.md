# Chapter 305: Chapter 5 — Sir Rendlegore

## Summary
- **The Meatball Pet**: Carl releases his unbonded *Used Pet*, a Level-1 Tummy Acher. The creature is a tiny, giggly meatball with a Linus-like patch of hair and a single tooth underbite.
- **Mongo's Indigestion**: Mongo attempts to eat the pet, swallowing it whole, but is forced to yak it back up due to the creature's giggly, suicidal nature.
- **Tummy Acher Mechanics**: Tummy Achers (originally parasites in Jotan-class Titans) have high defense and a *Wrecking Ball* attack. This particular pet has died 5 times, giving it a 125% stat buff but leaving it with 5 random weaknesses. It reverts to level 1 and loses memories every 5 deaths. It will permanently die if it dies before reaching level 5.
- **Bonding and Naming**: Carl bonds with the pet by feeding it seafood chowder. Louis suggests the name **Sir Rendlegore** to honor the late [[ren]] and fit the army's royal theme.
- **Hair and Officers**: Carl's hair is cut but immediately grows back to waist length due to the tonic. Florin configures the army officer hierarchy (Generals, Sergeants, Adjutants). Fast-travel doggie doors are installed.
- **Samantha and Tish**: Samantha continues to rant about her arch-enemy [[tish]] (Ghazi's ex-girlfriend), threatening to expose Louis's alleged affair with Tish to [[juice-box]] if she isn't allowed to blow Tish up.
