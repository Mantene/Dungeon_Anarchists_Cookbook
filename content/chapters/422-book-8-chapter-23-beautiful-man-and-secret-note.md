# Chapter 422: Chapter 23 — The Beautiful Man and the Secret Note

## Summary
- **Dong's Quest**: Dong learns that Corcunda is drugged and locked in the Lady Dominators' garage next door. He rushes out to save him, followed by Bucket Boy, Splash Zone, Donut, Samantha, and Bigs.
- **Wii Mario Kart**: In the driveway, Jasha and Radoslav watch the commotion while sharing beers. Bigs drinks beer poured on the ground.
- **Zev's Loudspeaker Announcement**: Zev, reading a script dictated by the AI, explains the "special guest creatures" rule. If crawlers choose "vote" after defeating them, the audience can vote on their fate (e.g. sending them to the 11th floor or sending a stand-in).
- **The Eleventh Floor**: Zev reveals that Floor 11 is named **A Parade of Horribles**, where all surviving crawlers will work together. The AI notes it just teleported a Valtay suicide bomb elsewhere.
- **Cascadia's Torture**: During the broadcast, Cascadia refuses to say her catchphrase and is audibly tortured, losing several flippers.
- **Akuma's Secret Note**: A gremlin hands Carl a secret note written on a C&W&U invoice. The note is from **Akuma** (leader of the War Mage Rebellion from Faction Wars). The note tells Carl not to tell anyone, to kill two teams next heat, and to visit him after the race by asking for "top-shelf upgrades" to be led to the back. It hints that Akuma can smuggle friends to the **Pineapple Cabaret**.

The AI's grip on Zev and its horrific treatment of Cascadia illustrate its growing, unchecked power. Akuma's appearance and the mention of the Pineapple Cabaret introduce a potential exit strategy for the crawlers, though it requires Carl to commit PKs during the next race.
