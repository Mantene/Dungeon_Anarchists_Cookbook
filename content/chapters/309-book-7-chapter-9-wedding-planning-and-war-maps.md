# Chapter 309: Chapter 9 — Wedding Planning and War Maps

## Summary
- **Juice Box Revealed**: Hunger Hammy is revealed to be [[juice-box]] in disguise. She reverts to her human form (Level 35). She is co-warlord of Team Retribution.
- **Shanty Town and its Warlord**: Juice Box shares maps, troop strengths, and details about *Shanty Town*, a massive slum ring that sprung up around Larracos's top entrance. Its former mayor, a gnoll named Theora, was assassinated in single combat. The current mayor is the cocker spaniel [[ferdinand]].
- **Drick the Adjutant**: They discuss [[drick]], a Valtay-worm-infected Dream elf who acts as Team Retribution's non-combatant adjutant. He has permanent invulnerability and is treated as a ghost.
- **Warlord interface**: Juice Box does not have a full warlord menu, but can receive voice messages and notices.
- **The Small Wedding**: Juice Box insists on a quick "small wedding" with Louis to bond their houses. Louis, though terrified and messaging Carl in panic, agrees. Juice Box shifts into a blonde pop star cheerleader form and kisses him.
- **Dwarven Diggers**: Britney spots plans for dwarven automatons (diggers) on the desk, hinting at the NPCs' defense capabilities.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The political alliance between Louis and Juice Box takes the form of a literal marriage of houses, mirroring medieval fantasy politics. Louis's willingness to go through with it despite his terror shows his dedication to the team's survival. The presence of Drick, a Valtay-infected elf who is untouchable, highlights the Syndicate's continued surveillance and interference.
