# Chapter 362: The Nest and the Hairbrush

## Summary
Two hours remain until Phase Three: Open Hostilities. The Princess Posse stands ready with "Operation Ruin," defending three fronts: Florin's crocodiles in the north, Tran's bulwark in the south, and Li Na's white dragons in Shanty Town (supported by Juice Box's Semeru dwarves).

In Shanty Town, the final "synergy tower," named **the Nest**, is completed. Toyotomi, a rhino former-Prism-guard, oversees its construction from master-quality granite blocks. The Nest will allow [[princess-donut]] to use her sniper skills across the battlefield with direct line of sight, while the tower itself synergizes with Atrocity-class spells. Defensive mages apply a lattice of protections, backed by four deflector towers.

[[katia-grim]] plans to consume the **Orchid of Eileithyia's Grace** immediately after the battle, which will send her into a coma. Under her deal with [[huanxin-jinx]], she is supposed to be teleported to the 12th floor to become a celestial attendant, fulfilling the conditions of the Sepsis Whore deal and freeing Donut to descend. If the deal fails, Katia will face pregnancy or a massive constitutional enhancement, which would prevent either Katia or Donut from descending as long as the other is alive.

Carl and Katia undergo a live broadcast interview hosted by Baroness Victory, Drick, and Kevin. The interview highlights the celestial-tier items they received in Club Vanquisher:
1. **The Enchanted Spiked Knee pads of the Munificent Goddess Kina** (awarded to Katia): A unique divine item providing damage reflection, water breathing, level 15 swimming, level 10 Iron Stomach (allows consumption of magic/cursed items to increase intelligence), and level 10 Eater of the Dead.
2. **The Enchanted Hairbrush of the Beefmaster** (awarded to Carl): Requires long hair (25cm+) and a male user. Brushing for 5 minutes grants Charisma, Speedster (rapid short-distance movement), +25% Constitution, and level 10 Entourage, but the brush screams like a rock concert crowd and yells "Encore!" for two minutes.

As the countdown ticks closer to the offensive, Carl is gripped by intense anxiety over the impending battle, questioning what gives him the right to lead so many to their potential deaths.

## Themes & Plot Threads
* **The Burden of Leadership**: Carl struggles with the ethical implications of commanding an army of former crawlers and NPCs, knowing many will die in the offensive.
* **Administrative Safeguards**: The celestial interview showcases the corporations' attempts to avoid financial liabilities related to the AI's recent loot handouts.
* **Sepsis Whore Loophole**: Katia's planned ascension to become a celestial attendant is the group's gamble to save both Katia and Donut.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The introduction of the Beefmaster Hairbrush is a classic example of the system's absurd humor, granting Carl incredible benefits (particularly Speedster) at the cost of public embarrassment. Meanwhile, the looming choice regarding Katia's Orchid represents the tragic, zero-sum nature of the dungeon's rules, casting a shadow over the upcoming offensive.
