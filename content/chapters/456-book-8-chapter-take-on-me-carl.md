# Chapter 456: Chapter 56 — Take on Me: Carl's Debut and Dwight's Attack

## Summary
The chapter opens with Elle, Zhang, and Tran departing to return to the safe room, intending to communicate a new plan to [[na]]. Meanwhile, [[princess-donut]] has acquired the [[fleshmancer]] and the soother alien, [[linus]], as mercenaries. Linus immediately begins making inappropriate comments and gestures towards Donut, leading [[elle-mcgibbons]] to force him to accompany her group to keep him away.

[[carl]] and Donut prepare for their guild entrance song performance, with Carl feeling nervous despite [[mordecai]] having brewed special compounded potions to ensure they are in sync and bypass potion cooldowns. Carl takes "Honeyed" for better singing, "Yo-Yo Ma'd" for perfect pitch, and "Dio'd" to hold notes longer. Donut reveals they will perform "Take on Me" by A-ha, a song requiring two singers, with Carl taking the lead. Donut plans to use Auto-Tune and double microphones, while Carl activates a "Gelded Choirboy" scroll at the last second to hit the higher notes, which makes his voice squeaky.

Donut summons [[mongo]] into the room to watch Carl's debut, despite Carl's concern for [[hedy]] being left in the garage with [[jamal]]. [[imani-c]] expresses her excitement for Carl's performance. The [[gremlin-dj]] introduces Carl and Donut. The bar quickly fills with an audience, including [[chris-andrews-2]], [[florin]], [[bautista]], [[britney]], [[jurgen]], [[dong-quixote]], [[bucket-boy]], [[dekoki]], [[yuto]], and a [[bleak-congregation-skeleton]]. As the music begins and Carl prepares to sing with his helium-like voice, [[dwight]] the unicorn suddenly stabs the DJ in the face with his horn, ending the chapter in chaos.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
This chapter masterfully builds a sense of anticipation and lighthearted comedy, only to smash it with a brutal, unexpected act of violence. The humor derived from [[carl]]'s nervousness, [[princess-donut]]'s over-the-top theatricality, and the specific effects of the performance potions and scrolls (like the "Gelded Choirboy" making Carl's voice squeaky) provides a delightful respite from the usual dungeon horrors. It showcases [[mordecai]]'s ingenuity and Donut's meticulous planning, even for something as seemingly trivial as a musical performance.

The interlude of [[linus]]'s creepy behavior serves as a brief but pointed reminder of the darker elements lurking in the dungeon, and [[elle-mcgibbons]]'s decisive action to remove him highlights her protective nature. However, the true genius of the chapter lies in its ending. Just as the audience is engaged, the music starts, and Carl is about to overcome his stage fright, [[dwight]]'s sudden, gruesome attack on the [[gremlin-dj]] serves as a stark, shocking reminder that the dungeon is never truly safe, even during moments of celebration or performance. This abrupt shift from comedic triumph to shocking violence is a signature of Matt Dinniman's storytelling, perfectly setting up a tense cliffhanger.
