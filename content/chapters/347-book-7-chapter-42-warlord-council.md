# Chapter 347: Chapter 42 — Warlord Council

## Summary
[[carl|Carl]], [[princess-donut|Donut]], and [[ferdinand|Ferdinand]] enter the Warlord Council room. Juice Box joins virtually. The host [[chaco|Chaco]] explains that the co-host, Magnificent Troy, refused to descend to Earth. [[cascadia|Cascadia]] appears, looking sickly and stressed, and drinks from a tequila ball.

The warlords materialize: the newly crowned King [[prince-stalwart|Stalwart]] (wearing a golden crown), Warlord Fang (Reavers, appearing as a thin 20-year-old human), Architect Houston (Madness, white mask with a dent), Epitome Tagg (Dream, elf in bloody armor), and [[princess-vinata|Princess Vinata]] (Blood Sultanate, a massive, glossy-white naga with six arms and solid red eyes). 

Vinata sits coiled next to Carl, projecting a powerful, electric aura of authority. She introduces herself and offers her hand (which Carl's hand passes through). 

Donut and Ferdinand laugh hysterically at Stalwart wearing a crown. When Chaco tries to initiate a public vote on whether Stalwart looks stupid, Stalwart punches Chaco in the face, knocking him unconscious and causing him to disappear. The tense silence is broken by a rare system notification: `System Message: Warning: Scolopendra Stirs.` The warlords are shocked.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The introduction of Princess Vinata establishes her as a highly dangerous opponent. Her charisma and six-armed form project divine authority, showing that the Blood Sultanate is not a faction to be taken lightly. The punch-out of Chaco and the sudden Scolopendra warning add to the atmosphere of mounting chaos, signaling that the rules of the dungeon are rapidly dissolving.
