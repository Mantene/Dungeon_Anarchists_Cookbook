# Book 6 Chapter 2: Hills Cyclops and Nipple Ring

## Summary
Carl and Donut enter their saferoom to open their reward boxes and inspect their new abilities and gear.
- **Mordecai's New Form**: Carl and Donut meet Mordecai, who is now in his Floor 8 form: a Level 50 **Hills Cyclops** (a large, pink, bald, single-eyed conehead). He resides in the saferoom alongside Bomo, the last remaining rock bodyguard.
- **Achievements and Loot**: Carl opens a massive haul of high-tier boxes from his exploits on Floor 6:
  - *Oh shit! A country boss!* (Imogen).
  - *The Scourge* (Butcher's Masquerade champion) — grants a Legendary Hunter Champion Box.
  - *Marked for Life* (Surviving a god's death mark) — grants a Platinum Apostate Box.
  - *Apex Predator* (Killing all and the last hunter) — grants a Celestial Predator Box.
  - *Ménage à Quatro* (Four-way boss battle survival) — grants a Platinum Orgy Guy Box.
  - *Fuck This Floor* (Speed-running a floor in under an hour) — grants a Platinum Here's-Some-Good-Shit Box.
  - *Collective Bargaining* (Shared box payout) — grants a Gold Scab Box.
- **Key Items**:
  - Donut receives the *Enchanted Fur Brush of the Ecclesiastic* and a *Mercenary Coupon* (worth 100,000 gold).
  - Donut receives the [[enchanted-nipple-ring-hand-maiden]], which offers fire/lava immunities and the *Bitch, What?* spell.
  - Carl receives the [[tome-of-legionnaires-of-the-damned]] spellbook.

## Characters Introduced
- [[bomo]] — The sole surviving rock bodyguard from Floor 6 (previously introduced; first appearance in Book 6).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **Crawler Scaling**: Carl and Donut obtain game-breaking gear (Celestial and Divine tier) as rewards for surviving Floor 6's extreme challenges.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The achievements Carl unlocked highlight how chaotic the end of Floor 6 was. The rewards reflect this: the nipple ring and the tome are extremely high-tier tools that give them celestial-level powers, preparing them for the escalation of Floor 8 and beyond.
