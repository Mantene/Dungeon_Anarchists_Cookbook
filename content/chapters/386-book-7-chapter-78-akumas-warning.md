# Chapter 386: Chapter 78 — Akuma's Warning

## Summary
Carl, Donut, and the group prepare to rescue Louis from the Reaver castle. In the warlord chat, [[warlord-akuma|Warlord Akuma]] messages Carl. He explains that Agatha was forced upon his faction and is an enemy to everyone, warning that she has a method to escape to the 10th floor waiting room. He states the War Mages found the "Scavenger's Daughter" inside Larracos castle and took her with them. Akuma then leaves the realm, defeating the War Mage Rebellion. 

Carl tells [[justice-light]] that the [[the-gate-of-the-feral-gods]] is in the stomach of the merchant [[paca]] at the Midnight Market. Justice Light, knowing the location well, prepares to retrieve it with the sluggalos. 

Prepotente arrives astride Bianca and reports that Li Na is hunting in the woods. He gives Carl a tight, screaming hug that smells like root beer. Carl receives a quest update from Emberus: retrieve *Memorial Crystal: Apito* from Prepotente (who took it from Osvaldo). Carl, Donut, and their guards head toward the Reaver castle.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Akuma's warning about Agatha expands the lore, confirming she is a dangerous residual entity who is not aligned with any faction in the crawl. Prepotente's sudden affection (the root beer-scented hug) shows his eccentric way of expressing respect for Carl, even as the system forces Carl into a quest to steal Apito's crystal from him.
