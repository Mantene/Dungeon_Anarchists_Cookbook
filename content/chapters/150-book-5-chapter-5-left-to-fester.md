# Book 5 Chapter 5: Left to Fester

## Summary
Carl and Donut execute a high-risk preemptive raid on the sequestered hunters in Zockau, resulting in massive casualties and a narrow escape into the jungle.
- **The Teleportation & Setup**: Bomo casts *Teleport to Stairwell*, sending the invisible Carl and Donut to the central park of [[zockau]]. Carl disarms an alarm trap set by hunter [[ontario]] and places a Multi-Launcher box. Donut puddle-jumps them to the alleyways between the pubs, where Carl lays high-yield barrel bombs.
- **The Ambush**: Carl equips the [[ring-of-divine-suffering]], marking the drunk Draconian hunter [[chin-dua]] and spreading the *Left to Fester* debuff to the flying Mantis hunter [[xindy]] (Vrah's sister) using his toe ring's *Super Spreader* perk.
- **The Detonations**: Carl fires homing missiles that shoot down and kill Xindy. He then beats and crushes Chin'Dua to death to clear his debuff. Carl detonates the barrel bombs, destroying the facade of the Desperado Club and trapping the hunters inside.
- **The Escape**: Rushing back through the flaming town, Carl is targeted by the Level 70 Grand/Mayor of Zockau, taking crossbow bolts to his shoulder and thigh, which shatter his leg. They escape into the jungle boundary of [[zockau|The Selva]], where Carl collapses from blood loss and hallucinations.
- **The Rescue**: They are found in the jungle by [[miriam-dom]] (now a vampire) and [[prepotente]], who heal Carl and keep him safe from pursuers.

## Characters Introduced
- [[chin-dua]] — Level 50 Draconian hunter (Striver).
- [[xindy]] — Level 61 Mantis hunter of the Dark Hive, Vrah's sister.
- [[ontario]] — Hunter who laid alarm traps in Zockau (minor).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[the-butchers-masquerade]]**: The preemptive strike introduces the mechanics of hunting the hunters and collecting their severed appendages as trophies.
- **[[survival-of-the-collapse]]**: The crawlers actively target the intergalactic elites, demonstrating that they are no longer just surviving but fighting back.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's raid on Zockau is his most audacious act of asymmetrical warfare yet. By attacking the hunters while they are sequestered and partying, he completely disrupts their coordination, collapses their primary club hub, and demonstrates the lethal potential of combining the Ring of Divine Suffering with *Super Spreader*. However, the severe injuries he sustains from the city's Grand prove that the hunters possess devastating high-level capabilities once alerted.
