# Chapter 371: Bear Witness Ritual

## Summary
The 105th Scream Warriors teleport into the woods. A level 60 Fire Dryad named [[charmander]] complains about the trip. The rest of Carl's assault squad—[[jurgen]], [[jamal]] (equipped with Tserendolgor's modified flamethrower, "Velma"), [[daniel-bautista-2]], [[gluteus-maxx]], [[doctor-bones]], [[areson-the-wise|Areson]], [[chris-andrews-2|Chris]], and [[clay-ton]]—arrive individually. Carl summons Rend to his side using the *Hi-Yo Silver* mount tooth cap.

The Madness dune buggies halt a kilometer away, setting up shields. [[imani-c]] warns the troops to watch for mind magic. 

Carl sits against a tree and falls into a deep trance. The system warns him that his mind is being hammered with psionic debuffs (*Endless Thread of Distraction*, *Self Harm*, and *Intrusive Thoughts*). Under the influence, Carl begins to contemplate surrendering. 

In his mind, Carl hears a friendly, familiar male voice: *"You're family now, after what you did. I will help you. Look. Look at your feet. You've been standing in this so-called river this whole time."* Carl's **Mind Balance** skill hits level 16, unlocking the achievement **You now tread in the realm of gods!** and snapping him awake. Carl realizes the tree he was leaning on was an **All Tree root**, which now lies dead and withered. 

With his level 16 Mind Balance, Carl can see the invisible mind mages. He marks them for target focus, and the remaining missiles eliminate most of them.

Suddenly, a yellow radiance covers every soldier and NPC on the battlefield, rendering them invulnerable. Rosetta warns that the goddess [[eris]] has cast **Bear Witness**. All combatants outside of structures (including [[elle-mcgibbons]], [[imani-c]], [[katia-grim]], [[zhang]], [[florin]], and the 101st) freeze with white eyes and a back-to-back "C" symbol on their foreheads. Carl (protected by Mind Balance), the tattoos (such as [[dong-quixote]]), the sluggalos (such as [[kandy-newton]]), and pets/summons are unaffected.

Eris then casts **Bring Forth the Witnesses**, causing all frozen combatants to float into the sky and teleport toward her location near the Dream castle, disabling all long-range teleportation. Prepotente is swept away in the rapture. His goat-dragon mount, [[bianca]], screams in fury and flies off southeast to find him.

* [[jurgen]]: Teleports in to lead the Scream Warriors.

* [[gluteus-maxx]]: A dancer/crawler from the Penis Parade.
* [[imani-c]]: Warns the troops before being frozen and carried away.
* [[bianca]]: Prepotente's goat-dragon mount, who escapes the freeze and flies to Eris to rescue her master.
* [[kiwi]]: Distraught because her daughter Tina was frozen and swept away.
* [[princess-donut]]: Messages Carl from safety inside the Nest tower.

## Themes & Plot Threads
* **Mind Balance Breakthrough**: Carl's mental fortitude reaches a god-like level (16), allowing him to resist high-tier mental magic and perceive hidden casters.
* **The All Tree Root**: The withered root hints at a connection between the dungeon's vegetative structures and the mental "river" Carl perceives.
* **The Ritual of Eris**: Eris's rapture completely disrupts the military campaigns, gathering the entire floor's armies into a single location.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The voice that wakes Carl is a mystery, but its reference to "family now" suggests a lingering influence from the AI, Dante, or another entity sympathetic to the Cookbook. Carl's level 16 Mind Balance is a massive development; it cements his role as the floor's anchor, immune to the mental manipulations that easily disable regular crawlers.
