# Chapter 323: Code Red Mobilization

## Summary

The chapter begins with Cookbook entries: a note from Crawler [[justice-light]] (8th Edition) expressing remorse for killing an NPC in anger, and a reply from Crawler Drakea (22nd Edition) comforting him and urging him to make their enemies see what they have wrought.

At the start of Phase One (Ramp-Up), which lasts 180 hours with no offensive magic or teleportation allowed, Carl's base is in chaos. Most of the Princess Posse forces head ten miles north to block the Operatic Collective, while a smaller detachment prepares to head south to meet the Naga. Jurgen leads the 105th Scream Warriors. Justice Light confirms he has successfully set up the team's Throne Room before it locked in place. 

Chemical-laden artillery projectiles rain down on the FUPA stronghold. Carl and Donut mobilize their 15-member Personal Guard platoon, which includes [[dong-quixote]], [[splash-zone]], Bomo, Sledge, and Holger. Carl is warned that keeping his glowing jacket active is causing him **Soul Poisoning**.

In the skies, Louis reports that Samantha has sneaked onto the *Party Planner* airship. Since she is not a designated scout, she will be ripped apart by the border barrier. Carl orders Louis to dump Samantha out of the bomb bay, which Louis does despite her protests.

Meanwhile, young flesh golems [[clint-smashgrab]] and [[nadine]], alongside bear cubs Randy and Todd, try to join the fight, but Holger turns them back to the safety of the base. Carl mounts the Royal Chariot and prepares to head for the southern front.

## Characters

- **[[carl]]** — Suffers from Soul Poisoning due to his glowing jacket; prepares the Royal Chariot for the southern front.
- **[[princess-donut]]** — Leads her Personal Guard; wears her glittering tiara; complains about the magic bans.
- **[[jurgen]]** — Barbarian colonel of the 105th Scream Warriors.
- **[[justice-light]]** — Skyfowl Origami-class crawler and Cookbook author; secures the Throne Room.
- **[[louis-santiago-2]]** — Pilots the *Party Planner* airship; drops Samantha out of the bomb bay on Carl's orders.
- **[[samantha]]** — Sneaks onto the airship to "save" Louis, but is dumped out of the bomb bay to prevent a border collision.
- **[[jamal]]** — Giant hammerhead shark; ordered to stay behind to defend the FUPA under Tipid since the trenches aren't built for him.
- **[[clint-smashgrab]]** — Kindergarten-aged Chee child, now a flesh golem; eager to fight.
- **[[splash-zone]]** — NPC stripper mercenary; drives a flatbed transport toward the southern front.

## Themes & Plot Threads

- **Cookbook Legacy** — Drakea and Justice Light's notes highlight the psychological toll of the crawl and the transformation of peaceful souls into warriors.
- **Warlord Guard Platoon** — The warlord system permits a 15-member personal guard with a 25% Constitution buff.
- **Soul Poisoning** — Carl's armor patch is actively poisoning his soul in exchange for power.
- **Border Barrier Mechanics** — Border crossings without a scout endorsement are physically destructive.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections

This chapter emphasizes the logistical and operational realities of the Faction Wars. Carl must balance the restrictions of the system (limited war machine slots, no magic, border mechanics) with the erratic behavior of his allies (Samantha sneaking onto the airship, children wanting to fight). The tragic history of the Chee children (Clint and Nadine), now living as ageless flesh golems, highlights the cruel whims of the system's showrunners.
