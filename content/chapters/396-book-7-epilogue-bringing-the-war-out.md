# Chapter 396: Epilogue — Bringing the War Out

## Summary
In orbit, [[york]] (advocate tenodera and author of the 10th edition of the Cookbook) pilots the *Homecoming Queen*. He vaporized the orc yacht containing the children to test if his tenodera biology's lack of emotion would break, but felt nothing. Boomer docks and reveals they are preparing Cronus suits, power armor, and lasers to bring the war out of the dungeon to the surface to back up the pregnant Katia. Porthus's pre-recorded video is released, announcing the "Open Intellect Action Network" resistance group, demanding the Syndicate end the crawl. 

Carl meets [[quasar]] in a zero-zone cubicle in the Outreach Guild. Quasar reports that 20,000 crawlers reached floor 10, creating massive legal work. He details the exit deals, noting that the joint "Chaco replacement" deal is still available at the end of the 10th floor. Carl declines all deals, as does Donut. Quasar warns Carl that Donut's lawyer is Princess Chandra, Esquire... who is also Carl's newly registered wife. 

Quasar outlines the 10th floor: "Don't Come in Last," a race/conveyance-based floor. If a crawler's vehicle comes in last, their survival containment is removed. 

At the Squirties refueling waystation, Zander complains about the orbital embargo. The chaos goddess [[eris]] docks and attacks Zander when he asks if she is wearing a costume, rigidifying his facial tentacles and making them spew burning liquids.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **The Open Intellect Action Network**: The official launch of the out-of-dungeon resistance, led by Porthus, Rosetta, and Boomer.
- **The 10th Floor Rules**: Introduction to the racing mechanics of the 10th floor ("Don't Come in Last").
- **Syndicate Bureaucracy**: The absurd marriage of Carl to Princess Chandra as a legal exploit or contract complication.
- **Out-of-Dungeon Operations**: The preparation of Cronus power suits to intervene on Earth's surface.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The Epilogue shifts the scale of the narrative from a struggle inside the dungeon to a full-scale intergalactic war. The launch of the Open Intellect Action Network by Porthus and the preparation of power armor to back up Katia on the surface signals that the resistance is taking the fight directly to the Syndicate. The reveal of the 10th floor's race mechanics sets up a high-speed, high-stakes sequel, while Carl's sudden "marriage" to Donut's lawyer Princess Chandra is a hilarious, bureaucratic twist that highlights the bizarre contract law of the crawl.
