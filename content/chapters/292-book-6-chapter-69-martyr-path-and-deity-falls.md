# Chapter 292: Martyr's Path and Deity Falls

## Summary
- **Martyr's Path Activated**: Stuck in Samantha's head (Nothing/Sheol), Carl's health drops to 2%, allowing him to manually trigger the Floor 8 *Martyr's Path* benefit. This covers all of his outside footsteps—including his previous path under the demon—with Sheol fire, buffing Amayon.
- **Deity Slain**: [[paz-lo]] uses a specialized explosive arrow to decapitate [[ysalte|Goddess Ysalte]], permanently killing her in the realm. Paz Lo dies in the blast, but is credited with slaying a deity, dropping a Memorial Crystal.
- **Amayon's Portal**: With Ysalte dead, Amayon is freed and begins casting his return portal spell.
- **Emberus's Query**: Held in Amayon's hand, Carl asks the demon about Emberus's son, Geyrun. Amayon reveals that Geyrun was murdered, and that he witnessed the deal.
- **Betrayal**: The *Why Are You Hitting Yourself?* spell times out. Free, Amayon dumps Carl and Samantha toward the Sheol fire below, declaring their debt even.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Paz Lo's decapitation of Ysalte is an unprecedented triumph for a crawler totem, proving that even "invulnerable" deities can be slain on lower floors if their physical anchors are destroyed. Amayon's revelation regarding Geyrun's murder sets up a major lore mystery involving the gods.
