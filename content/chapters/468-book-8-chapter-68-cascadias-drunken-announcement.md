# Chapter 468: Cascadia's Meltdown

## Summary
[[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] exit their truck in the garage. [[bucket-boy]], [[bigs]], and [[samantha]] enter; Carl apologizes for the deaths of [[dong-quixote]] and [[corcunda]]. Samantha spots [[grigori]] and demands he sculpt her a compatible body, suggesting a prostitute named "Vertical Smile" in the "High-Classes Bitches" club. Grigori agrees to examine her, and she drags him into the safe room, summoning Jamal.

Cascadia, the show manager, makes a drunken, rambling dungeon-wide announcement. She rants about her ex-husband "Cumberland" who was recently blown up in his orbital habitat. She outlines the rules for the remaining races:
- Crawlers will have access to an Outreach Guild (to take deals) between Heats 6 and 7.
- Heat 7 will feature 3 teams per heat, with only the winner surviving. If a driver takes a deal, the remaining teammates must still finish.
- The floor will feature a floor boss.
- The 11th floor (the Arena) will have a strict 3-hour time limit.

In the chat, [[elle-mcgibbons]] and [[imani-c]] discuss Cascadia's breakdown, comparing her to Francine Herrada, a Meadow Lark resident who tried to stab her husband's new wife with a knitting needle. Gremlin mechanic Hedy asks Carl if he wants to know their new audience-voted upgrade.
