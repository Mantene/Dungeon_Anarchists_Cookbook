# Book 3 Chapter 16: Babababoons and Witch Decapitation

## Summary
Elle and Imani message Carl with a major discovery: a secret, downward-sloping tunnel exists behind the deactivated automatons in the robot rooms. The tunnel opens after the robots wake, which is triggered when the monsters begin suffering withdrawal symptoms (the DTs). They are following it to see where it leads.

Princess Donut realizes that due to a combination of equipment boosts and their "Good Rest" + shower buffs, her intelligence is effectively 52, allowing her to read the **Tome of Minion Army** (which requires 50 spell points). Carl cautions her not to cast the spell recklessly, as it has a five-minute casting time that freezes the caster in place, a five-hour cooldown, and only a base 2% chance to turn intelligent mobs within its 30-meter area of effect.

Upon opening the door to the transit station outside their safe room, the party is met with a chaotic pack of **Babababoons** (Level 17)—Earth baboons crossed with the inmates of a Florida jail drunk tank. These creatures are suffering from Stage 2 DTs, which doubles their strength but robs them of reason, causing them to attack anything. To protect the *Nightmare Express*, the party engages them. Carl uses his upgraded *Powerful Strike* and *Bang Bro* skill. Donut uses *Second Chance* on a dead babababoon wearing a top hat to create a zombie minion, and splits her *Magic Missile* into a three-way laser using her new *Prism Goggles*. Carl throws custom-made scrap-metal **Banger Spheres** from his xistera. Katia Grim fights in her doppelganger form and learns how to shift the metal scrap inside her body to absorb attacks and snap a babababoon's spine, discovering how to grow "traveling spikes" at the cost of breaking her own bones.

The party boards the engine cab and heads to the switching station. Carl examines the *Station Repair* portal and discovers it is a **Pass-Through two-way portal** with tracks running directly through the threshold. A red steam locomotive rolls out of the portal, switches tracks, and disappears into another tunnel, demonstrating that the switches are controlled from the other side of the portal. Inside the cab, Fire Brandy chats with Donut and Katia about motherhood and gives birth to another baby in the firebox.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The Babababoons showcase Matt Dinniman's signature blend of absurdity and visceral danger. The introduction of the Stage 2 DTs highlights the systemic cruelty of the floor: the dungeon administration hooks mobs on sedative drugs to keep them on predictable patrol routes, and their forced withdrawal transforms them into hyper-violent, terminal threats. Katia's development of traveling spikes marks her growth from a passive crawler to an active combatant, even as she endures horrific pain to do so.
