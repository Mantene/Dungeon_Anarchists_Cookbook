# Chapter 479: Chapter 79 — Final Preparations and Britney's Crisis

## Summary
Carl attempts to convince Osvaldo to take a deal, but Osvaldo defiantly refuses. However, Osvaldo's teammate, Filipe, secretly takes a deal with Princess Donut's help, confirming he is leaving the dungeon via the Outreach Guild. An updated count reveals that 715 crawlers are taking deals and leaving, leaving 274 crawlers (103 teams) for the final race. Many leaving crawlers are using Milk’s method to add a spell to The Book of Voodoo before their departure.

The chapter details the confirmed heat matchups: Carl and Donut face Team Sparkles and Team Flamengo, while Elle and Imani are up against the "Tracksuit Troika" (a trio of Russian crawlers) and the Minister of Blood-Letting with his Wienermobile. Chris, while refusing to leave if Imani stays, convinces his teammates, the Erins, to exit, leaving him alone. Florin's teammate Lucia also refuses to leave despite Jurgen's persuasion. Prepotente observes the Outreach Guild, noting that no one has returned, which suggests the deals are legitimate. He also expresses concern that the final race might be anticlimactic if Carl's escape plan works too well, prompting a sharp rebuke from Donut.

Carl and Donut head to Hungry Eyes for food buffs and drop Pontiff off at the Desperado Club to investigate the missing strippers and a potential dimensional exit. They encounter Britney leaving the club, visibly distressed and agitated. She reveals a conflict with Elle, who wants Louis and her to leave, while Louis and Britney refuse. When Carl touches her arm, Britney telepathically communicates with him, urging him to survive. Carl recognizes this as a manifestation of possession, similar to Maggie My's influence on Chris, and notes its acceleration. Mordecai, Imani, and Rosetta believe they need to understand it before confronting her.

Pontiff messages Carl with a critical update: the Desperado Club's new guards include former assassins, and the "Nothing" spot on the Wheel of Fortune is back, now in "suspicious quotes," implying it might be a functional dimensional exit. Carl realizes their initial escape plan—using the casino's Wheel of Fortune "Nothing" spot to access a holding area from which Herot could extract them—might be viable again, though he finds it risky. Rosetta confirms the existence and accessibility of such holding areas, and believes the showrunners now just want the crawlers out. With time running out, Carl instructs Pontiff to be careful, gather info, and then extract himself from the club. The chapter ends with Carl and Prepotente sharing a "good-race hug" before the final race.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- [[survival]] — The overarching goal, intensified by the race mechanic of disintegration for coming in last and the desperate escape plans.
- [[solidarity-and-duty]] — Crawlers grapple with personal survival versus loyalty to teammates or a larger cause, exemplified by Osvaldo's refusal to leave, Chris's loyalty to Imani, and Britney's conflict regarding Louis.
- [[over-city-mechanics]] — The chapter delves into the intricacies of dungeon escape routes, including the Outreach Guild deals, the "Nothing" spot on the Wheel of Fortune, holding areas, and the showrunners' shifting intentions.
- [[systemic-oppression]] — The dungeon forces crawlers into impossible choices: take a deal (and disappear), die in the race, or attempt a risky escape. Britney's possession hints at deeper, possibly supernatural, control.
- [[the-magical-connection]] — Carl experiences a raw telepathic connection with Britney, reminiscent of his previous connection with Imani and Katia, signifying a powerful and potentially dangerous interaction.
- [[possession]] — Britney's erratic behavior and telepathic communication strongly indicate she is possessed, echoing Chris's past experience with Maggie My, and suggesting a looming threat.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
This chapter cranks up the tension significantly, shifting the focus from just winning the race to the chaotic, last-minute execution of a desperate escape plan. The dwindling number of crawlers and the stark reality of those taking deals versus those remaining underscores the brutal nature of Floor 10. The humor injected by Donut's outburst at Prepotente provides a momentary, much-needed release in an otherwise fraught atmosphere, highlighting the characters' ingrained superstition and fear of jinxing their precarious situation.

Britney's accelerating possession is a chilling development, adding a personal, tragic layer to the systemic pressures of the dungeon. Carl's telepathic connection with her is a pivotal moment, confirming the severity of her condition and hinting at larger, unseen forces at play. This crisis, combined with Pontiff's updates on the "Nothing" spot, forces Carl to make rapid, high-stakes decisions about their escape route, demonstrating his adaptability under extreme pressure. The chapter brilliantly portrays the escalating chaos and the race against time, making the "anticlimactic" ending Prepotente fears seem utterly improbable.
