# Chapter 387: Chapter 79 — Into the Reaver Castle

## Summary
The rescue team travels to the boxy, plastic-like Reaver castle. Donut's new druid-like spells (*Raise Forest*, *Gather Ye Vermin*) are discussed, as well as the constraints of the Champion system: Nekhebit's enemies have a bounty on Donut, and the goddess can order her to perform tasks under threat of a smite. 

At the castle, the team meets Victory, Drick, and the Reaver team adjutant, [[tart]] (a demon-like caprid/deer alien, former political analyst). Donut's glasses detect an invisible Fopsy nearby, signaling a potential trap. 

Juice Box transforms into her non-corporeal *Hunter Wraith* form. Carl uses *Find Crawler* to locate the unconscious Louis inside a honeycomb room. Juice Box goes invisible and enters the castle to pull Louis out.

- **The Champion Mechanics**: The double-edged sword of becoming a deity's champion.
- **Hunter Wraith Lore**: The dangerous, non-corporeal nature of Hunter Wraiths and their ability to possess targets.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The banter between Bigs, Dong Quixote, and the Reaver adjutant Tart provides comic relief before the storm. Donut's realization that she is now Nekhebit's champion shows how the system wraps the crawlers in layers of control, trading autonomy for raw power.
