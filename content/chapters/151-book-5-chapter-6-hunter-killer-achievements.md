# Book 5 Chapter 6: Hunter Killer Achievements

## Summary
Carl recovers in a cart pulled by Miriam's demon pet Bianca, counts his massive achievement rewards, and has a weird interaction with Prepotente.
- **Miriam's Vampire Form**: Carl wakes up in a cart pulled by **Bianca** (Miriam's Level 32 Hellspawn Familiar). Miriam is now a Level 52 Vampire Shepherd, who floats and feeds on the blood of creatures killed by Prepotente.
- **Massive Achievement Loot**: Carl has leveled up to 54 (Donut to 41). He opens his rewards:
  - **Gold Junkie's Box**: Gives a new elbow tattoo: **The Night Wyrm's Nasty Little Web of Suffering**, granting access to the [[the-guild-of-suffering|Guild of Suffering]].
  - **34 Gold Hunter-Killer Boxes**: Gained for permanently killing 34 hunters (including Xindy and Chin'Dua). Gives 595,000 gold and physical **Hunting Trophies** (severed hands/claws of the hunters).
  - **Platinum Asshole's Box**: A potion upgrading Carl's *Fear* spell by 5 levels to **Level 10** (can paralyze).
- **PR Updates**: Zev contacts them, informing them they will appear on Odette's show tomorrow, with new security protocols in place.
- **Prepotente's Tantrum**: Prepotente (Level 55, Profane Vitiate) is angry at Carl for detonating Zockau's traps, but Miriam forces them to apologize. Prepotente hugs Carl and screams right in his ear.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[the-butchers-masquerade]]**: The introduction of the severed hands as hunting trophies (tickets) to be spent at the Masquerade prize counter.
- **[[solidarity-and-duty]]**: Despite Prepotente's instability and hostility, Miriam and Prepotente rescue the heavily injured Carl, maintaining crawler solidarity.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The system's award of 34 Gold Hunter-Killer boxes and a Platinum Asshole's box confirms that Carl's preemptive strike was highly successful. The physical hands of the hunters serving as "prize tickets" for the Masquerade highlights the televised game show's brutal commodification of death. Prepotente's intense emotional instability, combined with his high level (55) and scream attacks, makes him a dangerous but valuable ally.
