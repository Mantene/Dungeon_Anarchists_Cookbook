# Book 2 Chapter 15: The Desperado Club

## Summary
[[carl]], [[princess-donut]], and [[mordecai]] visit the members-only section of the garish **Desperado Club**. They pass the crocodilian bouncer [[clarabelle]], who notes that the pet playroom is shut down due to an incident the previous night. 

In the club, they use basic privacy bubbles to filter out the loud dubstep. They meet the pharmacist [[quint]], a Level-75 opossum/djinn hybrid. Donut drinks a vodka-filled Shirley Temple and gets drunk, but is cured by Quint's *Hair of the Dog* potion. Mordecai trades three bottles of Krakaren moonshine for an **Alchemy Chest Starter Kit** containing various chemical supplies. 

Quint shares that the night before, a human crawler with a goat leg ([[lucia-mar]]) went berserk in the club after her dogs broke out of the playroom. She froze the room, stole tequila, and bludgeoned an elite gnoll's brains in with a mace. 

As they leave, Carl asks Mordecai about his guide status. Mordecai explains that starting on Floor 4, the Syndicate's "system protection" will kick in for managers. If he dies, he won't really perish, just be kicked from the game. He stands to earn a massive payout if Donut survives past Floor 9.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The revelation that Lucia Mar went on a rampage in the club highlights her terrifying power and unstable nature. Carl's conversation with Mordecai reveals the tragic commercial incentives for guides: they are kept subservient but can buy their freedom if their crawlers succeed.
