# Chapter 441: Chapter 42 — Forest Flight

## Summary
The race begins, and the crawlers zoom onto the dirt track. Team Free Love takes an early lead, and the Lady Dominators zoom past [[carl]]'s truck. Carl and [[princess-donut]] take the middle path of the forest split. The windshield starts to fog up because the truck lacks wipers, though the shield protects them from the acid rain.

Within the cab, [[quemada]]'s panic attack intensifies. [[olga]] explains that their former ice fairy companion, [[helado]], was killed by a chain demon, leaving Quemada emotionally unstable. Olga admits Quemada once killed a client in a panic. [[finley]] uses a sedative claw pinch to put Quemada to sleep, placing her in the sink.

The Wienermobile (driven by the Bleak Congregation, led by the Minister of Blood-Letting) merges onto the path and blocks Carl. Donut notes she befriended the Minister at a karaoke bar, and he promised not to blood-sacrifice them to Baal. 

[[imani-c]] messages Carl from behind. She reports that crawler [[henrietta]] from Angola and her partner were killed when a trap swallowed their mount whole. Carl shares their route and agrees to wait for Imani's team at the cliff ahead.
