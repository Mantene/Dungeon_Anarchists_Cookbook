# Book 4 Chapter 9: The Cyborg Buzzard

## Summary
Carl's party steals the telescope, tests their new missiles against Ruckus, and receives a major floor update.
- **Stealing the Telescope**: Carl knocks on Skarn's door, telling the kid to run to the Toe if bombs drop (planning to shelter the local NPCs in their safe room). While they talk, Donut climbs the wall and steals the Gnomish FarSeerer telescope from the roof, "forgetting" to leave the 30 gold coins Carl requested.
- **Sunglasses Upgrade**: Donut's sunglasses receive a benefactor update from Princess D'Nadia, adding a **Surface and Subsurface Temperature overlay**. This allows her to spot changelings by their elevated brain/head temperatures. Donut identifies two disguised changelings among Hump Town's gate guards.
- **Ruckus Boss Fight**: Carl mounts a swiveling four-chamber missile launcher onto the Chariot. They drive a mile and a half out into the desert to target **Ruckus** (Level 55 Borough Boss).
  - Carl's first two-stage missile fires, but the warhead explodes prematurely when the second stage ignites.
  - Carl locks his three remaining guided missiles and instructs Katia to drive *toward* the boss. Katia sprouts a second mouth and eyes on stalks on the back of her head to navigate in reverse while keeping her front shielded.
  - The three missiles hit Ruckus simultaneously, blowing off her wing and destroying her. The boss's massive 25-foot tree-trimming buzzsaw spear wedges itself into the stone, still chugging. Carl has Katia prepare her inventory to store it.
- **Water Quadrant Conquered**: A massive earthquake rumbles through the dome. A system announcement declares that the **Bridge of the *Akula*** (a giant submarine that served as the Water Quadrant castle) has been successfully occupied and the quadrant liberated. The conqueror is revealed to be **Chris Andrews 2**, allowing Water Quadrant crawlers to cross boundaries.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival-of-the-collapse]]**: The conquest of the Water Quadrant by Chris Andrews 2 is the first major step toward popping the bubble.
- **[[televised-exploitation]]**: Donut's obsession with the *Penis Parade* show bar demonstrates how effectively the system's entertainment distractions siphon crawler gold.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The revelation that the Water Quadrant castle was a giant submarine (*Akula*) parked by the deceased bugbear Shamus Chaindrive recontextualizes the floor's structure. Chris Andrews' solo conquest of this castle confirms his extreme lethality as an Igneous rock crawler, but his isolation and sudden aggression make him an unpredictable wildcard. The failure of Carl's second-stage missile engine forces him to rethink his long-range bombardment strategy for the Wasteland.
