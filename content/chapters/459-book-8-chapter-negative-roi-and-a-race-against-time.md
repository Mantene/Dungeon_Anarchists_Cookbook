# Chapter 459: Chapter 59 — Negative ROI and a Race Against Time

## Summary
With just over an hour and a half before the next race heat, Carl's group finalizes plans for Li Na's departure and attempts to merge Porky and Corky. Elle confirms Li Na is ready, while Imani reports that Dong Quixote is emotionally distraught, having picked up all available nickels but with his sock having reverted to its original size and no longer speaking to him. Carl attempts to contact his mercenary strippers—Splash Zone, Gluteus, and Doctor Bones—but receives a system warning that they are no longer on the floor and he's still being charged. Bucket Boy confirms they were last headed to see Hamed, the new leader of the Guild of Suffering, at the Desperado Club, causing Carl concern.

The plan to merge Porky and Corky involves breaking into Garage 2, disabling Genesis and Rapture, and bringing Corky back to their safe room for Grigori to perform a joining spell. Grigori explains the process is rare and he has never done it, making Carl apprehensive. He mentions "Meld" spells as similar but with a dominant personality. Carl briefly asks about Samantha's situation, and Grigori dismissively states he's familiar and offers solutions for a body, noting Carl "wasted the flesh golem." Meanwhile, Princess Donut engages Porky in a conversation about show business, seeking advice for her singing career, which Porky enthusiastically provides, highlighting the importance of presence and audience connection.

The group's urgent plans are interrupted when Tipid reports that Dong Quixote's health is rapidly declining with a "Negative ROI" debuff. Bucket Boy adds that the sock has teleported away. Mordecai identifies "Negative ROI" as a curse called "Separation Anxiety," which requires the sock and all the lost nickels, which are scattered among numerous NPCs, making recovery nearly impossible. Imani and Jacobus rush to the garage to try and save Dong.

As Donut prepares to cast her Hole spell, Dong Quixote contacts Carl, expressing his heartbreak and love for Corky, feeling like he's dying and being excluded. Despite feeling like an "asshole," Carl pushes forward, promising Dong help after the merging. However, Garage 2 unexpectedly opens, and Genesis drives out in a GTO with Rapture, Corky passed out in the back. They taunt Carl, revealing they knew about his plan and intend to "unzip" Corky rather than let Carl have him, referring to Dong as Carl's "gimp." A road to the starting blocks appears, leading to a newly manifested, ominous "Gasworks Screw Factory, Steel Mill, and Doll Workshop" building. Porky is enraged to discover Dong's involvement.

Realizing they can no longer wait, Carl decides they must attempt the merging *during* the race, instructing Grigori to learn to drive. The chapter concludes with Carl's group preparing to say goodbye to Li Na before the race.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
This chapter is a masterclass in escalating tension and forcing difficult choices. Carl, already under immense pressure, is hit with multiple crises simultaneously: the mysterious disappearance of his mercenaries, the gut-wrenching emotional and physical collapse of Dong Quixote, and the frustrating thwarting of his meticulously laid plans by Genesis and Rapture. The "Negative ROI" curse is a particularly cruel dungeon mechanic, perfectly embodying the System's predatory nature by turning Dong's emotional distress into a literal health drain, dependent on a lost sentient weapon and scattered coins. Carl's internal conflict regarding Dong—his initial dismissiveness versus his eventual realization of Dong's suffering—is a poignant moment, showcasing his continuous moral growth. The decision to attempt the merging *during* the race is a desperate, yet characteristic, Carl move, setting up a chaotic and unpredictable next heat. The chapter brilliantly uses humor (Donut's singing career advice from Porky) to punctuate the encroaching dread, culminating in an ominous cliffhanger that promises high-stakes action and profound emotional challenges ahead.
