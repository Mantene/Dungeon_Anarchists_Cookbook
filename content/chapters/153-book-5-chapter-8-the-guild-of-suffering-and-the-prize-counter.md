# Book 5 Chapter 8: The Guild of Suffering and the Prize Counter

## Summary
Carl analyzes his new Night Wyrm sigil, watches the recap show outlining crawler changes and standings, and learns about the newly implemented Guild System.
- **The Guild of Suffering**: Carl inspects his elbow tattoo: **The Night Wyrm's Nasty Little Web of Suffering**. Mordecai explains that the web is linked to [[the-guild-of-suffering|The Guild of Suffering]], a cult that worships the demigod Night Wyrm.
- **The Recap Show**: The show highlights Floor 6 start, showing the Floor 5 climax (Dmitri/Maxim Popov, the demon Slit, and Katia flooding Larracos) and lists crawler specialization updates:
  - Elle McGibbons has specialized as a **Tundra Princess**, aiming for a highly complex "four seasons" triple-class build.
  - Quan Ch is now a one-armed **Sergeant-at-Arms**.
  - Current Top 10 Ranks: 1. Carl, 2. Lucia Mar, 3. Prepotente, 4. Donut, 5. Popov brothers, 6. Miriam Dom, 7. Elle McGib, 8. Bogdon Ro, 9. Eva Sigrid, 10. Quan Ch.
- **Katia's Vow**: Katia comments coldly that Eva Sigrid won't be on the leaderboard for long.
- **The Guild System**: The system launches the Guild System. Parties are capped at 30, but larger groups can buy a guild charter from a Bopca for 500,000 gold.
- **Butcher's Masquerade Gala**: The system announces the Masquerade gala at the end of the floor for the top 50 crawlers. Donut is thrilled about the Pet Beauty Contest and demands they buy the guild charter and collect more hunter trophies.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[the-butchers-masquerade]]**: The official announcement of the endgame party, including invitations, competitions (such as the Pet Beauty Contest), and the Prize Counter.
- **[[valtay-skull-empire-conflict]]**: The system disables the controversial private message subscription feature, resolving a legal battle that Quasar was tracking.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Katia's targeted hostility toward Eva Sigrid points to an impending conflict. Katia, now Level 52 and free of the party constraints to lead the former Daughters of Hekla, seems to have set her sights on executing justice against Eva Sigrid for her role in Floor 4 and Floor 5 events.
