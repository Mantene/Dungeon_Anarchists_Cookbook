# Book 6 Chapter 7: Shadow Boxer and Preproduction

## Summary
The crawlers secure a saferoom, and Zev schedules two major media and administrative appearances.
- **Capturing Geraldo**: Carl's squad successfully captures [[geraldo]] the Monk Seal, adding him as their first active squad totem.
- **Media Bookings**: [[zev]] messages Carl and Donut in Havana:
  - **Shadow Boxer Interview**: Due to Odette's pre-Faction Wars orbital travel, their live show is delayed. Instead, they are booked on *Shadow Boxer*, a documentary-style program hosted by former 9th-edition crawler [[rosetta]] (Rosetta Thagra) and funded by sponsor Open Intellect Pacifist Network. The show will probe Carl's teenage years (ages 15-18) between his mother's suicide and his Coast Guard enlistment, and Donut's media perceptions.
  - **Preproduction Meeting**: Zev arranges for Carl and Donut to virtually attend a policy preproduction meeting for Floor 9 (Faction Wars) with representatives from all nine participating factions.
- **Duende Attack**: While navigating Old Havana, the crawlers encounter and kill a Level 20 **Duende** folklore mob. Carl uses the kill to charge his [[scavengers-daughter-patch]], unlocking the *Daughter's Kiss* discharge attack.
- **Donut's Suspicions**: Donut continues to express distrust toward [[sister-ines-quiteria]], finding her feline nature highly suspicious since "she doesn't even use a litterbox."

## Characters Introduced
- [[rosetta]] — Former 9th-edition crawler, host of *Shadow Boxer* (first appearance).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **Floor 9 Politics**: Introduction of the Faction Wars preproduction meetings, establishing the administrative scale of the upcoming floor.
- **[[ghosts-of-earth]]**: Crawlers face minor local folklore threats (Duendes) while navigating Havana.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Zev's scheduling highlights how corporate sponsorship drives the dungeon. The Open Intellect Pacifist Network is forcing Carl to rehash his traumatic youth for media consumption. The introduction of Duendes shows that Floor 8 is packed with small, annoying folklore spirits that populate the urban areas of Cuba.
