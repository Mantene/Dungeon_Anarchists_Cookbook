# Chapter 355: Chapter 50 — Tricking the Ram

## Summary
[[elle-mcgibbons|Elle's]] team secures the Blood Sultanate stronghold, while [[louis-santiago-2|Louis]] poisons the exterior. [[carl|Carl's]] group prepares to enter the Temple of Dagda, the Shanty Town entrance to [[club-vanquisher]]. Carl and [[prepotente|Prepotente]] drink high-quality "Closed Off" potions made by [[mordecai|Mordecai]], protecting them from external aura Dreads and Blessings. 

They enter the temple, which is filled with Celtic harp music and a boiling cauldron of stew. [[daniel-bautista-2|Bautista]] distracts the red-haired, Welsh-accented cleric NPC by pretending to want to join the Dagda religion. [[li-jun|Li Jun]] and [[zhang]] trail wire from [[li-na|Li Na's]] Extend Aura clocktower synergy tower into the temple. 

Carl and Prepotente enter the vestibule of Club Vanquisher, where they are confronted by Mork, a Level 40 ram cleric of Hellik. Since Carl worships Emberus, Mork is hostile, but he is distracted by Prepotente, who has a crusade bounty on him. Prepotente dramatically fake-sobs, pretending Carl captured him. Carl tricks Mork into calling the guards to verify and split the 500,000 gold bounty. Emberus offers Carl a quest to kill the blasphemer Mork. Carl calls [[katia-grim|Katia's]] group to move in.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Prepotente's exaggerated performance as Carl's captive is highly entertaining, showing a rare comedic chemistry between the two crawlers. Carl's quick thinking in tricking Mork into calling the guards allows them to bypass the initial security check and lure the defenders out of position. The trailing of the capacitor wires from Li Na's synergy tower indicates a major trap is being prepared, promising explosive action inside the club.
