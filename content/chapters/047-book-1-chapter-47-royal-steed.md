# Book 1 Chapter 47: The Royal Steed

## Summary
[[princess-donut]]'s attempt to bond with [[mongo]] becomes an intergalactic viral hit, boosting their metrics to 124.8 trillion views. [[carl]] ignores [[zev]]'s advice to stop using the slingshot, raising the skill to 5, which allows him to ground flying Brindled Vespas. 

They consult [[mordecai]], who explains they must turn Mongo's dot white (neutral) to begin bonding. Donut learns the **Heal Critter** spell, and Carl drinks another *Determine Value* potion, unlocking a **History** tab in his inventory. Carl gets the **PETA Enthusiast!** achievement. 

Mordecai details the Floor 3 transition: crawlers descend, select their race, select their class, and distribute stats (except Donut, who has auto-scaling stats). Mordecai reveals he was a Changeling Fire Mage Arcanist, but his brother was lost after choosing a Storm Commander class recommended by [[odette]].

Mongo is calmed by seeing other mobs. Donut trains Mongo by cooperatively hunting Level 2 Brindle Grubs, unzipping their bellies. Mongo eats the remains, turning white-tagged. 

With under 10 hours left on Floor 2, Carl and Donut grind on Vespas, reaching Level 13. When a Vespa grabs Mongo to eat him, Donut flies across and decapitates the hornet, which finalizes the bonding process. Mongo joins the party as Donut's orange-tagged **Royal Steed**.

As they camp at the stairs, Carl checks his inventory History tab and discovers three high-value, illegal technology items from the **Valtay Corporation** at the top of the list:
1. **Trans Tunnel 7C Orator Relay** (Inter-tunnel communications device).
2. **Valtay Perso-Shield Platinum Edition** (Tech personal shield).
3. **Mag 3040 Valtay Corp Pulse Pistol** (Tech pulse rifle).

Carl realizes these items were in [[agatha]]'s shopping cart when he temporarily looted it during the Swine battle. 

Agatha enters the stairwell chamber, still Level 4. When Carl mentions her shopping cart, Agatha warns him with her eyes to stay silent, stating: "Them critters already know I'm here. They just don't know what to do about it. You worry about you, and I worry about me." She pushes her cart down the stairs to Floor 3.

Zev pings them, reporting a brief feed outage (which Carl suspects was caused by Agatha's presence). They descend the stairs to Floor 3.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The chapter ends Book 1's dungeon run with a massive cliffhanger. Agatha is not just an eccentric homeless woman; she is carrying highly advanced, illegal tech from the Valtay Corporation (the parasitic technological system poised to seize the crawl from Borant). Her cryptic warning and the system feed outage suggest she is working against the Syndicate.
