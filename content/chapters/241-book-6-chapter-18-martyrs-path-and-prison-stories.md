# Book 6 Chapter 18: Martyr's Path and Prison Stories

## Summary
Carl enters Club Vanquisher, receives a divine boon, learns new details about the murder of the god Geyrun, and returns to the saferoom to address the brewing crisis.
- **Club Vanquisher Entry**: Carl travels to Club Vanquisher to cure his *SlugPox*. The entrance is guarded by [[potsy]] (Level 40 ram-headed priest of Hellik), who is hostile but bound by club safety rules. A Level 10 Pox Slug pops in Carl's boxers; Carl rips it out and flings it at the stained glass window, prompting [[pater-coal]] (Level 101 Hobbledehoy High Cleric of Emberus) to let Carl in.
- **The Ending Sun**: Pater Coal takes Carl to the *Emberus High Temple of the Ending Sun*. He performs a foot-washing ritual (which Carl finds highly uncomfortable) that suppresses the slugpox and grants Carl the floor boon **The Martyr's Path** (+10% Strength; allows releasing gouts of flame from previous footsteps when health drops below 5% or upon death).
- **The Apito Mystery**: Pater Coal reveals that the *Apito memorial crystal* (from Floor 6) shouldn't exist because the goddess [[apito]] (the Oak Mother, sister-in-law to Emberus) is still alive. Solving the murder of Emberus's son Geyrun requires using this crystal to trace [[katia-grim]]'s crossbow (formerly Hekla's) and Donut's oak bracelet (looted from a deceased relative of [[daniel-bautista-2]] on Floor 3).
- **Healed and Banned**: Carl pays 10,000 gold to the healer to cure his slugpox. A mantaur bans him from the club for three days due to the mess he caused.
- **Brewing Storm in the Saferoom**: Carl returns to the saferoom, joining Donut, Katia, Mordecai, and Bautista. Carl lies about the oak bracelet to avoid upsetting Bautista. Katia (who now worships Eileithyia) prepares to explain the [[crown-of-the-sepsis-whore]] curse to Donut.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **Geyrun's Murder**: The mystery of the deceased deity Geyrun expands, linking trivial Floor 3 loot (Donut's oak bracelet) to the divine conspiracy.
- **System Narrative Railroading**: Carl feels manipulated by the AI's insistence on threading random items (the bracelet and crossbow) into a grand plotline.
- **Secret Alignments**: Katia's conversion to Eileithyia begins to surface as she prepares to explain her curse.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The foot-washing scene is one of the most bizarre instances of system-mandated religious roleplay, showing how the AI forces crawlers into narrative templates ("The Martyr") for the audience's entertainment. Carl's growing realization that the AI is shaping a tragic storyline around him, Katia, and Donut makes him increasingly resistant, even as he is forced to follow its path.
