# Chapter 294: Combined and the Eye of the Bedlam Bride

## Summary
- **Loss of Control**: Combined with Shi Maria, Carl becomes a passenger in his own body. Shi Maria browses his inventory and notices his restricted items (like the cookbook and the nuke).
- **Ink and Self-Immolation**: She extracts the toraline root to brew black ink. She forces Carl to cast *Self-Immolation*, which burns through the floor to free Imani and Tran, but severely burns Mongo and Katia.
- **Li Jun's Mutilation**: When [[li-jun]] slides down to help, Shi Maria stabs him in the eye with the tattoo kit needle, devours his eye, and uses the bloody needle to tattoo [[eye-of-the-bedlam-bride-tattoo|The Eye of the Bedlam Bride]] onto Carl's chest.
- **Warning Voice**: A mysterious male voice in Carl's mind warns him not to trust Shi Maria and to get her out of him as soon as possible.
- **Timeout and Collapse**: Shi Maria drops healing potions for Katia, Mongo, and Li Jun. The combination times out, and Carl regains control, passing out as the fresh tattoo blinks, closes, and disappears into his skin.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
This is one of the darkest and most violating moments of Carl's crawl. Shi Maria bypasses the card discard system by tattooing herself directly onto Carl's flesh using Li Jun's blood and toraline ink. The mutilation of Li Jun's eye creates a permanent, painful mark of her control.
