# Book 3 Chapter 24: The Spoils of Hekla

## Summary
Immediately following Hekla’s death, Hekla's mages burst into the cab. Eva lunges at Katia, but Donut casts *Second Chance* and *Clockwork Triplicate* on Hekla's corpse, creating three zombie Hekla minions. One minion throws Eva out the window onto the tracks, where she survives. The other minions push the outraged daughters back into the passenger cars. Carl and Donut go up two levels each (to 34 and 32 respectively).

Katia loots the **Vermillion key** and the **Enchanted Repeating Crossbow of the Scavenger Mother of Mothers** from Hekla's corpse. Carl puts the train in reverse, escaping Station 101 as stage three tentacled monsters swarm the platform. Carl locks the cab door to keep the daughters out. 

Donut detects **Silfa** hiding in the rear apartment; Donut pins her, and Carl defuses the situation. Carl explains Hekla's full plot to Silfa: Hekla wanted Katia dead and Carl enraged so that Hekla could kill Carl in "self-defense," forcing Donut and Mordecai to join her guild. Silfa reveals that Hekla had instructed her daughters (the two mages) to kill Carl if anything went wrong. Silfa, a former baker, laments Hekla's death, noting that Eva is now the leader and Brynhild’s Daughters has collapsed, leaving them with no personal space and a neglectful sponsor (the "crab ranch"). Carl lets Silfa and her daughters go.

Carl inspects Hekla's looted crossbow. It is a female-only weapon that increases in power by 25% for each female in the party (up to 30) and casts *Birth Defect* on spawning monsters. When Carl touches it, the system mocks him: `Warning: You have a dick.`

**Li Jun** (now a Level 28 Street Monk) enters the cab. His sister Li Na, friend Zhang, and a group of 12 are safe in the cargo cars. Carl coordinates a plan: they will stop at Station 75, detach the engine car (housing Carl, Katia, and Donut), and Li Jun will drive the main train back to Station 36 to make a stand with Imani. Carl, Katia, and Donut will then use the engine car to head to the end of the line and rescue Bautista's group.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[threat-of-frank-q]]**: The threat of Hekla is resolved, but the political fallout leaves the guild of Brynhild's Daughters shattered.
- **[[the-iron-tangle-escape-plan]]**: Carl devises a plan to split the train, sending the rescued crawlers to Station 36 while he takes the engine to rescue Bautista.
- **[[solidarity-and-duty]]**: Carl and Li Jun cooperate immediately, showing strong crawler solidarity, contrasted with Hekla's manipulation.
- **Enchanted Repeating Crossbow of the Scavenger Mother of Mothers**: A powerful unique weapon looted from Hekla, providing Katia with a massive upgrade.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's quick thinking in using Hekla's corpse as a clockwork minion shield saved the party from a bloody civil war in the cab. The collapse of Brynhild's Daughters reveals the fragile nature of large crawler organizations, which are often built entirely around a single charismatic leader and collapse instantly upon their death. Carl's decision to split the train and head back for Bautista shows his refusal to accept the dungeon's "acceptable trade-offs."
