# Chapter 469: The Pet Stables Meeting

## Summary
[[carl]], [[princess-donut]], [[imani-c]], [[elle-mcgibbons]], [[rosetta]], and [[prepotente]] meet in the pet stables. The stables, being a secondary guild room, has an active bug that blocks the gods' surveillance, allowing the crawlers to speak freely about divine quests (though external viewers can still watch, and they cannot perform permanent actions like removing god-given gifts).

Imani has only one hour left on her blood bar and is furious about Carl's plan (posted in the *Book of Voodoo*) to leave Donut's party for the 11th floor to keep her safe while he kills Hellik.

They coordinate the [[pineapple-cabaret|Pineapple Cabaret]] escape. Only non-god-worshipping, human crawlers are eligible due to "zero zone" compatibility issues; flying classes, fairies, [[louis-santiago-2]] (due to gills), and Chris (as a rock creature) are excluded. Roughly 200 are set to escape, but they need 1,500 crawlers to take deals to minimize crawler-on-crawler matches in Heat 7. Meanwhile, [[minus]] (impersonating [[linus]]) is helping Bautista serve tea at a massive crawler farewell party in Hungry Eyes.

Rosetta explains the mythology of the **Scavenger**:
- Nekhebit (the vulture goddess) had three children: Apito, Scolopendra, and a mysterious son.
- The **Scavenger's Daughter** is a legendary child of Apito's power, capable of erasing the current pantheon and stealing the powers of gods.
- The war mages believe [[samantha]]'s sand-ooze daughter is this entity.

Carl asks Prepotente to show him the memorial crystal he possesses.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **The Scavenger Storyline**: Deepening lore regarding Nekhebit, Apito, Scolopendra, and the "Scavenger's Daughter" who can absorb the powers of gods.
- **The Stables Bug**: Strategic use of dungeon bugs to hide plans from the pantheon.
- **Crawler Estrangement**: Donut refusing to speak to Carl due to his plan to leave their party to fight Hellik alone.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The Scavenger lore explained by Rosetta links various plot threads—Scolopendra, Apito, Samantha's daughter—into a unified cosmic mystery. The stable bug provides a clever sci-fi mechanism for the characters to plan treason against the gods.
