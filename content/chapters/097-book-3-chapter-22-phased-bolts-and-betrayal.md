# Book 3 Chapter 22: Phased Bolts and Betrayal

## Summary
Katia Grim molds her doppelganger clay into a bulldozer scoop and spikes on the front of the Vermillion line train, insulating herself with rubber to avoid the third rail. Joining Carl, Donut, Hekla, and Eva in the cab is **Silfa**, a Level 25 Wisteria Fairy "Holistic Healer" who has over two dozen boss kills but acts highly anxious.

The train speeds down the tunnel, easily pulverizing a small group of ghouls. However, at Station 72, they hit a large group of Level 20 **Blister Ghouls** spawned by soul crystal-powered generators. The train is showered in blood and gore, and a body part gets wedged under Katia's scoop, causing her to get shocked by the third rail and fall unconscious. Carl has to scream at the panicked Silfa to cast heals, as she insists on waiting until Katia's health drops to 25%.

During the 1.5-hour ride to Station 101, Elle messages Carl with a warning: they discovered that when too many festering ghouls gather at Station 48, they combine into a gargantuan, mouth-covered province boss. Furthermore, their manager, **Mistress Tiatha**, is acting useless, spending her time drinking and stealing Elle's gold. Imani's team is planning to make a stand at Station 36 to kill the wrath ghouls before they can merge.

As they reach the massive horde at Station 101, they plow into the ghouls, and gore fills the cab waist-deep. Silfa runs away to hide in the back apartment. Katia's health plummets to 10% despite Carl's heals. Using her sunglasses, Donut witnesses Hekla shooting Katia with invisible arrows.

Carl orders Katia to disengage. She collapses onto the cab floor, unconscious and bleeding out. Carl reaches into her shoulder and pulls out two invisible projectiles: **Dirty Little Phased Bolts**. The system properties reveal these bolts inflict *Temporary Amnesia* and *Suffering Bleed*, reducing health to 10% and then 1%, and attributing the death to "alternate sources" so the shooter avoids PvP skulls.

As the train stops at Station 101, Hekla's *Enraged* buff fades. Eva has lost a hand. Although Carl attempts to de-escalate the situation via private chat to avoid a civil war, Donut is too furious to remain silent. She screams, "You tried to kill Katia you fucking bitch!" and fires a full-strength *Magic Missile* directly into Hekla's face.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival-of-the-collapse]]**: The rescue team arrives at Station 101, but the mission is derailed by Hekla's attempted murder of Katia.
- **[[threat-of-frank-q]]**: The legacy of crawler-on-crawler murder is shown to be highly organized; Hekla uses specialized gear to execute allies without getting skulls.
- **[[solidarity-and-duty]]**: The betrayal of Katia by her former guild leader shatters the illusion of crawler solidarity, while Donut's immediate defensive action shows her absolute loyalty to Katia.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Hekla's use of the Dirty Little Phased Bolts is a chilling example of systemic manipulation. The system provides tools specifically designed to let crawlers murder each other while avoiding the legal and social consequences (PvP skulls). Hekla, a former psychiatrist, chose to murder Katia quietly to prevent Katia's doppelganger form from becoming a liability or joining Carl. Donut's protective rage is a turning point: she chooses Katia over her idol Hekla, showing how deep the bonds of their small group have grown.
