# Chapter 419: Chapter 20 — Prison Pocket

## Summary
- **Boss Encounter**: The racers reach a checkpoint blocked by two giant kangaroo-like bosses, **Prison Pocket** (Level 170 Province Boss) and **Party Foul**. Vehicles and mounts cannot pass until they are defeated.
- **The Joeys' Pouch**: The bosses have pouches containing dead joeys that died of starvation. Carl notices a clue: their inventory consists only of their skins.
- **Upgrade Engineer**: A human dressed in a joey skin acts as an upgrade mechanic trapped inside Prison Pocket's pouch, screaming to be saved.
- **Race Truce**: Genesis (womantaur) and Rapture (womantaur) suggest a temporary truce to defeat the bosses, noting that they don't want Carl's team dead just yet.
- **Tigran Melodrama**: Nico (Tigran) acts highly possessive of Penelope the pig, warning Carl and Donut to stay away from her, prompting an argument with Donut. His partner, Dario, tries to calm him down.
- **Pontiff's Mercy**: Pontiff (Taurin mercenary) participates in the planning and mentions he worked with Corcunda before and knows Bucket Boy.
- **The Fight**: Across the road, Chiyome and the razor foxes fight Party Foul. Chiyome warns in chat that Party Foul heals when it eats people, and its only weak spot is inside the pouch.
