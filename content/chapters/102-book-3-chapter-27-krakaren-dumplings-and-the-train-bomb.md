# Book 3 Chapter 27: Krakaren Dumplings and the Train Bomb

## Summary
Carl, Donut, and Katia use rope to lash Growler Gary’s severed hands onto the console plates of the Rapid-Response Carts. Bautista reports that his group reached their station but lost fifty crawlers to a "black hole" gravity effect over the center of the abyss and collapsing gangways. Carl reviews portal entries in the Cookbook by Milk (6th edition).

The three rescue trains will take 8-10 hours to reach the abyss. To alert the stranded crawlers of the silent carts, Carl mounts alarm traps on them to blast Billboard Hot 100 hits. The first cart, dialed to the Sinopia Line, successfully launches playing **\"Physical\"** by Olivia Newton John. The other carts are dispatched on the Grullo and Mindaro lines, followed 15 minutes later by backup carts tuned to return the crawlers to Trainyard Q. Carl keeps one cart tuned to Trainyard E.

When a horde of stage-three DT monsters approaches, Carl and crew hide in the Downward Dog. Once they pass, Carl rigs their backward-facing Vermillion engine car with hobgoblin dynamite and an impact-detonated hob-lobber on a pole, launching it down the track to smash the horde and seal the tunnel. 

Bautista messages that the rescue carts are working, raining monsters into the abyss. He mentions having "pets" he is embarrassed to explain. Carl's train bomb detonates, killing dozens of mobs and leveling him up to **Level 35** (Explosives skill rises to 11). Meanwhile, a crawler team trying to disable a generator at Station 72 accidentally detonates the soul crystal, killing their entire group. Carl instructs Imani to send a squad to Station 60 to ask Tizquick which platform colors lead to that line to check if the stairwells survived.

Because the tunnel to Station 36 is sealed, ghouls redirect toward Station 75. Carl's team blocks the tunnel with a portal cart tuned to the abyss, dropping the ghouls like lemmings. During the lull, Katia trains with Eva's looted saber, **The Left Fang of the Green Sultan**, which is currently non-magical since it is separated from the right fang.

They test their combat skills against a stage-three DT monster (a Level 22 **Razor Fox**). By stage three, all infected mobs mutate into hippo-sized monstrosities with tentacles, a toothy circular mouth, and transparent jelly skin. Katia forms a sentinel gun tripod using Hekla's crossbow, while Donut and Mongo fire spells. The monster's body acts as living armor, but it explodes when Carl fires explosives into its mouth.

Donut discovers that the explosion released thousands of rice-sized parasites: **Krakaren Crotch Dumplings** (Level 1). The system details that these are clones of the Krakaren designed to grow rapidly. Carl burns them with moonshine. A warning message arrives from the now-deceased crawler **Donita Grace**, warning that millions of these parasites are swarming Station 24.

## Characters Introduced

- **Fannar** — Katia's ex-husband (already introduced)

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[the-iron-tangle-escape-plan]]**: Carl's rapid-response portal carts successfully begin clearing the lines and rescuing the stranded crawlers at the abyss.
- **[[survival-of-the-collapse]]**: The threat of the Krakaren clones propagates across the floor, particularly at Station 24.
- **[[systemic-oppression]]**: The Krakaren clones represent the final, devastating stage of the floor's systemic collapse, transforming all infected mobs into hosts for parasitic expansion.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The introduction of the Krakaren Crotch Dumplings adds a ticking clock to the floor's collapse. The "delirium tremens" infection isn't just a debuff; it is a gestation process. When the host dies or is damaged in stage three, it releases thousands of rapidly growing clones. Donita Grace's final, desperate warning from beyond the grave reveals that Station 24 has become a massive, hyper-dangerous breeding ground, making it a death trap for any crawlers hoping to escape there.
