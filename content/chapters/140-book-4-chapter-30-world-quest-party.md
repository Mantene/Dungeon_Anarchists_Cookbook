# Book 4 Chapter 30: World Quest Party

## Summary
Carl and Donut take flight in the biplane *Nightmare II* to lure Orthrus to Emberus, but the system launches a World Quest to kill the puppy.
- **Worshipping Gods Warnings**: Carl reads Sinjin and Azin's warnings in the Cookbook: worshipping a god provides boons but strict rules, and telling a god to fuck off leads to a fatal smiting.
- **Flight & Luring**: Carl flies the biplane drop bear with Donut. Donut casts *Scroll of Meat Hooks* to lure Orthrus out of Bubble 543's waters into the lacuna, completing the quest.
- **New Group Quest**: A new quest **The Dumber of the Flunkies** begins: they must reunite Orthrus with Emberus (who is outside bubble 18) or be smote by Emberus one by one.
- **The World Quest**: The system launches a World Quest **Get Orthrus**, offering 1 million gold, 5 level-up potions, and a pet monkey named Jimbo to anyone who kills the puppy. Carl and Katia warn all crawlers in chat not to touch the puppy.
- **Quan Ch Attacks**: Orthrus is suddenly attacked and severely injured. Carl spots a crawler flying through the darkness and sniping the puppy: **Quan Ch**. Carl calls for someone to tell Quan Ch to back off.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- [[the-gate-of-the-feral-gods]]: The quest shifts from escaping the bubble to navigating the outside lacuna and managing the fire god's wrath.
- [[systemic-oppression]]: The system launching a World Quest to kill an innocent puppy for gold and a pet monkey highlight the extreme, cruel absurdity of the game show's design.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The contrast between the pure innocence of the giant hell-hound puppy and the ruthless greed of crawlers like Quan Ch, who are willing to sacrifice everyone's lives for a prize, shows how the dungeon systematically strips crawlers of their humanity.
