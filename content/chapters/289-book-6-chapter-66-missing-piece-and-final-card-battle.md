# Chapter 289: Missing Piece and Final Card Battle

## Summary
- **Vinegar Curse**: Ysalte, the Goddess of Hopelessness and Insanity, manifests. Her presence turns all handled potions to vinegar until she is banished.
- **Samantha's Intercession**: Samantha taunts Ysalte. When Ysalte strikes Samantha with her pickaxe, the blow harmlessly bounces off the sex doll head.
- **The World Freezes**: The system freezes the world. Amayon sheds his snake shell but remains **Enslaved** under Ysalte's control.
- **Quest: The Missing Piece (Part 1: The Big O)**: A hybrid world quest is initiated with a 10-minute timer. Crawlers must free Amayon and power him up so he can cast a global return portal spell to save all crawlers.
- **Card Battle Declared**: The system initiates a final card battle: Amayon has 6 totems, while Ysalte commands Sister Ines's remaining deck.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The card battle rules are weaponized by the system AI to resolve a cosmic dispute between a goddess and a demon prince. The requirement to keep the invulnerable goddess's deck from running dry while defeating the demon's totems presents a highly complex tactical puzzle.
