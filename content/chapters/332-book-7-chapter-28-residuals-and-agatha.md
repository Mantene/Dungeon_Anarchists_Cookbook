# Chapter 332: Chapter 28 — Residuals and Agatha

## Summary

Outside the Prism throne room, Carl instructs his pet [[sir-rendlegore]] to stop gnawing on the boots of the duct-taped rhino NPC, [[sergeant-toyotomi]]. Toyotomi is a Level 70 Sai Outpost Guard Mercenary Leader who was traumatized by his squad's deaths during the Desperado Club demon outbreak. Because the Prism castle is too large for him to cast his Edifice Shield spell, Carl reassigns him to defend Shanty Town alongside Li Jun.

Li Jun messages Carl, warning him that [[agatha]] (a Level 23 residual with no class) has been searching for Carl by name and is now drinking at the same bar as [[lucia-mar]] near the Reaver border. 

Shortly after, Zev messages Carl with an urgent warning originating directly from [[prime-minister-glory]] of the Syndicate council: Carl is strictly ordered to ignore and avoid all contact with Agatha. Zev explains that residuals are hyperspatial alien lifeforms inhabiting host bodies to contact the AI and trigger the failsafe. She reveals that while the AI recently withdrew protections on other residuals (killing most of them), it has a "soft spot" for Agatha and spared her life. Zev also confirms that the warlord roundtable tomorrow is mandatory.

Before evacuating the Prism castle, Carl designs a tactical play to sow discord between the Madness and the Dream. He locks the Madness mercenary [[lockjaw]] in the throne room with snacks, triggering a countdown timer. If unoccupied, the throne room and its lands will cede to the Dream. Carl sends the Dream mercenary [[mange]] back to his territory. 

Carl, Donut, and Mongo head out to reinforce the failing northern front: "It's blob-hunting season."

- **Residuals & Hyperspatial Entities** — Alien entities occupying host bodies to influence the dungeon AI into triggering failsafes.
- **Syndicate Panics** — The direct intervention of Prime Minister Glory shows that Agatha's presence and potential contact with Carl is a high-level security threat to the Syndicate.
- **Territorial Discord** — Carl exploits throne room capture timers to force political clashes between the Madness and the Dream.
- **AI Favorites** — The dungeon AI has a protective bias toward both Carl and Agatha.
