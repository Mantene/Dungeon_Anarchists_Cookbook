# Book 2 Chapter 22: The Burgundy Revelation

## Summary
[[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] execute their break-in. Carl places the *Suppurating Eye* letter (the spy camera) on the roof tiles and talks loudly about a fake plan to steal Miss Quill's Beanie Babies. A clockwork Mongo carries the letter and pounces onto the magistrate's roof, drawing the krasue away. Carl detonates the dynamite hidden inside the reception room shelves. 

The blast destroys half the building. Carl is rewarded with the *Assassin* achievement (Bronze Assassin's Box), the *Ultimate Extreme Power* achievement (Platinum Tyrant's Box, making him Magistrate of the settlement), and the *CockBlock* achievement (Silver Quest Box for killing Miss Quill, who was the actual magistrate). 

They explore the collapsed ruins and find a serial killer's room. Upside-down naked female corpses are hanging from the ceiling. A mummified Magistrate Featherfall is lended to a bone dreamcatcher. 

The room is a muted zone. Carl orders Mongo to bite the head off a dark elf corpse to break the curse. The remaining krasue heads wake up and detach. Carl throws a smoke curtain, while Donut leaves the muted zone to cast *Torch*. Carl uses his 50/50 lottery ticket, casting a level-15 Fireball that detonates Remex's body and collapses the room. 

Under the debris, they find [[burgundy]] pinned and charred. Burgundy explains that Miss Quill was the mastermind who used her husband [[remex]] as a capacitor to fuel a three-part spell called **The Final War** to slaughter all non-flying creatures in the city. The krasue were her forced army. Donut kills Burgundy with a *Magic Missile* to grant her mercy before the roof collapses.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The reveal of Miss Quill's Beanie Baby collection as a shield for her genocide plot is a peak Dungeon Crawler Carl twist. Burgundy's tragic request to be executed shows the grim choices forced upon the residents of the crawl, who would rather die than be turned back into puppets.
