# Book 3 Chapter 29: Summoning Toys and Inventory Purges

## Summary
At the Mindaro Line transition, Bautista explains his "babies": collectible stuffed monster figures manufactured by his sponsor, **Jaxbrin Amusements, Ltd.** Pulling the tag acts like a grenade pin, executing a *true summoning* that teleports the creature to fight. Common figures last only 15–30 seconds. Bautista previously summoned a legendary Sage Sprite Lecturer named Atwin, who taught him the summoning mechanics. Bautista owns five legendary figures in *Sheol Glass* cases but is too afraid of the cases' explosive fire traps to open them. Carl advises Bautista to train more with his sword rather than relying on sponsor gifts.

Bautista's group heads for a free Station 72. Tizquick remains at Station 60 assisting crawlers with routing. Carl's team travels on the zomp line, pulling the "E" and "Q" carts behind them to create a safe portal-sandwiched zone. While passing Station 24, they see thousands of baby Krakarens. Carl throws a *Jug o' Boom* to clear them, and Katia uses her crossbow to kill the rest.

They enter the safe room at Station 59 (a Costa Rican restaurant called \"Soda\"). Katia opens her boxes before Mordecai's return:
- **Fan Box**: Gained the **Enchanted Shade Gnoll Riot Forces Crowd Control Shield** (+5% Con, +5 to *Rooted in Place* skill, and upgrades *Rush* to *Crowd Blast*, a cone-damaging ram usable every 5 minutes) and the **Enchanted Shade Gnoll Riot Forces Telescoping Crowd Control Baton** (adjustable length, casts level-5 *Cone of Knockback*).
- Because she cannot equip both the shield and her wrist bracer, Katia gives Carl the **Enchanted Auto Buckler of the Peach Pit** (a wrist-worn buckler that has a 1.5% chance to disarm enemies and potentially transfer their weapon to the wielder's inventory).
- **Benefactor Box**: Gained **The Bolt of Ophiotaurus** (1 of 100), which pauses a deity's invulnerability for 15 seconds if shot directly into their eye. This suggests Katia's sponsor, Princess Formidable, is plotting against her brother, Prince Stalwart (who is currently in the dungeon as the war god Grull).

They watch the daily recap, which shows crawlers getting devoured by the Station 48 province boss. It also reveals a cave stairwell going both up and down at Station 433, which was cleared when Carl teleported the mimic. The show negatively spins Carl's actions, showing him repeatedly butchering Growler Gary for hands without explaining the rescue context.

A system announcement introduces three updates:
1. Parties for Floor 5 are locked to those formed *before* the message ended, cementing Katia in Carl and Donut's group.
2. Premium viewers can now pay to read crawler private chats.
3. Effective immediately, liquids can no longer be stored in inventory without a container.

Mordecai teleports back from his seven-day time-out as a toad, overwhelmed by notifications. Immediately after the announcement, the system resolves the liquid inventory paradox by violently ejecting all the ghoul blood Katia had stored on the train. The room is blasted with gallons of blood, spraying everyone except Katia, who remains perfectly clean.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The introduction of public chats completely changes the survival dynamic: the crawlers' only secure channel of tactical planning is gone, forcing Carl and his allies to communicate through double meanings or spoken word. The Bolt of Ophiotaurus is a massive chekhov's gun, proving that the intergalactic elite (like Princess Formidable) are actively using the crawl to run their own proxy wars and assassinations. The comical blood-explosion of Katia's inventory is a grim reminder of how the system's indifferent programming logic enforces patches with zero regard for crawler dignity.
