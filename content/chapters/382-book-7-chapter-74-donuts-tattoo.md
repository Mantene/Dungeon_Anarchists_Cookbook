# Chapter 382: Chapter 74 — Donut's Tattoo

## Summary
The group discusses the mechanics of Donut's *War Crime* spell. It combines two spells at level 8+ in unpredictable, horrific ways, knocks the caster's health down to 1%, and renders the targets permanently incapacitated and undying (requiring a finisher to actually kill them). 

To ensure the fleshers/symbiotes only pull in enemies and do not harm allies when the massive battle ball is broken, Rosetta proposes combining *Wall of Fire* with *Bijanbi* (a Dinner Bell variant spell extracted from a cleric that drags all targets to a single spot). Since Donut does not have *Bijanbi*, Edgar proposes tattooing it onto her right flank to give it to her immediately. After Carl promises to get a matching ass tattoo later, Donut reluctantly agrees. Edgar shaves a bald patch on her flank and prepares to tattoo a line art image of a dog using a special ink.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **Atrocity Spell Mechanics**: The system rules surrounding Faction Wars and the terrifying constraints of *War Crime*.
- **Sacrifice & Trust**: Donut's willingness to mark her body permanently to save their army, and Carl's solidarity.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The details of the *War Crime* spell highlight the dark, cruel humor of the AI. The system explicitly enjoys suffering and rewards players who creatively inflict it. Donut's anxiety over getting a permanent mark ("a tramp stamp") shows that despite the existential dread, she remains a cat with high standards of fashion and dignity.
