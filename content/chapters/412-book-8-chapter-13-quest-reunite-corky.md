# Chapter 412: Chapter 13 — Quest: Reunite Corky

## Summary
The doors of garages 3 and 2 open, revealing their occupants. 

Team 3 (**Lady Dominators and the Gimp**) consists of two female mantaurs (womantaurs) who walk awkwardly on all fours using boots on their lower hands, and their "gimp." The two womantaurs are **Genesis** (Level 95 Lady Mantaur Dominator, black hair, Southern Kentucky accent) and **Rapture** (Level 95 Lady Mantaur Dominator, curly red hair, chewing tobacco). They wear white racing suits and white cowboy hats and worship Enyo.

Radoslav offers them beer. Rapture shoots a wrist-mounted harpoon bolt to snatch a rolling Busch Light can, shreds it, sniffs it with disgust, and mocks Carl and Donut as "roadkill." The two womantaurs leave for the local bar, **The Hairpin**.

Looking at garage 3, they spot a figure in a black vinyl gimp suit with a zipper mouth standing on a single leg. He is a vertically split half-mantaur who must wear the suit to prevent his organs and brain from spilling out. Chiyome explains that a *Split Personality* spell split him down the middle, and he can only be restored by a fleshmancer.

Donut realizes this is [[dong-quixote]]'s long-lost stripper friend, **Corcunda** (Corky). The discovery triggers a new quest:
- **Quest**: **"I have dreamed thee too long"** (Part 1 of 2)
- **Goal**: Reunite Dong Quixote with Corcunda. Part 2 will require locating Corcunda's other half, **Porky**, and finding a fleshmancer to fuse them back together.
- **Reward**: Sending two stand-ins of their choice to the Arena on the 11th floor.

Jasha the bugbear wakes up and asks where his beer is.

## Characters

- [[genesis]] — A Level 95 womantaur who insults Carl and Donut.
- [[rapture]] — Uses a harpoon wristbolt to destroy a beer can and behaves aggressively.
- [[corcunda]] — A vertically split half-mantaur stripper who waves at them from the garage.
- [[chiyome]] — Explains half-mantaur biology and the Split Personality spell.
- [[radoslav]] — Offers beer to the womantaurs and calls them dumbasses.
- [[jasha]] — Wakes up from his nap asking for beer.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- [[npc-sentience]] — Corcunda's history as a dancer and his split condition emphasize the tragic, mutated nature of the dungeon's NPCs.
- [[system-exploitation]] / [[crawler-cooperation]] — The quest reward of sending stand-ins to the 11th-floor arena suggests a way to manipulate the next floor's hazards.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The design of the womantaurs is another example of the system's body-horror humor. Carl's instinct to avoid the quest is sensible, but the dungeon forces their hand, proving that they cannot remain passive observers in the lives of their companion mercenaries.
