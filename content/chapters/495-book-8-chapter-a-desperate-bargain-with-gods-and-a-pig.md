# Chapter 495: Chapter 94 — A Desperate Bargain with Gods and a Pig

## Summary
The chapter begins with a chillingly calm System Message announcing the arrival of [[scolopendra]], the Level 500 Dungeon Boss, in addition to the Level 235 Floor Boss, [[krakaren-prime]]. The Dungeon AI, sounding uncharacteristically stern, explains the monumental consequences of Carl's suicidal gambit to summon the Dungeon Boss. If [[carl]] kills [[scolopendra]], dungeon level timers will cease, floors will no longer collapse automatically, and the [[ascendency-battles]] will immediately trigger, leading to the dungeon's collapse 12 hours after a winner is crowned—leaving Carl a narrow window to reach Floor 18. If he fails to kill Scolopendra but escapes the floor, he will never be able to exit the dungeon. A critical warning is issued: whether Scolopendra dies or is abandoned, all nine of its remaining attacks will trigger simultaneously.

With 100 seconds until Scolopendra's second attack, Carl initiates "Plan C" (or "Plan Carl"). The massive centipede, the size of an aircraft carrier, begins wrapping itself around the giant tree, causing it to crack and shatter, while Krakaren Prime roars and rains green mist. As the [[rv]] zips across the field, Carl sends a message to [[emberus]], offering proof of his son's killer in exchange for a meeting, which Emberus immediately accepts.

Carl then finds [[penelope]] (Penny), the pig he made sapient, inside the truck. Despite feeling a pang of guilt over her predicament, comparing her to the [[the-hoarder]], he straps her into the special quadruped backpack designed for [[princess-donut]]. He gives the struggling pig "Splooge," which calms her and she declares her craving for more. With Penny secured, Carl pulls out his father's gun and yells a challenge to [[taranis]], threatening to shoot Penny in the head if Taranis doesn't show himself in "Club Scolopendra."

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
This chapter throws the entire Dungeon Crawler Carl narrative into an unprecedented level of chaos and desperation. The introduction of Scolopendra, the actual Dungeon Boss, and the calm, almost paternal tone of the Dungeon AI are incredibly unsettling, making the situation feel even more dire than Carl's usual frantic encounters. The rules laid out for Scolopendra's engagement are a masterclass in dungeon design, creating a no-win scenario where both killing and abandoning the boss lead to catastrophic outcomes, underscoring the relentless [[systemic-oppression]] of the crawl.

Carl's moral struggle with Penny is a poignant moment that grounds the fantastical stakes in human (or, in this case, pig) empathy. Despite his past brutal actions and the constant pressure to survive, he still feels the weight of turning an innocent animal into a sentient being for his own purposes. This internal conflict highlights his core humanity, even as he executes a plan that involves holding a gun to Penny's head and using her as leverage against a god. The introduction of "Splooge" as a calming agent is a classic Dinniman touch, providing a moment of absurd humor that momentarily cuts through the intense tension, only to be immediately followed by Carl's audacious challenge to Taranis, demonstrating his willingness to use any means necessary to secure his objectives.
