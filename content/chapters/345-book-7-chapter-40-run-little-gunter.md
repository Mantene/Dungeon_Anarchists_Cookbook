# Chapter 345: Chapter 40 — Run, Little Günter, Run

## Summary
[[carl|Carl]] opens his 1914 box and receives a crying, Austrian-accented spellbook named *Run, Little Günter, Run*. The spell summons a child-like hominid creature named Little Günter with a single hit point. Attacking Günter stores the attack, and he runs toward a designated target, triggering the stored damage upon impact or timeout. Carl learns the spell.

[[katia-grim|Katia]] drains buffs from hats for [[princess-donut|Donut]] (the tiara now has 23 buffs). Katia then uses a toraline yam potion mixed with [[li-na|Li Na's]] blood to tattoo Carl's right arm with a chain-dragon design. This temporary tattoo grants Carl Li Na's level-13 Chain Dragon skill for the rest of the floor, though Li Na temporarily loses 5 levels. Carl feels guilty about Li Na and Li Jun's eye.

[[edgar|Edgar]] the tortoise scout uses his Level 15 *Sense Movement* and *Part of the Background* skills to monitor the nagas. [[zev|Zev]] messages that the Warlord Council is going ahead, broadcasted in real time. Juice Box will join virtually, and Ferdinand will be in Carl's room. Carl prepares to teleport, marking the official launch of **Operation: Snake Pit**.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The introduction of the *Run, Little Günter, Run* spell adds another chaotic tool to Carl's arsenal, allowing him to deliver stored high-damage attacks remotely. The tattoo system shows how the crawlers are exploiting the toraline yam loopholes to pool their resources and skills. Katia's role as a tattoo artist highlights her support, but also the dangerous burden of the contracts forcing her and Donut into a potential conflict.
