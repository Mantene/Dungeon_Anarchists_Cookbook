# Book 6 Chapter 17: Siren and Childbirther

## Summary
The goddesses [[yemaya]] and [[eileithyia]] award gifts and boons, [[bomo]] is teleported to Floor 9, and Eileithyia offers Carl a secret deal.
- **Siren and Midwife Appearances**: Yemaya appears as a siren with dual fish tails (resembling the Starbucks logo). Eileithyia, a pregnant, multi-armed, multi-eyed alien sponsored by [[huanxin-jinx]] (CEO of Icon Industries, Grixist Swarm), manifests next to her.
- **Yemaya's Gifts**: Yemaya gifts Donut the Mythical card [[the-thief]], which steals an opponent's card in T'Ghee combat.
- **Bomo's Departure**: Yemaya offers Bomo a wish for constructing her shrine. Bomo asks to be reunited with his friend "The Sledge" on the ninth floor (Larracos). Yemaya teleports him there immediately, leaving Carl and Donut without their key defender.
- **Eileithyia's Secret Bargain**: Eileithyia grants a floor boon (+10% Constitution, 10% Healing speed, and protection from possession) and issues a public quest to kill spiders. However, she hides a secret deity message within the quest: she wants Carl to accept [[odette]] as Floor 9 Adjutant. If he does, Eileithyia will provide the votes to turn off crawler protections (enabling Carl to hunt the hunters) and cure [[katia-grim]]'s [[crown-of-the-sepsis-whore]] curse. In exchange, she expects Carl, Donut, and Katia to help her claim the Celestial Throne on Floor 12. Eileithyia warns Carl that if he speaks of this deal to anyone (including Donut, [[mordecai]], or Odette), she will destroy everyone he loves. Carl scratches his arm to agree.
- **Party Dissolution**: [[sister-ines-quiteria]] loots Anton's body and silently abandons Carl and Donut, walking out of the graveyard temple.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **Deity Ambitions**: Eileithyia's secret deal reveals she is plotting to seize the Celestial Throne on Floor 12 using the crawlers as pawns.
- **Tactical Setbacks**: Bomo's abrupt departure removes a vital shield from the party.
- **Secret Alignments**: Carl is forced to keep a massive secret from Donut and Mordecai to protect them and save Katia.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Eileithyia's proposal represents a significant escalation in the faction game. By blackmailing Carl with Katia's curse and the threat of violence, she forces him into a double agent role where he must deceive his closest allies. The contrast between her benevolent, motherly persona in myth and her cold-blooded calculations as a sponsored deity highlights the corrupting nature of the system.
