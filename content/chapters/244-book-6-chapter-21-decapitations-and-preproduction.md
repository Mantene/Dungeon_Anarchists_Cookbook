# Book 6 Chapter 21: Decapitations and Preproduction

## Summary
Carl and Donut attend a secure preproduction meeting with the showrunners and other Faction Wars representatives, which is violently disrupted by an NPC rebel.
- **Secure Warning**: At the underwater production facility, [[zev]] initiates a secure channel (acting as a sponsor conversation hidden from standard feeds) to warn Carl and Donut about selecting [[odette]] as their Floor 9 Faction Wars Adjutant. If they do, [[mordecai]] will inevitably attack her, violating system rules and triggering his ejection, which would also result in the safehouse attendants (including the changeling children) being exiled.
- **Preproduction Meeting**: Carl and Donut are escorted to a round table by U56KL ("Urkel"), a Mexx robot. They represent the *Princess Posse* (Team #3).
- **The Competitors**: The meeting is attended by various faction leaders and representatives:
  - **Cascadia**: Zev's boss, a highly stressed, rebreather-wearing Kua-tin who is on the verge of a breakdown.
  - **Crown Prince Stalwart**: The angry orc Warlord of the orcs (Team #1), who threatens to skin Donut and use her pelt.
  - **Voting Admin Cell Mass Number Three**: A three-tiered green blob representing the Operatic Collective.
  - **Rishi**: A four-armed naga representing the Blood Sultanate (Team #4, the team they must destroy). He is polite but warns that Donut is technically in the succession line.
  - **Empress D'Nadia**: The friendly Saccathian Warlord of the Prism Kingdom Clan (Team #5).
  - **Epitome Tagg**: The wealthy dream elf representing Team #6, whose company controls solar-system food operations.
  - **Luke**: A surfer-like representative from the democratic Lemig Sortion (Team #7).
  - **Viceroy of the Madness**: A silent, masked representative representing the necromantic team "The Madness" (Team #8).
- **Juice Box's Breach**: Suddenly, the Viceroy is decapitated. His head rolls onto the table, and [[juice-box]] (the rebel crawler-attendant soul) reveals herself, possessing the body of [[epitome-noflex]] (Epitome Tagg's mother NPC). She twirls the Viceroy's mask and taunts the executives. Cascadia refuses to intervene, noting that Juice Box is an independent NPC and the system AI forbids administrative interference.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **NPC Rebellion**: Juice Box's violent intervention proves that the system's NPCs are actively resisting and that the AI is allowing them to break narrative boundaries.
- **Corporate Distancing**: Zev explains that the Valtay Corporation is stepping back from day-to-day operations to protect investor confidence, leaving the Borant Corporation to take the fall for the season's chaos.
- **Odette's Adjutant Trap**: The warning about selecting Odette highlights a trap designed to force a confrontation between Mordecai and his former manager.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The preproduction meeting exposes the corporate structure of Faction Wars, showing how faction leaders view the crawl as a highly profitable game of chess while treating the crawlers themselves as minor inconveniences. Cascadia's breakdown and Prince Stalwart's impotent threats show that the showrunners are losing their grip on the narrative. Juice Box's spectacular entrance—using the body of Tagg's mother to decapitate a viceroy—is a direct act of terror that highlights the growing instability of the system.
