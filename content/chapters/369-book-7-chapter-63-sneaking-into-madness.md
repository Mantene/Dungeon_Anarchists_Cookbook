# Chapter 369: Sneaking into Madness

## Summary
[[carl]] and [[elle-mcgibbons]] land at the edge of the woods in the Madness territory, hidden under Elle's Subterfuge spell. The flat prairies before them are covered in a dense grid of traps, alarm traps, teleport traps, and summons. In the distance, [[eris]] and Meatus continue their noisy struggle, and the Dream castle collapses.

Carl summons [[katia-grim]], [[imani-c]], and [[splash-zone]] using teleport traps. However, the trap intended for [[holger]] is instead triggered by [[dong-quixote]], who arrives mounted on the swamp yak Gonk. Dong also brings [[kandy-newton]] (Bigs) riding pillion. Dong explains that Holger stayed behind to protect the children and Miss Nadine, and that they realized a mount could convey all its riders through a single teleport trap.

Despite Carl's annoyance at having a giant yak in a stealth operation, Dong insists he is an expert yakman and that Gonk has a light touch. Carl orders them to stay invisible, follow behind him, and disarm only alarm traps while Carl collects the active summon traps (such as fire ants).

In Reaver territory, the portal times out. Samantha is cornered by the Feral God **Yarilo**, who was banished from the Nothing because he disrupted one of [[apito]]'s parties. Yarilo is sexually attracted to animals and hates Samantha for pretending to be him to escape. 

Carl makes Samantha an officer to grant her the *Paint the Target* spell, directing her to paint the bleached drake (dragon) circling the Reaver castle so the missile crew ([[the-sledge]] and [[bomo]]) can shoot it down to protect Louis's upcoming bomb run. Boomer admonishes Carl to focus on his own Madness operation and stop micromanaging. 

As they prepare to advance on the Madness castle, the portal behind them crackles out, and Yarilo enters the realm.

## Characters
* [[carl]]: Coordinates the minefield crossing, summons his assault team, collects active summon traps, and manages Samantha's target-painting.
* [[elle-mcgibbons]]: Maintains the Subterfuge spell and guides the group.
* [[katia-grim]] & [[imani-c]]: Summoned to the frontline, crossing their arms in frustration at Carl's micromanagement.
* [[splash-zone]]: Summoned to assist.
* [[dong-quixote]]: Arrives riding Gonk (the swamp yak) with his crust sock flail and a long war lance.
* [[kandy-newton]] (Bigs): Arrives with Dong, eager to "get her chop on."
* [[samantha]]: Stays in Reaver territory, paints the drake, and confronts Yarilo.
* [[louis-santiago-2]]: Prepares to take off for his second bomb run.
* [[boomer]]: Orders Carl to focus on the Madness castle.

## Themes & Plot Threads
* **Improvised Tactics**: Dong and Bigs exploiting the mount mechanics of the teleport traps shows the crawlers' ingenuity.
* **The Interconnected Nothing**: Yarilo's arrival and connection to Apito's past parties hints at the shared history of the Nothing's residents.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's collection of active summon traps is a clever exploitation of the game's mechanics. Rather than setting them off, keeping them in his inventory turns them into pocket deployments for later use. Meanwhile, Boomer's warning is a necessary reality check: Carl's anxiety makes him try to control every piece of the board, which risks distracting him from his own critical objective.
