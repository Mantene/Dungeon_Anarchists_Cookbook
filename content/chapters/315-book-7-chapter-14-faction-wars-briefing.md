# Chapter 315: Faction Wars Briefing

## Summary

[[baroness-victory]] delivers a comprehensive briefing on Faction Wars rules in the flag room. The war has four phases: Ceasefire (current), Ramp-Up (180 hours, no offensive magic or teleportation), Open Hostilities, and the Peeling of Larracos. During the Ramp-Up, ingress into enemy territory is only through narrow kilometer-wide gates. Key spells like Donut's Puddle Jumper and the Gate of the Feral Gods won't work, though Carl's Gloom Wraith Phase will since it's a skill, not a spell.

Victory explains that most enemy mercenaries are security forces with limited real battle experience—they'll look for an overwhelming knockout punch. She describes the enemy's strategy: mass at the corridors, use superior numbers and weapons to crush the Princess Posse before magic returns. The team has two entry points to defend. Florin details trench construction and fallback positions.

[[samantha]], [[louis-santiago-2]], [[katia-grim]], and others arrive. Samantha is upset about missing the wedding. [[bonnie]] the gnome girl reveals she's been helping with the airship. Bonnie delivers a chilling line about outworlders when Carl suggests she shouldn't think about fighting. The chapter establishes the overwhelming military challenge ahead with 100,000+ enemies massed at the borders.

- **Four Phases of War** — Ceasefire → Ramp-Up → Open Hostilities → Peeling of Larracos
- **Magic Suppression** — Offensive magic and teleportation banned during Ramp-Up
- **Enemy Analysis** — Mercenaries are security forces, not warriors—they want fast, easy victory
- **Children in War** — Bonnie's chilling maturity represents how the dungeon has aged its youngest
- **Defensive Strategy** — Two corridors, trenches, fallback positions, and watchtowers

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections

Victory's briefing transforms the abstract threat into a concrete military challenge. The magic ban during Ramp-Up is devastating for the Princess Posse, who rely heavily on spells. Bonnie's line—"Have they stopped thinking about trying to kill me?"—is one of the most unsettling moments, showing how war has consumed even the children. The math is brutal: defending two fronts against 100,000+ enemies with no offensive magic.
