# Chapter 286: Personal Space Upgrade and Doggie Door

## Summary
- **Meadowlark Reunion**: The Meadowlark crawlers—including Imani, Katia, Elle, Louis, Britney, Bautista, Tran, Li Jun, Li Na, Zhang, and Chris—reunite with Carl and Donut in Cuba.
- **The Doggie Door Upgrade**: The group traveled using a [[tier-3-doggie-door|Personal Space Upgrade: Tier 3 Doggie Door]] module, which enables fast travel to previously utilized doors. 
- **Lucia Mar's Actions**: Lucia Mar left five of these modules for the crawlers before descending to Floor 9. In Beijing, she killed Fang's team for a key and left a message warning that she plans to kill Donut and Mongo on the next floor.
- **Reflections on Loneliness**: Carl experiences a breakthrough, realizing that isolation and loneliness are the primary weapons the dungeon uses to break crawlers, and that solidarity is their ultimate counter.
