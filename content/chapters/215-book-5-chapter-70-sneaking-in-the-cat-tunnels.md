# Book 5 Chapter 70: Sneaking in the Cat Tunnels

## Summary
- **Cat Tunnels**: Louis explains that the castle has secret cat-sized tunnels built by [[ferdinand]] to spy. [[samantha]] sneaks through them to the third-floor security room.
- **Airship Crash**: Sledge's airship is overrun by mantises. He decides to crash the *Twister* into the castle's sentinel towers to destroy them and protect [[bonnie]] and the bear cubs.
- **Dead Guards**: Samantha reaches the security room but finds the guards already dead and the crystals for Ballroom B already pulled. Queen [[imogen]] suddenly appears.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Sledge's sacrifice of the airship is a brutal but necessary action to clear the turrets. The pre-killed security guards suggest another faction is acting independently in the castle.
