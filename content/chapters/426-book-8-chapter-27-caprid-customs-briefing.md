# Chapter 426: Chapter 27 — Caprid Customs Briefing

## Summary
- **The Greenroom**: Carl and Donut are teleported to a production facility greenroom on the ocean floor to prepare for their live appearance on the show **Plenty of Plenty**.
- **Lexis' Return**: They find Lexis (Odette's former production assistant) working on loan to Borant. She is terrified and visibly shaking.
- **Orbital Rebellion**: Lexis reveals that the **Open Intellect Action Network** (OIAN, the Faction Wars insurgents) achieved total system domination in a single day. They boarded and captured every major ship in orbit using enhancement-zone weaponry, which works now that the AI has escaped containment.
- **Florida Skirmish**: The Syndicate's temporary headquarters (led by Prime Minister Victory) landed on the Eastern Seaboard (Florida, USA) in an area they thought was empty, but they were immediately attacked by surviving Earth natives.
- **Katia's Status**: Katia is safe in South Asia (OIAN territory), but a confused "third party" has appeared and is protecting the kinder facility.
- **Caprid Interlocution**: Lexis briefs them on Caprid (goat-like) customs. They will sit in the center of a roundtable called an "interlocution" and be judged. If judged worthy, the Caprids will ask a question, which is a compliment, and they must answer and then "exert" themselves.
