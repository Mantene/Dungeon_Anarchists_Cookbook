# Chapter 392: Chapter 84 — Heart Transplant

## Summary
Emberus gives the crawlers 66 minutes to deliver Samantha, triggering the team quest *This is How the Gristle Grinds*. Carl orders Katia to consume the *Orchid of Eileithyia* to summon Huanxin Jinx, rendering Katia unconscious in a 1% health coma. 

To save Louis, the team performs a rapid, high-stakes heart and lung transplant. Because Princess Lunette's body is a naiad, Mordecai grafts a kua-tin rebreather into her bronchial system so the organs can function in a land-dwelling host. Carl and Tipid hold Louis's chest cavity open. Donut enrages Warlord Tagg in the chat, renaming herself "EPITOME TAGG'S SEXY MOTHER" to distract him from shutting down the biomechanical organs before they can be removed. 

The transplant is successful; Louis wakes up with gills on his neck, breathing through the new naiad lungs. Mongo retrieves and eats the discarded biomechanical heart and lungs, killing Warlord Tagg and earning the kill credit. The Dream is defeated.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The heart transplant is a masterclass in tension, combining the countdown of Emberus's wrath with a grotesque surgical operation and Donut's hilarious trolling of a corporate CEO in chat. Tagg's death via Mongo eating his discarded heart/lungs is a darkly satisfying end for one of Faction Wars' most arrogant antagonists.
