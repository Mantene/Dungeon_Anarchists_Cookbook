# Chapter 301: Chapter 2 — Feral Gods Gate

## Summary
- **Sniper Gear**: Donut examines her Deathbed Hellcat class items: a ghillie suit (Cone of Silence) and a boonie hat (targeting and range boosts).
- **Mordecai's New Form**: Mordecai appears as a Canadian game manager wearing a snowsuit and lumberjack gear. He explains the conditions that could end the ceasefire early (unanimous vote, warlord withdrawal, or all strongholds locked).
- **Carl's Achievements**: Carl reviews his Floor 8 achievements:
  - *Lord of the Flies* (teleport trap PK, gets Gold Conch Box)
  - *Demons Hate This One Trick* (beach teleport)
  - *Chickenshit Exploit* (Tserendolgor Box)
  - *Celestial Loot* (Loot Punch Card for Quan's Cloak)
  - *Used Pet* (for Garret/Tummy Acher)
  - *Uh oh. It talks* (sapient weapon sock earplugs)
  - *Touched by a Demon* (Dio cassette, Celestial Heavy Metal Box)
  - *Crab Chowder* (Raul)
  - *Unbirthing* (Samantha teleport, gets Legendary Spicy Box)
- **Emberus Quest Update**: Carl's quest for [[emberus]] updates: he must travel to the Temple of Issitoq in Club Vanquisher to get permission to break the truce, and obtain Apito's Soul Crystal.
