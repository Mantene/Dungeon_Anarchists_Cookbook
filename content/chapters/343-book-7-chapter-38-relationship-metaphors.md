# Chapter 343: Chapter 38 — Relationship Metaphors

## Summary
The system AI avatar (using [[growler-gary|Gary's]] body) insists on having a "time-out" to seek relationship advice. The AI teleports [[princess-donut|Princess Donut]] into the room. Donut is startled to see Gary and quickly critiques [[orren|Orren's]] fashion sense and [[carl|Carl's]] naivety regarding girlfriends.

Gary presents a convoluted metaphor: he is seeing a new "chick" ([[agatha|Agatha]]) who is teaching him new things, but she has a massive "ex-boyfriend who could rip a phone book in half" (a Primal/sleeping entity) who is currently in "prison" (contained). He wonders if she is using him to fight the ex, or to make him jealous. Furthermore, the ex-boyfriend's "older girlfriend" is also in the picture, giving Gary pointers on how to get stronger so he will kill the ex-boyfriend. The two women hate each other but want the same thing.

Donut advises Gary to be careful, as it sounds like the current girlfriend is using him. She recommends staying out of it until he knows if the ex is good or bad. Appreciative of the advice, the AI declares the game will go on as planned, and Gary suddenly collapses. 

The real Growler Gary awakens in a heavily intoxicated state, confused, asks for Rory, and teleports away. Carl, Donut, and Orren are left in stunned silence.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
This chapter uses a bizarre relationship metaphor to describe the cosmic power struggle happening behind the scenes of Dungeon Crawler World. The "chick" is Agatha, who is guiding the AI. The "ex-boyfriend in prison" represents the Primals or the Apothecary, who are trapped. The "older girlfriend" is likely the Bedlam Bride or another ancient entity who is providing Carl (or the AI) with powerups to kill the Primals. Donut's advice—to stay out of it and refuse to be used—is ironic, given that the crawlers are trapped in the center of this very conflict.
