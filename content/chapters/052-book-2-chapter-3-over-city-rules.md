# Book 2 Chapter 3: Over City Rules

## Summary
[[carl]], [[princess-donut]], and Mongo (a Level 1 Royal Steed velociraptor) leave the guildhall and step out onto the wooden streets of the Over City. [[mordecai]] explains the structure and rules of Floor 3. The crater of a massive volcano spans 300,000 square kilometers, hosting small medieval villages separated by vast, dangerous ruins. 

Day and night cycles are active: villages are safe during the day, and only main streets are safe at night; the ruins are populated by mobs that grow far more dangerous at night. The village guards are Level 75. 

Carl checks a concealed map legend (concealed rock tooltip), which outlines directions and distances to Exits, Saferooms, and nearby settlements. They decide to travel 34 kilometers east to a medium-sized village to access better shops and the tutorial guild.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[over-city-mechanics]]**: Introduces the day/night cycle, village guards, and layout of Floor 3.
- **[[survival-of-the-collapse]]**: Carl and Donut begin their trek through the ruins to reach the next safe zone.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The revelation that NPCs have their memories wiped and altered every season to fit custom Syndicate storylines emphasizes the cruelty of the dungeon. It shows that both the planetary natives and the crawlers are treated as disposable props for intergalactic entertainment.
