# Chapter 363: Achievements and Quests

## Summary
[[carl]] meets up with [[li-jun]] and [[zhang]] as they head toward the hidden brig complex near the Nest tower. Li Jun has equipped a new celestial-tier mechanical eye, **The Engine of Tomorrow**. The black, clockwork eye allows him to see muscles tensing and temporarily slow his perception of time (akin to "Bullet Time"), enabling him to predict and react to attacks with extreme speed.

Carl reviews the 32 loot boxes and over 50 achievements he received after the raid on Club Vanquisher:
* **Varg Vikernes!**: Permanently bans Carl from all temples except [[emberus]], and those where he is "friend of the church" (including [[apito]], [[eileithyia]], [[eris]], [[grull]], [[hellik]], [[issitoq]], [[nekhebit]], [[theia]], and [[yemaya]]).
* **Liquified.**: Awarded for blowing himself up while immortal. Rewards a **Legendary Lucky Bitch Box** containing the *Lucky Shot* patch (deflects a fatal blow to a party member once per floor). Carl decides not to wear it to protect Donut.
* **You in my house now, boy!**: For fighting a god ([[khepri]]) in their own temple. Saved by Hellik, leaving him marked for death by Khepri.
* **Celestial Orgy of Chaos!**: For surviving the presence of ten gods at once. Rewards a **Celestial Who Let the Gods Out This Time?** box.
* **Hey Larry, can I borrow your chainsaw?**: For using a borrowed skill (Li Na's *Profane Iron*) to kill an opponent. Rewards a **Platinum** box containing a Pugilism skill potion, raising his skill to 15.

Carl receives a new quest and message from his sponsor god, Emberus:
* **Quest: Fuck my brother. Fuck him to the fires of Sheol.**: Kill 100 worshipers of Hellik before the collapse of the 11th floor. Reward: Celestial quality permanent boon.
* **Emberus's Message**: Confusingly warns Carl that Hellik treated with [[issitoq]] to breach the ceasefire, allowing Emberus to kill other Sheol princes (such as Geyrun) in exchange for information from [[amayon]]. Emberus warns that he loves Carl but may turn his rage and grief on him if Carl succeeds in killing Hellik.

Zhang shows Carl his celestial prize, **Zhang's Cape of Odds** (+15% to all stats, level 10 Dodge, and a once-per-hour *Roll the Dice* skill with 333 outcomes, 66 of which are bad, including summoning the god who hates him most. After 19 spins, he can pick the outcome).

Finally, Carl notes that he gave his extra **Ring of Divine Suffering** to [[li-na]] after she and [[princess-donut]] strongly requested it. The group enters the brig to confront their lone remaining prisoner.

## Characters
* [[carl]]: Reviews his achievements and Emberus's warnings, struggles with the moral trap of his god quests, and prepares to interrogate the naga prisoner.
* [[li-jun]]: Equips and trains with his new celestial eye, "The Engine of Tomorrow."
* [[zhang]]: Shows off his "Cape of Odds" and assists in guarding the brig.
* [[li-na]]: Received Carl's extra Ring of Divine Suffering, making her even more formidable.
* [[princess-donut]]: Urged Carl to give the ring to Li Na.
* [[emberus]]: Sends Carl a contradictory, threatening, and affectionate message regarding the quest to kill his brother Hellik.

## Themes & Plot Threads
* **Divine Manipulations**: Emberus's warning that he will avenge his brother's death on Carl, even though he demands Carl kill him, highlights the double-binds imposed by the gods.
* **The Burden of Power**: The celestial items (the "Lucky Shot" patch, the "Cape of Odds," and the "Ring of Divine Suffering") offer incredible advantages but carry dangerous, self-destructive risks.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Emberus's message exposes the deep dysfunction and tragic nature of the Sheol pantheon, where grief and rage are indistinguishable. Carl's decision to hide the "Lucky Shot" patch shows his protective instinct; he would rather face death himself than allow the system to redirect a fatal blow onto Donut or his friends.
