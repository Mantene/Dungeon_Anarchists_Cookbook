# Book 6 Chapter 15: Resurrecting Yemaya

## Summary
In a Celestial Thorn Room, Carl makes a critical choice that resurrects the goddess Yemaya, defeats Asojano, and summons the wrathful god Ogun.
- **Katia's Warning**: Before the battle begins, [[katia-grim]] sends Carl a private message. She reveals she is wearing the cursed *Crown of the Sepsis Whore* and warns him to ignore all other options and repair the shrine of [[yemaya]]. Resurrecting Yemaya is the first step in a complex process to save both Katia and Donut.
- **The Celestial Thorn Room Choices**: The crawlers enter a Celestial Thorn Room containing seven shrines and one *Sun of Reawakening*. They must choose one shrine to repair:
  - *Shrine 1: Ogun* (prevents smites for Paz/Anton, no other benefit).
  - *Shrine 2: Legba* (smiting protection, Legba ascends).
  - *Shrine 3: Shango* (lightning spell, Shango ascends).
  - *Shrine 4: Oshun* (fatal blow protection, Oshun ascends).
  - *Shrine 5: Asojano* (instantly completes quest without a fight, Asojano remains mortal).
  - *Shrine 6: Inle* (health-seep immunity, Inle returns to Nothing).
  - *Shrine 7: Yemaya* (Yemaya ascends, gifts each squad a special card, and her friend Eileithyia grants a boon).
- **Yemaya's Ascension**: Guided by Katia's warning, Carl chooses to repair Yemaya's shrine. This choice triggers a boss battle with [[asojano]], who attacks and floods the temple with Ghommids.
- **Boss Defeat**: The crawlers defeat Asojano, curing the Ghommids and completing the quest. Yemaya successfully re-ascends. Carl is infected with *SlugPox* (spawning Level 1 Pox Slugs).
- **Ogun's Appearance**: Because they bypassed Ogun's shrine, the god [[ogun]] manifests physically in the Havana region. Anton Lopez weeps with fear, crying that it is too late.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[pueblo-de-los-olvidados]]**: Climax and resolution of the cemetery quest, ending in Ghommid cure and Asojano's defeat.
- **Deity Conflict**: Resurrecting Yemaya directly defies Ogun, bringing him down to the floor as an active threat.
- **Katia's Curse**: Introduction of the *Crown of the Sepsis Whore* plotline.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's decision to trust Katia's warning and prioritize Yemaya's ascension over immediate party safety (avoiding Ogun's smite or Asojano's combat) shows his long-term strategic thinking. By selecting Yemaya, he secures a future path to cleanse the *Crown of the Sepsis Whore*, but at the price of summoning the hostile, ascended god Ogun directly into their starting region.
