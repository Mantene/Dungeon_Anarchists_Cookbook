# Chapter 461: Li Na's Escape

## Summary
In the guild common room, [[li-na]] prepares to be sent to the [[pineapple-cabaret|Pineapple Cabaret]] via a cleaner bot. Her brother Jun is dead, and she is being sent to test the escape route and ensure the war mages don't betray the crawlers. She is placed in an XL pet carrier by [[zhang]], which is then sucked into the white cleaner bot named **Suckira** (named by [[louis-santiago-2]]). [[princess-donut]] lists the cleaner bot for sale at the terminal, and [[herot]] (in the Pineapple Cabaret) purchases it, successfully transferring [[li-na]] out of the active dungeon area. [[mordecai]] briefly experiences the "Oak Fell" personality again. A cactus plant that [[li-na]] cast a dread trap on is kept in [[carl]]'s room as an indicator of her status. [[prepotente]] examines the pet carrier and expresses interest in linking his personal space to the guild.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Li Na's confession of love to Zhang before entering the carrier is a bittersweet moment. It highlights the desperation of the crawlers on the 10th floor, choosing to jump into a literal void rather than face the inevitable slaughter of the remaining races.
