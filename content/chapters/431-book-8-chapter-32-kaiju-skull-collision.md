# Chapter 431: Chapter 32 — Kaiju Skull Collision

## Summary
- **Tem's Beetle Skull**: The racers approach a three-story beetle skull blocking the road, belonging to **Tem**, a deity from the Nothing who was killed by Odette.
- **Drugging Crash**: Gluteus Maxx passes out at the wheel from his overdose. Donut breaks free of her chair's straps, crawls under Gluteus' legs, and slams the accelerator.
- **Pasta Shield**: Donut casts *Temporary Macaroni*, creating a defensive tunnel of raw, giant dried cannelloni tubes of pasta that shields the truck from the falling Sugar Hermits.
- **Mercenary Deaths**: Both hired ghouls die during the descent: the passenger-side one is crushed, and the driver-side one has his head ripped off by a flying Sugar Hermit.
- **Riding the Hood**: Carl climbs out of the shattered windshield and lies across the hood of the truck, steering by holding the wheel through the open windshield frame.
- **The Finish Line Clash**: Dwight (Team Sparkles) fires their vine-mount cannon at the food truck. Carl steers to avoid it, flipping the truck. Carl activates the *Scavenger's Daughter* wrath, and his feet collide with Tem's beetle skull, causing it to explode.
- **Wreckage and Results**: All three vehicles (the food truck, the GTO, and the vine) spin across the finish line simultaneously. Carl's kangaroo suit is flayed off in the crash. Carl heals himself and checks on the survivors. Corky (the gimp suit half of Corcunda) is found injured but alive.
- **Heat Three Standings**: First Place: The Royal Court of Princess Donut. Second Place: Team Sparkles. Third Place: Lady Dominators. Fourth Place: Team Free Love. Fifth Place: One Fine Pig. The Wild Hunt (Chiyome's team) and the Jugglers are eliminated.
