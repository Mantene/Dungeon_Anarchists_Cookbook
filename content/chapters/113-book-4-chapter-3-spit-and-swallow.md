# Book 4 Chapter 3: Spit and Swallow

## Summary
The party upgrades their safe room and begins gathering the crawlers in Hump Town.
- **Safe Room Upgrades**: They install food synthesizers (which grant a **Full** buff: +5% healing speed, -5% debuff duration, -5% indirect damage) and a marketplace interface (placed on Donut's account for a Charisma discount). Mordecai warns them against using the marketplace while carrying *The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook* to avoid drawing faction attention due to the book's 50-million-gold tag.
- **Spit and Swallow**: Carl, Donut, and Katia visit the Spit and Swallow bar to find crawlers. They encounter **Firas M** (Level 22 Hammersmith) and **Louis Santiago 2** (Level 22 Pest Exterminator), who are drunk and paying changeling prostitutes to mimic Jessica Rabbit. Donut threatens them with expulsion from Hump Town if they don't cooperate.
- **Day/Night Cycles**: A Dromedarian NPC explains the dual-sun mechanics:
  - Taranis (the yellow sun) is up for 22 hours; Hellik (the red sun) is up for 8 hours, causing extreme heat.
  - The storm occurs when Hellik catches Taranis.
  - The Red Equinox in 11 days will double the storm and dark periods.
- **Helsinki Archers**: They meet **Langley** (Level 24) and his party of five other Finnish car salesmen, who all chose the Archer class. Langley's party is cooperative and agrees to meet.
- **Thorny Devil Encounter**: Outside the town, the party fights a **Male Thorny Devil** (Level 34 lizard-class mob). Carl's Extinction Sigil tattoo increases the damage it inflicts by 20%. They kill it using Donut's enhanced Magic Missiles and a quarter-strength hob-lobber, looting its liver and teeth.
- **Gnomish Drop Bear Attack**: A fast-attack plane, a **Gnomish Drop Bear**, attacks them, dropping two egg-shaped deployment containers. The party teleports away to avoid the bomb, and Carl's full-strength hob-lobber forces the plane to deploy its escape balloon.
- **The Dead Goose**: The dropped egg fails to open because it is old and rusty, crushing the creature inside. Carl inspects it and finds a dead **Feral Goose** (Level 45 Neighborhood Boss). Carl loots a Neighborhood Map, takes the goose's body into his **Mob Morgue** inventory tab, and loots the broken egg shell.
- **Mobility Plan**: With gnomish aircraft patrolling, Carl decides they must return to Hump Town and construct a dune buggy and anti-aircraft weapons.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival-of-the-collapse]]**: The dual-sun and Equinox mechanics put a strict timer on the level.
- **[[the-anarchist-cookbook]]**: The warning about the marketplace interface highlights the risk of using system tools while carrying illegal knowledge.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The dead Feral Goose inside the faulty drop container suggests that the dungeon's automated traps are decaying or poorly maintained, hinting at systemic decline. Carl's decision to construct a dune buggy and anti-aircraft weapon shows his tactical flexibility, turning Floor 4 mechanical knowledge into Floor 5 mobility.
