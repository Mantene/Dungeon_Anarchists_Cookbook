# Book 6 Chapter 32: Killing Astrid and Minge's Wrath

## Summary
A demon breach in the casino throws the Desperado Club into chaos, disrupting the ambush plan, but Carl and Katia corner and brutally kill Astrid in the kitchen.
- **Ambush Setup**: Carl finishes setting up his claymores and teleport traps in the secret corridor. He heads upstairs to place the beacons in a top-floor utility closet.
- **Crawler Confrontation**: On the top floor, Carl encounters four crawlers. He warns them to leave. Three comply, but a drunk **Zebani Ghazi** named **Emir Akbas** (jealous of Carl and part of top crawler [[burcu]]'s family) attacks Carl, throwing a punch that lands. Zeynep (his mage teammate) *Mutes* Emir, and Carl strips his boot and ejects him.
- **The Containment Breach**: Samantha ignores Carl's warnings and enters the middle floor casino. She breaches the Nothing, releasing **Minge** (Level 225 Minor Feral Demon, sister to Slit). Minge is a grotesque, melting pink creature covered in dozens of spindly arms with kite-sized claws. She starts devouring crocodilian guards and NPCs.
- **Astrid's Response**: Astrid flies down the main stairs to deploy "the vorpals" and contain the breach, bypassing Carl's secret corridor. Carl throws a "Material Girl" alarm ball as a distraction and slips into the kitchen.
- **Kitchen Chaos**: The kitchen is a bloodbath as Minge flings torn samurai rhinoceros guards (**Walter the Sai, Level 101**) and cretins into the room. Astrid is panicked. She tells Carl that her husband is **Hamed (the Night Wyrm)** and she was trying to get free to save her children. She begs him for help.
- **The Kill**: Katia enters the kitchen, invisible, and summons her totems: a mossy, Hook-nosed female troll and a skull-faced crow. Astrid boils the troll's blood, but the crow is immune. Katia shifts into a spiked metal canine beast, grabbing Astrid, but hesitates to strike.
- **Ripping the Wings**: To break the deadlock, Carl reaches forward and rips Astrid's wings off (negating her magic). The skull-faced crow pecks out Astrid's eyes, and Katia impales her through the head, killing her. 
- **The Escape**: Katia shifts her face to look like an ogre, growls at the cowering kitchen NPCs to make them believe a monster killed Astrid, and runs back out to the bar with Astrid's body.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **Systemic Lies and Betrayal**: The revelation that Hamed (The Night Wyrm) ordered the assassination of his own wife (Astrid), who was secretly working to save their children, highlights the absolute corruption of the dungeon's relationships.
- **Katia's Breaking Point**: Katia's hesitation before killing Astrid shows her deep emotional exhaustion and moral resistance to the Pk/assassin path.
- **The Screamer's Instability**: Samantha's reckless behavior in the casino demonstrates her growing, chaotic influence, acting as a beacon for Nothing-dwelling demons.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The scene where Carl rips Astrid's wings off is a brutal moment of pragmatic violence. Carl is forced to act as the executioner because Katia is paralyzed by her own conscience. Her hesitation—visualized in her human face screaming in rage and terror within the metallic beast body—shows she is spiritually collapsing. Astrid's final words ("I was almost done... Save my children") reveal that their assassination quest was a tragic setups, engineered by her husband Hamed to eliminate her just as she was on the verge of escaping.
