# Book 6 Chapter 13: Crawler Rosetta's Legacy

## Summary
The interview with Rosetta delves into Carl's past and her hidden identity before being terminated by Valtay authorities.
- **The CookBook Backstory**: The chapter features historical notes from the [[the-anarchist-cookbook]]: Crawler [[milk]] (6th Edition) laments the loss of their traditional family migration stew, and Rosetta (9th Edition) replies, promising that the crawlers' pain and words will never be lost.
- **The Interview Continues**: Rosetta presses Carl on his mother's suicide, noting that his father survived the poisoning, child abandonment warrants were issued, and Carl was briefly suspected of murder. Carl notes that Odette's documentary had already exposed these facts.
- **Coded Messages**: Rosetta shifts to talking in code to Carl: "Apparently Carl needs a proper recipe to do his best work." She uses television displays of shows set in Miami to secretly guide their choice of starting location for Phase 2.
- **Harbinger's Intervention**: The Valtay representative Harbinger abruptly shuts down the broadcast and teleports Rosetta away. Harbinger bans Rosetta from future crawler interviews, accusing her and Carl of cheating.
- **Safe Room Reflection**: Back in Havana, Donut regrets Rosetta's ban. Carl reviews his memory of the cookbook, confirming Rosetta's identity and her pledge to Milk to make the galaxy "choke on our pain."

## Characters Introduced
- [[milk]] — 6th-edition crawler and cookbook contributor (mentioned).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **Cookbook Legacy**: Confirms Rosetta's status as a veteran cookbook contributor and co-conspirator.
- **[[systemic-oppression]]**: The Valtay enforce strict censorship, banning former crawlers who try to assist current contestants.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Rosetta's interview is a crucial turning point. It demonstrates that the cookbook authors are not just historical ghosts; they are active agents outside the dungeon trying to guide current crawlers. Rosetta's coded instruction to choose Miami is a lifeline, though it cost her her show and freedom.
