# Chapter 407: Chapter 8 — Donut's Claws

## Summary
In the safe room, Donut presents her new class accessories:
- **The Assassin's Veil** (beaded headdress): Offers +10% dexterity and the Precognition Benefit (warns of nearby spells/skills). Mordecai warns that draining this headdress into her tiara will transfer its drawbacks—specifically, making all of [[odette]]'s enemies automatically hostile.
- **The Nemes of Pestilence** (striped pharaoh hat for Mongo): Grants temporary invulnerability and 2x strength if owner's health falls below 10%, plus a 75% chance to inflict the Cruel Sepsis debuff on bites.
- **The Wrap of Sekhmet’s Compassion** (red headband for [[ren]]): Grants temporary invulnerability, the Heal Party Member skill under low-health conditions, and a 75% chance to resurrect killed targets as level 10 "Bootlickers."

Donut opens her accumulated loot boxes:
- Numerous bronze boxes contain clothes, healing scrolls, and around twenty **Emergency Gremlin** summoning scrolls to repair vehicles during races.
- **The Lorena Bobbitt Box** (Legendary) yields ten **Danforth Crossbow Bolts**. These do no physical damage but anchor the struck body part in place for 2 seconds multiplied by the shooter's Crossbow skill level.
- **The Default Winner Box** (Legendary) yields a spell book.
- **The Celestial Slap-Chop Box** yields the **Crupper of the Benevolent Champion** (chain mail matching her champion cloak).
- **The Celestial Godslayer Box** (from the death of [[meatus]]) leaves no physical item. Instead, Donut's front right paw glows and hurts. When she flexes, she reveals newly upgraded claws that cleanly slice the solid kitchen table in half.

During the box opening, [[rosetta]] laments [[justice-light]]'s death, noting it is sad that [[tipid]]'s wiped memory prevents him from remembering his close friend.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's celestial upgrade to her claws is a physical manifestation of her growing power as a godslayer. The contrasting pet accessories are highly practical, giving Mongo and Rend powerful defensive reactions to keep Carl and Donut alive in worst-case scenarios.
