# Book 6 Chapter 4: The Experience

## Summary
Carl and Donut venture out into Havana, observe environmental gridlock, and defeat their first local folklore boss.
- **Old Havana Inception**: Carl and Donut step out into Havana, Cuba. Samantha (the floating severed head) accompanies them, complaining that the ghostly locals are ignoring her.
- **Traffic Gridlock**: Reports from other regions show a growing problem: although the human drivers are ghosts, the physical cars collide. Katia in Iceland and Imani/Elle on the West Coast report massive pile-ups blocking saferoom exits. Crawlers start building roadblocks far from saferooms to redirect traffic and keep stairwell accesses clear.
- **The Experience Boss**: Carl and Donut encounter their first regional folklore boss, **The Experience** (Level 55), the pre-Columbian Taíno guardian of the dead. It is a powerful, magical monstrosity.
- **Loot**: After defeating the boss, Carl receives a Very Rare T'Ghee card that allows the squad leader to temporarily combine two monster cards into a single creature during combat.

## Characters Introduced
- [[samantha]] — The floating, sentient severed head (previously introduced; first appearance in Book 6).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[ghosts-of-earth]]**: Crawlers deal with the unexpected physics of a split corporeal/non-corporeal environment, resulting in city-wide traffic jams.
- **[[t-ghee-card-combat]]**: Carl obtains his first tactical utility card for deck combat.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The gridlock mechanic shows how the dungeon's physics can cause unexpected logistical disasters. The introduction of regional folklore (the Taíno *Experience*) grounds the starting regions in actual cultural myths, adding flavor to the survival challenges of the floor.
