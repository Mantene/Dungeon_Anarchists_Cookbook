# Book 5 Chapter 25: Future Huntress

## Summary
- **The All-Tree Outcropping**: Clint explains the [[all-tree]] rules: forest kin within range are protected because the tree absorbs and reflects spell magic, destroying the outcropping.
- **The Talent's Demise**: Orc hunter [[the-talent]] (from [[the-skull-empire]]) zapped himself by casting lightning near the All-Tree. Carl has Donut loot his 75,000 gold and a credit chit.
- **Assault on Fort Freedom**: The group arrives at Fort Freedom's slope. [[edgar]] the tortoise casts *Secreted* to hide their army. Bush elves disarm traps on the slope.
- **Orc Interrogation**: Areson brings the third captive orc: [[future-huntress]] (Level 50 fighter). She warns that Vrah has a court communication ban, and if she dies, Carl becomes "free game" for the Dark Hive.
- **Langley Wiped Out**: A System Message announces a champion has fallen: [[bogdon-ro]] was killed, and Katia reports in chat that [[langley]]'s entire Finnish archer team was wiped out.
- **Orc Execution**: Carl marks Future Huntress with the *Ring of Divine Suffering* and beats/kills her to obtain +2 Charisma (her highest stat).
- **Infiltrating Samantha**: Carl duct-tapes an alarm trap playing Oasis's "Wonderwall" to Samantha's head and throws her into the river as a distraction. The alarm triggers, and Signet's amphibious team charges Fort Freedom.

## Characters Introduced

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The death of Langley and his Helsinkian team is a tragic blow. Carl's rage boils over, causing him to show no mercy to Future Huntress, despite her fear. The tactical use of the All-Tree's spell-reflect property by Edgar highlights the native NPCs' mastery of their environment. Carl's weaponization of Samantha with an endless loop of "Wonderwall" is a brilliant bit of psychological warfare.
