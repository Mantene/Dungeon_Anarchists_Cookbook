# Book 4 Chapter 25: Gelato and Gills

## Summary
In preparation for the dive to disable the pump inside the submarine *Akula*, Carl and the crawlers gather information and descend into the flooded structure.
- **Preparations and Gelato**: In Pandinus, Carl, Donut, Katia, Gwen, and Tran meet with two Ukrainian crawlers: Britney Proskurina (Level 27 Pit Fighter) and Dr. Vadim Zbar (Level 28 Kuhli healer/rogue, aka "Gut Rearranger"). Vadim agrees to dive, but Britney refuses to go back in the water.
- **Chum Bomb Distraction**: Carl has the biplane *Nightmare II* crew drop garbage bags filled with corpse pieces and timed explosives (chum bombs) to distract the concierge sharks.
- **The Descent**: Katia shapeshifts into a diving bell to transport Carl, Tran, and Vadim down 500 meters to the *Akula*.
- **Exploring the Akula**: They enter the flooded sub, where Vadim explains that they previously dived with water-breathing scrolls but had their mini-sub blow up, flooding the vessel. They find a Blown Heavy-Duty Fuse inside the neighborhood boss's control panel and Carl loots the neighborhood map.
- **Quetzalcoatlus and Lusca**: The ghost of Quetzalcoatlus screeches and severely injures Vadim. Katia messages Carl that a giant creature (the Level 82 City Boss, Lusca) is approaching the submarine and reaching for it.
