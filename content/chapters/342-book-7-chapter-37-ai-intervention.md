# Chapter 342: Chapter 37 — AI Intervention

## Summary
The newcomer is revealed to be an avatar of the system AI using [[growler-gary|Growler Gary's]] body. Gary easily overrides [[orren|Orren's]] zero zone, restoring [[carl|Carl's]] menus, and mockingly threatens [[harbinger]] and the Syndicate council if they try to harm Carl or Orren outside the game. 

Gary demonstrates that the AI has extended its control outside of Earth's orbit all the way to the star, disabling the planetary failsafe. To prove this, Gary teleports [[princess-formidable|Princess Formidable]] directly into the room. Formidable, now Level 1, is furious and demands to know if her father [[king-rust|King Rust]] is dead. Gary confirms that [[rosetta]] decapitated and looted him, and tells her Carl has his head in his inventory. 

With the failsafe disabled, Gary offers Formidable four choices: Death, the Scolopendra Club, the Cabaret backstage, or Earth's surface. Formidable chooses Earth's surface and is teleported away to the ocean. Gary exits the room, leaving the actual, now level 175 and heavily debuffed Growler Gary in his body. The real Gary is disoriented, asks for [[rory]], and teleports away.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The system AI's avatar displays a terrifying level of control and madness. By bypassing Valtay tech and teleporting Formidable out of her ship, the AI proves that the Syndicate has lost all control over the game. The AI's playful, chaotic behavior under the guise of Gary contrasts sharply with the cosmic threat it represents, creating a sense of inevitable ruin that aligns with the book's title.
