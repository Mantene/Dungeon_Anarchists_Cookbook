# Chapter 287: Spunky Jefferson and the Vinegar Bitch

## Summary
- **The Vinegar Bitch**: Mordecai explains that Sister Ines's patron deity, [[ysalte|Ysalte]], is known as the *Vinegar Bitch*. She is a trickster goddess sponsored by the Nebulars, a faction of pacifist religious extremists whose ultimate goal is to shut down the crawl by destroying all host planets.
- **Spunky Jefferson**: Carl gives Dong Quixote a sapient, incremental-damage weapon: [[spunky-jefferson|Spunky Jefferson the Enchanted Nickel Sock of the Elderly Miser]], which speaks directly into the wielder's mind and gains damage with each kill.
- **The Antipodal Warning**: Prepotente warns Carl that Indonesia is the antipodal point of Havana and will be in the most danger if Carl does not buff Amayon enough to make the Sheol portal cover the entire planet. Prepotente then descends to Floor 9.
- **Entering the Capitol**: The team prepares to storm the FOCSA building to defeat Sister Ines.
