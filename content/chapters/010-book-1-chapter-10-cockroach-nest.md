# Book 1 Chapter 10: The Cockroach Nest

## Summary
After sleeping for ten hours, showering, and eating breakfast, [[carl]] notes the remaining crawler count has dropped to 4,148,111. Carl and [[princess-donut]] head out to hunt cockroaches (**Scatterers**). They encounter a **Scatterer Brood Guardian** (Level 4, described as "baptized in rage and Monster Energy drinks") and two Level 2s. Carl crushes the smaller ones, but his cudgel and poker prove ineffective against the guardian. Carl uses a snap-kick and jumps on its back, causing it to explode. 

They spend hours farming the cockroaches, reaching Level 5. Carl's unarmed skills rise to Level 5, and Donut's Strength rises to 15. They find the entrance to a dark room marked with a weathered Spanish sign: *"No entrar por orden del Centro de Salud"* (Do not enter by order of the Health Center). Donut mentions the room smells like Carl's dirty gym socks—specifically recalling the smell on days "that other guy" came over to the apartment while Carl was at the gym, confirming [[beatrice]]'s infidelity. Reckless and angry, Carl pulls out a stick of volatile **Goblin Dynamite**, lights it with his Zippo, throws it into the pitch-black room, and runs.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival]]**: Carl and Donut farm Scatterers for experience and test their abilities.
- **[[carls-class-selection]]**: Carl and Donut level up to Level 5, increasing their physical combat skills.
- **[[search-for-beatrice]]**: Carl receives confirmation of Beatrice's infidelity from Donut's casual remarks.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's discovery of Beatrice's cheating, delivered offhandedly by Donut, triggers a reckless reaction. His anger leads him to throw unstable dynamite into a dark room without verifying what's inside, setting off a dangerous chain reaction.
