# Chapter 285: Sidewalk Ends and Amayon Quest

## Summary
- **Isolate the Stairwells**: Carl proposes a daring plan: rather than fighting for individual keys, crawlers should isolate their stairwell chambers and dig trenches underneath them. Carl plans to trigger a massive Sheol portal that will suck the remaining crawlers up.
- **Prepotente's Endorsement**: Despite some resistance, Prepotente endorses the plan, estimating a 35-40% chance of success. This convinces other crawlers to cooperate and stop fighting.
- **Quest: Where the Sidewalk Ends**: The system triggers a regional quest of the same name. Carl's squad is designated as the host.
- **The Options**: 
  - *Option 1*: Do nothing. In 2 hours, the portal will suck all of Cuba into Sheol.
  - *Option 2*: Free Amayon from his snake shell, resetting the timer and making it a World quest.
  - *Option 3*: Kill Amayon (invulnerable on this floor).
- **Carl's Decision**: Carl chooses to free Amayon to turn the quest into a World quest, hoping to expand the portal globally. To do this, they must first eliminate Sister Ines.
