# Book 1 Chapter 7: Princess Donut

## Summary
[[princess-donut]] introduces herself as "GC, BWR, NW Princess Donut the Queen Anne Chonk." [[carl]] checks her stats, finding she is stronger and more intelligent than him, with an initial Charisma of 25. She has the "Enhanced Growth" buff (+1 Str/Int and +2 Cha per level, but no manual points). Donut opens her five boxes, learning the *Magic Missile* spell and obtaining the **Enchanted Crown of the Sepsis Whore** from her Legendary Quadruped Box. 

Despite warnings that the crown permanently places her in the line of succession for the Blood Sultanate on Floor 9 (forcing her to slay the entire royal family to descend to Floor 10), Donut immediately equips it because "purple is her color." Donut renames their party to **The Royal Court of Princess Donut**, designating herself as "Grand Champion Best in Dungeon" and Carl as "Royal Bodyguard." [[mordecai]] warns Carl that he can leave the party at any time to avoid the Floor 9 death trap, but Carl stands by her as she opens the guild door to head back out.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival]]**: Donut becomes a powerful offensive spellcaster, but equips a highly dangerous cursed item.
- **[[search-for-beatrice]]**: Donut remains focused on her connection to [[beatrice]] and her cat show background.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's vanity overrides tactical safety when she puts on the Crown of the Sepsis Whore. This sets up a major future plot obstacle on Floor 9. Mordecai's warning highlights the individualistic nature of the crawl, but Carl's choice to remain with Donut solidifies their bond.
