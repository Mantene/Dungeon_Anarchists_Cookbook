# Book 5 Chapter 13: Bravvo Shot Down

## Summary
- **Yenk Downed**: Point Mongo's guards shoot down the Yenk hunter [[bravvo]] with a missile. Carl casts *Ping*, detecting elites and other entities hiding in the woods.
- **Valtay Contract Negotiations**: Details of Carl's negotiation with Borant admin and Valtay liaison [[orren]] are revealed. Quasar bluffs that Sensation Entertainment (producers of *Vengeance of the Daughter*) agreed to pay Borant a 20% premium to extend their license to the 9th floor, giving them an incentive to keep Carl alive.
- **Meeting the Elites**: Carl and Donut leave Point Mongo's defenses to [[mordecai]] and [[miss-nance]] and head into the jungle. They meet the elite [[tsarina-signet]] (Level 60).
- **Were-Castors and Ogre**: Signet is accompanied by [[areson-the-wise]] (Level 45 Ogre Elite) and six Rambo-style were-kin (were-beaver) warriors of the Chee race, including [[clint-smashgrab]] (Level 50, missing a leg).
- **The Healed Captive**: They find the injured hunter Bravvo tied to their caterpillar mount. Signet offers the kill to Carl. Rather than killing him directly, Carl says "Heal him" and equips his Ring of Divine Suffering to execute him for stat boosts.

## Characters Introduced
- [[bravvo]] — Level 50 Yenk bounty hunter.
- [[clint-smashgrab]] — Level 50 Were-Kin (were-castor) Light Recon Specialist, Elite.
- [[areson-the-wise]] — Level 45 Ogre Elite, brother of Apollon.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[the-butchers-masquerade]]**: The negotiation details show the corporate politics behind Floor 6 and the fight for 9th-floor licensing.
- **[[systemic-oppression]]**: The were-kin warriors' tragic background as the Chee race, who were invaded and transformed by the High Elves.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's action to "heal" Bravvo just to execute him with the Ring of Divine Suffering is a cold, calculated move. It shows how Carl is adapting to the dungeon's cruelty, choosing to maximize his stat gains (gaming the system) over simple mercy or quick kills. The were-castors are introduced as an elite force with a deep, tragic history of displacement by the High Elves, setting up the political landscape of the floor.
