# Book 5 Chapter 2: Night Weasels and Pocket Kumas

## Summary
Carl enters the jungle, arrives at the Small Bugbear Settlement, and is immediately ambushed by invisible hunter pets before meeting Mordecai in his new form.
- **The Selva Entrance**: Carl steps out into the rainy jungle of Floor 6. He finds a port-a-potty that serves as his entrance, and heads toward Donut and Katia's coordinates just outside town.
- **Bugbear Settlement**: Carl arrives at the gates of the [[small-bugbear-settlement]], guarded by Level 95 Funeral Bells (mushroom-men).
- **The Weasel Ambush**: The Funeral Bell guards are suddenly paralyzed. Carl activates *Protective Shell* and uses a *Ping* spell (sonar mapping) and gunpowder to locate invisible attackers. However, a second invisible Level 25 **Night Weasel** scout (pet of hunter [[zabit]]) bites his neck, paralyzing him. Donut and Mongo defeat the weasels, and Chris carries Carl into the saferoom personal space.
- **Mordecai's New Form**: In the personal space, Carl finds [[mordecai]] has been transformed into a **Pocket Kuma** (Level 50, Non-Combatant NPC, extremely cute half-fairy bear-sugar-glider creature). Carl and Katia laugh hysterically at his appearance, while Mordecai is furious that Carl chose the explosives class over his recommended melee upgrade.
- **The Knock**: Someone knocks on the personal space door. Carl says it is "collateral" from his lawyer.

## Characters Introduced
- [[zabit]] — Hunter who owns the Night Weasel scout pets (mentioned).
- [[goiter]] — Bugbear council member (mentioned).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival-of-the-collapse]]**: The immediate ambush by invisible weasel scouts shows that Floor 6 has no safe zones outside the walls, even before the official hunt begins.
- **[[manager-benefit]]**: Mordecai's forced transformation into the Pocket Kuma strips him of his direct combat presence and limits his mobility, though he remains an expert manager.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The contrast between the immediate life-threatening ambush at the settlement gates and the comedy of Mordecai's Pocket Kuma transformation highlights the series' tone of dark comedy. Carl's paralysis by a Level 25 scout proves that even at Level 47, status effects and crowd control can leave him completely vulnerable.
