# Book 6 Chapter 14: La Iglesia de los Olvidados

## Summary
The crawlers execute their plan to breach the Ghommid church and secure a new rare totem.
- **Breaching the Church**: Carl coordinates a plan with Paz, Anton, and Sister Ines. The heavily armored Bomo opens the door, and Carl throws a stun bomb to disable the Ghommids while avoiding hitting [[asojano]].
- **Party Planning**: Donut suggests they should recruit Katia back into their party and use her iron-sheathed form for breaches instead of Bomo.
- **Combat Complications**: During the fight, Sister Ines attempts a bard song that increases party dexterity by 50%, but she loses her tune and is muted for five minutes. Carl is hit by a fear effect, triggering the *Crybaby!* achievement (which prevents him from moving for 15 seconds).
- **Clearing the Iglesia**: The team fights their way into *La Iglesia de los Olvidados*. After clearing the Ghommid waves, they capture a Rare T'Ghee Card: **[[skylar-spinach]]** (a Level 90 non-corporeal, affliction-dealing splitter Ghommid).
- **The Sun of Reawakening**: The system generates a "magical scoop of light" (the *Sun of Reawakening*) required to activate the shrines. Anton bends down to examine the cooling shrine pieces just as ominous music begins to play.

## Characters Introduced
- [[skylar-spinach]] — Level 90 primary splitter Ghommid card totem.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[pueblo-de-los-olvidados]]**: Breaching the church and acquiring the *Sun of Reawakening* puzzle key.
- **[[t-ghee-card-combat]]**: Carl captures his first Rare totem, Skylar Spinach.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The temple breach showcases the tactical coordination between the two crawler squads. Bomo's armor and Carl's explosives are essential, but the failure of Sister Ines's bard song highlights how unreliable adapting bards can be without the proper equipment (which Donut now has). The capture of Skylar Spinach provides Carl with a robust, splitting tank totem.
