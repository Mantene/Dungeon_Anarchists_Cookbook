# Chapter 313: Stronghold and Big Tina

## Summary

[[tipid]] gives Carl a tour of the base camp, explaining the fortification efforts. Former crawlers chose the Fortification Engineer race for rapid construction, with alchemists making materials and enchanters (including a yenk named Langrim) strengthening walls. The stronghold is being built around a wood shack saferoom to allow airship docking. Tipid explains new saferoom rules: a blood bar system returns once fighting starts (requiring kills to fill up to 9-10 hours, draining inside saferooms), and mercenaries are banned from guildhalls during later phases except for a staff of nine.

Carl examines the stronghold door—an oversized, cumbersome apprentice-quality fortification with three enchantments (Battering Resistance, Force Reflection, Heat Absorption). As they pull the heavy door open, [[big-tina]] the allosaurus in a pink tutu escapes the stronghold and joyfully reunites with [[mongo]]. The two dinosaurs hop up and down together. Florin's giant caiman card summon helps with construction work. The chapter emphasizes the urgency of building defenses and the organizational challenges ahead.

## Themes & Plot Threads

- **Fortification Strategy** — Building the stronghold around the saferoom for tactical advantage
- **Blood Bar System** — New mechanics forcing active combat participation
- **Mercenary Restrictions** — Sleeping quarters needed; no reserve forces in guildhall
- **Reunion** — Tina and Mongo's joyful meeting mirrors the emotional bonds throughout the story

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections

The blood bar mechanic is a clever narrative device that prevents turtling strategies and forces confrontation. The Tina-Mongo reunion provides a lovely moment of levity before the storm. Tipid's knowledge of the rules and construction logistics shows how valuable the veteran crawlers truly are—not just as fighters, but as strategists and builders.
