# Book 1 Chapter 16: Goblin Copper Chopper

## Summary
[[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] inspect their new Goblin Copper Chopper, a steam-powered vehicle built by the goblin engineers. Carl sits on an alligator-skull seat, and Donut sits in a flat-headed orc-skull sidecar that the engineers paint purple. An engineer warns Carl about the spigots used to release heat and pressure. Carl hoards three stacks of 999 coal lumps from the workshop's pile. 

Before they depart, [[lorelai]] propositions Carl, but Donut declines on his behalf. [[rory]] hands Carl a bag of dynamite and bombs. In return, Donut reveals that they got the "drugs" (methamphetamine) from a llama in a neighboring sector. This instantly triggers a meth war between the goblins and the llamas, and the goblins roll out in a battle frenzy, leaving the workshop abandoned. Carl and Donut decide to loot everything left behind, including a cart of black powder and a plastic explosive called "Hobgoblin Pus," before heading to the chieftain's boss room.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[survival]]**: Carl hoards coal and explosives, recognizing that physical preparation is key to surviving the dungeon.
- **[[televised-exploitation]]**: Donut remains highly conscious of their audience, noting that their antics will make "exquisite television."
- **[[survival-of-the-collapse]]**: The acquisition of the copper chopper provides Carl and Donut with a reliable means of high-speed travel to search for staircases.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The contrast between Carl's practical survival instincts (stealing tons of coal and weapons) and Donut's media-savvy showmanship (decorating the sidecar, managing their narrative) continues to define their dynamic. The casual way they trigger a faction war between the goblins and llamas shows how the system's absurd setup encourages crawlers to manipulate the dungeon's inhabitants to their own ends.
