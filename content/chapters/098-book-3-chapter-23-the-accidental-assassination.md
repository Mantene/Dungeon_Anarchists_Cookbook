# Book 3 Chapter 23: The Accidental Assassination

## Summary
The engine cab erupts into combat: Eva lunges at Carl with dual sabers, but Carl blocks her strikes with his gauntlet and kicks her back. He casts *Wisp Armor* as Hekla's mages fire spells at him. Hekla, fully healed by Silfa, calls for a cease-fire and orders her mages and Silfa out of the cab to keep the confrontation private.

Outside, the trapped crawlers continue to pack into the Vermillion train cargo cars as stage three monsters breach the platform stairs, killing five defenders. Hekla admits to Carl that her attempt on Katia was a "stupid risk" and proposes a reset. However, Donut is furious, refusing any future alliance with the "traitor."

Katia awakens from her unconscious state, confused and suffering from *Temporary Amnesia* due to the phased bolts. She believes Donut's *Magic Missile* (aimed at ghouls) was the source of her injury. Eva aggressively demands Katia shut up and rejoin them, mocking Katia's past personal failures (including a divorce and failed adoption) and calling her a useless screw-up.

Hekla tries to quiet Eva, but the psychological abuse pushes Katia over the edge. In a fit of rage, Katia activates her **Rush** skill, aiming to ram Eva. Due to the skill's erratic pathing, Katia misses Eva by inches and slams directly into Hekla, splattering the number two crawler in the game against the cab wall. Hekla is instantly killed. 

Katia claims Hekla's 500,000 gold bounty, gains a golden skull over her head, and instantly skyrockets from Level 24 to Level 37.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Katia's accidental execution of Hekla is a masterpiece of dark irony. Hekla orchestrated an elaborate, untraceable assassination attempt on Katia to preserve her reputation and secure Donut. Instead, her own psychological abuse of Katia via Eva drove Katia to use an unpredictable combat skill that literally splattered Hekla. The sudden level-up of Katia to Level 37 places her at the top of the crawler rankings, completely upending the political dynamics of the crawl.
