# Chapter 265: Prepare for Trouble and Impaled Eyes

## Summary
- **Double Boss Battle**: The Level 5 turkeys turn hostile due to the boss event. Tom summons a *Wakinyan Temple Guard* (Level 80) and plays persistent cards *Idolized* (blinds) and *Ralphie* (dispels magic and mutes totems).
- **Stomp Execution**: Carl uses the *Daughter's Kiss* soul release to stomp-obliterate Tom. This ends Tom's boss status, but the zombie turkeys remain hostile.
- **Carl Impaled**: Sharp-Elbows bear-hugs and impales Carl with her spiky body, piercing his chest, limbs, and both eyes (blinding him).
- **Ogre Decapitated**: Raul the crab snaps the ogre's staff and decapitates her, ending the fight. Donut and Mongo rescue Carl and *Puddle Jump* them away as the barn collapses.
