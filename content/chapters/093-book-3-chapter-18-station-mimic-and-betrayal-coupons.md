# Book 3 Chapter 18: Station Mimic and Betrayal Coupons

## Summary
Daniel Bautista II messages Carl with a dire update: their train arrived at Terminus Station 433, which is a massive Station Mimic. When 400 crawlers disembarked and went up the stairs, the mimic swallowed the first 20 people. Bautista's group is currently fighting off its tongue and "mouth thing" minions while waiting for a train to escape. Half of his group sold their conductor hats early on, leaving them trapped. Carl warns Bautista to head to Station 435 to use the employee portal, but cautions that the portal is lifebound to the Kravyad and will snap shut if the Kravyad is killed.

Carl interrogates Madison about the Kravyad and the employee portal. Madison explains that the [[kravyad]] are naga-like, blue, six-armed HR associates who hypnotize employees and eat "troublemakers" marked with special Gold Armbands. She confirms the employee portal is lifebound to the Kravyad. Madison also rants about her rival, [[doris]], the manager of Substation B who tried to impress CFO [[rod]] (Madison's ex-husband) by using transit lines rather than conveyors to dispose of ghouls.

Imani and Elle message Carl from their hike. They discover that the secret path out of the robot rooms is a high-speed, roller-coaster-style conveyor system. Ghouls are locked in cages by robots and rolled down the conveyor to the trainyards for disposal. Imani notes they are fighting "Wrath Ghouls" (mutated Stage 2 DT sufferers) and plan to ride the conveyor carts themselves. In the engine cab, Carl shuts the firebox door on Fire Brandy, who is arguing with Madison over demon labor contracts and paychecks while in labor.

Carl and Katia climb the ladder at Station 24 to inspect the trap door. It leads to a large, empty chamber containing five pulsing, inactive stairwells and exits to train platforms, including the named backtracking loop train, **Escape Velocity III**. 

While alone in the chamber, Katia reveals a doppelganger secret: she has sprouted a third eye on the back of her head. Although it causes nausea, she can use it to see behind her. With this eye, she witnessed Princess Donut hide a black **PVP Coupon** under the tracks. Katia hands the coupon to Carl; it bears his name on the back, offering a Gold Savage Box, Gold Weapons/Apparel boxes, and +1 Player Level for killing him. Katia is terrified that Donut still holds a coupon with Katia's name on it. Carl messages Imani, who confirms that a party's first player-killer receives a Savage Box containing coupons for all party members, but that Donut was distressed by them and intended to throw them away.

After checking stations 36, 48, 50, 58, and 59, the party arrives at the employee residential hub, Station 60. The station is derelict and empty—the "residential apartments" were merely false memories programmed into the employees to force overtime. Refusing to accept this, Madison stays behind on the platform to wait for transit security or Rod. Carl and Katia abandon her and head toward the safe room at Station 41. Donut compares Madison to Beatrice's mother's mean cat-breeder personality.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The PVP coupon is a brilliant piece of psychological warfare by the dungeon system. It rewards players for betraying their allies, feeding on the paranoia inherent in high-stress environments. Katia's third eye is both a tactical advantage and a curse, representing her loss of humanity as she adapts her doppelganger clay to survive. The abandonment of Madison at the empty Station 60 highlights the tragic, disposable nature of the NPCs, who are programmed with dreams of home that do not exist.
