# Book 5 Chapter 15: The Hunting Map

## Summary
- **Arwick's Corpse**: Areson kills a pale elf hunter named [[arwick]] (from [[the-dream]] faction) and dumps his body at Carl's feet. Carl loots him using his blast-chest method to bypass hunter traps.
- **The Gonorrhea Arrow**: Carl loots an *Arrow of Enthusiastic Double Gonorrhea* (a horrific poison that sets genitals on fire and heals them repeatedly, removable only by gelding or passing it on).
- **The Hunting Map**: Carl finds a kids' diner menu with a hand-drawn map of the Hunting Grounds. The map shows that:
  - Factions partitioned the map: The Hive has the bottom-left, The Dream has the top-middle (Point Mongo), and [[the-skull-empire]] has the southeast.
  - Crawlers are "claimed" in colors: Carl and Donut are claimed by both the Hive and the Skull Empire.
  - The Dream only has one target: [[louis-santiago-2]]. This is because Louis disrespected their leader [[epitome-tagg]] by having Juice Box shapeshift into his mother, [[epitome-noflex]], for sex. Carl sends a warning to Katia's group.
- **Quasar's Message**: Carl's lawyer pings him with corporate updates. Sensation Entertainment agreed to the 9th-floor license but is hesitating to sign. Carl needs 150 million credits to buy out something major, and currently has only 117 credits.
- **Signet's Camp & Quest**: Carl and Donut reach Signet's camp of 70 forest dwellers, including [[edgar]] the talking tortoise. Signet triggers the quest [[vengeance-of-the-daughter]] (Part One of Two), requiring Carl and Donut to help her assault **Fort Freedom** to assassinate the Naiad Confederacy leaders.

## Characters Introduced
- [[arwick]] — Deceased High-Elf hunter from The Dream.
- [[edgar]] — Ancient giant talking tortoise, Shell Mage.
- [[epitome-noflex]] — High-elf noblewoman, mother of Epitome Tagg (mentioned).

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[the-dream]]** & **[[the-skull-empire]]**: Faction partitions and targeted crawlers are revealed on Arwick's map.
- **[[vengeance-of-the-daughter]]**: Part One of Signet's questline is triggered to assault Fort Freedom.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The hand-drawn map is a vital piece of intelligence. It exposes the commercialization and coordination of the hunters, who divide the floor and claim crawlers like sports trophies. The vendetta against Louis is a hilarious but terrifying example of the consequences of his vulgarity: an entire high-elf hunter faction is dedicated to hitting him with "Enthusiastic Double Gonorrhea" because he made Juice Box shapeshift into their leader's mother.
