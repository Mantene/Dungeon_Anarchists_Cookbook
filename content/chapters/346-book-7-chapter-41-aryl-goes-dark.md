# Chapter 346: Chapter 41 — Aryl Goes Dark

## Summary
[[carl|Carl]] and [[princess-donut|Donut]] arrive at the Earthbound production facility for the Warlord Council. Donut critiques Carl's new chain-dragon tattoo, calling him a "white rapper doodle notebook guy," and complains about the base's hygiene facilities. 

A high-strung, disheveled [[ferdinand|Ferdinand]] teleports into the room, followed by three gnoll guards who conduct a security check. One guard, Neelii, is friendly with Donut. They scan the group for weapons and explosives. 

[[zev|Zev]] teleports in and shares shocking news: after [[princess-formidable|Princess Formidable]] was teleported away, her crew manually activated the system failsafe near Venus. While nothing happened in the Earth system, the **Aryl system** (the location of the previous crawl, the 22nd Edition) instantly went dark, and its tunnel node went offline—an event unprecedented in history. Zev notes that the Aryl system's AI had gone silent during Floor 6 of the current crawl. Zev leads them to the meeting room.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The revelation that the Aryl system went dark and its tunnel node vanished introduces a massive cosmic mystery. It suggests that the failsafe did not fail, but was redirected by the rogue AI to target a different node, or that the AI is actively pruning the Syndicate's network. The fact that the previous season's AI had gone silent during Floor 6 implies a coordinated, long-term rebellion among the system AIs.
