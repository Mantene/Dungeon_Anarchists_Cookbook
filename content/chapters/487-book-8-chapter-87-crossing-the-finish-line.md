# Chapter 487: Crossing the Finish Line

## Summary
In the blood-splattered casino, the crawlers activate the Wheel of Fortune to open the portal for the remaining members. [[louis-santiago-2]] bids a poignant farewell to [[princess-donut]] and [[elle-mcgibbons]] before stepping into the portal. [[samantha]] tries to get [[prepotente]] to take her with him, but he refuses. The crawlers leave a warning note for Clarabelle about the mimics. 

During their drive to the finish line, Elle asks [[carl]] about the Eye of the Bedlam Bride. Carl promises to explain everything once they reach the next floor. Donut slips into the garages of the defeated razor foxes and Jugglers, looting their keys to transfer their mounts, Onikuma and Old Shuck, to their own garage. 

Near the end of the track, they find the giant shell-projection of Imani. She is weeping and filled with regret for staying. Donut uses her headset microphone to comfort her, telling her that in the real world she made a wonderful life with [[chris-andrews-2]], Elle, and Yolanda. Carl fires the truck's rockets, crossing the finish line and leaving the tenth floor behind.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's speech to the giant shell of Imani is a deeply emotional highlight of the chapter, blending real-world hope with the tragic reality of the game's copies. By gathering mounts and leaving the floor together, the crawlers show they are building an army for the battles ahead.
