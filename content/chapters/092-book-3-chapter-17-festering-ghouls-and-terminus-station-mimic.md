# Book 3 Chapter 17: Festering Ghouls and Terminus Station Mimic

## Summary
Carl drives the *Nightmare Express* through the Station Repair portal, entering **Station and Repair Hub E**. Carl reasons that there must be dozens of these repair hubs and Abyss sites to support the thousands of active stairwells. Hub E is a fenced-in, mile-wide yard filled with thousands of mutated creatures. The engine is automatically routed to a dead-end track in **Service Bay 32**, which features a massive turntable for rotating locomotives.

Carl observes two talking, moss-covered, wood-skinned creatures: **Festering Ghouls** (Level 18). Festering Ghouls are not undead, but rather a new lifeform born from Stage 1 DT sufferers whose minds rotted away. Their purpose is to devour organic matter and find their own kind to reach critical mass.

A watchtower at the edge of the turntable fires a chain onto the service bay roof. **[[widget]]**, a Level 19 Grease Gremlin technician NPC, slides down the chain. Protected by his mandated employee "sensitivity training" from attacking, Widget rants that the station is closing due to the ghoul infestation. He tells Carl they should have taken the *Escape Velocity* train, which loops through Tangerine and Plum lines to the stairwell stations. Widget unhooks the engine from the destroyed trailing cars, climbs onto the turntable, and prepares to return to the tower to align the tracks. However, a multi-limbed "spider ghoul" that Donut had previously knocked unconscious (but which regenerated because ghouls are not undead and require a "double-tap" to kill) reanimates, decapitates Widget, and devours him.

To save the party, Princess Donut climbs the chain to the watchtower. After accidentally tipping over a freight car, she successfully operates the turntable and Puddle Jumps back to the engine cab. Carl drives the engine out of the yard, passing the burning **Substation E**, where spawning dwarves and porters are instantly devoured by ghouls. 

**[[madison]]**, a Level 10 Human Resources Associate NPC who resembles a suburban soccer mom with a tight facelift, runs from the flaming building chased by a wolf ghoul. Donut kills the wolf ghoul, and they pull Madison aboard the cab. Madison demands they take her to Yard A, but Carl interrogates her using the Cookbook's "Worn Path Method" (challenging NPC memories). Madison becomes confused, breaks down, and reveals she is the benefits manager for the Kravyad and the **six Station Mimics**—massive mimics disguised as Terminus Stations (like 433 and 435) designed to devour passengers. 

Carl immediately sends a warning broadcast to [[daniel-bautista-2]] (Bautista), who is approaching Station 433, telling him not to get off at the Terminus Station.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The revelation that the Terminus Stations are massive Station Mimics adds a horrific twist to the fourth floor's layout. The system actively tricks crawlers into their own slaughter to feed these gargantuan creatures. Carl's utilization of the "Worn Path Method" shows his growing sophistication in exploiting the dungeon's software limits and NPC cognitive gaps, turning a corporate bureaucrat into a valuable source of intelligence.
