# Chapter 428: Chapter 29 — Harbinger Inverted

## Summary
- **Caprid Panic**: Carl's revelation triggers a herd-wide panic among the Caprids. All the spotlights turn on, and the assistant Caprids stampede out of the room, running through the walls.
- **Gamori's Judgment**: The Matriarch of the Plenty, **Gamori** (who is also Harbinger's mother), remains behind. She judges Carl too dangerous to walk with the herd because his actions have ruined her ability to slowly manage her people's panic. She breaks down sobbing and smacking herself.
- **Harbinger's Confrontation**: **Harbinger** teleports into the room carrying a giant pulse rifle. He confronts Gamori, accusing her of failing to prepare the herd for the truth. He reveals he saw how many Residuals were in the dungeon and how unstable the AI was, but nobody helped him.
- **The Inversion**: Harbinger raises the gun at Carl. Before he can fire, the Primal AI intervenes and turns Harbinger's body inside out with a sludgy sucking sound. His blood and organs spray across Carl, Donut, Lexis, and the entire room.
- **Gamori's Exit**: Gamori mutters, "All stampedes have their casualties," and teleports away.
- **High Ratings**: Covered in goat guts, Lexis picks up her blood-spattered datapad and grins, noting that the viewer ratings are the highest they have ever been.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The sudden, grotesque execution of Harbinger by the Primal AI highlights the absolute lack of safety for even high-level administrators in the dungeon. Harbinger's speech reveals his tragic isolation—he knew the system was collapsing and was trying to prevent a galactic disaster, only to be turned into "warm jam" by the AI.
