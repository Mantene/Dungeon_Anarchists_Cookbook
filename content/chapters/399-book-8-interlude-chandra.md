# Chapter 399: Interlude — Chandra

## Summary
In the communications room of the damaged Naga warship *Ventilator*, Princess Chandra, Esquire, meets with [[princess-donut]] in a type-A containment zone during exit-deal negotiations. Chandra, who became a princess after a coup placed her uncle on the Naga throne, is using the crawl's legal loopholes to secure extreme wealth and visa fees to move to the galaxy's center system.

Chandra explains her legal maneuverings:
- She has invoked "Widow's Rights" in Naga court to claim [[carl]] (the killer of her late husband [[rishi]]) as her new husband. The court granted the request and awarded her the holdings of the defeated Blood Sultanate as a wedding gift.
- Because Donut's species is not legally recognized as sapient by the Syndicate, Chandra has placed Donut's earnings under a conservatorship, naming Carl (and effectively herself) as the beneficiary and custodian.
- She has appointed herself as Donut's attorney, gaining access to both Carl's and Donut's financial empires.
- She is blackmailing the "Donut Holes" fan group for 38% of their profits (demanding to review their books) and has sent two Taurin associates to eliminate Carl's lawyer, [[quasar]].

Donut refuses all exit deals, preferring to follow Carl's lead. When Chandra reveals the conservatorship and marriage certificate, Donut remains surprisingly calm. She compares Chandra to Carl's greedy father, painting her as a sad and pathetic figure, which outrages Chandra. 

Donut then exploits the fact that the negotiations of top-ten crawlers are being broadcast live to media accounts. She loudly declares that she is *not* offering 50 million credits to anyone who turns Chandra inside out, and *not* offering 5 million credits to turn Chandra's head into a hat—cleverly creating an enormous public bounty on Chandra's life. 

Almost immediately, Chandra receives a message from the Taurin hitmen she hired to kill Quasar, asking for her coordinates. Terrified that they are turning on her for the massive bounty, Chandra panics and flees the room. Donut mockingly delivers "lesson one of being a princess": don't put yourself in situations where you are so afraid you piss yourself.

## Characters
- [[princess-chandra]] — A Naga attorney who attempts to steal Carl and Donut's wealth through legal loopholes.
- [[princess-donut]] — Refuses exit deals and outmaneuvers Chandra by placing a public bounty on her via the live broadcast.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- [[syndicate-corruption]] — The legal system of the Syndicate allows parasitic exploitation of crawlers.
- [[crawler-resistance]] — Donut's clever manipulation of the media broadcast to threaten Chandra demonstrates how crawlers can use the system's own rules against their oppressors.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
This interlude highlights Princess Donut's growth as a character. She is no longer just a pampered cat; she is politically savvy, sharp-tongued, and capable of wielding her massive popularity and media presence as a weapon. Her comparison of Chandra to Carl's abusive/neglectful family shows a deeper understanding of Carl's trauma and their shared bond.
