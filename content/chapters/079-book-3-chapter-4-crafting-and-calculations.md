# Book 3 Chapter 4: Crafting and Calculations

## Summary
Carl sets up his three crafting tables (Alchemy, Sapper, Engineering) in their base. Using the *Goo-inator 3000*, Carl shapes a non-enchanted iron breastplate from a swordsman guard, earning the "Martha Stewart" achievement and a Bronze Crafter's Box (which contains pliers, a tape measure, and a bucket of pink glitter). Carl communicates with [[daniel-bautista-2]] (on the Azure/Brown lines at station #199), who also conjoined a safe room base.

The party visits Limp Richard's shop. Donut buys a padded mallet for 150 gold. Carl notices a **Battery Fabricator** (75,000 gold) but cannot afford it. Carl trades 5 Louis L'Amour western novels for 5 sci-fi/horror books from Limp Richard's stash.

They investigate the Yellow Line platform. The tracks are laid out like a fishhook. The loops are 90 minutes. They camp the platform, killing incoming Cave Mudge Bonkers (Level 19) and Shock Chompers (Level 20 nagas) in the open doors. They then board Car 2 of the train. In close-quarters combat, they use "Formation One" (defensive V-shape in the door vestibule) to defeat a pack of Bonkers. Katia reinforces her arms with metal to block their clubs, and Carl uses his *Talon Strike* to finish them off.
