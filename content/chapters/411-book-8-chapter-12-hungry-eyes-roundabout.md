# Chapter 411: Chapter 12 — Hungry Eyes Roundabout

## Summary
Carl and Donut exit their garage, which has the number "7" painted on it, indicating their seventh-place finish in Heat 1. They enter the **Team Roundabout** of **Hungry Eyes Village**, a cul-de-sac where safe room rules apply. The roundabout is a grassy, suburban-looking cul-de-sac containing nine garages. The ninth garage (representing the eliminated Trauco Triplets) has a skull painted on it. A pedestrian path leads to a portal showing a neon-lit town.

They meet [[radoslav]] and [[jasha]] of Team Free Love, who are drinking beer on lawn chairs. Radoslav shares cans of Busch Light from their magical never-ending beer cooler. 

Garage number 4 opens, revealing a bear mount (Onikuma). Three bipedal, four-foot-tall ninjas somersault into the cul-de-sac. The ninja in the center wearing a red suit is **Chiyome** (a Level 80 Mistress of Nunchaku, belonging to the Razor Fox race, representing **Team The Wild Hunt**). 

The three ninjas perform a nunchuck dance, which Chiyome explains was a team casting spell called **Size Up** to evaluate the capabilities of the other teams. The other two ninjas flip away through the portal, and Chiyome joins Carl and Radoslav for a beer.

Chiyome warns Carl that they need to collaborate to take down the top three teams:
- **Team 1 (One Fine Pig)**: Tigran racers.
- **Team 2 (The Jugglers)**: Racers with a giant dog mount named Old Shuck.
- **Team 3 (Lady Dominators and the Gimp)**: Womantaur racers.

Just as they are discussing Team 3, the door to garage number 3 starts to open.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The cul-de-sac environment is designed to evoke a peaceful Earth suburb, a psychological trick that makes the violent death races even more jarring. Chiyome's immediate offer of a beer and her tactical alliance proposal show that the NPCs are thinking ahead, treating the race as a tournament of wits rather than just a mindless gauntlet.
