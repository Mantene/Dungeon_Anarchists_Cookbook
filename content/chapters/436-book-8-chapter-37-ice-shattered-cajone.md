# Chapter 436: Chapter 37 — The Ice-Shattered Cajone

## Summary
- **diner Buffs**: The group enters the C&W&U diner, which is exempt from safe room rules. The diner is run by flightless imps (Darla-Dean rockabilly wig waitress). Louis and Prepotente order strawberry milkshakes and sodas that grant stat buffs (like +5% reaction time). Prepotente orders 20 of each soda to go and messages Jurgen.
- **Dead Imp and Note**: Carl pushes the jukebox (playing "Sledgehammer") to reveal the Upgrade hallway. On the counter, they find the corpse of **Hickster** (Level 25 Upgrade Imp, killed by Akuma). Carl loots a note written in crayon on a kiddie menu: "Carl, you were supposed to come alone, you moron. Ditch them and come back."
- **Elle's Retribution**: Carl finds a hidden door with a disintegration trap, deactivates it, and opens it to find Akuma sipping a purple milkshake. Donut immediately casts *Mute* on Akuma. Elle, furious about Faction Wars, shoots an ice bolt at Akuma's crotch, freezing and shattering his left testicle.
- **The Threat**: Elle threatens to shatter his remaining testicle and have Jacobus perform painful dental surgery on him if he doesn't answer Carl's questions.
- **Anti-Magic Shield**: Elle tells Akuma that Imani will encase him in an anti-magic shield, which will slowly drain his health but prevent him from casting spells while Donut un-mutes him. The mercenaries (Team Helix) watch in absolute terror. Olga mutters they should have charged quintuple.
