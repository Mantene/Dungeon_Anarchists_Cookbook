# Book 6 Chapter 37: Stasis Stories and Robert the Human

## Summary
Only squad leaders transfer first for Faction Wars, allowing Odette to reveal the tragic origins of her guilt and the true, dark purpose of the Dungeon Crawl to Carl, while Donut faces her first boss card fight.
- **Squad Leader Separation**: Zev messages Carl and Donut that only the squad leaders (like Donut) will transfer first to the choosing chamber. Other crawlers are placed in a temporary stasis. Donut is terrified but Carl reassures her.
- **Security Response**: Zev is pulled away as an unmanned liaison spy ship is shot out of Earth's orbit, triggering an automatic security response and giving Odette and Carl fifteen minutes to talk off-the-record.
- **Odette's Guilt**: Odette explains her history. She entered the dungeon with her cousin Pieter and friend Armita. She was forced to choose between them, selecting Armita. Pieter and the rest of her family died. Odette became a guide, managing [[mordecai]] because he reminded her of Pieter.
- **The Dodola Deal**: Obsessed with escaping (staring into the metaphorical "eye of the Bedlam Bride"), Odette made an illegal deal with [[huanxin-jinx]] (who drove the goddess Dodola) to get Mordecai to the 11th floor. The deal collapsed when Huanxin demanded more money; Odette trapped Dodola, leading to [[chaco]] killing the goddess. Mordecai escaped, but his brother Uzzi died.
- **Huanxin's Revenge**: Odette confessed to her crimes out of guilt. In retaliation, Huanxin murdered Armita. Odette was arrested, but her husband purchased her warrant. She did not fix her legs as a form of self-punishment and has set aside a trust for Mordecai and Chaco.
- **Suicide Mission**: Odette plans to use her entry as Nekhebit to kill both herself and Huanxin.
- **The Crawl's True Purpose**: Odette explains that the crawl exists to mine elements—the byproduct of biological overgrowth in seeded systems—which are grafted by the mantises into planetary engines for fuel. The entertainment aspect simply funds the operation. Shutting it down must be done responsibly to avoid killing half the universe.
- **Donut's Selection**: Donut picks Miami (other options were Bahamas, two in US, and one in Mediterranean).
- **The First Card Fight**: Donut enters a room in an older house and faces **[[robert-the-human]]** (Level 40, wearing a tight sailor outfit with a "Gene" nametag). Robert summons **[[robert-the-doll]]** (a tall faceless doll in a sailor suit) and **[[dog]]** (a stuffed dog with rolling bug eyes).
- **Instant Kill**: Donut summons [[geraldo]], but before the fight can drag out, [[mongo]] flies through the air and chomps Robert the Human's throat, killing him instantly. Donut loots his sailor hat and examines the cards.
- **Carl's Transfer**: Carl is transferred to Donut's location as the stasis ends.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **The Fuel Extraction System**: The revelation that the dungeon crawl is actually a planetary fuel extraction and mining system, with entertainment as a secondary funding mechanism, changes the scale of the conflict.
- **The Bedlam Bride Metaphor**: The "eye of the Bedlam Bride" represents destructive obsession that blinds individuals to the suffering of those around them.
- **Pieter and Mordecai**: Odette's maternal/protective obsession with Mordecai stems from her guilt over abandoning her cousin Pieter.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Odette's confession is a powerful moment of vulnerability, showing she is not just an exploiting show host but a victim and perpetrator warped by the system's cruelty. Her explanation of the crawl's fuel extraction purpose elevates the narrative from a reality TV parody to a cosmic industrial tragedy. Donut's instant kill of Robert the Human is a comical contrast to the heavy lore reveal, showing that despite the massive cosmic stakes, the immediate survival of the crawlers still relies on quick, brutal action.
