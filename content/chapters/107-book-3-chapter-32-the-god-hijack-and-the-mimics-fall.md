# Book 3 Chapter 32: The God Hijack and the Mimic's Fall

## Summary
The chapter begins with Carl explaining his plan: he wants to summon the war god Grull in Trainyard E using the first mantaur to destroy the Mimic Rex, then immediately use the second mantaur to re-summon Grull and teleport him into the abyss, where he will clear the Krakaren swarm. However, Carl’s foot is grabbed by the first mantaur, dragging him through the portal into Trainyard E right as the summoning begins.

In Trainyard E, Carl is trapped in a *Death Challenge* broadcast between the Level 90 **Mimic Rex** and the Level 250 **War God Grull** (who is revealed to be sponsored by **Prince Maestro**, who faked his death and is using Grull as a vessel). Grull is invulnerable and colossal. As Grull and the Mimic clash, Carl uses an Invisibility Potion (lasting 30 seconds) and *Mordecai's Special Brew* (granting 30 seconds of rapid-healing invulnerability) to survive. Carl is repeatedly crushed by shockwaves and rolling trains but is instantly healed.

To save Carl, Katia shape-shifts into Carl's likeness and stands on a derailed train in the distance, flipping off Grull while Elle flies around to draw his aggro. Grull's heat-breath melts half the trainyard as he chases the decoy.

Donut, Mongo, Li Jun, and Li Na jump through the portal cart to join Carl. Li Na reveals she has prepared the second mantaur as a portable vessel by amputating his lower body and arms, healing him repeatedly to keep him alive. 

As Grull prepares a massive attack, Carl punches the armless mantaur to trigger the summoning. Because a deity can be "stolen" from one summoning to another, Grull is instantly yanked from his pursuit of Katia back into the vessel. Carl and his team throw the vessel into the portal to the abyss with one second left. Grull is successfully teleported to the bottom of the abyss.

The music stops, and the Mimic Rex is dead. The *Nightmare* train arrives, driven by Tizquick, and Carl collapses in exhaustion.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Li Na's pragmatic, ruthless preparation of the second mantaur is a terrifying showcase of crawler survival logic, mirroring Carl's own harvesting of Growler Gary's hands. By hacking the summoning rules—using a deity's lack of cooldown and the ability to "steal" a summoning—Carl pulls off a brilliant tactical escape. Grull's presence in the abyss will turn the pit into a warzone, pitting the invulnerable war god against the infinite Krakaren clones and ghouls, effectively neutralizing two massive threats at once.
