# Book 2 Chapter 10: Ink Marauders and Grimaldi

## Summary
[[tsarina-signet]]'s 2D paper army (Blood and Ink Elementals) marches on the circus. Carl follows her, marveling at the flat, wax-paper-like appearance of the giant summon monsters. They are attacked by lemurs and stilt clowns. Carl uses his Big Top Ticket to bypass [[clammy-the-clown]] (Level 9) at the entrance and slips into the main big top tent. 

Inside, he discovers [[ringmaster-grimaldi]] (Level 85 City Boss), who has transformed into a massive Pestiferous Vine swallowing the center pole. 

Carl encounters [[apollon-the-mighty]] (Ogre Elite) at his stand. Carl decides to execute Mordecai's poisoning trick: plants are vulnerable to poisoned blood. He removes his nightgaunt cloak to lower his poison resistance, drinks a poison potion, cuts his palm with a juggling knife, and drips the poisoned blood onto Grimaldi's roots. He then takes an antidote and puts his cloak back on.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The description of the "paper army" highlights the theatrical, budget-friendly nature of some in-game productions. The scene inside the big top evokes a strong sensory recall of Carl's childhood, mixing nostalgia with the horrific reality of the dungeon.
