# Chapter 378: Zarogoths and the Nest Under Siege

## Summary

Carl and his forces fight through zarogoths—generated mobs created from corrupted souls—while defending their position. The zarogoths are disposable ground troops that leak black liquid when pierced and die after losing 51% of their volume. Their primary purpose is to delay Carl's forces.

[[princess-donut]] spots the war mage generator antenna inside the Piccadilly Theater on the 9th level of Larracos, visible only through a reflection. She can't shoot it due to the angle. [[juice-box]] arrives with [[britney-proskurina]] and [[ferdinand]], and Carl coordinates a plan to bomb the Piccadilly Theater using waffle makers.

[[juice-box]] desperately asks about [[louis-santiago-2]], who hasn't responded to messages. Carl discovers Louis is paralyzed and bound. He sends [[samantha]] to rescue Louis from the Reaver castle.

A devastating sequence follows: [[bomo]], Toyotomi, and several defending mages are killed when a mysterious woman with a dog enters the Nest tower. The message notifications indicate they die rapidly. Carl realizes this is [[lucia-mar]] and her rottweiler, attacking the tower.

## Themes & Plot Threads

- **Zarogoths as Delay Tactics** — War mage forces are designed to slow, not necessarily kill
- **Louis in Peril** — Louis's paralyzed/bound status raises the stakes
- **Lucia Mar's Attack** — A terrifying solo assault that kills multiple defenders
- **Samantha's Loyalty** — Despite her chaotic nature, she agrees to rescue Louis

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections

The rapid-fire deaths of Bomo and the mages underscore the brutality of this war. Juice Box's genuine desperation for Louis is a surprisingly humanizing moment, and Samantha's negotiation scene provides dark comedy relief. The chapter's pacing mirrors the chaos of war.
