# Book 3 Chapter 21: Brynhild's Daughters and the Train Rescue

## Summary
Carl and Donut return to their personal space to find it packed with thirty members of **Brynhild’s Daughters**. Hekla (Level 33) explains that they safely bypassed the Kravyad at Substation H using Sadie's *Glass Prison* spell. They hiked to Station 60, which contains a switch allowing travelers to choose between any of the 12 Homeward Bound platforms. Carl immediately sends a mass broadcast to all crawlers informing them of this critical routing hub.

Carl notices Katia's friend, **Eva Sigrid**, a Level 27 Half Nagini, Half-Orc Nimblefoot Enforcer with four arms and thirteen player-kill skulls over her head. Carl deduces she acts as Hekla's enforcer. Donut eagerly suggests merging parties, but Carl privately warns her to stay alert. He suspects Hekla might try to eliminate him to acquire Donut and Mordecai. Carl hotkeys his Invisibility Potion and *Mordecai's Special Brew* just in case.

Hekla proposes a massive rescue mission: over 1,000 crawlers, including **Li Jun** and his team, are trapped by mobs at Station 101 on the Vermillion line. The Vermillion line has active power and an unused subway train with cargo containers. Eva has rigged a second engine to the back of the train to allow it to reverse. However, subway trains are not designed to plow through large ghoul hordes.

To solve this, Hekla reveals their plan: **Katia Grim** will act as a living cowcatcher on the front of the train. Using her doppelganger abilities, Katia has designed a wide, metal-and-rubber-layered form to absorb the impact of colliding with ghouls. Katia insists this was her own idea. Carl reluctantly agrees on the condition that he and Donut ride in the cab and Katia remains in their party. When Zev complains about them using private chat to plan, Carl tells her to go fuck herself out loud.

As they take the *Nightmare* to Station 60, Katia shares that Eva Sigrid was an economics professor in Reykjavik, and Hekla was her psychiatrist before the collapse.

At Station 60, they see Madison being berated by angry dwarf and gremlin NPCs. They also pass a weeping dwarf conductor named **Tizquick**, who has realized his programmed memories of his daughter are a lie. Carl consoles him, saying, "One day, this pain you’re feeling right now will matter," which Hekla observes, warning Carl not to give himself an ulcer. Hekla urges speed, noting that the ghouls are beginning to enter Stage 3 of their delirium.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
The dynamic between Hekla and Eva Sigrid adds a psychological layer: Hekla, a psychiatrist, has her former patient Eva as her heavily-armed enforcer. This relationship hints at manipulation and dependency, which Carl must navigate carefully. Katia's choice to put herself on the front of the train shows her desperation to feel valued and protect her friends, even if it means risking her life. The tragedy of Tizquick reinforces Carl's burning hatred for the Syndicate's casual cruelty toward the NPCs they program and discard.
