# Book 3 Chapter 9: Desperado Assassination Attempt

## Summary
Carl and Donut enter the Desperado Club. The bouncer [[clarabelle]] warns them about security and offers private guards for 500 gold. Carl hires two Level 35 rock cretin bodyguards, Bomo and The Sledge. 

They meet [[elle-mcgibbons]] (now a small, blue-skinned Frost Maiden) at the bar. She is drinking "Knockout" (dungeon Everclear) and behaves with a loud, aggressive personality. [[imani-c]] (now an Obsidian Butterfly Fire Spiritualist with a 12-foot wingspan) joins them. Imani explains that Chris Andrews took an Igneous rock race, became aggressive, and split from Brandon An. Chris is still alive, blocked messages, and is looking for Carl. 

Suddenly, a Level 21 Knife Sharpener crawler named [[ji-hoon]] bypasses their guards and stabs Donut in the back with a double-damage dagger. Donut's *Cockroach* passive saves her from instant death, leaving her in the red. Donut slashes Ji-Hoon's throat, killing him, earning her first skull. A second assassin (a woman casting spells) is instantly killed by Elle, who shoots a two-foot icicle through her eye socket. 

Assistant Club Manager [[astrid]], a Level 125 Bloodlust Sprite, apologizes for the security failure, refunds their gold, and grants them free permanent security. Carl requests to keep Bomo and The Sledge as their personal guards.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's first crawler kill is a massive psychological shock. Unlike monsters, she views crawlers as "real people." Her grief and fear of being disliked contrast with Elle's bloodthirsty pragmatism.
