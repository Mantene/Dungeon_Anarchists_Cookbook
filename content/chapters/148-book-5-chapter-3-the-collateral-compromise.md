# Book 5 Chapter 3: The Collateral Compromise

## Summary
Carl explains his meeting with his new lawyer, Quasar, and integrates the four mercenaries received as collateral for the Gate of the Feral Gods.
- **The Lawyer Meeting**: Carl describes his meeting with [[quasar]] (a gray alien, a "Nullian" in a tan suit with a gyrating hula girl tie). Quasar warned Carl about Orren's Valtay schemes and negotiated the "collateral" deal: Carl surrenders the Gate winding box to the Syndicate, to be returned on Floor 9.
- **The Mercenary Package**: In exchange, Carl receives four free mercenaries for Floors 6-8: [[bomo]], [[the-sledge]], Clay-ton, and Very Sullen. Bomo and Sledge stay in the personal space, while Clay-ton and Sullen join Chris and Katia.
- **The Teleport Spells**: To ensure the Syndicate returns the Gate, Quasar negotiated two temporary spells bound to the mercenaries:
  1. Bomo has **Teleport to Stairwell** (Level 15, 25-day cooldown, lists all exits).
  2. Sledge has **Zerzura** (a legendary teleport spell that leads to massive carnage).
- **Katia's Stats**: Katia reached Level 52 by flooding Larracos, unlocking the perk *Steely Skin* (+Constitution). She prepares to travel south to meet other crawlers.
- **Upgrades Purchased**: Carl and Donut spend their gold on personal space upgrades: the **Advanced Bomb Maker's Workshop** (enabling virtual simulations of custom explosives) and the **Magic Studio**.

## Characters Introduced
- [[quasar]] — Carl's gray alien Nullian defense attorney.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[the-gate-of-the-feral-gods]]**: Resolved for Floor 5; the winding box is handed over, but Quasar binds teleportation spells to the collateral mercenaries to ensure its return on Floor 9.
- **[[systemic-oppression]]**: The introduction of Quasar and the Syndicate's legalistic checks and balances reveal the complex, bureaucratic mechanisms governing the crawl.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Quasar is a brilliant addition, introducing a dirty, cynical, legal perspective on the crawl. His negotiation of the Bomo/Sledge spells shows that the rules of the dungeon can be exploited defensively by using temporary spells as leverage against the administrators.
