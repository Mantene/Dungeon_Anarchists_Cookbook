# Book 6 Chapter 30: Psychedelic Cures and Desperate Moves

## Summary
Katia's severe drug addiction is treated with magical cures, Mordecai urges Carl to let her self-destruct, and Carl forces the Astrid quest immediately before the Phase Two transition.
- **Odette's Show Warning**: Zev messages Carl that they must return in four and a half hours for Odette's show, which they will walk straight from to the Phase Two onboarding chamber.
- **Katia's Addiction Cures**: Carl and Donut meet the others at the Desperado Club. Katia is in a terrible state, shivering and vomiting. [[mordecai]] (via chat) explains she is addicted to four different drugs:
  - *Travis Priest*: A heavy painkiller (cured first, causing her colorful vomiting).
  - *Creamsicle Charm*: Ground fairy wings that function like mushrooms to ease anxiety (cure pending).
  - *Blitz Sticks*: Hallucinogenic weed that shows false memories (cured via a skin patch).
  - *Glory Bound*: The most dangerous drug, which puts the user in a coma to fully re-experience a past memory. Curing it requires a "bad trip" where she must relive one of her worst memories.
- **Scrambled Stats**: Mordecai notes that Katia's stats are permanently scrambled: her intelligence has risen slightly, but her Constitution, Charisma, and skill points took a heavy hit, and she is now permanently vulnerable to addictive spells.
- **Mordecai's Harsh Advice**: In a private message, Mordecai advises Carl to let Katia self-destruct. He points out that it is ultimately "her or Donut" due to the [[crown-of-the-sepsis-whore]] curse, and that she self-sabotaged her own escape. Carl refuses to give up on her.
- **Forcing the Quest**: Carl insists they execute the quest to kill [[astrid]] immediately, before the Phase Two teleports. He wants Katia to have the *Orchid of Eileithyia's Grace* before they go to Odette, securing [[huanxin-jinx]]'s support.
- **Bautista's Rage**: [[daniel-bautista-2]] is furious, warning Carl that they can do this in Phase Three. He storms out of the club when Katia agrees to proceed.
- **Debuff Treatment**: [[imani-c]] takes Katia to the bathroom to apply the blitz patch and administer buffs to prevent her from passing out. Donut follows them.
- **Elle's Warning**: [[elle-mcgibbons]] warns Carl that if anything happens to Katia, Daniel Bautista ("Tony the Tiger") will be furious and will stop brewing the morning tea that keeps Imani together. She notes that Imani traded her alien totem for a unique Mothman and a Reverse Tooth Fairy card, while Elle's squad utilizes a "squatch squad."

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **Addiction and Trauma**: Katia's addiction to memory-reliving drugs highlights the immense psychological trauma of the crawl.
- **Crawler Fractures**: The argument between Carl, Bautista, and Imani shows the deep, fracturing stress within the alliance.
- **The Toll on the Managers**: Mordecai's pragmatism shows he is willing to accept Katia's death to ensure Donut's survival, demonstrating the cold math managers must make.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Katia's addiction to memory-reliving drugs is a heartbreaking coping mechanism, allowing her to escape the nightmare of the crawl by literally reliving her pre-apocalypse life. Carl's reaction—initial anger, followed by recognition of his own father's struggles—forces him to push through the disgust to help her. His refusal to accept Mordecai's advice to let her self-destruct highlights Carl's stubborn moral core; he rejects the system's "either/or" compromises, even when his insistence on saving everyone threatens to get them all killed.
