# Book 2 Chapter 12: The Street Urchins

## Summary
[[carl]] sprints back to Signet's building. Donut's Cockroach skill has kept her alive at 5% health. The building is swarmed by Level 8 **Street Urchins**—nocturnal janitor mobs that consume refuse and dead bodies. Because Carl built a makeshift wooden/metal pyramid around the sleeping Donut, the Urchins attacked the barrier to consume it. Mongo fought them off fiercely, getting riddled with spikes in the process. 

Carl recalls the refuse into his inventory, dispersing the Urchins. He heals Donut with a scroll and cures Mongo using a custom alchemical potion (cinnamon stick + thistle rot inside a healing potion) suggested by Mordecai. 

Mongo rises to Level 10 and grows to waist-height. Donut wakes at dawn as the *Water Lily* spell ends. The circus tents are gone, and a new staircase to Floor 4 is revealed in the park. 

They set out toward the Skyfowl settlement, practicing spells (Panic play: *Fear* + *Magic Missile*). They reach the town, noticing the revolving knife tower of **The Desperado Club**.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[over-city-mechanics]]**: Introduces the Street Urchins (janitor mobs) and the hidden staircases revealed by quests.
- **[[survival]]**: The importance of Mongo's defense and custom alchemical recipes for healing pets.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's realization that his own defense pyramid caused the Urchin attack is a classic dungeon irony. The emotional bonding between Carl, Donut, and Mongo solidifies Carl's resolve: "This is my family." Donut's comparison of Zev's talk show bookings to the television show *Stranger Things* highlights her retained pop-culture reference base.
