# Chapter 353: Chapter 48 — Best Friends

## Summary
[[prepotente|Prepotente]] lands on his level 70 goat-dragon Bianca. Carl recruits him into the army as a Colonel, stowing away his Conscription potion in relief. 

Suddenly, Prepotente pulls [[carl|Carl]] into a tight hug and begins weeping hysterically, confessing how lonely he has been since his owner's death and admitting he needs to communicate more. Bianca appears embarrassed. Just as suddenly, Prepotente screams directly in Carl's face, startling the pet Rend. 

[[samantha|Samantha]] hops onto Bianca's back, whispering to the familiar and yelling "Kiss! Kiss!" at Carl and Prepotente. Prepotente demands she remove herself. [[katia-grim|Katia]] grabs Samantha and warns her not to mention Prepotente's mother. Prepotente rides off to inspect [[li-na|Li Na's]] clocktower. 

[[elle-mcgibbons|Elle]] messages that they breached the throne room and killed the head of the guard naga (who was also Vinata's boyfriend/cousin). This is the first of the four targets. [[edgar|Edgar]] detects non-corporeal movement coming from below the castle and moving up, confirming the nagas are initiating their escape ritual. Li Na climbs the clocktower to prepare.
