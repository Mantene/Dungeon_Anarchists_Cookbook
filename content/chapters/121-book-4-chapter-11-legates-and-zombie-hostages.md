# Book 4 Chapter 11: Legates and Zombie Hostages

## Summary
Carl, Donut, and Katia carry out their desperate plan to infiltrate the gnomish airship *Wasteland* by faking negotiations using a resurrected hostage and a series of shape-shifting decoys.
- **Preparations**: Alone in the safe room, Carl opens his boss loot box from killing Ruckus, finding 25 guided missile upgrades ("surefires") and *Jellyfish Salve* (to cure pain from Pain Amplifier Jelly stings). He also opens a fan box and receives an *Enchanted Roll of Never-ending Duct Tape* (50 meters, regenerates 1 meter per hour). He uses this to bind their hostage.
- **The Negotiation Meeting**: Carl's team meets the gnomes in the desert. The gnomes descend in a decorated, shielded hot-air balloon named *The Vahana*. The occupants are identified as **Hicks** (Level 25 Aerostat Pilot), **Leon** (Level 44 Negotiator/Commissar), and **Crixus** (Level 52 Sniper Captain).
- **The Ruse**: Katia plays triple duty using her doppelganger abilities, mimicking the back seat of the chariot, the body of the deceased hostage Wynne (resurrected as a temporary zombie by Donut's *Second Chance* spell), and the body of Henrik. To make the ruse convincing, Firas cuts Henrik's "throat" (releasing a bag of real ghoul blood), and Katia retracts Henrik's fake form, exposing the previously slain changeling principal Svern's body to make it look like Henrik was a changeling spy.
- **Boarding the Balloon**: Leon conducts magic spells to test Louis, Donut, and Carl for changeling status. They pass. Just before the spell on Wynne expires and his head turns to dust, Katia regenerates a new head. Carl offers a fake watch as collateral. Leon accepts the trade: Carl, Donut, and the "hostage" Wynne board the balloon, and Carl administers a "healing potion" (actually Mountain Dew) to keep the zombie Wynne calm.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- [[solidarity-and-duty]]: Carl and the team go to extreme lengths, risking their own lives in a complex, multi-layered deception, to save Hump Town and its residents.
- [[systemic-oppression]]: The gnomes' history reveals they were once peaceful flying communities ("sky gardens") who were forced to adapt into dreadnaughts to survive winged predators introduced to their world.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's Glass Cannon class gives her a massive boost to her *Second Chance* spell, enabling this entire plan. The detail of the gnomes smelling of rationed water suggests that despite their aerial dominance, the *Wasteland* is in a state of decay and resource desperation. Carl's father memory highlights his lingering trauma and his coping mechanism of emotional numbness.
