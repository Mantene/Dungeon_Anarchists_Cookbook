# Chapter 274: Evacuated Desperado and Gondii Deal

## Summary
- **Goff's Speech**: Paulie (real name Goff, a gondii Residual) delivers a pre-recorded speech, explaining that Residuals are a hyperspatial, collective-mind race without a home. Their purpose is to teach primal Macro AIs how to speak with ancestral AIs. Before he can explain 'how to shut off the...', the world flashes.
- **The Seizure**: Orren teleport-yanks Carl to the Desperado Club. He claims the Residual had nothing of substance to say and demands the seizure of the plastic bin containing neural enhancement pills.
- **The Deal**: Supported by Quasar, Carl trades the bin to Orren in exchange for keeping their four cretin bodyguards on Floor 9, 3 *Cracker Jack* scrolls, and 1 wand recharge scroll. Carl teleports back to Lightning Lou.
