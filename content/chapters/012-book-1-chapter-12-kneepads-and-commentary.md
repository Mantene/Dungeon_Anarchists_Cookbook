# Book 1 Chapter 12: Kneepads and Commentary

## Summary
The Hoarder drops a Neighborhood Map that reveals a square mile of the surrounding dungeon, including a large pulsing X marking the **Corpse of Crawler Rebecca W (Level 3)**. [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] consult [[mordecai]] at a nearby tutorial guild. Mordecai explains the hierarchy of the six boss types (Neighborhood, Borough, City, Province, Country, Floor) and confirms that many bosses are recycled human stock. 

Donut opens her Boss Box and learns the *Torch* spell, much to her annoyance. Carl reviews his achievements, gaining the **Iron Punch** skill (Level 3). He opens his Gold Crowd Control Box to obtain the **Enchanted Spiked Kneepads of the Shade Gnoll Riot Forces** (reflects 10% damage, cancels momentum attacks). Mordecai mentions that prominent crawlers are sometimes interviewed on post-show televised specials. Donut, eager to get featured and find [[beatrice]] on the 5th or 6th floor, urges Carl to make it happen. Carl keeps his doubts about Beatrice's survival to himself.

## Characters Introduced
- [[rebecca-w]] (Crawler, deceased, first appearance as map icon)

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[televised-exploitation]]**: The mention of televised contestant interviews and specials highlights the show's entertainment-first structure.
- **[[carls-class-selection]]**: Carl gains spiked kneepads and the Iron Punch skill, developing his physical brawler build.
- **[[search-for-beatrice]]**: Donut plans to reunite with Beatrice on deeper floors, unaware Beatrice is likely dead.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Donut's denial about Beatrice's death is heartbreakingly clear here. Carl makes the compassionate choice to shield her from the truth, using the goal of finding Beatrice as a motivator for her to keep crawling.
