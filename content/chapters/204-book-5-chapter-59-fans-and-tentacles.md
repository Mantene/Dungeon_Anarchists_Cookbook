# Book 5 Chapter 59: Fans and Tentacles

## Summary
- **Orren's Farewell**: [[orren]] the liaison explains that his kind is leaving the office until Faction Wars starts. He warns them about other liaisons who meet the job requirement of 'vehemently disagreeing with their home nations' a little too much.
- **Quasar's Legal Freeze**: [[quasar]] appears and explains that all litigation has been frozen by the courts.
- **Obsessive Fan**: Quasar is accompanied by an emotional female fan wearing a scrolling 'Goddamnit, Donut!' shirt, who has a weeping, drooling mouth-tentacle with a bow on her head.
