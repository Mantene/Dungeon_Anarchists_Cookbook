# Book 6 Chapter 11: Asojano in the Saferoom

## Summary
The crawlers interact with the saferoom intruder, Asojano, receive a quest update, and prepare for their media interview.
- **The Interloper Achievement**: Carl receives the *Interloper* achievement and a Bronze Stranger Danger Box for allowing a red-tagged boss inside a saferoom.
- **Asojano's Identity**: The boss is identified as **[[asojano]]** (the Lord of Smallpox), a Level 130 City Boss and former Orisha deity turned Ghommid spirit. He came to visit his past domain, but his divine presence corrupted the local veil and drove the residents mad. He is trapped in the town and his health is slowly draining.
- **Quest Update**: The [[pueblo-de-los-olvidados]] quest updates: crawlers must locate Asojano's shrine in the central temple and repair it to cure the town, or alternatively kill him. Anton plans to wait until Asojano's health drops to 5% to capture him with a flag, while Carl advises caution and stalling.
- **Donut's New Microphone**: Donut equips [[bards-golden-throat]] (an amethyst-encrusted headset microphone) which auto-tunes her songs and amplifies her voice.
- **Interview Call**: Zev interrupts the standoff, messaging that they must transfer immediately to the production trailer for their scheduled interview on *Shadow Boxer*.

## Characters Introduced
- [[asojano]] — Level 130 City Boss, Orisha Lord of Smallpox.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[pueblo-de-los-olvidados]]**: Narrative progression of the cemetery quest, introducing the shrine-repair mechanic.
- **System Anomalies**: An NPC boss entering a saferoom demonstrates the erosion of standard system rules on Floor 8.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Asojano is depicted not as a malicious monster, but as a tragic, fading god visiting the remnants of his history. Donut's acquisition of the *Bard's Golden Throat* is a major upgrade to her combat utility, correcting her off-key notes and enhancing her spellcasting stability.
