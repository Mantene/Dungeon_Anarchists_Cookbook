# Book 1 Chapter 42: Death Watch Extreme

## Summary
[[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] are transported onto the live holo set of *Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem*, hosted by [[the-maestro]], a wild-boar-like orc with a gold tusk and hot pink silk shirt. He refers to the teenaged male audience in Earth orbit as "piglets," who respond with the chant "Glurp, glurp!"

Sitting to their right are two warehouse crawlers, [[li-jun]] and [[zhang]] (both Level 7). A frozen video screen shows their party members—Li Jun's sister [[li-na]] and their manager, whose face is melting from a Brindled Vespa's acid—cornered by Troglodytes and hatching hornets. The Maestro explains the game: Li Jun and Zhang must guess if other crawlers live or die in video clips to earn "teleport points" to save themselves or their friends.

Carl is disgusted by the spectacle. He climbs onto the table and attempts to walk off the set. Kua-tin Admin [[mukta]] and the producer threaten them with drowning or dungeon acceleration. Carl agrees to return on the condition that they are put on screen immediately.

Li Jun and Zhang win two points. The Maestro tries to force them to decide which party members to abandon. Li Jun refuses to choose, declaring they will die together. Zhang tries to sacrifice himself to save Li Jun and Li Na. 

Carl interrupts, mocking the host and challenging the Maestro to use the two points to teleport Carl and Donut into the fight instead. The crowd roars in approval. The Maestro cannot comply because Carl and Donut are too high-value and expensive to be sent into a lethal trap. 

During the host's hesitation, Carl quickly whispers tactical instructions to Li Jun: "grab your sister and the manager, ignore the sword, and run straight through the troglodytes." 

In a fit of red-faced rage, the Maestro slams a button, teleporting the group back to the dungeon. The scene unfreezes. Following Carl's advice, they bypass the hornets, rush through the slow troglodytes, and escape to a safe room. 

Carl smiles at the humiliated host and says: "Glurp on that, motherfucker."

## Characters Introduced
- **[[li-jun]]**: Level 7 warehouse crawler.
- **[[zhang]]**: Bald Level 7 warehouse crawler with 15 Constitution.
- **[[li-na]]**: Li Jun's sister, warehouse crawler.

## Themes & Plot Threads
- **[[televised-exploitation]]**: The show represents the most sadistic end of intergalactic entertainment, forcing crawlers to gamble for their companions' lives.
- **[[survival]]**: Carl uses the show's live broadcast format and his own high appearance fee to manipulate the host and save the trapped crawlers.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl's rebellion against the show's structure is a brilliant exploitation of the Syndicate's capitalistic model. He realizes that as a highly popular and "expensive" crawler, the showrunners cannot afford to kill him off-screen on a low-budget show. His quick thinking saves Li Jun's party, proving that cooperation and solidarity can still exist under extreme duress.
