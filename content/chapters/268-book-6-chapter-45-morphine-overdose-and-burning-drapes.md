# Chapter 268: Morphine Overdose and Burning Drapes

## Summary
- **Robert's Illness**: Carl watches the memory of his father, Robert, wasting away from terminal illness in a hospital bed.
- **Asher's Entrance**: Carl's six-year-old half-brother Asher enters. Robert aggressively demands his cigarettes, and Asher flees to his room. Stepmother Tami-Lynn returns from helping the neighbors.
- **The Overdose**: Carl loots the master bedroom (Glock 9mm, Centipede arcade cabinet, Marlboro Reds) and watches Tami-Lynn inject Robert with a lethal overdose of morphine (hidden in the allium pots). Carl sets the drapes on fire to burn the trailer down.

## Personal Interpretation & Reflections
Carl discovers his half-brother Asher and watches his stepmother poison his father to protect the child. Setting the drapes on fire is Carl's refusal to fight within the showrunners' constructed emotional space.
