# Borant Corporation

**Species:** Kua-tin (aquatic, fish-like humanoids; about 2 feet tall; wear deep-sea suits in non-aquatic environments)  
**Role:** Planetary regent for Earth's dungeon season; producer of *Dungeon Crawler World: Earth*

---

## Overview

Borant was assigned regency over Earth's solar system by the Syndicate under the Precious Elemental Reserves Code. They chose Option 3 (the 18-Level World Dungeon) as the manner of resource reclamation. The dungeon is both a mining operation and a for-profit entertainment venture broadcast galaxy-wide.

Borant produces the main program *Dungeon Crawler World: Earth*. They retain all broadcast, exploitation, and control rights as long as they adhere to Syndicate regulations.

---

## Financial Situation (Book 1)

Borant is effectively bankrupt. The ultra-nationalist kua-tin political party (The Bloom) has bankrupted the kua-tin system government, which in turn is starving Borant. Borant doesn't receive advertising/tourism/patronage income until the season ends. The Party is pressuring them to end the game quickly, while the company's internal factions want to keep it running for maximum profit.

The early opening of Earth's dungeon (2 years ahead of schedule) is tied to this financial crisis — they needed the cash sooner. The Rage Elemental punishment, the shortened Floor 2 timer, and other "bugs" are attributed to the Party trying to accelerate crawler deaths.

## Structure

- **The Party (The Bloom):** Ultra-nationalist faction within kua-tin government; controls Borant politically; party members wear distinctive badges
- **Non-Party employees:** Like Zev, who are afraid for their families; these folks are more pragmatic and sometimes sympathetic
- **Damien:** Location manager for Floor 2 "city" areas; Mordecai's manager's manager
- **Zev:** Assistant Communications Representative; assigned as Carl and Donut's PR agent

---

## Key Rules Imposed on Crawlers

1. Don't disparage Borant or the Syndicate on camera (violators get their "experience accelerated")
2. No urinating/defecating outside designated bathrooms (Floors 1–2 → Rage Elemental punishment; removed Floor 3+)
3. Premature stairwell descent = stasis until floor collapse (no viewer access = no social number gains)
4. Safe rooms close 1 hour before floor collapse

## Zev

[Zev](../characters/zev.md): Assigned PR agent for Carl and Donut. Non-Party kua-tin in an elaborate retro deep-sea suit (her first dungeon visit). Fears for her family if she displeases Party members. Genuinely on Carl and Donut's side because their survival = her bonus. Bonded with Donut over *Gossip Girl*.
