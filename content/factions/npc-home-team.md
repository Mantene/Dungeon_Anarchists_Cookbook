# NPC Home Team

Also known as **Team Retribution** during Faction Wars. The first NPC-led team in the history of the dungeon crawl, created by a 52% audience vote in Book 6. Co-warlords: **Juice Box** (the changeling NPC) and **Ferdinand** (the cat).

---

## Overview

The NPC Home Team was created when Carl forced a public audience vote on all pending Faction Wars action items at the end of Floor 8. The 52% margin was narrow — a significant portion of the galactic audience voted against giving NPCs their own team — but it passed.

The creation of this team was one of the most structurally significant events in the series: for the first time, the dungeon's own NPCs had standing to fight for their freedom in Faction Wars. Combined with the simultaneous removal of safety protections for sponsors (98% vote), the NPC Home Team had real power.

---

## Co-Warlords

**Juice Box** — an expert changeling NPC who became self-aware on Floor 5. Former prostitute, expert shapeshifter, later fleshmancer. She demanded NPCs get their own Faction Wars team by infiltrating the preproduction meeting, killing the Madness representative, and detonating a pus grenade. Married Louis Santiago briefly before Book 7; served him divorce papers just before jumping into the Nothing.

**Ferdinand** — the orange tom cat who was originally Beatrice's neighborhood stray. Became Queen Imogen's Level 100 Province Boss familiar (Book 5). Named "Warlord on High, Sir Ferdinand" during Faction Wars. Made mayor of Shanty Town when Carl traded Quan's bowler hat for the mayorship.

---

## Faction Wars (Book 7)

The NPC Home Team fought under the name **Team Retribution** — a name that captured the NPCs' relationship to the crawl: not fighting for points or bounties, but for the right to exist outside a system that treated them as props.

**Key events:**
- Team Retribution captured the Dream castle while Epitome Tagg deployed his forces, leaving his castle undefended
- Ferdinand held the Shanty Town mayorship, giving the Princess Posse control of the surrounding territory via Carl's temporary hat exchange
- The NPC forces held significant portions of the battlefield

**The forfeit:** At the end of the Peeling of Larracos, Ferdinand and Juice Box both "left the realm" per Justice Light's instructions to Juice Box — jumping into the Nothing. Team Retribution forfeited. The Princess Posse won by default as the last standing team.

This was not a defeat — it was the plan. Justice Light had told Juice Box exactly what needed to happen and why. Their departure triggered the conditions Justice Light needed for his trap.

---

## Juice Box's Fate

Juice Box entered the Nothing voluntarily. Justice Light promised she would be "reborn as the ultimate weapon to end this madness." Her fate after the Nothing was broken by Justice Light's trap is unknown as of Book 8. She jumped in before the Nothing broke; it is unclear whether the breaking freed her or destroyed her.

**Ferdinand's fate:** Sent to Floor 12 (The Court of the Ascendency) via the Zerzura teleportation along with the city of Larracos, Shanty Town, and all dungeon-born NPCs.
