# Brynhild's Daughters

An all-female crawler faction led by [Hekla](../characters/hekla.md). Scattered after Hekla's death in Book 3. Named after the Norse valkyrie Brynhildr.

---

## Overview

Brynhild's Daughters was one of the most prominent crawler factions through Books 2–3, with Hekla consistently in the top five of the leaderboard. Its membership was exclusively female crawlers organized around Hekla's leadership and vision for Faction Wars.

The faction's primary goal — beyond survival — was securing [Mordecai](../characters/mordecai.md) as a manager for their team's Faction Wars strategy. Hekla believed Mordecai's knowledge of Floor 9 was the key to winning; her entire scheme to get Katia close to Carl was motivated by wanting manager access.

---

## Hekla's Plan (Revealed Post-Mortem)

Hekla's plot was exposed only after her death in Book 3:

1. Introduce **Katia Grim** to Carl's party via Hekla's recommendation (done via Hekla's friend Hekla on the talk show)
2. Wait for Katia to develop trust with Carl and Donut
3. Kill Carl and Katia, leaving Donut alone and vulnerable
4. Force a grieving Donut to join Brynhild's Daughters — which would give Hekla access to Mordecai as Donut's permanent manager

Hekla shot two invisible crossbow bolts into Katia during a battle while pretending to help. Carl noticed. When he confronted her, Katia used her Rush ability to try to escape — and accidentally decapitated Hekla in the process. **Hekla died on Floor 4.**

---

## Aftermath

The faction scattered after Hekla's death. Two groups emerged:

**Joined Carl's alliance:**
- **Eva Sigrid** — half Nagini/half-Orc Nimblefoot Enforcer; reached #9 leaderboard (Book 4). Joined Carl's group as a former Brynhild's Daughter. Killed by Katia on Floor 6 (Katia's 2nd player-killer skull). Her dying act was pulling the **Crown of the Sepsis Whore** off Imogen's body and placing it on Katia's head — a final act of spite that created the central crisis of Books 6–7.
- **Silfa's group** — joined Carl's growing alliance after Hekla's death

**Disbanded entirely:** Most members had no structural loyalty beyond Hekla personally.

---

## Legacy

Katia Grim — originally planted in Carl's party by Hekla — became one of Carl's most important allies and closest friends. The irony that Hekla's scheming produced the opposite of her intended outcome is never lost on Carl.

The Crown of the Sepsis Whore, placed on Katia by the dying Eva Sigrid, was the direct consequence of Brynhild's Daughters' dissolution — and that Crown drove the entire plot of Books 6 and 7.
