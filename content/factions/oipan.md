# OIPAN / OIAN

The **Open Intellect Pacifist Action Network** — a galaxy-wide anti-crawl organization operating as a Non-Force Corporation (NFC). The hidden hand behind many of Carl's most significant allies and resources, including the [Princess Posse](princess-posse.md) and the Homecoming Queen's army of 50,000 former crawlers.

---

## Overview

OIPAN is not simply a charity. Its goal is the fundamental dismantling of the dungeon crawl system — not just rescuing individual crawlers, but ending the Syndicate-backed planetary exploitation that makes crawls possible. Because openly opposing the crawl violates numerous Syndicate contracts, they operate through shell organizations, coded communication, and indirect influence.

By Book 7, the organization has reorganized under the name **OIAN** (Open Intellect Action Network, dropping "Pacifist") and its leadership is wanted by Syndicate Security.

## Actions by Book

### Book 4 — First Contact

OIPAN became Carl's sponsor under the name **Open Intellect Pacifist Action Network, NFC**. Their first gift: a **toraline yam** (a root vegetable Mordecai didn't recognize). Leaders Dr. Hu and Porthus said Carl was "already shaking things up" and they were "going all in on him."

The yam's purpose remained mysterious until Book 6, when its root was used as portal ink for Shi Maria's tattoo.

### Book 5 — Princess Posse Sponsorship

OIPAN sponsored the Princess Posse under the shell name **The Society for the Eradication of Cocker Spaniels (TSECS)**, an NFC. This gave Carl and Donut a Faction Wars slot and legal backing without triggering Syndicate scrutiny.

### Book 6 — Shadow Boxer and Coded Messages

OIPAN funded the talk show **Shadow Boxer**, hosted by **Rosetta Thagra** (9th edition cookbook author). During Carl's interview, Rosetta passed him a hidden message via all-Miami TV screencaps shown to Donut — directing them to Miami for Phase 2, and clarifying that the toraline yam "has a purpose related to portals/mapmaking ink."

**Harbinger** (the Plenty caprid liaison) ended the interview early, claiming they were "talking in code." He was right.

**The Homecoming Queen Reveal (Book 6 epilogue):** Tipid and Rosetta emerged as organizers of a converted garbage freighter with **50,000 former crawlers**. The AI approved their entry as Princess Posse mercenaries. Porthus had been organizing the mission for years.

### Book 7 — The Full Deployment

The 50,000 former crawlers deployed as Princess Posse mercenaries. Porthus organized:
- The **generation ship purchase**: Using King Rust's retina scan and bank codes (obtained post-decapitation by Rosetta), they purchased two naga generation ships for humanity to use post-crawl
- **Families' escape**: Organized evacuation routes to the Plenty system for Earth survivors
- **Phase 2**: After Faction Wars, the Homecoming Queen began loading for the next phase — bringing the war out of the dungeon and into the physical world. Boomer's soldiers prepared breaching suits and Cronus suits.

Porthus is now listed as missing/wanted by Syndicate Security.

## Shell Organizations Used

- **TSECS** (The Society for the Eradication of Cocker Spaniels) — Princess Posse sponsor
- **OIPAN/OIAN** — primary identity; NFC status gives legal protection against some Syndicate enforcement

---

## Relationship to the Cookbook Network

OIPAN and the Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook network are functionally the same organization viewed from different angles: the Cookbook is the tactical knowledge base passed between seasons; OIPAN is the strategic organization of survivors who used that knowledge to crawl out and stay out to fight the system.
