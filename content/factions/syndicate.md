# The Syndicate

**Type:** Galactic government  
**Scale:** 3+ billion independent star systems

The Syndicate is the overarching galactic legal framework. Borant operates under Syndicate law (specifically the Precious Elemental Reserves Code and the Indigenous Planetary Species Protection Act).

## Key Laws Relevant to Earth

- **Precious Elemental Reserves Code, §543:** A planet whose inhabitants fail to file an appeal within 50 Solars of First Contact can be seized and mined.
- **First Contact:** Made when the pyramids were built, thousands of years ago. Humanity had 50 years to file an appeal. They didn't.
- **Mined Material Reclamation Act + Indigenous Planetary Species Protection Act §35:** Surviving inhabitants get a chance to reclaim their matter via the World Dungeon (Option 3 of available reclamation methods).
- **World Dungeon completion:** If a crawler completes Level 18, planetary regency reverts to "the successor."

## Neutral Observer AI

A Syndicate-created AI was dispatched to Earth to supervise the dungeon and ensure rules are followed. This AI narrates events, manages mob spawning, enforces rules, and generates loot. It has a darkly humorous personality and appears to have at least some aesthetic preferences (the "foot fetish AI" that sends Carl footcare items).

## Six Starter Species

The Syndicate seeds worlds with one of six base species. Humans are one of them. Earth was seeded thousands of years ago.

## Other Corporations / Seasons

Different corporations run each dungeon season (~every 2.25 years). Borant is known for the most elaborate dungeons. The Squim Conglomerate runs a battle royale format. The Skull Empire / Valtay Corporation are mentioned as upcoming.
