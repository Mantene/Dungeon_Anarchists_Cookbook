# The Bloom

Also called **"the Party."** The ultra-nationalist kua-tin political party that seized control of the kua-tin government and, by extension, their corporate instrument [Borant Corporation](borant-corporation.md). By the time of the series, the Bloom is the effective power behind the dungeon.

---

## Overview

> "The Bloom (ultra-nationalist kua-tin 'Party,' like Nazi Germany) now controls the kua-tin government. Borant is bankrupt; the Party is rushing the dungeon to end quickly and collect its payout."
> — [Zev via Mordecai's whispered warning, Book 1](../sources/book-1-dungeon-crawler-carl.md)

The kua-tin are a small species — Borant's PR agent [Zev](../characters/zev.md) is described as two feet tall, in a retro deep-sea suit. Zev is notably **not** a member of the Bloom; she fears for her family precisely because she is a non-Party kua-tin.

The Bloom's ideology is consistent with an aggressive ethno-nationalist movement: swift resource extraction, consolidation of power, and the prioritization of the Party's interests over any external considerations including the crawlers, the audience, or even Borant's contractual obligations.

---

## Role in the Crawl

The Bloom's agenda explains several critical dynamics in Book 1:

1. **Borant is bankrupt.** The company has no money. The entire dungeon operation is running on fumes and the Bloom's political backing.
2. **They are rushing the dungeon to end.** A normal dungeon season might be extended for entertainment value; the Bloom wants it concluded quickly so the payout can be collected before Borant's creditors arrive.
3. **The timer on Floor 2 was shortened.** The expected 10-day timer was cut to 6 days — consistent with the Bloom's push to accelerate.

This pressure from the Bloom is why Borant agents like Zev are anxious, why some editorial choices feel rushed, and why the dungeon's production quality occasionally slips.

---

## Relationship to Other Factions

- **Borant Corporation**: The Bloom controls Borant's government. Borant is the operational face of the crawl; the Bloom provides the political backing and the urgency.
- **The Valtay Corporation**: Borant's financial situation created leverage for Valtay, which in Book 2 orchestrated the destruction of Prince Stalwart's yacht to shatter the Skull Empire/Valtay alliance (possibly as a move against the Bloom's backers).
- **The Syndicate**: The Bloom operates within the Syndicate's legal framework; they cannot openly break Syndicate contracts without losing legal standing.

---

## Book 1 Implications

The Bloom's existence is revealed in the context of Zev's situation: she is a low-level corporate employee in a system now controlled by an extremist party she is afraid of. Her kindness toward Carl and Donut is partly genuine affection (she bonds with Donut over *Gossip Girl*) and partly the action of someone with nothing left to lose.

After Book 1, the Bloom is not directly referenced by name in later books — it functions as background context for why Borant behaves as it does.
