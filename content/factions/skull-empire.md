# Skull Empire

An orcish empire and one of the most politically powerful entities in the galaxy. Consistently hostile to Carl throughout the series, though the Empire fractures progressively as its royal family is decimated. By Book 8, Princess Formidable rules.

---

## Overview

The Skull Empire is a major galactic power. Their species (orcs) occupies a dominant position in galactic politics and entertainment, evidenced by the Empire having its own dungeon-hosted talk show ("The Maestro's show") by Book 1. They view Carl's success — a human from a barely-significant planet — as an insult to the natural galactic order.

## History with Carl

**Book 1:** The first antagonism. Carl's appearance on "The Maestro's show" (hosted by Crown Prince Maestro) ends with Carl antagonizing the Maestro live on galactic TV. The "Pork Boy Snick" deepfake video goes viral, publicly humiliating the Skull Empire.

**Book 2:** Crown Prince Stalwart fires a pulse at Carl's production trailer. Due to the trailer swap (Manasa offered her nicer trailer), the pulse kills Manasa instead. Valtay retaliates: destroys Stalwart's yacht, killing Queen Consort Ugloo and (apparently) Prince Maestro. King Rust is now furious at Carl. The Valtay/Skull Empire alliance is shattered.

**Book 3:** Prince Maestro is revealed to be alive — he is secretly sponsoring War God Grull. He appears as commentator during the Mimic Rex fight. The Maestro's survival was apparently staged.

**Book 4:** Princess Formidable (King Rust's only surviving daughter, now heir after Stalwart's presumed death) becomes **Katia Grim's sponsor** — the only positive relationship between the Skull Empire and Carl's party.

**Book 6:** Formidable watches the situation deteriorate from orbit. She is increasingly alarmed by the dungeon AI's behavior and demands the failsafe be pulled; the council refuses.

**Book 7:** 
- King Rust arrives in person for a parlay. He warns Carl that Formidable is heading to a failsafe satellite to destroy the solar system.
- Rosetta decapitates Rust mid-parlay with a whip. She needed his retina scan and bank PIN to buy humanity two naga generation ships. (Rust had casually killed her friend Brooks with the same whip decades earlier during a previous Faction Wars.)
- The Skull Empire's **Bone Clan** was their Faction Wars team.

**Book 8:** York (Homecoming Queen captain) killed the orc yacht carrying King Rust's underage children, clearing the succession and making Princess Formidable the new Skull Empire ruler. Formidable is now the most pragmatic member of the royal family left standing.

## Faction Wars Team

The **Bone Clan** represented the Skull Empire in Faction Wars (Floor 9). They were part of "The Bloc" — the nine attacking teams that united against both the Princess Posse and Team Retribution.
