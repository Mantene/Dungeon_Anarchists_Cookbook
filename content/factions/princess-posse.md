# Princess Posse

The Faction Wars team co-warlorded by [Carl](../characters/carl.md) and [Princess Donut](../characters/princess-donut.md). Won Floor 9's Faction Wars in Book 7, eliminating all eight opposing teams. Secretly sponsored by [OIPAN](oipan.md).

---

## Overview

The Princess Posse was formed at the end of Book 5 after the Butcher's Masquerade, when Carl and Donut inherited two abandoned faction spots. The team started with zero mercenaries and no infrastructure — just a valid faction war slot and a sponsor.

By Book 7, it had grown into the winning faction through a combination of 50,000 former-crawler mercenaries, strategic sponsor kills enabled by an audience vote Carl orchestrated, and the general organizational chaos Carl brought to every floor.

---

## Formation (Book 5)

After the Masquerade, Carl and Donut became Co-Warlords by inheriting:
- **The Dreadnoughts** (abandoned)
- **The Burrowers** (the mantis hunter faction, abandoned after Vrah and Circe Took's deaths)

**Sponsor:** The Society for the Eradication of Cocker Spaniels (TSECS) — a shell organization for [OIPAN](oipan.md), the Open Intellect Pacifist Action Network.

Starting resources: 0 mercenaries, 0 territory, 2 inherited faction spots, and Carl's pending motion to remove safety protections for sponsors.

---

## Book 6 — Rule Changes

Carl used one of three Faction Wars emergency action items to force a public audience vote on all pending action items. Two measures passed with massive margins:

| Vote | Result | Effect |
|------|--------|--------|
| **Remove Safety Protections for Faction Sponsors** | **98% YES** | All sponsor leaders can now be killed during Faction Wars — transforming it from a proxy war into a real one |
| **NPC 10th Team ("Home Team")** | **52% YES** | A new team led by Juice Box and Ferdinand the cat was created, giving dungeon NPCs their own faction |

The first vote was the decisive strategic move that made the Princess Posse's Book 7 victories possible. Without it, killing King Rust, Empress D'Nadia, Huanxin Jinx, and the other warlords would have been illegal.

## Book 7 — Faction Wars

**The Homecoming Queen arrives:** 50,000 former crawlers (led by Tipid, Rosetta, and Justice Light) entered as Princess Posse mercenaries. Combined with ~30,000 surviving active crawlers, the Posse's total force reached ~80,000 against the Bloc's ~150,000 enemy mercenaries.

**Teams eliminated by the Princess Posse and allies:**

| Team | How Eliminated |
|------|----------------|
| Prism Kingdom (Empress D'Nadia) | Infiltrated; Mongo given credit for kill |
| Operatic Collective (Hortense) | Volteeg's suicide tank bombing |
| Blood Sultanate (Sultana Vinata) | Killed in Club Vanquisher battle |
| Skull Empire / Bone Clan (King Rust) | Rosetta decapitated Rust; army charged; ultimately eliminated |
| Dream (Epitome Tagg) | Mongo ate his extracted organs |
| Reavers | Colonel Boomer's forces |
| Democratic Sortion | Eliminated before Floor 9 fighting began |
| War Mage Rebellion (Agatha) | Held castle near-6 hours; defeated by Donut's sniping + city defenses |

**Win condition:** Hold the Larracos throne room for 6 consecutive hours. The Princess Posse held it.

**Team Retribution (NPC Home Team) forfeited** when Ferdinand and Juice Box jumped into the Nothing per Justice Light's plan.

**Princess Posse wins Faction Wars.**
