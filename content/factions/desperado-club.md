# The Desperado Club

A members-only nightclub and casino that appears on every floor from 3 onward. Membership requires a **Desperado Pass tattoo** — awarded for killing 20 or more non-combatants in a single attack. The Club is neutral ground, accessible to any crawler or NPC with the mark.

---

## Overview

> "Desperado Pass tattoo: Granted for killing 20+ non-combatants in one attack. Grants access to the Desperado Club."
> — [Book 1: Dungeon Crawler Carl](../sources/book-1-dungeon-crawler-carl.md)

The Club occupies a unique structural position in the dungeon: it is neither a faction in the political sense nor a dungeon-controlled location. It operates as an independent neutral space — a place where the dungeon's normal hostility rules are suspended, where crawlers can rest, gamble, trade, and plot without the floor's mobs interfering.

Both Carl and Donut received Desperado Pass tattoos in Book 1 when they killed the Goblin War Chieftain via cart + black powder + hobgoblin pus (killing goblin non-combatants). Katia received hers in Book 3 when she accidentally killed Ji-Hoon at the club.

---

## Physical Layout

**Public section:** A pub open to anyone with a Desperado Pass. Mordecai is known here; he "knows people" in the Club. NPCs and crawlers mingle freely.

**Members-only section:** Requires active membership status (not just the tattoo). Contains the casino, private meeting rooms, and the secret passages.

**The Casino:** Features the High Roller's Roulette Table and the Wheel of Fortune. Carl had a **100,000 Gold Casino Chip** (non-cashable, non-transferable comp chip) from Book 2; he used it at the Scroll of Upgrade station in Book 3 to upgrade his toe ring to +10 STR.

**The Guild of Suffering:** A secret sub-organization within the Club. Carl joined in Book 6 (gaining access via stripper Damascus Steel and the Penis Parade's back room), which gave him a full map of the Club's secret passages.

## Key Events

**Book 2 — First visit:**
Carl receives a 100,000 Gold Casino Chip from a Lucky Bastard box. Details of the club are not fully explored this visit; Mordecai knows regulars there.

**Book 3 — Casino use:**
Carl uses the Scroll of Upgrade at the casino, upgrading his toe ring to +10 STR and Powerful Strike to Level 5. Katia accidentally kills Ji-Hoon during her visit, earning her Desperado Pass tattoo.

**Book 5 — Floor 6 dealings:**
The Desperado Club on Floor 6 is used by Carl and Donut for supplies and planning. It remains a neutral-ground refuge during the otherwise intensely hostile Hunting Grounds floor.

**Book 6 — Assassination of Astrid:**
Carl entered the Guild of Suffering through the Penis Parade's back room, gaining a full map of the Club's secret passages. This intelligence was used to plan the assassination of **Astrid** (Level 125 bloodlust sprite, assistant manager of the Desperado Club). Carl and Katia executed the assassination using card combat, Katia's shapeshifting, and the Club's secret passages.

> "Katia's Guild of Suffering quest complete."
> — [Book 6: The Eye of the Bedlam Bride](../sources/book-6-eye-of-the-bedlam-bride.md)

## Relationship to OIPAN and the Cookbook Network

The Desperado Club's neutrality makes it a natural meeting ground for the informal network of crawlers, former crawlers, and NPC sympathizers that includes the [Dungeon Anarchists Cookbook](../mechanics/game-systems.md) network and OIPAN adjacent figures. Mordecai's connections here date back to his own crawl decades earlier.
