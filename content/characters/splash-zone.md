# Splash Zone

**Splash Zone** is an NPC mercenary and former stripper from the Penis Parade who serves in [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]]'s Personal Guard platoon during the Ninth Floor Faction Wars.

## Profile & Traits
- **Affiliation**: Princess Posse (Personal Guard platoon).
- **Role**: Defender and transport driver.
- **Personality**: Cooperative and battle-ready, willing to take on logistics and defense roles in the army.

## History
Splash Zone was hired alongside other Penis Parade staff using a "Meat Shields" coupon. When the ceasefire collapses early in Book 7, Chapter 20, she is assigned to the Personal Guard platoon. She takes control of one of the flatbed troop transports and drives the defenders toward the southern front to face the incoming Naga forces.
