# Ji-Hoon

**Ji-Hoon** was a Level 21 Asian crawler who specialized as a Knife Sharpener. He is deceased.

## Biography and Role
Ji-Hoon was an approximately 25-year-old crawler who was killed in a high-profile assassination inside the members-only lounge of the Desperado Club. He was targeted by an assassin in Chapter 84, triggering a security panic and leading to an official apology from the club's representative, Astrid.

## Attributes
- **Race**: Human (Asian)
- **Level**: 21
- **Class**: Knife Sharpener
- **Status**: Deceased (assassinated in the Desperado Club)
