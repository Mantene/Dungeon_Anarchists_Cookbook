# Alexandro (Parvati / Agent 21)

**Alexandro** (originally known as **Parvati** or **Agent 21**) is an ancient entity belonging to the same unnamed ancient species as [[agatha]] (Agent 22).

## Biography & Role in the Epilogue
Parvati peeled away from her species' collective and entered Earth at the same time as Agatha. 

In the Epilogue, she contacts Agatha from the Mumbai site. She has taken a massive risk by killing a Borant agent and infiltrating their transport, discarding her previous shell. In doing so, she has fully taken over the host, adopting the name **Alexandro** to hide her identity. She reports that a Borant forward team has landed on the Mumbai site with all their historical mapping activity.

Agatha warns Alexandro that the traitor (the Apothecary) is calling her agents to the same area.
