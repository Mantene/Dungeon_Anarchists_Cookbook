# Arwick

**Arwick** is a deceased High-Elf hunter belonging to the hunter faction [[the-dream]], who was active on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Profile & Traits
- **Appearance**: Like all members of the Dream, Arwick is pale-skinned and bald. 
- **Gear**: He carried a quiver of unenchanted arrows, a magical arrow of *Enthusiastic Double Gonorrhea*, and wore a flowing enchanted robe (+Con and speed).

## Floor 6 Events
- **Death**: In [[160-book-5-chapter-15-hunting-map]] (Chapter 15), Arwick is killed in the forest by [[areson-the-wise]], who dumps his body at [[carl]]'s feet.
- **The Map**: Carl loots Arwick's corpse, finding a hand-drawn map of the hunting grounds on the back of a Canadian kids' diner menu. The map reveals the partition of the Hunting Grounds among the hunter groups and their targeted crawlers.
