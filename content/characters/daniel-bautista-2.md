# Daniel Bautista 2

Daniel Bautista 2 is a high-performing crawler who survives the collapse and progresses through the dungeon.

## Biography
Daniel is the brother and cousin of the Bautista sisters ([[grace-bautista]], [[nica-bautista]], and [[lea-bautista]]), who were killed by lemurs on Floor 2. He and another cousin escaped, but the cousin gave up and died the next day, leaving Daniel as the sole survivor of his family of nineteen. He meets [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] in person on Floor 3 (Chapter 23, [[072-book-2-chapter-23-daniel-bautista]]) after Carl accidentally detonates the boss corpse Daniel had killed (The Divider). 

Daniel reveals that his own quest was to assassinate [[miss-quill]] to gain access to the Desperado Club, but Carl's explosion stole his kill. Despite this "cockblock," Daniel is grateful to Carl for avenging his family on Floor 2. He shares the location of another stairwell to Floor 4 and establishes a chat connection with Carl.

In Book 3 (*The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook*), Daniel reaches **Level 28**. During Floor 4 ("The Iron Tangle"), he helps Carl map out the stations, explaining the Desperado Club and Club Vanquisher station naming rules. 

Daniel's group faces a series of massive crises on Floor 4:
*   In [[093-book-3-chapter-18-station-mimic-and-betrayal-coupons]], they arrive at Terminus Station 433, which transforms into a massive Station Mimic that swallows several crawlers. Daniel's group fights off the mimic's tongue and "mouth thing" minions.
*   In [[095-book-3-chapter-20-planet-beautiful-and-frank-qs-death]], they attempt to paralyze the Kravyad at Station 435 to bypass security, but accidentally kill her. This causes the employee portal to snap shut, leaving over 150 crawlers stranded—including Daniel, who had sold his conductor hat.
*   Following Carl's advice, Daniel leads the survivors on a 50-kilometer hike to the abyss portal, fighting Wall Monitor lizards and losing fifty crawlers to gravity effects and collapsing walkways.
*   In [[103-book-3-chapter-28-the-abyss-rescue-and-the-station-mimic]], Carl arrives at the abyss and distributes the 600 conductor hats he gathered, allowing Daniel and the other crawlers to safely step through the static portal. In gratitude, Daniel and the survivors drop their hats in a pile for Princess Donut as a royal tribute.

## Race & Class
*   **Race**: **Tigran**. A humanoid big-cat race resembling a Siberian tiger with vertical-slit eyes. The race grants high physical capabilities but lacks footwear compatibility. It also grants specific summoning magic; on Floor 4, Daniel summons lion-like **Jaxbrin** beasts as combat pets.
*   **Class**: **Swashbuckler**. A melee specialist class. Daniel wields a curved sword that glows orange.

## Achievements
Daniel holds nine neighborhood boss markers by the end of Floor 3, indicating his high efficiency in combat.
