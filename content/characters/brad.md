# Brad

Brad was a human survivor on the surface of Earth in Seattle. He was [[beatrice]]'s new partner, having been with her on a flight returning from the Bahamas when the collapse occurred.

## Biography & Death
- **Surface Survival**: Brad lives with Beatrice in the New Queen Anne settlement in Seattle. He is frustrated by Beatrice's emotional instability and her obsession with finding her neighbor's cat [[gravy-boat]] (Ferdinand), and regrets not entering the dungeon himself, fantasizing that he would be a "king" inside.
- **Death**: In [[145-book-4-epilogue-epilogue]], when [[sadir]] and [[gennrik]] break into their tent, Brad grabs a crowbar. After Gennrik is shot by [[lexis]], Brad tries to grab Gennrik's dropped alien weapon. Lexis shoots Brad and leaves him to bleed out and die under Odette's orders.
