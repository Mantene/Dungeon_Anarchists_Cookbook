# Tom

Tom is a Level 75 Shaman turkey boss on Floor 8 (The Ghosts of Earth). He governs a massive spliced-in turkey barn in Iowa/Florida containing tens of thousands of Level 5 turkeys. He activates when Mongo goes wild eating the turkeys, initiating a double deckmaster battle alongside Sharp-Elbows. Tom is killed when Carl uses the Daughter's Kiss soul release to stomp-obliterate him.
