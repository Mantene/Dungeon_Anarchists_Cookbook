# Quemada

**Quemada** is a fire fairy mercenary hired by [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] for the tenth-floor vehicle races in Book 8 (*A Parade of Horribles*).

## History & Dungeon Presence
Quemada suffers from a severe panic disorder, which worsened drastically after the death of her ice fairy companion [[helado]]. In [[441-book-8-chapter-42-forest-flight]], she experiences a full-blown existential panic attack, crying and asking what she can burn, which forces [[finley]] to pinch her with a sedative to put her to sleep.

In [[443-book-8-chapter-44-pit-stop-tragedy]], while still sedated, she is carried by Finley out of the garage at the Lost City of Upano pit stop. The group is immediately run over and killed by the speeding APV of Team One Fine Pig. Upon her death, she poofs out in a flare, leaving a scorch mark on the pavement.
