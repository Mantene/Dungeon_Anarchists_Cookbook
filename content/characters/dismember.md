# Dismember

**Dismember** was a War Mage NPC on the fourth floor of the World Dungeon. He is deceased.

## Biography and Role
Dismember was a silver-haired elf-like War Mage who controlled a pack of Fleshers (Level 10 Swamp Orc Fleshers) on the *Dismemberment Limited* train. He served as the primary antagonist of the recurring quest "Levi is On the Menu!"

Dismember sought to capture Levi the Seventh to "re-sleeve" him, claiming he only needed eight more iterations of the loop to reveal "what we are." He laughed as his minions tried to break down the metal barrier Carl's party used to block them. 

## Death
Carl's party successfully defeats Dismember in Chapter 88, which causes his Fleshers to lose their master and revert to "Bereft Minions." After his death, one of his former minions devours his corpse to re-sleeve themselves, continuing the cycle.
