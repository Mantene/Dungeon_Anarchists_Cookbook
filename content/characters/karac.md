# Karac

**Karac** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

Karac is a Level 62 crawler identified as an 'Armchair Pacifist' who is paradoxically known for 'particularly brutal chainsaw kills.' He is seen receiving tea from Linus at the gathering in Hungry Eyes Village.
