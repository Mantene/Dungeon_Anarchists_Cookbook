# Bruna the Gnu

**Bruna the Gnu** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A gnu participating in the dungeon's race. She demonstrates unusual abilities, such as spontaneously sprouting Pegasus-like wings, allowing her to gain an advantage during the descent into apartment 712.
