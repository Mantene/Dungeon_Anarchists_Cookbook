# Dungeon AI

**Dungeon AI** is a antagonist character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

The primary artificial intelligence that controls the Dungeon and orchestrates the crawl. The AI actively manipulates events and interacts with crawlers, as demonstrated by its past actions, including taking control of an Emberus priest to interact directly with Carl. Carl suspects the AI has a vested interest in the current mysteries but may not fully comprehend them itself.
