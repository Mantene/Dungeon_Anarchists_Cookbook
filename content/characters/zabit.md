# Zabit

**Zabit** is a hunter on Floor 6 (The Hunting Grounds). 

## Profile
- **Abilities**: Zabit possesses high charisma, which allows him to tame and coordinate a pack of invisible **Night Weasels**—vicious, six-legged ferret-like pack predators that hunt and paralyze targets.
- **Actions**: In [[136-book-4-chapter-26-mouthbrooding-mayhem]] (Chapter 2), Zabit's Night Weasel scouts (including one named Tootsie) ambush and paralyze [[carl]] and the Bugbear Settlement's guards. The attack is thwarted by [[princess-donut]] and [[brandon-an]] (Chris).
