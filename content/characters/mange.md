# Mange

**Mange** is a gnoll mercenary who fights for the Dream faction during the Ninth Floor Faction Wars.

## Profile & Traits
- **Race**: Gnoll.
- **Affiliation**: The Dream (mercenary squad).
- **Relationships**: Cousin of [[corporal-pillbox]] and close friend of [[lockjaw]].

## History
In Book 7, Chapter 27, following the death of Empress [[princess-dnadia]], Mange and his gnoll unit enter the Prism throne room. While eight of his comrades are conscripted into the Princess Posse, Mange remains aligned with the Dream. To prevent a slaughter, Corporal Pillbox convinces Mange and Lockjaw to disarm and surrender. Carl agrees to spare them and orders Mange to accompany Pillbox without looting.
