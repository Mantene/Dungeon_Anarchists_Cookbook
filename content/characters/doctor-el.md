# Doctor El

**Doctor El** is a mushroom-like Funeral Bell guard in [[point-mongo]] who is under the command of [[princess-donut]] (Mayor Donut). 

## Floor 6 Events
- In [[156-book-5-chapter-11-the-recital]] (Chapter 11), [[carl]] hands Doctor El an anti-hunter missile launcher to help defend the town. Donut named him Doctor El for reasons Carl did not understand.
