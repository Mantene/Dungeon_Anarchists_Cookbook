# Alpha Male Carl

Alpha Male Carl is a Level 69 Totem opponent summoned by Judge Lucian during the Reminiscence Hydra fight. He appears as a parody of Carl with gelled-back hair, star-patterned boxers, and a ridiculous crotch bulge. He uses a xistera to throw shrapnel bombs and speaks with the voice of Carl's father.
