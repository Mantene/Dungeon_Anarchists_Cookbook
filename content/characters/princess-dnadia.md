# Princess D'nadia

Princess D'nadia of the Prism is a Saccathian (commonly called a Sac) NPC who serves as a recurring guest panelist on the talk show *Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton*.

## Profile
Princess D'nadia is a squid-headed humanoid creature with bumpy gray skin and a cephalopod face covered in tentacles. She is royal member of the Prism Kingdom, a small but powerful trading force. 

During the interview, D'nadia is highly supportive of Carl and Donut. She repeatedly attempts to hold Carl's hand and laughs (making trumpet noises with her tentacles) at Donut's insults toward the comedian [[tucker]]. Following the assassination of her close friend [[manasa]] by the Skull Empire, D'nadia is devastated (weeping black oil) and officially condemns the action, declaring that the Prism Kingdom will file an immediate grievance with the Syndicate Court.
