# Minge

**Minge** is a Level 225 Minor Feral Demon NPC who invades the Desperado Club on Floor 8.

## Biography and Role

Minge is the younger sister of Slit and a native of the Nothing. She is depicted as a grotesque, melting pink creature covered in dozens of spindly arms with massive, kite-sized claws. She is summoned into the Desperado Club casino after [[samantha]] accidentally tears open a rift to the Nothing during a knife-game mishap.

Upon emerging, Minge begins slaughtering guards, merchants, and NPCs, demanding the location of Samantha (whom she calls "Psamathe," her former friend). Carl deploys holy-water-infused smoke screens and snares to delay her, but Minge breaks through the kitchen, rips badger bartender Charles Ellis in half, and tears down the club's structure. She is eventually shredded and killed by the vorpals unleashed by Carl.
