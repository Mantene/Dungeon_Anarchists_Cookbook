# Kiwi

**Kiwi** is a Level 60 Mongoliensis (velociraptor) Pack Leader in the jungle surrounding [[point-mongo]] on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Profile & Traits
- **Appearance**: Kiwi is a particularly large, battle-scarred velociraptor. She has pink and blue feathers giving her a lighter hue, a jagged scar running the length of her face, is missing her left eye, and has a pitted beak. Her claws are ten inches long.
- **Leadership**: She is the leader ("head bitch in charge") of an all-female pack of Mongoliensis.
- **Abilities**: Kiwi can emit a terrifying barking noise that inflicts the *Deer in the Headlight* status effect, paralyzing targets in place for fifteen seconds.

## Floor 6 Actions
- **Mating and Alliance**: In [[156-book-5-chapter-11-the-recital]] (Chapter 11), Kiwi calls out to [[mongo]] (Level 33), attracting him to the jungle. Rather than fighting, they mate. As a result, Kiwi and her pack's tags turn white (neutral/friendly) for [[princess-donut]] (who is now Kiwi's "mother-in-law"), though they remain red (hostile) for [[carl]].
- **The Recital Quest**: Kiwi's presence and the dinosaur pack's attraction to Point Mongo trigger [[the-recital]] quest.

## Graphify Deductions
- **Faction Integration**: Kiwi's shift from hostile to neutral for Donut represents a unique exploitation of mob behaviors. Because she is the pack leader, aligning her with Mongo pacifies the immediate Mongoliensis threat to Point Mongo, showing that narrative-level bonds (mating) can override standard system hostility mechanics.
