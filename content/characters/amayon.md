# Amayon

**Amayon** is a Prince of Sheol (a Level 250 demon lord) who serves as a major figure in Book 6 (*The Eye of the Bedlam Bride*).

## Biography & Role on Floor 8
- **Enslaved by Sister Ines**: Expelled from Sheol by one of his three brothers, Amayon was trapped on Floor 8 inside the shell of the Level 125 sea serpent city boss [[madre-de-aguas]]. He was bound to the control of [[sister-ines-quiteria|Sister Ines]] and forced to spout Sheol fire.
- **Ysalte's Control**: After Ines's death, Amayon was claimed by his former lover and mother of his child, the goddess [[ysalte|Ysalte]], who sought to use him as her retainer for the Celestial Ascendency.
- **The World Quest**: During the final card battle, Carl and the team defeated his totems and refilled the world with souls (via Raul the Crab's sperm) to give Amayon the power to cast his return portal.
- **Emberus's Deal**: Held in Amayon's hand, Carl asked the demon about Emberus's son Geyrun. Amayon revealed that Geyrun was murdered and that he witnessed the deal, offering to reveal the details if Emberus helps him kill his brothers.
- **Banishment/Ascension**: Free of Ysalte, Amayon ascended into a massive Sheol portal, which grew globally and sucked up the remaining crawlers into Floor 9.

## Attributes
