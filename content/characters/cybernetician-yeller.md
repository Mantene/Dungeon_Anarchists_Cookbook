# Cybernetician Yeller

**Cybernetician Yeller** is a commander or specialist within the Reavers faction during the Ninth Floor Faction Wars.

## Profile & Traits
- **Faction**: The Reavers.
- **Specialty**: Beast-taming and pet modification, specifically turning creatures into biological weaponry.

## History
In Book 7, Chapter 21, Yeller orchestrates a massive biological assault against the Princess Posse's southern trenches. He unleashes a horde of approximately 1,000 modified pets named "Dumplin'" (Level 40 Juiced Rot Sticker Behemoths). Yeller raised these pets from infancy to trust him, only to inject them with size-altering steroids, infect them with highly contagious debuffs (including the Gurgles, Rabies, and Insanity), and send them to detonate as suicide-bomber seeking missiles.
