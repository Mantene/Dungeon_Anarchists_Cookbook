# Chiyome

**Chiyome** is a razor fox NPC racer on the tenth floor of the dungeon.

## Biography and Role

Chiyome is a Level 80 Mistress of Nunchaku and a member of **Team The Wild Hunt**. She is a bipedal razor fox who wears a red ninja bodysuit and fights with nunchucks and throwing stars. Her mount is a giant bear named Onikuma.

In [[411-book-8-chapter-12-hungry-eyes-roundabout]], Chiyome and her two ninja teammates perform a somersaulting team casting spell (Size Up) in the cul-de-sac. Afterward, she shares a beer with [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] and proposes working together to stop the frontrunners, particularly the Lady Dominators. In [[412-book-8-chapter-13-quest-reunite-corky]], she explains the mechanics of half-mantaurs like Corcunda to Carl and Donut.
