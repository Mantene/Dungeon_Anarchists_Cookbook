# Wendita

**Wendita** is a Bopca Protector NPC who serves as the proprietor of a safe room tavern on the fourth floor of the World Dungeon.

## Biography and Role
Wendita runs the Nirula's (a fast-food-themed red and white restaurant) safe room located at Station 83 on the fourth floor (The Iron Tangle). She is a shaggy, female Bopca. 

When Princess Donut, Carl, Katia, and Mordecai enter her establishment, Donut initiates negotiations to purchase and combine their Level 3 personal space coupons. Wendita initially quotes 50,000 gold for a consolidated space, but Donut uses her high Charisma to aggressively bargain. Wendita reluctantly drops the price to 37,500 gold, tearfully noting that if she goes any lower she will have to pay the difference out of her own pocket. 

Ultimately, the party combines their three coupons to unlock a combined Level 5 personal space, paying a final fee of 15,000 gold. Wendita is left sullen and weeping from the intense negotiation, but later cooks a free meal for the party.
