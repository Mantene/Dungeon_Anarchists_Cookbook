# Yago

**Yago** is a Level 70 Monk Seal Picket Sentry. He is a local folklore creature spawned on Floor 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth").

## Biography and Role

Yago belongs to the Monk Seal faction, who hold the ocean sacred and are locked in [[the-chowder-war]] against the Red *Maníseros* Land Crabs. He is trained in Caribbean Kung Fu and guards the coast from land crabs and other intruders. 

Yago is defeated by Team Sister Ines and captured into an Uncommon T'Ghee card totem. Because Team Sister Ines has empty slots, Yago is automatically activated and added to their squad as a melee tank. He proves highly effective in combat, easily defeating a Level 65 *Qa* chimpanzee monster in medium-difficulty mock battle practice.
