# Li Na

Li Na is a human-turned-Changbi crawler and the sister of [[li-jun]]. 

## History & Dungeon Presence
Li Na crawls the dungeon in a party with her brother Li Jun, [[zhang]], and their warehouse manager. 

In [[042-book-1-chapter-42-death-watch-extreme]], she is shown on the *Death Watch Extreme* screen frozen in time, reaching for her fallen manager's saw-toothed sword while surrounded by Troglodytes and hatching Brindled Vespas. After the Maestro teleports her brother and Zhang back to the dungeon, she is rescued by Li Jun and escapes safely to a safe room.

In Book 3 (*The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook*), Li Na selects the **Changbi** race and the **Slave Driver** class, which gives her acid immunity and uses chains as weapons. During the scramble across Floor 4 ("The Iron Tangle"), she resides safely in the cargo cars driven by Li Jun back to Station 36. 

In [[107-book-3-chapter-32-the-god-hijack-and-the-mimics-fall]] and [[108-book-3-chapter-33-the-second-deity-summoning]], she joins Carl and the others in the trainyard. Li Na prepares the second mantaur as a portable summoning vessel by amputating his arms and lower body and keeping him alive with healing potions. This brutal hack of the summoning system allows the crawlers to steal the war god Grull's summon and eject him to the abyss, neutralizing the threat.
