## Background

Li Na, her twin brother **Li Jun** (Street Monk), and their brother **Zhang** (Earth Mover) were a Chinese crawler group who joined Carl's coordinated effort during the Krakaren crisis on Floor 4 in Book 3.

---

## Book 3 — Floor 4 Krakaren Crisis

Li Na joined Carl's coordinated push to save crawlers during the Iron Tangle's collapse. She was Level 30 with a chains-based fighting style as a Slave Driver. She participated in the final station defense and the Krakaren-killing push at station 36's stairwell chamber.

---

## Books 5–7 — Ongoing Presence

Li Na remained part of the broader group of allied crawlers through Floors 5–9. During Floor 9's Faction Wars, her twin **Li Jun** made a sacrifice to buy Donut time during the War Mage Rebellion's assault — his fate is unclear but his actions are described as buying critical seconds.

---

## Book 8 — The Pineapple Cabaret

Li Na's defining moment in the series. She organized the **Pineapple Cabaret** — a secret unofficial exit from the dungeon using the cleaner bot infrastructure — during Floor 10's racing heats.

The coded signal: **one flower bloomed on Carl's cactus plant**. Hundreds of crawlers began escaping via the cleaner bots once the signal appeared.

Li Na herself escaped via the Pineapple Cabaret and arrived **safely** — unlike Louis Santiago, Britney, and Chris Andrews 2, who ended up in Sheol instead of the surface.

> "Li Na safely reached the Pineapple Cabaret."
> — [Book 8: A Parade of Horribles](../sources/book-8-parade-of-horribles.md)
