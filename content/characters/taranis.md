# Taranis

**Title:** Celtic Thunder God  
**Species:** Alien in Soul Armor (per dungeon cosmology)  
**Relationship:** Deeply attached to Penny the pig; killed his own brother Emberus  
**Current status:** Departed the arena with Krakaren and Samantha; location unknown

The god of thunder whose love for a Yorkshire pig was the lever Carl used to kill Emberus. Departed the Floor 11 arena carrying Krakaren and Samantha.

---

## Background

Taranis is a thunder deity in the dungeon's god-pantheon. Like all dungeon gods, he wears **Soul Armor** — a biological suit worn by a powerful alien entity. His power domain is lightning, which is relevant because lightning is **Scolopendra's hidden weakness**.

The AI had been using Taranis's love for **Penny** (a sapient Yorkshire pig, Level 10 party member) as a "banana peel" distraction — keeping him occupied and out of alignment against the party's plans.

---

## Book 8 — The Arena

Carl's plan for Floor 11's arena fight had multiple layers. The Taranis layer:

1. Bring **Penny** into Scolopendra's body (Carl used Gloom Wraith Phase to enter carrying Penny, then installed a nipple ring on Penny to let her breathe inside)
2. Donut teleported in via Lucia Mar's loop-de-loop trap, replacing Penny
3. Donut used **Katia's Bolt of Ophiotaurus** (the reusable god-killing bolt, second use) on **Emberus** — anchoring and blinding him
4. Taranis **saw Emberus fire at Penny** and killed his own brother

**Emberus is dead.** Killed by his own brother Taranis, defending a pig.

---

## Departures

After killing Emberus, Taranis:
- Took **Krakaren Prime** from the arena (Krakaren had been severely weakened by the fight)
- Took **Samantha** — she went willingly, saying she had a plan to kill "the demon mother lady and her jerk son" (Kimaris's mother; connected to a creature Samantha believes will destroy all creation)

Penny is described as "apparently in a relationship with Taranis (and also 'broke up' with him)" — the exact nature of their arrangement is left ambiguous.

---

## Penny

**Penny (Penelope 3)** is a sapient Yorkshire pig, Level 10 party member. She was originally bait for Taranis — the AI had been leveraging his love for her to distract him. During the arena fight, Penny was used as the key piece of the plan. She survived. Post-arena, she is with Taranis.
