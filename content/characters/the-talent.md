# The Talent

**The Talent** is a deceased orc hunter belonging to [[the-skull-empire]], who was active on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Floor 6 Events
- **Ambush & Death**: In [[170-book-5-chapter-25-future-huntress]] (Chapter 25), The Talent and his partners attempt to ambush Signet's camp. He stupidly casts a *Lightning* scroll near the **all-tree** outcropping. The tree absorbs the magic and turns it back on him, frying him to death.
- **Loot**: The Talent had an unusually large amount of gold (75,000 gold pieces, which [[princess-donut]] loots to bypass Carl's tithe tax), three lightning scrolls, and an undescribed credit chit (credit card).
