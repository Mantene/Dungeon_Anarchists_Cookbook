# Gluteus Maxx

**Gluteus Maxx** is a crawler who works as a dancer/performer at the Penis Parade cabaret club on the ninth floor in *[[dinniman-2024-tir|This Inevitable Ruin]]*.

## Role in the Crawl
Gluteus Maxx joins the [[the-princess-posse]]'s military forces. He is summoned as part of Warlord [[carl]]'s frontline assault squad in the Madness woods, where he is subsequently frozen by Eris's *Bear Witness* spell and swept away.
