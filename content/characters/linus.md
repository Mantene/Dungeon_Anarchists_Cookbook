# Linus

**Linus** was a biological license engineer from Teelan-3 and the brother of [[minus]]. 

## Biography & Fate
Linus earned a fortune cycle ago in biological license engineering for the Dream and purchased full citizenship, living in a garbage-filled orbital habitat around Teelan-3. He was an isolated individual whose only known associates were on the "Snow Cones" message board. Linus was heavily obsessed with the crawler [[elle-mcgibbons]], decorating his apartment walls with stained posters of her. 

In [[451-book-8-interlude-soother-infiltration]], Linus is executed by Syndicate security forces so that his brother, Minus, can assume his identity and infiltrate the crawl as a soother attached to Elle.
