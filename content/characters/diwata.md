# Diwata

**Diwata** is a Level 250 Immortal and forest deity in the World Dungeon. She is the mother of the forest dryads and the main target of the dryad summoning spell on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Profile & Traits
- **Background**: Diwata is a powerful deity worshipped by the forest factions on Floor 6. She is noted for being a ruthless immortal who hunts down those marked as enemies of her church.
- **Role**: She serves as a secondary boss and massive threat during the climax of Floor 6. She is summoned into the realm in [[206-book-5-chapter-61-diwata-summoned]] after crawlers corner and attack hunters affiliated with Emberus inside her temple.
- **Power**: Diwata is immortal and cannot be killed by conventional means. However, she has a loophole: she cannot leave the floor as long as a spell she cast is active. She bypasses her floor duration limit by transforming into a giant female mantis and continuously giving birth to swarms of nymphs, meaning she remains on the floor but is locked into her mantis form.

## Narrative Connections
- **The Climax at the Masquerade**: In [[216-book-5-chapter-71-crystal-explosions-and-diwata-knockout]], [[samantha]] confronts Diwata in the castle's third-floor security room. Samantha eats and detonates the room's soul crystals, knocking Diwata unconscious.
- **The Final Battle**: During the final cage match in [[219-book-5-chapter-74-the-final-cage-match]], Diwata is introduced as Team #4. In her weakened, giving-birth mantis form, she is eventually eliminated and returned to the Halls of Ascendency, completing the quest.
