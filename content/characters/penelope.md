# Penelope

**Penelope** (also known as **Penny**) is a pig companion and former member of **Team One Fine Pig** during the tenth-floor vehicle races in Book 8 (*A Parade of Horribles*).

## History & Dungeon Presence
Penelope was originally part of a throuple with the Tigran crawlers [[dario]] and [[nico]], along with their gremlin driver [[lipstick]]. She possesses exceptionally high Charisma and Constitution, rendering her immune to mind-attacks like soul-sucking. She squeals in panic when her teammates are threatened, which correlates with electrical storms and lightning in the sky.

In [[444-book-8-chapter-45-carls-rage]], after Dario and Nico are forced to cower in their vehicle following a confrontation with Carl, Penelope is left in the truck. In [[446-book-8-chapter-47-one-fine-pig-eliminated]], Dario and Nico are killed by Carl's disintegration traps, leaving Penelope driverless. In [[453-book-8-chapter-53-penelope-rescued]], Carl uses his key to access the abandoned garage of One Fine Pig and rescues Penelope, who happily follows them back.
