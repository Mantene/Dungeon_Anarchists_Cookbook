# Widget

**Widget** was a Level 19 Grease Gremlin NPC who worked as a steam engine train technician on the fourth floor of the World Dungeon. He is deceased.

## Biography and Role
Widget was a small, furry, black-haired gremlin about the same height as Zev. He wore a railroad cap, carried a tool belt with a massive wrench three times his size, and spoke with a Creole-inflected cockney accent. He was employed at Station and Repair Hub E to maintain the steam locomotives.

Widget slides down a chain from his watchtower to the *Nightmare Express* in Chapter 92. He is non-hostile due to mandated Sensitivity Training from HR. He rants about the severe ghoul infestation in the trainyard and tells Carl they should have taken the *Escape Velocity* train to the stairwells. He unhooks the engine from the destroyed cars and prepares to return to the tower to rotate the turntable to the employee line so he can ride out with them. He also makes crude, flirtatious comments toward Princess Donut.

## Death
As Widget prepares to climb back to his tower, a multi-limbed spider ghoul (which had regenerated after being knocked unconscious) lunges, decapitates him, and devours him instantly.
