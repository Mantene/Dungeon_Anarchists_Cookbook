# Epitome Tagg

**Epitome Tagg** is the leader of the hunter faction [[the-dream]], active on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Profile & Narrative Connections
- **Family**: He is the son of [[epitome-noflex]] and a member of the powerful, space-faring high-elf family of the Dream.
- **The Vendetta against Louis**: Tagg is enraged because the crawler Louis besmirched his mother's honor by having the changeling Juice Box transform into her likeness. Consequently, Tagg ordered all the hunters of the Dream to ignore the standard crawl mechanics and focus exclusively on hunting down and killing Louis.
