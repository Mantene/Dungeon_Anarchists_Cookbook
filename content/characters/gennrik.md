# Gennrik

Gennrik was a Saccathian (tentacle-faced alien) walk-on bounty hunter who worked as [[sadir]]'s partner on the surface of Earth.

## History & Death
- **Bounty Hunt**: In [[145-book-4-epilogue-epilogue]], Gennrik and Sadir infiltrate Seattle to track down the orange cat [[gravy-boat]] (Ferdinand).
- **Death**: While breaching [[beatrice]]'s tent, Gennrik is shot in the chest and killed by [[lexis]], who is executing a black-ops operation for [[odette]].
