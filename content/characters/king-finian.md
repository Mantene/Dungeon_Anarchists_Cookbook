# King Finian

**King Finian** is the deceased High-Elf King of the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Narrative Connections
- **Family**: He is the father of [[tsarina-signet]] (whom he had with a Naiad princess, making Signet a half-naiad bastard) and [[imogen]] (his pure-blooded high-elf heir).
- **History**: King Finian banished Signet from the Hunting Grounds when she was a child. Following his death, his pure-blooded daughter Imogen took the throne.
