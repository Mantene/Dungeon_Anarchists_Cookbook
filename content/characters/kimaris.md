# Kimaris

**Kimaris** (also referred to as **Kimmy**) is one of the Princes of Sheol (the brothers of Sheol).

## Profile & Lore
- **Relationship with Samantha**: Samantha describes Kimaris as one of her best friends. When she was unable to get pregnant, Kimaris provided her with a potion that enabled her pregnancy. He also gave her relationship advice.
- **System Item**: Carl possesses a summonable figure of Kimaris with a summoning time lasting the remainder of the floor. Bautista warns Carl to be careful when using it.
