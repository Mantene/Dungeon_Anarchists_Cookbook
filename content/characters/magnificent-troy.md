# Magnificent Troy

Magnificent Troy is an orc sports commentator who co-hosts the televised Special Event boss fight against [[lusca]] on Floor 5 in [[136-book-4-chapter-26-mouthbrooding-mayhem]]. He provides live play-by-play commentary alongside Kevin, highlighting the gore and the danger to the crawlers.
