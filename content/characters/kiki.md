# Kiki

**Kiki** is a Dromedarian attorney and the close friend of [[quasar]]. She represents crawler [[daniel-bautista]].

## Biography
- **Background**: Kiki is the daughter of one of the galaxy's most notorious mob bosses, and has spent her life trying to run from her past. Hailing from a humpbacked alien race, she is noted for her high alcohol tolerance.
- **Relationship with Quasar**: She met Quasar at orientation in law school, and they have been best friends since. 

## Actions
- **Chapter 488**: Kiki is seen drinking at the attorney bar, watching the end of the tenth floor. She reports that her client Daniel Bautista has successfully gone into stasis to escape the floor, though he has been disqualified. After Quasar's family is attacked by Taurins sent by Princess Chandra, Quasar pays Kiki a retainer to legally and physically help his family.
