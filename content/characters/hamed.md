# Hamed (The Night Wyrm)

**Hamed**, widely known as **The Night Wyrm**, is the leader of the Guild of Suffering and the newly appointed manager of the Desperado Club on Floor 9.

## Relationships and History
- **Shi Maria**: Hamed was previously married to the spider demigoddess [[shi-maria]] (The Screamer), who was banished from the pantheon for allegedly eating him—a rumor disproven by his continued survival.
- **Astrid**: Hamed was also married to the Bloodlust Sprite [[astrid]]. He engineered the quest to assassinate Astrid on the sixth floor, leading to her death at the hands of Katia Grim.
- **Family**: He is the father of the strippers [[damascus-steel]] and [[anaconda]]. Following their mother Astrid's assassination, his sons have sworn a suicide mission to hunt down and kill him.
- **Desperado Club Management**: In Book 7 Chapter 10, it is revealed that Hamed has taken over management of the Desperado Club following Minge's destruction of the facility. He has banned all crawlers and replaced the staff and guards with heavily armed, custom security forces that are neither traditional crawlers nor standard NPCs.
