# Grace Bautista

Grace Bautista was a Level 8 Filipino human crawler. She was the sister of Nica and Lea Bautista.

## Death
Grace and her sisters were ambushed and killed by Circus Lemurs on Floor 3 of the dungeon. Their corpses were left in a street as a trap. Their inventory was looted by surviving members of their group, leaving them in street clothes. Carl discovers their bodies and pulls two strength rings and an oak anklet from them.
