# Di-we

**Di-we** is a deceased three-headed ogre nodling who was a companion and protector to [[tsarina-signet]] on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Profile & Narrative Connections
- **The Protector**: Di-we died protecting a young Signet from the agents of her half-sister, Princess [[imogen]]. 
- **The Split**: Upon his death, Di-we split into three distinct ogre offspring: [[areson-the-wise]] (retaining a third of his knowledge, stayed in the Hunting Grounds), [[apollon-the-mighty]] (went to the Over City circus), and [[herman-the-fleet]] (went to Larracos).
- **Tattoo Memorial**: Di-we's body was used to ink a three-headed ogre tattoo onto Signet's thigh, which she can summon as part of her battle squad.
