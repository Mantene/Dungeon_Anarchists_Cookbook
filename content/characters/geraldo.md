# Geraldo

**Geraldo** is a Level 70 Monk Seal Picket Sentry spawned on Floor 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth").

## Biography and Role

Geraldo is a Monk Seal warrior who defends the coastline of Havana, Cuba. Following the capture of [[yago]] by Team Sister Ines, Geraldo attacks the crawlers. He is defeated by Carl and Princess Donut and captured into a T'Ghee card totem. Because Carl's squad has empty slots, Geraldo is automatically activated and becomes the first minion squad member in Carl and Donut's deck.
