# Gash

**Gash** is a harem demon from the Nothing who is mentioned in *[[dinniman-2024-tir|This Inevitable Ruin]]*.

## Background
Gash is one of the recurring demon entities associated with the Nothing (similar to [[slit]] and [[minge]]). According to [[samantha]], she previously teamed up with a boyfriend to easily defeat Gash. Samantha anticipates Gash's eventual emergence through the feral gates on the ninth floor.
