# Yarilo

**Yarilo** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A banished god of lust, son of Taranis, and both grandson and stepson of Apito. He was exiled to the Nothing by Apito for sexually assaulting a familiar during her Butcher's Masquerade spell. His re-emergence from the Nothing was a significant event, coinciding with the activation of Apito's memorial crystal.
