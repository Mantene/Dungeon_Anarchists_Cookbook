# Pontiff

**Pontiff** is a bullheaded Taurin NPC and former stripper who becomes a mercenary for the crawlers on the tenth floor.

## Biography & Role in the Dungeon

Pontiff was originally an NPC dancer at the Desperado Club. After the club fell and the floor progressed, he was hired by [[rosetta]] and [[tipid]] as a mercenary for [[carl]]'s guild. 

In Book 8, Pontiff is assigned to be the driver of Carl's truck for the sixth and seventh heats, navigating his massive horns to fit inside the cab. He is shown talking quietly with [[bucket-boy]], expressing a desire to make amends for his past actions. He expertly drives the truck through the shrunken apartments of the L'Engle Building during Heat 6.

Before the final heat (Heat 7), Pontiff volunteers to test the casino's "Nothing" portal at the Desperado Club. He uses a portal-examination spell to determine if it connects to the Pineapple Cabaret's holding area. To verify this, he steps into the portal; if he survives and is removed from Carl's mercenary contract (instead of dying), it confirms the portal is a viable exit. Before he goes, Carl asks him to relay a message to [[herot]].
