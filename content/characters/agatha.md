# Agatha

Agatha is an eccentric homeless crawler who traveled with the Meadow Lark retirement home group.

## History
Agatha accidentally started the barrel fire behind the Meadow Lark facility in Wenatchee, causing the evacuation. When the dungeon opened in the middle of the street, Agatha voluntarily pushed her shopping cart onto the stairs. The system accommodated her by transforming the stairs into a wheelchair-accessible ramp, enabling the workers to evacuate the elderly patients.

## Characteristics & Dungeon Presence
Agatha is Level 2 (reaching Level 4 after the Borough Boss fight due to party experience sharing). She refuses to go through the tutorial and thus has no inventory menu, instead wheeling her items around in a shopping cart. She carries an IKEA bag filled with spray paint cans, blankets, and a plastic pink flamingo with an arrow in its head. She is erratic, mutters to herself, and spray-paints graffiti circles on walls. 

During the Ball of Swine boss fight in [[026-book-1-chapter-26-ball-of-swine]], she insists on staying in the center of the redoubt and is injured by a flying bolt but remains oblivious.

In Book 3 (*The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook*), Agatha reaches **Level 8** while remaining a human and classless. She continues to push her shopping cart through the dungeon. In [[109-book-3-chapter-34-sacrifice-and-level-five-transition]], when the surviving crawlers gather at the open stairwell at Station 36, they witness Agatha pushing her shopping cart down the stairs to Floor 5.

## Floor 5 (Air Quadrant)
Agatha survives Floor 5, reaching **Level 12** while remaining classless. In [[143-book-4-chapter-33-breaking-up-the-band]], just before the 6-hour level collapse mark, she pulls up out of nowhere with her shopping cart and descends the stairwell to Floor 6.

## Discovery of Valtay Technology
In [[047-book-1-chapter-47-royal-steed]], Carl inspects Agatha's shopping cart and discovers illegal, highly advanced military technology from the [[kua-tin-politics|Valtay Corporation]] (including a Relay, Shield generator, and a Pulse Pistol) hidden under her blankets, which she refers to as "junk." This technology is responsible for the feed blackouts on Floor 2.
