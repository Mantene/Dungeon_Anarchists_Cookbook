# Pustule

Pustule is a Level-30 Hobgoblin NPC who runs the shop **Hobgobs and Boom Sticks** in the Silk Road market of the Desperado Club.

## Profile
Pustule is a muscular, six-foot-tall hobgoblin who wears a pink graphic t-shirt featuring a unicorn wearing sunglasses. She has an open sore on her cheek that leaks black and green ooze, and she smells of rot. Pustule is slightly eccentric and tells rambling, nonsensical stories, but she is highly knowledgeable about chemistry and explosives. 

[[princess-donut]] negotiates with Pustule using her high Charisma, buying two cases of goblin dynamite and a case of fused **Hob-lobber** bombs for 1,000 gold. Carl also purchases **Hobgoblin Smoke Curtains** (heavy-smoke-producing fused bombs) from her. Pustule values Carl's custom alchemical *Boom Jugs* at 7,500 gold retail and offers to buy them for half price.
