# Ian

**Ian** is a Level 30 Bush Elf NPC who works as a Registration Arena Attendant. 

## History & Actions
- **Crawler Origin**: Ian reveals to [[carl]] that he was once a human crawler himself in a very distant past season, and that most of the attendants and NPCs on the floor share similar origins.
- **Role on Floor 6**: He usually works as an attendant at Club Vanquisher, but was assigned to urban janitor duty and arena setup for the opening of Floor 6.
- **Meeting Carl**: In [[146-book-5-chapter-1-agent-provocateur]], Ian recognizes Carl as "the one who bombed Larracos" and warns him about the dangers of the Hunting Grounds. He tells Carl to stay on the trails, avoid the northern city of Zockau, and watch out for the mantis hunter [[vrah]], who killed Ian's brother in a previous season for no reason.
