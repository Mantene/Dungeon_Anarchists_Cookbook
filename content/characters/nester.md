# Nester

**Nester** is a blue-skinned Zebani mercenary hired by [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] for the tenth floor races.

## Biography & Abilities

Nester is a **Level 65 Zebani Cartographer**. She is hired to sit in the back of Carl's truck for the sixth and seventh heats of the Parade of Horribles. Her class grants her access to gravity spells and a specialized **Radar** skill. 

During the shrunken race through the L'Engle Building (Heat 6), she utilizes her Radar ability to scan the walls and floors of the apartments, locating hidden passages, service conduits, and identifying the locations of the dimensional gates. Her abilities are crucial in helping the team bypass various traps and navigate from floor to floor.
