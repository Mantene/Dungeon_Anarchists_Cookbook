# The Juicer

The Juicer is a Level 9 Neighborhood Boss on Floor 1, located in a fitness gym-themed cavern.

## Identity and Transformation
The Juicer was originally a human bodybuilder from Earth who was merged with a Troglodyte Basher and heavily mutated. He stands taller than [[carl]], has a lizard head, scales, a tail, black gym shorts, and a comically oversized bodybuilder physique with veins the size of fingers. He is described by the system as a caricature of a steroid-using gym bro who is angry about his shriveled testicles.

## Combat Mechanics
The Juicer flings burning manhole-cover-sized free weights at crawlers. His room is filled with 6 Troglodyte Bashers and 3 Virtuosos. His weakness is the high-pressure vein in his neck.

## Death
During the battle in [[024-book-1-chapter-24-the-juicer]], Carl swings a 250-pound barbell to stagger him. The Juicer grabs Carl's neck, but [[princess-donut]] pounces with her claws and tears open his neck vein, spraying blood everywhere. Carl finishes the Juicer off with a punch and steps on his head, caving it in.
