# Katia

**Katia** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A character whose mind Carl had previously accessed, creating a less intimate but purer mental connection compared to what he experienced with Britney. Her past interaction serves as a point of reference for Carl's understanding of different types of mental links.
