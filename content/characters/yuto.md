# Yuto

**Yuto** is a large red Oni crawler and member of **Team Yokai** during the tenth-floor vehicle races in Book 8 (*A Parade of Horribles*).

## History & Dungeon Presence
Yuto is first mentioned as performing onstage at the Lollipop bar in [[455-book-8-chapter-pineapple-cabaret-plan]]. In [[456-book-8-chapter-56-take-on-me]], he congratulates Carl before his stage debut. 

In [[457-book-8-chapter-singing-through-chaos]], after Dwight the unicorn stabs the DJ, Dwight's horn-flung corpse hits Yuto. Infuriated, Yuto attempts to punch the unicorn but hits a scarecrow crawler instead, sparking a massive bar brawl.
