# Firas M

Firas M is a human crawler who progresses to Floor 5.

## Class & Stats
- **Class**: **Hammersmith**. A melee/crafting class.
- **Dungeon Status**: Firas is Level 22 and is situated in the Air Quadrant of Bubble #543.
- In [[113-book-4-chapter-3-spit-and-swallow]], Firas is found drinking and slackening off at the Spit and Swallow brothel bar in Hump Town alongside [[louis-santiago-2]]. Princess Donut threatens them with expulsion from the town if they refuse to cooperate, forcing them to attend the crawler meeting.
