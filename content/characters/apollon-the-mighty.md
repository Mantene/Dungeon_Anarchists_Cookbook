# Apollon the Mighty

Apollon the Mighty is an Ogre Elite and member of Grimaldi's Traveling Circus who serves as "The Over City's Strongest Citizen."

## Background
Apollon was a performer in the circus before the cataclysm. He was transformed into an ogre with a vine branch (parasite conduit) sprouting from the side of his neck. During the circus performances, he runs an "Iced Cream" stand selling cones filled with writhing parasite worms.

## Connection to Signet
Signet's primary Blood and Ink elemental—the three-headed ogre—is modeled directly after Apollon and bears the same jagged scar on the side of his head.

## Interaction with Carl
In Chapter 60, Carl approaches Apollon at his stand and eats the worm ice cream to deliberately infest himself with Grimaldi's parasites. This enables Carl to speak directly into the collective mind of the vine.
