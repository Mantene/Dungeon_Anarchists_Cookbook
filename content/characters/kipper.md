# Kipper

**Kipper** is a Level 30 Unsettled Ghommid spirit who resides in [[pueblo-de-los-olvidados]].

## Biography and Role

Kipper was a peaceful Ghommid who worked as a dishwasher at *The Dirt Nap* tavern in the cemetery settlement of Pueblo de los Olvidados. After the deity [[asojano]] arrived in the town, Asojano's presence corrupted the local veil and drove the residents mad. Kipper was turned into a non-corporeal undead monster that attacks Carl and Team Sister Ines. He is later cured when the crawlers defeat Asojano.
