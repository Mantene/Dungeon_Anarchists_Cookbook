# Carmine

Carmine is a Level-16 City Elf NPC who serves as a Wind Mage for the **201st Security Group** at the *Apito Education Center* on Floor 3.

## Profile
Carmine is a recruiter for the cult who stands outside the Apito Education Center alongside [[salvatore]]. He is killed during Carl's *Mic Drop* boom-jug detonation on the temple entrance in Chapter 20 ([[069-book-2-chapter-20-temple-confrontation]]).
