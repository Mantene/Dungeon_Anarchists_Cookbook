# Ringmaster Grimaldi

Ringmaster Grimaldi is the Level 85 City Boss of the Over City (Floor 3) and a Pestiferous Vine Elite. He was originally a dwarven ringmaster of Grimaldi's Traveling Circus before the cataclysm.

## Background
Redstone Grimaldi valued his circus family above all else. During the global collapse, the poison cloud of Scolopendra hit his circus during a performance. Grimaldi was transformed into a massive Pestiferous Vine in the center ring. To protect his family, he used the vine's parasitic properties to control the transformed circus workers (clowns, acrobats, animals), allowing them to regenerate and return to life every time they die.

## Characteristics & Symbiosis
- **Appearance**: A giant-ass pale green bush/shrub that swallows the center pole of the main tent. It has python-like roots that snake across the floor of the Over City.
- **Parasitic Symbiosis**: Grimaldi's vine excretes mold spores that infect parasitic worms, which in turn infect other creatures (like [[heather-the-bear]] and the clowns). The parasites connect the hosts' minds to Grimaldi's collective consciousness.
- **Vulnerability**: The vine consumes blood and moisture. If it drinks blood from a poisoned creature, the symbiont link is broken and the vine dies within 15–20 minutes. Carl exploits this by poisoning himself and dripping his blood on Grimaldi's roots. However, if the same blood is healed with an antidote and dripped again, the vine is cured.
- **Mind Link**: The parasites allow Grimaldi to communicate telepathically with infected hosts. Carl speaks directly with Grimaldi's mind using Wisp Armor to prevent being controlled. He convinces Grimaldi to let Signet go.
