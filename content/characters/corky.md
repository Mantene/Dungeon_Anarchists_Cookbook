# Corky

**Corky** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

Corky is one half of a 'halfsie' who was subjected to the Split Personality spell, separating him from his other half (implied to be Genesis/Rapture or one of the womantaurs). He is currently held captive by Genesis and Rapture, who refer to him as their 'gimp' and keep him 'passed out.' Carl's group plans to rescue him and have Grigori fuse him back with his other half.
