# Vincente

Vincente is a Level-16 City Elf NPC who is a member of the **201st Security Group - Magical Ops** on Floor 3.

## Profile
Vincente is a cultist who confronts Carl and Donut in Chapter 16 ([[065-book-2-chapter-16-alley-ambush]]). He uses dark magic to lure Mongo into an alley using the *Meat Hooks* spell (burning flesh smell). 

During his confrontation, Vincente claims he has trained his entire life for the "final battle" and begins casting a spell that glows red. However, Carl kicks him in the groin, and Donut simultaneously fires a *Magic Missile* at his neck. The dual strike blows his head off, and his headless body is flung into the air, landing in a heap. Carl loots 1,500 gold, the *Meat Hooks* spell scroll, and his magical uniform shirt.
