# Tam

**Tam** is a Level 45 Ursine Light Cleric residing in [[point-mongo]] (formerly the Large Ursine Settlement) on the sixth floor.

## Profile & Traits
- **Background**: An elderly Ursine cleric with a gray muzzle, he is a devout adherent to the strict, puritanical rules of the settlement.
- **Beliefs**: Tam is highly concerned with piety, order, and avoiding "sin." He distrusts [[princess-donut]] (whom he calls a "pagan monkey") and her dinosaur pet [[mongo]].

## Floor 6 Actions
- **Demanding Piety**: In [[156-book-5-chapter-11-the-recital]] (Chapter 11), Tam confronts [[mordecai]] and [[princess-donut]], demanding they re-institute the strict rules banning dinosaurs and naiads to keep the town safe and maintain the "holy shield of piety."
- **Triggering the Quest**: Tam's complaints about the dinosaur attacks directly trigger [[the-recital]] quest.

## Graphify Deductions
- **Cultural Clash**: Tam represents the conservative, puritanical elements of the Ursine population who believe security is tied to rule adherence (piety). His conflict with Donut's liberal rule reforms highlights the friction between crawler leadership and native NPC belief systems.
