# Rishi

**Rishi** is a four-armed naga NPC who serves as the representative for the **Blood Sultanate** (Team #4) in the Faction Wars preproduction meetings on Floor 8.

## Biography and Role

Rishi is polite, soft-spoken, and diplomatic, yet represents a hostile faction that Carl and Donut must destroy during the Faction Wars. In Book 6 Chapter 21, he warns Donut that due to her class and race, she is technically in the succession line for the Blood Sultanate.

In Chapter 22 (Global 244), Rishi attempts to negotiate a compromise with Carl: the Blood Sultanate will support giving unaffiliated NPCs invulnerability and a safe zone this season if [[juice-box]] stops her violent terror attacks. He warns Carl that the Sultanate is prepared to react with extreme force if the compromise is rejected.
