# Fitz

Fitz is a Level-30 human NPC who operates as the safe room tavernkeeper and proprietor of **The One-Eyed Narwhal** pub in the medium skyfowl settlement on Floor 3.

## Profile
Unlike the Bopca safe room caretakers, Fitz is a large, bald human who is depicted in a lewd pose on his pub's exterior sign. He welcomes [[princess-donut]] and [[carl]] warmly, offering them food and shelter at higher rates than previous safe rooms. Fitz is close friends with [[gum-gum]], an orc NPC who cleans the town's streets. Following GumGum's death, Fitz is devastated and helps raise the town's NPC militia to defend the city against the threat of a lich. He accepts custom alchemical *Hair of the Dog* potions from Carl as payment for lodging.
