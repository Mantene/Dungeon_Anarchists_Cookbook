# Gordo

Gordo is a Level 21 Bopca Protector NPC who runs "The Belly-Rubbed Pug" tavern safe room on the Third Floor of the World Dungeon.

## Interactions
Gordo initially appears subservient and is heavily influenced by Princess Donut's high Charisma (which stands at 70). He gets annoyed by her demanding attitude, prompting [[mordecai]] to brew a sleep potion for him. Mordecai passes Gordo the potion so he can sneak into the back kitchen to steal herbs for potions and read the local Bopca newsletter. Gordo later complains to Carl and Donut about their exploitation of his kind nature.
