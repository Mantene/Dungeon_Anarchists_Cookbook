# Miriam Dom

Miriam is a crawler who entered the crawl alongside her goat, [[prepotente]].

## Background
Miriam is a middle-aged human woman who acts as Prepotente's handler, coordinator, and emotional anchor. Unlike other crawlers who view their pets as tools or weapons, Miriam loves Pony (Prepotente) as her child and works tirelessly to protect him and keep him calm.

## Narrative Arc (Book 2)
Introduced in [[062-book-2-chapter-13-desperado-rewards]] at the Desperado Club, Miriam guides Pony through his class and stat selections. 

During the final conflict of Floor 3 (the municipal collapse and the activation of Miss Quill's spell), **Miriam is killed** in action. Her death removes Pony's emotional stabilizer, unleashing his rage against the dungeon and leaving him traumatized.
