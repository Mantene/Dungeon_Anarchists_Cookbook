# Britney Proskurina

Britney Proskurina is a Level 27 Ukrainian human **Pit Fighter** crawler. She wears a fur bikini and carries a spike-covered stick. In the real world, she was visiting Dr. [[vadim-zbar]]'s cosmetic surgery clinic when the collapse happened, and they were the last survivors of their group.

## History & Actions
- **Floor 5 Infiltration**: Britney refuses to enter the water again in [[135-book-4-chapter-25-gelato-and-gills]], having survived the initial flooding by sheer luck. She stays on the biplane house *Twister* with [[princess-donut]].
- **Aftermath**: Following Vadim's death, she is devastated but remains with the team. At the end of Floor 5, she accompanies [[firas-m]] and the changelings down to Floor 6 in [[143-book-4-chapter-33-breaking-up-the-band]].
