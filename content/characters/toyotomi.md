# Toyotomi

**Toyotomi** is a rhinoceros NPC who appears during the Faction Wars on the ninth floor in *[[dinniman-2024-tir|This Inevitable Ruin]]*.

## Role in the Crawl
Toyotomi is a former guard for the Prism Kingdom. Following the Prism's elimination, he aligns with the [[the-princess-posse]] and takes charge of constructing the synergy tower known as **the Nest** in Shanty Town. 

He is a meticulous builder, ensuring the tower is constructed from master-quality granite blocks and properly enchanted with protective runes by defensive mages. He coordinates the tower's synergy setup with [[princess-donut]].
