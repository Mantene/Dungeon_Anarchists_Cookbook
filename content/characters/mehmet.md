# Mehmet

**Mehmet** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A Turkish man renowned for creating the 'Gutter Bumper' support spell. He was known for his focus on support abilities and skills, which saved countless lives during Faction Wars. Carl never met him but holds him in high regard as a true hero of the dungeon, akin to Imani.
