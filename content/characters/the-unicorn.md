# The Unicorn

**The Unicorn** is a antagonist character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A rival crawler and participant in the race who is known to harbor intense hatred for Carl and Donut. His girlfriend, Lucienne, was killed during the race, leading Donut to speculate whether this event might alter his antagonistic stance towards them.
