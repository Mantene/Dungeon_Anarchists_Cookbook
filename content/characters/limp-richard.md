# Limp Richard

**Limp Richard** is a Level 36 Mole Man NPC and merchant who operates a sundries store in the World Dungeon.

## Biography and Role
Limp Richard is introduced at Station 83 on the fourth floor (The Iron Tangle). He runs a general store called **Limp Richard's Sundries**, located near the Mace and Bashing Weapons Guild and the Nirula's safe room. He is a short, squat Mole Man who wears glasses and is often seen reading books. 

When Carl and Princess Donut first arrive at Station 83, Limp Richard welcomes them and offers to trade supplies. He expresses a love for reading and mentions that shopkeeper NPCs have limited access to entertainment feeds or digital media, making physical books and comics highly valuable trade commodities. Carl later trades his collection of Louis L'Amour books to Limp Richard in exchange for items.
