# Mehmet Or

**Mehmet Or** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A human Sipahi crawler, #3,756,330, who reached Level 62. Formerly a bowling alley owner in Istanbul, he tragically lost his pregnant wife in the hydra. Driven by fear for his surviving daughters, Azra and Yaz, he accepted a deal to ensure their perceived safety. He provides Carl with the Gutter Bumper spell, hoping Carl will save his daughters and avenge those who took deals.
