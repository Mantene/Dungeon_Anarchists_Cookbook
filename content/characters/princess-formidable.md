# Princess Formidable

**Princess Formidable** is a member of the royal family of [[the-skull-empire|The Skull Empire]]. She is the daughter of King Rust and the sister of Prince Stalwart and Prince Maestro.

## Biography & Role in the Epilogue
Unlike the rest of her family, Princess Formidable displays a sense of honor (inherited from her mother) and opposes the corrupt leadership of the Skull Empire. 

During the emergency session of the Syndicate Council in orbit, Formidable demands that they pull the planetary failsafe to blow the star and vaporize the system. She argues that the rogue AI has trapped them and is holding them hostage, and warns that the Valtay's plan to deploy 150,000 mercenaries to Floor 9 is a trap. 

Her proposal is rejected, and the council mutes her from the session by an 86% vote. She remains in orbit watching the surface of the Earth.

## Affiliation & Status
- **Affiliation**: The Skull Empire
