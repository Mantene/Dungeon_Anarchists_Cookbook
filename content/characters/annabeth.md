# Annabeth

**Annabeth** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A "human shell" encountered in the fifth-floor apartment simulation. She is found sitting on the toilet and screams when she notices Carl and Donut, but does not leave the bathroom.
