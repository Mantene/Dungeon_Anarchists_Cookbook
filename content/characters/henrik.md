# Henrik

Henrik is a Level 30 Dromedarian-impersonating Changeling Principal NPC in Hump Town.

## Characteristics & Role
Henrik is a member of the Changeling Principals, a power-hungry sect obsessed with collecting race templates by physically touching rare creatures.

In [[116-book-4-chapter-6-the-collateral-secret]], Henrik is seen leaving the back entrance of the Hump Town City Hall. He was responsible for holding the gnome uncle [[wynne]] hostage in the subterranean vault, drugging him with shriveled black mushrooms to force him to cast a temporary resurrection spell to give flesh to the legendary beast "Quetzalcoatlus."
