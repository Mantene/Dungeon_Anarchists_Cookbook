# Capa

**Capa** is a deity (the beaver god) residing in the Nothing, mentioned on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Narrative Connections
- **The Hat Deity**: In [[169-book-5-chapter-24-obsidian-bracelet]] (Chapter 24), [[samantha]] mentions Capa to Carl, describing him as a "fuck" who is used as a literal hat by another deity, [[hehaka]]. Consequently, Capa is always crying because he cannot do anything about it.
