# Eva Sigrid

**Eva Sigrid** is a crawler and a prominent member of [[hekla]]'s guild, Brynhild's Daughters. 

## Background
Before the dungeon collapse, Eva was an economics professor at a university in Reykjavik. She was friends with [[katia-grim]], with whom she occasionally had lunch. Eva also has a pre-existing relationship with Hekla, who served as Eva's psychiatrist before the world ended.

## Role in the Crawl
Eva is a Level 27 Half Nagini, Half-Orc **Nimblefoot Enforcer**. She stands about five feet tall, has green scales, two regular legs, and four arms. She wields dual sabers in an X formation on her back.

Unlike most of Brynhild's Daughters, Eva has one large skull and three small skulls floating over her head, indicating thirteen player-kills (twelve of which were acquired during the third or fourth floor). Carl deduces that Eva acts as Hekla's personal enforcer and hitman, handling the guild's "dirty work" to keep Hekla's reputation clean. 

In Chapter 96, she helps coordinate the train rescue mission, rigging a second engine to the back of the Vermillion line subway train to enable reversing.

## Graphify Deductions
- **Psychiatrist-Patient Dynamic**: Eva's absolute loyalty to Hekla is complicated by their pre-dungeon relationship as psychiatrist and patient. This suggests a deep psychological dependency that Hekla leverages, using Eva to perform lethal actions on behalf of the guild.
