# Ronit

**Ronit** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A druid who developed an improvement on the dread system, creating the "Garden of Deliverance." This system assigns a small plant to each crawler, with its condition indicating the crawler's life status (wilted for death, bloomed for escape).
