# Waldrip Chris

**Waldrip Chris** is a gremlin animal trainer NPC who applies for the position of garage manager for [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] on the tenth floor.

## Biography and Role

Waldrip Chris appears in [[400-book-8-chapter-1-entering-your-garage]], where he and [[hedy]] engage in a violent physical fight to determine whether Carl and Donut's garage will use biological or mechanical vehicles. He tries to convince them to choose a biological mount (such as a Charm Wombat or Cloud Cheetah) and attacks Hedy with a tan-colored gourd full of bugs. 

However, Hedy retaliates by hitting him with a giant mallet. Once Donut chooses a mechanical vehicle, the system automatically selects Hedy as the mechanic and disintegrates Waldrip Chris in a spray of red and green mist.
