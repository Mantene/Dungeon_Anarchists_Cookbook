# Kina

**Kina** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A goddess referred to as the "Munificent Goddess." She is the original giver of the Enchanted Spiked Kneepads, an artifact that provides benefits like underwater breathing and temporary deity effects, currently equipped by Penny.
