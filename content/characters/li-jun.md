# Li Jun

Li Jun is a human crawler who worked in a warehouse before the collapse.

## History & Dungeon Presence
Li Jun crawled the first two floors alongside his sister [[li-na]], his friend [[zhang]], and their warehouse manager. He has a bronze star next to his name.

In [[042-book-1-chapter-42-death-watch-extreme]], Li Jun and Zhang are captured and forced onto the show *Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem* hosted by [[the-maestro]]. The host attempts to force them into a cruel game to win "teleport points" to save themselves or their party, who are frozen in a corridor cornered by Troglodytes and hatching Brindled Vespas. 

Li Jun shows strong solidarity, refusing to play the Maestro's game when it is clear the manager and his sister cannot both be saved. He states he would rather die with his friends. When Carl intervenes and mocks the host, the Maestro in a fit of rage teleports them back to the dungeon early. Following Carl's whispered advice, they bypass the monsters, grab the manager and Li Na, and successfully escape into a safe room.

In Book 3 (*The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook*), Li Jun reaches **Level 28** and selects the **Street Monk** class, which gives him acid immunity. 

During the chaos on Floor 4 ("The Iron Tangle"), Li Jun is rescued along with a large group of crawlers trapped at Station 101 on the Vermillion line in [[096-book-3-chapter-21-brynhilds-daughters-and-the-train-rescue]]. In [[099-book-3-chapter-24-the-spoils-of-hekla]], after Hekla's death, Carl coordinates a split train plan. Li Jun drives the main train housing over 1,000 survivors back to Station 36 to make a stand with [[imani-c]] and defend the station. 

Later, in [[107-book-3-chapter-32-the-god-hijack-and-the-mimics-fall]] and [[108-book-3-chapter-33-the-second-deity-summoning]], Li Jun jumps through the portal cart into the trainyard alongside Carl, Donut, Mongo, and Li Na, helping to execute the deity hijack trick to bypass the station mimic's defenses.
