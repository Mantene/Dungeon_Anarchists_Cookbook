# An (Human Shell)

**An (Human Shell)** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A 'Human Shell' construct, appearing as an old man, initially passed out on a couch in a simulated apartment (712, representing a 'what-if' scenario for Tran's father). Similar to Hoa, his eyes are sewn shut with black wire and marked with an 'X'. He awakens in a rage, attacking the crawlers when they appear in his apartment.
