# Tipid

**Race:** Crest  
**Class (dungeon):** Poisoner (original crawl); **Liquid Dervish** (as Floor 9 mercenary, Level 60)  
**Original crawl level:** 96 (took deal at start of Floor 12)  
**Role:** Colonel in the Princess Posse; one of three key leaders from the Homecoming Queen  
**Author:** 4th Edition of The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook

---

## Background

Tipid was a crawler from an earlier season who reached Level 96 as a Poisoner. He took his exit deal at the start of Floor 12. During the deal negotiation, he and his companion Horatio were the last two alive, and a single key would only let one person out. Tipid chose himself. He was haunted by this for the rest of his life.

Post-dungeon, his exit health was so poor (brain damage from a creature's attack) that he spent nearly his entire pension on Viceroy brain surgery to stay functional. Eventually settled in a Crest colony and worked for a waste management company for a very long time.

He eventually connected with **Dr. Porthus** (2nd edition cookbook author) through the Crawler Project and the Open Intellect Pacifist Network. He joined the mission to help the Princess Posse on Floor 9.

---

## Book 7 (Floor 9)

Arrived with the Homecoming Queen as one of the three leaders of the 50,000 former crawlers. His scarred head (a scar running from temple to temple, as if he'd been literally scalped once) and his grin gave the impression of someone who'd seen everything and found most of it funny.

Served as Colonel of the base defense, commanding the **104th Naughty Little Piggies** (defense battalion around the FUPA). Present for all major planning sessions. Stayed at the FUPA to defend during Operation Ruin.

He is warm where Rosetta is cold. Optimistic where Justice Light is methodical. He functions as the heart of the former-crawler team.

He is aware of Porthus's full plan, including the Apothecary's involvement — something he accepts on faith even though he doesn't have all the details. Rosetta doesn't trust the Apothecary. Tipid believes in Porthus.

---

## The Cookbook

His entries in the 4th edition reflected the guilt he carried about leaving Horatio behind — "stab stab stab" entries that suggested his mind was not in a healthy place. By the time he wrote the book, the rage and numbness had already set in. He never felt fully free despite exiting the dungeon.

---

## Book 8

Tipid is confirmed on **Floor 12** as one of the 19 remaining crawlers. He lost both legs during the Faction Wars fighting on Floor 9. He reached Floor 12 despite this — the wiki does not specify how he is moving without legs, but arriving at the Court of the Ascendency with Rosetta after everything they did to get there is the point.
