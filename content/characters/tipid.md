# Tipid

**Tipid** is a Crest poisoner crawler from the 4th Edition of the Dungeon Crawl who survived and is currently a **Colonel in the Princess Posse Faction Wars team**.

## Biography and Role

- **Crawl and Exit**: Tipid reached the 12th floor during his season, where he took an exit deal. He survived the 11th floor by using an exit key that only allowed one person to escape, leaving his friend Horatio behind to die—a decision that caused him lifelong trauma.
- **Indentured Servitude**: Following his exit, he worked as a Game Guide and Guildhall Instructor in the Poisoner guild.
- **Post-Dungeon Life**: Tipid suffered from severe brain lesions and physical injuries due to his crawl. He had to spend his entire pension and take out loans to pay for Viceroy medical transport and repairs. He eventually retired to a Crest system to work for a waste management company.
- **Cookbook Contribution**: Tipid was the author of the **Fourth Edition** of the *Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook*. His notes in the Cookbook reflect his deep survivor's guilt.
- **Faction Wars**: In Book 7, Tipid serves on Earth as a Colonel in the Princess Posse Faction Wars team.
