# Sneaky Tyler C

**Sneaky Tyler C** is a lead sluggalo sapper who assists [[justice-light]] during the Faction Wars on the ninth floor in *[[dinniman-2024-tir|This Inevitable Ruin]]*.

## Role in the Crawl
Tyler C is a procedurally generated sluggalo. He is responsible for managing the sapper squad placing manual tripwires and proximity traps under Justice Light's direction. Prior to the launch of "Operation Ruin," Justice Light orders him and his squad to retreat to the surface to support [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]]'s forces.
