# Dong Quixote

**Dong Quixote** is a Level 45 (later 51+) human stripper and mercenary who becomes part of Carl and Donut's personal guard.

## Profile & Traits
- **Appearance**: A stripper from the *Penis Parade* club who resembles a 70-year-old Spanish conquistador. His physical form changes from buff to flabby depending on his breathing.
- **Weapon**: He wields [[spunky-jefferson|Spunky Jefferson the Enchanted Nickel Sock of the Elderly Miser]], a sapient weapon that speaks directly to his mind and increases in damage with each kill. Dong frequently speaks to the sock over his shoulder, debating financial investments.
- **Mount**: In Faction Wars, he rides **Gonk**, a large swamp yak that previously belonged to King Rust.

## Actions in Faction Wars
- **Personal Platoon**: Donut conscripts Dong Quixote into Carl and Donut's personal guard platoon.
- **Net Trap**: During the final battle, Carl orders Dong to retrieve the marble-sized magical net trigger left by Lucia Mar, which allows Carl and Donut to escape the Nest tower.
- **Post-Wars**: As Larracos is transferred via *Zerzura*, Dong and the other strippers remain with Carl and Donut's group, moving to their saferoom barracks.
