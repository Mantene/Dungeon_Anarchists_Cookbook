# Katia Grim

**Katia Grim** (Crawler #9,077,265) is a crawler and a core member of [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]]'s party, **The Royal Court of Princess Donut**. Formerly a mousy Earth art teacher, she has become one of the most powerful and high-level crawlers in the dungeon.

## Biography
Katia struggled early in the dungeon, falling behind in levels. She was befriended by [[hekla]], leader of *Brynhild's Daughters*, who asked Carl and Donut to help train and level her. Katia officially joined their party on Floor 3 to share experience.

## Race & Class
- **Race**: **Doppelganger**. Katia can shape her clay-like body into any form of equal mass, though she must manually sculpt her appearance. She does not inherit the racial traits of the shape she mimics. Her default state is revealed when she sleeps. She default-sculpts herself to look human with black hair.
- **Class**: **Monster Truck Driver** (a Juggernaut-style hybrid of Staunch Barrier and Jouster). She acts as a dedicated tank with high Constitution and Dexterity.

## Book 3 (Floor 4) Developments
- **Sponsor**: Sponsored by Princess Formidable of the Skull Empire.
- **Doppelganger Eyes**: Katia learns to sprout multiple functional eyes, including a third eye on the back of her head, though it causes intense nausea and headaches. She uses this eye to witness Donut hiding a PVP coupon.
- **The Living Cowcatcher**: Katia volunteers to act as a living cowcatcher on the front of the Vermillion train, molding her body into a massive bulldozer scoop and kebab spikes, insulated with rubber layers to avoid the electrified third rail. 
- **Accidental Assassination of Hekla**: After discovering Hekla's attempt to murder her with invisible *Dirty Little Phased Bolts*, and pushed over the edge by Eva Sigrid's verbal abuse, Katia activates her **Rush** skill to ram Eva. Due to the skill's erratic pathing, she misses Eva and slams into Hekla, splattering the number two crawler against the train wall.
- **Bounty & Level-Up**: The accidental kill earns Katia a 500,000 gold bounty, a golden skull, and instantly raises her level from Level 24 to **Level 37**, making her one of the highest-level crawlers in the dungeon.
- **Decoy Duty**: During the battle with the War God Grull, Katia shape-shifts into Carl's likeness (complete with heart boxers) and stands in the distance flipping off the god, successfully drawing Grull's aggro to enable the team's re-summoning plan.

## Gear, Skills & Buffs
- **Enchanted Repeating Crossbow of the Scavenger Mother of Mothers**: Looted from Hekla. A powerful female-only repeating crossbow with infinite basic ammo (buffet enchantment) that increases in damage by 25% for every female in the party (up to 30) and casts *Birth Defect* on spawning monsters.
- **Enchanted Shade Gnoll Riot Forces Crowd Control Shield**: Gained from a Fan Box. Grants +5% Con, +5 to *Rooted in Place* skill, and upgrades *Rush* to **Crowd Blast** (a devastating cone-damaging ram usable every 5 minutes instead of once a day).
- **Enchanted Shade Gnoll Riot Forces Telescoping Crowd Control Baton**: Gained from a Fan Box. An adjustable-length truncheon that casts Level-five *Cone of Knockback* when swung at groups of 10+ mobs.
- **The Bolt of Ophiotaurus**: Gained from a Silver Benefactor Box. A unique god-killing bolt that pauses a deity's invulnerability for 15 seconds if shot into their eye.
- **Find Crawler (Level 3)**: A skill potion consumed from a Gold Savage Box that places all nearby crawlers on her map.
- **Club Vanquisher Membership**: Obtained from a loot box, allowing her to officially join Carl and Donut's social lounge party.

## Floor 8 Actions
- **Sepsis Crown Curse**: Katia is afflicted with the Sepsis Crown curse. The goddess [[eileithyia]] offers a cure (or transfer out of the crawl) if the team selects Odette as adjutant.
- **Astrid's Assassination**: The Guild of Suffering assigns Katia a quest to kill [[astrid]]. During the demon breach at the Desperado Club, Katia corners Astrid in the kitchen. She summons a mossy troll and a skull-faced crow totem, and after Carl disables Astrid's magic, Katia impales her through the head, completing the quest and gaining the orchid.
- **Physical Toll**: The strain of the crawl and her curse leaves Katia physically deteriorating, characterized by heavy rings under her eyes and a tremor in her left hand, requiring alchemical treatments from Mordecai and Imani.
- **The Orchid Dilemma**: Katia has the orchid in her inventory, which would cure her and transfer her to the 12th floor as an NPC intern. However, she refuses to eat it until the team reaches the 9th floor, unwilling to abandon her squad early.

## Graphify Deductions
- **The Accidental Champion**: Katia's trajectory from a terrified, insecure survivor to a Level 37 Juggernaut highlights the chaotic nature of the dungeon. Her accidental killing of Hekla thrusts her into the intergalactic spotlight, forcing her to accept her role as a top-tier crawler. Her sponsor Princess Formidable's gift of the Bolt of Ophiotaurus indicates she is being groomed as a key player in the inter-faction conflicts on deeper floors.
