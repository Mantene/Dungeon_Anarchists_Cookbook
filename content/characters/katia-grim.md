**Race:** Doppelganger

**Level:** 9 (when she joined Carl's party)  
**Party:** The Royal Court of Princess Donut

## Background

Former art teacher. Has never fixed an actual engine despite her "gear head" Earth Hobby Potion skill.

Her Doppelganger race means she can reshape her body into anything of equal mass — but must mentally sculpt it herself, which is hard and painful. She originally appeared with severely misaligned features ("a Juan Gris painting"). With Mordecai's coaching, she improved to look basically human, though her hair and facial structure remain slightly off. She appears as a blond woman with one pale blue eye and one brown eye (like a Siberian husky). When she sleeps or uses the bathroom, she reverts to her natural form.

---

## Key Abilities

- **Constitution 51:** Can absorb enormous physical and magical damage
- **Pathfinder skill:** Finds stairwells easily — this was desperately needed by Carl's team
- **Second Fuel Tank:** Absorbs and reflects a portion of incoming damage once per fight
- **Rush:** Body-as-battering-ram once per 30-hour day — smashes through walls (used in Book 2 to escape the Remex explosion)
- **Immune to wind spell damage**
- **Good at fixing engines** (Earth Hobby Potion — Gear Head)
- Weakness: no offensive capacity, low STR and CHA

## Book 2

Introduced by Hekla of Brynhild's Daughters, who asked Carl to help her level up. Odette later warned Carl that Katia was sent as an unwitting plant — Hekla wanted Donut to join Brynhild's Daughters so she could access Mordecai's manager expertise. Katia herself is likely unaware of this.

Katia participated in the Remex/Magistrate quest's final sequence. Her **Rush** ability punched through a wall during the escape from the Doomsday Scenario precursor bursts, saving Carl, Donut, and Mongo. She joined Carl's party and appeared on Odette's show at the end of Book 2.

Mordecai genuinely likes her. She is shy and speaks in one-word answers under pressure.

## Book 3

Mordecai coached her to put all stat points in STR until 50, then CON until base 100. Invented the "Ugly-Ass Backpack" rig with Carl: by loading 15 tons of transformation mass into an equippable backpack, she can become a giant battle-form. Used as a living cowcatcher (plow) attached to the front of trains to clear tracks.

**Hekla's betrayal and death:** Hekla revealed she had been trying to kill Carl and Katia so Donut would be forced to join Brynhild's Daughters (for Mordecai access). During the confrontation, Katia accidentally decapitated Hekla with her Rush ability. Katia jumped from Level 24 → **Level 37** largely from killing Hekla.

**Katia inherits Hekla's enchanted automatic repeating crossbow** — legendary weapon.

Katia accidentally killed Ji-Hoon at the Desperado Club dance floor → received Desperado Pass tattoo (now a club member). Also received Find Crawler skill L3 from a gold savage box.

Her Eva (former friend/Daughter) had deliberately left her on Floor 3 to join Hekla. This revelation hit Katia hard. By the end of Book 3, she's fully committed to Carl and Donut's team.

Sponsor: Princess Formidable of the Skull Empire (King Rust's daughter, now heir after Maestro/Stalwart's presumed deaths).

Final level: **41** (after the Floor 4 endgame battles). **#8 on leaderboard** at 100K bounty.

## Books 4–5

Fell off the leaderboard by choice (better to not be targeted). Separated from Carl/Donut entering Floor 6 (different stairwell). Level 60 by Book 5 end. Hunted down Eva Sigrid (former best friend, player killer). Accidentally killed Eva in the final confrontation at the Butcher's Masquerade. Eva's dying act: placed the **Crown of the Sepsis Whore** on Katia's head, making Katia also a member of the Blood Sultanate's succession line.

In a romantic relationship with **Daniel Bautista** (Level unknown; shapeshifting ring; formerly ran a tea shop with his family). They're in Iceland for Floor 8.

## Book 6

### Floor 8 (The Ghosts of Earth)

Based in Iceland for Phase 1. Squad includes Bautista, Louis, Britney, and Tran. Connected with the surviving daughters of Hekla's team. Carl and Katia planned to assassinate **Astrid** (Level 125 bloodlust sprite, Desperado Club assistant manager) — this is Katia's Guild of Suffering quest. Successfully completed.

**Key revelation to Carl:** Katia knew about the Crown on her head early in Book 6, and discovered a way out — the goddess Yemaya's ascension could begin the chain. She messaged Carl mid-combat in the Thorn Room telling him to repair Yemaya's shrine instead of Ogun's. This is the first step in Huanxin's plan to save both Donut and Katia from the Crown obligation.

**Ysalte's death:** During the Book 6 climax battle in Havana, Katia instructed Paz Lo (as a T'Ghee totem) how to use the **Bolt of Ophiotaurus** from her inventory. Paz beheaded the goddess Ysalte with it. Critically: the bolt was recovered intact and **can be reused**. Katia smiled when Carl pointed this out — she'd been saving it to potentially use on her Skull Empire sponsor's brother.

**Drug use:** During Phase 1, Katia found a "drug den" in the Desperado Club and passed out after using a "bliss surf" potion (reliving memories). Imani discovered this may not have been the first time. Bautista was making excuses for her. The issue is unresolved.

**Crown / Huanxin deal:** Huanxin (sponsoring goddess Eileithyia) had promised: if Carl helped ensure Odette didn't become his Faction Wars adjutant, she would take Katia to the 12th floor via Faction Wars and protect her from the Crown's obligation. With Odette now entering the dungeon as the goddess Nekhebit (a direct rival), this deal is in serious jeopardy.

> [!warning] Crown complication — unresolved
> Katia now has the Crown of the Sepsis Whore on her head. On Floor 9, only one person with the Crown can descend to Floor 10. Either Donut OR Katia can descend; the other cannot. Huanxin's deal (taking Katia to Floor 12 via Faction Wars) was the proposed solution, but Odette entering as Nekhebit complicates everything.

## Book 7

### Floor 9 — Resolution of the Crown

The Orchid of Eileithyia's Grace was consumed with 65 minutes left before Emberus destroyed the floor. Katia was unconscious for an hour.

**Key revelation:** Through a bliss surf connection, Carl accidentally shared Katia's most painful memory — she had been trying to adopt a terminally ill baby girl named Annie (brain cancer). Her ex-boyfriend Fannar sabotaged the adoption. The baby died. Katia had been carrying this loss silently for years. This is why she chose the skull-bird card named Annie.

**Huanxin Jinx killed.** While Eileithyia was frozen by the "choice pending" mechanic, Donut ripped Huanxin out of the goddess's soul armor using Laundry Day. Donut fired Katia's crossbow (via the mount on her back) and killed Huanxin with a bolt tipped with Mordecai's mother's ashes.

**Katia's choice:** She chose **pregnancy**. The reason: she would be ejected from the dungeon to Earth's surface, where she could protect the children (the NPC children from the Zerzura transfer who now live near the 12th floor). Eileithyia confirmed the wisdom. Louis Santiago agreed to be the technical father (Bautista is no longer human; the goddess couldn't allow Carl). *The child will be a boy.*

**Human form and cybernetics:** When Katia reverted to human, she was missing her right arm, both legs, and one ear — accumulated losses from the dungeon that her doppelganger form had been compensating for. Bautista had secretly built her cybernetic replacements. She left the dungeon with them installed.

**Current status:** On Earth's surface. Pregnant. Human. Has cybernetic limbs. The Homecoming Queen is sending 4 Cronus suits + a landing skiff to protect her.

> [!warning] Crown of the Sepsis Whore RESOLVED
> Katia's ejection from the dungeon via pregnancy fulfilled the conditions of the Crown deal. Donut is now free to descend from Floor 9. Both crowns are resolved.
