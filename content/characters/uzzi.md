# Uzzi

**Uzzi** was a Skyfowl warrior crawler who participated in the dungeon crawl hundreds of cycles ago. He was the brother of [[mordecai]] and a key member of their original party.

## Biography and Role

During their climb, Uzzi chose the most difficult path on the tenth floor, which ultimately led the party into a crisis. With the level collapse imminent and the exit puzzle requiring the magic of a storm god to activate, the party was stranded. 

To save the party and ensure [[odette]]'s freedom, Odette coerced [[chaco]] into worshipping the storm goddess [[dodola]] and sacrificing Uzzi using the [[dart-of-ophiotaurus]]. Chaco threw the dart into Uzzi's breast, which initiated a forced summoning of Dodola inside a containment field. The summoning killed Uzzi, but the resulting explosion destroyed Dodola, creating the key necessary for the surviving party members to escape to the eleventh floor. 

Uzzi's tragic death and Odette's calculated betrayal fractured the party permanently, prompting Mordecai to vow lifelong vengeance against Odette.
