# Mackenzie

**Mackenzie** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

The simulated wife of Archie Mu and mother of Oliver in the fourth-floor apartment. She is distressed by strange events and her missing son, and later begins tearing at her own eyes as the simulation breaks down around her, revealing her status as a 'shell'.
