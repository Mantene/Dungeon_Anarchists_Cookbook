# Drakea

**Drakea** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

Drakea was a crawler from a long time ago who attempted a quest similar to Carl's current objective to kill Hellik. His efforts were ultimately sabotaged by the Nagas, leading to his death and the failure of his quest, which Donut cites as a cautionary tale.
