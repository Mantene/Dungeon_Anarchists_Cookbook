# Tart

**Tart** is the team adjutant for the Reavers during Faction Wars on the ninth floor.

## Profile & Traits
- **Appearance**: A demonic-looking caprid/deer alien with large antlers and glassy eyes, resembling a hybrid between a caprid liaison (like Harbinger) and a mule deer.
- **Personality**: Despite a name that gets mocked by the crawlers as a "stripper name," Tart speaks with a proper accent and is a self-proclaimed galaxy-foremost political analyst.
- **Actions**: During the final hours of Faction Wars, Tart accompanies Victory and Drick at the Reaver castle to oversee the defeat of the Reaver team.
