# King Croissant

King Croissant is a Level 60 Totem kitten opponent summoned by Judge Lucian. He appears as a tiny, fuzzy black kitten with a headset microphone, representing the replacement cat Beatrice wanted. He has a loud baritone voice and can cast a teleportation spell.
