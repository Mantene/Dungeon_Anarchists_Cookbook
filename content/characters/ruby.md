# Ruby

Ruby is a minor Changeling child NPC in Hump Town.

## Characteristics & Role
Ruby is one of the changeling children brought to Carl and Donut's personal space for shelter in [[120-book-4-chapter-10-the-collateral-code]]. She suffers from **Compression Sickness**, which has left her with no arms, a noticeble limp, and a head that is sunken at the top like a deflated soccer ball.
