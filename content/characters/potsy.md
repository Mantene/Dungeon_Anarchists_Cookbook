# Potsy

**Potsy** is a ram-like NPC who serves as a guard at Club Vanquisher on Floor 8.

## Biography and Role

Potsy is an Ares Warrior Cleric Middle Priest (Level 40). He and his twenty brothers are highly devout door guards who spend their shifts praying to the stained-glass image on the door. They believe the image depicts their deity, Hellik, bringing truth to Apito, although the window actually depicts Hellik's older brother Taranis. They stubornly refuse to believe or accept that the image is Taranis, despite the creator of the window confirming it.

In Book 6, Chapter 18, Potsy guards the entrance to the inner sanctuary of the club and insists on proper paperwork before allowing Carl to enter, until Pater Coal intervenes and bypasses him.
