# Skylar Spinach

**Skylar Spinach** is a Rare T'Ghee card totem representing a "Two-Thirds of an Enraged Ghommid Splitter."

## Biography and Role

Skylar Spinach is a non-corporeal, affliction-dealing Ghommid splitter monster defeated by Carl and Princess Donut inside [[pueblo-de-los-olvidados]] and captured into a T'Ghee totem card. 

The card is automatically activated and placed into Carl and Donut's squad. As a "splitter" type mob, Skylar Spinach breaks into two pieces upon sustaining a fatal blow, one of which acts as the primary. It continues to split until the primary is killed twice, with each successive iteration of the primary having increased Constitution.

## Attributes
- **Class**: Ghommid Splitter (Non-corporeal / Affliction-dealing)
- **Card Rarity**: Rare
- **Status**: Captured in Carl and Princess Donut's T'Ghee deck
