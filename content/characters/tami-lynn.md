# Tami-Lynn

Tami-Lynn is Carl's stepmother, married to Robert the Human. She has a neck tattoo of a pitbull named Chance. To save Asher from Robert's abuse, she administered a lethal overdose of morphine to Robert shortly before the collapse. In the dungeon, her likeness forms one of the heads of the Reminiscence Hydra.
