# Yolanda Martinez

A CNA crawler and member of [Brandon An](brandon-an.md)'s Team Meadow Lark. Killed on Floor 2 by a Level 93 Rage Elemental summoned when Resident Jack urinated in the dungeon.

## Background

Yolanda Martinez was one of four able-bodied CNAs in Brandon An's group — the Team Meadow Lark party consisting of the CNAs and 38 elderly nursing home residents from Wenatchee, Washington. Brandon's group had traveled with Carl and Donut on Floor 1 and descended together to Floor 2.

---

## Book-by-Book Progression

### Book 1

Yolanda's death is one of the defining events of Floor 2's middle section.

**The Rage Elemental incident (Chapters 38–40):** Resident Jack urinated in the dungeon, triggering one of Floor 1–2's harshest punishments: the summoning of a **Level 93 Rage Elemental**. The elemental killed Yolanda and another group member named **Randall**, then trapped the survivors in a safe room.

> "It kills Yolanda Martinez and Randall, then traps the group in a safe room."
> — [Book 1: Dungeon Crawler Carl](../sources/book-1-dungeon-crawler-carl.md)

Carl's response to the elemental is one of Book 1's major set pieces: he built the **"Bomb Chicken" (MOAB)** — a bicycle trailer bomb launcher — and used the indestructible alchemy table as a shield to lure the elemental to the stairwell chamber. The elemental dissolved when it hit the void below the stairwell. Carl and Donut received no XP from the kill but achieved a new record, and views hit 212 billion.

Yolanda was not saved; she died before Carl could stop the elemental.

## Notes

The designation "13" that appears in some early wiki references (e.g. "Yolanda Martinez 13") likely refers to an internal numbering convention used in the character table in Book 1's source summary, not a dungeon suffix. DCC uses number suffixes for characters who are duplicates in the dungeon system (e.g. "Chris Andrews 2," "Louis Santiago 2"), but no such context is given for Yolanda.
