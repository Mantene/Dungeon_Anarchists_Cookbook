# Geyrun

**Geyrun** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

The deceased son of the deity Emberus. His murder is the central focus of a critical quest given to Carl, which Carl believes is key to unraveling a larger conspiracy. Samantha was reportedly present when someone was hired to commit the murder.
