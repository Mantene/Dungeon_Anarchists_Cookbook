# Brother Widespread Tempest

**Brother Widespread Tempest** is a level 125 Ogre Basilica Cleric Guard who appears during the Faction Wars on the ninth floor in *[[dinniman-2024-tir|This Inevitable Ruin]]*.

## Appearance & Role
Widespread Tempest is an eight-foot-tall, golden-skinned ogre dressed in flowing robes. He serves as a Basilica Guard, a group of ogres who normally guard the Basilica Entrance of Club Vanquisher on the 12th floor. 

As a devout worshiper of [[taranis]], he possesses the **Untouchable** status, which prevents deities from attacking him. Basilica Guards are among the few non-deities capable of temporarily blocking a deity from passing thresholds, making them highly necessary but widely disliked "tow truck drivers of the Ascendency."

## Biography
During the chaos in the temples, Brother Widespread Tempest confronts [[carl]] with a squad of mantaurs. Carl bypasses his "Untouchable" status by throwing a **Black Nimbus** potion at him. The potion removes his immunity, instantly marking him for death by numerous angry deities. Panic-stricken, Brother Widespread Tempest flees the hallway.
