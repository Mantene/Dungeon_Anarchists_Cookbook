# Silfa

**Silfa** is a crawler and a member of [[hekla]]'s guild, Brynhild's Daughters. 

## Biography & Role
Silfa is a Level 25 **Wisteria Fairy** who specializes as a **Holistic Healer**. She is described as a plump woman about fifty years old and half the size of [[princess-donut]]. Despite having over two dozen boss kills, she displays extreme anxiety in combat.

In Chapter 97, she rides in the engine cab during the Vermillion line train rescue. When the train plows into the massive ghoul hordes, she panics and refuses to cast heals on [[katia-grim]] until she is at 25% health, prompting Carl to yell at her. When the cab is subsequently swarmed and filled with gore, she flees and hides in the train's rear apartment.

- **Incompetence Under Pressure**: Despite her level and boss kills, Silfa's panic and adherence to arbitrary healing rules (waiting until 25% health) highlight the unreliability of some guild-sheltered crawlers when faced with chaotic, high-intensity combat.
