# Jessica

**Jessica** is a powerful Class A/B changeling NPC working for [[juice-box]] and Team Retribution during the Faction Wars on the ninth floor in *[[dinniman-2024-tir|This Inevitable Ruin]]*.

## Role in the Crawl
Jessica is an old and advanced changeling capable of partial transformations (e.g. changing only her head while leaving the rest of her body unchanged). She has a vast library of copied forms from her past lives, including the gentle, fairy-like ice elves she lived among several lifetimes ago.

During the trap construction phase, Jessica mimics the sluggalette sapper [[sick-ass-worgo]]. [[justice-light]] catches the deception and confronts her. Jessica reverts to a blank humanoid shape to communicate with Justice Light, acting as a channel for Juice Box's commands.
