# Koki

**Koki** is a human crawler from Japan who participates in the fourth-floor crawl.

## Biography and Role
Koki is introduced in Chapter 89 as one of the crawlers on Carl's newly established communication network. He is a warrior-class crawler. 

Koki discovers a method to broadcast messages to everyone on one's contact list simultaneously. This discovery allows crawlers to share intelligence and trade items quickly, but it also creates a flood of uncoordinated requests and alerts, forcing Carl to set up a filter folder to manage his interface.
