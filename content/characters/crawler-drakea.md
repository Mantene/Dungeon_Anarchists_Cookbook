# Crawler Drakea

**Crawler Drakea** was a crawler from a previous season who authored notes in the 22nd Edition of [[the-anarchist-cookbook]].

## Cookbook Insights
- **Floor 6 Notes**: In [[169-book-5-chapter-24-obsidian-bracelet]] (Chapter 24), Carl reads Drakea's notes regarding Floor 6 (The Hunting Grounds). Drakea advises that:
  - Hunters avoid elites because they know they cannot easily defeat them under showrunner rules.
  - Elites are highly numerous and dangerous, and should be avoided.
  - The most effective strategy is not to fight hunters or elites directly, but to "bring hunter and elite together" and set traps that target their coins/sponsorships, hurting the Naga and the showrunners on a systemic scale.
