# Mrs. Parsons

Mrs. Parsons was [[carl]]'s grumpy first-floor neighbor in Seattle. 

## History
Known for being a complainer who filed complaints, she stuck her head wrapped in a towel out her window on the night of the collapse to yell at Carl for trying to retrieve [[princess-donut]]. She was talking to Carl when the building collapsed, crushing her instantly. Carl found her severed head shortly after the transformation.
