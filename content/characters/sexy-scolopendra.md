# Sexy Scolopendra

**Sexy Scolopendra** is the level 1 crawler version of the Dungeon Boss [[scolopendra]], created at the end of the eleventh floor in Book 8.

## Background & Transformation
- **Origin**: Scolopendra is the Level 500 Dungeon Boss. 
- **The Mistake**: Carl intended to turn Scolopendra into a regular pet companion to keep her safely contained in his inventory. However, due to his unorganized inventory, Donut accidentally handed him the wrong pet biscuit. 
- **Crawler Status**: As a result of the incorrect biscuit, Scolopendra was transformed into a sapient, level 1 crawler (Crawler #12,953,456) named Sexy Scolopendra.
- **Appearance**: She appears as a human-sized centipede with a humanlike face, giant lips, and twin yellow antennae (one of which has a red bow). She speaks in a soft, sultry voice.

## Actions
- **Chapter 499**: Upon her transformation, Sexy Scolopendra skitters forward, wraps herself around Carl, touches his nose with an antenna, and says, "Boop." She urges Carl to go through the stairwell portal before the level collapses.
