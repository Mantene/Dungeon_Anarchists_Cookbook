# Vadim Zbar

Dr. Vadim Zbar was a Level 28 gecko-like **Kuhli** crawler from Ukraine. His class was **Gut Rearranger** (a healer/rogue hybrid). In the real world, he was a famous cosmetic surgeon who advertises clinics, gets sued for botched surgeries, and is known for fathering dozens of children with clients.

## History & Death
- **Floor 5 Dive**: Vadim agrees to dive with [[carl]], [[katia-grim]], and [[tran]] to disable the submarine *Akula*'s pump in [[135-book-4-chapter-25-gelato-and-gills]].
- **Panicking and Death**: When the submarine is swallowed by [[lusca]] in [[136-book-4-chapter-26-mouthbrooding-mayhem]], Vadim panics and flees up to the escape pod chamber. Despite Carl's warnings that they are inside the giant monster, Vadim seals himself in the room and launches the final escape pod in [[137-book-4-chapter-27-confronting-lusca]]. The pod is intercepted by the system's pain-amplifying jellyfish, and Vadim dies before reaching the surface.
