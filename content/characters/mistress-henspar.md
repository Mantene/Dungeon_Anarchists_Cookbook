# Mistress Henspar

**Mistress Henspar** was a crawler and the original owner of [[volteeg]] when he was a pet starling before entering the dungeon.

## Biography

Before the dungeon crawl, Mistress Henspar was Volteeg's owner and best friend. She cared for him, kept his cage clean, and allowed him to perch on her shoulder when they visited the market, sneaking him berries to eat. 

When they entered the dungeon, she protected Volteeg. On the third floor, she fed him a pet biscuit, which gave him intelligence and speech. She died on the seventh floor of the dungeon. Her death deeply affected Volteeg, driving his rage, leading to his acquisition of the [[the-anarchist-cookbook|Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook]], and ultimately motivating his suicide attack on Floor 9 in Book 7.

## Status
- **Status**: Deceased (died on the 7th floor of the dungeon)
