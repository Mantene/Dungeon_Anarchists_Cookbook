# Dr. P. Hu

Dr. P. Hu is the CEO and President of Outreach Operations for *The Open Intellect Pacifist Action Network, Intergalactic NFC*, a major sponsor group.

## History & Actions
- **Floor 5 Sponsor Gift**: Dr. Hu's group sends Carl the highly expensive Emergency Platinum Benefactor Box containing the custom Xistera extension slot (decapitated head-tosser attachment) in [[144-book-4-chapter-34-a-clash-of-demons]].
- **Epilogue**: In [[145-book-4-epilogue-epilogue]], Dr. Hu and [[porthus]] discuss their massive financial support of Carl, stating they are "going all in on him" to shake up the system.
