# Mukta

Mukta is a hostile Kua-tin Admin who temporarily replaces [[zev]] as Carl and Donut's PR outreach associate.

## History & Dungeon Presence
When Zev is placed in a "time out" (suspended by the System AI for warning Carl and Donut about the Rage Elemental), Mukta takes over as their substitute. Unlike the sympathetic Zev, Mukta is cold, authoritarian, and unsympathetic to the crawlers. 

She overrides Zev's balanced scheduling and forces Carl and Donut to appear on the exploitative and cruel show *Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem* hosted by [[the-maestro]], warning them that failure to comply will result in their execution or acceleration of the dungeon timeline.
