# Joel the Camel

**Joel the Camel** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

An undead camel who serves as the driver for Carl and Donut's truck during the race. Despite sustaining significant damage, including Carl's hand passing through his chest and later a broken neck, he continues to operate the vehicle, often groaning in the process.
