# Anaconda

**Anaconda** is a crawler who works as a dancer/stripper at the Desperado Club on the ninth floor in *[[dinniman-2024-tir|This Inevitable Ruin]]*.

## Role in the Crawl
Anaconda, along with fellow dancer [[damascus-steel]], goes missing from the Desperado Club prior to the launch of "Operation Ruin." [[dong-quixote]] and others search the club for them, but the new concierge and staff claim to have no knowledge of their whereabouts.
