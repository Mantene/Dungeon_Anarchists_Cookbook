# Doctor Bones

Doctor Bones is a Level 90 Skin Skellie Bard and former Desperado Club DJ/announcer. After Astrid's death, he pledges his patronage to Carl and trains in the Mercenary Arcade. He can cast 'Conscript' to mind-control weak-minded opponents into minions.
