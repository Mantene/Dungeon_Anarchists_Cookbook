# Tizquick

**Tizquick** is a dwarf NPC conductor for the Mango line on Floor 4 (The Iron Tangle).

## Biography
Tizquick is encountered by [[carl]] and [[hekla]] at Station 60, weeping on the ground. He has realized that his programmed memories of his little daughter are a complete fabrication designed by the dungeon system to enforce overtime work. Devastated, he asks Carl, "She was never real, was she? My little girl was never real." Carl comforts him, saying, "One day, this pain you're feeling right now will matter."

## Graphify Deductions
- **NPC Tragic Consciousness**: Tizquick's existential despair highlights the cruel systemic design of Floor 4, where the Syndicate programs complex emotional backstories (like families and homes) into NPCs to control them, only for the NPCs to discover their lives are artificial illusions.
