# Maurice

**Maurice** was a Level 99 neighborhood boss and Chieftain of the northern ginger cannibal settlement on the tenth floor of the dungeon in Book 8 (*A Parade of Horribles*).

## History & Dungeon Presence
Maurice's death is made mandatory by the **"Pasty Inferno"** quest triggered in [[442-book-8-chapter-43-ginger-chieftain]]. Since the quest is mirrored across different race tracks, different iterations of Maurice exist:
- On [[imani-c]] and [[elle-mcgibbons]]'s track (the slums), Maurice appears as an orangutan named the "Red Menace of Tinker Town." They kill him in [[449-book-8-chapter-luciennes-demise]].
- On Carl and Donut's track (Satan's Water Park), Maurice appears as a large Shrek-like human. In [[449-book-8-chapter-luciennes-demise]], as Carl's truck slides down the frozen River of Sloth dragging a makeshift brush hog, Donut casts *Summon Ye, Vermin*. A massive wave of drill-headed zipper shrews swarms Maurice, drilling through him and killing him. Carl's vehicle then runs over the remains, completing the quest.
