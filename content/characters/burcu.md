# Burcu

**Burcu** is a badger-headed porsuk crawler who appears during the Faction Wars on the ninth floor in *[[dinniman-2024-tir|This Inevitable Ruin]]*.

## Appearance & Role
Burcu is a swashbuckler-class crawler. She is part of the group of crawlers who competed for the memorial crystal of [[apito]] during the Butcher's Masquerade on the sixth floor.

## Biography
During the ninth floor, Burcu is captured by Warlord [[architect-houston]] of the Madness. Houston brutally tortures her, cutting out her tongue and voice box, and executes her mother in front of her. She is bound to a surgical table in the Madness castle's operating theater.

When Warlord [[carl]] infiltrates the castle, Burcu uses the [[the-anarchist-cookbook]]'s *Book of Voodoo* to sketch a map of the surgical theater and warn Carl of the fireball traps. Carl uses the *Wand of Nighty-Night* to put her to sleep to prevent her from setting off the floor traps. However, during the subsequent chaos, she falls from the table, triggering the Sheol fire traps which incinerate her.
