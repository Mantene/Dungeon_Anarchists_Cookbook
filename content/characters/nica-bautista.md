# Nica Bautista

Nica Bautista was a Level 8 Filipino human crawler. She was the sister of Grace and Lea Bautista.

## Death
Nica and her sisters were killed by Circus Lemurs on Floor 3. Carl loots two +2 Strength rings from her corpse.
