**Na** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

A complex and powerful AI entity who has previously been a member of Carl's guild and is fundamental to aspects of the Dungeon. Elle, Zhang, and Tran plan to communicate with her to discuss a new strategic plan.
