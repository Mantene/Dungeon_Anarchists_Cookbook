# Gluteus

**Gluteus** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

Gluteus is a companion of Splash Zone who was unexpectedly stabbed by a croupier in the casino. Despite this injury, he is accompanying Splash Zone and Doctor Bones to seek an audience with Hamed, the new leader, to discuss finding Splash Zone's wife.
