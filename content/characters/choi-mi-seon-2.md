# Choi Mi-seon 2

**Choi Mi-seon 2** is a crawler who appears in *[[dinniman-2024-tir|This Inevitable Ruin]]*.

## Role in the Crawl
Choi Mi-seon 2 is active in the global chat during the Faction Wars. She requests the song "Detachable Penis" by King Missile for [[princess-donut]] to play over the PA system. The song is used to successfully confuse the god [[harpocrates]].
