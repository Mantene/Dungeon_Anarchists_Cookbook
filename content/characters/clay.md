# Clay

Clay is a Level 30 Dromedarian NPC guard at Hump Town.

## Characteristics & Role
Clay is an 8-foot-tall camel who walks on two legs. He wears a headscarf and robes. Clay carries a long spear, a bazooka/mortar-style rocket launcher, and Guided Anti-Air Rockets.

When Carl, Donut, and Katia arrive at the gates of Hump Town during a sandstorm in [[111-book-4-chapter-1-welcome-to-the-bubbles]], Clay meets them and charges a toll of "gold, drugs, or pleasure." Carl pays him with a blitz stick. Clay warns them to stay out of City Hall, triggering the quest **"Stay out of city hall."**
