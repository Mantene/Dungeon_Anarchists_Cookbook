# Henrietta

**Henrietta** was a crawler from Angola who participated in the tenth-floor vehicle races in Book 8 (*A Parade of Horribles*).

## History & Dungeon Presence
Henrietta is mentioned by [[imani-c]] in [[441-book-8-chapter-42-forest-flight]]. She was a new addition to their heat, but shortly after the race started, her vehicle hit a trap that swallowed the mount whole, resulting in the deaths of Henrietta and her partner.
