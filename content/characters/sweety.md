# Sweety

**Sweety** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

Prepotente's tapir companion. In chapter 444, Sweety is noted for enjoying the water tube sections of the track and seems to be instrumental in Prepotente's team finding the best paths.
