# Gravy Boat

Gravy Boat (referred to by [[beatrice]] as **Ferdinand**) is a yellow and orange stray cat from the real world. He survived the collapse on the surface in Seattle. He was a neighbor's cat who used to visit Beatrice's apartment to get close to [[princess-donut]].

## History & Capture
- **Found on Surface**: Beatrice and [[brad]] find Gravy Boat half-frozen in a tree near their old apartment and bring him back to the New Queen Anne settlement in Seattle. Beatrice, who is mentally unstable, treats him like Ferdinand and locks him in her tent in [[145-book-4-epilogue-epilogue]].
- **Capture**: [[sadir]] and [[gennrik]] infiltrate the camp to capture the cat for a Borant Walk-On bounty. After Lexis kills Gennrik and shoots Brad, she captures Gravy Boat in a bounty cube under [[odette]]'s orders, planning to hand him over to Borant.
