# Edgar

**Edgar** is an ancient, talking giant tortoise who is a **Shell Mage** and fights alongside [[tsarina-signet]] on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Profile & Traits
- **Background**: Edgar is over 150 years old and has been with Signet since the night her mother was killed. He has a close friendship with [[princess-donut]].
- **Abilities**: Edgar can cast a massive protection/invisibility spell called *Secreted*, which can hide a whole army from sight. He also uses a silver, needle-like stick to apply magical elementals as tattoos onto Signet's skin.

## Floor 6 Actions
- **Assault on Fort Freedom**: In [[170-book-5-chapter-25-future-huntress]] (Chapter 25), Edgar casts the *Secreted* spell to hide Signet's assault team near Fort Freedom. He actively participates in the submerged assault.
- **Nadine's Memorial**: In [[174-book-5-chapter-29-tattooing-nadine]] (Chapter 29), Edgar uses his tattoo stick to ink [[nadine]]'s ashes/remains onto Signet's thigh, preserving her and [[clint-smashgrab]]'s memories.

## Graphify Deductions
- **The Role of the Shell Mage**: Edgar's utility in stealth and magic applications (invisibility zones and tattoo elemental manipulation) makes him the primary magical support for Signet's rebel faction, illustrating how native NPCs can utilize high-level protective spells to counter the surveillance of the dungeon's showrunners.
