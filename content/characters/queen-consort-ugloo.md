# Queen Consort Ugloo

Queen Consort Ugloo is the Queen of the Skull Empire, mother of [[prince-stalwart]] and [[the-maestro|Prince Maestro]], and wife of King Rust.

## Profile
Queen Consort Ugloo is killed alongside her younger son, the Maestro, when the Valtay Corporation destroys Prince Stalwart's personal yacht in retaliation for the assassination of [[manasa]].
