# Ripper Wonton

Ripper Wonton is a Setonix (commonly called a Quokka) NPC who hosts the intergalactic political talk show **Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton**.

## Profile
Ripper Wonton is a small, four-foot-tall, furry brown creature that is incredibly cute but possesses strong opinions. He is a good-hearted and fair host who moderates debates on his show. 

During Carl and Donut's interview in Chapter 19 ([[068-book-2-chapter-19-danger-zone]]), Ripper leads the discussion about the Valtay debt disputes and the political unrest in the Skull Empire. He is highly professional but gets deeply upset when his long-time friend and guest panelist [[manasa]] is killed in a orbital pulse attack meant for Carl and Donut. He reports the retaliation by the Valtay that destroyed Prince Stalwart's yacht.
