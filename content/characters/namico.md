# Namico

**Namico** is a changeling crawler or NPC who appears posthumously in *[[dinniman-2024-tir|This Inevitable Ruin]]*.

## Role in the Crawl
Namico is killed and dissected by Viceroy [[architect-houston]] in the Madness castle's operating theater. Houston attempts to physically and neurologically merge himself with Namico's dead form using the **Grease Monkey** surgical unit, believing the changelings hold the key to reaching a location called "the Beautiful Place." During the operation, Namico's dead body begins to mutate, growing roots and vines before being incinerated in the Sheol fire traps.
