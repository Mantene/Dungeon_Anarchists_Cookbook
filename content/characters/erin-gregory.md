# Erin Gregory

**Erin Gregory** is a human crawler introduced in Book 8.

## Biography and Role

Before the tenth floor, Erin Gregory was a member of [[florin]]'s party and gained notoriety for killing orcs by the hundreds during the Faction Wars. 

In [[414-book-8-chapter-15-the-lollipop]], she is part of the racing team **"Chris and the Erins,"** alongside [[chris-2|Chris]] and [[erin-stratos]]. The team drives a slow semitruck with a trailer on the tenth floor. She meets [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] during their crawler meeting at the Lollipop bar.
