# Ricky Joe

Ricky Joe is a Level-2 Juvenile Fathom Dwarf NPC who lives in the medium skyfowl settlement on Floor 3. He is the son of [[eunice]].

## Profile
Ricky Joe is a rowdy child who tortures a pet salamander named Benjamin by tying him to a stick. When Benjamin is eaten by [[mongo]], Ricky Joe is furious. During the guards' confrontation in Chapter 20, Ricky Joe attempts to punch Mongo to avenge his salamander. In response, Mongo bites off Ricky Joe's arm below the elbow. 

Carl rescues the bleeding boy by carrying him under his arm and puddle-jumping to a rooftop. Carl feeds him a healing potion to stop the bleeding, though the arm does not grow back. Mongo eventually regurgitates the severed arm. 

Later, Ricky Joe provides Carl with clues about the female skyfowl ([[miss-quill]]) who visited the temple with female recruits. He returns home to assist with his mother's labor, where his job is to hold her left leg.
