# Hekla

**Real name:** Hekla (Icelandic)  
**Race:** Amazonian  
**Class:** Shieldmaiden (Earth class)  
**Level:** 28 (end of Book 2)  
**Leaderboard:** #2 (500,000 gold bounty)  
**Party:** Brynhild's Daughters (leader)

---

## Description

Tall, broad, powerful blond woman from Iceland. Formerly pretty; now the physique of a professional bodybuilder after choosing the Amazonian race. Has an enchanted crossbow that functions like a ranged chainsaw. Extremely capable, emotionally cold and disciplined.

Brynhild's Daughters is an all-female crawler group. They were scattered when they entered Floor 3 (different guild guides means different entry points). Hekla has been reforming them through Floor 3.

---

## Book 1

Seen briefly on the recap show on Floor 2. Identified as the crossbow-wielding "Valkyrie" woman.

## Book 2

Makes direct contact via Katia during the _Danger Zone_ interview. Asks Carl to help Katia level up. Appears on the interview itself briefly. Largely quiet and observational.

**Hidden agenda (per Odette's warning):** Hekla is obsessed with recruiting Donut for Brynhild's Daughters — specifically to access Mordecai's manager expertise. She sent Katia as a deliberate scout. Hekla thinks Carl is "unstable" and doesn't want him in the party. She is described as "not evil, not psychotic like Lucia Mar, but very practical and cunning — doesn't have Carl's sense of justice."

Her husband died in the dungeon. She left him first. She regrets it.

---

## Book 3 — Death

Hekla's true plan was revealed on Floor 3: she had been building toward recruiting Donut into Brynhild's Daughters to gain access to Mordecai's managerial expertise. When this plan failed and she felt cornered, she attempted to kill both Katia and Carl. During the confrontation, **Katia accidentally killed Hekla** with her Rush ability — Katia was attempting to escape and didn't intend to decapitate her. Katia gained two levels from the kill, jumping from around Level 24 to **Level 37**.

Katia inherited Hekla's enchanted automatic repeating crossbow.

> [!warning] Status correction
> Hekla is **DEAD** as of Book 3. Killed accidentally by Katia Grim.
