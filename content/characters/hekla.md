# Hekla

Hekla is an Icelandic crawler who leads an all-female crawler faction called **Brynhild's Daughters**.

## History & Dungeon Presence
Hekla is first shown in the recap broadcasts in [[031-book-1-chapter-31-follows-and-favorites]] wearing a Valkyrie helmet and teaming up with other women to defeat a yeti borough boss. 

By [[039-book-1-chapter-39-platinum-scratchers]], she and her faction have grown in numbers, systematically clearing their way through the dungeon. The system AI feeds Hekla loot to match a "Norse shieldmaiden" persona, including a glittering breastplate. She is armed with a crossbow. Donut is highly impressed by her and suggests they should join her group, though Carl doubts he would be welcome.
