# Milk

**Milk** was a crawler from the 6th Edition of the dungeon crawl.

## Biography and Role

Milk is the author and editor of the 6th edition of the [[the-anarchist-cookbook]]. In his cookbook notes, Milk laments being abducted by the Syndicate before he could cook the yearly migration-route stew for his family. This stew was a sacred tradition on his home planet that bonded families and awakened ancestral navigational knowledge in their youth. 

Filled with grief for the younger members of his species left behind, Milk wrote that while there is honor in feeding one person, he wished to "cook a feast for the whole galaxy" and make the Syndicate "choke on our pain." His words resonated with [[rosetta]] (9th Edition), who replied in the cookbook and vowed to ensure their words were not lost.
