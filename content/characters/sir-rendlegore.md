# Sir Rendlegore

**Sir Rendlegore** is a Tummy Acher (Belly Acher) pet bonded to [[carl]] on the ninth floor.

## Profile & Traits
- **Appearance**: A round, giggly meatball-like creature half the size of a fist, featuring a sparse patch of black hair (resembling Linus from Peanuts) and a pronounced underbite with a single tooth.
- **Origins**: Tummy Achers were originally parasites that lived in the intestinal tracts of Jotan-class Titans. They are voracious eaters, resistant to most types of damage, and possess a powerful *Wrecking Ball* attack.
- **Mechanics**:
  - Sir Rendlegore has died 5 times, giving him a permanent **125% stat buff** but saddling him with **5 random weaknesses** that cause instant death (e.g. contact with water).
  - Every 5 deaths, the pet reverts to level 1, loses its memories, and must be renamed.
  - If he dies before reaching level 5, he will permanently die and fail to regenerate.
- **Bonding & Name**: Carl bonded with the pet by feeding him a bowl of seafood chowder. He was named *Sir Rendlegore* (suggested by [[louis-santiago-2]]) to fit the army's royal theme and honor the late crawler [[ren]].
