# Lexis

**Lexis** is the producer and personal assistant to talk-show host [[odette]], who operates in the production trailers outside the World Dungeon.

## Profile & Traits
- **Appearance/Behavior**: Lexis is a professional who helps manage the schedule and production of Odette's show. She often smokes a vape that emits rainbow-colored, burnt-candy-scented smoke. 
- **Sympathies**: While officially working for the show, Lexis is sympathetic to [[zev]] and the crawlers, and serves as a channel to filter highly sensitive, restricted information from Odette to Carl.

## Floor 6 Actions
- **Managing the Reunion**: In [[163-book-5-chapter-18-beatrice-is-alive]] (Chapter 18), Lexis manages the timeline for Carl and Donut's interview and reunion with [[beatrice]].
- **Leaking the Valtay Climax**: In [[165-book-5-chapter-20-country-boss-imogen]] (Chapter 20), during the system handover from mudskipper to Valtay control when no surveillance "sniffers" are online, Lexis quickly leaks critical information to Carl. She warns him that:
  - Queen [[imogen]] is a Country Boss and a repurposed human.
  - The top 50 crawlers will be forced to attend a party at the High Elf Castle (The Butcher's Masquerade), which is a trap set up to "zero out" the top crawlers so the Valtay can replace them with their preferred choices.
  - She advises Carl and Donut to descend the stairs to the next floor early to avoid this trap.

## Graphify Deductions
- **Clandestine Information Pipe**: Lexis acts as a crucial informational bridge between Odette (a veteran of the crawl with deep corporate insights) and Carl. By leveraging system handover blind spots, she provides the crawlers with warning of showrunner plans, highlighting how sympathetic production staff can disrupt the game's televised manipulation.
