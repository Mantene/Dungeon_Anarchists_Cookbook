# Tsarina Signet

**Race:** Elite (tall, powerful, humanoid; not a standard crawler race)  
**Class:** Unknown; her power comes from her magical tattoos  
**Level:** Unknown (very high; boss-tier NPC)  
**Affiliation:** Former queen of her world, now crawling for revenge; subject of the _Vengeance of the Daughter_ questline

---

## Background

Signet is covered head to toe in enchanted, prophetic tattoos. Each tattoo is a living memory, a helper, or a weapon. The tattoos were placed by a master artist named Edgar (a tortoise), who used stick-and-poke technique with magical ink.

Her mother was queen and was murdered by **Queen Imogen** of the high elves — the same high elf queen who is the country boss of Floor 6. Signet descended into the dungeon specifically to kill Imogen and reclaim her crown/revenge. The _Vengeance of the Daughter_ questline tracks this arc across multiple floors.

Her body has been resurrected multiple times via tattoo magic. Her tattoos include helpers, warriors, and protective entities. One of the most significant: a tattoo of her mother, **Princess Lunette** — which was animated at the end of Book 5 into a flesh golem body. Carl now carries Princess Lunette's body in his inventory (Samantha wants to inhabit it).

---

## Books 2–3

First seen on a TV recap show. Made direct contact with Carl's party on Floor 3. Carl was contractually bound via the _Vengeance of the Daughter_ quest to help her reclaim her crown and defeat Imogen. They became allies across Floors 3–6.

---

## Book 5 — The Butcher's Masquerade

The questline culminated at the Masquerade. Signet finally confronted Imogen — but to deliver the killing blow, she had to use a sacrificial spell that consumed herself. **She killed herself to kill Imogen.** The self-sacrifice was the only way to complete the quest as written.

However, in the chaotic final moments of the Masquerade, **both Signet and Imogen were pulled into "the Nothing"** — the celestial prison/void where disgraced or banished entities are sent. Whether Signet truly died or is trapped there is ambiguous.

Before she died/disappeared: she gave Carl the tattoo kit (Enchanted Prison Tattoo Kit, quest reward from _Vengeance of the Daughter_) and used her final power to animate Princess Lunette's body as a flesh golem for Samantha.

---

## Carl's Quest Rewards

The _Vengeance of the Daughter_ quest gave Carl:
- **Enchanted Prison Tattoo Kit** (Book 6 loot box reward)
- The ability to loot the high elf castle — though it was teleported to Floor 9 via Zerzura before Carl could use this

---

## The Nothing

"The Nothing" is the celestial prison where banished entities go. It is referenced multiple times — Inle (the deaf/mute Orisha) spent time there. It's unclear if those sent there can escape. Signet may still be alive inside it.
