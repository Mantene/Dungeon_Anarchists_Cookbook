# Hedy

**Hedy** is a gremlin vehicle mechanic NPC who manages [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]]'s garage on the tenth floor.

## Biography and Role

Hedy first appears in [[400-book-8-chapter-1-entering-your-garage]], where she and another gremlin, Waldrip Chris, scuffle to apply for the position of garage manager. Hedy defeats Waldrip Chris by hitting him with a mallet, and after Donut selects a mechanical vehicle, Waldrip Chris is disintegrated by the system.

Hedy manages the garage, coordinates mechanical upgrades, and maintains the Big Shot Chicken food truck. She recommends selecting the spider legs upgrade to help with the terrain of the heats.
