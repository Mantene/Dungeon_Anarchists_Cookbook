# Paulie

**Paulie** is a human host inhabited by the gondii Residual entity **Goff** in "The Shepherd's House" homeless shelter in Homestead, Florida.

## Biography and Role

- **The Residual Message**: Paulie is a "Residual," a class of entities that exist within primal engine worlds to teach the infant AI how to speak to its ancestors.
- **Primal Engines**: In the Prologue of Book 7, Goff explains that living things on pre-seeded worlds have a miniature primal engine in their brains. When they die, the showrunners harvest these activated elements to feed the comatose center system, expanding its zone for profit.
- **The Star Failsafe**: Using the permission of the system's AI, Goff installs an illegal, unregistered neural upgrade in Carl's natural interface. This gives Carl access to "the fuse," a failsafe trigger that can turn the system's star into a nova, destroying the AI and the system.
- **Cross-Contamination**: Goff reveals that there is a war among the Residuals. While Goff's official mission is to convince Carl to trigger the failsafe to disrupt the crawl, his long residence in Paulie's body has "cross-contaminated" him with human compassion. Goff warns Carl to treat the failsafe as a last resort.
