# Cleaner Bot

**Cleaner Bot** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A diligent but grumpy robot that Carl recognizes as a "goddamned hero" for its contributions and the abuse it endures. It is observed listening intently to conversations and is expected to perform a similar heroic function again soon.
