# Akuma

**Akuma** is a level 100 high-elf War Mage and the leader of the **War Mage Rebellion** during the Faction Wars on the ninth floor in *[[dinniman-2024-tir|This Inevitable Ruin]]*.

## Origin & Characteristics
Akuma is a dungeon-born entity who was originally a high-elf cobbler with a human skin. He has been resleeved 12 times, causing the ambient combat magic to fuse his flesh, skeleton, and spell pool into a single, sapient symbiote. 

Like all war mages, he is an exceptionally powerful magic caster. He is described by the system as a "complete prick," possessing a cold, cruel demeanor. 

## Biography
During the ninth floor campaign, Akuma and his rebel war mages broke their conscription allegiance to the Skull Empire. They cornered and executed Warlord [[prince-stalwart|Stalwart]] in the woods of Reaver territory, causing him to explode. 

When [[carl]] confronted him, Akuma stole the [[the-gate-of-the-feral-gods]] from the unconscious [[elle-mcgibbons]], mocked Carl's bare feet, and teleported away with his squad. It is later revealed that the War Mage Rebellion aligned with [[juice-box]] and Team Retribution during their adjudication hearing.
