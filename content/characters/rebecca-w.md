# Rebecca W

Rebecca W was a Level 3 human crawler who was murdered on Floor 1.

## Death & Investigation
In Chapter 13, [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] found her naked corpse leaning against a wall on the edge of the Hoarder's neighborhood, less than 200 meters from the safe room. The system identifier revealed she was killed by [[frank-q]]. She died of a single gunshot wound to the chest (right in the middle of a moth tattoo). Her body had been completely looted, leaving only an apple core. 

She was about 30 years old, Asian, with a ponytail and a colorful koi and crane sleeve tattoo on her left arm.
