# Very Sullen

Very Sullen is a Level 35 Rock Cretin NPC hired as a bodyguard.

## Characteristics & Role
Very Sullen is a rock monster cretin hired by Carl, Donut, and Katia at the Desperado Club in Hump Town. He works alongside [[bomo]] and [[the-sledge]] as part of their security detail. He first appears in [[117-book-4-chapter-7-total-utter-failure]].
