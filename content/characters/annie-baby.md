# Annie (Baby)

**Annie** was a terminally ill infant in Iceland who appears in [[katia-grim]]'s backstory and dream sequence in *[[dinniman-2024-tir|This Inevitable Ruin]]*.

## Biography
Annie was a baby girl who suffered from aggressive brain cancer. She was abandoned at an Icelandic children's hospital. Katia fell in love with her and applied to adopt her, but the application was blocked by Katia's ex-husband, [[fannar]]. 

Annie died alone in the hospital ward a few weeks later. Katia's summon/totem bird, Annie, is named in her memory.
