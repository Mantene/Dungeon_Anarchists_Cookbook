# Everly

**Everly** was a Primal Sward Mantis crawler and the author of the **Fifth Edition** of the [[the-anarchist-cookbook]].

## Profile & Traits
- **Class**: Explosives Engineer
- **Race**: Primal (Birth Race: Sward Mantis)
- **Top Level**: 62
- **Dungeon Exit**: Crushed to death on the Eighth Floor
- **Sponsor**: Dictum Waystation Controls, Limited
- **Personality & Philosophy**: Fiercely individualistic. She abandoned her colony’s collective mindset before the collapse and refused to work with other mantises in the dungeon, preferring the company of mercenary NPCs.

## Biography & Death
During her run on the Eighth Floor, Everly entered a 50-level tower in the center of the grassland. She used an item gifted by her sponsor, *Gordon’s Compass of Vertical Labyrinth Management*, which guided her through the safest paths. 

Upon reaching the 50th floor, she discovered that the tower was a marketing stunt. Dictum Waystation Controls had lured her there to air a corporate advertisement attacking their competitor, Gordon Waystation Outfitters. With the tower's exit blocked and the ceiling slowly crushing downward, Everly was trapped. 

As she lay dying, she reflected on her life and realized that her "Inevitable Ruin" was not the trap itself, but her choice to isolate herself from others. Before she was crushed, one of her mercenaries spoke to her with a sudden flash of awareness (hinting at AI/Apothecary intervention), suggesting that death might not be inevitable. She managed to write her final entry in the Cookbook before dying.
