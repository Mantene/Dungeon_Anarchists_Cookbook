# Corcunda

**Corcunda** (also known as **Corky**) is a half-mantaur NPC dancer on the tenth floor of the dungeon.

## Biography and Role

Corcunda is the long-lost best friend of [[dong-quixote]]. He is a dancer/stripper who was split vertically down the middle by a *Split Personality* spell. He wears a black vinyl gimp suit with a zippered mouth to keep his organs and brain from spilling out, standing on a single leg. He is a member of **Team Lady Dominators and the Gimp** alongside [[genesis]] and [[rapture]].

In [[412-book-8-chapter-13-quest-reunite-corky]], Corcunda is spotted waving at [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] from his garage before the door closes. His appearance triggers the quest **"I have dreamed thee too long"**, which tasks Carl and Donut with reuniting Corcunda with Dong Quixote and finding his other half, Porky, to fuse them back together with a fleshmancer.

In [[418-book-8-chapter-19-penelope-the-pig]], Corcunda is seen in the back seat of the Lady Dominators' Pontiac GTO, put to sleep by a spell.
