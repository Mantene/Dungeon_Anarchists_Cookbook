## Background

Served in the US Coast Guard before working as a marine tech in Seattle. Lived with girlfriend Beatrice in a two-bedroom apartment. Beatrice had taken her Persian show cat Princess Donut on the road for shows. The night of the Transformation, Carl was outside in below-freezing weather in boxers and Crocs retrieving Donut from a tree after she jumped out a cracked window. He survived because he was outdoors when every building on Earth was crushed.

He was in the process of breaking up with Beatrice (she'd brought her ex on a Bahamas trip without telling Carl) and had been considering keeping Donut even after the breakup.

## Book 1

### Floor 1
Entered the dungeon in boxers, a leather jacket, and one pink Croc. Met Mordecai in the tutorial guild. Opened 10 loot boxes; key prizes: Nightgaunt Cloak of Stoutness, Trollskin Shirt of Pummeling, Toe Ring of the Splatter Skunk, Goblin Pass tattoo.

Donut transformed after eating the Enhanced Pet Biscuit. They formed The Royal Court of Princess Donut.

Built up an explosives-focused + bare-knuckle fighting style. Invented the Goblin Copper Chopper (traded meth and a chieftain's lair location to goblins Rory and Lorelai). Fought through neighborhood bosses (The Hoarder, The Juicer, goblin chieftain). Collaborated with Brandon An's Meadow Lark group; helped 36 elderly nursing home residents descend to Floor 2 via the borough boss stairwell (Ball of Swine fight).

Got Desperado Pass tattoo (dagger dripping blood on neck) for killing goblin babies in mass explosion.

Both Carl and Donut were interviewed by **Odette** aboard a production yacht on the ocean surface after reaching Floor 2. Key advice received: loot boxes aren't random; listen to Mordecai's subtle class/race clues on Floor 3.

Invented **Carl's Jug O' Boom** (moonshine + goblin oil + torch). Entered in the Dungeon Codex; earns 1 gold per kill when anyone else uses the recipe.

### Floor 2
Faced Krakaren Clone (anti-vax octopus neighborhood boss). Made the Rev-Up Immunity Smoothie (moonshine + blended laminak fairy) to counter taint/measles debuffs.

Rage Elemental incident: resident Jack urinated in the dungeon, summoning a Level 93 Rage Elemental. It killed Yolanda and Randall. Carl built the "Bomb Chicken" (MOAB) — a bicycle trailer bomb launcher. Used the indestructible alchemy table as a shield. Lured the elemental to the Floor 3 stairwell chamber, used Protective Shell + oil slick to slide it down the stairwell void. It dissolved; no XP awarded.

Antagonized Crown Prince Maestro of the Skull Empire on live galactic TV, triggering a "Pork Boy Snick" viral video that elevated Carl and Donut's appearance fee to equal Lucia Mar's.

At end of Floor 2: Level 13, heading into Floor 3 (The Over City) for race/class selection.

## Book 4

### Floor 5 (The Bubbles) → Entering Floor 6

Reached **Level 47** entering Floor 6. Leaderboard: **#1 (1,000,000 bounty × 2)**. Key acquisitions: **Ring of Divine Suffering** (stacks stats for each hunter killed; Mordecai furious about risks), two Valtay Neural Enhancer brain implants (permanent, shows elevation/airspeed/etc), **Pawna's Tears potion** (legendary: +5 to ANY skill when a skill reaches 15 naturally — saved for perfect moment), Toe Ring of the Leprous Bandit (sticky feet + super spreader).

Carl flooded the faction market city of Larracos with sharks before descending to Floor 6. Made his Floor 6 entrance speech: *"By the time the sixth floor collapses, every single hunter who dares to set foot on the same floor as us will be dead."*

## Book 5

### Floor 6 (The Hunting Grounds) → Entering Floor 8

Reached **Level 63** at end of Book 5. Class upgraded to **Agent Provocateur** (subclass of Compensated Anarchist; +INT per level). Gained Advanced Bomb Maker's Workshop for personal space.

Killed all hunters on Floor 6 → **Apex Predator achievement** → Celestial Predator Box → **Scavenger's Daughter back patch** (divine). Killed Vrah (the last hunter) and helped defeat Queen Imogen (country boss). Leaderboard dropped to **#3 (1,600,000 bounty)** — Donut went to #1.

Subclassing raised INT per level significantly. Bounty jumped to 1.6M gold due to all hunter kills. Floor 7 never happened (Prepotente destroyed it). Jumped directly to Floor 8 with Carl at Level 63.

## Book 8

### Floors 10–11 (Don't Come in Last / Parade of Horribles)

**Final ranking: #2** (Donut #3; leaderboard suspended at <30 crawlers). Level unknown.

**Key new items:** Oozy Form spell (become slime, pass through narrow spaces), Lucky Shot patch (one fatal blow redirected to party member per floor — was redirected to Penny), Hotlist Expansion (+10 slots, now 20), Memorial Crystal of Emberus, **Tracking Tag from the Kyryap** (unknown entity claimed Carl post-battle), **Mark of the Damned** (sun tattoos on hands changed to raised angry scars).

**Princess Chandra situation.** Carl is now legally married to Naga lawyer Princess Chandra via Widow's Rights. She tried to steal all of Carl and Donut's assets. Donut intimidated her. She sent Taurin assassins after Quasar's family (Tempest killed them). Chandra is still alive, still filing legal claims.

**The plan that went wrong.** Carl's plan to feed Scolopendra the Nothing Special enriched pet biscuit worked mechanically — the biscuit hit Scolopendra. BUT Eris swapped everyone's inventories mid-battle, and Donut accidentally gave Carl the wrong biscuit (party companion instead of pet biscuit). Scolopendra became a Level 1 **Crawler** ("Sexy Scolopendra") rather than a pet. She is now in Carl's party, not in a pet carrier.

**Emberus killed by Taranis.** Carl trapped Emberus inside Club Scolopendra (invulnerable space) using Gloom Wraith Phase. Donut teleported in, anchored Emberus with Danforth bolts + second use of Katia's Bolt of Ophiotaurus, then Puddle Jumped them out. Taranis saw Emberus fire at Penny and killed his own brother.

**The Kyryap and Mark of the Damned.** Post-battle, Carl found a dart in his leg — a Tracking Tag from something called "the Kyryap." He also noticed the Emberus sun tattoos are now the "Mark of the Damned." Details unknown.

## Book 7

### Floor 9 (Faction Wars)

Level unknown at end of floor (started at 73). #1 leaderboard (6,000,000 gold bounty).

**Key new items:** Gloom Wraith Phase Level 13 (non-corporeal lunge attack; scrambles unarmored biological targets; halves mana), Enchanted Toe Ring of the Well-Balanced (Mind Balance protects from Conscription and Shi Maria's control; Heart Balance auto-triggers heal at 2%; Walk on Air from Shi Maria's eye-eating), Carl's Book of Boom (second Book of Voodoo filled with skills from departing crawlers), **Containment Interface** secretly installed in brain by Paulie (used to DISABLE the failsafe — Carl didn't tell anyone).

**Ring of Divine Suffering discarded.** Donut swallowed the ring when Carl was contemplating using it on thousands of captured mercenaries. He let her. The ring's influence was removed.

**Shi Maria situation.** The Mind Balance skill was triggered hundreds of times per day as Shi Maria attempted to seize Carl's body. By Book 7 end, the balance was uncertain — she was winning the race to break through.

**Key events:**
- Led Operation Ruin (the coordinated four-front assault)
- Used the Gate of the Feral Gods multiple times
- Participated in the assassination of Empress D'Nadia (Prism Kingdom)
- Was present at King Rust's parlay and watched Rosetta decapitate him
- Held Louis's ribcage open during the heart transplant surgery
- Threw Samantha to Emberus via xistera to stall him
- Walked into the Floor 10 stairwell with Donut after Katia left

**The failsafe is disabled.** Carl secretly used the Containment Interface (installed by Paulie in the homeless shelter during Floor 8) to deactivate the failsafe. When Princess Formidable hit the button, nothing happened. Carl referenced it as "a hunch" to Donut.

**New wife?** At the Floor 10 outreach guild, Quasar told Carl his new floor lawyer is "Princess Chandra, Esquire" — who is apparently Carl's new wife. The circumstances are completely unexplained.

## Book 6

### Floor 8 (The Ghosts of Earth)

Reached **Level 73** by end of Book 6. Phase 1: Cuba (Havana). Phase 2: Florida Keys.

**Major new items:** Scavenger's Daughter back patch (divine — Soul Reaper builds essence from kills for one powerful melee strike; Climb; Mysterious Bone Key), Enchanted Right Back Atcha Personal Shield bracer, Book of Voodoo, Wand of Nighty-Night, Book Burner patch (+15% INT, once/day make opponent forget a spell), **Self-Immolation spell** (Devotee rank with Emberus — Sheol fire sphere around body), **Eye of the Bedlam Bride tattoo** (Shi Maria is now permanently attached to Carl's chest; exact effects unknown), Valtay Perso-Shield Platinum.

**T'Ghee Card System:** Carl serves as bodyguard while Donut is squad leader. His Soul Reaper benefit from the Scavenger's Daughter patch grew significantly stronger as he drained it across the floor.

**Family revelations:** The Phase 2 keymaster boss was a hydra using his family members as heads: Carl's coworkers, Bea's mother, his stepmother Tami-Lynn, and — devastating revelation — **Asher**, a half-brother Carl never knew existed. Bea had intercepted a message from Tami-Lynn about Asher to keep Carl from learning of him and going to Iowa. Carl's father died during Phase 2 (stepmother poisoned him).

**Faction Wars groundbreaking:** Used Emergency Action Item to have the audience vote on all pending Faction Wars actions. Result: **Safety Protections for Faction Sponsors removed (98% vote)** — all sponsors in Faction Wars can now be killed. NPC 10th team created (Juice Box and Ferdinand as co-warlords).

**Shi Maria incident:** During the Faction Wars battle in Havana, a Golden Combo card accidentally merged Carl with the spider city boss Shi Maria. She briefly controlled his body, eating Li Jun's eye and using the blood + toraline root to tattoo herself onto Carl's chest. He now carries **the Eye of the Bedlam Bride tattoo** — Shi Maria embedded in him. Donut ripped Shi Maria's card, but the tattoo remains. Shi Maria says she will teach Carl "how to become a god."
