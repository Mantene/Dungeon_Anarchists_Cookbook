# Carl

**Carl** (often referred to by his system/party designation as **Royal Bodyguard Carl**) is a 27-year-old former US Coast Guard marine technician who becomes one of the primary crawlers on Earth after the collapse. He is the author of the 25th Edition of [[the-anarchist-cookbook]].

## Background
Before the collapse, Carl lived in Seattle with his girlfriend [[beatrice]] and worked fixing electrical systems on boats. After discovering Beatrice lied about her ex-boyfriend accompanying her on a trip, Carl broke up with her but agreed to watch her show cat, [[princess-donut]]. He was outside in freezing weather to retrieve Donut when the Earth's buildings collapsed.

## Race & Class Selections
- **Race**: **[[primal-race|Primal]]** (blank slate progenitor race; imposes -1 to all base stats but unlocks all skills to be trained up to Level 20).
- **Class**: **Compensated Anarchist** (specialized as **Agent Provocateur** in Book 5: focuses on being the ultimate saboteur/explosives expert; +1 Intelligence on level-up, +1 skill level to Sapper's table, +2 to Explosives Handling, access to advanced *Bomb Maker's Workshop*, faster explosives training. Imposes -25% damage penalty with bladed weapons and +25% mana cost penalty for damage spells).

## Climax of Floor 4 (The Iron Tangle)
Carl organizes the floor-wide rescue plan:
1. He constructs a crane over the central Station 36 escape shaft, suspending a rapid-response portal cart tuned to the abyss to catch the emerging Krakaren clones.
2. He is accidentally dragged into Trainyard E, where he survives a televised *Death Challenge* against the Level 90 **Mimic Rex** and the Level 250 **War God Grull** (vessel of Prince Maestro). Using invulnerability potions and a summoning-hijack strategy (using an armless mantaur vessel), Carl teleports Grull to the abyss.
3. He coordinates the detonation of the *Nightmare* train (piloted by the suicidal NPCs Fire Brandy and Tizquick) at the bottom of the abyss. The massive explosion overloads the floor's remaining soul crystals and shuts down the ghoul generators.

## Floor 5, 6, & 8 Actions
- **Floor 5 (The Bubbles)**: Carl worships [[emberus]] to gain 60 seconds of invulnerability from a celestial grenade, crashes the biplane into him, and saves the puppy [[orthrus]], becoming a cleric-adherent of Emberus.
- **Preemptive Zockau Raid**: Carl teleports to Zockau, marks [[chin-dua]] with the [[ring-of-divine-suffering]], and uses the *Super Spreader* toe ring ability to link the debuff to mantis hunter [[xindy]]. He detonates high-yield thermobaric barrel bombs that devastate the city, trap the hunters, and kill 34 of them (including Xindy and Chin'Dua). He is severely injured by a Level 70 Grand/Mayor crossbow sniper and rescued in the jungle by [[miriam-dom]] and [[prepotente]].
- **Floor 8 (The Eye of the Bedlam Bride)**: Carl makes a deal with the goddess [[eileithyia]] to select [[odette]] as Floor 9 Adjutant in exchange for turning off crawler protections and curing Katia's Sepsis Crown curse. He visits Club Vanquisher, where [[pater-coal]] performs a foot sacrament that allows Carl to speak with [[emberus]] without system eavesdropping, and helps him acquire a ring that wards off debuffs. In the Desperado Club, Carl rips the wings off [[astrid]] to disable her magic, allowing Katia to complete her assassination quest. He subsequently uses a Meat Shields coupon to hire the Penis Parade dancers as Faction Wars mercenaries, while Damascus and Anaconda set off to fight the Night Wyrm. During a private conversation, [[odette]] reveals to Carl that the crawl's true purpose is planetary fuel/byproduct extraction to fund the Syndicate's planetary engines. Carl then transfers to Phase Two of the Faction Wars.


## Inventory & Magic Gear (Floor 6)
- **[[the-anarchist-cookbook|The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook]]**: A physical, non-system book passed down by previous crawlers to exploit system flaws.
- **Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering**: Involuntary legendary ring (+5% to all stats, Marked for Death skill). Equipped on his finger to track and kill hunters for stat boosts.
- **Trophy Hands/Claws**: 34 severed hands and claws of killed hunters (including Xindy's mantis claw and Chin'Dua's draconian hand) to be exchanged at the Butcher's Masquerade Prize Counter.
- **Nitro Sludge**: Inert blocks that act as an oxygen-enhancing amplifier, used to construct high-yield explosives.
- **"Just Wait Until Your Daddy Gets Home" Wheeled Bomb**: A 5-foot-tall high-yield thermobaric explosive cart stored in his personal space.
- **Motley Crue "Kickstart My Heart" Alarm Trap**: A triggered audio alarm trap stored in inventory.
- **Trollskin Shirt, Nightgaunt Cloak, War Gauntlet, BigBoi Boxers, and Pedicure Kit**: Equipped gear.
- **Enchanted Toe Ring of the Splatter Skunk**: Equipped (grants *Super Spreader*).
- **Tattoos**: Sun disc tattoos on both hands (Emberus adherence) and a worm-like green spiderweb on his left elbow (**The Night Wyrm's Nasty Little Web of Suffering**).
- **Carl's Doomsday Scenario**: Unstable custom explosive in inventory (safely simulated in the Bomber's Studio).

## Graphify Deductions
- **Insurgent Mindset**: Carl's entry in the 25th Edition of the Cookbook marks a transition from a survivalist to an active insurgent. He recognizes that both NPCs and monsters are victims of the system, and vows to "break" the Syndicate itself rather than merely trying to escape. His use of system exploits (portal manipulation, summoning hijacking, and crystal-overload detonations) shows his growing mastery over the dungeon's rules.
