# Penny (Penelope 3)

**Species:** Yorkshire pig (sapient)  
**Crawler number:** #12,953,456 (same as Scolopendra — they were processed together)  
**Level:** 10  
**Status:** Alive; with Taranis after Floor 11 arena

A sapient Yorkshire pig who served as bait for Taranis during the Floor 11 arena fight and is now apparently in some form of relationship with a Celtic thunder god.

---

## Background

Penny (full name Penelope 3) entered the crawl as a party member — a sapient pig. The AI had been using Taranis's deep affection for Penny as a "banana peel" distraction, keeping him occupied.

---

## Book 8 — The Arena

Carl's plan for the Floor 11 confrontation required summoning Taranis. The method: use Penny as bait.

1. Carl used Gloom Wraith Phase to enter Scolopendra's body carrying Penny
2. He installed a nipple ring on Penny to let her breathe inside
3. Donut teleported in using Lucia Mar's loop-de-loop trap, replacing Penny
4. Donut used the Bolt of Ophiotaurus on Emberus — anchoring and blinding him
5. Taranis saw Emberus fire at Penny and killed his own brother

Penny survived the encounter. A **Lucky Shot patch** Carl wore ("+4-leaf clover; one fatal blow redirected to party member per floor") was used on Penny during the fight.

---

## After the Arena

Taranis took Penny when he departed the arena (along with Krakaren and Samantha). Penny is described as "apparently in a relationship with Taranis (and also 'broke up' with him)" — the exact nature of their arrangement is left ambiguous. She is alive and with Taranis.
