# Miss Nance

**Miss Nance** is a female Funeral Bell sergeant in [[point-mongo]] under the command of [[princess-donut]].

## Floor 6 Events
- In [[156-book-5-chapter-11-the-recital]] (Chapter 11), Miss Nance flanks Donut as Carl hands out weapons. Donut lectures her on how Ursine ears look like wet slippers.
- In [[157-book-5-chapter-12-double-billed]] (Chapter 12), Miss Nance looks down at the collapsed Mongo with Donut.
- In [[158-book-5-chapter-13-bravvo-shot-down]] (Chapter 13), Donut leaves Mordecai and Miss Nance in charge of Point Mongo's defenses while she and Carl go to meet Tsarina Signet. Donut orders Miss Nance to protect Mordecai and to try not to kill Kiwi if she attacks.
