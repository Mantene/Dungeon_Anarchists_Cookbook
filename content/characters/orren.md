# Orren

**Orren** is an independent Syndicate Liaison who is a member of the Valtay race. He takes the appearance of a hooded, grim reaper-like figure, but under the hood, he is a parasitic gondii worm floating in a swirling bowl of dark liquid.

## Actions & Role
- **Chapter 129 (Book 4)**: Orren interrogates [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] in a back office of the Desperado Club regarding the death of Admin Loita. He tries to pressure Carl into naming his outside informant in exchange for a Legendary Box and safe passage, which Carl refuses. Orren punitive-bans third-party crawler teleports on Floor 4.
- **Chapter 146 (Book 5)**: After Floor 5, Orren holds Carl in his office to deal with the Gate of the Feral Gods. He attempts to force Carl to pick up the artifact while under administrative lock (which would delete it), but Carl demands a lawyer, halting the attempt. Orren eventually agrees to the compromise negotiated by Carl's attorney [[quasar]]: Carl surrenders the winding box in exchange for four mercenaries, with the promise that he gets the Gate back on Floor 9.
