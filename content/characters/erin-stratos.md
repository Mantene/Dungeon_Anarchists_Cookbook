# Erin Stratos

**Erin Stratos** is a human crawler introduced in Book 8.

## Biography and Role

In [[414-book-8-chapter-15-the-lollipop]], Erin Stratos is introduced as a member of the racing team **"Chris and the Erins,"** alongside [[chris-2|Chris]] and [[erin-gregory]]. The team drives a slow semitruck with a trailer in the races on the tenth floor. 

During a gathering of crawlers at the Lollipop bar, she meets [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]].

## Attributes & Class
- **Level**: 66
- **Class**: **Moodright** (a type of magic user or mage)
- **Appearance**: Covered in glowing tattoos.
