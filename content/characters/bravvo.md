# Bravvo

**Bravvo** is a Level 50 Yenk bounty hunter who was active on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Profile & Traits
- **Race**: Yenk (humanoid alien with ridged foreheads and bony exoskeletons, male bounty hunter/mercenary).
- **Behavior**: Yenk males are solitary fighters who cannot cooperate with other males of their kind without fighting.

## Floor 6 Events
- **Shot Down**: In [[158-book-5-chapter-13-bravvo-shot-down]] (Chapter 13), Bravvo is hit by an anti-air missile fired by a guard at [[point-mongo]] while scouting the town with a levitation spell. He is knocked out of the sky and ends up impaled in a tree across the river.
- **Captured by Elites**: [[tsarina-signet]] and the were-castors bind him to their caterpillar mount, [[nadine]]. Signet offers his kill to [[carl]].
- **Death**: In [[159-book-5-chapter-14-were-castors]] (Chapter 14), Carl heals Bravvo just to mark him with the *Ring of Divine Suffering* before executing him to gain a permanent Dexterity boost.
