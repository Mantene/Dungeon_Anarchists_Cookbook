# Rapture

**Rapture** is a lady mantaur NPC racer on the tenth floor of the dungeon.

## Biography and Role

Rapture is a Level 95 Lady Mantaur Dominator and a member of **Team Lady Dominators and the Gimp**, alongside [[genesis]] and the half-mantaur [[corcunda]]. She has freckles, curly red hair, wears a white cowboy hat, and chews tobacco. She worships the deity Enyo and is equipped with a wrist-mounted harpoon launcher.

In [[412-book-8-chapter-13-quest-reunite-corky]], Rapture uses her wrist harpoon to snatch a beer can from the ground, which she then sniffs and discards. She and Genesis mock [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] before walking away. In [[418-book-8-chapter-19-penelope-the-pig]], Rapture is targeted by Carl and Donut's ambush plan at the start of Heat Two, as she is the driver of their Pontiac GTO convertible.
