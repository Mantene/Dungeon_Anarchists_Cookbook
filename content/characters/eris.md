# Eris

**Eris** is the Level 250 **Goddess of Chaos**. She is the half-sister of Taranis and a key contender for the Ascendent Throne.

## Profile & Traits
- **Appearance**: A tall, elf-like deity with a long neck, serpentine features (indicating naga lineage), long blonde hair, and Valkyrie armor beneath a long black-and-white striped coat. Her symbol features two back-to-back "C" shapes pierced by a flaming lance. Her lips are tattooed with a spiral pattern.
- **Personality & Lore**: Described by the system as the Ascendency's "worst, most unapologetic gossip" and akin to a wealthy "wine aunt." As Goddess of Chaos, she is all-knowing and rarely lies, preferring the devastating truth.
- **Mechanics**: Her presence inflicts a random effect. On the ninth floor, she is invulnerable. She was piloted by the influencer Nami until Nami's soul armor was stripped, ejecting her.

## Faction Wars Actions
- **Intervention**: In [[384-book-7-chapter-76-the-oak-fell]], Eris appears before Carl while the world is frozen. Her chaos effect makes a permanent Level-15 *Pied Piper* skill for Carl (saving him from Meatus's castration curse).
- **Warnings**: She tells Carl that the War Mages traded the [[the-gate-of-the-feral-gods]] to [[paca]] at the Midnight Market, that [[britney-proskurina]] is possessed by an axe, and offers to help him kill Taranis, Emberus, and Hellik.
- **Epilogue**: In the Epilogue, Eris docks at [[zander]]'s waystation Squirties in orbit and attacks him after he asks if she is wearing a costume.
