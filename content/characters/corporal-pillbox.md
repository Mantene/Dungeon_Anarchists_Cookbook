# Corporal Pillbox

**Corporal Pillbox** is a gnoll mercenary who participated in Faction Wars on the ninth floor.

## Profile & Faction Wars
- Pillbox was originally employed by Empress D'Nadia's faction but survived the conflict.
- After the Princess Posse defeated the other armies, Pillbox and other surviving enemy mercenaries helped with the rapid cleanup and repair of Larracos.
- Before Larracos was transferred to the 12th floor, Pillbox nodded and wept as the former crawlers and outworlders showered Carl and Donut with flowers and streamers.
- He was subsequently transferred out of the dungeon.
