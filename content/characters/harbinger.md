# Harbinger

**Race:** Caprid (goat-like bipedal alien)  
**Role:** Liaison for the Plenty system; production-side representative  
**Alignment:** Hostile to Carl, Donut, and OIPAN

A caprid liaison who previously attempted to have Carl killed and consistently works to obstruct Carl's communication with OIPAN-affiliated contacts.

---

## Background

Harbinger serves as an official liaison between the dungeon production and the Plenty system — the same system OIPAN operates out of. This creates an inherent conflict: he is nominally a neutral production representative but functionally antagonistic to anything that smells like OIPAN influence on the crawl.

The quasar.md entry confirms Quasar "despises this liaison and has filed numerous complaints" — their mutual antagonism is well-established.

---

## Key Actions

**Attempt on Carl's life:** At some point before Book 6, Harbinger previously tried to have Carl killed. The exact method and timing are not described in detail in the source summaries.

**Book 6 — Shadow Boxer interruption:** Harbinger ended the _Shadow Boxer_ interview (hosted by Rosetta Thagra, OIPAN-funded) early, citing that Carl and Rosetta were "talking in code." He was correct — they were exchanging hidden messages about Miami and the toraline root's purpose. He then **banned Rosetta from future crawler interviews**.

Zev was furious at Harbinger's overreach. He had the authority but was using it as a harassment tool.
