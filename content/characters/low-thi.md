# Low Thi

Low Thi is a human crawler who progresses to Floor 5.

## Class & Stats
- **Class**: **D-Bag Geek**.
- **Dungeon Status**: Low Thi is Level 25 and operates in the Subterranean Quadrant of Bubble #543.
- In [[116-book-4-chapter-6-the-collateral-secret]], Low Thi leads a group of Subterranean crawlers into the vault beneath City Hall. He kills the gnome hostage [[wynne]] with a spear, hoping to loot a map from his corpse. Instead, this fails Carl's quest and earns Low Thi the **"Cockblock"** achievement.
