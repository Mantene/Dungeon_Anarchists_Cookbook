# Volteeg

**Volteeg** was a former crawler, a Weeper-class tank commander for the [[the-operatic-collective|Operatic Collective]], and the author of the **seventh edition** of the [[the-anarchist-cookbook|Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook]].

## Biography & Role in the Dungeon

### Early Dungeon Life
Volteeg entered the dungeon as a pet starling (*Sturnus*) belonging to **Mistress Henspar**, who loved and cared for him. On the third floor, Volteeg was fed a pet biscuit and chose to transform into a gargoyle to gain intelligence and speech. He deeply regretted this choice later, as it transformed his body permanently and took away his ability to sing. 

Mistress Henspar died on the seventh floor, leaving Volteeg alone and filled with rage. Around this time, he received the *Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook*. Out of shame for his perceived inaction and inability to protect his owner, he only left a single entry in the Cookbook at the very end of the ninth floor.

### Exit and Mercenary Work
At the end of the ninth floor, Volteeg took a deal on the tenth floor, exiting the crawl to serve as an unprotected NPC guard at the Rampart Guild for 26 seasons. He managed to survive and later took a job with the Outreach Guild as a security guard for the Operatic Bankers Federation compliance barge.

Because of his pet status at entry, a Pacifist Network mole deep in the Syndicate bureaucracy altered his designation code so he was classified as an indentured refugee rather than a former crawler. This prevented him from being sent to the Bone Clan like other former crawlers when he was redeployed to Earth for the Faction War Games in Book 7. He served as Weeper Commander Volteeg for the Operatic Collective.

### Sacrifice & Demise
While on the surface of Earth during the Faction War Games, Volteeg's Cookbook authorship was discovered by [[rosetta]], who notified [[porthus]]. Porthus encouraged Volteeg that "it's not too late to do something about it." 

During the Bloc's advance following the death of [[king-rust]], Volteeg chose to act. He manually bypassed the safety mechanism of his Weeper tank, filling the interior with volatile, humming energy balls. Listening to Mozart's classical piano music, he steered his tank directly into the center of the Bloc army next to Warlord [[warlord-hortense|Hortense]]'s command caravan and detonated it. 

The massive blast killed Volteeg and over 10,000 fellow soldiers, instantly killing Warlord Hortense and eliminating the Operatic Collective from Faction Wars, providing the crawlers with a critical reprieve.
