# Jurgen

**Jurgen** is a Level 65 Harii human crawler active in Faction Wars on Floor 9.

## Profile & Traits
- **Class**: Harii (a barbarian-type class).
- **Appearance**: A large, muscular human with a shock of blonde hair and steel-bending arms.
- **Deity**: Worshipper of Donar.
- **Interactions**: He is highly grateful to Carl for saving his and his companion Heidi's lives through Carl's actions at the end of Floor 8. Upon meeting Carl in Larracos, he pulled Carl into a tight hug and kissed him on the cheek in thanks. He and his companions are recruited into the army.
