# Louis Santiago II

Louis Santiago is a human crawler who has the **Pest Exterminator** class. He is Level 22 and progresses through the dungeon alongside his partner [[firas-m]].

## History & Actions
- **Floor 5 (Air Quadrant)**: In [[113-book-4-chapter-3-spit-and-swallow]], Louis is found drunk at the Spit and Swallow in Hump Town paying changeling prostitutes to mimic famous human figures. Under threat of expulsion, he agrees to cooperate with Carl's group.
- **Biplane House**: Louis and Firas help prepare, navigate, and repair the biplane-rigged flying house (named the *Twister*).
- **Juice Box**: Louis is responsible for telling [[juice-box]] that she is an NPC with past lives. The two fall in love and decide to get married.
- **Floor 6 Descent**: At the end of Floor 5, Louis descends through the stairwell to Floor 6 to recover the *Twister* after it is sent through the portal with the changeling refugees, and joins [[katia-grim]]'s new independent crawler group.
