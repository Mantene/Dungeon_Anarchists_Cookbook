# Reid Gunn

**Reid Gunn** was a human mage crawler who fought for the Princess Posse during the Ninth Floor Faction Wars.

## Profile & Traits
- **Affiliation**: Princess Posse (shield-casting unit).
- **Status**: Deceased (puppeted by a brain worm).

## History
In Book 7, Chapter 29, Reid Gunn was infected and killed by a Valtay brain worm (Gondii). The worm puppeted his corpse, using his active status dot on the battle map to bypass suspicion while he slaughtered other shield-casting mages in a defensive bunker. [[carl]] noticed the discrepancy on the casualty list and ordered [[rosetta]] to eliminate the puppeted corpse.
