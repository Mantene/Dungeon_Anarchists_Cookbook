# Kristen Cronin

**Kristen Cronin** is a Level 56 Crisper crawler who fights for the Princess Posse during the Ninth Floor Faction Wars.

## Profile & Traits
- **Class & Level**: Level 56 Crisper.
- **Weaponry**: Utilizes a heavy, Tserendolgor-style flamethrower.
- **Personality**: Resilient but emotionally strained by the horrors of the war.

## History
In Book 7, Chapter 21, Kristen is stationed in the southern trenches during the Reavers' biological attack. When several soldiers are infected with the Gurgles and turn into violent, blood-spewing hostiles, Kristen is forced to incinerate them with her flamethrower. Following the incident, she is visibly shaken, crying and criticizing the system for allowing biological exploits during the magic-free Ceasefire/Ramp-Up period.
