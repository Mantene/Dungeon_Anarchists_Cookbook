# Hapanzi

**Hapanzi** is a spider god and the husband of [[shi-maria]].

## Profile & Traits
- **Race**: Deity / Spider. He is a spider god who can assume both arachnid and human forms.
- **Role**: He appears in Shi Maria's worst memory/nightmare.
- **Personality**: Calculating and vengeful, yet cooperative with [[carl]] to keep Shi Maria imprisoned.

## Biography
During Carl's Glory Bound drug treatment (Book 7, Chapter 17), Carl enters Shi Maria's memories. Within the dream, Shi Maria encounters Hapanzi, flies into a rage, kills, and devours him. However, Hapanzi uses the opportunity to help Carl trap Shi Maria in the memory. He tells Carl: "I have her now, son. She is good and trapped. You can always set her free, if you must. But do not unless all is lost." Thanks to Hapanzi's assistance, Shi Maria is sealed like an egg inside Carl's chest tattoo, giving Carl access to her power but keeping her contained.
