# Herot

**Herot** is a former crawler of the skink race and the author of the sixteenth edition of the [[dungeon-anarchists-cookbook|Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook]]. 

## Biography & Role in the Dungeon

During his time as a crawler, Herot was a prominent figure who, along with another former crawler named [[menerva]], discovered exploits in the dungeon system regarding how the AI recycles NPCs and mobs. He realized that the recycling process was flawed, allowing many mobs and NPCs to retain memories of their previous seasons and develop self-awareness.

In Book 8, Herot is revealed to be located on the other side of the portal inside the **[[pineapple-cabaret|Pineapple Cabaret]]**, a secret dimension created to hide and protect awakened NPCs and mobs from being destroyed by the System or the Ascendency battles. Although trapped there, he has limited powers to interact with the dungeon through a shop interface.

He coordinates with [[carl]]'s group to help crawlers and NPCs escape the dungeon. Herot sets up a standing purchase order for all cleaner bots (such as [[suckira]]) put up for sale in the shop terminal. Crawlers can be smuggled inside these bots using XL pet carriers to bypass the dungeon's boundary and escape into the Cabaret, where Herot retrieves them. He also works with [[pontiff]] to test using the casino's "Nothing" portal as a secondary escape route to pull crawlers from stasis.
