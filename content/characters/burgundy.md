# Burgundy

Burgundy is a Level-30 human NPC who works as an assistant in the administrative offices of Floor 3. She is a victim of [[miss-quill]]'s krasue curse.

## Profile
Burgundy is a young, dark-haired human woman with one pale blue eye and one brown eye, resembling a Siberian husky. Carl first encounters her on the stairs of the municipal building. 

At night, Burgundy is cursed to become a [[krasue]] (a floating, disembodied head trailing organs). She was originally a prostitute who was lured to the town by the [[201st-security-group|201st Security Group]] under the false promise of a job at the Desperado Club that would allow her to descend to the safety of the deeper floors. Instead, Miss Quill and Remex cursed her. 

In Chapter 22 ([[071-book-2-chapter-22-burgundy-revelation]]), after Carl detonates the soul crystal and collapses the building, Burgundy is found pinned under the debris, her lower body severely charred. She explains the details of the krasue curse, Miss Quill's spell, and the presence of [[remex]] across the street. She begs to be killed before the sunrise turns her back into a monster, and Donut grants her mercy by hitting her with a *Magic Missile* before the roof collapses.
