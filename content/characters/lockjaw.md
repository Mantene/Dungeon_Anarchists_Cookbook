# Lockjaw

**Lockjaw** is a gnoll mercenary who fights for the Madness faction during the Ninth Floor Faction Wars.

## Profile & Traits
- **Race**: Gnoll.
- **Affiliation**: Team Madness (mercenary squad).
- **Relationships**: Close friend of [[corporal-pillbox]] and [[mange]].

## History
In Book 7, Chapter 27, following the defeat of Empress [[princess-dnadia]] at the hands of [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]], Lockjaw and his fellow gnoll unit enter the Prism throne room. While eight of his comrades are automatically conscripted into the Princess Posse, Lockjaw remains aligned with the Madness. On Corporal Pillbox’s recommendation, Lockjaw disarms and surrenders, and Carl orders him to stay behind so they can talk.
