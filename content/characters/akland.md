# Akland

**Akland** is a deceased Level 51 Moon Elf hunter belonging to the faction [[the-dream]], who was active on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Profile & Traits
- **Background**: In the real world, Akland is not a professional soldier or hunter, but an **accountant** who works with soy crop shipping manifests. He won a spot in the hunting army after doing well at a "fantasy hunter camp" on a moon. He took the opportunity to come to Earth because the showrunners offered him a large amount of money.
- **Gear**: He wore a magical bracelet that gave +10 Constitution and +5 Dexterity, and carried a quiver of *Enthusiastic Double Gonorrhea* arrows.

## Floor 6 Events
- **Artillery Attack**: In [[166-book-5-chapter-21-crawlcon-invitation]] (Chapter 21) and [[167-book-5-chapter-22-samantha-bottle-rocket]] (Chapter 22), Akland and his partner set up an *Enchanted Venomous Elven Rock Chucker* (trebuchet) in the woods to lob poison boulders at [[point-mongo]].
- **Capture**: Carl locates them using a *Brain Juice* enhanced *Ping* spell and throws [[samantha]] (strapped to missiles) at their position. Akland's partner is killed, and Akland is knocked unconscious. Samantha bites his ankle, and when Carl teleports her back, she drags Akland with her.
- **Interrogation & Death**: In [[167-book-5-chapter-22-samantha-bottle-rocket]] (Chapter 22) and [[168-book-5-chapter-23-artillery-specialist]] (Chapter 23), Akland is healed and handcuffed. He reveals that:
  - The Dream only cares about killing [[louis-santiago-2]] because he insulted the mother of their leader, [[epitome-tagg]].
  - Hunters buy "hunting licenses" to hunt top crawlers anywhere.
  Carl marks Akland with the *Ring of Divine Suffering* and beats/kills him to obtain the stat bonuses (+1 Con, +2 stats from the ring leveling up).

## Graphify Deductions
- **The Reality of the Hunter Force**: Akland's background exposes the true nature of the hunter forces—many are not battle-hardened mercenaries, but ordinary citizens (like accountants) who treat the crawl as a high-paying vacation or corporate team-building exercise ("fantasy hunter camp"). This highlights the disconnect between the tourists' view of the crawl as a game and the crawlers' brutal reality of survival.
