# Herman the Fleet

**Herman the Fleet** is an Ogre NPC mentioned on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Profile & Traits
- **Background**: Herman is one of three brothers (along with [[areson-the-wise]] and [[apollon-the-mighty]]) born from the splitting of the three-headed nodling [[di-we]], who died protecting [[tsarina-signet]].
- **Status**: While Areson remained in the Hunting Grounds and Apollon joined the circus in the Over City, Herman traveled down to Larracos. His current fate is unknown.
