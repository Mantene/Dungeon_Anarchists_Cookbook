# Conrad E

Conrad E is a Level-12 Ranger crawler with a Russian accent.

## Profile
During the Floor 3 event quest escape, Mordecai warns the group to unequip and store all magical items to prevent them from turning against them. Conrad unequips his bow but forgets about his magical arrows. During the second precursor burst of wild magic, Conrad's arrows explode in his quiver, killing him instantly.
