# Tempest

**Tempest** is [[quasar]]'s niece and the first member of his family to appear in the series.

## Appearance & Personality
- **Species**: Presumed Nullian (same race as Quasar), though not explicitly described.
- **Occupation**: Student. She has a teacher named "Miss Guss" and is writing a school report about the events of the crawl.
- **Personality**: Smart, foul-mouthed, and unintimidated by her uncle's abrasive demeanor. She gives as good as she gets in their banter, threatening to tell her father that Quasar claimed to be her "real dad" to get him to cooperate.

## Key Scenes
- **[[297-book-7-recap-tempests-floor-8-school-report]]**: Tempest's only appearance so far. She visits Quasar to record a school report about Floor 8. While fixing Quasar's non-functional sink ("it's more an art piece"), she extracts a profanity-laced recap of Floor 8 events. The scene reveals significant character depth for Quasar — his divorce from "Aunt Nova," his genuine fear about the dangers of representing Carl, and his affection for Tempest despite the constant insults.

## Relationships
- **[[quasar]]** — Uncle. Their relationship is antagonistic on the surface but clearly affectionate underneath. He tries to teach her "life lessons" through menial tasks.
- **Father** — Quasar's brother (or brother-in-law). Mentioned but not named. Quasar threatens to "bang your mom" in their banter.
- **Aunt Nova** — Quasar's ex-wife. Referenced when Tempest says "It's no wonder Aunt Nova left you."

## Narrative Significance
Tempest serves as the framing device for Book 7's Floor 8 recap, but her appearance also grounds Quasar as a character with a personal life beyond the crawl. Her closing question — "Are you in danger?" — and his honest answer mark one of the series' most emotionally vulnerable moments for a character who typically hides behind vulgarity and humor.
