# Bomo

**Bomo** is a Cretin NPC employee/bouncer at the [[desperado-club]].

## Biography
Bomo is first encountered assisting and guarding crawlers in the Desperado Club. In Chapter 94, Bomo purchases a physical protection shield spell ("smack protect") to complement the magical protection spell cast by [[the-sledge]]. Together, the two spells make crawlers in the club practically invulnerable to both physical and magical attacks, demonstrating the Cretins' protective nature toward their clientele.

## Graphify Deductions
- **Cretin Loyalty**: Bomo's actions show that Cretin employees in the Desperado Club are highly protective of crawlers, spending their own gold to ensure their safety within the club's confines.
