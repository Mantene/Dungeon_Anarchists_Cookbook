# Ren

**Ren** was a human crawler active on Floor 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth").

## Biography and Role

Ren was a combat specialist who utilized a heavy flamethrower to torch mobs. Her regular party was originally seven or eight people, but after a devastating second phase on Floor 8, only three of them survived: Ren, her stone-skin main tank [[chuluuna]], and Chuluuna's wife [[khulan]]. Ren owned a powerful Level 70 *Belly Acher* (Tummy Acher) pet named Garret.

Ren maintained a deep rivalry with [[quan-ch]], who was previously a card game champion. In Chapter 5, Ren messaged Carl via chat, providing crucial tactical intelligence about the capabilities and history of Team Sister Ines.

### Ambush and Self-Sacrifice
In Chapter 57, Ren's tank Chuluuna was ambushed and killed by Quan Ch. Following the fight, the exit doorway was heavily damaged, starting a 5-minute collapse timer. Because they could not safely transfer a key to Ren's squad due to the risk of random card loss, Ren chose to sacrifice herself alongside the grieving Khulan in Chapter 59. 

Ren detonated a directional self-destruct bracer, leaving all her gear, cards, and pet carrier (with [[garret|Garret the Tummy Acher]]) to Carl.
