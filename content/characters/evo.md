# Evo

Evo is a Forsoothed (commonly called a Soother) NPC who works as the director of the talk show *Danger Zone*.

## Profile
Evo is a tall, hairless, glowing white humanoid with large black eyes and a bony mohawk. He meets Carl and Donut in person before the show starts to brief them on the other panelists. He notes that Forsoothed oddsmakers visited Earth in the past to sample human stock for crawl predictions, which explains Earth's alien abduction folklore. Evo refers to the Grays (or Nulls) as perverts who conducted anal probing.
