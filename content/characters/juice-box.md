# Juice Box

**True nature:** Expert changeling NPC, now fully self-aware  
**Race:** Changeling (can shapeshift into any person or creature she has observed)  
**Level:** Unknown (very dangerous — she's been killing Faction Wars representatives)  
**Affiliation:** NPC Home Team (co-warlord); romantically involved with Louis Santiago  
**Current location:** Larracos, Floor 9, coordinating the NPC army

---

## Background

Juice Box was an expert changeling in Larracos — effectively a prostitute who used her shapeshifting to become whoever clients wanted. She was a non-player character, not a crawler. During the events of Book 4, she became **fully self-aware** — aware of her own nature as an NPC, the nature of the dungeon, and what was happening to Earth.

She chose to stay in the dungeon after Floor 5 ended, going to Floor 6 to help rally the NPCs.

---

## Books 4–6

**Book 4:** She and Carl had a connection — he talked to her, treated her as a person, and she became aware. She helped strategically during Floor 4 events. Carl sent her to Floor 9 ahead of time to rally the NPCs.

**Book 5:** Gone to Floor 9 as planned. Making contact with the elf castle NPCs, Signet's changelings, and others.

**Book 6:** Juice Box has become operationally terrifying. She infiltrated every Faction Wars preproduction meeting, killed the Madness faction's representative, impersonated Epitome Noflex (the Dream elf's mother — specifically to enrage Epitome Tagg), and detonated a hobgoblin pus grenade in a meeting room. She can now organize the NPCs across the Floor 9 battlefield.

She demanded the Faction Wars teams either give NPCs their own team with full protections or she would keep attacking. The audience voted 52% in favor. **Juice Box and Ferdinand are now co-warlords of the NPC Home Team** ("Home Team") in Faction Wars with Larracos as their base.

She told Louis: *"I send my love."*

## Book 7

### Floor 9 — Co-Warlord, Fleshmancer, Jump into the Nothing

Juice Box served as Co-Warlord of Team Retribution throughout Faction Wars. She had spies (changeling infiltrators) everywhere — in all factions' bases. The NPCs' real goal: touch as many gods as possible.

**The fleshmancer surgery.** Juice Box revealed she could take a male fleshmancer form. She performed the organ transplant to save Louis — installing naiad organs from Princess Lunette, activating them to bond with Louis's body.

**Negotiation with Justice Light.** When Justice Light discovered Sick-Ass Worgo was her spy, he proposed a deal: he would show her how to touch a god safely; she would allow his trap to proceed. They agreed. This partnership changed everything.

**Level display.** In human form: Level 35. In blank form: Level 120. Her real level is unknown (400+ past lives).

**Served Louis divorce papers** just before jumping into the Nothing per Justice Light's plan. She is to be "reborn as the ultimate weapon to end this madness" in the Nothing. Justice Light believed this was possible. What this means in practice is unknown.

**Current status:** In the Nothing. The Nothing is broken. Her fate is uncertain.
