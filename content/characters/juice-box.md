# Juice Box

Juice Box is a changeling NPC prostitute who originally resides at the Toe tavern in Hump Town. She is the sister of [[henrik]], one of the changeling principals who died trying to save their people.

## Characteristics & Role
Juice Box initially appears as a human woman. In [[111-book-4-chapter-1-welcome-to-the-bubbles]], she is pouting in the corner of the Toe after Carl, Donut, Katia, and Mordecai reject her services.

## Floor 5 (Air Quadrant)
During Floor 5, Juice Box is made aware of her NPC status and past lives by [[louis-santiago-2]]. She deals with the grief of her brother's death and agrees to help Carl with his plan. She and Louis fall in love and plan to get married. 

In [[144-book-4-chapter-34-a-clash-of-demons]], she helps protect the Gate of the Feral Gods winding box from [[slit]] and [[carl]]'s fight. At the end of the floor, she carries a facsimile of the gate watch and enters the Floor 9 city of Larracos in skyfowl form to hide and spread the truth to other NPCs about the reality of their existence, preparing them for future floors.
