# Bobby D.J.

Bobby D.J. is a human crawler who progresses to Floor 5.

## Class & Stats
- **Class**: **Spy**. A rogue class.
- **Dungeon Status**: Bobby is Level 24 and operates in the Subterranean Quadrant of Bubble #543.
- In [[117-book-4-chapter-7-total-utter-failure]], Bobby is met at the Desperado Club along with [[morris-sp]]. He has an eye twitch and trembling left hand, serving as his group's primary trap-disarmer in the trap-heavy Necropolis catacombs. Carl coordinates with them to have Katia transcribe the Necropolis map for their group.
