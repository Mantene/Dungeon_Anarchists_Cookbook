# Dante

**Dante** was a crocodilian thief crawler who survived his crawl and became the author of the **Third Edition** of the [[the-anarchist-cookbook|Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook]].

## Biography

- **Crawl History**: Dante was a crocodilian thief who reached the 11th floor in his season, where he took a deal to survive.
- **Indentured Servitude**: Following his exit, Dante worked as a Game Guide and a Guildhall Instructor in the Trap Master guild. He was close to his freedom, with only 14 seasons left on his contract.
- **Role in Faction Wars**: During his season's Faction Wars (corresponding to Floor 8, a cold mountain floor with gondolas), Dante and his skyfowl neighbor [[justice-light]] were temporarily reassigned as Ice Castle guard captains.
- **Execution**: Dante refused to follow orders to fight crawlers and was subsequently executed by administrative action.

## Cookbook Contribution
Dante was the author of the Third Edition of the *Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook*. He only wrote a single entry due to fear of being caught, a decision he deeply regretted after seeing subsequent authors pour themselves into the book.
