# Pater Coal

**Pater Coal** is an NPC who serves as the High Priest of the god [[emberus]] (the blind Sun and Ash deity) in Club Vanquisher on Floor 8.

## Biography and Role

Pater Coal is a Hobbledehoy High Cleric Supreme of Emberus (Level 101). He is described as an even-tempered, small, and fast-talking priest who manages the shrines of Emberus in Club Vanquisher. Unlike many other NPCs, he is in constant contact with the deity.

In Book 6, Chapter 18, Pater Coal performs a sacrament on [[carl]] (a feet massage and toe-sucking ritual) which allows Carl to communicate with Emberus without the other deities or the system eavesdropping. During this sacrament, Pater Coal warns Carl about the upcoming quests and the high stakes of his actions. He also facilitates the transaction for Carl to "buy" a special ring (disguised as his strength ring) that helps keep debuffs at bay.
