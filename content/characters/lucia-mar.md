# Lucia Mar

**Race:** Lajabless (half-woman, half-skeleton, goat-legged)  
**Class:** Black Inquisitor General (triple Cleric/Magic-User/Warrior hybrid)  
**Level:** 48+ (Book 4 leaderboard); higher by Book 5–6  
**Leaderboard:** #2 in Book 4 (500,000 bounty ×2); dropped off leaderboard top by Book 5  
**Pets:** Cici and Gustavo 3 (rottweilers)

---

## Description

Pre-teen or early-teen girl, South American (Spanish accent, yellow/blue soccer jersey initially). Transformed into a Lajabless: half the day she appears as a beautiful adult woman (magic doubled, strength halved); the other half as a skull-faced skeleton. Her right leg became a cloven-hoofed goat leg.

Two pet rottweilers: Cici and Gustavo 3.

---

## Books 1–2

Introduced on recap show as a street kid with rottweilers. Manufactured feud with Donut by TV producers (Donut insulted her dogs on air). Lucia issued death threats. Per Mordecai: she won't appear in person until Floor 6.

---

## Books 3–5 — Floor 6 (The Hunting Grounds)

Met Donut in person on Floor 6. Donut killed one of Lucia's dogs. Conflict escalated. Later, it became clear something unusual was happening with Lucia — Florin (the crocodilian crawler whose girlfriend Lucia had killed previously) became obsessed with discovering her "full story" and switched from wanting to kill her to being in a strange, uneasy alliance with her.

Lucia teleported away before the Butcher's Masquerade boss fight, escaping. She did not die on Floor 6. **Status: alive, location uncertain.**

A strange element: Lucia's behavior is inconsistent with her reputation. She may have been acting under a compulsion or playing a longer game.

---

## Book 6

Florin was in Ecuador for Floor 8, and reported occasional contact with Lucia Mar. She was not in his party and they were not directly allied. She **did not obtain a key** for Phase 2 (Florin noted she wasn't in his area and he couldn't confirm her status). No key = she either died or had to fight through Phase 3 as a keyless squad.

Her current status as of the end of Book 6 is uncertain.

---

## The Lucia Mar Mystery — Resolved

Florin killed his girlfriend and developed a complex relationship with Lucia Mar — hating her for the killing but suspecting she was being controlled or manipulated. **Books 7–8 explain why.**

Lucia has a **water goddess living inside her head**: **Chalchiuhtlicue** (nicknamed "Chachi") — along with "the children." Samantha warned Carl: *"She kills children despite claiming to protect them."* Chalchiuhtlicue sacrificed a village day care when the meatball appetizers had cilantro. The compulsion/control Florin sensed was real.

The Book 8 quest "Half a Prayer, Half a Song" involved bringing Chalchiuhtlicue and Lucia Mar together during the racing floor.

**Floor 11 — Parade of Horribles:** Lucia's group was in the **"Decision"** float group. When she stepped through the Floor 11 stairwell, a **~5-year-old girl** was seen holding her leg — nobody else saw it. Possibly one of Chalchiuhtlicue's children or a manifestation from inside Lucia's head.

**Floor 12:** Lucia Mar is confirmed on Floor 12 as one of the 19 remaining crawlers.
