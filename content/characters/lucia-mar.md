# Lucia Mar

Lucia Mar is a 13-year-old human crawler who is featured on the premiere episode of *Dungeon Crawler World: Earth*.

## Performance
Lucia is likely from South America and is shown wearing an oversized yellow soccer jersey with a thick blue stripe. She is accompanied by two Rottweilers, [[cici]] and [[gustavo-3]]. In the broadcast highlights, she is shown wearing silver armor over her soccer jersey, wielding a mace. One of her dogs wears a chainmail sweater and the other shoots lightning when it barks.
