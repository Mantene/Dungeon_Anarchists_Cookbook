# Gershwin Banks

**Gershwin Banks** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

The late CFO of Dictum Waystation Controls, LTD. His fanged rabbit 'Horribles mask' was found on the battlefield by Carl, confirming his presence among the 'Horribles' and his demise.
