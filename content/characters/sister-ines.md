## Background

Sister Ines entered the dungeon alongside Paz Lo and Anton Lopez, both of whom were prison guards at the women's facility where she was incarcerated. She had been convicted of poisoning all the nuns in her convent — a crime that earned her the nickname **"El Segador de La Habana"** (The Reaper of Havana).

In the dungeon, she has not exhibited overt evil behavior and has fought alongside allies effectively. She appears genuinely religious and horrified by the idea of worshipping false gods. Paz and Anton, after worshipping Ogun to protect themselves from her passive charm effect, repeatedly forgot about it — the charm made them forget why they'd worshipped Ogun in the first place.

---

## The Passive Racial Ability

> [!warning] Toxoplasma-like passive racial ability
> Cat Girls (at least Ines's subtype) can have a passive Toxoplasma-like compulsion ability. Those near her gradually feel the need to protect her, without consciously realizing it. The effect strengthens over time. Worshipping Ogun was supposed to protect Paz and Anton against charm effects — but they still couldn't retain the memory of why.
>
> Ines is aware of this ability. She has told her companions about it on multiple occasions, but they forget again afterward. She reportedly sometimes feels guilty about it, and sometimes doesn't. Carl and Donut were warned by the dying Paz.

## Book 6

### Floor 8 (The Ghosts of Earth)

Met Carl and Donut near the second Havana stairwell (both heading to the same Phase 1 location). Initially tense; Anton nearly started a fight. Their group joined together to clear the **Colón Cemetery** ghommid settlement.

**The Thorn Room:** In the Club Vanquisher temple at the center of the cemetery, seven Orisha shrines and a Sun of Reawakening gem forced a single choice. Ines urged repairing Ogun's shrine to avoid smiting Anton and Paz. Carl secretly repaired Yemaya's shrine instead (Katia's instruction). Ines froze Anton to try to force him to stop, but the situation escalated.

**Aftermath:** Ogun appeared and killed Anton directly. He removed his divine protection from Paz, leaving Paz with a permanent fatal debuff. As Paz lay dying and accepting death, Ines — unable to tolerate his departure — **stabbed him with a team flag**, turning him into a T'Ghee card against his will. Paz remained a totem card, "The God Predator," until the card was ripped at the end of Book 6 to free him.

**Phase 1:** Ines got entangled in the **Chowder War** between the Monk Seals and the Red Maníseros Land Crabs. She warned the Monk Seals about the crabs' sneak attack (which was supposed to be helped by "sideing with the seals"), but this turned the conflict into a prolonged war of attrition instead of a quick conclusion. She also attempted to flag the **Madre de Aguas** (Level 125 giant freshwater snake city boss) and charm it.

**Phase 2 / key acquisition:** Ines obtained a key, making her one of two keyholders in the Cuba zone.

**Phase 3:** Using her Poet Laureate charm abilities (spoken poetry as a charm spell), Ines had charmed the Madre de Aguas and amassed an army of crabs, seals, and other creatures. When Carl's xistera accidentally teleported everything within range back to his location (breaking her charm), the resulting chaos — combined with the demon eviction event — led to an enormous battle. Ines survived.
