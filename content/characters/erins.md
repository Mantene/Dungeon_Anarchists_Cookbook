# The Erins

**The Erins** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A group of crawlers, operating a "big rig," who are participating in the same apartment race as Carl and Donut. They are mentioned by Louis as being ahead of his group on the fifth floor.
