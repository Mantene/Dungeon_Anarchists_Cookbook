# Lucienne

**Lucienne** is an NPC mandagot racer on the tenth floor of the dungeon.

## Biography and Role

Lucienne is a Level 81 Fink Mandagot and a member of **Team Sparkles**, along with [[dwight]]. She is a Fairy-class creature resembling a mix between a rat and a chinchilla with bulging red eyes, and she speaks with a high-pitched French accent. She is a worshipper of [[diwata]] and is automatically hostile to [[carl]] due to Diwata's hatred of him.

In [[404-book-8-chapter-5-team-sparkles]], Lucienne rides on Dwight's head and insults [[princess-donut]] as they pass the crashed Pontiac minivan. In [[417-book-8-chapter-18-hail-and-heavy-weapons]], she supports Dwight in his insults against Masterplan before Heat Two.
