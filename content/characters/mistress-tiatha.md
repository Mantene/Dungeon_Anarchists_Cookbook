# Mistress Tiatha

**Mistress Tiatha** (also referred to by the Meadow Lark crawlers as **Miss T**) is a non-combatant NPC guide and tutorial guildmaster who later becomes the manager for the **Meadow Lark** group.

## Biography & Early Mentions
Mistress Tiatha was the guide for the Meadow Lark residents when they first entered the crawl. She explained basic mechanics, such as the bosses guarding stairwells, and was the contact to whom [[imani-c]] had to bring injured residents, which led to Imani's mercy-killings.

## Role as Manager (Floor 4)
In Book 3, Mistress Tiatha is selected as the manager for the Meadow Lark crawler group. However, [[elle-mcgibbons]] reports that she is highly negligent and ineffective in this role. Tiatha spends most of her time drinking alcohol, smoking blitz sticks, crying about a "space pony" she owned when she was a crawler, and stealing gold from Elle to fund her drinking. She rarely offers useful advice, though she did suggest the option of blowing up the soul crystal-powered generators.

## Graphify Deductions
- **Crawler PTSD & Manager Burnout**: Mistress Tiatha's self-destructive habits (drinking, drug use, mourning her space pony) suggest she is a former crawler suffering from severe trauma. Her neglect of her managerial duties reflects a system where even the designated guides are broken by the psychological weight of the crawl.
