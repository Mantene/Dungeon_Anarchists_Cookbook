# Tito

**Tito** is a human-looking NPC enforcer who works as the croupier for the Wheel of Fortune inside the Desperado Club.

## Biography and Role
Tito is a massive, bald enforcer type with only a single tooth, described by the system as the "Desperado Club Wheel of Fortune Croupier. Also, the guy who will stab you." 

Carl encounters Tito in Chapter 84 when he decides to spend a complimentary club chip on the Wheel of Fortune. Tito explains that betting the maximum value removes red spots (negative outcomes) from the wheel, leaving only two red spots: *Nothing!* and *Get stabbed in the stomach by the croupier*. Tito spins the wheel for Carl, who wins a Scroll of Upgrade. Tito is visibly disappointed that he does not get to use his knife on Carl and tries to coax him into another spin, which Carl declines.

## Attributes
- **Race**: Human (NPC)
- **Occupation**: Croupier and club enforcer
