# Ifechi

Ifechi was a human crawler who was killed at the end of Floor 4.

## Biography & Abilities
- **Background**: Ifechi was a rail-thin African woman who initially entered the dungeon as a timid, unarmed healer alongside a group of soldiers called Le Mouvement. After her team was wiped out on Floor 1, she partnered with [[florin]].
- **Abilities**: Ifechi wielded a staff that summoned and flung leeches that drained monster fluids. These engorged leeches could then be consumed by Florin to grant him powerful stat buffs. She was also a dedicated healer who trained defensive protection spells that could only be cast on others, leaving herself vulnerable.
- **Death**: In [[114-book-4-chapter-4-the-royal-chariot]], the recap show reveals she was killed by [[lucia-mar]]'s *Rubber* spell. When Florin shot Lucia, the damage was redirected to Ifechi's head, killing her instantly.
