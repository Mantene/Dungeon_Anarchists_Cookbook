# Lingyun

**Lingyun** is a crawler of the Monkey King race who participates on the eleventh floor. 

## Appearance & Abilities
- **Race**: Monkey King. He appears mostly human but with an elongated face and a hulked-out body.
- **Combat**: He is noted as a hero of the Faction Wars battlefields.

## Actions
- **Chapter 491**: Lingyun is grouped with [[elle-mcgibbons]], [[imani-c]], [[muchen]], and [[qianfan]] on the "Betrayal" parade float team (Float 3).
- **Chapter 493**: During the parade performance, Lingyun holds up a sign that reads: "You did this to us." He flips it to show: "Your betrayal will never be forgotten or forgiven."
