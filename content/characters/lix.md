# Lix

**Lix** is a young shade gnoll child who entered the juvenile quickdraw art contest at CrawlCon.

## Profile & Narrative Connections
- **Family**: She is the grandpup of the older shade gnoll security officer in orbit.
- **The Bribe**: In [[175-book-5-chapter-30-valtay-landing-vessel]] (Chapter 30), the security officer pats down [[carl]] and requests that Carl "look approvingly" on Lix's entry during the CrawlCon art contest judging. In return, the officer promises to overlook any items Carl steals from the production trailers.
