# Eunice

Eunice is a Level-30 Fathom Dwarf NPC who lives in the building adjacent to the *Apito Education Center* on Floor 3.

## Profile
Eunice is an enormously-pregnant dwarf mother of a large family (including [[ricky-joe]]). She becomes fed up with the city elves' constant chanting and recruitment, and when an elf reflects a rock back at one of her children, she storms out and pummels the cult recruiter [[salvatore]] on the head with a wooden rolling pin. 

When another elf (an Icer) hits her with a freezing pulse, she is frozen solid. Carl protects her from the town guards' execution attempt by initiating a fight. Eunice and her children escape the ensuing *Mic Drop* explosion when neighbor NPCs pull them inside. In Chapter 21, she goes into labor during the night.
