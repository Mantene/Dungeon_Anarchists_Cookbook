# Bigs

**Bigs** is a sluggalette NPC in the dungeon.

## Biography and Role

Bigs is a member of the sluggalos who helps [[samantha]] redecorate [[carl]]'s room. During [[398-book-8-recap-samanthas-floor-7-report]], she listens to Samantha's recap of the ninth floor. In [[406-book-8-chapter-7-moon-reaper]], she is present in the safe room when [[mordecai]] undergoes his Moon Reaper glitching, where he foretells that she will "drown in fire."
