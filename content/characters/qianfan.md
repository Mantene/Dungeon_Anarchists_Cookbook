# Qianfan

**Qianfan** is a Chinese crawler on the eleventh floor.

## Actions
- **Chapter 491**: Qianfan is grouped with [[elle-mcgibbons]], [[imani-c]], [[muchen]], and [[lingyun]] on the "Betrayal" parade float team (Float 3).
- **Chapter 493**: During the parade performance, Qianfan holds up a sign that reads: "We breathe, we dream, we love." He flips it to show: "We bleed, we suffer, we die."
