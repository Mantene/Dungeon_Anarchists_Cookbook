# Lea Bautista

Lea Bautista was a Level 8 crawler of a tree-like race (similar to an Ent). She was the sister of Grace and Nica Bautista.

## Death
Lea and her sisters were killed by Circus Lemurs on Floor 3. Carl recovers the Enchanted Anklet of the Fallen Oak (+1 Dex, +1 Con, +3 Double Tap) from her ankle.
