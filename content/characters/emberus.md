# Emberus

Emberus is the Level 250 **God of Sun and Ash**. He is a blind deity who plucked his own eyes out in grief after his favorite son (Geyrun) was murdered.

## Biography & Actions
- **Rampage**: In [[138-book-4-chapter-28-extractions-and-sun-shrines]], Emberus is summoned to the Air Quadrant when Carl opens the Gate of the Feral Gods. He goes on a blind rampage seeking his son's pet puppy [[orthrus]]. He pounds on [[tserendolgor]]'s bubble because she is a Dog Soldier crawler, mistaking her for Orthrus.
- **Worship & Rescue**: In [[141-book-4-chapter-31-a-deposit-on-quan]], [[carl]] opts to worship Emberus to invoke *Divine Intervention* and throws a celestial grenade to summon him to his exact location. The biplane *Nightmare II* crashes into Emberus, but the invulnerability saves Carl. Emberus finds the unconscious puppy Orthrus and happily returns to the Celestial Halls with him.
- **Quests**: As Carl's patron deity, Emberus issues two quests to him in [[138-book-4-chapter-28-extractions-and-sun-shrines]]:
  - **Find Out Who Killed My Son**: Investigate the murder of Geyrun (must complete before exiting Floor 18).
  - **Kill Hellik**: Slay his twin brother Hellik, God of Sun and Life, before Floor 12.
