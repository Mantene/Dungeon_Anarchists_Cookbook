# Emberus

**Title:** Fire God  
**Species:** Alien in Soul Armor (per dungeon cosmology)  
**Son:** Geyrun (deceased — someone in Carl's party knew how he died)  
**Death:** Killed by his brother Taranis in the Floor 11 arena (Book 8)

A fire deity deeply connected to Carl throughout the series via the Ring of Divine Suffering. His discovery that Samantha knew about his son Geyrun's death triggered the Book 7 crisis on Floor 9.

---

## Background

Emberus is a fire god in the dungeon's pantheon. He and [Taranis](taranis.md) are brothers. His son **Geyrun** was killed at some point before the series began, and knowledge of how Geyrun died has been a recurring mystery — and a threat.

Per the dungeon's cosmology (Book 3), gods are not truly divine: they are rich aliens wearing Soul Armor (biological suits). Emberus cannot be killed on floors below 12 under normal circumstances. The **Bolt of Ophiotaurus** bypasses this protection.

---

## Connection to Carl — The Ring of Divine Suffering

Carl looted the **Ring of Divine Suffering** from Frank Q's body (Book 3). Mordecai was furious — hunters on Floor 6 specifically target its holders. Carl wore it anyway.

The Ring marks enemies with Emberus's mark, generating a stacking stat bonus on each kill. After Emberus's death in Book 8, the sun tattoos the Ring left on the backs of Carl's hands became **raised, angry scars called the Mark of the Damned**.

**Carl became a Devotee of Emberus** in Book 6, gaining the **Self-Immolation spell**. Ogun told Carl: *"I hold Emberus in high esteem"* — suggesting Emberus was respected by peers in the pantheon.

---

## Book 7 — The Floor 9 Crisis

**Samantha** admitted out loud during Faction Wars that she had knowledge of **Geyrun's death** — Emberus's son. Emberus entered the realm immediately.

With only 65 minutes remaining:
- Emberus threatened to destroy the floor entirely
- He burned **Justice Light** to death
- Carl threw Samantha to Emberus via the xistera to stall him — Emberus held her while questioning her about Geyrun, then threw her into a volcano
- Carl retrieved Samantha just in time

The 65-minute countdown from Emberus's arrival drove the pace of the entire Book 7 endgame.

---

## Book 8 — Death

Carl's arena plan involved summoning Emberus inside Club Scolopendra (where his planetary-melt ability was contained) and using the **Bolt of Ophiotaurus** to anchor and blind him.

What happened:
1. Carl entered Scolopendra carrying Penny; Donut teleported in replacing Penny
2. Donut fired the Bolt at Emberus — anchoring and blinding him
3. **Taranis saw Emberus fire at Penny**
4. Taranis killed his own brother

Carl looted the **Memorial Crystal of Emberus** after the battle.
