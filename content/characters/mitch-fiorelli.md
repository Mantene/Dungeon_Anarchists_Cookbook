# Mitch Fiorelli

**Mitch Fiorelli** (real name or alias used by the entity) is an Adolescent Shadow Mimic who acts as an Enforcer and croupier for the Wheel of Fortune game in the [[desperado-club]] on the tenth floor during Book 8.

## Description & Abilities
- **Lore**: Shadow Mimics are the archenemies of changeling principals. They grow in intelligence and strength as they age without limit, and they are prone to becoming self-aware (ideation breaking). They are almost impossible to detect and love eating flesh.
- **Role**: He replaced the previous croupier Tito. He is a Level 120 Enforcer for a redacted organization, sent to disrupt the operations of the despot [[hamed]].
- **Mimic Form**: His human puppet disguise dangles from his upper half when he reveals his true form—an elongated serpentine creature with a massive mouth filled with razor-tipped fangs.

## Actions
- **Chapter 485**: Mitch charges the crawlers gold to use the "Nothing" spot on the Wheel of Fortune to send their friends into stasis. He allows [[chris-andrews-2]] and [[britney-proskurina]] to pass, but attempts to double the price, sparking a confrontation.
- **Chapter 486**: When [[princess-donut]] detects that the table has no magic and is a mimic, Mitch orders the other mimics in the room to attack. He is defeated by the crawlers after attempting to consume [[carl]], who freezes him using the Eye of the Bedlam Bride. Donut finishes him with a *Magic Missile*, and [[mongo]] tea-bags his corpse.
