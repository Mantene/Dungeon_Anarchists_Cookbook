# Marjory

**Marjory** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

Marjory is encountered in a 'what-if' scenario or alternate timeline within the dungeon, where Carl finds her chatting with Alicia on a couch. In this specific altered reality, she possesses a bag of Donut's special-ordered cat food, an unusual detail Carl notes for this timeline. In the broader context of the Dungeon, Marjory is known as a Manager, having previously overseen the fifth floor.
