# Sharp-Elbows

Sharp-Elbows is a Level 80 Necromancer ogre boss on Floor 8 (The Ghosts of Earth). She coordinates a double deckmaster battle with Tom inside the turkey barn. During the fight, she bear-hugs and impales Carl with her spiky body, piercing both of his eyes, before being decapitated by Donut's crab totem, Raul. Her staff, the Worm Fulcrum, is looted by the crawlers.
