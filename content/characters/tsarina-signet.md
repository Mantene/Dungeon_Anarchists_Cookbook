# Tsarina Signet

Tsarina Signet is a Level 60 Elite Summoner who is half-Naiad and half-High Elf. She is a former member of Grimaldi's Traveling Circus and the protagonist of the in-game serial drama *Vengeance of the Daughter*.

## Background
Signet is the daughter of High-Elf King Finian and a Naiad mother. Because of her mixed heritage, she was hunted by both factions. Ringleader [[ringmaster-grimaldi]] rescued her as a child, and they eventually fell in love. During the collapse, everyone in the circus except Signet was transformed by the poisonous cloud of Scolopendra. Grimaldi became a Pestiferous Vine, controlling the others as mold-infested puppets. Signet seeks to free them from this eternal cycle by destroying the circus and killing Grimaldi.

## Abilities & Combat Style
- **Blood Magic (Ink Marauder)**: Signet's body is covered in living, moving tattoos of monsters. When she performs a blood sacrifice, these tattoos leave her skin to form a 2-dimensional "paper" army of Blood and Ink Elementals (e.g., a three-headed ogre, dragons, hammerhead shark, octopus). The strength of the elementals depends on the quality of the blood used in the sacrifice.
- **Water Lily Spell**: An heirloom magic curse that puts a target into a deep, indefinite sleep. If Signet dies, the target dies as well. She uses this spell on Princess Donut to force Carl to help her.
- **Charm / Aura**: As a high-level elite, she has an extremely high Charisma, granting her a passive charm aura (Puppy Dog effect) that makes male crawlers mad with desire. Carl is forced to break his own pinky finger to break the charm.
- **Fairy Class**: As a fairy-class NPC, she inflicts 20% more damage against crawlers with a goblin pass.
