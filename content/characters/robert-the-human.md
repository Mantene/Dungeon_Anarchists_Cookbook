# Robert the Human

Robert the Human is Carl's estranged father. Wasting away from a terminal illness in a spliced-in summer trailer yard in Monroe County, Florida, he was murdered with a lethal morphine injection by his new wife, Tami-Lynn, shortly before the system collapse. In the dungeon, his memory-ghost/replicant forms the base of the Reminiscence Hydra's trailer and his motorcycle is looted by Carl.
