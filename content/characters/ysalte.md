# Ysalte (The Vinegar Bitch)

**Ysalte** (also known as the *Downward Spiral* or the *Vinegar Bitch*) was a Level 250 deity, specifically the Goddess of Hopelessness and Insanity, who appeared on Floor 8 of the World Dungeon.

## Lore & Background
- **History**: Originally the Goddess of Dirt, then of Tears, and finally of Hopelessness and Insanity. She is the sister and niece of Taranis, her former lover. She previously fled to Sheol before returning to the Halls of Ascendency.
- **Worshippers & Patronage**: She takes no new worshippers except the Semeru Dwarves of Larracos. She is a patron of bards and performers, sponsoring bards like [[sister-ines-quiteria|Sister Ines]].
- **Sponsorship**: Sponsored in the Ascendency by Pontifex Shine of the Nebular Balance.

## Role on Floor 8
- **Summoned**: Ysalte entered the realm in Chapter 65 following the death of Sister Ines, immediately marking [[louis-santiago-2|Louis]] for death. Her presence inflicted a curse that turned all potions handled by the crawlers into vinegar.
- **Control of Amayon**: She attempted to keep [[amayon]] enslaved as her retainer to support her claim to the Ascendent throne.
- **Death**: During the chaotic final card battle, Katia's totem [[paz-lo]] used a specialized explosive arrow to target her physical anchor, decapitating and permanently killing her in the realm.

## Attributes
- **Level**: 250
- **Status**: Deceased (killed by Paz Lo)
- **Patron**: Nebular Balance
