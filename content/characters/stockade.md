# Stockade

**Commander Stockade** is the warlord of the Democratic Sortion during the Ninth Floor Faction Wars.

## Profile & Traits
- **Race**: A giant, seven-foot-tall caterpillar-like alien creature.
- **Class & Level**: Level 76 Phase Sorcerer.
- **Personality**: Speaks with a distinctive Southern California "surfer dude" accent. He is hot-headed, emotional, and prone to dramatic outbursts, waving his caterpillar body like an inflatable tube man.
- **Affiliations**: Warlord of the Democratic Sortion.

## Biography
During the Ninth Floor Ceasefire, Stockade colluded with Architect Houston and external observers to execute a high-risk sabotage plan. They targeted [[carl]] during his Glory Bound drug rehabilitation treatment, intending to lock Carl in a vegetative state and unleash the demonic entity [[shi-maria]] to massacre the Princess Posse.

The attempt backfired catastrophically when the AI ruled the possession an "AI action" exempt from Ceasefire penalties. A possessed Carl teleported into the Sortion headquarters in Queen Imogen's private ballroom, slaughtering fifty of Stockade's soldiers, including Stockade's close companion, General [[luke]]. Following the slaughter, Stockade fiercely argued with Sortion Adjutant [[panford]], demanding an appeal and threatening him, which led to heavy sanctions against his faction.
