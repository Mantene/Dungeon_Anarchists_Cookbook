# The Hoarder

The Hoarder is a Level 7 Neighborhood Boss on Floor 1, located in a trash-filled cavern next to the fast-food safe room.

## Identity and Transformation
The Hoarder was originally a Spanish-speaking human woman (approximately 35 years old and very fat) who was transformed during the Earth's collapse. She was turned into a 15-foot-tall "garbage troll" with open sores. Her lair was filled with piles of household garbage and debris. She was disoriented, terrified, and wept, saying in Spanish: *"Please help me. I don't know what's happening. My stomach hurts. I don't know where I am. Please, I'm scared. ... I'm sorry if I was a bad person. I didn't want my daughter to get sick. I don't want to be in hell. Please. Please send me to Jesus."*

## Boss Mechanics
The Hoarder vomits Level 2 and Level 4 cockroaches (Scatterers). 

## Death
[[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] damaged her with dynamite before the fight formally began, taking her down to 1/4 health. Carl choked her with a metal dog leash until it broke, then pummeled her to death with his chain-wrapped fists, granting them a **Bronze Boss Box** and a bronze star next to their names.
