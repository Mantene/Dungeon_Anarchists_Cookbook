# Beatrice

Carl's ex-girlfriend and the original owner of [Princess Donut](princess-donut.md). She survived [the Transformation](../lore/the-transformation.md) on Earth's surface and was eventually removed from Earth by Odette's agents — nominally as a rescue, practically as a kidnapping.

## Background

Beatrice was Carl's girlfriend before the Transformation. She had left him (taking Donut, her Persian show cat, and moving in with Brad) sometime before the events of Book 1. On the night of the Transformation, Carl was outside Beatrice's building retrieving Donut from a tree — placing him outdoors when the buildings were crushed, which saved his life.

Carl initially assumes Beatrice is dead. She is not.

## Book-by-Book Progression

### Book 1
Referenced only. Carl notes Donut was Beatrice's cat. He does not know her fate.

### Book 2
A framed Instagram photo of Bea and her ex-boyfriend Brad (from a "Bahamas" trip) appears in Donut's fan box. Donut loves it; Carl pointedly does not care.

### Book 4
**Beatrice is alive.** The Book 4 epilogue reveals she survived in a Seattle camp called **New Queen Anne** with Brad and Ferdinand ("Gravy Boat," the neighborhood orange tom cat Carl knew). Alien bounty hunters from the **Walk-On List** were actively targeting Ferdinand as an A-tier production asset.

Odette's agent **Lexis** arrived to "rescue" Beatrice. Brad was shot by Lexis. Odette instructed Lexis to "let him expire." Brad's fate after this point is not confirmed.

> "Beatrice is alive. She's in a survivor camp in Seattle called 'New Queen Anne' with her Bahamas companion Brad."
> — [Book 4: The Gate of the Feral Gods](../sources/book-4-gate-of-the-feral-gods.md)

### Book 5
Beatrice appeared on **Odette's show** — drugged and disoriented, at an undisclosed off-world location. She is now a Syndicate citizen; Lexis "saved her from bounty hunters." The framing is Odette's.

A darker reveal: The showrunners had been planning to bring Beatrice into the dungeon as **Queen Imogen** (the Floor 6 country boss). The plan fell through — a different person (Ifechi, Florin's dead girlfriend) became Imogen instead.

### Book 6
Donut reveals she had Bea's Facebook notifications on her phone and had intercepted a message from **Tami-Lynn** (Carl's stepmother, who poisoned Carl's father). The message concerned **Asher** — Carl's half-brother that he didn't know existed. Donut got a co-worker named Sally fired through deception to keep this from Carl, trying to prevent him from going to Iowa.

This revelation recontextualizes Donut's behavior in earlier books and shows Beatrice as an indirect source of significant information about Carl's family that was deliberately withheld.
