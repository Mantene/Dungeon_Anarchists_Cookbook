# Beatrice

Beatrice (often called "Bea" or "Miss Beatrice") is [[carl]]'s ex-girlfriend and [[princess-donut]]'s former owner.

## History
Before the collapse, Beatrice went on a New Year's trip to the Bahamas with her new partner, [[brad]]. When Carl saw an Instagram picture of her sitting on Brad's lap, he broke up with her. She asked her parents to pick up Donut, but because of a massive winter storm blocking the Washington mountain passes, Carl kept Donut. 

Donut remains extremely attached to Beatrice, believing she is still alive and that they will reunite. Carl and [[mordecai]] both initially suspected Beatrice did not survive the collapse.

## Surface Survival & Capture (Book 4 Epilogue)
In [[145-book-4-epilogue-epilogue]], it is revealed that Beatrice survived the collapse on the surface of Earth in Seattle. She lives in the New Queen Anne settlement, though she is mentally unstable and has cuts all over her face and arms from repeatedly holding a stray orange cat she calls "Ferdinand" (whose real name is [[gravy-boat]]). 

When walk-on bounty hunters [[sadir]] and [[gennrik]] break into her tent to capture the cat, a black-ops team led by [[lexis]] (associate producer for Dungeon Crawler After Hours, acting under [[odette]]'s orders) ambushes them. Gennrik is killed, Brad is shot and left to die, and Beatrice is captured and locked in a bounty cube. Odette plans to use Beatrice for her own purposes rather than selling her to Borant.

## Floor 6 Talk Show & Resolution (Book 5)
- **Reunion and Blackmail**: In [[163-book-5-chapter-18-beatrice-is-alive]] (Chapter 18) and [[164-book-5-chapter-19-reunion-on-set]] (Chapter 19), [[odette]] and [[zev]] reveal that Beatrice is safe off-planet. Odette used Beatrice's rescue to blackmail the Borant Corporation into signing a higher revenue share contract. 
- **The Interview**: Beatrice appears as a virtual guest on Odette's show. She is extremely thin, pale, and heavily drugged, wearing high heels and a glittering red gown that exposes a poorly drawn Princess Chonkalot tattoo on her lower back. She has memory lapses regarding her capture and is confused by the gamified reality and the fact that Donut can talk.
- **The Confrontation**: Donut drops her aloof act and unleashes a furious tirade against Beatrice, calling her a "vile, disgusting bitch." Donut condemns her for her abuse of Carl and her plan to abandon Donut. Donut declares that Carl is now her "person." Carl tells Beatrice that they are done, and Odette removes her from the screen, ending her presence in their lives.
