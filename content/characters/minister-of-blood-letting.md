# Minister of Blood-Letting

**Minister of Blood-Letting** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

The Minister of Blood-Letting is a skeletal, pope-like creature encountered driving the Wienermobile with other 'weird pope creatures'. He carries a bucket of a revolting, white liquid. Donut greets him by name, and he responds with a silent bow as he passes.
