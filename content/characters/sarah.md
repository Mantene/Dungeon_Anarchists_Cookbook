# Sarah

**Sarah** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A hexcrafter and one of the crawlers from the Destruction float. She was killed during the events of global chapter 499 when her Abrams tank was hit by a Krakaren tentacle, leaving her body 'spread like jelly over the playing field.'
