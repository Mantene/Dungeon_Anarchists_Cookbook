# Gore-Gore

**Gore-Gore** was a Level 40 ManTauR train engineer NPC on the fourth floor of the World Dungeon. He is deceased.

## Biography and Role
Gore-Gore was a ManTauR (a centaur-like creature with a humanoid torso and lower body, but with four arms) who worked as a train engineer. He was an adherent of Grull, the war god, and a "berserker extreme." His upper chest was painted with blue body paint.

Gore-Gore is encountered at a transit station after the party crashes the Ochre Line train. Initially friendly, he greets the party and explains the station layouts and the mechanics of the employee return system (governed by the Kravyad at Station 435). However, after entering the Club Vanquisher, he returns in a berserk rage, calling the crawlers "vile traitors" and "saboteurs." He attacks them with metallic claws, initiating a boss-style *VERSUS* battle.

Gore-Gore proves to be a formidable opponent with high physical strength, easily throwing Mongo aside and dealing significant damage to Carl.

## Death
Carl drop-kicks Gore-Gore into the solar plexus, sending his body onto the electrified third rail of the train tracks. The resulting short circuit shortens the track and kills Gore-Gore, while also shocking Carl. Carl loots his body, acquiring the **Ochre Line Engineer's Key**, a bottle of Magic Hair Restoration, and a complete Ochre Line station map.
