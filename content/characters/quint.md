# Quint

Quint is a Level-75 Half-Djinn, Half-Garbage Scowl NPC who operates as a pharmacist (drug dealer) in the Desperado Club.

## Profile
Quint has the upper body of a sharp-toothed, beady-eyed opossum, while his lower half is a swirling mini-tornado (due to his Djinn heritage). He is a non-combatant NPC and a former crawler who chose this race. Quint is an old friend and former drug dealer for [[mordecai]]. 

In Chapter 15 ([[064-book-2-chapter-15-desperado-club]]), Quint trades an **Alchemy Chest Starter Kit** containing various chemicals and bujold sap to Carl and Donut in exchange for three bottles of Krakaren moonshine. He also gives Carl three **Blitz Sticks** (hallucinogenic licorice that can permanently raise Intelligence if smoked). Quint warns them not to take deal options on the 10th floor and to push through to Floor 11.
