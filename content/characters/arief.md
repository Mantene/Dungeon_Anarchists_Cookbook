# Arief

**War Leader Arief** is a Level 80 Semeru Dwarf who serves as the Commander of the Larracos Defense Force Infantry.

## Profile & Traits
- **Appearance**: Squat, wide-bodied, darker-complexioned dwarf with an Asian-like round face, flat nose, and a thin mustache. He wears ornate samurai-like bell armor and a rounded helmet adorned with various feathers and a metallic fern ornament.
- **Combat Style**: Wields a large, enchanted curved sickle blade slung over his shoulder.
- **Deity & Debuff**: Arief suffers from the **Despondent** debuff because his goddess, Ysalte, is dead. This imposes a 15% reduction to his stats and makes him take 10% more damage from worshippers of other deities. He is unable to worship any other deity.
- **Role in Faction Wars**: Arief coordinates the city's defenses with Team Retribution. He is a key contact for Carl and Donut in Larracos, facilitating meetings with the co-warlords [[juice-box]] and [[ferdinand]].
- **Family**: His uncle is one of the *Jatismara* (memory-quickened NPCs) who remembers past seasons of the crawl. His brother was tortured and killed by Architect Houston's Viceroy surgeons.
