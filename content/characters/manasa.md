# Manasa

Manasa is a famous cobra-headed Naga singer who serves as a guest panelist on the talk show *Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton*.

## Profile
Manasa is a giant cobra-headed pop star whose body is controlled by a Valtay parasite worm after her original death. Her career is highly successful in the universe, with her latest single ranked 8th. 

During the talk show interview, Manasa is friendly to Carl and Donut, laughing at Donut's remarks and complimenting Mongo. She swap trailers with Carl so he can use the trailer's advanced cleaning facilities. Because of this switch, Prince Stalwart's orbital pulse attack targets Manasa's trailer instead of Carl's, vaporizing her instantly. 

Her death causes massive outrage and prompts immediate military retaliation from the Valtay Corporation, who destroy Prince Stalwart's yacht, killing Queen Consort Ugloo and the [[the-maestro|Maestro]].
