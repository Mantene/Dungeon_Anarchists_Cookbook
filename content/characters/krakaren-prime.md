# Krakaren Prime

**Krakaren Prime** is a antagonist character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A powerful entity, potentially a floor boss, who was banished to the Nothing and later escaped to the eighteenth floor. Carl assumes the massive, roaring mob atop the towering tree in the arena is Krakaren Prime.
