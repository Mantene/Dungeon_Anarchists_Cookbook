# Daniel

**Daniel** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

Daniel is a crawler who deeply values serving his friends and community, viewing it as a holy act. He expresses finding peace despite his past actions and is honored to serve his companions one last time, believing he will be recognized on the other side.
