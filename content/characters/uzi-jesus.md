# Uzi Jesus

**Uzi Jesus** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

Uzi Jesus is a diminutive, enigmatic figure often seen in the company of deities, particularly Eris. He carries a toy gun and demonstrates an unusual awareness of spiritual energies, noting the auras present within the defeated Scolopendra. In this chapter, he observes Eris's powerful new form and her interactions with Carl, showing surprise at her appearance and later climbing onto her serpentine body.
