# Clarabelle

Clarabelle is a Level-40 Crocodilian NPC who works as a bouncer at the Desperado Club.

## Profile
Clarabelle is a thick-skinned, lizard-faced enforcer who guards the red members-only door at the back of the Desperado Club's public room. She is a non-combatant NPC who recognizes [[mordecai]] despite his incubus form and shares information about club events, including [[lucia-mar]]'s rampage in the playroom the night before.
