# Paca

**Paca** is a merchant at the Midnight Market and an ambassador/agent representing [[krakaren|Krakaren Prime]] (a deity stuck in the Nothing).

## Profile & Role
- **Faction Wars**: Eris reveals that the War Mages traded the [[the-gate-of-the-feral-gods]] to Paca at the Midnight Market.
- **The Gate**: Paca keeps the Gate of the Feral Gods stored inside her stomach. Carl tasks [[justice-light]] with retrieving it from her, which he and the sluggalos successfully accomplish.
