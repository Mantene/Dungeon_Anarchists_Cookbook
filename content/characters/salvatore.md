# Salvatore

Salvatore is a Level-16 City Elf NPC who serves as a Root Druid and Recruit Wand Bearer for the **201st Security Group** (specifically the Magical Ops division) at the *Apito Education Center* on Floor 3.

## Profile
Salvatore is an fanatical member of the city elf cult that worships skyfowl as angels of Apito. He stands outside the temple attempting to recruit members using acorn-tipped branches. Salvatore recognizes Carl and Donut, calling Donut the *Oak Fell* (the antichrist of their religion) due to her wearing the **Enchanted Anklet of the Fallen Oak**. He admits that the cult tortured [[gum-gum]] to find out who they were. 

During the confrontation in Chapter 20 ([[069-book-2-chapter-20-temple-confrontation]]), Salvatore eats an acorn to activate the *Magical Fervor* buff but is suddenly struck on the head with a rolling pin by [[eunice]] the dwarf. As the town guards arrive, Salvatore is cleaved in half by a village swordsman's blade.
