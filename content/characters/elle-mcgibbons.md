# Elle McGibbons

Elle McGibbons is a 99-year-old wheelchair-bound crawler from the Meadow Lark retirement home group.

## History & Dungeon Presence
Elle is one of the elderly Meadow Lark residents escorted through the dungeon by Carl, Donut, and the Meadow Lark caretakers. Her crawler ID is #12,330,800, under the name "Elle McGib."

In [[031-book-1-chapter-31-follows-and-favorites]], Carl helps wheel her into the safe room, where she asks for *Divorce Court* because it reminds her of her deceased husband, Barry. 

During the fight with the Level 93 Rage Elemental in [[038-book-1-chapter-38-rage-elemental]], the monster casts a *Reverse Gravity* spell, causing Elle to slam into the ceiling. Carl runs upside-down on the ceiling to rescue her and carries her to safety, force-feeding her a healing potion. 

In [[041-book-1-chapter-41-goodbyes-at-stairs]], Elle descends the stairs to Floor 3. Suffering from confusion, she mistakes Carl for her late husband Barry, jokingly comments that the dungeon seems like a "sex thing," and lets Donut curl up on her lap.

In Book 3 (*The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook*), Elle selects the **Frost Maiden Blizzardmancer** race/class, transforming her into a small, blue-skinned figure with flight capabilities and powerful frost magic. By [[078-book-3-chapter-3-well-rested]], she is ranked #10 on the global leaderboard.

Elle's combat prowess and actions on Floor 4 ("The Iron Tangle") include:
*   In [[084-book-3-chapter-9-desperado-assassination-attempt]], she drinks "Knockout" at the Desperado Club and exhibits a loud, aggressive personality. When assassins strike, she kills one by shooting a two-foot icicle through her eye socket.
*   She explores the secret conveyor tunnels with [[imani-c]]. During their escape on the conveyor in [[093-book-3-chapter-18-station-mimic-and-betrayal-coupons]], she uses freezing magic to clear out ghouls.
*   In [[106-book-3-chapter-31-defending-the-shafts-and-madisons-fate]], she fights Krakaren Juvenile Clonelings near Station 24, freezing pools of caustic acid blood to prevent them from burning the crawlers' feet.
*   In [[107-book-3-chapter-32-the-god-hijack-and-the-mimics-fall]] and [[108-book-3-chapter-33-the-second-deity-summoning]], she flies around the trainyard to draw the aggro of the war god Grull, helping Katia's decoy distraction succeed.
