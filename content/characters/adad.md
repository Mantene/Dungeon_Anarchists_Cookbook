# Adad

**Adad** is an AI-controlled storm deity in the dungeon.

## Biography and Role

Adad was summoned on the tenth floor during Mordecai's original crawl, appearing alongside the sponsored storm goddess [[dodola]]. Because Adad was AI-controlled rather than driven by a sponsor, the party could not negotiate with him. Shortly after appearing, Dodola stabbed Adad's leg and teleported him away to ensure she was the sole deity present.

## Attributes
- **Type**: AI-Controlled Storm Deity
- **Status**: Teleported away during Mordecai's original crawl
