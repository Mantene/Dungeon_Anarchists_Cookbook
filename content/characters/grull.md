# Grull

**Grull** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A deity traditionally worshipped by most mantaurs, though Porky now considers him a 'very unpleasant deity' after his split. Female mantaurs worship Enyo instead.
