# Sister Ines Quiteria

**Sister Ines Quiteria** is a human crawler who is the namesake of Team Sister Ines on Floor 8. Prior to the dungeon collapse, she was incarcerated in a women's prison in Cuba.

## Biography and Role

Sister Ines was protected early in the dungeon crawl by [[anton-lopez]] and [[paz-lo]], who were prison guards at her facility on Earth. Anton killed two fellow crawlers (including [[elian]]) early in the crawl when they tried to murder her. She has the class Poet Laureate and serves as the bard for her team, reciting religious poetry that grants powerful buffs to her party (such as a 50% dexterity boost).

During her climb, Sister Ines acquired feline physical adaptations. This led to comedic friction with [[princess-donut]], who dislikes Havana Browns and suspects Sister Ines because "she doesn't even use a litterbox."

### The Reaper of Havana and Party Dissolution
In Book 6, Chapter 16, Paz Lo reveals Sister Ines's true history. She possesses the passive racial trait *Toxoplasmosis*, which mind-warps those around her, making them highly protective of her and causing them to forget or ignore her misdeeds. On Earth, she was known as "El Segador de La Habana" (The Reaper of Havana) for poisoning and murdering all the nuns in her convent. 

Following Anton's death by Ogun, Sister Ines uses a T'Ghee squad flag to turn the dying Paz Lo into a card totem. She then loots Anton's corpse and silently walks out of the graveyard temple, abandoning Carl and Donut. After Donut sends her a threatening message promising to hunt her down and destroy Paz's card to punish her, Ines blocks Donut on chat, making her difficult to track down.

### Climax and Death
In Phase Two, Sister Ines was sponsored by Pontifex Shine of the Nebular Balance, who helped her gain control of the sea serpent boss [[madre-de-aguas]] (possessed by [[amayon]]) and three Level 140 pleasure demons. She threatened to block the exit unless Carl surrendered himself to her. 

During the infiltration of the FOCSA building in Chapter 65, she charged at the party chanting. Carl knocked her unconscious using the *Wand of Nighty-Night*, and [[louis-santiago-2|Louis]] stabbed her to death in order to stop the boss battle threat and spare the other crawlers the player killer skulls. 

Upon her death, her patron deity [[ysalte]] entered the realm.
