# Hold Steady

**Hold Steady** was a Skyfowl crawler who was a member of [[mordecai]]'s original party alongside [[uzzi]], [[chaco]], and [[odette]].

## Biography and Role

During the tenth-floor crisis detailed in the Book 6 Prologue, Hold Steady was tasked with keeping the exit puzzle pieces primed with magic. He survived the caustic rain thanks to Uzzi's *Climate Refuge* / protection magic. When Chaco murdered Uzzi to summon the storm goddess [[dodola]], Hold Steady reacted with horror and anger, demanding to know why Chaco had betrayed their teammate. He is assumed to have escaped to the eleventh floor along with Chaco and Mordecai.

## Attributes
- **Race**: Skyfowl
- **Affiliation**: Mordecai's original party
- **Role**: Support/Mage (responsible for keeping the exit puzzle primed)
