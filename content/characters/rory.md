# Rory

Rory is a Level 7 **Goblin Shamanka** encountered by [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] in the Goblin Workshop on Floor 1.

## Encounter
Rory is a 5-foot-3-inch goblin covered in about fifty facial piercings. When she confronted Carl and Donut, Donut used her high Charisma (37) to charm Rory, asking if her facial modifications were "a daddy thing." Rory broke down in tears, admitting it was true, and let Donut sit in her lap. 

Rory negotiated with Carl, trading a steam-powered chopper with a sidecar, coal, and grenades in exchange for two baggies of llama-produced methamphetamine. She explained that goblins cannot leave their floor and will sleep when the floor collapses, hoping to eventually wake up deep enough in the dungeon to live in peace away from crawlers.
