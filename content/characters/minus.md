# Minus

**Minus** is a Syndicate security forces soldier and "soother" from Teelan-3 who infiltrates the tenth floor of the dungeon in Book 8 (*A Parade of Horribles*).

## Biography & Suicide Mission
Minus is recruited by [[captain-fresh]] for a suicide mission to infiltrate the World Dungeon. Syndicate security forces execute Minus's estranged brother, [[linus]], so Minus can assume his identity. Because Linus had purchased full citizenship and made posts about the fairy crawler [[elle-mcgibbons]], Minus is sent to onboard as her attached soother tourist. 

The goal of the mission is to assassinate his primary and secondary crawler targets (disguised as accidents) to destabilize the crawler groups and force the Ascendency game to kick off early. Because the system AI is highly suspicious of injected memory recall, Minus is forced to rapid-study Linus's behavior and act like a perverted tourist. 

In [[456-book-8-chapter-56-take-on-me]], Minus (impersonating Linus) appears in the Lollipop bar, acting like a perverted fan of Elle and touching Donut's tail, which leads Elle to order him to stay close.
