# Prime Minister Glory

**Prime Minister Glory** is the leader of the Syndicate, the intergalactic conglomerate running the dungeon crawl.

## Biography and Role

Prime Minister Glory belongs to the Gleener species. In Book 6 Chapter 8, he makes a rare appearance on the recap show broadcast next to Kevin the announcer. Mordecai notes that it is highly unusual for the Syndicate's top executive to appear on screen, hinting at high-level political shifts and complications behind the scenes of the crawl.

- **Race**: Gleener
- **Affiliation**: The Syndicate (Leader)
- **Role**: Antagonist (Syndicate Leader)
