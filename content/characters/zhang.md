# Zhang

Zhang is a bald human crawler who worked in a warehouse before the collapse.

## Characteristics & Dungeon Presence
Zhang has a bronze star next to his name and has an exceptionally high Constitution stat of 15 (one point higher than Carl's initial constitution of 14).

In [[042-book-1-chapter-42-death-watch-extreme]], he is forced onto the show *Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem* alongside his friend [[li-jun]]. During the high-pressure game, Zhang tries to make the hard choice to save Li Jun and [[li-na]], but the Maestro's game is derailed by Carl's intervention. 

After being sent back into the dungeon, Zhang acts quickly on Carl's advice, retrieving their manager while Li Jun grabs his sister, and they escape to a safe room.

In Book 3 (*The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook*), Zhang reaches **Level 28** and selects the **Earth Mover** class. 

During Floor 4 ("The Iron Tangle"), Zhang participates in the defense of Station 36. In [[106-book-3-chapter-31-defending-the-shafts-and-madisons-fate]], Carl routes a rapid-response cart (the "Def Leppard" cart) to Station 36. Because the hallways leading to the main chamber are too narrow for the heavy cart, Zhang uses his **shrinking wand** to reduce its size. They push the shrunken cart into the center of the main room, positioning it directly over the Syndicate logo in the center of the closed stairwells to serve as an improvised defensive fortification.
