# Goiter

**Goiter** is a Level 42 Bugbear and a council member of the Small Bugbear Settlement on Floor 6 (The Hunting Grounds). 

## Profile & Meeting
- **Appearance**: Like all bugbears, Goiter is a large, bulky, furry humanoid with pointed ears and a goblin-like face. He has orange-brown fur and speaks with a thick Eastern European accent.
- **Meeting Carl**: In [[155-book-5-chapter-10-the-ursine-mayors-fate]] (Chapter 10), Goiter is hammering wood on a roof to repair the settlement after previous hunter raids. He tells [[carl]] that crawlers are not welcome. However, when Carl declares his intent to kill the hunters, Goiter becomes friendly and offers to buy him a drink. Carl warns him that the hunters will be released soon, and Goiter prepares the settlement for the incoming clash.
