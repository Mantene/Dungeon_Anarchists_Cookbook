# Dwight

**Dwight** is an NPC sparkling unicorn racer on the tenth floor of the dungeon.

## Biography and Role

Dwight is a Level 87 Enchanter Sparkling Unicorn and a member of **Team Sparkles**, along with his partner [[lucienne]]. Unlike typical gentle unicorns, Dwight is a notorious jerk, described as the type of person who would call the police on a kid's lemonade stand. He rides on an Avernus Creeper plant mount.

In [[404-book-8-chapter-5-team-sparkles]], Dwight and Lucienne insult [[princess-donut]], calling her "fatty" and a "corpulent swine." Their mount runs over Carl and Donut's trailer when startled by the truck's horn. In [[417-book-8-chapter-18-hail-and-heavy-weapons]], Dwight gets into a shouting match with the guck elemental Masterplan before Heat Two, which culminates in Dwight firing a tank cannon at Girth the Trouble's Chevy Bel Air, blowing them up.
