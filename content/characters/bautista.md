# Daniel Bautista

**Race:** Tiger-like (humanoid feline; also has a shapeshifting ring)  
**Class:** Unknown  
**Level:** Unknown  
**Party:** Katia's squad; in a romantic relationship with Katia Grim  
**Pre-dungeon:** Owned a tea shop with his family

---

## Background

Daniel Bautista entered the dungeon alone (not in a party). He is religious — one of the series' more openly devout characters, alongside Imani. He used to own a tea shop with his family; his tea is described as excellent (Imani and Elle are both "addicted to it").

He appears as a large, humanoid, tiger-like being — most similar to Tony the Tiger. Donut described him as "a generic brand Tony the Tiger," which is mean but accurate.

---

## Relationship with Katia

Bautista and Katia entered into a romantic relationship during Floor 6. He is gentle with her, protective without being controlling. He has been present during Katia's struggles with the drug/bliss-surf issue discovered on Floor 8 — and was observed making excuses for her behavior, suggesting awareness of the problem.

When Katia grew back a lost arm (from Carl's Self-Immolation) during the Havana battle, she simply said "It's nothing." Bautista's expression suggested it wasn't nothing, but he followed her lead.

---

## The Shapeshifting Ring

Bautista has a ring that allows him to change shape. During the Havana battle (Book 6), he shifted into a four-armed creature similar to the AI combat trainer for better utility in the rescue operation.

---

## Book 6

In Iceland with Katia, Louis, Britney, and Tran. Made it through the Phase 2 key battle. During Phase 3, was with Katia in the Havana battle on the FOSCA building, fighting alongside her. Made it to Floor 9.

---

## Book 7 — Katia's Cybernetics

Bautista's most significant contribution in the series: he built **Katia Grim's cybernetic replacement limbs** in advance — a right arm, two legs, and one ear — before Floor 9's end, anticipating that Katia might need to leave the dungeon quickly. When Katia chose pregnancy as her Eileithyia option and began reverting to her human form (missing the limbs lost in battle), Bautista immediately attached the cybernetics. The transition was seamless — he was ready.

Katia disappeared from the dungeon. Bautista remained.

**Book 8:** Bautista used the **Pineapple Cabaret** unofficial exit during Floor 10. Unlike Louis, Britney, and Chris (who ended up in Sheol), Bautista's destination is listed as **unknown** — he may have made it to the surface or ended up elsewhere.
