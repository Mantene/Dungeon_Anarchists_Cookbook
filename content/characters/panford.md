# Panford

**Panford** is the designated Adjutant for the Democratic Sortion during the Ninth Floor Faction Wars.

## Profile & Traits
- **Race**: Tall, purple-skinned Dreadnaught alien.
- **Role**: Faction Adjutant. He is responsible for enforcing Faction Wars rules and coordinating system communications for the Democratic Sortion.
- **Personality**: Professional, strict, and highly bureaucratic. He does not tolerate rule-breaking or threats from faction leaders.

## Biography
In Book 7, Chapter 17, Panford confronts Commander [[stockade]] in the aftermath of a disastrous, backfired sabotage attempt against [[carl]]. Panford informs Stockade that the AI ruled the incident—which resulted in the slaughter of fifty Sortion soldiers—as an "AI action" exempt from Ceasefire penalties because of the demonic entity involved. Panford reprimands Stockade for colluding with external forces (Architect Houston) without notifying his adjutants, calling a foul and imposing heavy sanctions on the Democratic Sortion.
