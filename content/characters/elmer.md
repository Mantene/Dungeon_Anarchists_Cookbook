# Elmer

**Elmer** is a Level 40 Ursine and the Mayor of the Large Ursine Settlement on Floor 6 (The Hunting Grounds). 

## Profile & Traits
- **Appearance**: Elmer is a large, brown bear-like humanoid who walks on two legs. He wears no pants, but wears a vest and glasses.
- **Personality**: The System AI description characterizes Elmer and the Ursine as deeply hypocritical, self-righteous, and repressed. Their society is compared to the town from the movie *Footloose* due to their strict, moralistic laws (banning dancing, singing outside temples, alcohol, etc.).

## Death
- **Encounter**: In [[155-book-5-chapter-10-the-ursine-mayors-fate]] (Chapter 10), Elmer stops [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] at the covered bridge leading to his town, enforcing the rule banning dinosaurs ([[mongo]]) and claiming Donut is a "monkey."
- **Execution Fee**: Elmer offers to execute both Mongo and Donut for a fee of 150 gold. When he steps forward to collect the money, Donut casts *Astral Paw* to push him down the steep riverbank.
- **Demise**: Elmer tumbles down the slope, cracks his head on the rocks, and dies instantly. Before Carl can loot him, a Level 80+ Naiad leaps from the river, grabs Elmer's corpse, and drags it under. Because Elmer died as a result of her push, Donut is credited with his kill and gains the title of Mayor (Grand) of the settlement.
