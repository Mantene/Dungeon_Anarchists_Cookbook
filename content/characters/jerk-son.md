# Jerk Son

**Jerk Son** is a antagonist character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

The 'Jerk Son' is a character described by Samantha as someone she once considered a friend. However, he allegedly betrayed her, tricking her into giving birth to a monster that threatens to destroy all of creation. He is associated with the 'Demon Mother Lady' and is a primary target of Samantha's wrath and plans for vengeance.
