# Prepotente

**Species:** Goat (sapient; race picked in the dungeon)  
**Class:** Unknown  
**Level:** 71 (at the end of Floor 6 / start of Floor 7)  
**Leaderboard:** #3 in Book 4 (400,000 bounty ×2); rank shifted across books  
**Previous owner:** Miriam Dom (dead — killed protecting him on Floor 6)  
**Familiar:** Bianca (hellspawn demon goat dragon — Miriam's other pet, still alive; traveling with Prepotente)  
**Answers to:** Barely anyone; communicates via terse chat messages; rarely responds

---

## Background

Prepotente is a goat who entered the dungeon with Miriam Dom. He chose a goat race in the dungeon and became massively powerful. He has a habit of not answering messages, speaking in single words or grunts, and making incredibly consequential decisions without warning anyone.

Pre-dungeon appearance: wore a little outfit. Post-race selection: still a goat, but now a giant, sapient, terrifyingly powerful fighting goat.

---

## Key Events

**Book 3:** Introduced as Miriam Dom's companion. Already high level and unusual.

**Book 4 (Iron Tangle):** Leaderboard #3. One of the most powerful non-human crawlers. Miriam Dom was turned into a vampire somewhere during Floor 4-5.

**Book 5 (The Hunting Grounds/Butcher's Masquerade):** Miriam Dom died protecting Prepotente on Floor 6. He watched her die. He has Bianca with him still — his familiar/companion.

In the final moments of Floor 6, Prepotente encountered the **Fairy Ring** puzzle mechanic on the brief Floor 7 opening. The puzzle was based on a fairy tale where a child discovers a fairy ring and must perform specific actions. Prepotente looked at the puzzle, looked at the fairy ring, and then **jumped straight through it** — the exact same action the fairy tale child performs but which took him thousands of years in the story. He broke Floor 7 in 60 seconds, causing the floor to collapse and dumping all crawlers directly onto Floor 8.

The Valtay engineers (not Prepotente) are blamed for building a floor that could be broken this way.

**Book 6 (Floor 8):** Prepotente's Phase 1 and 2 location: the Bahamas (the place Carl had the Book of Lore for). He asked Carl to read the Bahamas lore book to him. Carl made him pay in Size-Up potions. He obtained a key and successfully exited to Floor 9. He put all the squads blocking his stairwell to sleep before exiting, reporting: *"I am going to finish my training and proceed to the exit when it is time."*

---

## Relationships

- **Miriam Dom** — dead; she died for him. He does not openly grieve but presumably carries it.
- **Bianca** — hellspawn familiar (demon goat dragon); traveling with him. Bianca ate Gimli (an ally's hawk) during the Masquerade, causing tension with Osvaldo's group.
- **Carl** — occasional reluctant ally; Carl has to bribe him with potions to get any cooperation

---

## Notoriety

Prepotente is in a unique position: he accidentally destroyed an entire dungeon floor, has never been punished for it (the engineers are blamed), and continues moving through the dungeon at his own pace with no apparent concern for anything. He is one of the most powerful individual crawlers in the game.

---

## Books 7–8

**Floor 9 — Faction Wars (Book 7):** Prepotente participated in Faction Wars. He handled the orc front, fighting the Skull Empire's Bone Clan forces. He remained characteristically independent — fighting at his own pace, not taking orders from anyone.

**Floor 11 — Parade of Horribles (Book 8):** Prepotente's defining moment in the series. During the parade procession on Floor 11, his group carried the "Vengeance" float. He sang **"In the Air Tonight"** by Phil Collins — a capella, using his trained tapir **Sweety** to beat the drum tempo. The performance was stunning. He had been practicing for this his entire life. It was not a joke.

**Floor 12:** Prepotente is confirmed on Floor 12 as one of the 19 remaining crawlers.
