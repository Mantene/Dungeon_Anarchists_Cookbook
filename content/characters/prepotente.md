# Prepotente

Prepotente (also known as "Pony") is a goat crawler who becomes one of the top-ranked crawlers and a major character in the series.

## Background
Prepotente was a black goat with curled horns before the collapse. Awakened by a pet biscuit, he gained human intelligence and speech (though his voice is extremely high-pitched and he often screeches). He is deeply devoted to his owner and handler, [[miriam-dom]].

## Crawler Profile
- **Class**: Screaming Goat / Brawler archetype.
- **Key Abilities**: 
  - **Scream**: A powerful sonic scream attack that deals heavy area-of-effect damage, shatters glass, and disorients enemies.
- **Floor 3 Performance**: Pony is extremely efficient at grinding, ending Floor 3 ranked **#3** overall.

## Narrative Arc (Book 2)
Pony is introduced in the Skyfowl settlement and the members-only section of the Desperado Club in [[062-book-2-chapter-13-desperado-rewards]]. 

During the chaos of the final battle on Floor 3, **[[miriam-dom]] is killed**. This tragedy devastates Pony, fueling a deep, burning hatred for the Borant Corporation, the crawl's producers, and the Syndicate. He descends into Floor 4 driven entirely by grief and vengeance, setting up his trajectory as a highly volatile crawler.
