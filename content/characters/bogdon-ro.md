# Bogdon Ro

Bogdon Ro is a human crawler who progresses to Floor 5.

## Class & Stats
- **Class**: **Legatus**.
- **Dungeon Status**: Bogdon Ro is Level 31 and rank #9 on the global leaderboard at the beginning of Floor 5.
- He is introduced in the Floor 5 recap announcement in [[114-book-4-chapter-4-the-royal-chariot]].

## Floor 6 Death (Book 5)
- **Death**: In [[170-book-5-chapter-25-future-huntress]] (Chapter 25), the system broadcasts an Admin Message that a champion has fallen. Katia confirms in chat that Bogdon Ro was cornered by Langley's team, but was killed, and a hunter claimed the bounty for him.
