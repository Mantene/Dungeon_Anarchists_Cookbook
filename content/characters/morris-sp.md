# Morris Sp

Morris Sp is a spider-centaur crawler who progresses to Floor 5.

## Class & Stats
- **Race**: **Spider-Centaur** (human head and torso on a tarantula body).
- **Class**: **Freelance Psychiatrist**.
- **Dungeon Status**: Morris is Level 23 and operates in the Subterranean Quadrant of Bubble #543.
- In [[116-book-4-chapter-6-the-collateral-secret]], Morris appears with [[low-thi]]'s Subterranean group. Because he has Desperado Club access, Carl instructs him to meet him at the club so Carl can transcribe the Necropolis map to their group.
