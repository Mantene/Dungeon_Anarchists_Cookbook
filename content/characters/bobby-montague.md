# Bobby Montague

**Bobby Montague** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

An Illusionist crawler whose cleaner bot transported 105 people to the Pineapple Cabaret, contributing to the overall survival efforts. He is noted for his "tall hair."
