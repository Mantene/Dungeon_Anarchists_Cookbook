# WriggleInDew2023

**WriggleInDew2023** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

WriggleInDew2023 is an external observer of the Dungeon, identified as the vice president of the Mongo Mommies Valtay chapter and a street team member of Elle's Snow Cones fan club. She is noted for correctly theorizing that the imposter Linus was indeed the real Linus's brother, demonstrating her astute observation and involvement within the fan communities that follow the Crawl.
