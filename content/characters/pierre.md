# Pierre

**Pierre** is a Level 25 Grapple NPC who works as a porter on the fourth floor of the World Dungeon.

## Biography and Role
Pierre is introduced in Chapter 80 on the Yellow Line train. He is a **Grapple**—a multi-limbed, spider-like or crustacean-like creature—and works in the porter car (Car 10), which houses rows of shelves containing passengers' luggage and prize suitcases.

When Carl, Princess Donut, and Mongo explore the porter car, Pierre welcomes them in a slow, gravelly voice and assists them in retrieving their luggage. He is depicted as a dedicated employee who complains about the "crazy grapples" always mixing up passengers' luggage.
