# Langley

Langley is a human crawler who progresses to Floor 5.

## Class & Stats
- **Class**: **Archer**.
- **Dungeon Status**: Langley is Level 24 and is the leader of a party of six Finnish car salesmen from Helsinki.
- In [[113-book-4-chapter-3-spit-and-swallow]], Langley and his party of archers are found in Hump Town. They are serious about their survival and cooperate with Carl. They have been trying to solve the City Hall mystery and agree to meet for a group coordination.

## Floor 6 Death (Book 5)
- **Wiped Out**: In [[170-book-5-chapter-25-future-huntress]] (Chapter 25), Katia reports that Langley and his entire party of Finnish archers were wiped out and permanently killed. They had cornered [[bogdon-ro]], but a hunter got to them first or they fell to the environment/combat. Carl is deeply saddened by his death, recalling Langley's Helsinki background and how hard his team worked to help others.
