# Alicia

**Alicia** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

Alicia is a friend of Marjory Williams, appearing in a simulated memory or "what-if" scenario in apartment 231. She is shown urging Marjory to call the police on Bill for violating a restraining order, reminding Marjory of Bill's abusive actions, including harming Marjory and her cat, Gravy Boat.
