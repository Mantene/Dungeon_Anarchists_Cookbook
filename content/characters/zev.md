# Zev

Zev is a low-level Kua-tin Admin and the assigned Public Relations Agent for [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]].

## Characteristics
Zev is a female Kua-tin, an aquatic humanoid race. She stands about two feet tall and wears a water-filled glass bubble helmet and a bulky, protective white mesh spacesuit designed to keep her safe inside the dungeon. 

## Role in the Crawl
Zev is the Assistant Communications Representative for the Borant Corporation. Under the Crawler Assisted Outreach Program, she is assigned to represent Carl and Donut after their high viewership triggers automatic PR representation. She manages their interview schedules, including appearances on [[odette]]'s show and other intergalactic programs. 

Zev bonds with Donut over their shared love for Earth TV dramas like *Gossip Girl* and *Riverdale*. She reveals to Carl that she is sympathetic to the crawlers and that the Kua-tin ruling party, the **Bloom** (or "the Party"), is bankrupt and forcing Borant to accelerate the dungeon timeline to cash out early, which is leading to lethal system tweaks and bugs. 

Zev is temporarily suspended by the System AI for "cheating" after she warns Carl and Donut of the incoming Level 93 Rage Elemental, but her suspension is overturned on appeal when it is shown Carl noticed the trigger simultaneously.
