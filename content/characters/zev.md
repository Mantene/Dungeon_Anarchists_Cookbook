# Zev

**Race:** Kua-Tin (small fish-person; wears a water-filled rebreather over her gills when not in an aquatic environment)  
**Role:** Carl and Donut's Borant Corporation PR/admin agent  
**Level:** N/A (NPC, non-combatant)  
**Current situation:** Borant is being liquidated after the crawl; Zev is doing the work of five people after the Valtay changeover cut staff

---

## Background

Zev is Carl and Donut's assigned Borant agent — their PR manager, liaison coordinator, and general go-between with the show's administration. She appears as a holo, always tiny and professional, usually juggling a tablet. She wears a rebreather when out of water.

She genuinely likes Carl and Donut. She is not as cold as Cascadia and gives them actual useful information when she can.

---

## Books 1–6

Present at every floor change and major show appearance. Coordinates their show schedule, monitors their sponsor relationships, and passes messages between them and the production staff. After the Valtay takeover of the Borant Corporation's crawl operations, she lost most of her support staff and has been working nonstop.

Key moments:
- **Book 1:** First contact aboard Odette's production yacht. Managed the Odette interview contract.
- **Book 6:** Arranged the Faction Wars preproduction meetings. Did not warn Quasar that Harbinger would be present at the Shadow Boxer interview (she forgot — she was overwhelmed). Apologized. Later told Carl there were emergency all-hands meetings related to Borant's collapse. Warned Carl off solo expeditions (the Homestead homeless shelter trip) but ultimately didn't stop him.
- She told Carl: *"Your sponsor disappeared after they sent you that box. They actually sent it to you a few days ago... Believe me, it was not [intentional]. And yes, everyone seems to think it has to do with whatever this is you're headed to."*
- She confirmed the quarantine is AI-enforced. She does not know what the failsafe does.

---

## Relationships

- **Carl and Donut** — genuine affection; tries to help them within her constraints
- **Cascadia** — her boss; stressed relationship
- **Quasar** — professional friction; he blames her for not keeping him informed
