# Captain Fresh

**Captain Fresh** is a captain within the Syndicate security forces in Book 8 (*A Parade of Horribles*).

## History & Dungeon Presence
In [[451-book-8-interlude-soother-infiltration]], Captain Fresh briefs [[minus]] on his suicide mission to infiltrate the World Dungeon. Fresh supervises the execution of Minus's brother, [[linus]], and provides Minus with Linus's behavioral logs and datasets to enable the identity theft. He explains that the Syndicate is losing to the dungeon AI and hopes a surgical crawler assassination will force the Ascendency early to allow their other assets to intervene.
