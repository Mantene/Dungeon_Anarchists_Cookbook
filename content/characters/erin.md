# Erin

**Erin** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

One of Chris's two identical teammates. She, along with her twin, was convinced by Chris to take a deal and exit the dungeon before the final race, leaving Chris to pilot their big rig alone. She had previously been a survivor of her heat alongside Chris, Prepotente, and Jurgen.
