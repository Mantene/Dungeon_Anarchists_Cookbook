# Eileithyia

**Eileithyia** is a goddess in the Celestial Ascendency associated with childbirth and midwifery.

## Biography and Role

Eileithyia is aligned with the Valtay faction in the Ascendency. She is a close friend of the Yoruban river mother deity [[yemaya]], and had missed Yemaya during her long absence from the Halls. 

In Book 6 Chapter 15, after Carl and his team repair Yemaya's shrine using the *Sun of Reawakening*, Yemaya re-ascends. Out of gratitude for her friend's return, Eileithyia rewards each crawler involved in the resurrection by granting them a single, powerful boon.

## Attributes
- **Role**: Goddess of Childbirth / Ascendency Deity
- **Affiliation**: Valtay Corporation / Yemaya
