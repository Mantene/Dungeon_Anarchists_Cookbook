# Igor

**Igor** is a Level 45 Crocodilian NPC who works as a guard at the Desperado Club on the fourth floor of the World Dungeon.

## Biography and Role
Igor is introduced in Chapter 84 as a crocodilian guard in the members-only section of the Desperado Club. He acts as a source of information for Carl regarding intergalactic politics. 

Igor explains that the Valtay Corporation and the Skull Empire are currently fighting a proxy war over Larracos, the capital city of the "disputed lands." He notes that their combat units are not permitted to engage in direct warfare inside the dungeon until the crawlers reach the ninth floor, so they use the Desperado Club to unwind and gamble with credits instead of gold.
