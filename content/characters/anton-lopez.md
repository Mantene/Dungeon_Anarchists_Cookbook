# Anton Lopez

**Anton Lopez** is a deceased human crawler who was the squad leader of Team Sister Ines on Floor 8.

## Biography and Role

Anton Lopez was the cousin of [[paz-lo]]. On Earth, he and Paz were prison guards in the women's prison in Cuba where [[sister-ines-quiteria]] was incarcerated as a convicted serial killer. Under the influence of her *Toxoplasmosis* trait, he became intensely protective of her. Early in the crawl (on Floor 1 or 2), Anton killed two members of his own party (including [[elian]]) who attempted to kill Sister Ines.

Anton had the class Fugitive and was highly skilled with a crossbow. During the [[pueblo-de-los-olvidados]] quest, he acted as squad leader, managing the team's T'Ghee deck. 

### Death by Ogun
In Book 6, Chapter 15, the group bypassed Ogun's shrine, causing the deity to physically manifest in Havana. In Chapter 16, Carl attempted to protect Anton using the *Black Nimbus* spell and healing scrolls. However, the god [[ogun]] clarified that the spell only removed Anton from his mercy, not his wrath, and casually stepped on Anton's head, crushing it and killing him instantly. Sister Ines subsequently looted his body and abandoned the group.
