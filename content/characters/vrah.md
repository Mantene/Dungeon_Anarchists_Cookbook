# Vrah

**Vrah** is a Level 70 Mantis hunter and the leader of the hunter faction [[the-dark-hive]]. She is one of the "core group" of 20 regular hunters who return to the Hunting Grounds every season. 

## Profile & Traits
- **Background**: Vrah has thousands of crawler kills to her name. She is known for taking the severed heads of her victims and wearing them on her body as trophies.
- **Sister**: She is the older sister of [[xindy]], another mantis hunter who is killed by Carl.

## Actions on Floor 6
- **Ambush & Warning**: In [[155-book-5-chapter-10-the-ursine-mayors-fate]] (Chapter 10), after Carl kills Xindy and bombs Zockau, Vrah leaves a threatening note pinned with a knife on the door to Carl's personal space.
- **Strategic Countermeasures**: 
  - She reveals that all hunters have obtained explosion protection buffs, neutralizing Carl's IED strategy.
  - She ordered the Zockau guards to worship [[emberus]] (Carl's patron deity), meaning that if Carl kills them, he will violate his cleric-adherent vows and be smote by his own god.
  - She promises to capture Carl, torture him by drinking his organs slowly, and place his head on Xindy's grave.
