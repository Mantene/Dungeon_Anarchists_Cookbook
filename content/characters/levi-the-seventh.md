# Levi the Seventh

**Levi the Seventh** is a Level 7 Troll Flesher and Hobgoblin Skellie Symbiote NPC who is involved in a recurring quest on the fourth floor of the World Dungeon.

## Biography and Role
Levi is a composite creature consisting of a Troll Flesher body and a Hobgoblin skeleton symbiote. He is introduced on the *Dismemberment Limited* train, where Carl and his party trigger the quest **"Levi is On the Menu!"** 

Levi's role in the dungeon is highly cyclical: he has been subject to this quest six times before (hence "the Seventh"). The quest requires crawlers to protect him from being devoured by other Fleshers, who seek to reclaim his bones and skin. Levi is terrified of this process and whimpers in fear throughout the encounter, screaming that the war mage [[dismember]] wants to re-sleeve him.

After Carl's party defeats the Fleshers and kills Dismember, Levi is saved and expresses hysterical gratitude to the group, waving as the train departs.
