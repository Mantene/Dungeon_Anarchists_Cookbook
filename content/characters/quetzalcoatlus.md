# Quetzalcoatlus

Quetzalcoatlus is an undead ghost NPC residing in the Necropolis of Anser.

## Biography & Role
- **Background**: Quetzalcoatlus was the wife of Emperor Anser. Following the emperor's death, she and his entire court were sealed alive within the Necropolis tomb.
- **Dungeon Presence**: She has survived as a non-corporeal undead ghost that travels through the catacomb walls, screaming like a giant bird. The Subterranean crawlers have a quest to find and eliminate her.
- **Lore**: [[henrik]] and the Changeling Principals were drugging the gnome mechanic [[wynne]] in order to force him to cast a temporary resurrection spell to give Quetzalcoatlus a physical form. The changelings desired to touch her resurrected body to steal her unique templates and powers.
