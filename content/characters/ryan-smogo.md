# Ryan Smogo

**Ryan Smogo** is a Level 59 human Blood Swimmer crawler who fights for the Princess Posse during the Ninth Floor Faction Wars.

## Profile & Traits
- **Class & Level**: Level 59 Blood Swimmer.
- **Role**: Infantry combatant stationed in the trenches of the southern front.

## History
In Book 7, Chapter 21, Smogo is stationed in the southern trenches when the ceasefire ends. He monitors the enemy lines, observing when they stop their artillery. When the Reavers launch their biological rot sticker pets, Smogo warns [[carl]] and the other soldiers not to let the exploding monsters touch them, as they carry the highly contagious "Gurgles" debuff.
