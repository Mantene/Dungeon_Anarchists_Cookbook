# Ferdinand

**Sir Ferdinand** (often called **Ferdinand** or **Gravy Boat**) is a Level 100 cat, former Province Boss, and the co-warlord of Team Retribution on the ninth floor.

## Profile & Traits
- **Background**: Ferdinand is a white cat from Princess Donut's past cat show circuit. Donut had a massive attraction to him, calling him a "bad boy."
- **Abilities**: Level 100. He possesses high dexterity, claws, a phase jump, and a lightning spell granted by his magical turban.
- **Truce & Alliance**: Sledge's cast of *Zerzura* brought him to Floor 9. When the Bloc declared war on the NPCs, Donut negotiated a truce with him ("Ferdinand and his Bird"). The alliance was later renamed to **"The Good Guys"**.

## Progression

- **Floor 9 Warlord and Mayor**: On Floor 9, Ferdinand serves as the Warlord on High of Team Retribution alongside [[juice-box]], and temporarily becomes the mayor of Shanty Town after the previous mayor is killed.
- **The Mayorship Trade**: In Book 7 Chapter 10, Ferdinand—sitting on the head of their adjutant [[drick]] and wearing a toilet-paper turban—demands his rhino mount (Simoom) and his turban hat back. Carl trades him gold and [[quan-ch]]'s boomerang bowler hat in exchange for the **mayorship of Shanty Town**.
- **Marriage to Juice Box**: Ferdinand claims to be Donut's fiancé, which Donut vociferously and disgustedly denies. He remains present during Juice Box's "small wedding" to [[louis-santiago-2]].
