# Ferdinand

**Also known as:** Gravy Boat, "Warlord on High, Sir Ferdinand"  
**Species:** Domestic cat (orange tom)  
**Role:** NPC Province Boss (Book 5), NPC Home Team Co-Warlord (Books 6–7)  
**Current location:** Floor 12 — the Court of the Ascendency (sent via Zerzura)

An ordinary neighborhood tom cat who became a Level 100 Province Boss, a Faction Wars co-warlord, and — as of Book 7 — a resident of the dungeon's final floor.

## Background

Ferdinand (nicknamed "Gravy Boat" by the neighborhood) was an outdoor tom cat who lived near Carl's apartment building before the Transformation. Donut knew of him as a neighborhood cat. [Beatrice](beatrice.md) later took him in at the New Queen Anne survivor camp in Seattle.

He is Donut's "secret" — referenced obliquely in Book 1 as something Donut knows about but Carl doesn't.

---

## Book 4 — Walk-On List Target

The Book 4 epilogue reveals Ferdinand is alive in Seattle's **New Queen Anne** survivor camp with Beatrice and Brad. He is listed as an **A-tier target on the Walk-On List** — alien bounty hunters were specifically sent to capture him for the dungeon production. Why he rated A-tier is not explained in Book 4.

---

## Book 5 — Province Boss

Ferdinand appeared at the **Butcher's Masquerade** as **Queen Imogen's familiar** — a Level 100 Province Boss. How an ordinary tom cat became a dungeon boss familiar is not explained. During the climactic fight:

> Ferdinand slammed into Imogen's head at a critical second, buying Carl just enough time.

He was launched away by the impact but survived. After the battle, he rejoined Carl's party — though the party noted he would leave when descending to the next floor (dungeon rules for NPC Province Bosses who don't belong to the crawl proper).

---

## Book 6 — NPC Home Team Co-Warlord

Carl's audience vote in Book 6 created the **NPC Home Team** with Juice Box and Ferdinand as co-warlords. Ferdinand's co-warlord appointment reflects his unique status: a real-world cat who somehow became embedded in the dungeon's NPC hierarchy.

---

## Book 7 — Faction Wars

Ferdinand operated as **"Warlord on High, Sir Ferdinand"** and was mayor of **Shanty Town** (a settlement that had grown around the city of Larracos). Carl temporarily traded Quan's bowler hat to Ferdinand for the mayorship of Shanty Town, giving the Princess Posse control of the surrounding territory.

**The forfeit:** At Justice Light's instruction (relayed through Juice Box), Ferdinand "left the realm" — jumping into the Nothing — which caused Team Retribution to forfeit at the end of Faction Wars. This was part of Justice Light's larger plan.

Ferdinand was then sent to **Floor 12** via the Zerzura teleportation that moved the city of Larracos, Shanty Town, and all dungeon NPCs to the Court of the Ascendency.
