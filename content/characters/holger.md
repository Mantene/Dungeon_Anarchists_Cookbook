# Holger

**Holger** is a Level 50 Were-Kin (were-castor/were-beaver) Elite who fights alongside [[tsarina-signet]] on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Profile & Traits
- **Background**: Formerly one of the peaceful **Chee** children under the care of [[nadine]], Holger's village was destroyed and he was transformed into a were-castor by the High-Elf princess [[imogen]]. 
- **Appearance**: In his Chee form, he is a pale-skinned, three-foot-tall warrior with a prominent **mullet** (described by Donut as "business in the front, party in the back"). He carries a sickle-shaped blade and a sharpened stick on his back.
- **Transformation**: Holger is a **were-castor** (were-beaver). In this form, he is covered in oily brown fur, has buck teeth, a lisp, and a flat black tail.
- **Combat**: Holger fights with high speed and durability, and is immune to the paralysis effect of night weasels. He can also sense magic spells being cast.

## Floor 6 Actions
- **Point Mongo Defense**: In [[161-book-5-chapter-16-were-beavers]] (Chapter 16), Holger and [[clint-smashgrab]] help Carl and Donut fight off night weasels and return to Point Mongo.
- **Fort Freedom Assault**: In [[170-book-5-chapter-25-future-huntress]] (Chapter 25), Holger participates in the assault. He is swallowed by [[claude-sludgington-the-fourth]] but vomited out alive when the boss is teleported to the shore.
- **Mourning Nadine**: Following the battle, Holger and the other were-castors mourn the deaths of Clint and Nadine, and assist Edgar with Nadine's tattoo memorial.

## Graphify Deductions
- **The Mullet of Dominance**: Holger's explanation that his mullet is a "sign of dominance" contrasts with the were-beaver form, which Donut calls "adorable" but "disgusting." The were-castor's ability to transition between their short Chee forms and beaver forms allows them to serve as construction units and agile fighters, showing the versatility of the transformed Chee elites.
