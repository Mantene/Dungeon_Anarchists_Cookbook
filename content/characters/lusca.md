# Lusca

Lusca was the Level 82 City Boss of Floor 5 (Air/Water Quadrant). She was a colossal Octo-Shark Brood Mother Queen.

## Biography & Combat
- **Special Event**: When the submarine *Akula* is swallowed by her in [[135-book-4-chapter-25-gelato-and-gills]], a televised Special Event broadcast is triggered in [[136-book-4-chapter-26-mouthbrooding-mayhem]].
- **Mechanics**: Lusca is a mouthbrooder whose mouth is filled with thousands of hungry Level 30 Juvenile Octo-Sharks that swarm anything inside.
- **Defeat**: In [[137-book-4-chapter-27-confronting-lusca]], [[carl]] uses a Potion of Bloodlust inside the submarine's bridge to make the baby octo-sharks eat each other. He then casts *Protective Shell* (static 12m sphere) while the shark swims at 35 km/h, peeling its top jaw off and exposing them to 1,500m depth pressure. [[katia-grim]] uses a pressurized steam propeller and a 25-foot buzzsaw ("Leatherface") to slice Lusca's brain, killing her.
