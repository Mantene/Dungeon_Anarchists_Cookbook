# Mongo

**Species:** Mongoliensis (velociraptor — Earth common name)  
**Party title:** Royal Steed  
**Owner/bonded:** [Princess Donut](princess-donut.md)  
**Level:** 40 (Book 6 end)

---

## Background

Pink-feathered velociraptor chosen by Donut from the Floor 2 pet reward room. Extremely hostile at first — bit everyone including Donut, required a gerbil cage. Bonded with Donut when she saved him from a Brindled Vespa. Food-motivated. Responds to "sic 'em."

Fully grown at Level 15. Donut calls herself "Mommy."

## Equipment

- Mongoliensis Saddle (Donut gets +15% spell damage when riding; Mongo gets +20% CON/damage)
- Enchanted fang caps (+15% movement speed, +15% STR)
- **Clockwork Triplicate**: Donut can summon two clockwork copies of Mongo in battle; they disappear when the battle ends

---

## Mate: Kiwi

Kiwi is a Level 60 Mongoliensis pack leader and the mother of Big Tina (an allosaurus who was changed from bear-form by Scolopendra). Kiwi's relationship with Mongo was traumatic for Mongo — Donut described his "innocence being savagely ripped away." Kiwi is now on Floor 9, having been sent there via the Zerzura teleportation along with the elf castle.
