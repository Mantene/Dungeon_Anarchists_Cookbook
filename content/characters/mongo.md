# Mongo

**Mongo** is a velociraptor (classified in-game as a *Mongoliensis*) who is [[princess-donut]]'s bonded pet and Royal Steed.

## Appearance & Characteristics
- **Book 3 Climax (Level 26)**: By the end of Floor 4, Mongo has reached **Level 26**. He is pony-sized, serving as an effective mount for Princess Donut.
- **Personality**: Aggressive, food-motivated, and fiercely loyal to Donut. He displays a child-like playfulness during downtime (such as licking blood off Carl and dancing in the blood spray after Katia's inventory purge).

## Gear & Abilities
- **Tracking Collar**: Highlights his orange dot on the crawl minimap.
- **Jump Attack & Earthquake Jump**: Basic combat and heavy landing jump attacks.
- **Enchanted Fang Caps of the Expectorating Tizheruk**: Incisor caps that increase bite damage by 50%, with poison/paralysis chances and electrical sparks on chomping.
- **Magical Pet Carrier**: Rubik's-cube-sized box used to carry and rest Mongo.

## Role in the Climax of Floor 4
- **Harvesting Gary's Hands**: Donut and Carl use Mongo to execute the resurrecting NPC [[growler-gary]] fourteen times, allowing Mongo to gain easy experience while harvesting Gary's hands for biometric cart keys.
- **Krakaren Combat**: Mongo acts as a frontline fighter against the Krakaren clones, helping defend Station 36.
- **Finding the Clones**: After defeating the Level 22 Razor Fox, Mongo sniffs out the first hatched Krakaren Crotch Dumplings.
- **God Encounter**: Mongo enters Trainyard E with Donut to defend Carl against the War God Grull.

## Graphify Deductions
- **Combat Evolution**: Mongo's rapid leveling (hitting Level 26) shows the benefits of pet-based experience sharing in high-stress zones. His physical growth and the addition of the Fang Caps make him a highly viable combat asset, rather than just a companion.
