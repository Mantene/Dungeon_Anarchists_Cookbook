# Mrs. McGibbons / Elle McGib

**Age:** 99 years old at dungeon start  
**Level:** ~47+ (Book 4); exact level at Book 6 end unknown  
**Race:** Selected in Book 3 — became a **fairy** (old woman transformed into powerful, diminutive winged being)  
**Class:** Frost Maiden → specialty: **Blizzardmancer** (ice/cold mage)  
**Leaderboard:** #7 in Book 4 (100,000 gold bounty)  
**Party:** Independent squad; allies with Carl and Donut's extended group  
**Calls Carl:** "Barry" (her late husband's name — occasionally)

---

## Background

Resident of the Meadow Lark eldercare facility. Wheelchair-bound pre-dungeon. Cheerfully inappropriate, sharp-witted, deeply in love with her deceased husband Barry. Carl saved her life during the Rage Elemental crisis on Floor 2.

On Floor 3, she chose the Fairy race — transforming her ancient body into a powerful, miniature winged creature. She has never looked back.

---

## Books 3–5

By Book 4, she is Level 47 and #7 on the leaderboard, having accumulated enormous XP. She fought alongside the Sea Quadrant group and killed **Ruckus the Cyborg Buzzard** (a major boss). She is genuinely dangerous — not a bystander anymore.

Her close friend **Gwendolyn Duet** died during the Butcher's Masquerade on Floor 6 (Book 5). Elle was devastated. She and Imani are both in the Meadow Lark/survivor alliance group.

---

## Book 6 — Floor 8

Elle's squad ended up in Belgium for Phase 2. She successfully obtained a key. She was present for the Havana battle during Phase 3, helping fight the demon/zombie/crab chaos. She has an ice staff that was a major upgrade.

She was kind to Samantha (warned Louis about Samantha's inappropriate crush, not out of jealousy but genuine protectiveness of him). Samantha described Elle as "a delight" and invited her to her imaginary bachelorette party.

Elle fought on the FOSCA building during the Faction Wars battle, with two totems helping clear demons.

---

## Relationships

- **Carl** — "Barry" when she forgets; genuine affection
- **Imani** — close ally since Book 1
- **Gwendolyn Duet** — dead (Book 5); Elle grieved her
- **Louis Santiago** — protective of him re: Samantha
- **Katia** — allied from Floor 6 onward

---

## Books 7–8

**Faction Wars (Book 7):** Elle fought with the Princess Posse forces on Floor 9. Confirmed among survivors to Floor 10.

**Floor 11 — Parade of Horribles (Book 8):** Elle and Imani's group were assigned the **"Betrayal"** float group. Rather than build an elaborate float, they carried handwritten signs: *"You are watching real people die"* and *"Fuck you"* — a deliberate act of refusal rather than spectacle. Imani and Elle together, the last of the original Meadow Lark group.

**Floor 12:** Confirmed among the 19 remaining crawlers on Floor 12.
