# Donar

**Donar** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A god of thunder and lightning, described as a 'manlier' version of Thor. He is worshipped by Jurgen, to whom he has granted several lightning spells, and is confirmed not to be a sponsored god this season.
