# Mordecai

**True race:** Skyfowl (golden eagle-like creature with folded wings)  
**Current dungeon form (Book 6):** Hills Cyclops — large, pink, bald, cone-shaped head, single eye the size of an ostrich egg, caterpillar eyebrow, wrinkly sausage fingers; sounds like Patrick Star. Previous forms: Rat Hooligan (Floor 1), Bugaboo (Floor 2), Grulke Infantry (Floor 3), Pocket Kuma (tiny fluffy bear, Floor 6), slime (Floor 7, had no arms for its 10 minutes)  
**Role:** Non-Combatant NPC Manager (permanently bound to Donut via Former Child Actor class)  
**Level:** 50

---

## Background

Mordecai is a former crawler from a different world who crawled to Floor 11 in his own dungeon season — the deepest anyone has reached except one human who hit Floor 13 and died within 30 minutes. Rather than risk Floor 12, he accepted an exit deal and became permanently indentured as a guildmaster NPC. His contract requires him to serve until one of his crawlers reaches Floor 4 (otherwise his indentureship extends to the next Borant season, which could be 7–8 seasons away given Borant's political situation).

He changes physical form with each new dungeon world, always taking the shape of a local mob type from the current floor. He has been preparing for Earth's dungeon since the 1960s, and knows human culture as well as any human.

He keeps a framed photo of his brother (in Skyfowl form) and his mother's ashes in his guild room. The ashes were disturbed by Donut on first meeting (she crapped in them).

He was trained by [Odette](odette.md) during her own crawl — see the **Odette/Uzzi revelation** below for the full history. His hatred of Odette is one of the most important unresolved threads in the series.

### The Brother — Exact Origin of the Hatred (Book 1 Raw Text)

This detail is established in Book 1 but easy to miss. In Ch. 29, discussing race and class selection:

> *"I almost chose a skyfowl class, something called a Storm Commander. But Odette talked me out of it. My brother ended up picking it instead, on her recommendation."*

And in the Book 1 Epilogue, Odette herself says about her history with Mordecai:

> *"I pushed him too hard, and he paid dearly for it."*

Read together: Odette steered Mordecai away from his species' native class (Storm Commander), then recommended his *brother* take it. The brother died. Mordecai's reaction to Odette's request that he seek her out: *"I would rather spend another 2,000 years in this room than exist in the same solar system as her. I'd rather meet a woman, sire children, and then devour those children than have anything to do with her again."*

The framed photo on his shelf is his brother — not just a decoration. His mother's ashes are also in that room. He is carrying both of his dead family members with him across every dungeon world he's forced to serve in. Odette's guilt over the brother is the engine behind her desire to reconcile.

## Book 1

### Floor 1 (Rat Hooligan form)
First NPC Carl encounters. Runs the tutorial guild. Explains the game mechanics: stats, skills, inventory, rating system, patron system, boss types. Warns Carl not to stay too long on each floor. Gives hint: "It's not worth it, not until Floor 12, and even then negotiate." Revealed meaning later: the floor 10–11 exit deals are bad; floor 12 deal is better.

Watched Donut eat the Enhanced Pet Biscuit and helped Carl understand her new abilities.

Key restricted information he couldn't say but hinted at:
- Race/class selection on Floor 3: listen for his clues even if he can't steer overtly
- The Krakaren boss's weakness (he couldn't say, but his questions helped Carl figure out the pattern: exploit the weakness)

### Floor 2 (Bugaboo form)
Transformed into a massive, terrifying Bugaboo — first time Carl almost punched him by accident.

Revealed (in whisper, off-camera): The Bloom (ultra-nationalist kua-tin "Party") now controls the kua-tin government. Borant is financially bankrupt. The Party is rushing the dungeon to end quickly. "The kua-tin government is working extra hard to get you killed."

His Floor 2 guild room had been cleaned and tidied by the time Carl and Donut returned after the Krakaren fight — suggesting he expected the visit and maybe cares more than he lets on.

At floor collapse: Mordecai is freed from his Floor 2 guildmaster position but re-bound as Donut's permanent manager. This actually extended his indentureship indefinitely.

## Books 3–5

Continues as Donut's manager across Floors 3–6. Forms change each floor. Deeply invested in Carl and Donut surviving. On Floor 4 (The Iron Tangle), he was working on a potion to extract Chris Andrews 2 from the Maggie My/Infiltrator possession — required "vile dill" as an ingredient. He has a grudge against **Chaco**, the bard from his own crawl group who killed his brother Uzzi by throwing the Dart of Ophiotaurus at him.

Notable: He was a former student of Odette's. She manipulated his entire crawl group. On Floor 6, he went from Grulke Infantry to **Pocket Kuma** (a tiny, adorable bear creature — he was deeply embarrassed about this). He fostered a growing affection for the changeling children he took in during Book 6.

## Book 6 — The Odette Revelation

> [!warning] Major backstory revealed
> Odette told Carl the truth about Mordecai's brother Uzzi:

During Mordecai's crawl, on the 10th floor, Odette was his manager and made an **illegal deal with Huanxin Jinx** (grixist heiress, CEO of Icon Industries) to rig the crawl. When Huanxin demanded more money, Odette broke the deal and orchestrated a trap using the summoning box. **Chaco**, the bard in Mordecai's party, threw the Dart of Ophiotaurus at **Uzzi** (Mordecai's brother) against his will, sacrificing him to summon and kill the goddess Dodola. Mordecai made it to Floor 11, but Uzzi died.

Mordecai tackled Chaco into the stairwell screaming. He does not yet know the full extent of Odette's guilt or her confession to Carl.

Mordecai's last message in the prologue: *"I am going to kill you, Odette. I am going to get free of this place, I am going to find where you are, and I am going to kill you."*

Odette entered the dungeon as the goddess **Nekhebit** during Book 6. When Mordecai finds out, Zev warns Carl it will be catastrophic — Mordecai will attack Odette on sight and lose his manager contract.

## Book 6 — The Foster Children

Mordecai secretly used money from Carl and Donut's treasury to buy an upgraded room in the personal space, and hired a **room attendant** — later revealed to be a companion for the **six changeling orphan children** he was secretly fostering (Ruby + five others). He let the children put makeup on him. He was embarrassed when Carl found out. A movie studio licensed the story (without Donut, who was furious about the licensing fee).

## Book 7

### Floor 9 — Canadian Human Form + The Surgery

Mordecai is now in **Canadian human form** (snowsuit, raccoon hat, thick glasses). He says "aboot" and gets mad when people point it out. Very excited about the alchemy table upgrades that let him analyze potions.

**Key contribution:** He performed the naiad organ transplant to save Louis — extracting Epitome Tagg's electronic heart and lungs, replacing them with Princess Lunette's naiad organs. He also installed a kua-tin rebreather into Louis's bronchial tubes to allow him to breathe. Louis now has gills.

**On Odette:** Mordecai did not encounter Odette in the dungeon during Floor 9 (she is on the 12th floor as Nekhebit). He loaded the bolt with his mother's ashes that Donut used to kill Huanxin Jinx. He said simply: "Thank you, Donut, for setting that up." He sat outside in the dirt, cigarette and Crown Royal, staring upward, after Huanxin died.

He continued to foster the changeling children (Ruby + others). Ruby transferred to the 12th floor via Zerzura.

## Book 8

### Floor 10 — Moon Reaper / Shadow Mimic / Animistic Spirit

Mordecai began Floor 10 in **Moon Reaper form** — but the form was glitching as a **Shadow Mimic**, copying any nearby shape and gaining Moon Reaper supernatural powers each time he was near a new species for the first time. This made him increasingly unpredictable to be around.

By the transition to Floor 11, Mordecai became an **Animistic Spirit** — a being that inhabits physical objects rather than existing in a body. His physical presence manifested as the kitchen table. He can communicate only via chat.

He presumably traveled with Carl and Donut to Floor 12 in this form — inhabiting some object in their possession.
