# Mordecai

**Mordecai** is a Non-Combatant NPC who serves as the designated guide, tutorial guildmaster, and manager for [[princess-donut]] and [[carl]].

## Background
Mordecai was originally a "skyfowl" from a previous world that was mined by the Syndicate. He survived down to the 11th floor of his crawl and chose to become a guide/guildmaster. As a Changeling, his physical form changes every floor, taking the shape of a common monster type:
- **Floor 1**: **Rat Hooligan** (Level 50)
- **Floor 2**: **Bugaboo** (Level 50)
- **Floor 3**: **Incubus** (Level 50)
- **Floor 4**: **Grulke Infantry** (Level 50) — A warty, slimy, mud-colored toad-like creature with hanging jowls and a throat pouch.
- **Floor 5**: **Rock Edge Skyfowl** (Level 50) — A cleric-build avian creature with clipped flight capabilities and no potion-brewing skills.
- **Floor 6**: **Pocket Kuma** (Level 50) — An adorable, knee-height, bipedal bear-capybara-sugar-glider hybrid with massive baby eyes, buck teeth, and a sugar-glider monkey tail. It has very low mobility, no combat capability, and struggles to reach doorknobs.


## Role as Manager & Floor 4 Suspension
Donut's class selection triggers **[[manager-benefit|the Manager Benefit]]**, binding Mordecai to her as manager. 
- **Floor 4 Banishment**: In Chapter 7 (ch-82), Mordecai flings a metal chair at the game show host [[chaco]] in a rage. He is immediately suspended and placed in a stasis stasis-timeout for seven days, depriving the party of their guide for almost all of Floor 4.

## Return from Suspension & Climax of Floor 4
Mordecai returns from stasis in Chapter 104 with only 1 day and 16 hours left. 
- **The Night Wyrm's Ring**: He reacts with horror when he sees the *Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering* on Carl's finger. He explains that faction leaders hunt down anyone holding one on Floor 6 to charge it for the Floor 9 Faction Wars. Frustrated, Mordecai smacks Carl on the head—violating his own promise to keep his temper. Carl reluctantly takes it off to sell on Floor 5.
- **Crafting Room Upgrades**: While the party sleeps, Mordecai upgrades the crafting room, adding a metalworking table, three benches, a second specialized alchemy table, and a **Bolt-Thrower's Workshop** to manufacture advanced bolts.
- **Tangle Map Analysis**: He helps identify that the named trains' loops form the Syndicate logo (representing galactic wormhole paths) and explains the political context (a Borant-sponsored racist cartoon targeting their rivals, the Plenty/Pooka, and the Krakaren Apothecary). He recalls a similar floor from his own season that spun on its axis, flipping everyone upside down.
- **Summoning Mechanics**: When Carl asks about summoning, Mordecai warns him not to summon the war god Grull but explains the rules: a deity can be "stolen" from one summoning to another, and their size scales to their containment vessel. Carl uses this advice to execute his re-summoning plan.

## Weakness & System Protection
Mordecai has a low alcohol tolerance. Under the manager agreement, if he dies in the dungeon, he is simply kicked from the game, and stands to earn a massive financial stipend if Donut survives past Floor 9.

## Past Connection to Odette
Odette was Mordecai's trainer during his crawl. She recommends he seek her out after his final guide tour. Mordecai is outraged, refusing any contact due to a past tragedy where his brother died after choosing a Storm Commander class based on Odette's recommendation.

## Embezzlement and Changeling Fostering
In Book 6, it is revealed that Mordecai has been embezzling 1,000 gold per day from the party's finances. He has been using these funds to foster changeling children—including Ruby—in safehouse slots to keep them safe from system recycling or deletion.

## Graphify Deductions
- **Veteran Cynicism vs. Hope**: Mordecai's warnings reflect the traditional survival strategy: keep your head down and avoid drawing the attention of the intergalactic elite (like the Faction Wars leaders). However, Carl's successes with high-risk plans force Mordecai to adapt, providing specialized alchemical and mechanical support (like the Bolt-Thrower's Workshop) to help the party survive these exploits.
