# GumGum

GumGum is a Level-5 Orc NPC who cleans the streets of the medium skyfowl settlement on Floor 3.

## Profile
GumGum is an older, muscular orc woman who wears a blood-stained shirt, making her look like a butcher. Despite her rough appearance and smell, she is kind-hearted. Her mother was a "lady of the night" who died alone from stab wounds, which gave GumGum a soft spot for the vulnerable women in town. 

GumGum discovers that the bodies of naked, bloodless prostitutes are being dumped in the alleys of the town every night. She attempts to recruit [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] to investigate the mystery, triggering the quest **The Sex Workers Who Fell from the Heavens**. Shortly after issuing the quest, GumGum is captured, tortured, and killed by the city elves of the [[201st-security-group|201st Security Group]] to extract information about Carl and Donut. Her chest is rent open, and she is left dead in an alley, where her body is found in Chapter 16 ([[065-book-2-chapter-16-alley-ambush]]).
