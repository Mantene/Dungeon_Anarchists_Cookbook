# Nami

**Nami** is a model and influencer sponsor who appears as a pilot/deity during the Faction Wars on the ninth floor in *[[dinniman-2024-tir|This Inevitable Ruin]]*.

## Appearance & Role
Nami is the sponsor of [[eris]], the goddess of chaos. She had extensive cosmic cosmetic surgery to make herself look and sound exactly like [[hekla]], the deceased former leader of the Walk of Shame team from the fifth floor. 

During the ninth floor, Nami originally stayed back from piloting Eris. However, following the approval of Sir Ferdinand's emergency action item, she was forced to inhabit and pilot the goddess's physical form on the battlefield, where she is heard screaming in panic and struggling to control Eris's chaotic laughter.
