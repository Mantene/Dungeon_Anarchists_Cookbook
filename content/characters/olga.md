# Olga

**Olga** is a Grulke (frog-like) driver and mercenary hired by [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] for the tenth-floor vehicle races in Book 8 (*A Parade of Horribles*).

## History & Dungeon Presence
Olga is first introduced as the driver of Carl and Donut's truck in [[440-book-8-chapter-41-twenty-seconds-to-start]]. She drives them through the jungle, but in [[442-book-8-chapter-43-ginger-chieftain]], she is knocked unconscious and receives the "Gingered" debuff from the Chieftain Great Rusty. 

In [[443-book-8-chapter-44-pit-stop-tragedy]], while still in a fugue state, she is carried out of the truck by [[finley]] at the Lost City of Upano pit stop. As they cross the street to find a temple/cleric, they are run over and killed by the speeding APV of Team One Fine Pig. Her corpse is later temporarily resurrected by Donut using *Second Chance* in [[447-book-8-chapter-48-satan-the-hedgehog]] to drive their truck.
