# Armita

**Armita** is a former crawler and a friend and former party member of [[odette]].

## Biography and Role

After her time as a crawler, Armita secured a position as a demigod attendant for the Celestial Ascendency. 

During the tenth-floor crisis of Mordecai's original party, Armita acted as the go-between, secretly communicating between Odette and [[huanxin-jinx]] (the Grixist heiress driving the storm goddess [[dodola]]) to negotiate help. When Huanxin raised her demands to 50% of the party's tenth-floor bonus and threatened to zero out the party, Odette rejected the deal. 

Instead, Odette ordered the sacrifice of [[uzzi]] to summon and kill Dodola. In response, Huanxin threatened to kill both Odette and Armita, starting with Armita. Since Armita had three seasons left on her indentureship contract, she was terrified of Huanxin's influence and power.
