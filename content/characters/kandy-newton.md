# Kandy "Bigs" Newton

**Kandy "Bigs" Newton** is a level 29 sluggalette (slug) crawler who is a member of the [[the-princess-posse]] during the ninth floor Faction Wars in *[[dinniman-2024-tir|This Inevitable Ruin]]*.

## Appearance & Personality
Bigs is roughly the size of a bulldog. She has little wisps of pink-dyed hair arranged in a circle around her round head, resembling a monk's tonsure. A row of metal piercings runs down the slide of her slimy head. Protruding straight up from the center of her head is a hatchet, which she uses as a primary weapon by swinging her head. 

She possesses a bloodthirsty, enthusiastic personality, frequently asking who she is supposed to "kneecap" or "chop" and expressing great excitement at the prospect of combat.

## Biography
During the assault on the temples and Club Vanquisher, Bigs managed to deliver the killing blow to [[princess-vinata]] (the Sepsis Whore of the Blood Sultanate) after the naga's immortality expired. Because of this kill, the Blood Sultanate was defeated, and its assets were forfeited to the Princess Posse.

Following this battle, she joined [[carl]]'s group as a sapper, helping him navigate the fields of the Madness and participating in the infiltration of the Madness castle.
