# Tally

Tally is a **Bopca Protector** (Level 63) who serves as the caretaker of the fast-food restaurant safe room on Floor 1.

## Role
Bopca Protectors are magical, gnome-like creatures whose sole purpose is to maintain safe rooms, prepare food, and clean. Tally speaks with a Slavic-like accent and smells like wet moss. Because of [[princess-donut]]'s high Charisma, Tally treats her as royalty, bowing to her and preparing special meals like salmon pâté. He makes delicious Murgh Vindaloo (spice level 4) for [[carl]].

## Background
Tally's world was visited by the Syndicate long ago. Rather than having the planet mined, his people agreed to enter the system as NPCs. Tally moves down the dungeon from safe room to safe room as the floors collapse.
