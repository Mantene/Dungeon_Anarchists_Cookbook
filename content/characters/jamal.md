# Jamal

**Jamal** is a giant, 17-foot-long hammerhead shark who originally existed as one of [[tsarina-signet]]'s paper tattoos. 

## Profile & Traits
- **Class & Level**: Level 72 beast.
- **Physical Form**: A massive hammerhead shark. Although he now breathes air and cannot breathe water despite having gills, he has lost his tattoo ability to fly and is forced to flop on the ground.
- **Mechanics & Equipment**:
  - He wears a voice-controlled automaton harness built by [[carl]] consisting of eight double-jointed legs, giving him a spider-like appearance.
  - He frequently breaks this prototype by attempting to "hop" (jump in the air), which he yearns to do but the joint mechanics cannot support upon landing.
  - He has lost his sense of smell, making him immune to scent-based attacks.
- **Personality**: Verbose, frequently speaking of himself in the third person ("Jamal has so many sorries..."), which deeply annoys [[princess-donut]]. He is a fierce apex predator with a history of consuming clowns and lemurs.

## History
After being materialized from Signet's tattoo form, Jamal joined the crawlers' efforts on the Ninth Floor. In Book 7, Chapter 15, he participated in clearing Skank Skunks, consuming the Skank Skunk Battle Wizard ("Fancy Boy") in a feeding frenzy.
