# Menerva

**Menerva** is a missing crawler from [[porthus]]'s original crawl who is mentioned in *[[dinniman-2024-tir|This Inevitable Ruin]]*.

## Background
Menerva was a crawler who participated in the same crawl as Porthus. Porthus survived his crawl but developed an unhealthy, cycle-long obsession with finding out what happened to her, searching for her across the galaxy. This search is mentioned by [[rosetta]] as a key driving force behind Porthus's actions.
