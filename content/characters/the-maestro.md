# The Maestro

The Maestro is a massive orc who hosts the exploitative intergalactic show *Death Watch Extreme Dungeon Mayhem*.

## Appearance & Background
The Maestro is a large, muscular orc (a larger, darker-skinned cousin to the tusklings, with bristly black hair). He has a gold left tusk, a line of earrings, and wears a hot pink silk shirt buttoned halfway to show his hairy chest and gold chains. 

He belongs to the royal family of the **Skull Empire** (or Skull Clan), one of the oldest and largest Syndicate systems. His family has won six of the last ten Faction Wars on the ninth floor. He is in his early twenties, arrogant, and sadistic, viewing crawlers as "savages" and "piglets."

## Role in the Crawl
In [[042-book-1-chapter-42-death-watch-extreme]], the Maestro forces [[li-jun]] and [[zhang]] to play a cruel game where they must guess the survival of other crawlers to earn "teleport points" to save their party members. 

When Carl objects to the cruelty, climbs onto the table, and verbally attacks the Maestro, the Maestro tries to regain control of his live broadcast. Carl goads him into teleposting Li Jun and Zhang back to the dungeon early, allowing them to escape. 

In [[043-book-1-chapter-43-pork-boy-incident]], the Maestro brings out [[frank-q]] and [[maggie-my]] to humiliate Carl. Out of spite, he gifts them a Legendary Skill Potion that raises their *Find Crawler* skill to level 15, allowing them to track Carl and Donut anywhere. 

A viral, sexually explicit fan-edit video called the "Pork Boy Snick" is released soon after, depicting the Maestro in a humiliating, submissive light with Carl. This makes the Maestro an intergalactic laughingstock (referred to as "Pork Boy" and "Carl's Naughty Little Piggie"). 

The Maestro vows revenge, stating he is traveling to Earth orbit and will appear on the ninth floor as the **War Leader Maestro of the Skull Clan** during the Faction Wars.
