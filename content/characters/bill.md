# Bill

**Bill** is a antagonist character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

Bill is Marjory Williams' abusive ex-partner, mentioned repeatedly in a simulated memory or "what-if" scenario in apartment 231. He is described as having violated a restraining order, drained Marjory's bank account, physically assaulted her, and repeatedly harmed her cat, Gravy Boat. Marjory expresses reluctance to report him, citing his claim of having found a job.
