# Sergeant Toyotomi

**Sergeant Toyotomi** is a bipedal rhinoceros mercenary and a former NPC of the Prism faction (led by Empress D'Nadia). In Book 7, he allies with Team Retribution and the Princess Posse.

## Biography & Role in Book 7

Sergeant Toyotomi is a skilled fighter and builder. During the Faction War Games on Floor 9, he helped direct last-minute construction at the Shanty Town base. He worked alongside [[li-na]], [[li-jun]], and [[zhang]] to construct a specialized clocktower synergy tower, which was used to channel and extend Li Na's aura abilities as part of the raid on the Blood Sultanate. Toyotomi's squad was also tasked with building a very tall, expensive synergy tower named "The Nest" near the hole leading down into Larracos for the next phase of the games.
