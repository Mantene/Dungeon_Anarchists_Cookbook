# Sadir

Sadir is a Null alien walk-on bounty hunter. Because he is a Null, he faces discrimination and lacks council race privileges, leading him to take high-risk surface bounties on Earth to support his children in the Filt system.

## History & Actions
- **Hunting Gravy Boat**: In [[145-book-4-epilogue-epilogue]], Sadir and his partner [[gennrik]] infiltrate the surface ruins of Seattle (specifically the New Queen Anne settlement) to capture the orange cat [[gravy-boat]] (Ferdinand) for a Borant bounty.
- **Ambush**: While cutting into the tent of [[beatrice]] and [[brad]], Gennrik is shot and killed by [[lexis]], and Sadir is captured.
- **Frame-up**: Under [[odette]]'s direction, Lexis shoots Sadir in the leg with a flechette gun and frames him for Gennrik's death and Brad's murder. Odette plans to buy out his warrant to guarantee his future cooperation.
