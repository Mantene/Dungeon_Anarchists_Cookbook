# Onikuma

**Onikuma** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A large bear who serves as a mount for Rosetta. In chapter 497, Onikuma actively participated in the chaotic arena combat, jumping onto groups of razor-backed monkey mobs. After a massive explosion, Rosetta was seen dismounted, but Onikuma's specific status was not detailed.
