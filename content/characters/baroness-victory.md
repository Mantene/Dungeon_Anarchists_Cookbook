# Baroness Victory

**Baroness Victory, Esquire** is an Orc attorney and judge who serves as the designated Faction Wars adjutant for the Princess Posse.

## Biography and Role

Baroness Victory was formerly associated with the Skull Empire and is the half-sister of the late Queen Consort Ugloo. She has an image of an hourglass carved onto one of her tusks. She is described as a humorless, matter-of-fact attorney and judge who is highly experienced in battle arbitration.

In Book 6, Chapter 36, she is presented as one of three candidates for the team's adjutant role. Despite Carl having killed several of her relatives (including her nephews and brother-in-law) during the Hunting Grounds, she asserts her absolute impartiality, noting she recently sentenced her own aunt to indentureship for tax evasion. On the strong recommendation of Carl's attorney [[quasar]]—who points out that the other factions actively sued to keep her out of the game—Carl selects her as their adjutant.
