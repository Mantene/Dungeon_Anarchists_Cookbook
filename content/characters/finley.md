# Finley

**Finley** is a Porsuk (badger-like) mercenary hired by [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] for the tenth-floor vehicle races in Book 8 (*A Parade of Horribles*).

## History & Dungeon Presence
Finley serves on Carl and Donut's racing team, assisting in the cab of the truck. He has a close bond with his fellow mercenaries, [[olga]] and [[quemada]], referring to them as his family. In [[441-book-8-chapter-42-forest-flight]], he uses a gentle sedative claw pinch to put Quemada to sleep after she suffers a severe panic attack.

In [[443-book-8-chapter-44-pit-stop-tragedy]], Finley attempts to carry the unconscious Olga and Quemada to a temple in the Lost City of Upano for healing. Shortly after stepping onto the street, all three mercenaries are run over and killed by the speeding APV of Team One Fine Pig.
