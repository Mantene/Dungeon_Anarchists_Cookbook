# Quan Ch

Quan Ch is a half-elf crawler who is a Level 48 **Imperial Security Trooper**. He is a highly competitive, opportunistic crawler who frequently steals kills and plays dirty.

## Floor 3
During the Floor 3 event quest (**The Fools Who Broke the Glass**), he joins the group chat and insults Carl and Donut, calling them "fucking assholes" for triggering the disaster. Donut promptly blocks him from the chatroom. Quan Ch survives the magical blast despite being severely injured. Because he survives, he receives the **Bandit** achievement and a Celestial Quest Box. He opens it on live television, receiving a glowing robe that grants him wisp-like wings, flight, wisp-shield, and a blue-glowing left hand capable of high-powered lightning attacks.

## Floor 5 (Air Quadrant)
During the Floor 5 World Quest **Get Orthrus**, which offers 1 million gold for killing the giant puppy [[orthrus]] in [[140-book-4-chapter-30-world-quest-party]], Quan Ch attempts to snipe the dog in [[141-book-4-chapter-31-a-deposit-on-quan]] to claim the bounty and kill Princess Donut. 

Carl and Donut engage him using their biplane *Nightmare II*. Carl throws a hobgoblin disco ball to stick to his wisp-shield, and Donut fires the machine gun and magic missiles, followed by Carl remote-detonating a hob-lobber to knock him out of the sky. After Carl invokes *Divine Intervention* by worshipping [[emberus]], he tackles Quan Ch. When his invulnerability expires, Quan Ch attempts to rocket-fly away, but Carl grabs his glowing left arm and rips it entirely off at the shoulder. Quan Ch flees to the Floor 6 stairwell missing his arm and three rings (which Carl loots).
