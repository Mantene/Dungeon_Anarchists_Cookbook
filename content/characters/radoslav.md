# Radoslav

**Radoslav** is a bugbear racer NPC on the tenth floor of the dungeon.

## Biography and Role

Radoslav is a Level 75 Jack of All Trades and a member of **Team Free Love**, along with [[jasha]] and the deceased Alevtina. In [[403-book-8-chapter-4-team-free-love]], their red Pontiac minivan crashes during Heat One, killing Alevtina. Radoslav and Jasha explain to [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] that they have recovered memories of their past lives and former crawls, and they are volunteering in the races to win their freedom or finally die. Carl and Donut tow their van to the finish line to keep them alive and avoid crawler-versus-crawler matchups.

In [[411-book-8-chapter-1-hungry-eyes-roundabout]], Radoslav welcomes Carl and Donut to the roundabout, offering them beer and sharing details about their never-ending cooler.
