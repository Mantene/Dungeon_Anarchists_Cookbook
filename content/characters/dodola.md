# Dodola

**Dodola** is a sponsored storm goddess in the dungeon.

## Biography and Role

Dodola is sponsored and controlled by the Grixist heiress [[huanxin-jinx]]. 

During the tenth-floor climax of Mordecai's original crawl, Dodola was summoned alongside the larger, AI-controlled storm deity [[adad]]. Dodola quickly teleported Adad away and began marching to zero out the crawler party due to [[odette]] rejecting Huanxin's demands. 

To survive, [[chaco]] worshipped Dodola and stabbed [[uzzi]] with the [[dart-of-ophiotaurus]], forcing Dodola to be summoned inside a *Circle of Containment* field. Confined to a small space and temporarily lacking deity immunity, Dodola was destroyed when a level-20 lightning spell exploded inside the containment shield. Her death yielded the key to the eleventh-floor stairwell.
