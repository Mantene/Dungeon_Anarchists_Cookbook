# Clammy the Clown

Clammy the Clown is a Level 9 round clown mob working as a guard at the main entrance of Grimaldi's Traveling Circus.

## Background
Originally a human boy named Clammy who joined the circus for escape, he was transformed after the collapse into a giant, portly clown. He is extremely wide, wearing a dirty pink and blue gown with pointed teeth, thick grease paint, and long badger-like fingernails. His belly rumbling indicates active parasites within his flesh.

## Interaction with Carl
In Chapter 59, Carl uses a "Big Top Ticket" to bypass Clammy, who sniffs the ticket and allows Carl to enter the main big top tent.
