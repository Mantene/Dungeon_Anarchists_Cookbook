# Hellik

**Hellik** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A deity, brother to Taranis, Emberus, and Eris. He was part of a planned succubus storyline on the seventh floor that did not come to fruition.
