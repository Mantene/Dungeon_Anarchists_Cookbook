# Shi Maria

**Shi Maria**, also known as the **Bedlam Bride**, is a spider-like demigoddess and a powerful pulpmancer mage residing in Havana, Cuba on Floor 8.

## Biography and Role

Shi Maria resides in a massive, sticky web covering a swamp near the mouth of the big bay on the southern coast of the Cuban starting region. 

In Chapter 8, [[samantha]] (the floating severed head) flies out to scout, gets captured, and is eaten by Shi Maria. From inside her stomach, Samantha reports that Shi Maria is lonely, is looking for her husband, and is a rare pulpmancer mage who claims she can transfer Samantha's spirit into a physical body. Samantha begs Carl and Donut to travel south to rescue her and negotiate with the demigoddess.
