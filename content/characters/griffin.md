# Griffin

**Griffin** is a white-skinned, sharp-toothed "Dream elf" former crawler who tapped out at the start of the tenth floor ("tenner") in a prior season of the crawl.

## Profile & Traits
- **Class & Level**: Level 60 Eviscerator.
- **Appearance**: White, pigment-free skin with sharp, goblin-like teeth. He has a large neck tattoo of a flaming, human-cat hybrid skull with the words "Princess Posse or Death" and their unit, "The Tenner Wraiths / 103rd Recon Legionnaires".
- **Weaponry**: Utilizes a collection of throwing knives.
- **Personality**: Exceedingly loud, talkative, and eager to fight, acting like a naive teenager who joined the military.

## History
Griffin and his friend [[morales]] exited their crawl as "tenners" a season or two before Rosetta's. They returned to participate in the Ninth Floor Faction Wars, volunteering to fight for the Princess Posse. During a hunt for Skank Skunk mobs in Book 7, Chapter 15, Griffin was sprayed by the skunks' aerosol attack, receiving the "Skanked" debuff and vomiting extensively.
