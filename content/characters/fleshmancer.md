# The Fleshmancer

**The Fleshmancer** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A highly skilled, albeit eccentric, human magic-user specializing in flesh manipulation. He is currently bound as a mercenary to Carl and Donut's guild and is observed brushing Donut before their performance. He is identified as Grigori.
