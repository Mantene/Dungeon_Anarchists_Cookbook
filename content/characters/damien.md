# Damien

**Damien** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

An employee of the Dungeon showrunners, Damien is described as a behind-the-scenes worker who is consistently present, regardless of who is managing the season. In Chapter 478, he temporarily replaces Zev as the system announcer, delivering critical updates regarding the final heat, the Outreach Guild, and the controversial Pineapple Cabaret exploit. He strongly encourages crawlers to take a deal, emphasizing the danger of the upcoming heat.
