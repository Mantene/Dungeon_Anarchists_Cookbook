# Heidi

**Heidi** is a crawler and the pregnant wife of [[jurgen]].

## Description
Heidi entered the dungeon while pregnant and subsequently disappeared, being moved to the surface "kinder facility" alongside the other children and pregnant women. Her husband, Jurgen, refuses to take a deal and remains in the crawl, vowing to protect [[lucia-mar]] because the children's fates (including Heidi and their unborn child) are tied to Lucia's survival due to a "dead man's switch" glitch.
