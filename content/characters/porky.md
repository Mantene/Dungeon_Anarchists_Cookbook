# Porky

**Porky** is a half-mantaur NPC and the guildmaster of the Stage Performance Guild on the tenth floor of the dungeon in Book 8 (*A Parade of Horribles*). He is the left half of [[corcunda]] (Corky).

## Biography & Dungeon Presence
Porky was split from Corky by a *Split Personality* spell. He wears a white latex body-containment suit covered in zippers to keep his organs from spilling out, and stands on a single foot. Unlike his right half, Porky is completely conscious and speaks with a muffled, deep voice. He lost his devotion to the god Grull upon being split and lived a lonely existence in the Stage Performance Guild theater.

In [[458-book-8-chapter-finding-porky]], Carl enters the guild and helps Porky free himself after his suit zipper gets caught on a piece of protruding flesh. Carl explains Corky's situation and the fusion plan, and Porky agrees to help, hoping to get reunited. However, Porky reveals a deep-seated anger toward the stripper [[dong-quixote]] for causing their splitting in the first place.

In [[460-book-8-chapter-unity-and-real-gs]], Porky and Dong Quixote have an awkward reunion in the safe room, discussing a red scarf that Corky had kept and passed to Porky.
