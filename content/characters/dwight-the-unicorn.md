# Dwight the Unicorn

**Dwight the Unicorn** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

Dwight is a unicorn and a key member of Team Sparkles, sharing a vehicle made of vines with Lucienne. He is highly temperamental and frequently engages in verbal confrontations with other teams, notably Masterplan. In this chapter, he uses a powerful, one-time weapon embedded in his team's vehicle to destroy Team Girth the Trouble's car, eliminating them from the race.
