# Bucket Boy

**Bucket Boy** is a crocodilian NPC and low-tier healer who formerly worked as an attendant at the Penis Parade strip club. 

## Profile & Traits
- **Race**: Crocodilian.
- **Appearance**: A young crocodilian covered in scales.
- **Role**: He was hired along with other Penis Parade staff as Faction Wars mercenaries/workers using a "Meat Shields" coupon. On the Ninth Floor, he acts as a low-tier healer and medical assistant in the field hospital under [[imani-c]].
- **Personality**: Nervous and easily frightened, especially regarding dangerous medical procedures or high-level entities.

## History
In Book 7, Chapter 16, Bucket Boy assists [[mordecai]] and [[imani-c]] as they prepare [[carl]] and [[katia-grim]] for their Glory Bound drug treatments. He expresses fear that Carl's head might explode due to the possession-clearing *Removal Order* potion. He is slated to run the field hospital's healing ward once open hostilities begin.
