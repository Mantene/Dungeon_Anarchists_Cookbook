# Porthus

**Porthus** is a High Elf rogue crawler who survived his crawl, became the author of the **Second Edition** of the *Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook*, and acts as an alien sponsor in the Pacifist network.

## Biography and Role

- **Crawl History**: Porthus reached the start of the 11th floor during his season, where he took a survival deal. The 10th floor was a nightmare of sentient, consuming objects. Porthus panicked and took the exit portal, leaving his friend Menerva behind with her foot stuck.
- **Indentured Servitude**: Following his exit, Porthus worked as a Game Guide for 100 seasons, surviving his indentureship and accumulating significant wealth.
- **Sponsor Role**: Operating outside the dungeon, Porthus joined Dr. [[dr-p-hu]]'s sponsor network to support Carl and Donut, seeking to disrupt the Syndicate.
- **Current Status**: In Book 7, Porthus is missing and wanted by Syndicate Security.

## Cookbook Contribution
Porthus was the author of the Second Edition of the *Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook*. He wrote entries in the Cookbook (seen in [[144-book-4-chapter-34-a-clash-of-demons]]), expressing guilt over his panic at the exit portal and a desire to help future crawlers survive.
