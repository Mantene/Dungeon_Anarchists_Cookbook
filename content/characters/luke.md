# Luke

**General Luke** was a Lemig Goblin and a general in the Democratic Sortion faction during the Ninth Floor Faction Wars.

## Profile & Traits
- **Race**: Lemig Goblin.
- **Level**: 64.
- **Status**: Deceased.
- **Role**: General in the Democratic Sortion. He had a close personal relationship with Commander [[stockade]].

## Biography & Death
In Book 7, Chapter 17, Luke was present in Queen Imogen's private ballroom when the Democratic Sortion attempted a ceasefire-suppressed possession attack on [[carl]]. The plan backfired, and Carl (possessed by [[shi-maria]]) went on a rampage. Luke's legs were torn off, and he was shot and killed by a fellow soldier under Shi Maria's control. His death deeply devastated Commander Stockade.
