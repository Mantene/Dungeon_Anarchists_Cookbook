# Warlord Akuma

**Warlord Akuma** is the leader of the War Mage Rebellion on the ninth floor (Faction Wars) and co-leader alongside [[agatha]].

## Profile & Actions
- **Appearance**: An older elf with mottled, sun-damaged skin. He is a Level 100 War Mage.
- **Faction Wars**: Akuma leads the War Mage Rebellion, which enters Faction Wars late, kills Prince Stalwart, and steals the Gate of the Feral Gods.
- **Sole Warlord**: After Agatha abandons her post, the system names Akuma the sole Warlord of the War Mage Rebellion.
- **Agatha's Threat**: He contacts Carl in [[386-book-7-chapter-78-akumas-warning]] to warn him that Agatha is a threat to everyone and has fled to the 10th floor. He offers to cooperate if Carl kills her.
- **Retrieval of the Daughter**: Akuma reveals that his faction successfully located and retrieved the "Scavenger's Daughter" from the castle of Larracos before leaving the realm.
