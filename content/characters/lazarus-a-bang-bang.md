# Lazarus-A-Bang-Bang

Lazarus-A-Bang-Bang is a Level 140 Unique combo totem card created by Donut by combining Uzi Jesus and Asojano. He appears as a robed, blindfolded man with a crown of thorns wielding a magical bazooka/RPG that fires metallic flesh-devouring beetles. He acts like an 80s action hero, calls opponents 'pansies', and retains memories of both source totems.
