# Emir

**Emir** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A deceased Zebani crawler, who Carl assumes was the father of Burcu. Burcu had previously accused Carl of killing her father.
