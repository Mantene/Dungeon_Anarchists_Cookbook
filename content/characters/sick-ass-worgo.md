# Sick-Ass Worgo

**Sick-Ass Worgo** (also referred to simply as **Worgo**) is a level 53 sluggalette sapper who appears during the Faction Wars on the ninth floor in *[[dinniman-2024-tir|This Inevitable Ruin]]*.

## Role in the Crawl
Worgo is a procedurally generated sluggalette sapper who carries a baseball bat dangling from the side of her neck. She possesses fine dexterity with her mouth, which she uses to set manual tripwires. 

During the trap construction phase, the changeling [[jessica]] ambushed the real Worgo in a side tunnel and assumed her identity to infiltrate the sapper squad. [[justice-light]] detected the swap immediately due to neural implant measurements of body temperature, weight, and hormonal emissions.
