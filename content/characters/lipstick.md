# Lipstick

**Lipstick** was a male gremlin driver for **Team One Fine Pig** during the tenth-floor vehicle races in Book 8 (*A Parade of Horribles*).

## History & Dungeon Presence
Lipstick was responsible for maintaining and driving the vehicle for Tigrans [[dario]] and [[nico]]. In [[444-book-8-chapter-45-carls-rage]], after their vehicle hit Carl's mercenaries, Carl confronted them. Lipstick cowered in the back of the garage and pleaded for Carl to stop. When the vehicle's electric shield activated to repel Carl, it pinned Lipstick against the back wall and barricade of the garage, causing him to explode.
