# Raul

**Raul** (full title: **Raul the Meditating Crab**) is a Level 70 Cloud Rank Warrior land crab who is captured and used as a T'Ghee totem card by the Princess Posse.

## Biography and Role

Raul is a land crab who parody's Xianxia/Wuxia cultivation tropes. He is first encountered in Book 6 Chapter 24, meditating atop a U-Haul truck in Havana, chanting chi cultivation self-help mantras and refusing to submit to his rivals, the Monk Seals.

In Chapter 25, Carl and Donut ambush and defeat him using phase potions and silence spells. He is converted into a Common (green) T'Ghee card totem. Raul is highly subservient to Donut, frequently groveling and shouting about chi. He has a decapitation spell called *Bris*. Raul has an ongoing, bitter conflict with the monk seal [[geraldo]]; in mock battles, Raul cowed to [[shi-maria]], who used him as a club, leading Geraldo to mockingly call him "the spider lady's jinetera."
