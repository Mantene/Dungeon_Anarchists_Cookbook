# Tserendolgor

**Tserendolgor** is a crawler participating in the Floor 4 crawl (The Iron Tangle).

## Biography & Role in the Crawl
Tserendolgor is a Level 27 **Dog Soldier** of the **Crisper** class. She physically resembles a walking, talking German Shepherd and wears a Vietnam-era combat helmet with holes cut out for her ears. 

During the defense of Station 36 in Chapter 106, she operates a magical flamethrower to burn incoming Krakaren clones. When [[princess-donut]] accuses her of stealing an experience kill, Tserendolgor replies, *"Yeah, go to a different position, cat,"* sparking a brief, territorial argument.

## Floor 5 (Air Quadrant)
In [[138-book-4-chapter-28-extractions-and-sun-shrines]], Tserendolgor and her group of 160 crawlers are trapped in a bubble that is targeted by the blind fire god [[emberus]], who mistakes her Dog Soldier appearance for his lost two-headed puppy [[orthrus]]. The bubble heats up dangerously, but after Carl teleports Orthrus out of the bubble in [[140-book-4-chapter-30-world-quest-party]], she pops her bubble by inserting the final gem and escapes down the stairs. As a reward for the quest completion, she receives an upgrade for her flamethrower, and someone in her party gets a *Heal Party* tome.
