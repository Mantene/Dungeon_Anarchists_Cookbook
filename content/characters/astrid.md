# Astrid

**Astrid** is a Level 125 Bloodlust Sprite NPC who represents the management of the Desperado Club.

## Biography and Role
Astrid is a high-level fairy-like creature (Bloodlust Sprite) with a deep, serious voice that contrasts with her delicate appearance. 

She appears in Chapter 84 following a security breach inside the Desperado Club members-only section, where an assassin killed crawler Ji-Hoon. Astrid hovers over Princess Donut and Carl to personally apologize on behalf of the club's management for the "inexcusable failure." She refunds Donut the 400 gold entrance fee and promises that she and Carl will receive complimentary security within the club in the future.

## Attributes
- **Race**: Bloodlust Sprite
- **Level**: 125
- **Occupation**: Desperado Club Manager/Representative
