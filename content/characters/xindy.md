# Xindy

**Xindy** is a Level 61 Mantis hunter and a Blood Tracker of the [[the-dark-hive]] faction. She is the younger sister of [[vrah]].

## Death in Zockau
- **The Hunt**: During Carl's preemptive raid on Zockau in [[150-book-5-chapter-5-left-to-fester]] (Chapter 5), Xindy flies above the city and casts a flare spell to locate the invisible crawlers.
- **The Debuff**: Carl uses his toe ring's *Super Spreader* ability to infect Xindy with the *Left to Fester* debuff, linking her life force to that of the marked hunter [[chin-dua]].
- **The Kill**: Carl fires a volley of homing missiles at Xindy. Although she activates a shield, the continuous missile strikes shatter it, causing her to explode and crash to the ground. Her severed mantis claw is collected by Carl as a hunting trophy.
