# HeyZoos

**HeyZoos (Uzi Jesus)** is a Level 140 Unique (orange) T'Ghee totem card minion utilized by the Princess Posse.

## Biography and Role

HeyZoos is an undead, ranged healer who wields an Uzi submachine gun and speaks with a Texan accent. He was originally owned by the crawler [[florin]], who was unable to control him due to his low charisma (HeyZoos actually shot Florin in the stomach). Florin gave the card to Princess Donut.

HeyZoos is highly volatile, childish, and gaslighting, with only a 50% chance of obeying direct commands. In combat, he lands in a flash of angelic music and immediately fires his weapon. During mock battles, he refused to resurrect [[geraldo]] (claiming Geraldo was "being a pansy" or "might be an atheist") and once accidentally shot himself in the shoulder.
