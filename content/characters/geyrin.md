# Geyrun

**Geyrun** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

Son of the deity Emberus, Geyrun is central to the current conflict as Carl has promised to reveal his killer. While Carl publicly claims responsibility to provoke Emberus, he secretly admits he doesn't know who truly killed Geyrun and plans to uncover the truth later.
