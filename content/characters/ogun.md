# Ogun

**Ogun** is the Yoruban deity (Orisha) of blacksmiths, iron, and warfare. He is a prominent deity within the Celestial Ascendency.

## Biography and Role

Ogun was the only Orisha deity who successfully ascended to the Halls of Ascendency on his own, maintaining a fresh following across the galaxy. He is a harsh, possessive, and grumpy deity who opposes the resurrection of his old family members (the other Orishas), wanting to keep them forgotten. 

Several crawlers on Floor 8 worship him, including [[paz-lo]] from Team Sister Ines. In Chapter 15, the dungeon system warned that failing to repair Ogun's shrine would result in a divine smite. Nevertheless, Carl and Team Sister Ines chose to repair [[yemaya]]'s shrine instead. As a direct consequence of this choice and Yemaya's ascension, Ogun physically manifested in the Havana region at the end of Chapter 15, terrifying his worshippers.
