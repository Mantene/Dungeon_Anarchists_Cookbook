# Vernon

**Vernon** was a Level 32 Dwarf train conductor NPC on the fourth floor of the World Dungeon. He is deceased.

## Biography and Role
Vernon served as the conductor of the Yellow Line train on the fourth floor (The Iron Tangle). He is introduced when Carl, Donut, and Katia slip into the conductor's compartment (Car 5) of the Yellow Line subway. Initially grumpy and insisting on "employees only" rules, Vernon is quickly swayed by Princess Donut's high Charisma and celebrity status.

Vernon provides vital information to Mordecai, helping him map out the stations along the Yellow Line. He also shares the limitations of his own existence: he reveals that he has no memories of what happens after his train pulls into Terminus Station 435; his memory gets "fuzzy," and he suddenly finds himself back at the starting railyard (Station 10) to begin the loop again, completely unaware of how much time has passed. Vernon carries a drawing of his dwarf wife and mentions saving up money to buy her a new house.

## Death
In Chapter 80, Princess Donut attempts to open one of her prize suitcases in Car 5. The suitcase is trapped, releasing a swarm of literal fire ants that immediately engulf the compartment. The drawings of Vernon's wife catch fire, and Vernon is incinerated by the ants. His final, terrified screams echo in the tunnel as the train speeds away, leaving the party behind on the platform.
