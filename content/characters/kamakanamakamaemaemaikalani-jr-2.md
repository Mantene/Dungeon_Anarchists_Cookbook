# Kamakanamakamaemaemaikalani Jr. 2

**Kamakanamakamaemaemaikalani Jr. 2** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A crawler with an exceptionally long Hawaiian name, often referred to by his nickname, Makana. He is mentioned by Donut and confirmed by Prepotente to still be an active participant in the Dungeon and is described as a pleasant individual.
