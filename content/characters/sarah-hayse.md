# Sarah Hayse

**Sarah Hayse** is a human crawler who holds the class of Hexcrafter Paladin.

## Actions
- **Chapter 491**: Sarah is introduced as part of the "Destruction" parade float team on the eleventh floor, partnering with [[makana]].
- **Chapter 497**: During the arena battle, Sarah stands out of the hatch of their Abrams tank and casts a spell to make the main gun glow black, blasting Krakaren Prime.
- **Chapter 499**: The tank is crushed by a Krakaren tentacle, killing Sarah and three other crew members.
