# Architect Houston

**Architect Houston** is the Viceroy leader of Team Madness during the Ninth Floor Faction Wars.

## Profile & Traits
- **Race**: Viceroy (a species that conceals its true demonic face—resembling a mix of a Predator and a lipless goblin—behind smooth white masks).
- **Appearance**: Wears long, pristine white robes resembling papal attire and a featureless white mask adorned with a small red teardrop in the center.
- **Personality**: Stoic, quiet, and deeply calculating. He rarely speaks in public, maintaining an unreadable facade.

## History
During the Ninth Floor Ceasefire (Book 7, Chapter 18), Houston colluded with Sortion Warlord [[stockade]] and external observers to sabotage [[carl]]'s Glory Bound treatment. Their objective was to use a *Summon Ally* spell to force Carl's teleportation, lock him in a vegetative state, and unleash [[shi-maria]] on the Princess Posse. 

Following the failure of the plan—which resulted in the slaughter of fifty Sortion soldiers—Houston attended the adjudication in Queen Imogen's kitchen. He remained silent and motionless during the proceedings. In the midst of the meeting, the ceasefire began to unravel as enemy movements on the borders prompted Carl to declare a Code Red.
