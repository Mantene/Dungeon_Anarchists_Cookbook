# Maggie My

**Maggie My** is a hostile crawler and major antagonist. 

## Background
Before the dungeon collapse, Maggie My was a Seattle PD detective. She divorced [[frank-q]] five years prior. She was highly protective of their teenage daughter [[yvette]] and blamed Frank for their daughter's issues. On the night of the collapse, they were in a sheriff's parking lot arguing over Yvette when the world ended.

## Role in the Crawl
### Floor 1
Maggie and Frank teamed up on Floor 1, accumulating five crawler kills. During their attempt to ambush Carl and Donut, they forced Yvette to wear the **Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering** and mark Carl. When Carl escaped into a safe room and Yvette was mutilated by Carl's dynamite trap, Yvette could not heal due to the ring's curse. Maggie choked Yvette to death to end her agonizing pain and clear the mark so they could heal.

### Floor 2 to 4
Following Yvette's death, Maggie and Frank had a bitter falling out. Maggie blocked Frank on her interface and separated from him. Maggie received a legendary skill potion from the Maestro on Floor 2 that maxes out the **Find Crawler** skill. According to Frank, she planned to wait until selecting a class that would allow the skill cap to rise to 20 rather than 15.

She is still active in the dungeon, and her obsession with avenging Yvette by killing Carl makes her a highly dangerous, unseen threat.

## Graphify Deductions
- **Symmetrical Vengeance**: Unlike Frank Q who has collapsed into self-destructive despair, Maggie's reaction to Yvette's death is externalized as a relentless, "biblical" pursuit of Carl. Her background as a Seattle PD detective makes her an efficient tracker, especially when paired with the Maestro's legendary "Find Crawler" skill potion.
