# Maggie My

**Race:** Infiltrator (allows full body takeover of a willing or incapacitated host)  
**Class:** Unknown  
**Level:** 9 (Book 1); higher by Book 4  
**Skulls:** 5 (Book 1)  
**Partners:** Frank Q (ICE agent partner)  
**Sponsors:** Prince Maestro (Skull Empire); Crown Prince Stalwart (Skull Empire)  
**Status:** Presumed dead — separated from Chris Andrews 2 by the Floor 5 flood

An ICE agent who became the series' most insidious antagonist by spending two books invisibly controlling Chris Andrews 2's body while hunting Carl.

---

## Background

Maggie My was a US Immigration and Customs Enforcement agent before the Transformation, entering the dungeon with 5 player-killer skulls — a high count that signaled her willingness to murder other crawlers. She was partnered with **Frank Q** (3 skulls).

---

## Book 1 — Safe Room Ambush

Frank Q and Maggie My attempted to ambush Carl and Donut in a safe room. Safe-room rules froze them. Carl booby-trapped a rat corpse with lit dynamite, leaving it for them.

They survived, and acquired the **Find Crawler** skill upgrade to track Carl and Donut.

---

## Books 1–4 — The Infiltration

Between the end of Floor 3 and the events of Book 4, Maggie used her **Infiltrator race ability** to take over **Chris Andrews 2's body**. Chris had separated from Carl's party at the end of Floor 3 after a falling out with his brother Brandon. Alone and isolated, he was vulnerable.

For two books, Maggie inhabited Chris's body completely. Chris was conscious but totally powerless — a prisoner inside himself.

She was sponsored by both **Prince Maestro** and **Crown Prince Stalwart** of the Skull Empire, and carried a grenade that could summon **Algos** (the god of pain) as a last resort.

---

## Book 4 — Exposed

"Chris" appeared in Hump Town on Floor 5. Carl realized immediately it wasn't Chris — wrong mannerisms, fabricated personal details. When confronted:

- Katia used a paralyzing crossbow bolt to incapacitate her
- Donut (drunkenly) used her _Hole_ spell to drop Chris/Maggie through the floor into the flooded subterranean level below Hump Town

Chris, trapped inside his own body and completely powerless, sent a message to Carl via Imani: **"Please kill me. I give you permission."** Carl refused, promising to save Chris after defeating all four castles.

---

## Book 5 — Separation and Presumed Death

By Book 5, Chris Andrews 2 is **free of Maggie** — apparently separated by the Floor 5 flood that inundated the subterranean level. He is Level 35, Igneous rock race, with a bodyguard named Clay-ton.

Maggie's fate is not explicitly stated. Given she was dropped into a sealed, flooding chamber, she is **presumed dead**.

---
