# Princess Donut

**GC, BWR, NW Princess Donut the Queen Anne Chonk** (commonly known as **Princess Donut** or **Donut**) is a tortoiseshell Persian cat who becomes a crawler alongside [[carl]]. Due to eating an Enhanced Pet Biscuit on Floor 1, she underwent a transformation that granted her human-level intelligence and speech. She is the leader of the party **The Royal Court of Princess Donut**, with the self-appointed title of **Grand Champion Best in Dungeon**.

## Background
Donut was a highly prized show cat owned by [[beatrice]]. She lived a pampered life in a room dedicated to her trophies. When the collapse occurred, Carl rescued her from a tree outside their apartment building, leading to both entering the dungeon.

## Race & Class Selections
- **Race**: **Cat** (Stays Cat, providing +2 Constitution, night vision, reflexes, slash, and a passive *9 Lives* which halves damage from the first nine physical attacks daily).
- **Class**: **Former Child Actor** (specialized as **Artist Alley Mogul** on Floor 3; selected the **Glass Cannon** specialty on Floor 5; chose the **Legendary Diva** bard subclass on Floor 6; chose the **Master Telephone Psychic** subclass on Floor 8, rejecting *Agony Urchin*. This subclass increases her mapping range, grants +5 Intelligence, and adds spells like *Brain Freeze*, *Bad Attitude*, and *Why Are You Hitting Yourself?*).

## Climax of Floor 4 & 5
- **Hekla's Betrayal**: Donut's Prism Goggles detect Hekla shooting Katia with invisible *Dirty Little Phased Bolts*. Furious, Donut fires a *Magic Missile* into Hekla's face. After Hekla dies, Donut casts *Second Chance* and *Clockwork Triplicate* on Hekla's corpse, using the clockwork Hekla minions to shield the party and throw Eva Sigrid out the window.
- **PVP Coupons**: Donut buries Carl's coupon and throws Katia's in the garbage to preserve the party's trust.
- **Royal Tribute**: After the Floor 4 rescue, 600 crawlers drop their conductor hats in a pile as a tribute to Donut.
- **Floor 5 (The Bubbles)**: Donut spins the quadrant wheels, selecting the Air Quadrant. She participates in the bubble-popping exploits.

## Floor 6 & 8 Actions
- **Floor 6 Actions**: Donut puddle-jumps the party past the stairwell traps. She shoots Magic Missiles to expose Zabit's invisible Night Weasels and kills one, earning her first *Hunter Killer* badge. At the covered bridge, she pushes Mayor [[elmer]] down the riverbank to his death using *Astral Paw*, gaining the title of Grand (Mayor) of the settlement.
- **Floor 8 Actions**: Donut acquires the mythic Icelandic card [[jola]] from Katia. To optimize their deck, she uses a consumable *Combo* card to merge two of their problem cards, using a *Glow-Up* card to make it permanent. She threatens to hunt down [[sister-ines-quiteria]] and destroy Paz Lo's card for abandoning them. During the interview with [[odette]], she attempts to rename Crown Prince Stalwart to "Captain Enormous" (rejected) and reacts with fury to Empress D'Nadia's attempts to delete them. As squad leader, she enters the choosing chamber, selects Miami, and defeats [[robert-the-human]] instantly when [[mongo]] bites his throat.


## Pets & Companions
- **[[mongo]]**: Her bonded velociraptor, who reaches **Level 26** on Floor 4, grows to pony-size, acts as a mount, and has learned the *Earthquake* jump attack.

## Graphify Deductions
- **Loyal Protector**: Despite her show-cat vanity, Donut's reactions to Hekla's betrayal and the PVP coupons show her deep, unshakeable loyalty to her small group. Her immediate choosing of Katia over her idol Hekla shows that her emotional bonds with her friends are stronger than the system's attempts to corrupt her.
- **Media Sensation**: Donut's status as a Legendary Diva and Mayor of the Ursine settlement positions her as a major media disruptor. Her determination to participate in the Butcher's Masquerade Pet Beauty Contest represents a clash between crawler vanity and survival.
