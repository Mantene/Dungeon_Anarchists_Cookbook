## Background

Beatrice's prize Persian show cat. Worth more than Carl's annual salary. Had a dedicated trophy room in their apartment. When Donut jumped out a window to explore on the night of the Transformation, Carl went outside to retrieve her — saving both their lives.

After eating the Enhanced Pet Biscuit in the tutorial guild, Donut transformed from a passive cat into a fully sapient, talking crawler with dramatically enhanced stats. She retains all her memories of being a cat, including years of watching TV with Carl and Beatrice. She "absorbed" human culture from hours of television. She remembered Carl was considering keeping her even after the breakup.

## Book 1

### Floor 1
Immediately asserted herself as party leader (highest combined stats = automatic party leader). Renamed party "The Royal Court of Princess Donut." Carl became "Royal Bodyguard." She used Charisma to charm mobs (goblins Rory and Lorelai), turning hostile dots white and negotiating for the Chopper.

Pivotal moment: Saved Carl's life during the Juicer fight by biting through the boss's main blood vessel (first time using melee). Donut's claws can do enormous damage — she just refuses to use them because it's "inelegant."

Appeared on *Dungeon Crawler After Hours with Odette* and absolutely stole the show. Revealed she'd been doing cat shows her whole life and is essentially a natural at performing.

### Floor 2
Developed Second Chance necromancy spell (raises dead mobs as zombie minions). Used it to lure mobs, raise tactical zombies, and creep herself out repeatedly.

During the Rage Elemental crisis, when Carl was near death from the alchemy table shield collapse, Donut dragged him to safety by his cloak across burning debris. Critical save.

Chose Mongo (Mongoliensis / velociraptor) as her pet in the Floor 2 pet reward room, rejecting the more practical options. Spent the last days of Floor 2 bonding with the hostile little creature.
