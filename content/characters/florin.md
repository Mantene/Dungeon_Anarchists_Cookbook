# Florin

**Race:** Crocodilian (anthropomorphic, bipedal crocodile)  
**Class:** Unknown (ranged/shotgun-based)  
**Level:** Unknown (high — obtained a city boss kill on Floor 6)  
**Origin:** Claims to be from France; has an Australian accent; was apparently in Africa. Nobody knows what's going on with this.  
**Party:** Solo (Floor 8); formerly in Katia's alliance group

---

## Background

A crocodilian crawler who speaks with an Australian accent despite claiming to be French. This contradiction is never explained and is apparently just how he is.

His girlfriend was killed by **Lucia Mar** before the series' current events. This is why he hated her — until Floor 6, when something changed.

---

## Books 4–5

Present on Floor 4 and 5 as part of the extended alliance group. On Floor 6, he was part of the group that killed **Queen Imogen** (country boss). He delivered the killing blow or a major blow on Imogen/Ifechi.

The Florin/Lucia Mar subplot: After killing his girlfriend, Lucia later began behaving strangely. Florin became obsessed with understanding what really happened — suspecting she had been acting under compulsion or manipulation. They reached an uneasy détente by Book 5. By Book 6, Florin was in contact with Lucia Mar occasionally, though she wasn't in his party.

He left Carl's party just before Carl and Prepotente destroyed Floor 7.

---

## Book 6 — Floor 8

Florin's Phase 1 region: **Ecuador**. Solo squad. He had a quest to track and capture a unique monster terrorizing his region — a unique-tier mob he eventually flagged.

**Equipment upgrade:** Florin obtained a **tank** (small, 1940s-era) during Phase 2 and used it to assist against a massive serpent city boss. He obtained his exit key.

He went to Floor 9 alone, having placed claymores at his stairwell entrance — though he didn't need to use them. He noted that **Lucia Mar was not in his area and did not have a key**, raising questions about her fate.

**T'Ghee deck highlights:** Saber-toothed tiger, giant caiman named Roy, plus HeyZoos (before trading it to Carl/Donut), and the creature he caught in Ecuador (Uzi Jesus equivalent — armed). He had 6 full slots.

---

## Relationships

- **Lucia Mar** — complex; she killed his girlfriend; he now suspects she was manipulated; uneasy alliance
- **Carl/Donut** — allied; not in the same party
- **Katia** — part of her alliance group during Floors 4–6

---

## Books 7–8

**Faction Wars (Book 7):** Florin fought on the orc front, handling the Skull Empire's Bone Clan forces. He captured two Weeper-class tanks from the Operatics — one of which Carl commandeered for bombing runs.

**Floor 11 — Parade of Horribles (Book 8):** Florin's team was assigned the **"Resolution"** float group — the first float in the procession.

**Floor 12:** Confirmed among the 19 crawlers on Floor 12.
