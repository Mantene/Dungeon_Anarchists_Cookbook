# Florin

Florin is a crocodilian crawler who progresses to Floor 5.

## Biography & Combat Stats
- **Race**: **Crocodilian**. This race triples the effectiveness of any buffs received from eating food or creatures.
- **Class**: **Shotgun Messenger**.
- **Weapon**: A heavily modified automatic shotgun with an unlimited-ammo upgrade, and [[ifechi]]'s leech staff.
- **Background**: Florin is a mercenary of French origin who was working in Africa when the collapse occurred.
- In [[114-book-4-chapter-4-the-royal-chariot]], the recap show details Florin's tragic confrontation with [[lucia-mar]] at the Floor 4 exit stairs. After Lucia threw a crawler, Florin insulted her. Lucia used her *Rubber* damage-reflect spell on Ifechi's head, meaning that when Florin blasted Lucia and her dogs with his shotgun, the damage was reflected, instantly killing Ifechi.
- Following Ifechi's death, Florin receives a player-killer skull and is devastated. He falls to #10 on the leaderboard and is shown refusing to spin the wheels or move from the transition room, clutching Ifechi's leech staff.
