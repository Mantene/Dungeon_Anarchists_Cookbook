# Nadine

**Nadine** is a Level 50 Bearded Sage Caterpillar who is aligned with [[tsarina-signet]] and acts as a mount for the six were-castor warriors.

## Profile & Traits
- **Background**: Formerly a Chee schoolteacher and actress, Nadine was transformed into a giant, fuzzy, black-and-yellow segmented caterpillar by the High-Elf general [[imogen]]. 
- **Role**: She serves as a mount, carrying six saddles on her back. She acts as a maternal figure to the surviving Chee schoolboys (the were-castors).

## Floor 6 Actions
- **Mount & Scout**: Nadine chews trails through the thick jungle and transports the six were-castors.
- **Death**: In [[172-book-5-chapter-27-claude-sludgington]] (Chapter 27), when [[claude-sludgington-the-fourth]] is teleported to the shore, Nadine charges the boss to protect the group. Claude shoots her with lightning, causing her to explode into green gore and black quills.
- **Tattoo Preservation**: In [[174-book-5-chapter-29-tattooing-nadine]] (Chapter 29), [[edgar]] the tortoise tattoos Nadine's remains onto Signet's thigh. The finished tattoo depicts Nadine as a young female Chee with a child (representing [[clint-smashgrab]]) hiding behind her skirt, preserving their memories as a blood and ink elemental.

## Graphify Deductions
- **Memorial Magic**: Nadine's transition from a living caterpillar to a blood-and-ink elemental tattoo on Signet demonstrates the unique magic of the Over City/dungeon native NPCs. By using the remains of a companion and a specialized needle, Signet can permanently "store" and summon the elementals, showing how the system allows NPCs to preserve their dead.
