# Fannar

**Fannar** is a character in [[katia-grim]]'s backstory who appears in a dream sequence in *[[dinniman-2024-tir|This Inevitable Ruin]]*.

## Biography
Fannar is Katia's ex-husband, a university professor in Iceland. He had an affair with a student, which Katia reported, though the university took no action against him. 

In retaliation, when Katia applied to adopt a terminally ill baby named [[annie-baby|Annie]], Fannar reported Katia's own medical history of cancer to the adoption board. This report caused the board to reject Katia's application, preventing her from adopting the baby.
