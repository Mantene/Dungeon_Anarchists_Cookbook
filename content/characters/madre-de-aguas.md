# Madre de Aguas

Madre de Aguas is a Level 125 City Boss giant sea snake from pre-Colombian Taino/Cuban folklore. She protects freshwater areas and can control local creatures. Sister Ines charms her with poetry, but the charm breaks when Carl teleports them, leaving the snake un-charmed, infected with pre-zombism, and furious.
