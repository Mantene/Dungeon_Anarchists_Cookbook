# Yolanda Martinez 13

Yolanda Martinez 13 is a 50-year-old human crawler who worked as a Certified Nursing Assistant (CNA) at the Meadow Lark facility.

## Role
Yolanda is short (4'11") and wears scrubs with her Meadow Lark CNA badge. She is compassionate and cares for the elderly residents in the safe zone. 

## Combat Stats
- **Level**: 5 (reaches Level 6 after the Borough Boss fight)
- **Weapon**: Carries a large bow and arrow.
- **Boss Star**: Gained a bronze star for participating in the neighborhood boss fight against a floating crystal.
- Yolanda was severely injured during the Borough Boss fight but was saved by [[imani-c]]'s healing potion.
