# Chaco

**Chaco** is a Level 66 Pterolykos Song Bard NPC who serves as a game show host in the World Dungeon.

## Biography and Role
During their crawl, Chaco was a member of [[mordecai]]'s original party. On the tenth floor, as a level collapse became imminent, the party needed the magic of a storm god to activate the exit puzzle. Under pressure from [[odette]], Chaco agreed to worship the storm goddess [[dodola]] and threw the [[dart-of-ophiotaurus]] into their teammate [[uzzi]]'s breast to force-summon the goddess. He then contained Dodola within a *Circle of Containment* field. The summoning killed Uzzi, but the resulting chain reaction destroyed Dodola, opening the exit key. This act saved the surviving party members but caused Mordecai's deep and lasting hatred of Chaco and Odette.

Chaco survived and eventually became a game show host for special prize segments, such as Carl's Platinum Fan Box selection. During the broadcast of Carl's prize selection, Mordecai (Carl and Donut's manager) recognizes Chaco from their shared past in the dungeon, which dates back hundreds of cycles. Mordecai, overcome by rage, lashes out and flings a heavy metal chair at Chaco's head. The dungeon system freezes the chair in midair, saving Chaco, and immediately suspends Mordecai for seven days for attacking a game show host.

Shaken but unharmed, Chaco continues the segment without his microphone, which had bounced away. He guides Carl through the carousel of nine potential prizes. After Carl selects the "nothing" option, Chaco mockingly departs, returning the party to their living area.
