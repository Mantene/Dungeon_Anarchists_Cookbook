# Muchen

**Muchen** is a Chinese crawler on the eleventh floor.

## Actions
- **Chapter 491**: Muchen is grouped with [[elle-mcgibbons]], [[imani-c]], [[qianfan]], and [[lingyun]] on the "Betrayal" parade float team (Float 3).
- **Chapter 493**: During the parade performance, Muchen holds up a sign that reads: "I had a wife. I had children." On the flip side, he displays their names, expressing defiance toward the audience.
