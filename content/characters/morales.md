# Morales

**Morales** is a white-skinned, sharp-toothed "Dream elf" former crawler and healer who tapped out at the start of the tenth floor ("tenner") in a prior season of the crawl.

## Profile & Traits
- **Class & Level**: Level 60 Plumbing Specialist (a healer class).
- **Appearance**: White, pigment-free skin and sharp, goblin-like teeth. Like [[griffin]], he wears a large neck tattoo depicting a flaming cat-human hybrid skull with the slogan "Princess Posse or Death" and their unit "The Tenner Wraiths / 103rd Recon Legionnaires".
- **Weaponry**: Carries a large saber.
- **Personality**: Boisterous and highly talkative.

## History
Morales and [[griffin]] exited the dungeon as "tenners" shortly before Rosetta's crawl. In the Ninth Floor Faction Wars, they joined the Princess Posse forces. In Book 7, Chapter 15, Morales participated in hunting Skank Skunks but was hit by their aerosol spray, suffering the "Skanked" debuff and vomiting continuously.
