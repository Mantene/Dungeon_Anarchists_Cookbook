# Samantha

**True nature:** Withering spirit (non-corporeal entity; goddess of unrequited love — unconfirmed but she claims it)  
**Physical form:** Occupies a sex doll head (plastic, pigtails, large mouth); can also float and emit electricity when powerful  
**Party:** Affiliated with Carl and Donut; returns to them each floor  
**Goal:** Get a body — specifically animated into Princess Lunette (a flesh golem body from Signet's tattoo magic)

---

## Background

Samantha is a withering spirit — a type of non-corporeal entity that should have extremely limited power. She latched onto the sex doll head at some point and uses it as her physical anchor. She has an obsessive, inappropriate crush on Louis Santiago.

She calls herself the "goddess of unrequited love" and makes references to having lived in celestial realms, having a "king" (her true love/husband, whose existence may or may not be real), a daughter, and a history with major players in the afterlife (she casually references Yarilo, Hellik, and other beings). How much of this is real vs. delusion is unclear.

---

## Powers (Growing Each Floor)

Withering spirits should be almost powerless. Samantha is not:

- **Floor 5–6:** Survived multiple explosions and full-body burns. Regenerated. Flew back to the saferoom through brambles under her own power.
- **Floor 6:** Went on solo reconnaissance missions. Reported on mobs from miles away. Emitted electricity.
- **Floor 8:** Flew for extended periods under her own power (though tiring). Emitted light. **Amplified Donut's _Laundry Day_ spell** during the Phantom Move (unexpectedly — she didn't admit to this ability earlier). Helped enhance other spells. Getting demonstrably more powerful.

Mordecai believes Samantha may be gaining power from death — specifically the deaths of people connected to the party. He wanted Carl to not give her the flesh golem body until they understood why and how she was gaining these abilities.

---

## The Flesh Golem (Princess Lunette)

Signet's final spell created **Princess Lunette** — a flesh golem body (a naiad, made from what was originally a fish tattoo on Signet's body). Carl carries this in his inventory. Samantha desperately wants Mordecai or another fleshmancer to animate her into it.

The pulpmancer who could do this was supposedly in Larracos — a city Carl flooded with sharks, and which is now the Faction Wars battlefield. The pulpmancer may be dead. Samantha doesn't know this yet.

---

## Shi Maria's Claim

The Bedlam Bride (Shi Maria) claimed she could get Samantha her body if they brought Shi Maria to Larracos. This was a lie — Shi Maria doesn't have the magic and was using Samantha to get herself to the next floor. After Donut ripped Shi Maria's card in Book 6, Samantha was briefly devastated but recovered quickly.

---

## Book 6

Samantha was trapped inside Shi Maria's stomach for Part 1 of the book, befriending the spider. She escaped when Carl negotiated Shi Maria's flag deal. She served as recon, used the xistera to teleport to Sister Ines during the Cuba battle, helped with the Amayon crisis, and was briefly grabbed by the demon (inside his fist with Carl).

The demon Amayon looked at Samantha and said: *"I know who you really are."* He was not scared of her, but he recognized her. This is significant.

After the Cuba battle, Samantha declared she and Carl were "dating now" after he was stuck inside her neck hole, then two minutes later broke up with him and returned to waiting for her king.

---

## Relationships

- **Louis** — obsessive inappropriate crush; he has her blocked in chat

## Book 7

### Floor 9 — The Greatest Sacrifice / Charred to a Crisp

Samantha voluntarily gave up the Princess Lunette flesh golem body (her only chance at a body for the foreseeable future) to save Louis's life by allowing it to be used for organ donation. This was described by the group as genuinely brave.

**Her daughter.** Samantha could feel her daughter somewhere in the castle during the battle. She lost the connection when the acid trench was used — suggesting her daughter was killed in it. She blamed herself.

**Kimaris revelation.** Carl has a stuffed animal of Kimaris (one of Amayon's demon-lord brothers) in his inventory. Samantha suddenly remembered: Kimaris was her friend who gave her the potion that let her conceive her daughter. She'd forgotten until reminded. His mother is apparently someone important ("I will kill his mother!").

**Emberus.** Carl threw Samantha to Emberus via the xistera to stall him. Emberus held her while questioning her about Geyrun's death. She was eventually thrown into a volcano. Carl retrieved her just in time. She is "charred to a crisp" — burned even worse than before. Still alive.

**Current status:** Burned, bodyless, but alive on Floor 10.

## Book 8

### Floors 10–11 — The Arena Plan

Samantha was used as a piece in Carl's arena plan on Floor 11. Specifically:
- Carl threw Samantha to **Emberus** via the xistera to stall him
- Emberus held her while questioning her about Geyrun's death, then threw her into a volcano
- Carl retrieved her just in time (she was charred even worse)
- Donut had separately used the **Bolt of Ophiotaurus** to anchor Emberus; Taranis saw and killed him

### Departure — Sheol

After the arena fight, Samantha went **willingly with Taranis** to Sheol (Floor 15). Her stated goal:

> Kill **Kimaris's mother** — "the demon mother lady and her jerk son" who tricked her into giving birth to a monster that will destroy all of creation.

Kimaris is one of Amayon's demon-lord brothers and the entity in Carl's stuffed animal item. Samantha believes killing his progenitor is key to preventing a cosmological catastrophe connected to her daughter's death.

**Current status:** In Sheol with Taranis; hunting Kimaris's mother.
