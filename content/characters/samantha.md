# Samantha

**Samantha** is the name given by [[carl]] to the **Withering Spirit of Psamathe**, a lesser deity of the Nothing and daughter of a Celestial Ascendency god. Her soul was split, with one half trapped inside Lika's latex sex doll head and the other in the ghost of the Quetzalcoatlus.

## Appearance & Personality
- **Latex Head**: Samantha's consciousness is housed in a latex sex doll head. She has an unblinking gaze and a posable jaw that Carl must manually tune like a radio so she can speak.
- **Profane & Loud**: She is extremely loud, profane, and antagonistic, frequently screaming threats to "kill Carl's mother" and hurling insults at anyone nearby.
- **Background**: She has a history in the Nothing, including a rivalry with [[slit]], a demon who claims Samantha slept with her king.

## Dungeon Status & Role
- **Trainer NPC**: Because her presence as a free entity would trigger automated god-summoning alerts and draw hostiles, [[mordecai]] suggests hiring her as a trainer NPC. [[princess-donut]] hires her using crawl funds, and Carl confines the head to their personal space's training room.
- **Lore Asset**: Despite her instability, Samantha possesses deep knowledge of the deities and the history of the Nothing, which becomes highly valuable.

## Floor 6 Actions (Book 5)
- **Signet Connection**: In [[162-book-5-chapter-17-samantha-screaming]] (Chapter 17), Samantha learns that Carl is meeting [[tsarina-signet]], whom she claims is her third cousin thrice-removed by marriage. She demands to be brought along, claiming Signet can give her a new body.
- **Biting and Rolling**: Samantha gains the ability to roll herself around and open/close her mouth to bite targets.
- **Missile Weaponization**: In [[167-book-5-chapter-22-samantha-bottle-rocket]] (Chapter 22), Carl utilizes Samantha's indestructible withering spirit nature (she has only 1 HP but cannot be destroyed by ordinary damage) by duct-taping seeking missiles to her head. Samantha triggers them in midair by pulling a string tied to her fangs. In this manner, she knocks out the hunter [[akland]] and drags him back to Carl by his ankle.
- **Claude Sludgington Teleportation**: In [[172-book-5-chapter-27-claude-sludgington]] (Chapter 27), Samantha bites the borough boss [[claude-sludgington-the-fourth]] inside the castle. When Carl teleports Samantha back, Claude is dragged with her, bringing the boss directly onto the shore.
- **Deity Identity**: In [[171-book-5-chapter-26-goddess-of-unrequited-love]] (Chapter 26), Donut reveals Samantha is the **Goddess of Unrequited Love** (or crazy ex-girlfriends). She is searching for her sand-ooze child on Floor 9 and wants to reach the floor where her former lover, a king, resides.

## Graphify Deductions
- **Indestructible Asset Exploitation**: Samantha's withering spirit status (1 HP but functionally indestructible) allows the crawlers to bypass system damage limits. By attaching explosives and missiles directly to her, Carl turns a screaming hazard into a reusable, self-seeking artillery shell, highlighting how crawlers can turn lore-based curses into tactical weaponry.
