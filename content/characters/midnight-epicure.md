# The Midnight Epicure

**The Midnight Epicure** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A deity revered by Prepotente, associated with an apocalyptic form that protects prey animals by bringing about the destruction of existence and transforming the consumed into devourers. This deity assigned Prepotente a quest concerning a specific crystal and expressed strong displeasure when it was lost.
