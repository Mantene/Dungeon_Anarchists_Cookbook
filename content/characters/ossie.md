# Ossie

**Ossie** was a crawler and the author of the **18th Edition** of the [[the-anarchist-cookbook]].

## Profile & Traits
- **Role**: Cookbook author (18th Edition).
- **Philosophy**: Advocated for using confusion and psychological tactics to destabilize stalwart opponents.

## History
In Book 7, Chapter 23, Ossie's advice is quoted in the Cookbook: "If you want to create fright amongst an otherwise stalwart enemy, confusion is always the key." Carl applies this philosophy during the assault on the southern trenches by loading strobing "Dazzler" traps that blast loud music to disorient the defenders.
