# Jasha

**Jasha** is a bugbear racer NPC on the tenth floor of the dungeon.

## Biography and Role

Jasha is a Level 65 bugbear and a member of **Team Free Love**, along with his cousin/brother [[radoslav]] and the deceased Alevtina. In [[403-book-8-chapter-4-team-free-love]], their red Pontiac minivan is overturned by a giant manatee in Heat One, killing Alevtina. Jasha and Radoslav explain their situation to [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]], revealing they recall memories of the cold between crawls. Carl and Donut tow them to the finish line to prevent team elimination.

In [[411-book-8-chapter-1-hungry-eyes-roundabout]], Jasha is seen sleeping in a kiddie pool filled with water at the roundabout. He later wakes up in [[412-book-8-chapter-13-quest-reunite-corky]] asking where his beer went.
