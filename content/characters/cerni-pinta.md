# Cerni Pinta

**Cerni Pinta** is a bristle alien tourist checked into Room 422 of Club Scolopendra with her three husbands during Book 8.

## Biography
- **Role**: She is the Vice President of Nova Client Services, LTD., a medical collections agency that specializes in implant reclamation and indenture captures for the Viceroy system.

## Actions
- **Chapter 496**: Carl encounters Cerni flayed and tortured, hung from spiderwebs inside Club Scolopendra. She begs Carl to kill her. She is subsequently hit by Scolopendra's second attack (Sin of Consumption) and devoured by worms, disappearing in a flash.
