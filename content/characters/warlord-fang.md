# Warlord Fang

**Warlord Fang** (also referred to as **Warlord Fangs**) is the leader of the Reavers on the ninth floor (Faction Wars). He is a heavily cybernetic entity and one of the wealthiest faction leaders in the crawl.

## Profile & Traits
- **Appearance**: He can change his shape due to cybernetic enhancements. While he previously appeared in the form of a robot, in Faction Wars he takes the shape of a thin, pink-skinned human male wearing a t-shirt and white underwear, identifiable as a cyborg only by a line of flashing lights at his temple.
- **Lore**: Fang is 99% cybernetic. His consciousness is linked to a physical brain chunk called "the Initial" kept on one of the Reaver worlds. If he dies, a new body is printed and seeded with a piece of the Initial.
- **Faction Wars**: Fang leads the Reavers, one of the wealthiest teams. During the final battle, he is targeted by Donut's *War Crime* spell but escapes by transforming into a microscopic piece of robot gore/fly. He hides inside his cybernetic pet dragon, [[calliope]].
- **Death**: Carl detonates Louis's *No Disassembles* (EMP bombs) which disables the security system but also detonates a mini tactical nuke, destroying the Reaver castle and crashing the dragon Calliope. Fang dies in the crash, and the Reavers are defeated.
