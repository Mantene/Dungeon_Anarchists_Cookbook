# Bodi

**Bodi** is a crawler who serves as a battalion commander for the [[the-princess-posse]] during the Faction Wars on the ninth floor in *[[dinniman-2024-tir|This Inevitable Ruin]]*.

## Role in the Crawl
Bodi is the commanding officer of the **103rd Battalion**. During the coordinated assault of "Operation Ruin," his battalion is tasked with marching on the front side of the Madness castle to draw the defenders away. 

When the operation's stealth is compromised by Osvaldo's beacon, [[carl]] orders Bodi to execute an early retreat and seed the field, allowing [[prepotente]] to redeploy and shield Carl's team from psionic attacks. Bodi successfully coordinates the withdrawal.
