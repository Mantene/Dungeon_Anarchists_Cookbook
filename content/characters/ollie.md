# Ollie (Oliver)

**Ollie (Oliver)** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

The simulated son of Archie Mu and Mackenzie in the fourth-floor apartment. His eyes are not X'd out in photographs, which Carl interprets as a sign that the real Oliver might still be alive in the dungeon. He briefly speaks to his mother during the simulation's collapse.
