# Jola

Jola is a Level 110 Persian cat totem card representing the Icelandic Yule Cat, gifted to Donut by Katia Grim. Jola appears as a house-sized dirty-brown Persian cat with a giant melted candle on his head that reveals invisible entities. He is highly food-motivated and gets a strength bonus against naked targets or children.
