# Dorota

**Dorota** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

Dorota is Carl's sentient car and a loyal companion. In chapter 447, she is a passenger during the race through Satan's Water Park. Initially prone to complaining, she becomes quiet and focused once she comprehends the perilous situation they are in, indicating her understanding and concern for Carl and Donut's predicament.
