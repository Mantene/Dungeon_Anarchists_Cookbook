# Justice Light

**Justice Light** is a skyfowl trap master crawler who survived his crawl and was a former owner of the [[the-anarchist-cookbook|Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook]].

## Biography

- **Crawl History**: Justice Light was a skyfowl trap master crawler who took a deal at the start of the 11th floor. During his crawl, he was infamous for killing a shopkeeper, a deed that caused him severe guilt.
- **Indentured Servitude**: To atone for the killing, he demanded his indentureship be that of a shopkeeper replacing the NPC he killed. He spent his seasons in the Trap Master guild building wood block puzzles and sewing intricate 3D fabric sculptures.
- **Reassignment**: He and his crocodilian neighbor [[dante]] were temporarily reassigned as Ice Castle guard captains during their season's Faction Wars. Like Dante, he adamantly refused to fight crawlers.
