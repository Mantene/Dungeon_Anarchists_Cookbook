# Justice Light

**Race:** Skyfowl (black feathers, gray tinge, twin head-tufts like horns)  
**Class:** Origamist (Floor 9 mercenary, Level 60); originally **Trap Master** (Top Level: 86)  
**One-winged:** Lost right wing during forced compliance in his indentureship  
**Author:** 8th Edition of The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook

---

## Background

Justice Light was a Skyfowl (golden eagle-type creature) who reached Level 86 as a Trap Master during his crawl. He was known for hunting and sharing food with the elderly of his world before the dungeon — a gentle, proud soul who became something else entirely.

He took his exit deal at the start of Floor 11.

During his indentureship as a trap supply merchant, he was forcibly reassigned to guard an ice castle. There, he befriended ice elf NPCs — gentle, simple creatures who loved puzzles and playing in the snow. He learned all their names.

At the end of the floor, he and his friend **Dante** (the 3rd edition cookbook author, a crocodilian) were ordered to kill all the ice elves. Dante refused twice and was erased by administrative action. Justice Light refused once, lost his wing to a beam of light, and then complied.

This event broke him. The ice elves had apparently killed themselves before he could do it, having overheard — but Justice Light never forgave himself, believing he had killed them.

Post-dungeon, he spent decades obsessively studying dungeon lore, trap exploits, gods, and the Ascendency. He eventually returned to the dungeon to join the Princess Posse.

---

## Personality

Methodical, obsessive-compulsive. Constantly moved his head back and forth (hearing things). Made intricate folded-paper art sculptures to calm himself. Few words, intense eyes. Muttered to himself. Had a deep anger and conviction hidden beneath the calm.

He was not a naive revolutionary — he was fully aware of the trap of tunnel vision, even as he fell into it.

---

## Book 7 (Floor 9)

Worked as Origamist class. Insisted on using the **sluggalos** as his trap assistants (partly because he thought they were uncopyable by NPCs — he was wrong, but he anticipated the deception and used it to negotiate with Juice Box).

He discovered that Sick-Ass Worgo (a sluggalo) was actually a powerful changeling spy for Juice Box. Rather than fight, he called Juice Box directly and proposed a deal. He told her the truth about the ice elves. He revealed his real plan to her.

**The Plan:**

His supposed "elaborate throne room trap system" was misdirection. The real trap was tiny, placed at the bottom of the dungeon, and required:
1. The Gate of the Feral Gods winding box (now broken, creating a permanent portal to the Nothing)
2. A bijou fairy trap (fairies connected to the 12th floor via thousands of threads)
3. A demon trap (evicted demons who could open portals to Sheol)

His deal with Juice Box: she would allow the trap to be triggered in exchange for him showing her how to touch a god without dying. He believed this was compatible with both their goals.

**The Result (triggered after the floor ended):**

Justice Light snapped a piece of twine. The trap dropped both items into the permanent Nothing portal. Four planes folded together. The Nothing was broken — and began draining into the Halls of the Ascendency, Sheol, and **Scolopendra's Lair**.

Scolopendra has awakened. Justice Light died in the fire of Emberus, alone on the floor.

---

## His Legacy

His trap likely changed the course of everything. He knew it would "make things much more complicated." He told Tipid and Rosetta about his plan but not about the full scope (they only knew what was necessary). Porthus knew.

He sent a final message: *"I am not sorry. Peace to you all, brothers and sisters."*

---

## The Cookbook

His 8th edition entries were filled with guilt over the NPC he killed in anger early in his crawl (a lizard merchant) and haunted references to the ice elf incident. He wrote about traps extensively, and his designs saved Drakea's (22nd edition) life multiple times — something he never knew. He was described by Drakea as a "brother."
