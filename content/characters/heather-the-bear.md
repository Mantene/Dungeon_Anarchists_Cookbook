# Heather the Bear

Heather the Bear is a Level 19 Neighborhood Boss of the Over City (Floor 3). She was originally a performing roller-skating bear at Grimaldi's Traveling Circus before the collapse.

## Background
Heather was near retirement when the cataclysm struck. She was transformed into a horrific skeletal beast under the control of [[ringmaster-grimaldi]]'s parasites. She hunts the ruins of the Over City to bring back prey and crawlers to feed the clowns.

## Appearance & Abilities
- **Parasitic Form**: In her boss form, she has no skin or fur on her face (exposed skull with glowing red eyes) and a pink tutu. She wears roller skates fused to her feet and a pink clown hat. Her front paws are replaced by massive, undulating white worms.
- **Worm Claws**: The parasite worms on her front paws burrow into targets to inflict damage and inject a mental link to the collective consciousness.
- **Roller Skating**: Heather moves at high speeds, gliding on her skates with a *whisk, whisk, whisk* sound.
- **Mind Liberation**: During Carl's solo fight against her, he scratches a scratcher ticket and rolls "Custard" (which heals the target). The custard heals Heather's body, which instantly kills the parasitic worms and frees her mind. Back in her natural bear form, she whimpers, expressing gratitude to Carl for ending her centuries of suffering, before Carl crushes her skull to put her out of her misery.
- **Blood Sacrifice**: Signet uses Heather's blood after her death to summon her entire elemental battle squad.
