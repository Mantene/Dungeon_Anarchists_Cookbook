# Leve Billings

**Leve Billings** is a gleener scientist who serves as the liaison to the science committee for Prime Minister [[victory]] during Book 8.

## Description
- **Appearance**: As a gleener, he wears an air rebreather over his gills.

## Actions
- **Chapter 502**: Leve watches the broadcast of the Parade of Horribles in the Prime Minister's ready room. Panicked by the dungeon AI's announcement that it has taken control of the tunnels and is expanding its influence, Leve rocks back and forth muttering, "This isn't real." When the AI details the stakes of the upcoming Ascendency games, Leve is left quietly sobbing in despair.
