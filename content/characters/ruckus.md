# Ruckus

Ruckus is a steampunk cyborg giant buzz-ard who acts as a Borough Boss on Floor 5.

## Characteristics & Role
Ruckus is a massive, half-biological, half-machine bird. She is believed to be either a failed gnomish invention from the second war or a relic of the "treasure hunter" era.

Ruckus roosts near Hump Town every night after the daily sandstorms, circling the structure of the Necropolis. She is extremely hostile to crawlers caught in the open, having destroyed two siege towers built by Gwendolyn Duet's team in the Land Quadrant. The Dromedarian patrol avoids her because a third camel city was destroyed in a past attempt to shoot her down. 

In [[118-book-4-chapter-8-ruckus]], Carl prepares a custom-built anti-aircraft missile to target Ruckus from a distance.
