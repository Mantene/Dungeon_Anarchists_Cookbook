# Krakaren

Krakaren is a Level 10 Neighborhood Boss clone encountered on the second floor of the dungeon. 

## Characteristics & Lore
Krakaren is a massive, immobile, 20-foot-tall octopus-like creature with screaming tentacles covered in human-shaped mouths with bright red lips and sharp teeth. For every Krakaren clone killed, Krakaren Prime births two more. The system design lampoons anti-vaccination and multi-level marketing (MLM) cultures.

She runs the **Rev-Up Moonshine** corporation, using Level 3 Unvaccinated Clurichauns as sales consultants and Level 6 Laminak fairies as Elite managers. Her lair contains a schedule of events including a "pox party." 

## Combat & Defeat
In [[034-book-1-chapter-34-krakaren-clone]], the Krakaren clone attacks Carl and Donut by bursting her tentacles through the walls of her filling room. She uses a specialized tentacle covered in tiny orifices to spray a green mist that inflicts **Vigorous Measles**. 

Carl counters this by drinking a **Rev-Up Immunity Smoothie** (crafted using a bicycle-powered blender, toilet-grade moonshine, and a Laminak fairy corpse). 

Carl defeats the boss by stuffing a stick of dynamite down the overalls of a clurichaun zombie raised by Donut's *Second Chance* spell and sending it into the boss room holding a *Carl's Jug O' Boom*. The resulting explosion kills the boss, though it denies them experience points since the boss's remains are incinerated/destroyed by the blast.
