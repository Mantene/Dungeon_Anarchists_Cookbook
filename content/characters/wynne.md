# Wynne

Wynne was a Level 50 Dirigible Gnome Flesh Mechanic NPC.

## Biography & Role
- **Background**: Wynne was the beloved uncle of Commandant Kane (commander of the flying dreadnaught *Wasteland*).
- **Abilities**: As a Flesh Mechanic, he possessed the rare healing ability to temporarily resurrect the deceased.
- In [[116-book-4-chapter-6-the-collateral-secret]], Carl finds Wynne chained in a vault beneath Hump Town City Hall, just past the quadrant forcefield boundary. Wynne was held hostage by the Dromedarians to prevent Commandant Kane from bombing the town. He was also tortured and drugged by [[henrik]] to force him to resurrect the beast "Quetzalcoatlus."
- **Death**: Mistaking Carl for Henrik, and desiring death to escape torture, Wynne begs a group of Subterranean crawlers to kill him. [[low-thi]] jabs him with a spear, killing him and causing Carl to fail the quest **"Free Wynne from his bondage."**
