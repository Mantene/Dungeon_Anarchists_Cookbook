# Great Rusty

**Great Rusty** was a Level 99 neighbourhood boss and Chieftain of the southern ginger cannibal settlement on the tenth floor of the dungeon in Book 8 (*A Parade of Horribles*).

## History & Dungeon Presence
Great Rusty is introduced in [[442-book-8-chapter-43-ginger-chieftain]] blocking the forest road. He is described as a large, balding man with curly red hair in a man bun, a pale protruding belly, and carrying an enchanted staff. Great Rusty attempts to cast a *Soul Suck* spell, which is negated by Carl's *Mind Balance* skill and Donut's high stats, though it leaves their driver [[olga]] unconscious and in a fugue state.

Donut immediately kills Great Rusty with a magic missile, which triggers the mandatory quest **"Pasty Inferno"** to kill the northern chieftain, [[maurice]], before the race can be completed.
