# Jason Manning FUCKS

**Jason Manning FUCKS** is a Level 55 Sluggalo combatant fighting for the Princess Posse during the Ninth Floor Faction Wars.

## Profile & Traits
- **Race**: Sluggalo (a slug-like humanoid creature).
- **Level**: 55.
- **Appearance**: Trails orange slime. He features unique dreadlocks shaped like small morning star weapons, which he spins by shaking his head.
- **Personality**: Boisterous, aggressive, and highly enthusiastic about the upcoming war.

## History
In Book 7, Chapter 19, following the sudden end of the ceasefire, Jason Manning FUCKS and a squad of fellow sluggalos slither into the empty field hospital where [[carl]] has just teleported. Finding a bleeding Carl on the floor, Jason tells him to quit bleeding and get out to the trenches because the war has officially begun.
