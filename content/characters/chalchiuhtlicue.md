# Chalchiuhtlicue

**Chalchiuhtlicue** (referred to by [[samantha]] and [[princess-donut]] as **Chachi**) is an Aztec goddess of water, fire, fertility, and childbirth who is active on the tenth floor of the dungeon in Book 8 (*A Parade of Horribles*).

## Biography & Dungeon Presence
Chalchiuhtlicue has entered the minds of the children trapped within the crawler [[lucia-mar]]'s head (the Scavenger's Daughter personalities). In [[450-book-8-chapter-azra-and-yaz]], when Lucia confronts Carl, she is temporarily possessed by Chalchiuhtlicue, who speaks of the twelfth floor and claims to be kind. However, the goddess also speaks through the child persona Azra, telling her it might be better if the children die.

In [[452-book-8-chapter-goddess-of-child-sacrifice]], Samantha warns Carl about Chachi, explaining that while she claims to be a protector of children, she is notorious for demanding child sacrifices at the slightest inconvenience. This triggers Carl's deep alarm regarding the safety of the children within Lucia's mind.
