# Prince Stalwart

Prince Stalwart is the Crown Prince of the Skull Empire, the older brother of the disgraced [[the-maestro|Prince Maestro]], and the son of King Rust. He is currently on Floor 9 preparing for the Faction War Games.

## Profile
Prince Stalwart is a lean, powerful orc (wild boar-headed humanoid) with cruel eyes. During the talk show interview in Chapter 19, Carl refers to King Rust and Stalwart's younger brother as "pussies" and calls on the citizens of the Skull Empire to rebel. 

In immediate retaliation, Prince Stalwart fires a pulse from his personal yacht to execute Carl and Donut. However, because Carl and Donut had swapped trailers with the pop singer [[manasa]], the pulse strikes and vaporizes Manasa instead. 

Stalwart releases a live statement claiming the execution is justified under independent system sovereignty rules. In response, the Valtay Corporation destroys Prince Stalwart's yacht, killing Queen Consort Ugloo and the Maestro, creating a major diplomatic crisis.
