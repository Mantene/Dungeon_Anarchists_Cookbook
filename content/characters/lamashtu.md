# Lamashtu

**Lamashtu** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A sentient donkey who was left behind by Chaco after the AI released control of the NPC Grigori. She was later observed interacting with the driver of a chicken truck and subsequently joined other animals moving towards the crawlers' garage.
