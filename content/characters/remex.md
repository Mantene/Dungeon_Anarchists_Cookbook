# Remex

Remex (Remex the Grand) is a Level-1 Soul Leech Capacitor NPC who is found on the second floor of the swordsmen guard depot on Floor 3. He was a former high-performing crawler who exited on Floor 10/11.

## Profile
Remex is a zombified, featherless skyfowl with swirling black smoke and worm-like energy wisps instead of wings. His eyes are swirling black orbs. A stream of golden light connects his chest to a floating **Soul Crystal**, which recharges the town's guards using nearby deaths. 

## History & Role in the Quest
Remex was once a legendary crawler (a "tenner"). After he retired and became a guide, he died and was succeeded as Magistrate by Featherfall. Miss Quill—Remex's wife—conspired to bring Remex back from the dead as a *Soul Leech Capacitor* to siphon immense reserves of mana. Featherfall was killed by the magical feedback during the spell casting, and Miss Quill took over the town under an illusion.

Miss Quill used Remex to collect soul power from incoming prostitutes who were turned into krasue. When Carl confronts Remex, the capacitor is in agony and explains the history of the quest, Miss Quill's spell, and the impending failure of the ritual. 

Following Carl's caging of the soul crystal, Remex is vaporized when the dynamite detonates. This controlled release of soul energy tempers the magical blast and saves the town.
