# Rosetta

**Rosetta Thagra** is a former human-like crawler from the 9th Edition of the crawl. She belongs to the Crest species.

## Biography and Role

During her time as a crawler, Rosetta was famous for her extensive use of explosives, and she contributed numerous recipes, warnings, and notes to the [[the-anarchist-cookbook]]. 

After being released from her indentureship, she directed the documentary film *The Other Side of the Glass* about her home planet. Following its success, the Open Intellect Pacifist Network sponsored her new show, *Shadow Boxer*, which presented earth history through raw, documentary-style interviews.

On Floor 8, Rosetta interviewed Carl and Donut. During the segment, Rosetta spoke in coded language to Carl, referencing recipes from the cookbook and feeding them television references designed to guide them to choose Miami for the second phase of the floor. Because of this, Valtay representative Harbinger intervened and banned Rosetta from future interviews. Soon after, the Pacifist Network sponsors and Rosetta vanished. Rosetta is later shown in the Book 6 Epilogue hiding on the garbage freighter *Homecoming Queen*.
