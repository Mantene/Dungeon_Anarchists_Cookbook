# Rosetta Thagra

**Race:** Crest (humans with no eyebrows; a common former-hunter type)  
**Former occupation:** Crawler (season two seasons after Mordecai's); then hosted _Shadow Boxer_ (OIPAN-funded documentary show about Earth); now fugitive  
**Current location:** Aboard the _Homecoming Queen_ garbage freighter, in Earth orbit  
**Status:** Fugitive. Her show was canceled and she disappeared after the Harbinger incident. Alive.

---

## Background

Rosetta Thagra is a former crawler who wrote the **9th Edition of The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook**. She crawled two seasons after Mordecai. Her specialty: explosives. After exiting the dungeon, she made a documentary called _The Other Side of the Glass_ (about her home planet), which was well-received. The OIPAN then funded _Shadow Boxer_, a weekly show interviewing crawlers to tell Earth's story through their voices.

She appears young (pale, pretty, early twenties appearance) with a blob of yellow/red hair on an otherwise bald head. Her real age is significantly older — the signs show in her voice.

## Book 6

### The Shadow Boxer Interview

Rosetta hosted Carl and Donut on _Shadow Boxer_. The interview was an elaborate attempt to pass secret messages to Carl:

1. **The screencap message:** While Donut was choosing TV show screencaps for the show, every single show Rosetta showed was set in Miami — signaling Carl that Phase 2 should be in the Miami area.

2. **The milk/potatoes dialogue:** Rosetta prompted Carl to talk about cooking "milk and potatoes stew" at the boys' ranch — referencing a note in the cookbook. In the cookbook, a previous author named Milk had written about potatoes being "versatile," and Rosetta (author of the 9th edition) had responded: "Very. Just like all root vegetables." This confirmed to Carl that: the OIPAN employs at least two cookbook authors (Milk + Rosetta), and the toraline root vegetable has a purpose related to portals/mapmaking ink.

Harbinger (the caprid liaison, antagonistic toward Carl) ended the interview early, claiming Carl and Rosetta were "talking in code." He banned Rosetta from future crawler interviews. Zev was furious at Harbinger's overreach.

After this, Rosetta and the entire OIPAN charity organization disappeared. Later confirmed: she is aboard the _Homecoming Queen_ with Tipid and 50,000 former crawlers.

### The Homecoming Queen

Rosetta is aboard the converted garbage freighter _Homecoming Queen_ with **Tipid** (author of the 14th edition of the cookbook) and 50,000 former crawlers who want to see the crawl fail. They have been approved by the AI to enter as **Princess Posse mercenaries** for Faction Wars.

Rosetta and Tipid both know they are likely to die. Tipid paraphrased Doctor Hu: *"I can only promise two things. Eventual death. And eventual justice."*

## The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook

Rosetta is the **author of the 9th Edition**. Her entries focused on portals, mapmaking, and special types of ink. She also made a note agreeing with Milk's entry about feeding information to crawlers: *"Comrade, I agree. One day, we will make certain they all know. Our words will not be lost."*

---

## Book 7 — Killing King Rust

Rosetta served as **Theater Commander** for the Princess Posse during Faction Wars, coordinating the offensive across all fronts.

Her defining act: during **King Rust's parlay**, he arrived in person to warn Carl about Princess Formidable. As he spoke, Rosetta decapitated him with her whip from a tunnel she'd dug beneath the meeting point — the same style of whip he had used to casually kill her friend **Brooks** decades earlier during a previous Faction Wars. She had been waiting for this moment for years.

She then used Rust's retina scan and bank PIN to complete a massive financial transaction: purchasing **two naga generation ships** for humanity's post-crawl use, funded entirely with stolen Skull Empire funds. The deal was organized through Porthus and Sultan Rishi.

**Book 8:** Rosetta is confirmed on Floor 12 as one of the 19 remaining crawlers. Tipid (her Homecoming Queen colleague) is also on Floor 12, having lost both legs during Faction Wars.
