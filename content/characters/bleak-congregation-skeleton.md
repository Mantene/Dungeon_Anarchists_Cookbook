# Bleak Congregation Skeleton

**Bleak Congregation Skeleton** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A member of the enigmatic and powerful Bleak Congregation, this skeleton is present among the crowd to watch Carl's performance and offers him a pat on the back for encouragement.
