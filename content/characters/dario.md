# Dario

**Dario** is a Tigran crawler who participates in the tenth floor's Great Race.

## Biography and Role

Dario is a member of Team One Fine Pig alongside [[nico]]. Unlike his dramatic partner, Dario is relatively level-headed and attempts to calm Nico down during his possessive outbursts. He wears a massive amount of gold chains around his neck and operates their APV.
