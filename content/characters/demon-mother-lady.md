# Demon Mother Lady

**Demon Mother Lady** is a antagonist character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

The 'Demon Mother Lady' is a powerful entity targeted by Samantha. Samantha asserts that this individual, along with her 'jerk son,' tricked her into birthing a monster destined to destroy all of creation, both inside and outside the dungeon. Samantha explicitly states her intention to kill her.
