# Britney

**Britney** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A member of Louis's racing team. In chapter 444, she is mentioned by Louis as speculating about the true identity of 'Eileen,' their team's lowrider.
