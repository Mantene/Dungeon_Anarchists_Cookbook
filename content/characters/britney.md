# Britney

**Race:** Unknown  
**Class:** Unknown  
**Level:** 55 (Book 6) — rapidly rising  
**Party:** Katia's squad (Iceland, Floor 8)  
**Origin:** Ukrainian

A Ukrainian woman who has been through severe trauma. She entered the dungeon in Carl's bubble group and is part of Katia's squad. She had an extinction sigil tattooed under her eye by Book 6 — meaning she wiped out an entire race of something.

---

## Key Events

**Book 5:** Her face was badly burned in the final moments of Floor 6. The burn didn't heal the way most dungeon injuries do — it left stretched, glossy scar tissue covering the left side of her face. She cried frequently after the floor ended, clutching Louis.

**Book 6:** Britney stopped crying and started *working*. She rose from unknown level to Level 55 across Floor 8 — rapidly, methodically. She talked to Tserendolgor (Ren), who apparently had a similar facial scar once. She got an extinction sigil under her eye. She went out fighting on her own.

Donut observed: *"I know a villain origin story when I see one."*

During the Havana battle (Phase 3), Britney was present fighting on the FOSCA building.

---

## Relationships

- **Louis** — close friend; she was clinging to him after Firas's death
- **Tran** — fellow bubble survivor
- **Tserendolgor** — unexpected connection over facial scars

---

## Books 7–8

**Faction Wars (Book 7):** Britney fought with Katia's former squad in Faction Wars. Confirmed among survivors reaching Floor 10.

**Floor 10 — The Pineapple Cabaret (Book 8):** Britney, Louis Santiago, and Chris Andrews 2 used the unofficial Pineapple Cabaret exit. It went wrong — all three ended up in **Sheol (Floor 15)** instead of the surface. Britney is alive in Sheol with Louis (who has gills and a demon-supplied blood-filtering leech) and Chris (who is becoming a werewolf from Scolopendra's first attack).
