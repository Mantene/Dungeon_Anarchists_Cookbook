# Donna

Donna (D-0NAH) is a frisbee-shaped robot NPC that manages the production trailer for the talk show *Danger Zone*.

## Profile
Donna operates the advanced cleaning facilities inside the production trailer. She resizes the cleaning treadmill to wash and polish Princess Donut, her metal crupper, and eventually a protesting Mongo. She insists that Carl also clean his mud-caked cloak, warning him that appearing dirty is considered an insult to the royalty on the panel.
