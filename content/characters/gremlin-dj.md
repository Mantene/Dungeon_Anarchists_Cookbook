# Gremlin DJ

**Gremlin DJ** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

An NPC gremlin tasked with managing the musical entertainment and announcements at the current establishment. He introduces Carl and Donut for their performance before being fatally stabbed in the face by Dwight the unicorn.
