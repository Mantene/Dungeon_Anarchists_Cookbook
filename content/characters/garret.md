# Garret (Garret the Tummy Acher)

**Garret** (often called *Garret the Tummy Acher* or *Belly Acher*) is a Level 1 monster companion (pet) originally belonging to the crawler [[ren]], and later inherited by [[carl]].

## Floor 8 Acquisition
- **Ren's Ownership**: Garret was originally a powerful Level 70 Tummy Acher pet.
- **Carl's Inheritance**: In Chapter 59, following Ren's self-sacrifice, Carl loots Ren's pet carrier containing Garret.
- **Level Reset and Weaknesses**: Due to the transfer rules, Garret is reset to Level 1 and carries five distinct weaknesses:
  1. Sylvan crystal
  2. Hobgoblin sweat
  3. Rivenwing bite
  4. Undead flesh
  5. *Reaper Gaze* (acquired upon transfer)

## Status
- **Affiliation**: Carl's pet/companion
- **Status**: Active (Level 1)
