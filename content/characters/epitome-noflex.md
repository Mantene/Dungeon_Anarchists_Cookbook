# Epitome Noflex

**Epitome Noflex** is a high-elf noblewoman mentioned on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Profile & Narrative Connections
- **Family**: She is the mother of [[epitome-tagg]], the leader of the hunter faction [[the-dream]]. 
- **The Photo Incident**: Epitome Noflex is described as a bald but strikingly beautiful high elf. Before Floor 6, the crawler [[carl]] obtained a photograph of her. The crawler Louis took the photograph and had the changeling Juice Box transform into her likeness so he could engage in sexual acts with her.
- **Retaliation**: This act of disrespect enraged her son Epitome Tagg, who ordered the entire Dream hunter faction to focus their efforts on hunting and killing Louis on Floor 6.
