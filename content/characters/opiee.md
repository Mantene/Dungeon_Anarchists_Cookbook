# Opiee

**Opiee** is the designated Adjutant for Team Madness during the Ninth Floor Faction Wars.

## Profile & Traits
- **Race**: Bune (a tiny, floating dragon-like creature).
- **Role**: Faction Adjutant for Team Madness.
- **Personality**: Objective and analytically minded, possessing a professional interest in tactical theory and system mechanics.

## History
In Book 7, Chapter 18, Opiee represents Team Madness during the minor quorum adjudication following the failed sabotage of [[carl]]'s Glory Bound treatment. Opiee reviews the event data alongside Adjutants [[panford]] and [[baroness-victory]]. Opiee notes that while the plan failed, Stockade's theory of utilizing the persisting *Summon Ally* prompt during unconsciousness was a fascinating concept and was worth the risk.
