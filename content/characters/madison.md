# Madison

**Madison** is a Level 10 human NPC who operates as a Human Resources Associate and benefits manager for the fourth floor of the World Dungeon.

## Biography and Role
Madison resembles a typical suburban, middle-aged soccer mom with a tight facelift. She wears a soot-stained white business dress and is missing one shoe. She works as the benefits manager for the Kravyad and the six **Station Mimics**. 

She is rescued by Carl's party in Chapter 92 as she flees from the burning Substation E while being chased by a wolf ghoul. Safe inside the cab of the *Nightmare Express*, Madison behaves like an entitled customer, complaining that passengers are not allowed in the engine cab and demanding they take her to Yard A so they can turn themselves in. 

Carl interrogates Madison using the Cookbook's "Worn Path Method," pointing out contradictions in her memories of her apartment and Yard A. Disoriented and weeping as her programmed reality breaks down, Madison reveals critical intelligence: she admits she is the benefits manager for the six Station Mimics—monstrous mimics disguised as Terminus Stations (such as Station 433 and 435) designed to devour passengers to feed them. This prompts Carl to immediately warn Daniel Bautista II to avoid getting off at Terminus Station.
