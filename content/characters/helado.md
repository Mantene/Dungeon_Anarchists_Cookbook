# Helado

**Helado** was an ice fairy mercenary who previously worked alongside [[quemada]], [[finley]], and [[olga]]. 

## History & Dungeon Presence
Helado is mentioned by Olga in [[441-book-8-chapter-42-forest-flight]]. Olga explains that Helado was highly skilled at calming down Quemada's panic attacks. Helado was killed by a chain demon prior to the events of the tenth floor, which triggered Quemada's severe psychological deterioration.
