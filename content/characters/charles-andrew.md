# Charles Andrew

**Charles Andrew** was a Level 58 human Armored Mage crawler who fought for the Princess Posse during the Ninth Floor Faction Wars.

## Profile & Traits
- **Class & Level**: Level 58 Armored Mage.
- **Status**: Deceased (puppeted by a brain worm).

## History
In Book 7, Chapter 29, Charles was killed and infected by a Valtay brain worm named Goldblum. The worm puppeted his corpse to infiltrate the FUPA stronghold. Charles's puppeted body, acting alongside a panicked [[bucket-boy]], carried a wounded Yenk into the castle lobby. However, [[carl]] cross-referenced Charles's presence with his system casualty list, realized he was officially dead, and tackled him. Carl beat the puppeted corpse's face to pulp with his spiked gauntlet and stomped the emerging brain worm.
