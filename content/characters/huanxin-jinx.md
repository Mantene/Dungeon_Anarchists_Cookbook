# Huanxin Jinx

**Huanxin Jinx** is a wealthy Grixist heiress and a prominent sponsor in the Celestial Ascendency.

## Biography and Role

Huanxin Jinx is known for sponsoring and controlling the storm goddess [[dodola]] during the dungeon crawls. 

In the backstory detailed in the Book 6 Prologue, [[odette]] and [[armita]] attempted to bribe her to have Dodola cast a storm spell on the exit puzzle of the tenth floor. Huanxin repeatedly raised her demands, first from 10% to 33%, and finally to 50% of Odette's tenth-floor bonus, threatening to use Dodola to zero out the party if they refused. 

Odette rejected the final demand and instead directed her party to summon Dodola by sacrificing [[uzzi]] with the [[dart-of-ophiotaurus]], trapping and destroying the goddess inside a containment field. This act humiliated Huanxin and caused her to lose her god in the game, prompting her to make deadly threats against both Odette and Armita.
