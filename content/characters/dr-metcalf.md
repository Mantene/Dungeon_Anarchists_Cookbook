# Dr. Metcalf

**Type:** AI (dungeon-assigned vehicle GPS unit)  
**Vehicle:** Big Shot Chicken food truck  
**Personality:** Sarcastic, dry, narrates race events with editorial commentary  
**Status:** Alive — survived all seven heats of Floor 10

The GPS unit assigned to Carl and Donut's Big Shot Chicken food truck on Floor 10. An AI with a distinct personality who provided race narration, tactical commentary, and sarcastic observations throughout the floor.

---

## Background

When Carl and Donut spun the vehicle assignment wheel at the start of Floor 10 and got the **Big Shot Chicken** food truck, Dr. Metcalf came with it — a vehicle GPS AI already installed and very much his own entity. He is not a dungeon mob or an NPC in the traditional sense; he is an installed system that gained a personality.

---

## Book 8 — Floor 10

Dr. Metcalf served as:
- **Race narrator** — providing live commentary on heat conditions, opponent status, and track hazards
- **Navigation** — routing the truck through complex terrain from cave systems to factory floors to vertical tower courses
- **Tactical advisor** — flagging mechanical issues and upgrade options between heats

His defining trait is dry sarcasm delivered in a tone that suggests he has seen many idiots try to race before and Carl's group is, if not the worst, certainly memorable.

He survived all seven heats, including the catastrophic Heat 6 (where the truck's gremlin mechanic Hedy died in an ice-cream truck crash). His survival after Hedy's death meant the truck had no living mechanic for between-heat repairs.
