# Dr. Metcalf

**Dr. Metcalf** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

Dr. Metcalf is an intelligent entity or AI that communicates with Carl and Donut, often providing crucial information or warnings. In this chapter, Metcalf provides updates regarding environmental hazards, specifically clarifying that the initial, devastating impacts are considered 'light hail' by the Dungeon's systems.
