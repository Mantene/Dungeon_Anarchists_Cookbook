# Elian

**Elian** is a deceased human crawler who entered the World Dungeon in the same party as [[sister-ines-quiteria]], [[anton-lopez]], and [[paz-lo]].

## Biography and Role

Elian was the party member who originally convinced the group to enter the dungeon, arguing that they "needed everybody they could get." 

Early in the crawl (on Floor 1 or 2), Sister Ines suffered an episode due to her untreated mental illness (or "toxoplasmosis" effects), prompting Elian and another party member to try to kill her. To protect her, Anton Lopez killed Elian and the other teammate. Paz Lo later expresses regret over Elian's death, highlighting the complex, tragic bonds within the squad.
