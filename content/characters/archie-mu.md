# Archie Mu

**Archie Mu** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A crawler known as "the ladybug guy" and the father of Oliver. In the simulated reality on the fourth floor, he is depicted as the divorced husband of Mackenzie, though the simulation shows a version where they are still married. He entered the dungeon with his son and disappeared, having been previously seen at the Pineapple Cabaret.
