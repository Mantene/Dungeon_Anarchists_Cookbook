# Sebastian

Sebastian is a **Bopca Protector** (Level 21) who serves as the caretaker of the safe room designed as a Peruvian Taco Bell on Floor 1. Like Tally, he falls in love with [[princess-donut]] due to her extremely high Charisma, brushing her hair and gifting her his brush, while serving [[carl]] T-bone steaks and Peruvian beer.
