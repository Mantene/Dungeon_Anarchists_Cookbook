# Princess Chandra, Esquire

**Princess Chandra, Esquire** is a Syndicate attorney assigned to represent [[princess-donut]] during the exit deal negotiations at the end of the ninth floor.

## Profile & Role
- **Faction Wars Epilogue**: During the negotiations in the Outreach Guild, [[quasar]] informs [[carl]] that Donut was assigned Princess Chandra as her lawyer. Donut rejected all of Chandra's exit deals.
- **Carl's Wife**: Quasar warns Carl that Princess Chandra is also Carl's newly registered wife, a development arranged through system/sponsorship machinations.
