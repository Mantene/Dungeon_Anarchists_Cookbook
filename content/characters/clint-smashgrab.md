# Clint Smashgrab

**Clint Smashgrab** is a Level 50 Were-Kin (were-castor/were-beaver) Elite who fights alongside [[tsarina-signet]] on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Profile & Traits
- **Background**: Formerly of a peaceful carpenter race called the **Chee** (which was completely wiped out/transformed by the High Elves), Clint was a schoolboy under the care of [[nadine]]. 
- **Appearance**: In his Chee form, he is a pale-skinned, bald, Rambo-like three-foot warrior wearing black studded leather and a red bandana. He is missing a leg and has a curved metallic bar prosthetic.
- **Transformation**: Clint is a **were-castor** (were-beaver). In this form, he is covered in oily brown fur with buck teeth, a lisp, and a flat black tail.
- **Combat Style**: He fights with a mace and a machete, has high constitution, and is immune to the paralysis effect of night weasels.

## Floor 6 Actions
- **Assisting Carl**: In [[161-book-5-chapter-16-were-beavers]] (Chapter 16), Clint and [[holger]] accompany Carl and Donut back to Point Mongo, helping them defeat a pack of night weasels.
- **Fort Freedom Assault**: In [[170-book-5-chapter-25-future-huntress]] (Chapter 25), Clint participates in the assault on Fort Freedom.
- **Death**: In [[173-book-5-chapter-28-big-tina]] (Chapter 28), it is revealed that Clint was killed (ripped apart) during the battle on the southern shore by the dinosaur [[big-tina]]. His memory is later preserved as a child form in Nadine's tattoo on Signet's thigh.

## Graphify Deductions
- **Tragic Cycle of Chee**: Clint's tragic life (transformed as a child, forced to fight as an elite, and ultimately dying in the wilderness) represents the Syndicate's cruel rewriting of peaceful populations into militarized assets. His transformation into a beaver (castor) is a literal play on the Chee's history as carpenters, showing the system's mocking irony.
