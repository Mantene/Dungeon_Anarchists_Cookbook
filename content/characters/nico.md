# Nico

**Nico** is a Tigran crawler who participates in the tenth floor's Great Race.

## Biography and Role

Nico is a member of Team One Fine Pig alongside his Tigran partner, [[dario]]. He is aggressively protective of his team's pig mount, Penelope, and acts with a dramatic, possessive personality. During the race, Nico gets into arguments with [[princess-donut]], ordering other racers to stay away from the pig. His team finishes in last place during Heat Three, forcing them to accept an audience-voted vehicle wrap featuring anime Tigrans kissing the backside of a pig.
