# Mei W.

**Mei W.** is a crawler participating in the fourth-floor crawl.

## Biography and Role
Mei W. is introduced in Chapter 90 as one of the crawlers on Carl's broadcast list. 

After Carl messages the network with the discovery that equipping the souvenir conductor hat allows safe passage through the portals at the end of the lines, Mei W. replies in distress. She reports that every member of her party sold their conductor hats for 5,000 gold each early in the floor, unaware of their critical role in escaping the Iron Tangle.
