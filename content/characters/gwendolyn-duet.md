# Gwendolyn Duet

Gwendolyn Duet (often shortened to **Gwen**) is a human crawler who progresses to Floor 5.

## Class & Stats
- **Class**: **Boring Ol' Fighter**. A melee class.
- **Dungeon Status**: Gwen is Level 27 and is situated in the Land Quadrant of Bubble #543. She has a Club Vanquisher ring.
- In [[112-book-4-chapter-2-the-wasteland-castle]], she messages Carl to update him on the Land Quadrant. She reports that her group is facing giant snakes, giant spiders, and half-human, half-scorpion warriors, as well as chainsaw-carrying bird monsters. Their castle is the *Sandcastle of the Mad Dune Mage*.
