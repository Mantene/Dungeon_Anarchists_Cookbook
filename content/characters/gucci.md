# Gucci

**Gucci** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A white Maltese dog belonging to Imani's family in the apartment 310 simulation. Unlike the human shells, Gucci's eyes are not X'd out, suggesting a different status or fate. Simulated Imani picks him up and hugs him tightly as the simulation collapses.
