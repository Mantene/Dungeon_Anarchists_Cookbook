# Joel

**Joel** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A Bactrian camel whose neck was broken in One Fine Pig's garage. His corpse is later used by Carl's team in the water park race, temporarily reanimated by Donut's 'Second Chance' spell to serve as a driver.
