# Warlord Hortense

**Warlord Hortense** (also referred to as **Hortense Leadership Unit**) was the leader of Team Two, [[the-operatic-collective|The Operatic Collective]], during the Faction War Games on Floor 9 in Book 7. Hortense was a collective entity consisting of multiple slime/blob creatures merged into a single mass (specifically, Voting Admin Cell Mass Number Three).

## Biography & Role in the Dungeon

Hortense first appeared in Book 6, Chapter 21, representing the Operatics during discussions. He was described as a blob-like creature who explained that his form was composed of three entities.

In Book 7, Hortense assumed leadership of the Operatic Collective's forces in the Faction War Games. During the battle on Floor 9, he stayed behind the front lines, carried on a long, flat litter surrounded by a dozen defensive shields and administrative staff to project "strength." After the death of [[king-rust]], Hortense declared himself the new leader of the entire Bloc alliance, a claim disputed by [[prince-stalwart]]. 

Hortense ordered the Operatic forces to attack the crawlers. However, the advance was halted when [[volteeg]], a former crawler turned Weeper tank driver for the Operatic Collective, deliberately drove his explosive-filled Weeper tank into the center of the Bloc army next to Hortense's caravan. The resulting massive explosion bypassed Hortense's defensive shields, killing him instantly and eliminating the Operatic Collective from the Faction War Games.

## Status
- **Status**: Deceased (killed by Volteeg's suicide detonation in Book 7, Chapter 35 / Global 340)
- **Affiliation**: The Operatic Collective
