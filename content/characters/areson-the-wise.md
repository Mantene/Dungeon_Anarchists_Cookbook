# Areson the Wise

**Areson the Wise** is a Level 45 Ogre Elite who fights alongside [[tsarina-signet]] on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Profile & Traits
- **Background**: Areson is one of three brothers (along with [[apollon-the-mighty]] and [[herman-the-fleet]]) born from the splitting of the three-headed nodling [[di-we]], who died protecting Signet.
- **Traits**: Despite his name, Areson has a simplistic way of speaking and behavior, representing a third of the knowledge of his progenitor. He has a taste for elf blood, which he describes as "yummy."

## Floor 6 Actions
- **Hunter Patrols**: Areson helps clear hunters from the forest paths. In [[160-book-5-chapter-15-hunting-map]] (Chapter 15), he brings [[carl]] the corpse of the elf hunter [[arwick]].
- **Fort Freedom Assault**: In [[170-book-5-chapter-25-future-huntress]] (Chapter 25), Areson captures the orc hunter [[future-huntress]] to use as a sacrifice for Signet's summoning. He remains on the shore during the assault on Fort Freedom to guard [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]].
- **Claude Sludgington Fight**: In [[172-book-5-chapter-27-claude-sludgington]] (Chapter 27), Areson rides the teleported borough boss [[claude-sludgington-the-fourth]] during the chaotic battle on the shore.

## Graphify Deductions
- **Nodling Splitting Mechanism**: Areson's origin confirms the nodling race splitting mechanic—when a nodling dies, their heads emerge as new individuals who share a portion of the progenitor's soul and memories. This explains the connection between Areson, Apollon, and Herman, and sets a precedent for how nodling crawlers like the Popov brothers operate.
