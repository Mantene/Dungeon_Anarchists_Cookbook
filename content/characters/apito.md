# Apito

**Apito** is a deity in the World Dungeon. She is associated with the High Elves' religious system on Floor 3 and Floor 6, and is the namesake of the Apito Education Center on Floor 3.

## Narrative Involvement
- **Floor 3**: Apito is first mentioned on Floor 3, where city elves of the 201st Security Group guard the Apito Education Center. Carl detonates a custom *Boom Jug* here in [[069-book-2-chapter-20-temple-confrontation]].
- **Floor 6 Climax**: During [[the-butchers-masquerade]], Apito is responsible for compiling the gala's guestlist. She invites [[tsarina-signet]], one of Imogen's greatest enemies, to test Queen [[imogen]]. In [[217-book-5-chapter-72-the-seal-breaks]], Apito "turns her back," breaking the ballroom's protection seal and allowing the final battle to commence.
- **Memorial Crystal**: Following her intervention, her soul energy crystallizes into the [[memorial-crystal-apito]], a key item Carl must obtain for the quest *Find Out Who Killed My Son*.
