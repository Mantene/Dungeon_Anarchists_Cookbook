# Donita Grace (Deceased)

**Donita Grace** was a human crawler participating in the fourth-floor crawl (The Iron Tangle). She is deceased.

## Biography & Role in the Crawl
Donita Grace was first introduced communicating with [[carl]] via his broadcast contact list, asking about the transit rules and the "Fulvous" line train. 

In Chapter 102, a final message from Donita Grace is received on Carl's broadcast list, marked with the system warning: `Warning: This message is from a deceased crawler.` Her final words are a desperate warning: *"Holy shit. Holy shit. Guys. Don’t go to stop 24. They’re everywhere. Millions of them. Worse than the grubs. Don’t..."* 

This warning reveals that Station 24 has been overrun by millions of **Krakaren Crotch Dumplings**—rapidly growing parasites spawned by the explosion of stage-three DT monsters—making the station a death trap.

## Graphify Deductions
- **Tragic Messenger**: Donita's death highlights the danger of the crawl's final days on Floor 4, where even the "safe" stairwell stations (like Station 24, which had no ghouls) become nesting grounds for the floor's ultimate parasitic threat. Her final broadcast acts as a critical piece of intelligence that redirects other crawlers away from a lethal trap.
