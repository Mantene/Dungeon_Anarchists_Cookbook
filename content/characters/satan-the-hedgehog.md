# Satan the Hedgehog

**Satan the Hedgehog** is a antagonist character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

Satan the Hedgehog is the invulnerable, kaiju-sized country boss and owner of Satan's Water Park, the current dungeon floor. He is not a boss meant to be fought directly, instead acting as a constant commentator on the racers' progress and occasionally interfering by poking them. He speaks with a British accent, which is a particular point of irritation for Donut, and he seems to enjoy the spectacle of the race.
