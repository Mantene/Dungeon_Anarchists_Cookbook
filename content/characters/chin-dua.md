# Chin'Dua

**Chin'Dua** is a Level 50 Draconian hunter who operates as an unaffiliated "Striver."

## Profile & Death
- **Encounter**: In [[150-book-5-chapter-5-left-to-fester]] (Chapter 5), [[carl]] finds Chin'Dua stumbling drunk outside *The Scuttlebutt* saferoom in Zockau, drinking a "Dirty Shirley" (a drink made famous by [[princess-donut]]).
- **The Mark**: Carl marks Chin'Dua with the *Ring of Divine Suffering*, giving Carl the *Left to Fester* debuff, which prevents healing until Chin'Dua is dead. Carl also uses his toe ring to spread this debuff to the flying hunter [[xindy]].
- **Death**: Carl beats Chin'Dua, fracturing his bones, and crushes his head under his foot to kill him and resolve the *Left to Fester* debuff. Chin'Dua's severed hand is harvested by Carl as a hunting trophy.
