# Miss Quill

Miss Quill is a Level-30 Skyfowl NPC who acts as the receptionist, gate guardian, and assistant to the Magistrate in the administrative building of Floor 3. She is revealed to be the actual Magistrate and the mastermind behind the krasue and elf cult in town.

## Profile
Miss Quill is an elderly, gray-tinged skyfowl with a cracked beak who smells of Icy Hot. She is a dedicated gatekeeper, refusing to allow crawlers to see the Magistrate. She has a massive collection of dungeon beanie babies (over 300) lined up on shelves in her office, including rare ones in glass cases. 

When Carl dumps a dead prostitute on her desk (Chapter 18, [[067-book-2-chapter-18-magistrates-office]]), she calls the guards, but they ignore her because Carl holds a Gate Pass. She frantically leaves the room to find a towel to clean the fluid from her desk, allowing Carl and Donut to inspect the locked door.

## Mastermind Reveal & Death
In Chapter 22 ([[071-book-2-chapter-22-burgundy-revelation]]), Burgundy reveals that the actual Magistrate, Featherfall, has been dead for a long time, and Miss Quill was running the settlement the whole time. Miss Quill was a granddaughter of the skyfowl royal family who wanted to reclaim the Over City for the skyfowl by completing a massive, three-part heirloom spell called **The Final War**. 

To fuel the spell, she used her undead husband [[remex]] as a soul capacitor, manipulated the [[201st-security-group|201st Security Group]] by pretending to be an angel of Apito, and turned incoming female prostitutes into [[krasue]] to gather skyfowl feathers (which served as focus items for the spell's protection list). Miss Quill is killed by Carl's dynamite explosion in Chapter 22, which triggers the *CockBlock* achievement because her death was required for [[daniel-bautista-2]]'s quest.
