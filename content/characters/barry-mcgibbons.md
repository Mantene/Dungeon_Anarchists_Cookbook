# Barry McGibbons

**Barry McGibbons** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

Bartholomew Eli McGibbons, known as Barry, was Elle's late husband of forty-three years. He passed away thirty-five years prior to the events of Book 8. Elle often reminisces about him, particularly his gentle nature and love for the song "Have You Ever Seen the Rain?", and his memory serves as a significant emotional anchor for her, reminding her of the family she had and now seeks to protect.
