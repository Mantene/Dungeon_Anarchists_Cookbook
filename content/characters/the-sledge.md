# The Sledge

**The Sledge** (often referred to as **Sledgie**) is a large rock-monster Cretin NPC employee at the [[desperado-club]].

## Biography
The Sledge is a taciturn rock monster who works at the Desperado Club. He frequently casts a blue magic protection spell over crawlers, such as [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]]. In Chapter 94, Donut playfully rides on his shoulder, and he wears a pink feather boa, a cowboy hat, and a giant pinback button from the "Penis Parade" strip club given to him by Donut.
