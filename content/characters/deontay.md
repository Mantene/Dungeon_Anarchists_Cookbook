# Deontay

**Deontay** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A "human shell" inhabiting apartment 310 in the simulated reality. He becomes aggressive when the protagonists disrupt the simulation by throwing a PlayStation controller, and later transforms into a host for the Sacred Feaster Scarab Beetles.
