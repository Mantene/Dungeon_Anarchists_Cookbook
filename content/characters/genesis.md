# Genesis

**Genesis** is a lady mantaur NPC racer on the tenth floor of the dungeon.

## Biography and Role

Genesis is a Level 95 Lady Mantaur Dominator and a member of **Team Lady Dominators and the Gimp**, alongside [[rapture]] and the half-mantaur [[corcunda]]. She has straight black hair, wears a white racing suit with a red-and-blue stripe, and wears a white cowboy hat. She speaks with a Southern-fried Kentucky accent and worships the deity Enyo.

In [[412-book-8-chapter-13-quest-reunite-corky]], she and Rapture confront [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] in the cul-de-sac, mockingly calling them "roadkill" before heading to the Hairpin bar. In [[418-book-8-chapter-19-penelope-the-pig]], she and Rapture argue with Nico at the start of Heat Two.
