# Princess Vinata

**Princess Vinata** (also known as the **Sepsis Whore**) is the daughter of the Sultan, a war veteran, and the lead advisor to her father outside the dungeon. Within the dungeon, she serves as the Warlord of Team Three, [[the-blood-sultanate|The Blood Sultanate]], during the Faction War Games on Floor 9 in Book 7.

## Appearance & Personality

Vinata is a massive, glossy-white naga with golden scales along the edges of her wide cobra hood. Unlike other nagas, she has six arms and solid red eyes with no pupils. She wears a golden breastplate embossed with a symbol of a tree branch and a single flower. Her arms are adorned with golden bracelets that jingle as she moves. 

Vinata possesses a supernatural, electric charisma and aura of authority that makes others feel small and awed in her presence. She maintains a polite, serene smile even when discussing grim matters or threatening her opponents.

## Biography & Role in Book 7

### Warlord Council
During the Faction War Games on Floor 9, Vinata represented the Blood Sultanate at the Warlord Council. She used the meeting to privately speak with [[carl]], offering him a separate peace. She proposed that Carl join her father's court after the crawl, and offered to "take care of the Katia problem" (referring to the conflict between Carl and [[katia-grim]] over the flower that determines who can leave the floor). Vinata revealed that her faction was fully aware of Carl's planned offensive and was prepared.

However, during the meeting, [[odette]] revealed that the Sultanate owned the studio behind the failed drama *Blood Hunter*, which had resulted in the death of Miriam Dom (the owner of [[prepotente|Prepotente]]). Carl realized that Prepotente had already slaughtered the majority of Vinata's standing army in revenge, leaving her forces severely depleted and forcing her to bluff at the council.

### The Escape Plan & Hunt
Traditionally, naga warlords in Faction Wars escape their strongholds when besieged by using a deity-based loophole to flee to [[club-vanquisher]]. This season, with the goddess [[theia]] sponsored, Vinata's court utilized the obscure light god [[khepri]]. 

Under Khepri's ritual, a female guard pledged to the god, causing Vinata and her remaining court to be transformed into tiny beetles. The guard swallowed the beetles and used the spells *Welcome Home* and *Travel the Path* to teleport directly to the Temple of Khepri in Club Vanquisher, where she would give birth to the beetles to restore them to their original forms.

To defeat the Blood Sultanate and free [[princess-donut]] and Katia from their contract constraints, Carl's team launched a raid to assassinate Vinata and the three members of her line of succession (her two Ikbal cousins and the head of her guard). While [[elle-mcgibbons]] and [[imani-c]]'s team secured the naga castle and killed the head of the guard, Carl, Katia, and Prepotente prepared to breach the Temple of Khepri inside Club Vanquisher to hunt down Vinata and her cousins.
