# Orthrus

Orthrus is a Level 10 two-headed hell-hound puppy. He is the pet of the deceased Geyrun, the son of the fire god [[emberus]]. Despite his gargantuan size (filling half the Air Quadrant bubble), he is fragile and acts like a playful, frightened puppy.

## Biography & Actions
- **Summoning**: He is accidentally summoned from the Nothing to Bubble 543 when Carl activates the Gate of the Feral Gods.
- **Rescue**: In [[139-book-4-chapter-29-where-the-red-god-glows]], the system issues the **Where the Red God Glows** quest because Orthrus is being shocked by lightning and bitten by concierge sharks. [[princess-donut]] uses *Heal Critter* to heal him, and *Scroll of Meat Hooks* to lure him out of the water and bubble, completing the quest.
- **World Quest & Rescue**: The system immediately starts a World Quest, **Get Orthrus**, offering 1 million gold to anyone who kills the puppy. [[quan-ch]] and other crawlers try to snipe the dog in [[140-book-4-chapter-30-world-quest-party]] and [[141-book-4-chapter-31-a-deposit-on-quan]]. Carl heals the puppy with Mordecai's Special Brew (granting 30s invulnerability) and summons Emberus using a celestial grenade. Emberus finds the puppy and returns with him to the Celestial Halls.
