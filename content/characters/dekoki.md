# Dekoki

**Dekoki** is a kappa NPC racer on the tenth floor of the dungeon.

## Biography and Role

Dekoki is a Level 84 Seductress Kappa and a member of **Team Yokai**, alongside a ghost, a red ogre, and a floating paper lantern. 

In [[414-book-8-chapter-15-the-lollipop]], she is seen performing an off-key rendition of Taylor Dayne's "Tell It to My Heart" on the stage of the Lollipop bar during a crawler gathering. To prevent the water in her head bowl from spilling while she bounces and sings, she wears a protective cover made of Saran Wrap and rubber bands on her head.
