# Yvette (Deceased)

**Yvette** was the teenage daughter of crawlers [[frank-q]] and [[maggie-my]]. She is deceased.

## Biography
Before the dungeon collapse, Yvette had a history of running away from her parents, who had separated five years prior. On the night of the collapse, she was picked up by the Pierce County Sheriff's office. Her uncle, a sheriff's deputy, called Frank (who was on surveillance for Customs Enforcement) and Maggie (a Seattle PD detective) to retrieve her. The family was in the middle of a massive argument in the parking lot at 2:00 AM when the world collapsed, forcing them all into the dungeon together.

## Role in the Crawl & Death
Unlike her parents, Yvette was not aggressive and refused to fight monsters, falling behind in levels. In an attempt to force her to grow stronger, her parents obtained the **Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering** (from a legendary "That's the Spirit" box) and forced Yvette to wear it and use its *Mark for Death* skill.

Yvette marked [[carl]] before her parents launched their ambush on Floor 1. However, Carl escaped into a safe room. Because the ring's curse prevents the wearer from healing as long as their mark is alive, Yvette's injuries from Carl's dynamite trap could not heal. To end her agonizing pain and free the mark, her mother, Maggie My, choked Yvette to death.

## Graphify Deductions
- **Paranoia Mechanics**: Yvette's story highlights the extreme lengths to which the dungeon system pushes families to betray their morals. The *Mark for Death* ring is a precursor to the PVP coupons, designed to drive crawlers into murderhobo behavior.
