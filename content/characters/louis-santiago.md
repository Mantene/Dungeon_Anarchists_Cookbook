# Louis Santiago 2

**Race:** Unknown  
**Class:** Unknown (has a spell called _Cloud of Exhaust_)  
**Level:** 30+ (Book 4); higher by Book 6  
**Party:** Katia's squad (Iceland, Floor 8); former bubble group  
**Relationship:** Fiancé of Juice Box (NPC changeling)

---

## Background

Louis was part of the Floor 5 bubble group with Carl and Donut — a group of crawlers trapped in a single bubble environment. He was initially described as a heavy drinker, but sobered up during Floor 4 events and became useful.

His best friend was **Firas M**, who died on Floor 6 during the Butcher's Masquerade (accidentally killed by a lightning redirect). Louis was devastated. He still hasn't fully recovered.

He ate so much Captain Crunch in the dungeon that his food dispenser now only offers Captain Crunch.

---

## Books 4–6

Part of the Sea Quadrant/bubble group. Formed close bonds with Firas, Britney, and Tran. On Floor 6, Firas was killed and Tran lost both legs. Louis organized the Christmas celebration in the guild on Floor 8 despite (or because of) the ongoing trauma, arguing that *tonight is fucking Christmas* regardless of what was happening. His speech to Carl about why the Christmas party mattered was one of the more grounding moments of the floor.

---

## Juice Box

Louis developed an unexpected romantic relationship with **Juice Box**, the expert changeling NPC who became self-aware. She is his fiancée. The relationship is complicated because: (1) Samantha has an obsessive inappropriate crush on Louis, (2) Juice Box has become extraordinarily dangerous (she decapitated a Faction Wars representative and detonated a pus grenade in a meeting), and (3) Juice Box is now a **co-warlord of the NPC Home Team** in Faction Wars alongside Ferdinand the cat.

Donut advised Louis: *"Make sure you tell her she's pretty every day. Don't eat potato chips in bed."*

---

## Book 6

Louis and his squad (Britney, Tran, Bautista, Katia) were in Iceland. They obtained a key. Made it to Floor 9 via the Iceland stairwell.

Louis delivered the Christmas party speech and organized the celebration. He sent everyone a present. He was present during the Havana battle.

## Book 7

### Floor 9 — Airship, Heart Transplant, Divorce

Louis captained **Party Planner** for bombing runs and scouting missions across the battlefield. He was given the rank of Army Air General.

**The Epitome Tagg organ incident.** Tagg was transitioning to a post-physical robot form and somehow his electronic heart/lungs ended up inside Louis (during an operation gone wrong). When Tagg's Dream castle was taken, he threatened to stop Louis's heart. Solution: Samantha volunteered the Princess Lunette flesh golem body for donor organs. Mordecai and Juice Box performed a transplant. Louis now has **naiad organs and gills**. Mongo ate Tagg's extracted organs — killing Tagg and the Dream team.

**Juice Box served Louis divorce papers** just before she jumped into the Nothing per Justice Light's plan. Louis stood there watching her go, not saying anything.

**Technical fatherhood.** When Katia chose pregnancy as her orchid option, she asked Louis to be the technical father. He agreed. Katia's son (will be a boy) is technically his. The goddess blessed the arrangement.

**Currently:** On Floor 10 with gills. Constantly spraying water from his new gills. Has a stolen Reaver airship in the hangar (Florin captured one).

## Book 8

### Floor 10 — Racing and the Pineapple Cabaret

Louis participated in Floor 10's racing heats. He had a stolen Reaver airship in the hangar from Floor 9 (Florin captured one; it couldn't be taken to Floor 10 per rules, so Louis left it).

**The Pineapple Cabaret:** Louis, Britney, and Chris Andrews 2 used the secret unofficial dungeon exit organized by Li Na — a route through the dungeon's cleaner bot infrastructure. For Li Na and others, it led safely to the surface. For Louis's group, it went wrong.

They ended up in **Sheol (Floor 15)** instead of the surface.

**In Sheol:**
- Louis is surviving with a **blood-filtering leech** provided by **Forkith** (a small red demon associated with the Sheol underground resistance)
- His gills are functional in Sheol's environment
- **Chris Andrews 2** was partially transformed into a **werewolf** by Scolopendra's first attack during Floor 11 — he had to be knocked unconscious
- **Britney** is with them

**Current status:** Alive in Sheol (Floor 15); has gills; with Britney and an unconscious werewolf Chris.
