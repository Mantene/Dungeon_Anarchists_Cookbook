# Lickity Spit

**Lickity Spit** was a procedurally generated sluggalo sapper who worked under [[justice-light]]'s direction during the ninth floor Faction Wars in *[[dinniman-2024-tir|This Inevitable Ruin]]*.

## Death
Lickity Spit was killed during trap construction when he accidentally took a wrong turn down a hallway and triggered a proximity scythe trap.
