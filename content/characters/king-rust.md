# King Rust

**King Rust** was the sovereign ruler of [[the-skull-empire|The Skull Empire]], husband of [[queen-consort-ugloo]], and father of [[prince-stalwart]], [[the-maestro|Prince Maestro]], and [[princess-formidable]]. He was a powerful orc who entered the dungeon in Book 7 to lead his faction's forces during the Faction War Games on Floor 9.

## Biography & Role in Book 7

In Book 7, King Rust personally entered the dungeon to lead the Skull Empire horde. He was seen wearing glittering, feather-covered armor and rode a giant swamp yak named [[gonk]]. Unlike his impulsive sons, Rust was portrayed as weary, defeatist, and deeply aware of the impending doom of his empire. 

During a registered parlay with [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] (Book 7, Chapter 34 / Global 338), Rust appeared distraught and emotional. He revealed that his daughter, Princess Formidable, had commandeered a cruiser and was heading to a monitor station near Venus to trigger a solar system failsafe, vaporizing the star and killing everyone in the system to prevent the "insane" dungeon AI from spreading to the galactic center. Rust asked Carl to send a plea to Formidable to stop. 

Carl negotiated a ceasefire and retreat of the Bloc armies under the guise of preparing for the failsafe, knowing privately that the failsafe was already deactivated. However, as the parlay bubble collapsed and Rust was returning to his camp, [[rosetta]] erupted from the ground under an invisibility cloak, whipped Rust's head off, and looted his corpse—retinal scanning his eyes to transfer billions of credits out of his bank accounts.
