# Skarn

Skarn is a Level 3 Changeling Juvenile NPC living in Hump Town.

## Characteristics & Role
Skarn initially appears as a grey, featureless humanoid mannequin but is capable of shifting his shape. Under the guidance of his guardian Flint, Skarn takes the shape of human visitors to make them comfortable, mimicking Carl's human form.

In [[112-book-4-chapter-2-the-wasteland-castle]], Carl visits Skarn's home and pays him a gold coin to use the Enchanted Gnome FarSeerer telescope on his roof. Skarn shares vital lore:
- Gnomes bombed their previous village, killing his parents.
- Gnomes don't bomb Hump Town because the town hall contains something the gnome leader wants to keep safe (and it eats mushrooms).
