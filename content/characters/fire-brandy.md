# Fire Brandy

**Fire Brandy** is a Level 75 Lesser Demon (specifically a Sheol MILF) NPC who operates as the stoker/firewoman of the *Nightmare Express* on the fourth floor of the World Dungeon.

## Biography and Role
Fire Brandy is a red-skinned, black-haired demon woman styled after a 1950s rockabilly pin-up, with two black horns rising from her hair, which is tied with a red bandana. She resides entirely inside the firebox boiler of the *Nightmare Express* (steam engine 666). Despite her demonic appearance, she speaks with a German accent and is a non-combatant NPC.

Lesser Demon MILFs who learn water magic are often employed as steam boiler stokers. While pregnant, they go into a sixty-day labor, delivering approximately 15,000–18,000 babies (one every five minutes). Only one in 10,000 babies is viable; the rest shrivel and harden into fuel-rich **Sheol Bricks** which burn hot and long, essentially running the train on the dried corpses of their failed young.

When Carl jacks the train and almost blows it up, Fire Brandy sticks her head out of the firebox window. She complains about the deceased engineer and war mage, then guides Carl through venting the overpressurized valves. She subsequently teaches Carl the basics of operating the steam locomotive. She later chats companionably with Donut and Katia about motherhood and the loss of Earth.
