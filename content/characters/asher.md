# Asher

Asher (Ash) is Carl's six-year-old half-brother, the son of Robert the Human and Tami-Lynn. Carl only discovers his existence during the spliced-in memory sequence of his father's death. In the dungeon, Asher's likeness is used as one of the heads of the Reminiscence Hydra, shooting lightning attacks (reflecting his fear of storms).
