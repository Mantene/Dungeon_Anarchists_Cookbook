# Growler Gary

**Growler Gary** is a Level 25 Shade Gnoll NPC bartender at the Downward Dog tavern in Station 75. He resembles a hyena rather than a typical jackal-like gnoll.

## Biography & Role in the Crawl
Growler Gary is one of the quest-essential, indestructible NPCs who resurrects minutes after being killed. When Station 75 was overrun by ghouls, Gary tried to leave the tavern to defend his friends, but was blocked by an invisible NPC boundary wall. His friend, the cart driver Jumping Jen-Jen, called him a coward before she was killed right outside the door. Later, a female crawler ripped Gary's throat out and stole his alcohol, but he regenerated.

When [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] discover that the **Interdiction Rapid-Response Railway Repair Carts** require two gnoll hands placed simultaneously on sensor plates to start, they realize they need to harvest Gary's hands. Because the carts shut off if the hands are removed, they must leave a pair of severed hands on each cart's console.

Carl and Donut return to the bar repeatedly to kill Gary and harvest his hands. Although terrified at first, once Carl explains the rescue plan and promises to give him alcohol, Gary cooperates and allows himself to be killed fourteen times to provide the necessary hands.

## Graphify Deductions
- **Tragic Resurrecting NPC**: Gary's role highlights the dark, mechanical utility of NPCs in the crawl. The system makes him indestructible to ensure quest progression, but this immortality becomes a curse as crawlers exploit it to repeatedly harvest his body parts for biometric authentication. His willing sacrifice, once he understands the plan, demonstrates a tragic nobility.
