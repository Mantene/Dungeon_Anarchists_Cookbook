# Imani C.

**Occupation:** CNA, Meadow Lark eldercare facility  
**Level:** ~24+ (Book 3); higher by Book 6  
**Race:** Obsidian Butterfly (Book 3) — insectoid; butterfly wings  
**Class:** Obsidian Butterfly → specialty: **Fire Spiritualist**  
**Skulls:** 12 mercy-kills (Book 1) + **4 player killer skulls** (Book 6 — her own totem killed her entire squad)  
**Party:** Sea Quadrant survivors alliance; went to Floor 9 with Carl/Donut

---

## Background

CNA at Meadow Lark. Roughly 20, 90 pounds, hollowed-out eyes. She mercy-killed 12 of her patients in the first hours of the dungeon — the patients were being burned alive by mobs and she used a sword. She chose swift over prolonged. She carries it in silence.

Designed the chain strategy for the Ball of Swine boss fight. Independently cleared the Mind Horror neighborhood boss. Final act in Book 1: turns to the camera and raises a middle finger.

---

## Books 3–5

Chose **Obsidian Butterfly** race and **Fire Spiritualist** class on Floor 3. Has butterfly wings. Part of the Sea Quadrant group with Louis, Britney, and the Popov brothers. Survived Floor 4 (the Iron Tangle) and Floor 5 (the Bubbles). Persistent advocate for saving Chris Andrews 2 from the Maggie My possession. Good at talking people through trauma.

---

## Book 6 — The Catastrophe

Imani's squad was sent to **Detroit** for Phase 2 — the city where she grew up. She still couldn't find the final token for the vending machine in time (one opponent teleported away). Her key location was inside her own family home.

During the final token battle, a possessed enemy totem called **Amhuluk** (a mossy disease lizard) cast a poison cloud that killed her entire squad — including a man named Randall who had frozen everyone with a spell to try to defuse the situation. The cloud killed them all at once. Imani came out of it alive but with **4 player killer skulls** — the system counted her own totem's kill as hers.

She did not attempt the Phase 2 keymaster fight in Detroit after this. When Phase 3 started, she was given a choice: Belgium (where Elle was) or Cuba. She couldn't face fighting Elle. She went to Cuba.

Carl brought her with them to the exit stairwell, protecting her from Quan Ch. She descended to Floor 9 with Carl and Donut. She was still shaking at the end of the floor.

> [!warning] Major trauma
> Imani's entire squad was killed by her own totem. She carries 4 new player killer skulls — all "teammates" by the system's logic. She is in a very fragile state entering Floor 9.

## Books 7–8

**Faction Wars (Book 7):** Imani made it to Floor 9 and participated in Faction Wars on the Princess Posse side, still carrying her 4 player-killer skulls.

**Floor 11 — Parade of Horribles (Book 8):** Imani, Elle, and their allies were assigned the **"Betrayal"** float group. Rather than build an elaborate float, they held handwritten signs: *"You are watching real people die"* and *"Fuck you"* with a middle finger. A deliberate act of refusal.

**Floor 12:** Confirmed among the 19 crawlers on Floor 12.
