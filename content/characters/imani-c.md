# Imani C

Imani C is a 20-year-old human crawler who worked on the night staff at the Meadow Lark retirement home. 

## Background & Trauma
Imani is thin (about 90 pounds) and wears scrubs under a magical cloak. She bears deep emotional scars from having to kill twelve of her former patients who had been horribly burned and mutilated by monsters, gaining the **Crawler Killer x 12** tag (indicated by one large skull and two smaller ones). This traumatic mercy-killing propelled her to Level 10.

## Combat Stats
- **Level**: 10 (reaches Level 11 after the Borough Boss fight)
- **Weapon**: Wields a longsword.
- **Boss Star**: Gained a bronze star for participating in the neighborhood boss fight.
- During the fight against the Ball of Swine, she stabs the boss through the redoubt's ceiling and fiercely protects [[yolanda-martinez-13]].

In Book 3 (*The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook*), Imani transforms into an **Obsidian Butterfly Fire Spiritualist**, giving her a 12-foot butterfly wingspan. 

Imani plays a major leadership role on Floor 4 ("The Iron Tangle"):
*   In [[084-book-3-chapter-9-desperado-assassination-attempt]], she meets Carl at the Desperado Club, sharing that Chris Andrews became highly aggressive after taking an Igneous rock race and split from their party. 
*   In [[090-book-3-chapter-15-abyss-pit-and-the-conductor-hat-secret]] and [[091-book-3-chapter-16-babababoons-and-witch-decapitation]], Imani and [[elle-mcgibbons]] discover a secret tunnel behind the robot rooms, which is a high-speed conveyor system for ghoul disposal.
*   In [[093-book-3-chapter-18-station-mimic-and-betrayal-coupons]], they ride the conveyor carts down to Trainyard D, fighting off "Wrath Ghouls" (Stage 2 DT sufferers) and barricading themselves in the administrative building.
*   In [[097-book-3-chapter-22-phased-bolts-and-betrayal]], she warns Carl that ghouls are merging into a gargantuan province boss and that their manager, Mistress Tiatha, is stealing their gold. She plans a defense at Station 36 to eliminate the ghouls before they can merge.
*   She co-leads the defense of Station 36 alongside Li Jun and others, holding the platform against Krakaren clones and ghouls, successfully surviving the transition to Floor 5.
