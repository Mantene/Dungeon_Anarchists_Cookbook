# Grigori the Placid

**Grigori the Placid** is a Level 93 human Fleshmancer NPC and mercenary who assists [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]]'s group on the tenth floor (A Parade of Horribles). He is later possessed by the dungeon AI to deliver key exposition about the history of the Primals and the Resolution.

## Profile & Traits
- **Role**: Fleshmancer. He possesses the ability to perform complex flesh-sculpting procedures.
- **Personality**: Quiet, nauseated by vehicle motion, and devoted to his flesh art. Samantha describes him as a "creepy-van grandpa."

## Role in the Crawl
- **Floor 10 (A Parade of Horribles)**: Grigori is hired as a mercenary helper. During the fifth heat, he rides in the back of Carl's truck, suffering from severe motion sickness and vomiting into the deep fryer. When Carl rescues [[corky]], Grigori uses his fleshmancy to clear debuffs and perform the fusion ceremony to merge Corky and [[porky]] back into [[corcunda]].
- **Samantha's Interest**: Samantha seeks Grigori out to find her a compatible physical body, dragging him into "Sam Town" (her private space) to discuss a flesh-sculpting procedure.
- **AI Possession**: On Floor 11, the dungeon AI takes over Grigori's body, using him as a vessel to deliver a five-part exposition about the Primals, their eugenics program, the "Resolution" doomsday weapon, and how the current crawl is exploiting the sleeping Eulogist.
