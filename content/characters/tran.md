# Tran

**Race:** Unknown  
**Class:** Unknown  
**Level:** Unknown  
**Party:** Katia's squad (Iceland, Floor 8)  
**Origin:** Vietnam (from Da Nang)

Quiet, steady Vietnamese man. Close friends with the now-dead Gwendolyn Duet (they were together from early in the dungeon; her death hit him hard).

---

## Key Events

**Book 5 (Butcher's Masquerade):** Lost both legs in the final moments of Floor 6. Received a magical floating wheelchair (pacifist loot box). Was devastated more by Gwendolyn Duet's death than by his own injury.

**Book 6 (Floor 8):** Katia's squad ended up partly in **Da Nang, Vietnam** for Phase 2. Tran's key location was in a building where both his **ex-wife** and his **estranged mother** lived (as memory ghosts). The mother's ghost rejected him. This was brutal.

During Phase 3's Havana battle, Tran and Imani were trapped together in a pocket of debris in the FOSCA building, surrounded by demons. They were rescued by the squad digging through from above. Tran lost his wheelchair at some point in the chaos.

---

## Relationships

- **Gwendolyn Duet** — dead (Book 5); he was closest to her
- **Katia** — squad leader; protective of him
- **Imani** — trapped together in the Havana battle
