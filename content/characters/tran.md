# Tran

Tran is a human crawler who survived the Floor 5 (Air Quadrant) challenges. He is a Level 28 **Swashbuckler**.

## History & Actions
- **Floor 5 Infiltration**: Tran joins [[carl]], [[katia-grim]], and [[vadim-zbar]] in the deep-sea dive to disable the pump inside the *Akula* submarine in [[135-book-4-chapter-25-gelato-and-gills]].
- **Lusca Battle**: During the fight in [[136-book-4-chapter-26-mouthbrooding-mayhem]] and [[137-book-4-chapter-27-confronting-lusca]], he helps clear out dead octo-shark babies. After the boss's defeat, he suffers from the massive depth pressure and has to be carried by Carl to the exit.
- **Orthrus Quest**: In [[139-book-4-chapter-29-where-the-red-god-glows]], he assists Carl on the ground during the sandstorm.
- **Alliance**: At the end of Floor 5, Tran decides to join [[katia-grim]]'s new independent crawler group on Floor 6 rather than descending directly with Carl and Donut.
