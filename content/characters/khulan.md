# Khulan

**Khulan** was a human crawler active on Floor 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth"). She was a member of [[ren]]'s squad.

## Biography
Khulan was a combatant in Ren's squad and the wife of the stone-skin tank [[chuluuna]].

After Chuluuna was ambushed and killed by [[quan-ch]] in Chapter 57, Khulan was left devastated by grief. Due to the damaged stairwell and the inability to safely transfer a key, she chose to sacrifice herself alongside [[ren]] in Chapter 59. Ren detonated a directional self-destruct bracer, killing them both and leaving their gear and totems to Carl.

## Attributes & Affiliation
- **Squad**: Ren's squad
- **Status**: Deceased (self-sacrifice)
