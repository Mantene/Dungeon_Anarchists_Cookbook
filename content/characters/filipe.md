# Filipe

**Filipe** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A crawler and member of Team 3, alongside Osvaldo. He is encountered by Carl in the cul-de-sac, discussing access to garages of teams from their original heats. He expresses a preference for Brahma beer over Busch Light.
