# Lorelai

Lorelai is a Level 7 **Goblin Shamanka** encountered alongside [[rory]] in the Goblin Workshop. 

## Encounter
Lorelai wears a bone necklace and was charmed by [[princess-donut]]'s high Charisma. She ended up crying along with Rory about having to eat her father raw to graduate Shamanka University. Lorelai tasted the llama-produced methamphetamine offered by [[carl]], confirmed it was dungeon-made, and ordered the goblin engineers to build a human-sized chopper for Carl and Donut.
