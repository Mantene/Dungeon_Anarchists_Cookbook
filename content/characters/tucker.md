# Tucker

Tucker is a middle-aged human stand-up comedian NPC who serves as a guest panelist on the talk show *Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton*.

## Profile
Tucker is a smug, pompous comedian who immediately makes snotty remarks about the show lowering its standards by having a cat ([[princess-donut]]) on the panel. This triggers a hostile response from Donut, who spends the rest of the interview relentlessly insulting him and calling him "Butler," humiliating him on live television. Tucker defends the Kua-tin and Borant systems regarding their debt restructuring. Following the orbital attack that kills [[manasa]], Tucker is left in shock and attempts to downplay the incident to protect his political positions.
