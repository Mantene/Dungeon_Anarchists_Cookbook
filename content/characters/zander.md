# Zander

**Zander** is a Saccathian merchant who operates **Squirties**, a waystation and refueling platform located just outside the main tunnel access route entrance to the Earth system.

## Profile & Actions
- **Business Trouble**: Due to the orbital embargo around Earth, Zander's business is ruined as no ships are passing through the tunnel gate, leaving him unable to pay his lease.
- **Encounter with Eris**: In the Epilogue of Faction Wars, the chaos goddess [[eris]] docks at his station. When Zander asks if she is wearing a costume, Eris snaps her fingers, causing Zander's facial tentacles to turn rigid and spew burning liquids.
