# Team Sparkles

**Team Sparkles** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A rival team participating in the water park race, known for harassing other competitors like Florin's team. They are highly effective, having "one-shot and shattered the boss" of a difficult section, and Lucia Prime is currently driving their vehicle.
