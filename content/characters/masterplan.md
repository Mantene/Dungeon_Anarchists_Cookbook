# Masterplan

**Masterplan** is a antagonist character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

Masterplan is a guck elemental, a translucent, human-shaped slime creature and a member of Team Girth the Trouble. He drives a bright yellow 1950s Chevy Bel Air and is known for his loud, vulgar shouting matches, particularly with Dwight the unicorn. His team is eliminated early in the race when Team Sparkles uses a powerful weapon to destroy their vehicle, resulting in Masterplan's demise.
