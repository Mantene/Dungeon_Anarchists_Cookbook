# Big Tina

**Big Tina** is a young, giant dinosaur (of Kiwi's mongoliensis pack) residing on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds). She is the central figure of [[the-recital]] quest.

## Profile & Traits
- **Appearance**: She is a large dinosaur who wears a pink feather boa. 
- **Behavior**: Big Tina carries an empty, uncharged magical wand, has a severe anger problem, and hates Ursine, whom she eats. She frequently runs away from the pack and gets into trouble.
- **Lore**: Areson describes her as "just a kid." She is the one responsible for attacking the towns downriver (including Point Mongo) and eating the residents. The other dinosaurs in the pack (led by [[kiwi]]) search for her to bring her back.

## Floor 6 Actions
- **Carnage on the Shore**: In [[173-book-5-chapter-28-big-tina]] (Chapter 28), it is revealed that Big Tina attacked the southern shore of the river, slaughtering and eating all the Ursine guards (and ripping apart the were-castor [[clint-smashgrab]]). 
- **The Recital Quest**: Carl and Donut's quest [[the-recital]] is updated, requiring them to either kill Big Tina or find out why she is the way she is and resolve her issues.

## Graphify Deductions
- **Quest Resolution Divergence**: The prompt for *The Recital* offers a choice: kill Big Tina or figure out her behavioral issues. Given that she is part of Kiwi's pack and Kiwi is now aligned with Mongo, killing Big Tina would likely turn the entire Mongoliensis pack hostile again. This suggests a peaceful resolution (addressing her anger or her empty wand) is the tactically superior choice for maintaining Point Mongo's safety.
