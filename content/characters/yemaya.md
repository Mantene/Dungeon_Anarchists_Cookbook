# Yemaya

**Yemaya** is the Yoruban river mother deity (Orisha) associated with water, life, and protection.

## Biography and Role

Yemaya almost achieved ascension to the Halls of Ascendency on her own in past eras but lost her power as her followers dwindled. She is close friends with the goddess [[eileithyia]] (the Valtay-aligned goddess of childbirth and midwifery).

During the climax of the quest [[pueblo-de-los-olvidados]] in Chapter 15, [[katia-grim]] privately messages Carl and warns him to ignore the other shrines and repair Yemaya's monument. Katia explains that resurrecting Yemaya is the first step in a complex plan to save both Katia (who is wearing the cursed *Crown of the Sepsis Whore*) and Donut. 

Carl and his allies repair Yemaya's shrine using the *Sun of Reawakening*, causing her to re-ascend. Upon her resurrection, Yemaya gifts each crawler squad a special card, and her friend Eileithyia grants each crawler a single boon.
