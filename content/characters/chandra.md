## Background

Princess Chandra is a naga attorney who was widowed when Carl used the **Ring of Divine Suffering** on **Sultan Rishi the Usurper** (the naga co-warlord who surrendered to Carl in Book 7). The Ring of Divine Suffering marks targets for a delayed kill; Rishi died from it.

Under old naga law, a widow can invoke **Widow's Rights** — claiming her husband's assassin as her new husband. Chandra filed the claim.

---

## Book 7 — The Deal Lawyer

At the end of Floor 9, Carl met his assigned Floor 10 deal lawyer in the Outreach Guild. The announcement: **"Princess Chandra, Esquire — apparently Carl's new wife."**

No further explanation was given in Book 7. The book ended on that note.

---

## Book 8 — Revealed Scheme

Chandra's full plan is revealed at the start of Floor 10:

1. **Widow's Rights claim**: Legally binding under old naga law because Carl killed Rishi. She is now Carl's wife whether Carl accepts it or not.
2. **Donut conservatorship**: Filed to place Donut under a legal conservatorship, naming Carl as guardian — ostensibly giving Chandra control over Donut's assets and decisions through Carl.
3. **Asset redirection**: Filed to redirect Carl and Donut's dungeon earnings to her trust.
4. **Assassination of Quasar**: Sent **Taurin assassins** to kill Quasar (Carl's actual lawyer). They were killed by **Tempest** (Quasar's niece, a nullian, with an illegal blaster).

**The confrontation:** Carl and Donut confronted Chandra directly. Donut's intimidation was so effective — convincing Chandra that the Princess Posse would kill her — that Chandra fled.

She is still alive and at large. Her legal filings have not been withdrawn. She remains a **legitimate legal threat** to Carl and Donut's assets even though she's not physically present.

---

## Legal Status

The Widow's Rights claim is not clearly illegal under Syndicate law — it exploits an old naga legal provision the Syndicate hasn't formally abolished. Chandra is a trained lawyer who specifically chose to invoke a provision that would be difficult to contest quickly.

Whether Carl's marriage to Chandra is legally recognized by the Syndicate is a thread left unresolved as of Book 8.
