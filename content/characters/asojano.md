# Asojano

**Asojano**, also known as the **Lord of Smallpox**, is a former Yoruban deity (Orisha) turned non-corporeal Ghommid spirit. He serves as a Level 130 City Boss on Floor 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth").

## Biography and Role

Asojano was once a powerful god, but as his worshippers became extinct, his power waned, and he faded into a Ghommid spirit. He returned to the dungeon to visit his historical domain in Cuba. However, his lingering divine energy was corrupted, turning the Ghommid town of [[pueblo-de-los-olvidados]] (located in a Havana cemetery) into a rabid settlement of undead monsters. 

Asojano entered the crawlers' saferoom (earning Carl the *Interloper* achievement), where he sat and interacted with them. In Chapter 15, Carl, Donut, and Team Sister Ines chose to bypass repairing Asojano's shrine (which would have cured the town without a fight but failed to ascend a deity) and instead resurrected [[yemaya]]. This triggered a compulsory boss battle where the crawlers defeated Asojano, successfully curing the surviving Ghommids and liberating the settlement.
