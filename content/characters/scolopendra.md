# Scolopendra

**Scolopendra** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A powerful entity or deity, believed to be a sibling of Apito and possibly two parts of the same overarching entity. An attack by Scolopendra was a significant threat, requiring specific protective measures in the High Elf Castle to survive.
