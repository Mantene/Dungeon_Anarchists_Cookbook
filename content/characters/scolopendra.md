## Background

Scolopendra is the 18th-floor final boss — the capstone threat that every dungeon crawl builds toward. Under normal circumstances, crawlers who survived to Floor 18 would face her there. She had been dormant for the entire series until [Justice Light](justice-light.md) broke the Nothing in Book 7, releasing its contents into her lair and waking her.

---

## Book 7 — Awakening

At the end of Floor 9's Faction Wars, Justice Light's trap dropped a bijou fairy trap and a demon trap into a permanent portal to the Nothing. The Nothing broke and began draining into three planes — one of which was **Scolopendra's Lair** on Floor 18.

System message:
> **Scolopendra stirs. Scolopendra has awakened.**

She arrived at Floor 11's arena instead of waiting on Floor 18.

---

## Book 8 — The Wrong Biscuit

**Carl's plan:** Use a **Nothing Special enriched pet biscuit** to reduce Scolopendra to a Level 1 pet that could be carried in a pet carrier — effectively making her the world's most dangerous piece of leverage. As long as she was kept alive, all remaining attacks would stay paused.

**What actually happened:** During the arena confrontation, the goddess [Eris](eris.md) appeared and swapped everyone's inventories. In the resulting chaos, Donut handed Carl the **enriched party companion biscuit** (which makes sapient beings into party members) instead of the **pet biscuit** (which makes pets).

Carl fired it into Scolopendra's mouth anyway.

> **Scolopendra became a Level 1 Crawler, not a pet.**

She is now "Sexy Scolopendra, Crawler #12,953,456, Level 1" — a humanoid-faced centipede with a red bow on her feeler. Her first word to Carl: **"Boop."**

## The Scolopendra Club

The galaxy's wealthiest and most powerful beings — the "Scolopendra Club" — had gathered on Floors 16–17 (the VIP observation area above the normal dungeon floors) to watch the final act of the crawl. When Scolopendra awakened and the Nothing broke:

- Floor 16 ("Agony of Mirrors") turned out to be an empty room with a single non-magical mirror
- Floor 17 is "The Backstage Death Maze"

Every member of the Scolopendra Club was trapped with an awakened ancient monster and no protection. The galactic elite — the most powerful beings in the known galaxy — became trapped prey.

---

## Penny Connection

A sapient Yorkshire pig named **Penny (Penelope 3)** was used as bait for [Taranis](taranis.md) during the arena confrontation. Scolopendra's proximity to Penny during the fight is part of the chaotic sequence that led to the wrong biscuit being used.
