# Ontario

**Ontario** is a hunter on Floor 6 (The Hunting Grounds). 

## Profile
- **Actions**: In [[150-book-5-chapter-5-left-to-fester]] (Chapter 5), Ontario is noted for having laid a minefield of alarm traps ("You Aren't Going Anywhere" traps) in the park surrounding the Zockau stairwell. [[carl]] disarms one of these traps and collects it in his inventory during his raid.
