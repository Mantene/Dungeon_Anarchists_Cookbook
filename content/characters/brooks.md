# Brooks

**Brooks** was a crawler who appeared in [[rosetta]]'s backstory in *[[dinniman-2024-tir|This Inevitable Ruin]]*.

## Biography
Brooks was a crawler who survived his crawl and became an indentured servant, working as a Castle Guard alongside Rosetta and [[justice-light]]. 

At the end of Faction Wars in their season, the orcs of the Skull Empire mocked the surviving crawlers as they prepared to transfer to the Scolopendra Club. In a fit of rage, Brooks cast spells at the portal screen to stop them. Then-Prince Rust lassoed Brooks with a whip and pulled him into the portal glass, which cut him in half and killed him instantly. Rosetta eventually avenged his death by decapitating King Rust with his own whip.
