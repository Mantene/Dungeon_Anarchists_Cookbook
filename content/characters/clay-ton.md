# Clay-ton

**Clay-ton** is a cretin crawler who is a member of the [[the-princess-posse]] during the Faction Wars on the ninth floor in *[[dinniman-2024-tir|This Inevitable Ruin]]*.

## Role in the Crawl
Clay-ton is a close companion of [[chris-andrews-2]]; the two are described as inseparable during their time on the ninth floor. He is summoned to Warlord [[carl]]'s frontlines in the Madness woods, where he is subsequently frozen by Eris's *Bear Witness* spell and swept away.
