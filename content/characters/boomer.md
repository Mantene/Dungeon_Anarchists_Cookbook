# Boomer

**Colonel Boomer** is a former crawler who leads the **106th Bloody Leeches** battalion during the Ninth Floor Faction Wars.

## Profile & Traits
- **Role**: Battalion Commander of the 106th Bloody Leeches.
- **Affiliation**: Princess Posse. His battalion consists of front-line warriors, trap mechanics, and thieves.

## History
In Book 7, Chapter 21, Boomer coordinates the reinforcement of the southern trenches alongside [[carl]] and [[tran]] of the 102nd Bulwark. His troops arrive just in time to shore up the defensive line against biological attacks launched by the Reaver faction.
