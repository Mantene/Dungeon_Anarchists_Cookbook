# Slit

Slit was a Level 200 Feral Minor Demon who escaped from the Nothing in [[144-book-4-chapter-34-a-clash-of-demons]]. She was a massive, rolls-of-flesh hybrid creature wearing a trashy dominatrix leather outfit, smelling of cigarettes, and wielding a giant electric chain whip.

## Biography & Death
- **Rivalry with Samantha**: Slit belonged to a demon harem in the Nothing. She held a grudge against [[samantha]] after Samantha claimed to have slept with Slit's king.
- **Encounter**: When Carl brought Samantha outside, Slit was drawn to her location and attacked the bubble with her whip.
- **Defeat**: Carl used the pacifying sponsor-gifted Xistera extension (Head-tosser attachment) to throw Samantha's head 50 kilometers away. Slit flew after her, but the sky cracked open and a giant, multi-armed deity (Samantha's mother god) appeared, ripped Slit in half, and vanished.
