# Alevtina

**Alevtina** is a bugbear NPC and a member of **Team Free Love**, along with [[radoslav]] and [[jasha]]. 

## Biography and Role

In [[403-book-8-chapter-4-team-free-love]], Team Free Love's red Pontiac minivan is overturned by a giant ladybug vehicle and a Screeching Death Manatee during the first heat of the race on the tenth floor. Alevtina is killed in the crash, breaking her neck. 

Her cousins/brothers Radoslav and Jasha mourn her death, mentioning that she was excited about the race and hoped to win their freedom and start a family. Carl and Donut help put out the fire in the van and tow her corpse along with the vehicle and the surviving bugbears to the finish line.
