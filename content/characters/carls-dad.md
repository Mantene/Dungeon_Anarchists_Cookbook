# Carl's Dad

**Carl's Dad** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

The father of Carl and Azra (and Yaz), he owns a bowling alley and previously provided Carl with a powerful spell (the Bumper spell from Carl's Book of Boom). Though not physically present, Carl relays a message of love and reassurance to his daughters through Azra, confirming his safety.
