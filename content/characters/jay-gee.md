# JayGee

**JayGee** is a bounty hunter who was permanently killed on the first day of Floor 6 (The Hunting Grounds) by a trap left by [[carl]].

## Floor 6 Events
- **Death**: In [[156-book-5-chapter-11-the-recital]] (Chapter 11), Carl receives a notification that a path trap he left after the bugbear settlement detonated, killing JayGee.
- **Trophy**: Carl opens the Gold Hunter Killer Box, retrieving 35,000 gold and JayGee's fat, mottled gray hand. Carl waves the hand on screen to taunt JayGee's friends and family.
