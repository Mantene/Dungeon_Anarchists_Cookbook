# Grigori the Placid

**Grigori the Placid** is a Level 93 human Fleshmancer hired as a mercenary by Team Meadow Lark on the tenth floor of the dungeon in Book 8 (*A Parade of Horribles*).

## Biography & Dungeon Presence
Grigori is a tall, gaunt, and highly poised mage in blue flowing robes who worships Gula. He is a specialist in healing and biological manipulation, particularly in fixing flesh-binding mistakes. 

In [[454-book-8-chapter-54-zhangs-grief]], Grigori is brought to the Lollipop bar by [[imani-c]] and [[elle-mcgibbons]] to assist Carl with the Corcunda quest. After Carl transfers Grigori's mercenary ownership to his guild, Grigori agrees to perform the Meld-like ceremony to fuse [[porky]] and [[corcunda]] (Corky) back together. In [[459-book-8-chapter-59-separation-anxiety-curse]], after the Lady Dominators escape to the racetrack with Corky, Carl instructs Grigori that they will have to perform the fusion ceremony during the race, and that Grigori must learn how to drive the truck.
