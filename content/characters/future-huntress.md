# Future Huntress

**Future Huntress** is a deceased Level 50 Wire-Haired Orc hunter belonging to [[the-skull-empire]], who was active on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Profile & Traits
- **Background**: She was a young, inexperienced orc hunter who had not leveled up since arriving on Floor 6. She chose a build focusing on Charisma, hoping she could "charm her way out of bad situations."
- **Nomenclature**: Princess Donut found her name "Future Huntress" absurdly specific and criticized her parents' naming choice.

## Floor 6 Events
- **Capture**: In [[170-book-5-chapter-25-future-huntress]] (Chapter 25), she is captured by [[areson-the-wise]] to be used as a blood sacrifice for [[tsarina-signet]]'s summoning ritual. 
- **Interrogation**: She is bound and gagged. When interrogated by [[carl]], she reveals that:
  - Hunters buy licenses to hunt top 10 crawlers anywhere.
  - Her group paid to have the first crack at Carl and Donut, and if she dies, Carl will become "free game" for the Dark Hive.
  - Vrah has a court injunction blocking hunter-outside communication, but Vrah cannot hunt Carl for three days.
- **Death**: Carl marks her with the *Ring of Divine Suffering* and beats/kills her. Because Charisma was her highest stat, Carl permanently gains +2 Charisma.

## Graphify Deductions
- **License to Hunt**: Future Huntress's dialogue confirms the existence of paid "hunting licenses" that grant specific hunter factions the exclusive right of first-refusal or "first crack" at the highest-ranked crawlers, showing how the Syndicate commercializes the crawlers' lives to raise production revenues.
