# Bonnie

Bonnie is a Level 5 Gnome child, daughter of Kane. She is one of the refugees saved by Carl's team on Floor 5 and stays in the personal space, often interacting with [[juice-box]] as seen in [[138-book-4-chapter-28-extractions-and-sun-shrines]]. She is interested in helping install upgrades to the *Twister* biplane house.
