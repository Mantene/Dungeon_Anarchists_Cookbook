# Hoa (Human Shell)

**Hoa (Human Shell)** is a minor character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A 'Human Shell' construct, appearing as a wrinkled, sixty-year-old Asian woman, found tending a pot in a simulated apartment (712, representing a 'what-if' scenario for Tran's father). Her eyes are mysteriously sewn shut with black wire and marked with an 'X'. Initially unaware of the crawlers, she becomes agitated and attacks them with a broom when they reveal themselves.
