# Imogen

**Queen Imogen** is the ruler of the High Elves, a powerful pure-blooded mage, and the Sorcerer General who led the eradication and transfiguration of the Chee race. She is the central antagonist of the second half of the Floor 6 storyline.

## Profile & Traits
- **Background**: Imogen is the daughter of King [[king-finian]] and the half-sister of [[tsarina-signet]]. She usurped control of the High Elves after her father's death.
- **Role**: She is a **Country Boss** on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds), residing deep within the High Elf castle. Her reveal as a repurposed human is highly anticipated by showrunners.
- **Power**: She cast the transfiguration spell that turned the Chee into were-creatures and caterpillars. Her stats are 201 down the board except intelligence, which is 300, and she has temporarily unlimited mana, making her extremely dangerous. She is immune to poison and curses, returns fire magic, and is susceptible to blunt-force attacks.

## Narrative Connections
- **The Butcher's Masquerade**: Imogen hosts the annual High Elf gala party, which doubles as the final event of the floor where showrunners plan to "zero out" the top crawlers.
- **Vengeance Quest**: Signet's primary goal is to assassinate Imogen to avenge her mother's death and reclaim her family's heritage.
- **Defeat**: In [[219-book-5-chapter-74-the-final-cage-match]], the AI freezes the battlefield to establish a four-way cage fight. Queen Imogen is defeated by the combined efforts of the crawlers and the summoned deity [[diwata]], completing the quest *Vengeance of the Daughter*.

## Graphify Deductions
- **The Country Boss Trap**: As a designated Country Boss, Imogen operates under system protection that prevents her from being easily killed before the scheduled final event. This sets a dangerous trap for Signet, who plans to assassinate her, as well as the crawlers who are drawn into her storyline.
