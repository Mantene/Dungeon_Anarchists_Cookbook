# Chuluuna

**Chuluuna** was a human crawler active on Floor 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth"). He was a member of [[ren]]'s squad.

## Biography
Chuluuna served as the main stone-skin tank for Ren's squad. He was married to [[khulan]], a fellow squadmate.

In Chapter 57, Chuluuna and his squad were ambushed by [[quan-ch]] at the Capitol stairwell. Chuluuna was killed during the confrontation before Carl could teleport in to help.

## Attributes & Affiliation
- **Class/Spec**: Stone-skin tank
- **Squad**: Ren's squad
- **Status**: Deceased (killed by Quan Ch)
