# Tuba

**Tuba** is a human crawler who appears during the Faction Wars on the ninth floor in *[[dinniman-2024-tir|This Inevitable Ruin]]*.

## Role in the Crawl
Tuba is a level 56 human "Boring Ol' Fighter" class crawler who was originally a member of Emir and Zeynep's party from the eighth floor. On the ninth floor, she is conscripted into the Madness army.

During the Princess Posse's raid on the Madness castle, she and her companion [[selvi]] refuse to fight. As a consequence of the conscription debuff, they are afflicted with a severe, non-healing bleeding effect. She is injured by [[jamal]]'s mechanical leg when he disarms her spear. She is captured and bound by [[kandy-newton]] (Bigs) in the castle's garage, and [[carl]] orders that she be kept safe until the Madness is defeated, which will break the conscription spell.
