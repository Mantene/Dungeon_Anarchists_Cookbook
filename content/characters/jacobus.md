# Jacobus

**Jacobus** is a supporting character in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, appearing in [[dinniman-2026-apoh]].

## Biography and Role

A reverse tooth fairy who serves as an unsettling companion to Carl and Donut. In this chapter, he is present during Carl's enraged confrontation with the Tigrans, offering maniacal laughter and disturbing, untranslated suggestions of violence against their vehicle and the pig within.
