# Makana

**Makana** (full name: **Kamakanamakamaemaikalani Jr. 2**) is a Hawaiian crawler active on the tenth floor in Book 8 (*A Parade of Horribles*).

## History & Dungeon Presence
Makana is mentioned by [[prepotente]] in [[453-book-8-chapter-53-penelope-rescued]] as a "pleasant fellow" who participates in the vehicle races on the tenth floor.
