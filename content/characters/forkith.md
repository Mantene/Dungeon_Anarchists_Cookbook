# Forkith

**Forkith** is a demon co-author of the *Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook* residing on the fifteenth floor (Sheol).

## Background
- **Role**: Forkith is one of the former crawlers and demon cookbook authors trying to rebel against the Syndicate and the crawl.

## Actions
- **Chapter 503**: After [[louis-santiago-2]] and [[chris-andrews-2]] are sent to Sheol instead of the Pineapple Cabaret holding area, Forkith welcomes them. He places a blood-filtering leech on Louis's skin to allow him to survive the extreme heat of the fifteenth floor, and has Chris knocked unconscious to prevent a werewolf transformation. Forkith introduces himself as a friend of [[carl]] and [[pontiff]], explaining that they needed soldiers on the Sheol front.
