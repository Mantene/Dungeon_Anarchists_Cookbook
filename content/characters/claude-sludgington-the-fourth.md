# Claude Sludgington the Fourth

**Claude Sludgington the Fourth** is a Level 65 Borough Boss (Eryops Gigantis, a giant amphibian) residing in Fort Freedom on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Profile & Traits
- **Appearance**: Claude is a neckless, green, lizard-like amphibian, about 8 feet tall and 15 feet long, resembling a mix of a frog and a squished alligator. He has short stubby legs and a tail stub.
- **Abilities**: 
  - Fires powerful multi-branch lightning bolts from his eyes.
  - Possesses a 10X Constitution bonus when rooted in place (making him extremely tanky).
  - Can swallow targets whole with his long tongue.
- **Lore**: The "Mud Kato" is famous for squatting in the homes of others. Claude entered Fort Freedom's throne room during a failed confederate fundraiser and refused to leave, effectively usurping the space.

## Floor 6 Actions
- **Teleportation**: In [[172-book-5-chapter-27-claude-sludgington]] (Chapter 27), when Carl teleports [[samantha]] back to the shore, Claude (who was being bitten by Samantha) is dragged along, teleporting onto the muddy riverbank.
- **The Battle**: Claude immediately attacks, vaporizing [[nadine]] with lightning. Carl uses his *Protective Shell* to launch Claude into the air. Donut hits his pink underbelly with a level-10 *Fireball*. Mongo dives directly into the belly wound and eats his way out the back of the creature. Claude's body explodes in gore upon death.

## Graphify Deductions
- **Overcoming the Rooted Buff**: Claude's 10X Constitution buff when rooted in place made him nearly invulnerable in the castle throne room. By utilizing the *Protective Shell* to launch him and Donut's *Fireball* to target his unarmored underbelly, the crawlers bypassed his primary defense, showcasing the tactical value of displacement spells against stationary bosses.
