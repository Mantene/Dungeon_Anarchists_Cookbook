# Charmander

**Charmander** is a level 60 Fire Dryad crawler who is a member of the [[the-princess-posse]] and the 105th Scream Warriors during the Faction Wars on the ninth floor in *[[dinniman-2024-tir|This Inevitable Ruin]]*.

## Role in the Crawl
Charmander participates in the frontlines under Warlord [[carl]] and Commander [[jurgen]]. During the teleportation of the 105th Scream Warriors reinforce squad into the woods of the Madness territory, she complains about the grueling nature of the Mass Teleportation spell. She is subsequently frozen by Eris's *Bear Witness* spell and swept away to the Dream sector.
