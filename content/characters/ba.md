# B.A.

B.A. is a Level 2 goblin on Floor 1. Originally hostile, his dot turned white under the influence of [[carl]]'s Goblin Pass and [[princess-donut]]'s high Charisma. Donut named him "B.A." (after B.A. Baracus from *The A-Team*) and commanded him to lead them to the goblin engineers.
