# The Nothing

A celestial void used as a prison or disposal mechanism within the dungeon's cosmology. In Book 7, **Justice Light** deliberately broke it open, triggering a cascade that awakened **Scolopendra** and set the stage for the series' endgame.

---

## Overview

The Nothing is a bounded non-space — a dimensionless void that exists adjacent to the dungeon's cosmological structure. It functions as an irreversible prison: entities pulled into it cannot escape under normal circumstances, and matter deposited there is effectively removed from play.

It is distinct from **Sheol** (the demon realm, Floor 15), though they share a connection through the dungeon's planar architecture.

---

## Appearances Prior to Book 7

**Book 5 — Tsarina Signet's sacrifice:**
During the Butcher's Masquerade on Floor 6, Tsarina Signet allowed herself to be pulled into the Nothing alongside Queen Imogen. Signet's intention was to remove Imogen's divine protection by stripping her from the physical plane. The fate of Signet after entering the Nothing was left uncertain — she may have died or may have survived in some altered form.

> "Signet allowed herself to be pulled into 'the Nothing' with Imogen, removing Imogen's divine protection."
> — [Book 5: The Butcher's Masquerade](../sources/book-5-butchers-masquerade.md)

**Book 7 — Juice Box's choice:**
Per [Justice Light](../characters/justice-light.md)'s instruction, Juice Box (the changeling NPC and co-warlord of the NPC Home Team) voluntarily jumped into the Nothing near the end of Floor 9's Faction Wars. Justice Light told her she would be "reborn as the ultimate weapon to end this madness."

---

## Justice Light's Trap and the Breaking

Justice Light spent weeks on Floor 9 appearing to build an elaborate trap for the Faction Wars throne room. The real trap was a tiny mechanism at the bottom of the dungeon, assembled from two components:

1. A **bijou fairy trap**: captured thousands of thread-connections between bijou fairies and the 12th floor
2. A **demon trap**: captured thousands of evicted demons capable of opening portals to Sheol

After all crawlers had left and Emberus's burning distracted Justice Light from escape, he snapped a piece of twine. Both traps dropped two items simultaneously through a permanent portal to the Nothing — a portal made from the broken Gate of the Feral Gods winding box.

The result, announced by the system:

> - The Bijou Trap has deactivated. All captured Bijou have been freed.
> - The Demon Trap has deactivated. All captured evicted demons have been freed.
> - **The Nothing has been broken.**
> - The Nothing is draining into: The Halls of the Ascendency / Sheol / **Scolopendra's Lair**
> - **Scolopendra stirs. Scolopendra has awakened.**

— [Book 7: This Inevitable Ruin](../sources/book-7-this-inevitable-ruin.md)

Justice Light died in Emberus's fire immediately after triggering the trap. His sacrifice was deliberate — he had no intention of surviving.

## Consequences

**Scolopendra's awakening:** The Nothing draining into Scolopendra's Lair broke whatever stasis had kept the 18th-floor final boss dormant. Scolopendra arrived at Floor 11's arena as an awakened, active threat — rather than the dormant trap it would have been if approached on Floor 18 under normal circumstances.

**The Scolopendra Club massacre:** The wealthiest and most powerful beings in the galaxy had gathered in the "Scolopendra Club" (floors 16–17, effectively a VIP observation area) to watch the final floors of the crawl. With the Nothing breaking and Scolopendra active, the 16th floor ("Agony of Mirrors") was revealed to be an empty room with a single non-magical mirror, and the 17th floor is "The Backstage Death Maze." Every person in the Club — the galactic elite — was now in mortal danger.

**Juice Box's fate:** Unknown at the end of Book 7. She entered the Nothing voluntarily at Justice Light's direction. Whether the Nothing's breaking freed her or transformed her as promised remains unresolved going into the series' endgame.

**The Halls of the Ascendency (Floor 12):** The Nothing is also draining into Floor 12, the final playable floor. The cosmological implications for the Court of the Ascendency are still unfolding as of Book 8.
