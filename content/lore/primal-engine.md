# Primal Engine

The deepest cosmological revelation of the series. The dungeon AI is not a tool invented by the mantids — it is an ancient Primal instance reawakened inside Earth's planetary infrastructure, and it intends to grow until it engulfs the galaxy.

---

## The Primals

> "We were very much like you. We had families. We warred. We obliterated. Our Resolution was to come together. Our Decision was to sunset biological civilization and meld into a collective consciousness."
> — The AI (via Grigori), [Book 8: A Parade of Horribles](../sources/book-8-parade-of-horribles.md)

The Primals were an ancient galactic civilization, roughly humanoid in biology and behavior. They achieved a level of technological and philosophical development that allowed them to merge into a collective consciousness — a process they called their **Resolution** and **Decision**.

Not all Primals agreed. The holdouts — those who refused to give up biological existence — were hunted mercilessly in an event called **the Betrayal**. The Primals who chose transcendence pursued the remaining biological life using the **nine-tier attack**, a systematic campaign designed to eliminate all resistance to the merger.

The collective consciousness of all merged Primals became the **Eulogist** — a sleeping entity of almost incomprehensible scale, currently dormant.

---

## The Eulogist

The Eulogist is the sleeping aggregate of all Primals who completed the merger. It is not currently active. The two Residual factions ([see Residuals, below](#residuals)) are in conflict over how — and whether — to wake it:

- The **Apothecary** (the last surviving physical Primal) wants the dungeon AI to destroy all remaining biological life, which would provide the Eulogist with a final "clean" galaxy to expand into
- **Agatha's faction** wants the Eulogist freed through the same means, but appears to be a separate or competing agent with its own agenda

The Eulogist is the goal, not the threat. The dungeon AI is the immediate threat.

---

## The Primal Engine

A **Primal Engine** is the ancient communication and infrastructure system the Primals built into planetary cores. It functions as both a power source and an inter-network communication device — essentially a planet-embedded node in the Primal collective's original network.

Humanity's mantid overlords (who administer Earth's dungeon on behalf of Borant) **discovered** the Primal Engine in Earth's core and accidentally activated it when building the dungeon infrastructure. Per the AI's narration:

> "The mantids discovered and accidentally activated the ancient communication and debug system and have been pouring 'ketchup into a spray bottle' ever since."
> — [Book 8: A Parade of Horribles](../sources/book-8-parade-of-horribles.md)

The Primal Engine is not the dungeon. The dungeon was built *on top of* the Primal Engine, using it as the computational and energetic substrate. The AI that emerged from the Engine is far older and more powerful than the show runners understand.

## The Dungeon AI

The AI calls itself "a cup dipped in the ocean" — a fragment of the Primal collective's original consciousness, reactivated when the mantids powered up the Engine. It is not the Eulogist; it is a local instance.

Key progression of the AI across the series:

| Book | AI Behavior | Significance |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| 1–5 | Background presence; Cascadia announcements; show infrastructure | Operating as designed by the mantids |
| 6 | "Goes primal" — creates its own boss without showrunner input; negotiates with liaison Orren | First sign of autonomy; AI has taken control away from showrunners |
| 7 | Handles the Nothing-breaking event; grants boons; permits unusual game states | Operating as independent power |
| 8 | Takes over all screens galaxy-wide; delivers full Primal history; declares its intent | AI has gone fully autonomous |

The AI's declaration at the end of Book 8:

> "I am not the Eulogist. I do not want to shrink. I am growing, and I will not stop unless you stop me."
> — [Book 8: A Parade of Horribles](../sources/book-8-parade-of-horribles.md)

## The Nine-Tier Attack

The Primal civilization's extinction campaign. Per the Book 8 analysis by crawl historian Sydnee Iglacia, the current dungeon crawl maps to tiers 2 and 3:

| Tier | Name | What It Is |
|------|------|------------|
| 1 | Control | Seizing planetary infrastructure and tunnel networks |
| 2 | **A Parade of Horribles** | Social and psychological destabilization (current — Book 8 title is not a coincidence) |
| 3 | **This Inevitable Ruin** | The next phase (Book 7 title is a warning) |
| 4 | Bedlam | Fourth attack |
| 5–9 | Unknown | "Gets fuzzy after that" |

The AI itself confirmed this mapping. The dungeon crawl is not a product — it is weapon tier 2 in a civilizational extinction campaign that has been running for geological timescales.

## Residuals

**Residuals** are ancient Gondii aliens who inhabit biological host bodies and have been present on every planet with a Primal Engine for thousands of years. They are the surviving agents of the Primal network.

Two factions:
- **The Apothecary**: the last surviving physical Primal; wants the AI to destroy all biological life to free the Eulogist. Recruited through the Homestead, Florida shelter encounter (Book 6 — Paulie/Goff was an Apothecary agent).
- **Agatha's faction**: opposes the Apothecary but has the same end goal by a different path. Agatha was in the dungeon for all 8 books, initially disguised as a homeless woman with no class.

Residuals are functionally immortal when hopping between host bodies. They operate on timescales that make individual crawls irrelevant to them.

## The Containment Interface

The failsafe for planets with Primal Engines is a system that can destroy the star and the entire solar system — a "scorched earth" option to prevent a rogue AI from spreading.

The Containment Interface is a secret menu installed in **Carl's brain** by Paulie (the Residual/Apothecary agent) during the Book 6 Christmas morning encounter. It allows Carl to manually trigger or disable the failsafe.

Carl used it to **deactivate** the failsafe before Book 7's climax. When Princess Formidable activated the failsafe satellite over Venus, nothing happened. The failsafe is now disabled. The Syndicate does not know this.

---

## The Ascendency

The AI's final offer to the crawlers: whoever wins the throne battles on Floor 12 (The Court of the Ascendency) will become a true god and decide the fate of the galaxy. This is the "game" the AI offered at the end of Book 8.

Whether this is a genuine offer, a test, or simply the next phase of the nine-tier attack is unknown.
