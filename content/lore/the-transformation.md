# The Transformation

The inciting event of the entire series — Borant Corporation's strip-mining of Earth, which killed most of humanity and forced the survivors into the World Dungeon.

---

## Overview

> "At 2:23 AM PST January 3rd, Earth's buildings are crushed by Borant Corporation's mining."
> — [Book 1: Dungeon Crawler Carl](../sources/book-1-dungeon-crawler-carl.md)

The event occurred without warning. Every structure on Earth's surface was simultaneously crushed from below, killing anyone who was indoors. Because Carl had gone outside at that exact moment (retrieving Princess Donut from a tree), he survived. The same random chance saved tens of thousands of others who happened to be outdoors.

Immediately after the destruction, a Syndicate AI broadcast announced the terms: humanity had until the eighteen-floor World Dungeon collapsed to reclaim enough of their planet's matter, or the dungeon would seal and the matter would be permanently absorbed into Borant's mining output.

---

## Mechanics of the Crawl

The Transformation was not merely a natural disaster — it was the execution of a [Syndicate](syndicate-legal-framework.md)-regulated corporate contract. Borant Corporation purchased the legal rights to strip-mine Earth, and the eighteen-floor dungeon is the standard "buyback" mechanism by which the affected population can reclaim their planet.

Key facts from the announcement:
- **18 floors** total; crawlers must reach the final floor
- **~150,000 dungeon entrances** were opened at Floor 1
- The dungeon is broadcast as live entertainment to the galactic audience; ratings determine loot quality
- Survivors who reach certain thresholds may negotiate official exit **deals** with Syndicate lawyers

---

## Earth's Surface After the Transformation

Earth's surface was devastated but not lifeless. Small survivor communities formed in the ruins:

- **New Queen Anne** (Seattle): A survivor camp where **Beatrice** lived with Brad and Ferdinand/Gravy Boat. Confirmed in the Book 4 epilogue.
- By Book 4, Odette's agent Lexis removed Beatrice from Earth on behalf of **OIPAN**; Brad was shot.
- Alien **bounty hunters** on the **Walk-On List** actively targeted Earth survivors to bring them into the dungeon as NPCs or special event participants.

Some 50,000 former crawlers spent years aboard the converted garbage freighter **Homecoming Queen**, hiding from the Syndicate and eventually returning as **Princess Posse** mercenaries in the Book 7 Faction Wars.

---

## Political Context

The Transformation was enabled by the [Borant Corporation](../factions/borant-corporation.md), which was already bankrupt by the time it occurred. The [Bloom](../factions/the-bloom.md) (the ultra-nationalist kua-tin party that had taken control of the kua-tin government) rushed the mining to collect the dungeon payout before Borant's debts came due. The dungeon is thus as much a financial instrument as a conquest.

---

## Galactic Significance

Per the full Primal history revealed in Book 8, the Transformation and the dungeon crawl are part of the second tier of the AI's [nine-tier attack](primal-engine.md) — "A Parade of Horribles" — designed to psychologically and socially destabilize biological civilizations as a precursor to full extinction. The dungeon's entertainment format, the suffering it inflicts on viewers as well as crawlers, and its function as a slow cultural erosion device are all deliberate design features of a Primal-era weapon system.
