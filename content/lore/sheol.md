# Sheol

The demon realm. Both a cosmological plane and the dungeon's **Floor 15**. Home of the evicted demons, the underground resistance, and — as of Book 8 — Louis Santiago, Britney, Chris Andrews 2, and Samantha.

---

## Overview

Sheol exists in the dungeon's planar architecture as a realm parallel to the main dungeon floors. It is the domain of demons and is connected to the surface dungeon through several mechanisms:

1. **Demon Eviction** — a mechanic that forces Sheol demons into the dungeon under specific conditions
2. **The Pineapple Cabaret** — an unofficial dungeon exit that, for some crawlers, led here instead of the surface
3. **The Nothing's breaking** — in Book 7, Justice Light's trap caused the Nothing to drain partly into Sheol

Sheol is also identified as **Floor 15** of the dungeon. Whether crawlers can reach it through the normal floor progression is unclear; Floors 13–14 have never been described.

---

## Demon Eviction

When the density of dead souls in a region exceeds a threshold, demons are **involuntarily ejected** from Sheol and forced upward into the physical dungeon floors. They must inhabit recently-dead shells (zombie bodies) to maintain cohesion in the material world.

First appeared in Book 6 (Floor 8, Cuba):
- The **Chowder War** between crabs and seals + Carl's zombie farm animals flooding the ocean created a massive regional death toll
- Dozens of demons were evicted, possessing zombie crab and seal shells
- When possessed shells were **teleported** (by Carl's xistera), the teleportation split the demon from its shell — freeing full-power demons loose on the floor

The major demon involved in Book 6 was **Amayon** (a major demon lord). He required millions of souls to power his return-to-Sheol spell; Raul the crab solved this problem by fertilizing millions of crab eggs (the AI counted crab sperm as mobs, so millions "died" providing the necessary souls). Amayon then cast his return-home spell and departed.

> "When too many souls die in a region, demons from Sheol are involuntarily ejected upward. They possess zombie/recently-dead shells. If the possessed shell is teleported, the demon is freed at full power."
> — [Book 6: The Eye of the Bedlam Bride](../sources/book-6-eye-of-the-bedlam-bride.md)

## The Pineapple Cabaret

An unofficial, unsanctioned exit from the dungeon running through the dungeon's **cleaner bot infrastructure**. Li Na organized its use during Floor 10's racing heats; hundreds of crawlers escaped through it.

For most, the Pineapple Cabaret was a genuine escape route. For **Louis Santiago**, **Britney**, and **Chris Andrews 2**, it went wrong — they ended up in **Sheol (Floor 15)** instead of the surface.

From the Book 8 epilogue:
- Louis is surviving with a blood-filtering leech provided by **Forkith the demon** (a small red demon associated with the Sheol underground resistance)
- Chris Andrews 2 was partially transformed by **Scolopendra's first attack** into a werewolf, and had to be knocked unconscious
- Britney is with them

The Pineapple Cabaret exit is neither safe nor reliable.

---

## Samantha in Sheol

**Samantha** — the withering spirit/naiad entity who has been with Carl's party since Book 5 — voluntarily went to Sheol in Book 8 alongside **Taranis** (the Celtic thunder god who took Krakaren and departed). Samantha's stated goal: kill **Kimaris's mother** — "the demon mother lady and her jerk son" who tricked her into giving birth to a monster that will destroy all of creation.

Kimaris is one of Amayon's brothers; he is also the entity in Carl's **stuffed animal item** (a summonable totem with "summoning time of the remainder of the floor"). Samantha believes killing Kimaris's progenitor is key to preventing a cosmological catastrophe.

## The Nothing Connection

In Book 7, when Justice Light broke the Nothing, the system announced:

> **The Nothing is draining into: The Halls of the Ascendency / Sheol / Scolopendra's Lair**
> — [Book 7: This Inevitable Ruin](../sources/book-7-this-inevitable-ruin.md)

The structural consequences of the Nothing pouring into Sheol are not yet described in the source material. However, given that:
- Sheol already houses numerous demons (including Amayon and Kimaris)
- Louis and others are physically present in Sheol
- The Nothing draining in is a cosmological-scale event

...the implications for Sheol's stability are significant and likely relevant to Book 9.
