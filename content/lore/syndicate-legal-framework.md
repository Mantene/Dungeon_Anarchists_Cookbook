# Syndicate Legal Framework

The galactic governance system that makes the dungeon crawl legal, profitable, and brutally difficult to stop. Understanding the Syndicate's rules is as important to surviving the crawl as understanding combat.

---

## Overview

The [Syndicate](../factions/syndicate.md) is the galactic governing body. It regulates inter-species commerce, territorial rights, and — critically — the use of **World Dungeons** as a corporate exploitation mechanism. The dungeon crawl is not a punishment or a war. It is a legally structured financial transaction operating under Syndicate contract law.

---

## How World Dungeons Work

A corporation (in this case, the [Borant Corporation](../factions/borant-corporation.md)) purchases the legal right to strip-mine a populated planet. The planetary population is then offered the "buyback" mechanism: enter the dungeon, survive the floors, and reclaim enough of the planet's matter to break the contract.

Key terms of a standard World Dungeon contract:
- **18 floors** is a standard format for planets of Earth's size
- The dungeon operates as a **broadcast entertainment product** — ratings directly influence the experience
- **Loot boxes are not random**: they are influenced by viewer engagement and sponsor interests
- **Patronage spots** are auctioned to wealthy alien viewers starting Floor 4
- Crawlers who reach the stairwells in the top rankings can negotiate **official exits** (deals) with Syndicate lawyers

The Syndicate does not operate the dungeon itself. The [Primal Engine](primal-engine.md) underlying the dungeon is increasingly autonomous; by Book 6, the AI has taken control away from the showrunners.

## The Rating System

```
Views → Followers → Favorites → Patrons
```

- **Views**: pure audience count; the broadest metric
- **Followers**: committed audience who track specific crawlers
- **Favorites**: strong preference signal
- **Patrons**: paying sponsors who fund loot boxes for their chosen crawlers

The higher a crawler ranks in the rating system, the better their loot — but also the higher their bounty, making them prime targets for other crawlers and Floor 6's hunters.

---

## Deals and Official Exits

Crawlers who survive long enough can negotiate with official Syndicate lawyers to take a **deal** — an early exit from the dungeon in exchange for some combination of:
- Their dungeon earnings and loot
- Rights to the broadcast of their crawl
- Other terms negotiated case by case

[Quasar](../characters/quasar.md) is Carl's deal lawyer. He is a Null (gray alien) who negotiated the Gate of the Feral Gods return in Book 5 and represented Carl through Books 6–8.

Deals can be rejected indefinitely. Carl repeatedly declined all offers. As of Book 8, Floor 12 is stated to be **the last floor where deals will be offered**.

## The Failsafe

Every planet with a Primal Engine has a failsafe mechanism: the ability to destroy the host star and the entire solar system, preventing the AI from spreading.

The failsafe is controlled by:
1. A physical satellite control (Princess Formidable attempted to activate this in Book 7 from orbit near Venus)
2. The **Containment Interface** — secretly installed in Carl's brain by the Residual/Apothecary agent Paulie (Book 6)

Carl used the Containment Interface to **deactivate** the failsafe before Book 7's climax. The failsafe is currently non-functional. The Syndicate and the galactic powers do not know this.

See [Primal Engine](primal-engine.md) for full context.

## Safety Protections for Faction Sponsors

Faction Wars (Floor 9) sponsors were originally protected from physical harm during the competition — they could send mercenaries but could not be killed themselves.

In Book 6, Carl used one of three emergency Faction Wars action items to put all pending votes to a **public audience vote**. The result:

> **Remove Safety Protections for Faction Sponsors: PASSED 98%**
> — [Book 6: The Eye of the Bedlam Bride](../sources/book-6-eye-of-the-bedlam-bride.md)

This single change transformed Floor 9 from a proxy war into a genuine war of extinction. It enabled the deaths of Huanxin Jinx, King Rust, and multiple Faction Wars warlords.

## The Walk-On List

An illegal but widely-tolerated practice: corporations hire alien bounty hunters to kidnap Earth surface survivors and bring them into the dungeon as NPCs or special event participants. Key examples:
- **Ferdinand/Gravy Boat** (the orange tom cat): an A-tier target on the Walk-On List; Book 4 epilogue revealed alien bounty hunters were specifically pursuing him
- **Beatrice**: removed from Earth by Odette's agent Lexis in Book 4–5, partially in connection with Walk-On List dynamics

---

## OIPAN / OIAN

The **Open Intellect Pacifist Action Network** (NFC; later OIAN in Book 7) is a galaxy-wide organization that opposes the dungeon crawl. They operate covertly within the Syndicate's framework because openly opposing the crawl violates numerous contracts.

Key actions:
- Sponsored Carl's **Princess Posse** faction war team (as "The Society for the Eradication of Cocker Spaniels")
- Gave Carl the toraline yam (which became portal ink in Book 6)
- Hosted **Shadow Boxer** (the OIPAN-funded talk show), where Rosetta passed Carl a hidden message
- Organized the **Homecoming Queen** with 50,000 former crawlers as a clandestine mercenary force
- Leaders: **Dr. Hu** and **Porthus** (2nd edition cookbook author; organized the Homecoming Queen and the generation ship purchase)
