# The Eye of the Bedlam Bride Tattoo

**The Eye of the Bedlam Bride Tattoo** is a permanent, magical physical enhancement and curse applied to [[carl]]'s chest on Floor 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth").

## Origin & Acquisition
In Chapter 71, while temporarily combined with Carl via the *Golden Combo*, the hostile spider totem [[shi-maria|Shi Maria]] gains control of Carl's body. 

Using ingredients from Carl's inventory (including the toraline root) and an Enchanted Prison Tattoo Kit, she brews black ink. She stabs [[li-jun]] in the eye, devours his eye, and uses the bloody needle to tattoo the "Eye of the Bedlam Bride" directly onto Carl's chest, just above his sternum.

## Characteristics & Mechanics
- **Permanent Attachment**: Unlike regular card totems, Shi Maria is now physically bound to Carl's body. Ripping her card does not delete her quest or her presence.
- **Physical Appearance**: The tattooed eye is normally invisible against Carl's skin unless active, closing and disappearing when not in use.
- **Lore Implications**: A mysterious male voice in Carl's head warns him that the tattoo is a dangerous spell and that he must expel Shi Maria's presence as soon as possible.
