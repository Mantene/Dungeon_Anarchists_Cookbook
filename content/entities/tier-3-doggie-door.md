# Tier 3 Doggie Door Personal Space Upgrade

The **Tier 3 Doggie Door Personal Space Upgrade** is a rare personal space modification module that introduces a fast travel system on Floor 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth").

## Description & Mechanics
- **Function**: Upgrades the door in the wielder's personal saferoom to allow selectable exits via any door the crawler has previously utilized on the same floor.
- **Fast Travel**: Effectively creates a waypoint fast travel system across the 99-kilometer exploration zones of Floor 8.
- **Shared Access**: Allows shared gated access among guests, party members, and guildmates.
- **Cost**: Upgrading the entire guild to use it collectively costs 25 million gold.

## Floor 8 Acquisition & Usage
In Chapter 63, [[lucia-mar]] drops five of these modules for the Meadowlark guild before descending to Floor 9. [[imani-c|Imani]] installs one in her saferoom, allowing the entire Meadowlark crawler party to instantly fast-travel from their respective global regions (such as Detroit, Washington, and Belgium) straight into Cuba to assist Carl and Donut.
