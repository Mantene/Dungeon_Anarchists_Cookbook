# The Dark Hive

**The Dark Hive** is a prominent hunter faction operating on Floor 6 (The Hunting Grounds) of the World Dungeon. 

## Profile & Actions
- **Leadership**: Led by the veteran mantis hunter [[vrah]], who has thousands of crawler kills across multiple seasons.
- **Key Members**: Includes [[xindy]] (Vrah's younger sister), who acts as a flying blood tracker.
- **Rivalry**: The faction marks [[carl]] as a high-priority target. After Carl kills Xindy during the preemptive Zockau raid, Vrah vows that the Hive will hunt Carl down, torture him, and place his head on Xindy's grave.
