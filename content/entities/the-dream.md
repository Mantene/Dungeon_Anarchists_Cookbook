# The Dream

**The Dream** is a powerful, galaxy-spanning High-Elf hunter faction active on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Faction Details
- **Leader**: Led by [[epitome-tagg]].
- **Members**: Composed of pale-skinned, bald Moon Elves.
- **Hunting Zone**: The Dream was allocated the northeast corner of the Hunting Grounds, which includes the area surrounding [[point-mongo]].
- **The Vendetta against Louis**: The faction's primary objective on Floor 6 is hunting and killing the crawler [[louis-santiago-2]], who insulted their leader's mother, [[epitome-noflex]], by having Juice Box impersonate her for sexual acts. Consequently, members of The Dream carry photographs/drawings of Louis and specialized poison arrows (such as the *Arrow of Enthusiastic Double Gonorrhea*) designed for his execution.
