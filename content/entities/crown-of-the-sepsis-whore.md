# Crown of the Sepsis Whore

The **Crown of the Sepsis Whore** is a unique, cursed tiara in the World Dungeon associated with the Blood Sultanate faction.

## Properties & Mechanics
- **Equip Restriction**: Once placed on the head, the crown equips automatically and cannot be removed without special means; it disappears from view, melding directly into the user's form.
- **Succession Curse**: Equipping the crown enters the wearer into the royal line of succession for the **Blood Sultanate** (Floor 9). Under dungeon rules, anyone in the succession line must kill the Sultan and all other family members to descend past Floor 9 to Floor 10.
- **Stair Access Restriction**: If multiple crawlers hold the succession mark, only one of them will be allowed to use the stairs from Floor 9 to Floor 10.

## History & Progression
- **Floor 1**: [[princess-donut]] receives and equips the crown, entering the Blood Sultanate line of succession.
- **Floor 3**: Donut loses the crown at the end of the third floor.
- **Floor 6 Climax**: The crown resurfaces in the possession of [[eva-sigrid]]. As Eva is killed, she successfully places the crown onto [[katia-grim]]'s head in [[221-book-5-epilogue-welcome-to-the-great-race]]. The crown melds into Katia, meaning both Donut and Katia are now in the line of succession, creating a conflict where only one of them can advance past Floor 9.
