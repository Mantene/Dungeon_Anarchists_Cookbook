# The War Mage Rebellion

**The War Mage Rebellion** is an NPC faction on Floor 8 that rebelled against their system-assigned orc and high-elf masters.

## Faction Details
- **Warlords**: [[warlord-akuma]] and [[agatha]] (who is revealed to be a Residual aligned with the Nebulars/Eulogist).
- **Actions**: They hijacked the Gate of the Feral Gods from Stalwart, seized control of the throne room of Larracos, and activated lethal rules for Floor 8.
