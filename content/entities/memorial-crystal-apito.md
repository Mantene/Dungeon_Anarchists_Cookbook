# Memorial Crystal: Apito

The **Memorial Crystal: Apito** is a rare, god-tier soul crystal created following the death/intervention of the goddess [[apito]]. 

## Properties & Mechanics
- **Nature**: Memorial crystals are generated when gods or demigods perish or manifest. They contain highly advanced knowledge and spells.
- **Charging**: To extract the information within a memorial crystal, it must be installed on a weapon, armor piece, or worn as jewelry, and charged by killing enemies in the dungeon.
- **Acquisition**: The crystal is a key item that Carl is directed to obtain as part of a new quest unlocked at the end of Floor 6.

## Quest Involvement
- **Find Out Who Killed My Son**: In [[220-book-5-chapter-75-co-warlord-of-the-princess-posse]], Carl receives a quest update requiring him to retrieve the *Memorial Crystal: Apito* to investigate the murder of Geyrun, the son of the high priest of Emberus.
