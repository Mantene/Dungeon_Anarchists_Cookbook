# The Bull Rush, Bespoke Personal Shield

The Bull Rush is a Bespoke Personal Shield manufactured by Hereford War Machines, Inc. Looted from a Soother mercenary and unlocked for Donut, it clips to her collar and surrounds her in a defensive bubble that can also protect a mount.
