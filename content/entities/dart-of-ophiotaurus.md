# Dart of Ophiotaurus

The **Dart of Ophiotaurus** is a rare and priceless summoner artifact.

## Mechanics and Usage

- **Forced Deity Summoning**: The dart is used to force the physical summoning of a deity into the dungeon.
- **Worship Requirement**: The summoner must first worship the targeted deity.
- **Sacrificial Strike**: The summoner throws the dart into a living crawler or mob (the dart cannot be used on oneself).
- **Summoning Climax**: The strike immediately initiates the summoning process, consuming and killing the struck target as a sacrifice to materialize the deity.

## History

In the backstory of the Book 6 Prologue, [[mordecai]]'s original party spent half their time on the tenth floor hunting down the Dart of Ophiotaurus. During the level collapse crisis, [[chaco]] worshipped the storm goddess [[dodola]] and threw the dart into [[uzzi]]'s breast. This forced Dodola to manifest inside a *Circle of Containment* shield. Although Uzzi was killed by the process, the party successfully destroyed the trapped Dodola and escaped the level.
