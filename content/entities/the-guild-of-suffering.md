# The Guild of Suffering

**The Guild of Suffering** is an intergalactic cult or faction that worships the demigod **Night Wyrm**, a lesser-known deity of the dungeon pantheon.

## Mechanics & Connection
- **The Sigil**: Access to the Guild of Suffering is unlocked via **The Night Wyrm's Nasty Little Web of Suffering**—a green, worm-like spiderweb tattoo branded on [[carl]]'s left elbow.
- **Lore**: The cult is closely associated with artifacts of divine suffering, such as the [[ring-of-divine-suffering]]. Wearers of these items are automatically marked with the cult's sigil.
- **Outreach Centers & Memorial Crystals**: According to [[mordecai]], the cult maintains outreach centers and utilizes "memorial crystals" (similar to soul crystals). In previous crawl seasons, the seventh floor was once centered around the cult attempting to raise the Night Wyrm.
