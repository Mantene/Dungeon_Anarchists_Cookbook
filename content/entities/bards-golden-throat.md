# The Bard's Golden Throat

**The Bard's Golden Throat** is an Enchanted Headset Microphone obtained by Princess Donut on Floor 8.

## Description and Benefits

The headset microphone is encrusted with amethyst and gold, designed to assist bards with their magical vocal spells. Equipping it provides the following benefits:
- **The Golden Throat Enhancement**: Automatically tunes the wearer's songs if they are within one and a half steps of the correct note. This dramatically assists in the successful casting of bard-based songs and spells.
- **Amplification**: Spoken words and songs are amplified at twice the normal volume by default, with a manual volume booster allowing up to five times normal volume.
- **Charisma Boost**: Grants a +5% bonus to the wearer's Charisma stat when equipped.
