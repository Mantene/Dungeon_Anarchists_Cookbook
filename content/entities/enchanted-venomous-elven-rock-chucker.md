# Enchanted Venomous Elven Rock Chucker

The **Enchanted Venomous Elven Rock Chucker** is a lightweight, mobile siege engine/trebuchet utilized by High Elf artillery specialists on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Item Mechanics
- **Description**: A light-duty rock thrower constructed by casting the *Bamboozled* spell on a patch of land and assembling the device from the resulting poisonous bamboo stalks.
- **Size**: Approximately the size of a windsurfing board, exceptionally light.
- **Weapon System**: Rather than a traditional counterweight, the machine uses two magnetic plates that want to snap together. Pulling a lever triggers the arm to sling heavy rocks.
- **Poison Cloud**: The bamboo frame magically infuses all thrown projectiles with a *Poison Cloud* effect.
- **Cooldown**: Has a 90-second cooldown between uses.

## Floor 6 Events
- In [[169-book-5-chapter-24-obsidian-bracelet]] (Chapter 24), [[carl]] loots the rock chucker from the clearing where [[akland]] and his partner were operating it. The device is stored in Carl's personal inventory under a new **Siege Equipment** tab, along with several heavy boulders.
