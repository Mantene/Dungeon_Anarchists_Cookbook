# Scavenger's Daughter Patch

The **Scavenger's Daughter Patch** is a Divine, Unique, and Fleeting back patch item obtained by Carl on Floor 8.

## Description and Properties

The patch depicts a neat pile of scary skulls. As a **Fleeting** item, it can be affixed to an eligible garment (Carl attaches it to his leather jacket), but removing it will destroy the patch. 

As a **Divine** item, its abilities, buffs, and potential debuffs change based on proximity to active deities and demons, presenting unpredictable combinations. 

## Affixed Benefits and Upgrades
When equipped in an area with no active deities, the patch grants:
- **Stat Doubling**: Doubles all existing patch stat point benefits, adding +25 to Dexterity and +25 to Strength.
- **Deity Detection**: Notifies the user whenever a deity enters the immediate area.
- **Climb Benefit**: Increases the wearer's climbing ability.
- **Soul Reaper**: Every melee kill charges the patch with the essence of the fallen. Once fully charged, the wearer can unleash the stored essence into a single melee attack, triggering varying effects based on the type of essence. The maximum soul storage capacity increases each time the essence bar is fully charged and then drained.
- **Mysterious Bone Key**: A one-time-use utility that creates a key to open *any* lock in the dungeon. However, utilizing this benefit completely destroys the patch and the garment it is affixed to. Furthermore, the key is constructed from one of the user's own bones (e.g., finger, clavicle, femur depending on the lock's difficulty), which will not grow back.
