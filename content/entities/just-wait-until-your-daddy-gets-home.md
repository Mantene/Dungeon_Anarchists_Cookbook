# Just Wait Until Your Daddy Gets Home

**"Just Wait Until Your Daddy Gets Home"** is the system-assigned name of a massive, custom-built high-yield thermobaric wheeled bomb engineered by [[carl]] in his Advanced Bomber's Studio.

## Composition & Engineering
- **Casing**: Constructed inside a large, egg-shaped, clamshell-like metal casing (originally a broken live-ammo ball).
- **Explosive Mix**: Packaged with a mix of traditional gunpowder, hobgoblin dynamite, and several blocks of **Nitro Sludge** (an oxygen-enhancing chemical amplifier left by [[mordecai]]).
- **Enchantment**: Imbued with Carl's Level 10 *Fear* spell, causing targets in the blast radius to panic.
- **Delivery**: Mounted on a small, custom-made wooden cart designed to be wheeled into position by a minion.

## Theoretical Yield & Status
- **Blast Radius**: The simulator indicates a detonation will flatten all structures and kill targets within a 1/3-mile epicenter, with severe injuries extending past a 1/2-mile radius.
- **System Status**: `250. Fortified.`
- **Tactical Plan**: Carl intends to have a clockwork duplicate of [[bomo]] wheel the bomb out of the Zockau saferoom and detonate it, though the plan is deferred in Chapter 10 after [[vrah]] warns that the hunters have obtained explosion protection buffs.
