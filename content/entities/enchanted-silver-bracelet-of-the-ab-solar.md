# Enchanted Silver Bracelet of the Ab-solar

The Enchanted Silver Bracelet of the Ab-solar is a garlic-gemmed silver bracelet awarded to Donut. It grants protection against lycanthropy and vampirism, and increases damage against sapient undead by 25%.
