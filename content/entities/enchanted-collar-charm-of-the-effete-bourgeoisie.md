# Enchanted Collar Charm of the Effete Bourgeoisie

The **Enchanted Collar Charm of the Effete Bourgeoisie** is a leadership item obtained on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Item Mechanics
- **Description**: The middle size of the chains of leadership, this pendant signifies the bearer as a member of the elite. Encrusted gems represent owned settlements. If held upon floor collapse, the bearer permanently receives a tax stipend every ten days.
- **Plain Turquoise Gem (Point Mongo)**:
  - +8 Dexterity.
  - +4 Intelligence.
  - **Snitch (Level 10)**: Within any owned settlement, activating this skill alerts all guards to converge. At Level 10, teleports 5 guards to the bearer's location (works up to 1 km outside city limits).
  - **Taxes**: Grants 3,592 Gold every 10 days.

## Floor 6 Events
- In [[156-book-5-chapter-11-the-recital]] (Chapter 11), [[princess-donut]] reluctantly swaps her butterfly charm for this collar charm to take control of [[point-mongo]], though she complains about the "turquoise color."
