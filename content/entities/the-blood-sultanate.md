# The Blood Sultanate

**The Blood Sultanate** is a naga faction active in Floor 8 Faction Wars.

## Faction Details
- **Warlords**: [[princess-vinata]] and her cousin/successor [[rishi]].
- **Deity Connection**: Supported by the goddess [[eileithyia]].
- **Conflict**: Wiped out after Prepotente slaughtered their standing army, and Carl's team assassinated Princess Vinata and her cousins in Club Vanquisher.
