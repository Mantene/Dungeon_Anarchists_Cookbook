# Tome of Legionnaires of the Damned

The **Tome of Legionnaires of the Damned** is a high-level summoning spellbook obtained by Carl on Floor 8.

## Spell Mechanics

- **Type**: Summoning Spell
- **Target**: May only be cast on recently-unequipped weapons or armor pieces.
- **Duration**: `(spell_level * 2 + intelligence_level)` seconds.
- **Cooldown**: One minute (limited to one active instance of the spell at a time).
- **Effect**: Reanimates the targeted physical weapons or armor using an undead, wailing spirit pulled from the river of falling souls. The reanimated items serve as temporary combat minions.
- **Disintegration Penalty**: In most cases, the weapons or armor utilized for the summoning will completely disintegrate into ash once the spell's duration ends. Cursed or highly enchanted items may react in unpredictable ways.
