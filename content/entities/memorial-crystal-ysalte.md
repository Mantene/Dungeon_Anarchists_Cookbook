# Memorial Crystal: Ysalte

The **Memorial Crystal: Ysalte** is a divine artifact generated upon the permanent death of the deity [[ysalte|Goddess Ysalte]] on Floor 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth").

## Origin
In Chapter 69, Ysalte is decapitated and slain in the realm by the totem [[paz-lo]] using the *Bolt of Ophiotaurus* and his self-destruct spell. Upon her death, the system generates her Memorial Crystal.

## Mechanics
- **Looting**: The system announces that the crystal has been generated and will be available for looting on a future floor, promising great riches to whoever finds it.
