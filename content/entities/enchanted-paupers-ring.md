# Enchanted Pauper's Ring of the Steadfast Emberus

The **Enchanted Pauper's Ring of the Steadfast Emberus** is an epic ring associated with the deity [[emberus]], acquired by [[carl]] on Floor 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth").

## Acquisition
In Chapter 56, Carl borrows 990,000 gold from the Temple of Emberus to purchase the ring from the system store, earning the *Columbia House* achievement (for getting something valuable while taking on massive future debt).

## Function & Role
- **Debt mechanic**: The ring represents Carl's massive debt to the Temple of Emberus, binding him further to the god's favor and influence.
- **Divine Connection**: Enhances Carl's fire resistance and connection to Emberus, which is later upgraded to Devotee rank, granting complete immunity to natural fire and the *Self-Immolation* spell.
