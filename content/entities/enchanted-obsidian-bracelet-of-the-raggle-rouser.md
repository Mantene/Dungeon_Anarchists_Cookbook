# Enchanted Obsidian Bracelet of the Raggle Rouser

The **Enchanted Obsidian Bracelet of the Raggle Rouser** is a powerful magic-gear item obtained on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Item Mechanics
- **Upgrades**: Upgraded three times (has a 50% chance to permanently break if upgraded again).
- **Obsidian Nature**: Made of volcanic glass that only forms under extreme lava-based conditions.
- **Effects**:
  - +10 Constitution.
  - +5 Dexterity.
  - +15% Fire Resistance.
  - **Fireball**: Allows the wearer to cast a level-10 *Fireball* spell once every twenty minutes.

## Floor 6 Events
- In [[168-book-5-chapter-23-artillery-specialist]] (Chapter 23), [[carl]] loots the bracelet from the unconscious Moon Elf hunter [[akland]] and gives it to [[princess-donut]]. She fits it onto her front left leg. Donut utilizes the level-10 Fireball to blow open the underbelly of the borough boss [[claude-sludgington-the-fourth]].
