# Enchanted Nipple Ring of the Superior Fire Demon's Hand Maiden

The **Enchanted Nipple Ring of the Superior Fire Demon's Hand Maiden** is a powerful piece of jewelry obtained by Princess Donut on Floor 8.

## Mechanics and Requirements

- **Equip Restrictions**: Can only be equipped on a female quadruped. Princess Donut fits these requirements.
- **Affixation**: Must be manually pierced/affixed the first time it is equipped.
- **Lore**: Superior fire demons reside on the fifteenth level (Sheol) and are notoriously chaotic and aggressive. They require chaperoning by hellspawn familiars, who use items like this nipple ring for protection.

## Benefits
Equipping the ring grants the following:
- **Curse Removal**: Allows the wearer to remove cursed items.
- **Fire & Lava Immunity**: Impervious to natural fire and lava attacks, and resistant to fire-based spells.
- **Sheol Protection**: Wearer will not lose health from the environmental effects of Sheol (Floor 15).
- **Fire Spell Boost**: Increases the damage of fire spells by the wearer's (Intelligence)%.
- **Bitch, What? Spell**: Allows casting of the unique berserking spell once every 30 hours.
  - **Type**: Berserking Spell + Partial Invulnerability.
  - **Target**: Self only.
  - **Duration**: 20 seconds.
  - **Effect**: The caster catches fire, becomes immune to blunt damage, and enters a berserk rage, attacking everything in the room.
