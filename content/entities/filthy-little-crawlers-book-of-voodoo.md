# The Filthy Little Crawler's Book of Voodoo

The Filthy Little Crawler's Book of Voodoo is an un-tradeable, un-destroyable hardback book obtained from a Gold Prepotente Box. It acts as an enchanted communication board, allowing crawlers in close vicinity to add their names and write messages to each other.
