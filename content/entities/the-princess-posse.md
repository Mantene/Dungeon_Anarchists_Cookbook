# The Princess Posse

**The Princess Posse** is the main crawler faction led by [[princess-donut]] and [[carl]] (co-warlords).

## Faction Details
- **Members**: Includes Carl, Donut, Katia, Louis, Prepotente, and various other allied crawlers and mercenaries.
- **Faction Wars**: They successfully purchased a spot on Faction Wars at the end of Floor 6 using subscriber fan club funds, establishing themselves as a primary warlord faction.
