# Worm Fulcrum

The Worm Fulcrum is a Level 50 Sentient Necro Staff card looted from Sharp-Elbows. It occupies a card slot while active and functions as a totem. The squad leader can imbue it with a spell once per combat, changing its appearance based on the spell.
