# TSECS

**TSECS** (The Society for the Eradication of Cocker Spaniels, NFC, DBA as Princess Posse Enterprises, NFC) is a faction and sponsor in the World Dungeon. 

## Overview
- **Role**: TSECS is the official sponsor of **The Princess Posse** (Team #3) in the Faction Wars starting on Floor 9.
- **Warlord Alignment**: Upon the completion of Floor 6, [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] are unlocked as **Co-Warlords** of the Princess Posse under the TSECS sponsorship contract.
- **Faction Wars Position**: TSECS has assigned spot three on the Faction Wars playing field. Because of the contract, Carl and Donut gain administrative and voting rights on team action items, such as the ability to vote to "Remove Safety Protections For Faction Sponsors," a key leverage point Carl uses to threaten the organizers.
