# Spunky Jefferson

**Spunky Jefferson the Enchanted Nickel Sock of the Elderly Miser** is a unique sapient, incremental-damage weapon introduced on Floor 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth").

## Description & In-game Lore
- **Origin**: Tanner's crusty tube sock, containing several US dollars' worth of nickels, which was cursed by a spirit to house the life force of a wealthy, solitary miser who treated everyone poorly.
- **Weapon Class**: Sapient weapon (can speak directly into the mind of its wielder).
- **Appearance**: A dirty, blood-splattered white tube sock with red and blue stripes at the top that sag heavily. It jingled with coins when swung.

## Mechanics & Stats
- **Sapience**: Communicates telepathically with its wielder. It has an annoying, talkative personality.
- **Incremental Damage**: Damage increases by 1.5% for every mob killed that is no more than 10 levels below the wielder's level.
- **Dexterity Buff**: Increases wielder's Dexterity by 10 points plus 10%.
- **Bonked Status**: Has a 5% chance to inflict *Bonked* status on any mob struck.
- **Gold Burst**: Mobs killed with this weapon have a 20% chance to burst into gold coins equal to the value of their inventory plus (level x 10).

## Floor 8 Usage
Carl loots Spunky Jefferson from Quan Ch's gear. Since it requires a one-handed flail-like style, Carl gives it to the elderly Penis Parade dancer [[dong-quixote]] in Chapter 64, who immediately bonds with it and uses it to defeat demon crabs in the FOCSA building.
