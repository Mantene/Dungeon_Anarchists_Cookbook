# Ring of Divine Suffering

The **Enchanted Night Wyrm's Ring of Divine Suffering** is an involuntary legendary ring of player-killer and hunter-killer themes. It was forcibly gifted to [[carl]] by [[frank-q]] before Frank's death.

## Attributes & Mechanics
- **Stat Boost**: When equipped, it increases all of the wearer's attributes by 5%.
- **Marked for Death**: The wearer can designate a crawler or a "non-dungeon generated combatant" (such as a hunter) as a mark. 
  - **Stat Gains**: Killing a mark permanently increases one of the wearer's stats by +1 (or more, depending on the kill count), based on the mark's highest attribute.
  - **Floor Cooldown**: There is no cooldown on Floor 6, and marks form instantly.
- **Left to Fester (Debuff)**: Once a mark is designated, the wearer is infected with the *Left to Fester* debuff. This debuff prevents the wearer from healing (by potions, spells, or scrolls) until the marked target is dead.

## Usage on Floor 6
In [[150-book-5-chapter-5-left-to-fester]] (Chapter 5), Carl equips the ring and marks [[chin-dua]] (whose highest stat is Dexterity). He also spreads the debuff to [[xindy]] using his toe ring's *Super Spreader* perk. After killing Chin'Dua, Carl's Left to Fester debuff clears, and he gains a permanent +1 to his Dexterity.
