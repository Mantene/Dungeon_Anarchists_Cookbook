# The Skull Empire

**The Skull Empire** is a powerful, galaxy-spanning Orc hunter faction active on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Faction Details
- **Members**: Composed of wire-haired orcs, including Prince Maestro's family and employees.
- **Hunting Zone**: Allocated the southeast corner of the Hunting Grounds near the High Elf Castle.
- **Claimed Crawlers**: The faction purchased hunting licenses to hunt [[carl]], [[princess-donut]], and [[katia-grim]].
- **Losses**: The faction suffered heavy losses in their attempts to hunt Carl. In [[170-book-5-chapter-25-future-huntress]] (Chapter 25), their remaining hunters on the floor are wiped out after [[the-talent]] zaps himself near the [[all-tree]] and [[future-huntress]] is captured and executed by Carl.
