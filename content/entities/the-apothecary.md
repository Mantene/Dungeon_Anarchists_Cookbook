# The Apothecary

**The Apothecary** is a powerful Syndicate sponsor faction on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds).

## Faction Alignment & Power
- **Background**: The Apothecary is believed to be the silent corporate partner of the **Plenty** (the goat people who own the universe's tunneling system), linked to the collective mind of the *real* Krakaren.
- **Wealth**: Possesses near-infinite financial reserves.
- **Floor 6 Sponsorships**: In [[161-book-5-chapter-16-were-beavers]] (Chapter 16), The Apothecary sweeps the sponsor auctions and becomes the third and final sponsor for most of the top 10 crawlers on Floor 6, including [[carl]], [[princess-donut]], [[prepotente]], [[katia-grim]], [[lucia-mar]], [[miriam-dom]], and [[daniel-bautista-2]].
- **Political Posturing**: Carl deduces that this sweeping sponsorship is a political move by the Plenty/Krakaren faction rather than a genuine concern for crawler survival.
