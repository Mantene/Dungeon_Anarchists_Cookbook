# Valtay Perso-Shield, Platinum Edition

The Valtay Perso-Shield is a Platinum Edition pinback button shield looted from hunters and unlocked by Carl. It provides an inch-thick personal buffer protecting against physical damage and magic bolt attacks for about two minutes before requiring a recharge.
