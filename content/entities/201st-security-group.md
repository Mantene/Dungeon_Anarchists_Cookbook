# 201st Security Group

The **201st Security Group** is a municipal security force and faction operating on Floor 3 (the Over City) in [[dinniman-2021-cds]].

## Description
The 201st Security Group consists primarily of City Elves who enforce order and guard municipal locations in the Over City, such as the *Apito Education Center*. The group includes specialized divisions like the Magical Ops division, which employs Wind Mages (like [[carmine]]) and Root Druids (like [[salvatore]]).

## Involvement in the Crawl
Many members of the 201st Security Group are fanatical followers of a cult that worships skyfowl as holy angels of Apito. This religious fanaticism was manipulated by [[miss-quill]], who posed as an angel of Apito to direct the group to carry out her commands.

Key actions of the group on Floor 3:
- Lured female prostitutes (including [[burgundy]]) to the municipal building under the guise of offering them jobs that would allow them to descend to safety. These women were subsequently turned into [[krasue]].
- Captured, tortured, and killed [[gum-gum]], an Orc street cleaner who was investigating the murders of the prostitutes, to extract information about Carl and Donut.
- Fought Carl, Donut, and Katia at the Apito Education Center in [[069-book-2-chapter-20-temple-confrontation]].
