# The Gate of the Feral Gods

**The Gate of the Feral Gods** is the fourth installment in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, published in 2023. It follows [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] as they navigate the fifth floor of the World Dungeon.

## Key Focus
- **Floor 5 (The Air Quadrant)**: Features a massive, open-air bowl containing floating bubbles, castles, and complex quadrant mechanics.
- **Feral Gods and summoning**: Focuses on the summoning and conflicts of various deities, divine interventions, and the mechanics of the god gates.
- **Factions & Crawlers**: Explores the alliance of crawlers fighting against the systemic traps and corporate manipulators trying to force crawlers into mutual slaughter.
