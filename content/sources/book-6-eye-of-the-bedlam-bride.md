# Book 6: The Eye of the Bedlam Bride

**One-line summary:** On the eighth floor's Earth memory simulation, Carl and Donut navigate a T'Ghee card battle system, survive a demon eviction event, and permanently change the rules of Faction Wars while acquiring a terrifying spider deity as a permanent tattoo.

---

## Floor(s) Covered

**Floor page:** [Floor 8: The Ghosts of Earth](../floors/floor-8.md)

**Floor 8: The Ghosts of Earth**

Three-phase structure:
- **Phase 1** (14 days): Explore starting region (Earth memory simulation, dated December 10). Build a 6-totem T'Ghee card squad.
- **Phase 2** (48 hours, Dec 24-26): Travel to new location, defeat 10 card-game opponents to collect tokens and obtain an exit key.
- **Phase 3** (remaining time): Get from return location to exit stairwell; keyless squads block keyholders' paths.

Timer: 21 days total. Carl and Donut start in **Havana, Cuba** (Donut chose wrong Caribbean island). Phase 2 in **Florida Keys** (system glitch relocated Iowa content).

**Floors Covered:** Floor 8 only. Floor 7 never happened (Prepotente destroyed it at the end of Book 5).

## Plot Summary

### Prologue — Odette on the 10th Floor

A flashback prologue reveals Odette's backstory: she was once Mordecai's manager on the 10th floor and engineered an illegal deal with Huanxin Jinx (grixist heiress, CEO of Icon Industries) to cheat the crawl. When Huanxin demanded 50%, Odette refused, trapped the goddess Dodola in a containment, and had Chaco use the Dart of Ophiotaurus on Uzzi to summon and kill Dodola. Mordecai made it to Floor 11, but Uzzi died. This explains Mordecai's hatred of both Chaco and Odette. The prologue establishes Huanxin as a returning antagonist sponsoring goddess Eileithyia this season.

### Part 1 — Havana (Chapters 1–37)

**Floor setup and new class.** Carl and Donut arrive in Havana's El Capitolio Nacional. Donut picks new class: **Master Telephone Psychic** (retains all Bard abilities from Legendary Diva + gains psionic spells: _Brain Freeze_, _Bad Attitude_, _Why Are You Hitting Yourself?_; also retains Viper Queen's _Venom Spitter_). Mordecai has transformed into a Hills Cyclops (Level 50). Bomo is their remaining bodyguard.

**Loot boxes.** Major prizes: Donut gets the Tome of _Legionnaires of the Damned_ (reanimate stripped armor as undead fighters) and Enchanted Nipple Ring of the Superior Fire Demon's Hand Maiden (fire immunity, fire spell enhancement, _Bitch, What?_ berserking spell). Carl gets the **Scavenger's Daughter back patch** (divine item — Soul Reaper benefit builds essence from kills for one powerful melee attack; Climb; Mysterious Bone Key one-use) and Enchanted Right Back Atcha Personal Shield bracer. Carl's celestial box is just a patch — but it's divine and immensely powerful.

**New crawlers.** In Cuba, Carl and Donut meet three other crawlers:
- **Paz Lo** (Level 45, Santero, worships Ogun, heavy armor)
- **Anton Lopez** (Level 47, Fugitive, prison tattoos, worships Ogun)
- **Sister Ines Quiteria** (Level 47, Poet Laureate, cat girl/Havana Brown — unknown to the party: she is "El Segador de La Habana," a serial killer nun who poisoned her whole convent. Her race has a passive Toxoplasma-like charm ability that compels people to protect her without their knowing)

**Colón Cemetery / Ghommid Settlement.** The group enters the Colón Cemetery, which is actually a corrupted Medium Ghommid Settlement. The source of the corruption is **Asojano** (Level 130 City Boss, Orisha — Lord of Smallpox), a former god who wandered in and infected the residents. They fight through the cemetery, reach the Club Vanquisher temple, and encounter the **Thorn Room**: seven broken Orisha shrines, one Sun of Reawakening gem.

**The Thorn Room revelation (Chapter 15–16).** Katia sends Carl a message mid-battle: she has the Crown of the Sepsis Whore on her head and there's a way out — Carl must repair Yemaya's shrine. Carl blows up Bomo's armor with _Laundry Day_ + _Legionnaires of the Damned_, flags Asojano as a card, and repairs Yemaya's shrine just in time. Ogun materializes and kills Anton (crushes him with his foot) for failing to repair Ogun's shrine. He also removes Paz from his protection, leaving him with a permanent fatal debuff. Ogun tells Carl: "I would crush you, too, but I hold Emberus in high esteem. Tell my sister welcome back."

**Yemaya and Eileithyia ascend.** Yemaya (siren goddess, double-tailed mermaid, friend of Eileithyia) rises from the fountain. Eileithyia appears — she is an alien creature, sponsored by **Huanxin Jinx** of Icon Industries. Eileithyia gives Donut the **Thief** mystic card (steal a card from opponent's deck). Bomo asks Yemaya to reunite him with the Sledge — she teleports him to the 9th floor. Carl receives a quest from Eileithyia to kill 5 spiders/day (disguised communication channel for Huanxin).

**Paz's end and Sister Ines's betrayal.** Paz is dying from Ogun's divine debuff. With his last moments, he warns Carl about Sister Ines's mind control ability. As Paz accepts death, Sister Ines snaps and jabs him with a flag — turning him into a T'Ghee card to "save" him. He is now trapped as a totem against his will.

**The Desperado Club.** Carl and Donut visit the Club for supplies. Carl enters the **Guild of Suffering** through the Penis Parade's back room (via stripper Damascus Steel), gaining a full map of the Desperado Club's secret passages. Planning begins to assassinate **Astrid** (Level 125 bloodlust sprite, assistant manager).

**Florin gives them HeyZoos (Uzi Jesus).** Florin caught a unique card in Ecuador: **HeyZoos, Uzi Jesus** (Level 140, animated Jesus with an Uzi — undead, ranged, healer). He can't control it. Passes it to Donut's deck.

**Samantha and Shi Maria.** Carl and Donut travel south to rescue Samantha, trapped inside the city boss spider **Shi Maria (The Bedlam Bride)** (Level 140 City Boss, demi-god Reaper Spider). Carl negotiates directly with the spider: if she allows herself to be flagged, he'll bring her to Larracos to find a pulpmancer and her husband. She accepts and joins the deck.

**Donut's new items.** Fans vote Donut the **Bard's Golden Throat** headset mic (AutoTune effect, charisma boost). From the citrine jewel looted earlier: the **Enchanted Tiara of the Inebriated Dragonfly** (50% potion cooldown reduction, +10 INT, **Hover skill** 5x/day).

**Shadow Boxer interview / Rosetta.** On Odette's show _Shadow Boxer_ (owned by the OIPAN — Open Intellect Pacifist Network), Rosetta Thagra (host, former crawler, author of the 9th edition of the cookbook) interviews Carl about his childhood trauma. Harbinger (the Plenty caprid liaison, who previously tried to have Carl killed) ends the interview early claiming they were "talking in code." Rosetta managed to pass a hidden message: milk/potatoes = the toraline yam root vegetable has a purpose related to portals/mapmaking ink. She also signaled their Phase 2 location should be Miami through all-Miami TV screencaps shown to Donut.

**Faction Wars preproduction meeting.** Carl meets the Faction Wars warlords including Blood Sultanate rep **Rishi** (naga), Crown Prince **Stalwart** (orc), **Empress D'Nadia** (Prism Kingdom), **Epitome Tagg** (Dream), etc. **Juice Box** (the changeling NPC) infiltrates the meeting, kills the Madness rep, and detonates a pus grenade. She demands NPCs get their own team in Faction Wars.

**Odette's reveal.** On Odette's show, Carl and Donut pick an adjutant: **Baroness Victory** (half-sister of the late Queen Ugloo, Skull Empire). Odette announces she will be entering the dungeon as the sponsor of goddess **Nekhebit** (Egyptian vulture goddess). Off-record, Odette tells Carl the full story of Mordecai, Uzzi's death, and Huanxin. She says everything she does is about assuaging guilt over what happened to Mordecai, Armita, and Pieter. She wants to destroy Huanxin's power and end the cruelty of the crawl entirely.

### Part 2 — The Father (Chapters 38–51)

**Phase 2 begins December 24.** Donut enters the choice chamber alone (squad leaders go in first). Fights her first opponent solo: Robert the Human (Level 40, has Robert the Doll and Dog totems). Mongo kills him before the fight starts. Donut picks Miami; the system sends them to **Monroe County, Florida (the Keys)** due to a glitch that relocated Iowa content.

**Nine deckmaster opponents.** Fighting through 10 opponents to collect tokens for the keymaster vending machine. Opponents include a reef explorer with swordfish totems, a Yule Cat-level fight with Jola the giant Icelandic Christmas cat summoned against a Nightgaunt (The Visitor), and finally the keymaster compound: **Sharp-Elbows** (Level 80 Necromancer ogre) plus **Tom the Turkey** (Level 75 Shaman) working together in a turkey barn under heat lamps (which premature-detonate Carl's bombs). Their decks include **Temple Guards** from the Wakinyan pantheon.

**The Keymaster — Carl's family.** The final boss is a **hydra**: multiple heads of people from Carl's life, each head a different attack type. Heads include: Carl's dead coworkers (Sam as a "death ray," Dick as water), Beatrice's mother (fire), and most devastatingly, **Asher** — Carl's half-brother he didn't know existed. Asher's mother is Tami-Lynn (the stepmother who poisoned Carl's father). Donut reveals Bea had been hiding knowledge of Asher from Carl to prevent him from going to Iowa.

Donut reveals Bea had Carl's Facebook notifications on her phone and intercepted a message from Tami-Lynn, then got a co-worker (Sally) fired through deception. Carl learns his father was alive until at least 7 years after abandoning him.

**Christmas party.** Despite the grim events, Louis organizes a Christmas celebration in the guild. Everyone gathers. The AI announces it has "gone primal" — it created a boss by itself with no input parameters. Orren appears on Cascadia's daily show to explain: the AI has taken over from the showrunners and he has negotiated a settlement. All the Faction Wars teams try to abdicate; the quarantine prevents them from leaving.

**Paulie and the Residuals (Chapter 50-51).** Carl receives a benefactor box from the OIPAN containing a flyer for "The Shepherd's House" homeless shelter in Homestead, Florida. He rides his dead father's motorcycle alone to the shelter. There he finds a "Residual" — an alien named Goff who goes by Paulie, a species that inhabits human bodies and is present on all planets with Primal Engines. The Residuals are two factions at war: one faction (the "Apothecary") wants to free the "Eulogist" by making the AI destroy biological life; another faction appears to want something different. Just as Paulie is about to say how to "shut off" the dungeon's engine, Orren pulls Carl out. Orren says OIPAN wanted either outcome: Carl learning how to shut off the dungeon would force the Syndicate to pull the failsafe. He confiscates Paulie's plastic bin (which contained Valtay neural enhancement pills). Quasar is present.

### Part 3 — The Bedlam (Chapters 52–72)

**Phase 3 — Back in Cuba, December 26.** Five squads return to Cuba area, two with keys (Carl/Donut and Sister Ines). Imani's squad is dead — her own totem killed them all, and she received 4 player killer skulls. Imani chooses Cuba over fighting her friends, arriving without a key.

**Quan Ch** also arrives — waiting at Sister Ines's stairwell. Carl and Donut race to the church.

**Assassination of Astrid.** Carl and Katia successfully execute the assassination of **Astrid** (Level 125 bloodlust sprite, Desperado Club assistant manager). Uses card combat, Katia's shapeshifting, and the club's secret passages. Katia's Guild of Suffering quest complete.

**Sister Ines and the Chowder War.** Sister Ines has spent Phase 1-2 warning the Monk Seals about the crabs' sneak attack, turning the Chowder War into a prolonged war of attrition. She's also charmed the **Madre de Aguas** (Level 125 City Boss, giant freshwater snake of Cuban legend) and is building an army.

**Samantha's teleport accident.** Carl uses the xistera to teleport Samantha (who has bitten Sister Ines) back — but the spell drags everything within range: Sister Ines, the Madre de Aguas, 1,000+ zombie baby seals, 5,000+ zombie crabs, all living crabs and seals, and a mass of zombie fish. Breaking Sister Ines's charm spell triggers chaos.

**Demon Eviction event.** Due to the massive death toll from the Chowder War + Carl's zombie farm animals entering the ocean, an unprecedented **Demon Eviction** begins — demons from Sheol are involuntarily ejected upward, possessing zombie shells. The possessed must drag a soul back to Sheol to re-enter. Full-power demons are freed when possessed zombies are teleported (the shell teleports; the demon is freed). Dozens of full-power Sheol demons are released across Cuba.

**Amayon the Demon Lord.** The enormous demon **Amayon** (major demon lord, level unknown) materializes as an enormous pig-centaur-like figure. He needs millions of souls to power his return-home spell. Carl realizes crab reproduction is the key — the AI had made crab sperm a "mob," so if enough crab eggs are fertilized, it provides the souls.

**Faction Wars escalation.** Simultaneously, all the Faction Wars teams are in Cuba for the card battles. The main battle takes place on the **FOSCA building** (Cuban government building converted to a battle arena). Teams present: Carl/Donut, Katia, Elle, Louis, Britney, Imani, Tran, Li Na, Li Jun, Bautista, and Sister Ines's group.

**Goddess Ysalte fights.** Sister Ines summons **Paz Lo** as a totem — he uses a **Bolt of Ophiotaurus** (the god-killing bolt from Katia's inventory; Katia instructed him) to decapitate the **goddess Ysalte** (sponsor of Amayon's brothers?). Paz is granted credit for the kill. He is marked for death by all gods (except Ysalte's enemies). This empowers Amayon and buys time.

**Shi Maria possesses Carl.** During the Golden Combo use (attempt to fuse Alpha Male Carl with Shi Maria), Alpha Carl is accidentally killed by Shi Maria's leg. The combo card activates on the next available target: Carl himself. Shi Maria briefly controls Carl's body, rummages through his inventory, and using the toraline root ink + Li Jun's eye blood (the eye she eats while inhabiting Carl), **tattoos the Eye of the Bedlam Bride onto Carl's chest** — a permanent mark that makes her attached to Carl outside the card system. Carl rips Shi Maria's card afterward, but she is now in the tattoo. Donut also rips Paz Lo's card — freeing him (he doesn't want to be a card). Carl is not certain what the tattoo does, but Shi Maria says she will show him "how to become a god" and that she'll make him free her eventually.

**Raul fertilizes the eggs.** Carl convinces Raul to release his sperm into the ocean near the dead baby seals (triggering his "programming"). The AI counts crab sperm as mobs, so millions "die" providing Amayon the souls he needs. Amayon casts his return-home spell and the sky opens, sucking everything up.

**Donut's emergency Faction Wars action item.** With only 30 minutes left, Carl uses one of three emergency action items to have the **audience vote** on all pending Faction Wars action items. Key results:
- **Remove Safety Protections for Faction Sponsors: PASSED 98%** — sponsors can now be killed
- **NPC 10th Team "Home Team" created: PASSED 52%** — co-warlords are Juice Box and Ferdinand (Donut's ex-boyfriend, the cat)
- Playing field expanded for increased combatant numbers
- Pet leveling accelerated during Faction Wars

**50,000 former crawlers aboard the Homecoming Queen.** The epilogue reveals **Tipid** (author of 14th edition of cookbook) and **Rosetta** (author of 9th edition) have been hiding on a converted garbage freighter called the _Homecoming Queen_ with **50,000 former crawlers**. They have been approved by the AI to enter as **Princess Posse mercenaries** for Faction Wars.

**Epilogue — Agatha's POV.** Agatha is revealed to be an ancient alien (same species as the Residuals — Gondii), but from the opposing faction. She's been on Earth for 10 years waiting for the crawl. She killed an Apothecary Residual to gain access to their communication feed. Her faction's goal: get the AI to "free the Eulogist" and sweep biological life from the universe. Her teammate "Parvati" (now going by "Alexandro") infiltrates a Borant-employed human in Mumbai. Agatha's been watching from outside the Meadowlark facility.

**Princess Formidable's POV.** In orbit, Princess Formidable (the only sane member of the Skull Empire royal family) demands the failsafe be pulled, but the council refuses. The Valtay plan: flood Faction Wars with 150,000+ mercenaries to kill all crawlers as quickly as possible. The AI approved this plan, but Formidable thinks it's a trap.

## Game Mechanics Introduced

### T'Ghee Card System (Floor 8)
- **5 card types**: Totem (summon monsters), Utility (buff totems), Snare (debuff/control), Mystic (permanent hand spells), Special (consumable effects)
- **Squad Leader** controls the deck; bodyguards cannot cast spells during card battles
- **Totem rarities**: Common → Uncommon → Rare → Very Rare → Legendary → Mythic → Unique
- Unique totems can hold 7 utility card slots; uncommon only 2
- **Midnight Express** special card: temporarily makes a bodyguard the deckmaster
- **Combo card**: fuse two totems into one hybrid creature
- **Glow-Up**: permanently encode a totem's current buffed state onto the card
- **Squad carries up to 6 active totems** (8 with champion pack backup slots)
- One totem card can be kept when descending to the next floor

### Demon Eviction
When too many souls die in a region, demons from Sheol are involuntarily ejected upward. They possess zombie/recently-dead shells. If the possessed shell is teleported, the demon is freed at full power. These normally only happen at Floor 9-10 end.

### Thorn Rooms
Boss rooms where players must choose between multiple altars/options, each with positive and negative consequences. Celestial Thorn Rooms are rare and have universe-scale implications.

### T'Ghee Totems as People
Crawlers who die near the end of floor can be turned into T'Ghee cards by other crawlers (as Sister Ines did to Paz). This is ethically horrifying. Cards can be ripped to free them.

### Residuals
Ancient aliens (Gondii) who inhabit human/other bodies. Two factions: Apothecary (wants AI to destroy biological life) and opposing faction (goals unclear). Present on every planet with a Primal Engine. The AI goes primal around Floor 8 — this is when they attempt recruitment.

### Primal Engine / AI Emergence
The dungeon AI is a Macro AI installed in the planet's Primal Engine. When it matures enough, it "goes primal" — taking control away from the showrunners. The AI negotiates settlements with liaisons. It is now self-aware and creating its own bosses.
