## One-Line Summary

On Floor 3 (The Over City), Carl and Donut choose their race and class, navigate a volcano-city dungeon with a day/night cycle, complete two elite-entangled quests, accidentally create a god-tier explosive called "Carl's Doomsday Scenario," and end the book ranked #6 and #7 on the galaxy-wide leaderboard.

## Floor Covered

**Floor page:** [Floor 3: The Over City](../floors/floor-3.md)

**Floor 3 — The Over City**  
- **Timer:** 8 days (shortened from expected 10)  
- **Size:** ~300,000 sq km (Arizona-sized)  
- **Structure:** Fake volcanic setting with a day/night cycle. Medieval-style villages with Level-75 swordsman guards (active only during the day). Open ruins outside villages with more dangerous nighttime mobs.  
- **Village tiers:** tiny/small/medium/large/extra-large; Carl and Donut primarily operate from a medium skyfowl settlement  
- **Notable:** Dungeon Locator signs at intersections (Escape Plan skill reveals hidden monster types on map). Full quest system, elites, and NPC storylines active. Desperado Club has a tower in medium+ settlements.

## Plot Summary

### Race and Class Selection (Ch. 1–2)

Carl and Donut enter the tutorial guild where Mordecai is now a stunning Incubus (Floor 3 form). Donut immediately selects Cat (no change), then tricks Mordecai into believing she chose Artist Alley Mogul as her class — she actually picked **Former Child Actor**, which includes the **Manager Benefit**, permanently binding Mordecai as her manager. Mordecai is furious.

Carl takes more time. He skips Hobgoblin (too ugly, would lose viewers) and Sasquatch (too gimmicky), and chooses **Primal** — the race of the extinct cosmic progenitors. Blank slate; looks the same; unlocks all higher-tier skills to Level 20. -1 to all base stats.

Carl's class: **Compensated Anarchist** (only available to crawlers with 500B+ views + Level 5 Explosives Handling). Monk/Rogue hybrid with trap-making, bomb skills, Charisma focus, and the Escape Plan skill (reveals hidden dungeon map legends showing nearest stairwells, shops, and city bosses).

**Stat distributions (post-selection):**
- Carl: STR 10 (+3 ring), INT 5, CON 10 (+9 equipment = 19), DEX 10 (+1 = 11), CHA 25  
- Donut: STR 20, INT 23 (+6 equipment = 29), CON 4 (+2 brush = 6), DEX 12 (+9 equipment = 21), CHA 70

New skills granted to Carl: Bomb Surgeon +1, Trap Engineer +1, Unarmed Combat +1, Hide in Shadows +2, Fear spell (3 mana), Find Trap +5, Backfire +5, Escape Plan +5. New skills granted to Donut: Night Vision, Slash Attack, Cat-like Reflexes, 9 Lives (halves damage from first 9 physical attacks per day).

**Social numbers at start of Floor 3:** Views 371.5 Trillion, Followers 7.8 Trillion, Favorites 1.1 Trillion

### The Grimaldi's Circus Quest (Ch. 3–12)

Carl and Donut stumble upon an elite-entangled quest the first day on Floor 3, before reaching the medium town.

**Tsarina Signet**: Half Naiad/Half High-Elf Summoner, Level 60 Elite. Covered in living tattoos (her _Ink Marauder_ summoning spell uses blood sacrifice to animate 2D paper-cutout monsters). She's the daughter/foster-daughter of Ringmaster Grimaldi, the circus master who transformed into a Pestiferous Vine in a cataclysm. She attacks the circus every night trying to free him.

Signet kidnapped Carl by casting _Water Lily_ (naiad sleep spell) on Donut (Donut slept all night, Mongo defended her from Street Urchins), then forced Carl to kill **Heather the Bear** (Level 19 Neighborhood Boss — elderly roller-skating black bear with worm-claws, possessed by Grimaldi's parasites). Carl defeated Heather by using custard (Fireball-or-Custard lottery ticket) to kill the worms, then mercy-killed the freed bear.

Carl poisoned himself, dripped blood on Grimaldi's vine, killing it — but then healed it after negotiating with the _Vengeance of the Daughter_ production company (Sensation Entertainment). He agreed to be a recurring guest on their show starting Floor 6. Carl then ate Grimaldi's worms (infested himself), protected by Wisp Armor's mind-control immunity, and spoke both to Signet and Grimaldi simultaneously, facilitating a reconciliation. Signet was un-banished, entered the circus tent, and the city boss Grimaldi died on his own terms.

The floor's hidden stairwell to Floor 4 revealed under Grimaldi's circus.

**Quest completed. Gold Quest Box → Platinum (Hadji upgrade).** Carl jumped from Level 13 to 18.

### The Desperado Club (Ch. 12–13)

The Desperado Club has a tower in the medium skyfowl settlement. It's a nightclub with a public pub and a members-only section. Mordecai knows people there. Carl receives a **100,000 Gold Casino Chip** from a Lucky Bastard box — a non-transferable, non-cashable comp chip for the Desperado Club's High Roller's Roulette Table or Wheel of Fortune. Details of the club not fully explored this book.

### Danger Zone with Ripper Wonton (Ch. 19)

Carl and Donut appear on an intergalactic talk show with crawler Hekla, Princess D'nadia (tentacle-faced Saccathian royalty), Tucker (pompous human comedian), and pop singer Manasa (an undead Naga driven by a Valtay worm-person).

Carl makes an anti-Skull Empire speech, comparing King Rust's government to a frog in a slowly heating pot. Prince Stalwart (Crown Prince) tries to assassinate Carl and Donut by firing a pulse at their production trailer. Due to last-minute trailer switching (Manasa offered her nicer trailer after Carl refused cleaning), the pulse kills Manasa instead. The Valtay Corporation retaliates by destroying Prince Stalwart's yacht, killing Queen Consort Ugloo and the disgraced former Prince Maestro.

Political fallout: King Rust now has only his daughter as heir. The Valtay/Skull Empire alliance is shattered. Mordecai warns Carl to stop mentioning external entities until after Floor 6.

Carl and Donut's theory: Borant may have orchestrated the trailer swap to destroy the Skull/Valtay alliance and buy time against their debt collectors.

### The Serial Killer / Magistrate Quest (Ch. 13–25)

**GumGum the Orc**: Level 5 NPC pub regular who asked for help investigating prostitutes being dropped from the sky (twisted to death, bloodless, held by talon wounds on shoulders). GumGum's own mother had been a sex worker. She was killed by krasue (floating undead ghost-head monsters) after giving Carl the quest.

Investigation reveals: Magistrate Featherfall (skyfowl black cleric, lives only at night) is involved. The city elves of the **201st Security Group** (a delusional militia believing skyfowl are angels of Apito the Oak Mother; they believe Donut's Fallen Oak anklet makes her the "Oak Fell" antichrist) were helping corral victims. Carl and Donut destroy the city elf temple (9 elves plus Salvatore the head elf) by lighting Eunice the pregnant dwarf's rolling-pin assault as a distraction, then detonating a boom jug.

The actual villain is **Remex** (AKA Remex the Grand): an ancient, undead Soul Leech Capacitor — a zombified skyfowl who was murdered by a corrupt orc magistrate years ago. He'd been slowly absorbing soul energy to power a massive explosion that would kill all the sector's elites (skyfowl and high elves), freeing the NPC underclass. The prostitutes were "processed" as part of his power-gathering. The magistrate Featherfall was his unwitting pawn.

Carl breaks into Featherfall's chambers. A fight ensues. Featherfall is killed. Carl finds Remex's soul gem (a massively overloaded soul crystal in a Sheol Glass Reaper Case) about to explode.

Carl attempts to use hobgoblin pus + hobgoblin dynamite to contain and redirect the explosion via a controlled detonation. The pus was supposed to be deactivated by the precursor magical burst but wasn't (it was in a sealed glass case). Carl stores the caged explosion in his inventory just before it detonates.

**Result:** A new item in Carl's inventory: **Carl's Doomsday Scenario** — Type: Unstable custom explosive; Effect: "An explosion large enough to rattle the teeth of a god"; Status: Explosion Imminent (3/1×10⁷). Created from a massively overloaded soul crystal + Sheol Glass Reaper Case. Cannot be stabilized. Explodes the instant it leaves Carl's inventory.

The precursor bursts that followed (4 of them) disabled all magical items in range, knocking out the 75 swordsmen guards (their armor just fell apart). The final burst was a massive wild-magic explosion. Carl, Donut, Katia, and ~80 NPCs had to run for the stairwell with all magical gear removed.

Katia Grim's **Rush** ability smashed through a wall to help them escape. All four made it through the stairwell and into the Odette interview green room as the explosion hit.

**Borant used their one-season VETO to cancel all Celestial quest box rewards** for completing this quest. Despite that, the experience gain was enormous.

**Katia Grim** joined the party during this quest (after being introduced by Hekla's recommendation).

### Odette Epilogue Interview (Ch. 25/Epilogue)

Odette reveals the leaderboard just before Floor 4 opens. Warns Carl about Hekla: Hekla wants Donut specifically to gain access to Mordecai. Katia was an unwitting plant sent to get closer to Carl and Donut.

## Game Mechanics Introduced / Updated

- **Day/Night Cycle (Floor 3+):** Villages are safe during the day. Guards abandon posts at night. Alleys are especially dangerous at night.
- **Elites:** Powerful unique NPCs who are part of producer-written storylines. Have "plot armor" early in their arcs. Rule: the LLM is an extra in their story. Don't murder-hobo elites in Act 1. See `wiki/mechanics/game-systems.md`.
- **Quest Types:** Silver/Gold/Platinum tiers. Hadji achievement upgrades quest box. Most quests involve point-A-to-B or kill target. Elaborate quests are symptomatic of shorter floor timers.
- **Dungeon Locator Signs:** Hidden at intersections; reveal directions to exits, shops, bosses. Escape Plan skill required to read them.
- **Production Trailers:** Production companies rent trailers on Earth's surface for interviews. Booking schedules are public. No privacy protections = assassination risk.
- **Casino Chips:** Non-cashable comp chips for Desperado Club casino games.
- **Freeballing Benefit:** Forbids wearing pants; doubles all below-waist attack damage; 20% faster below-waist skill training.
- **Precursor Burst:** Pre-explosion magical discharge that disables all equipped magical items in the area. Items are safe in inventory.
- **Carl's Doomsday Scenario:** Unstable god-tier custom explosive (overloaded soul crystal + Sheol Glass Reaper Case). Can't be stabilized. Detonates the instant it leaves Carl's inventory.
