# Book 4: The Gate of the Feral Gods

**Author:** Matt Dinniman  
**Full Title:** *The Gate of the Feral Gods*  
**Raw Directory:** `raw/Book 4 - The Gate of the Feral Gods - Matt Dinniman/`  
**Format:** Map + 34 chapters + Epilogue

---

## One-Line Summary

On Floor 5 (The Bubbles), Carl, Donut, and Katia are trapped in a 4-quadrant bubble with a flying gnome fortress, camel NPCs infested by shapeshifting changelings, Chris Andrews 2's body occupied by Maggie My, and the gate mechanism that releases ancient feral gods — ending with Carl reaching #1 on the leaderboard and a shocking epilogue revealing Beatrice is alive on Earth's surface and being targeted by Odette's agents.

---

## Floor Covered

**Floor page:** [Floor 5: The Bubbles](../floors/floor-5.md)

**Floor 5 — The Bubbles**  
- **Timer:** 15 days  
- **Structure:** 1,172 bubbles. Each bubble = 4 quadrants (Land, Sea, Air, Subterranean). All 4 castles must be taken before the stairwell opens. Crawlers cannot leave their quadrant until their castle falls.  
- **Carl's bubble:** #543, Air Quadrant. Setting: Desert dome atop the Necropolis of Anser (a 9-km-tall ancient tomb).  
- **Unique mechanic:** The "Gate of the Feral Gods" — sealed portals within each bubble that release ancient feral entities when opened. Dangerous but effective as a floor-clearing strategy.

## Plot Summary

### Arrival and Class Selection (Ch. 1–2)

Floor 5 opens with a sandstorm. Carl's party materializes in a desert inside a dome. Their quadrant is Air.

**Donut's Floor 5 Character Actor specialty:** Glass Cannon  
- Got: lowered spell costs, faster spell training  
- Did NOT get: +15 CON  
- New spell from floor book: **_Astral Paw_** — manipulates objects at distance; claws at Level 5; at Level 15 her combat abilities translate directly to the paw

**Carl's leaderboard achievement:** All top-10 crawlers who survive a floor with a bounty have their bounties **doubled** going forward. Carl's bounty became 800K → eventually 2M+ gold.

**Death of Ifechi:** Lucia Mar used her _Rubber_ spell (reflects incoming damage to a chosen target) — cast it on Ifechi, so when Florin shot Lucia, he accidentally killed his own partner. Florin now has a player-killer skull.

### The Bubbles Setting (Ch. 1–7)

Carl's Air quadrant contains:
- **Hump Town:** Dromedarian (single-hump camel) town with a brothel economy. The changelings pose as prostitutes.
- **Bactrian town:** Two-hump camel town, bombed by gnomes early in the floor.
- **The Wasteland:** The gnome flying fortress — 3 football fields wide, held aloft by giant balloons, floating thousands of feet up.
- **The Necropolis of Anser:** The giant tomb below their feet. A maze with traps, undead mole rats, and a ghost called Quetzalcoatlus (emperor's wife sealed inside when he died).

**Key discovery:** Hump Town's City Hall contained **Wynne** (a Dirigible Gnome Flesh Mechanic, Level 50), held hostage by the dromedarians as a guarantee against gnome bombing. Wynne is the beloved uncle of Commandant Kane, the gnome leader.

But changelings (disguised as dromedarians) also had their own agenda: they wanted to give flesh to the ghost Quetzalcoatlus using Wynne's spells.

**Town Hall disaster:** Carl infiltrated City Hall. The changeling guards were actually Changeling Principals (rare, powerful sect). A crawler from the subterranean team (Low Thi, a "D-Bag Geek") killed Wynne before Carl could free him. Town hall burned down. The gnomes eventually found out and bombed Hump Town (though the saferooms protected crawlers).

### New Vehicles and Weapons (Ch. 4–10)

Carl built the **Royal Chariot** — tracked ATV for desert navigation. Katia engineered the drive train (using her Gear Head earth hobby knowledge). Named by Donut before it was finished; the system adopted the name.

Carl built guided missiles using camel rocket launchers + Mordecai's modified chemistry + Surefire components to try to reach the Wasteland (9 km up).

### New Party Dynamics (Ch. 5–9)

**Louis Santiago 2** (Level 22 Pest Exterminator): Has a legendary-tier _Cloud of Exhaust_ spell (knocks out mobs for variable time; enhanced damage when they wake). Has been escaping fights instead of using it properly. Mordecai gave him and Firas a "Rapid Detox" potion disguised as an alcohol cure.

**Firas M** (Level 22 Hammersmith): _Puddle Jumper_ Level 10. Useful for extraction.

**Langley + 6 Finnish archers:** Level 24–32 Archer group. All chose the exact same class. Useful for covering fire and siege work.

**Juice Box** (Level 17 Changeling): Expert shapeshifter at the Toe. Able to switch forms in seconds (far beyond normal changeling abilities). Neutral. Later helps reveal the changeling infiltration of Hump Town.

**Skarn:** Level 3 Changeling orphan. Watched the gnome Wasteland with a stolen FarSeerer telescope. Critical intelligence source.

**Gwendolyn Duet (Gwen):** Level 27→32, Boring Ol' Fighter, in the Land Quadrant of the same bubble. Built siege weapons against the Sandcastle of the Mad Dune Mage.

### Maggie My / Chris Andrews 2 Revelation (Ch. 14)

A creature claiming to be Chris Andrews 2 appeared in Hump Town. Carl realized immediately it wasn't Chris — the wrong mannerisms, fabricated personal details. **Maggie My had taken over Chris's body using the Infiltrator race.** She had been in him since the end of Floor 3 when he separated from the party. She'd been hunting Carl ever since. She was sponsored by both Prince Maestro AND Crown Prince Stalwart of the Skull Empire. She carried a grenade that would summon a god named Algos (god of pain).

Katia used a paralyzing crossbow bolt to incapacitate her. Donut (drunkenly) used her _Hole_ spell to drop Chris/Maggie through the floor into the flooded subterranean level below. **Chris is still alive inside his own body, completely powerless.** He is now trapped in a sealed chamber below Hump Town. Mordecai is working on a potion to extract Maggie.

Chris sent a message (via Imani relaying): "Please kill me. I give you permission." Carl refused. He plans to save Chris after defeating all four castles.

### Storming the Wasteland (Ch. 12, 15+)

Carl's two-stage rockets damaged/brought down the Wasteland's balloon envelope. Carl and Donut stole a Vahana balloon (a stolen gnome airship) and used it to board the falling/falling Wasteland. Key fights on the airship: defeating Commandant Kane's elite guards, fighting their way to the throne room.

The Wasteland was ultimately dealt with through a combination of rocket attacks, onboard combat, and Carl's improvised weapons. Carl got his first air combat skills.

**Ruckus** the giant cyborg buzzard (Level 53 Borough Boss): a half-biological, half-mechanical bird that roosts near the bowl nightly. Carl used her to test his missiles. Elle killed her later using her _Graupel_ ice storm.

### Floor 5 Mechanics — The Feral Gods Gate (Ch. 20–34)

Every bubble has a "Gate of the Feral Gods" — a sealed portal that releases an ancient feral entity (a pre-Syndicate cosmic being, not the same as the sponsored gods like Grull). Opening the gate pops the bubble (instantly opens the stairwell) but releases something potentially dangerous.

Carl developed "Phase Plan": systematically open the gates on all bubbles by exploiting a chain reaction. Key insight: the feral gods themselves can summon corresponding deities when released — but Borant had to negotiate with the Syndicate to make only 33% of secondary summonings active (the math showed some would have been extinction-level events).

Carl flooded the city of Larracos (the faction market on Floor 9's lower levels, which had a gate connection to Floor 5's subterranean level) with sharks and salt water via an underwater portal — wiping out most of the faction armies' ability to resupply before the sixth floor. This was Carl's "I'll be coming for you" speech.

The gate plan was wildly successful — most bubbles were cleared faster than expected. The floor collapsed ahead of schedule. Prepotente killed a giant turkey that had eaten a feral god and tripled in size, jumping from Level 47 to Level 55.

**Agatha** appeared at Li Jun's stairwell to descend to Floor 6. She's now Level 12. Still no class. Still mysterious.

## The Epilogue Revelations

1. **Beatrice is alive.** She's in a survivor camp in Seattle called "New Queen Anne" with her Bahamas companion Brad. She found "Ferdinand" (Gravy Boat, the neighborhood tom cat) near Carl's destroyed apartment.

2. **Ferdinand/Gravy Boat** is an A-tier target on the Walk-On List — bounty hunters were specifically trying to capture him for the production.

3. **Odette is part of OIPAN.** She sent Lexis to "rescue" Beatrice from alien bounty hunters. Odette's true agenda involves bringing Beatrice (and likely Ferdinand) into the dungeon as narrative elements.

4. **Brad was shot** by Lexis. Odette told Lexis to "let him expire." Brad may be dead by Book 5.

5. **The Walk-On List** is an illegal but widely-tolerated practice: corporations hire alien bounty hunters to kidnap desired Earth survivors and bring them into the dungeon as NPCs or special events.

6. **OIPAN** (Open Intellect Pacifist Action Network, NFC) — the mysterious anti-crawl organization that gave Carl a yam. Their leaders Dr. Hu and Porthus believe Carl is "already shaking things up" even if he dies. The toraline/yam has a specific purpose Carl "already knows" (he does NOT know yet).

## Floor 6 Setup (entering at end of Book 4)

Floor 6 = **The Hunting Grounds** (the volcano city from Floor 3's lore). Features:
- The city of **Larracos** at the center — the faction market, now half-flooded by Carl's shark attack
- Factions have already been camped since Floor 3; they've been training and buying gear
- **Hunters** — wealthy aliens who paid to hunt crawlers; most are already there
- **Guild system** activates on Floor 6 (allows party coordination without forcing party membership)
- **Vengeance of the Daughter** storyline with Tsarina Signet continues here
- Mordecai spent time here with Odette studying plant/fungus monsters (still mysterious)
- The Naga Blood Sultanate royals are here — Donut's Crown obligation

Carl's final Floor 5 speech: "By the time the sixth floor collapses, every single hunter who dares to set foot on the same floor as us will be dead."
