# Book 7: This Inevitable Ruin

**One-line summary:** Floor 9's Faction Wars sees the Princess Posse win against all eight opposing teams while the 50,000-strong Homecoming Queen army of former crawlers fights alongside them; Katia escapes the Crown of the Sepsis Whore by choosing pregnancy; Justice Light's final trap breaks open the Nothing and awakens Scolopendra; and Carl learns the failsafe is already disabled — he turned it off.

---

## Floor(s) Covered

**Floor page:** [Floor 9: Faction Wars (Larracos)](../floors/floor-9.md)

**Floor 9: Faction Wars in Larracos, City of Dreams**

Four phases:
1. **The Ceasefire** (60 hours) — build army, recruit crawlers, prepare defenses
2. **The Ramp-Up** (180 hours) — no offensive magic; enter enemy territory only through designated kilometer-wide corridors; brutal hand-to-hand/artillery combat
3. **Open Hostilities** — full magic enabled; Operation Ruin kicks off
4. **The Peeling of Larracos** — triggered when 4 teams remain; NPCs win if they hold the castle for a week; attackers win if they hold the throne room for 6 hours

Timer: 30 days (or 90 hours after a winner is declared, whichever comes first).  
**Stairwells appear only after Faction Wars ends or 30 hours before floor collapse.**

Carl enters at #1 leaderboard (6,000,000 gold bounty). Remaining crawlers: 32,429.

## Plot Summary

### Prologue — The Full Paulie Message

The true nature of the Christmas morning visit to the Homestead, Florida homeless shelter is revealed in full. In the moment when Orren pulled Carl out, time had frozen. Paulie (the Residual alien inhabiting a homeless man's body) passed Carl:

1. **The Containment Interface** — a secret menu installed in Carl's brain giving him access to the AI's failsafe trigger. Paulie's "Goff" personality explained: the failsafe requires five or six separate conditions to trip the fuse. This interface allows Carl to manually trip it, destroying the star.
2. **A warning (from the "Paulie" personality):** "If there's time to do something else, don't be so quick. Be absolutely certain."

Carl secretly used the Containment Interface to **deactivate the failsafe** at some point before Book 7's climax. He never told anyone. This is revealed when Princess Formidable hits the failsafe button and nothing happens.

### Part 1 — The Ceasefire (Chapters 1–19)

**Entry and loot boxes.** Carl and Donut arrive in Larracos's drained lower levels. Carl vaporizes his soul poisoning by punching a dead shark with Daughter's Kiss. Key prizes:

- **Donut**: Cloak of the Benevolent Champion (Quan's celestial cloak — raises lowest stat to match highest over 66 hours; four random spells boosted to Level 15: Bad Attitude, Wall of Fire, Heal Critter, Puddle Jumper; one random skill boosted over 3 weeks; Find Weakened skill). Using Mordecai's **MySpace Photo** potion to fake 193 CHA, Donut's CON will eventually rise to 193. Also: **Tiara of a Thousand Lights** + Charge Pillow from Homecoming Queen (stores up to 99 hat-item magical properties permanently in the tiara). Second Charge Pillow from Apothecary. New spellbooks: Flak (anti-air), Shield, Sentry (war protection), **You're Not Done Yet** (Mordecai refused to elaborate — very ominous). New class: **Deathbed Hellcat** (sniper/assassin; invisibility, 4× spell range, instakill chance, ghillie suit, targeting boonie hat, Cone of Silence, Blend into Background).
- **Carl**: **Gloom Wraith Phase** Level 13 (celestial skill potion — non-corporeal lunge attack; biological entities without corporeal protection are "scrambled"; halves mana; Level 10 = stay non-corporeal 5 sec with 30-sec cooldown). **Enchanted Toe Ring of the Well-Balanced** (Heart Balance: auto-trigger heal potion at 2% health; Mind Balance: protection from Conscription/Enslavement and protects against Shi Maria's control attempts; Physical Balance: Walk on Air — already obtained via Shi Maria eating Li Jun's eye). **Containment Interface** secretly installed in brain by Paulie (already used to disable the failsafe).
- **Louis Santiago**: **Party Planner** — a 50-foot tilt-rotor airship from his celestial box. Has door guns, dimensional hangar, stealth mode.
- **Sir Rendlegore (Rend)** — baby tummy acher, Carl's new pet. Named after Tserendolgor. Has 5 previous deaths giving +125% stats. As he grows (must hit Level 5+ to survive death), he gains strength with each death.

**Mordecai's form: Canadian human** (snowsuit, raccoon hat, thick glasses, "aboot"). Very excited about alchemy table upgrades.

**Shi Maria ongoing threat.** The Mind Balance skill from the toe ring is repeatedly triggered — Shi Maria is attempting to cast spells and seize Carl's body hundreds of times a day. Carl's resistance (Mind Balance) is growing, but so is Shi Maria's attempts. A race between his resistance and her invasion power. She once warned him about a suicide bomber; she's dangerous but they're currently at a stalemate.

**Louis and Juice Box's wedding.** The "small ceremony" — a quick changeling wedding that is legally binding. Juice Box in blank form; whispered vows; they're married. A Confirmation ceremony is planned for a month later.

**Ferdinand and Shanty Town.** Ferdinand ("Warlord on High, Sir Ferdinand") is the NPC team co-warlord. He had been mayor of Shanty Town (a newly-grown settlement encircling Larracos). Carl traded Quan's bowler hat temporarily to Ferdinand for the mayorship, giving the Princess Posse control of Shanty Town.

**The 50,000 former crawlers arrive.** The Homecoming Queen (former garbage freighter) docks and deploys 50,000 former crawlers as Princess Posse mercenaries. Led by Tipid, Rosetta, and Justice Light (all cookbook authors — see Characters section). They're Level 60 with classes, stripped of original levels and gear. Key figures:
- **Tipid** (4th edition) — Crest Liquid Dervish
- **Rosetta Thagra** (9th edition) — Crest Barn Burner  
- **Justice Light** (8th edition) — one-winged Skyfowl, Origamist class

**Faction Wars structure.** Nine attacking teams formed a bloc ("The Bloc") against both the Princess Posse and Team Retribution:
1. Bone Clan (Skull Empire orcs)
2. Operatic Collective (blob creatures — Hortense)
3. Blood Sultanate (nagas — Vinata the Sultana)
4. Prism Kingdom (Empress D'Nadia)
5. Dream (Epitome Tagg)
6. Madness (Architect Houston, Viceroy)
7. Reavers (Warlord Fangs, robot-cyborg)
8. Lemig Sortion/Democratic Sortion — **already eliminated before Floor 9 fighting begins**

Total enemy mercenaries: ~150,000. Total Princess Posse forces: ~50,000 former crawlers + ~30,000 surviving crawlers + dungeon NPCs.

**Assassination of Empress D'Nadia (Prism Kingdom).** Carl and Donut infiltrated the Prism castle with just a small squad. Empress D'Nadia was stripped of her shields by Donut's clockwork Mongos. Carl used the Ring of Divine Suffering on her once she was healed. **Mongo was given credit for the kill** (Donut sent the Mongos in). *Prism Kingdom eliminated.*

**Volteeg's suicide bombing.** Volteeg (author of the 7th edition cookbook — a gargoyle who was originally a pet bird named after a starling) was posing as a Weeper tank commander for the Operatics. He deliberately removed the fire control chamber, filled his tank with the unstable energy balls, and drove it into the center of the Bloc force — detonating it and killing **Warlord Hortense** (the blob leader) along with 10,000 enemy soldiers. *Operatic Collective eliminated.* Credit ruled as either accident or fragging.

**Rishi the Naga's surrender and treaty.** Rishi (naga representative from the preproduction meetings) surrendered and offered Carl a signed treaty as "Sultan Rishi the Usurper" (he'd engineered a coup back home). Offered: Earth natives get citizenship in naga territories + 5 generation ships in exchange for rights to settle other planets in Earth's solar system post-crawl. Carl used the Ring of Divine Suffering on him.

### Part 2 — The Ramp-Up (Chapters 20–37)

**Trench warfare.** The front lines were south (nagas/prism territory) and north (orcs/operatics border). Ramp-Up rules: no offensive magic, only enter via designated kilometer-wide corridors. The enemy had superior firepower (pulse rifles, power armor, trebuchets) but lacked class-based dungeon abilities.

**Notable combat events:**
- Reaver-deployed **Rot Sticker Behemoths** as biological seeking missiles (pets weaponized with rabies, steroids, and the "Gurgles" debuff). Used infected crab sperm equivalents.
- **Gondii worm mercenaries** (Valtay worm-type) infiltrated and took over dead soldiers' bodies, turning them into spies. Compromised the Beefy Boys (mage wall defense team). Rosetta hunted them down.
- The **Reaver paratroopers** dropped directly into their base, forcing a defense of the FUPA itself.
- Carl accidentally saved the FUPA by triggering multiple enemy automatons loaded with explosives using his Tripper spell — collapsed the entire Reaver beachhead.

**Blood Sultanate killed in Club Vanquisher.** The sultana Vinata was killed in a fight in Club Vanquisher's temple (details: multiple gods appeared including Meatus and Harpocrates; the fight involved the NPCs attempting to touch the gods; Vinata died there). *Blood Sultanate eliminated.* Rishi survived by hiding in the dirt.

**King Rust's parlay and death.** King Rust (the actual Skull Empire king, father of Stalwart and Princess Formidable) arrived in person for a parlay. He revealed:
- Princess Formidable had gone rogue and was heading to a failsafe satellite in orbit around Venus, planning to activate the failsafe and destroy the solar system.
- He wanted Carl to send a message to stop her.
- The reason: Formidable believed the dungeon AI's "insanity" would spread to the center system, causing a mass extinction. The analysts said this was impossible.

Carl secretly knew the failsafe was already disabled. He agreed to "stall him" and send the message.

**Rosetta decapitated King Rust** mid-parlay with a whip. She'd been hiding in a tunnel beneath them. She needed his retina scan and bank PIN to complete a massive financial transaction (buying naga generation ships for humanity using stolen funds). She'd been waiting for this moment for years — Rust had casually killed her friend Brooks with the same whip decades ago during a previous Faction Wars.

The armies of the Bloc charged in indignant rage.

**Skull Empire effectively eliminated** (Stalwart also eliminated at some point; their throne room changed hands).

### Part 3 — Open Hostilities (Chapters 38–55 + interlude chapters)

**Operation Ruin** — the coordinated offensive across all fronts:
- **Donut** from the Nest Tower (tallest tower in Shanty Town, master quality, 150%+ strength) sniping long-range with Wall of Fire Level 15+ and new tiara upgrades
- **Carl + Louis** aboard Party Planner — bombing run over orc territory, then Carl went solo against the Madness
- **Florin** handled the orcs
- **Colonel Boomer** handled the Reavers  
- **Team Retribution** supported everywhere
- **Rosetta** as Theater Commander

**Dream Castle captured by NPCs.** While Tagg deployed his army, his castle was left open. Team Retribution took the Dream castle.

**Epitome Tagg inside Louis.** During the operation, Tagg's process of transitioning to a "post-physical form" (becoming a robot) was exposed. He'd somehow gotten his transitional electronic heart and lungs into Louis's body as leverage. When Tagg's Dream castle fell, he threatened to stop Louis's heart if they didn't help him. The solution:

**Samantha's sacrifice.** Samantha voluntarily gave up her only chance at a body — the Princess Lunette flesh golem — to save Louis. Juice Box (as a fleshmancer) and Mordecai performed the transplant. Louis now has naiad organs (heart, lungs) from Princess Lunette, plus a kua-tin rebreather built in. **He has gills.** When Tagg died (the Dream fell, his organs extracted and eaten by Mongo), the Dream was eliminated. *Dream eliminated.* Credit given to Mongo.

**Juice Box served Louis divorce papers** just before she jumped into the Nothing.

**Milk's gift.** Milk (6th edition cookbook author, still indentured as a guild master) organized the surviving crawlers who planned to take deals — teaching them to transfer skills/spells onto pages of their Book of Voodoo. They each transferred three skills/spells, losing them permanently:
- One page for Carl's second Book of Voodoo ("Carl's Book of Boom")
- One page for Donut's "Banquet Fit for a Princess"  
- One on their own entry page (first-come-first-served, consumable skills for anyone)

**Kiwi's babies.** Kiwi (now a pregnant Ursine bear) gave birth to Mongo's babies during the floor. The species crossbreed result is unknown (everyone wondered). They went to the 12th floor via Zerzura.

**Justice Light's plan.** Justice Light (8th edition cookbook author, one-winged skyfowl trap master) had been building a trap for weeks — but not the elaborate throne room trap everyone thought was the point. The real trap was tiny, at the bottom of the dungeon. He also negotiated with Juice Box, learning her secret: the NPCs wanted to touch as many gods as possible. He proposed an alliance and told her what he was really building.

He was also aware that Sick-Ass Worgo (a sluggalo) was actually a powerful changeling spy for Juice Box. He caught her but proposed a deal instead of fighting.

### Part 4 — The Peeling of Larracos (Chapters 56–87)

**Remaining teams:** Princess Posse, Team Retribution (castle occupied by War Mage Rebellion), Dream (castle held by NPCs), Reavers.

**The War Mage Rebellion.** A surprise 5th combatant team appears — the **War Mage Rebellion**, led by **Agatha** (the Residual alien who was secretly the enemy of the Apothecary faction all along). They took the Larracos castle. They generate **Zarogoths** (shadow monsters) using a soul crystal generator and deploy gondii worm mercenaries. Donut used her Flak spell from the Nest Tower to hold them off while looking for the generator.

**Li Jun's sacrifice.** Li Jun (Zhang's brother, Li Na's twin) made some form of sacrifice during the battle that bought Donut time. He may have been killed; his fate is unclear but his actions are referenced as buying Donut "enough time."

**Samantha and Emberus.** Samantha admitted out loud to having knowledge of Geyrun's death (Emberus's son). Emberus entered the realm. With only 65 minutes until he would destroy the floor, the clock was now ticking on everything else.

**Katia eats the orchid.** With 65 minutes left and Zerzura needing to be cast, Katia was forced to eat the Orchid of Eileithyia's Grace immediately. She was unconscious for one hour while waiting for the goddess.

**Huanxin Jinx killed.** While Eileithyia was frozen by the "choice pending" game mechanic, Donut used Laundry Day Level 15 to rip Huanxin Jinx out of the goddess's soul armor. With Huanxin stripped and exposed, Donut killed her using the crossbow bolt fired from the mount on her back. Mordecai loaded Katia's crossbow with a bolt tipped with his mother's ashes — "a funeral rite to avenge her son." *Huanxin Jinx is dead.*

**The War Mage Rebellion captured the castle.** They held the throne room for nearly 6 hours but were defeated before the clock ran out, partly due to Donut's sniping and the city defenses.

**Zerzura.** The Sledge cast Zerzura, teleporting the entire city of Larracos + Shanty Town to the **12th floor** (the Court of the Ascendency). This sent:
- All dungeon-born NPCs (Semeru dwarves, Larracos citizens, changelings including Ruby and the children, Tina, Kiwi and new babies, Ferdinand, Juice Box, etc.)
- The former crawler mercenaries went back to Homecoming Queen (embargo still in place)
- Sparks flew as the city departed

**Team Retribution forfeited.** Ferdinand and Juice Box both "left the realm" (jumped into the Nothing per Justice Light's instruction to Juice Box). Team Retribution forfeited. **Princess Posse wins Faction Wars.**

**Katia's choice.** When Eileithyia appeared (no longer frozen), she evaluated Katia's three options:
1. Constitution enhancement
2. Celestial attendant (12th floor)
3. **Pregnancy** — Katia chose this

Why: Katia wanted to protect the children who went to the 12th floor. The pregnancy clause would eject her from the dungeon to the surface, but she'd be near the children. When asked to name a father, she asked Louis. Bautista had made her cybernetic replacement limbs in advance (arm, legs, ear). Katia reverted to full human form, missing the limbs, then Bautista quickly attached the cybernetics. *"It will be a boy."* Katia disappeared.

**Justice Light's final trap triggered.** After everyone left, Justice Light (hiding from Emberus) snapped a piece of twine. A tiny trap fell two items into the permanent portal to the Nothing (made from the broken Gate of the Feral Gods winding box):
1. A **bijou fairy trap** — the thousands of thread connections between fairies and the 12th floor
2. A **demon trap** — thousands of evicted demons capable of opening a portal to Sheol

**Result:** Four planes folded together. System messages:
- The Bijou Trap has deactivated. All captured Bijou have been freed.
- The Demon Trap has deactivated. All captured evicted demons have been freed.
- **The Nothing has been broken.**
- The Nothing is draining into: The Halls of the Ascendency / Sheol / **Scolopendra's Lair**
- **Scolopendra stirs. Scolopendra has awakened.**

The 16th floor ("Agony of Mirrors") turns out to be an empty room with a single non-magical mirror. The 17th floor is "The Backstage Death Maze." Everyone in the Scolopendra Club (the richest, most powerful people in the galaxy who came to watch Faction Wars) are now in mortal danger.

Justice Light is dead (burned by Emberus).

**Samantha was thrown to Emberus** via the xistera. She stalled him but he nearly ate her. Carl retrieved her after Katia left. Samantha is "charred to a crisp."

**Carl and Donut enter the 10th floor.** ~20,221 crawlers made it. Most will take deals. Carl declined all deals. Donut declined all deals.

**Floor 10 preview:** "Don't Come in Last" — a racing floor. Choose biological or mechanical conveyance; travel from A to B repeatedly; come in last = lose survival containment. Carl and Donut can take the Chaco replacement deal at the end of the 10th floor if they survive.

**Epilogue — York on the Homecoming Queen.** York (10th edition cookbook author, Tenodera/mantis-type alien, emotionally numb) killed the orc yacht carrying King Rust's underage children (making Princess Formidable the new Skull Empire ruler by clearing the succession). He feels nothing about it. The Homecoming Queen is loading up soldiers again for "Phase 2" — bringing the war out of the dungeon. Boomer is preparing breaching suits, power armor, and Cronus suits. They're sending 4 Cronus suits to the surface to help Katia.

**Final scene.** In the Outreach Guild: Carl meets Quasar. 20,000+ crawlers are taking deals today. Carl's new deal lawyer for the next floor is "Princess Chandra, Esquire" — apparently **Carl's new wife** (the exact circumstances are unexplained).

**The Eris appearance.** The goddess of chaos appears at "Squirties" waystation (run by saccathian Zander) outside the tunnel gate. She snaps her fingers and makes all Zander's tentacles spray different liquids. Her presence is ominous foreshadowing.

## Game Mechanics Introduced

### T'Ghee Cards (Floor 9 continuation)
- Cards are still usable — summon once per day, double the summoning time.
- Faction Wars victory allows one card to be kept (previously one card kept from Floor 8).
- The card system is separate from the regular game mechanics on Floor 9 but supplements it.

### Faction Wars Rules
- Teams: 9 attacking + 1 defending (NPCs)
- To win: hold the Larracos throne room for 6 hours (attackers); defend for a full week (NPCs)
- Safety protections removed (sponsors can be killed)
- Ceasefire → Ramp-Up (no offensive magic) → Open Hostilities → Peeling of Larracos
- NPCs and former crawlers as teams

### The Containment Interface
- Installed in Carl's brain by Paulie. Allows him to manually trigger the failsafe (destroy the star).
- Carl already used it to **deactivate** the failsafe (not activate it). This was "the hunch" he referenced.
- Princess Formidable hit the failsafe button: nothing happened.

### Walk on Air / Claw / Monologue (natural benefits)
- When boosted stats top 300, crawlers gain natural benefits:
  - **Walk on Air** — can literally walk on air for short periods (Carl has this via Shi Maria's eye-eating)
  - **Claw** — Donut's natural claw skill, approaching Level 15 (due to the Benevolent Champion cloak)
  - **Monologue** — Donut's new charisma-based skill that terrifies enemies and generates experience during intimidating speeches

### The Nothing Breaking
- The Nothing was a celestial prison/void. Justice Light's trap broke it open.
- Contents of the Nothing are now draining into: the Halls of the Ascendency (12th floor), Sheol (15th floor), and Scolopendra's Lair.
- Scolopendra is the 18th-floor final boss. It has awakened.

### Bliss Surf / Therapy Potions
- Mordecai brewed special potions allowing guided dream sessions. Carl and Katia accidentally connected while taking them, sharing Katia's most painful memory (the baby Annie who died of brain cancer).
