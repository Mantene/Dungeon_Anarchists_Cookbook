# A Parade of Horribles

**A Parade of Horribles** is the eighth installment in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, published in 2026. It follows [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] as they enter the tenth floor of the World Dungeon: "Don't Come in Last."

## Key Focus
- **Floor 10 (Don't Come in Last)**: A racing-themed floor consisting of seven heats, where crawlers and NPCs compete in custom-designed tracks using mechanical or biological vehicles/mounts.
- **Level Timer Suspended**: The level timer is temporarily suspended, forcing crawlers to navigate individual heats where coming in last results in elimination.
- **Quarantine and Chaos**: The Nothing has been broken, leading to chaos on the twelfth and eighteenth floors, with cosmic entities invading the final boss.
- **Unlicensed Merchandise and Legal Battles**: Princess Chandra claims Widow's Rights to marry Carl and tries to take over Carl and Donut's financial empires.
