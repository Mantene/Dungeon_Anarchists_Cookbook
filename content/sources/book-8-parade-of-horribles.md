# Book 8: A Parade of Horribles

**One-line summary:** Floor 10's death-race through seven heats winnows the crawlers from 5,501 to 19; Carl feeds Scolopendra the wrong pet biscuit making the final dungeon boss a Level 1 crawler party member; Emberus is killed by his own brother Taranis; and the dungeon AI reveals itself to be a Primal instance that intends to keep growing until it engulfs the galaxy.

---

## Floor(s) Covered

**Floor pages:** [Floor 10: Don't Come in Last](../floors/floor-10.md) · [Floor 11: Parade of Horribles](../floors/floor-11.md) · [Floor 12: The Court of the Ascendency](../floors/floor-12.md)

**Floor 10: "Don't Come in Last"** — racing floor  
**Floor 11: "Parade of Horribles"** — parade + arena

**Floor 10 structure:** Seven racing heats. Teams of up to four (or solo). Assigned a vehicle (mechanical or biological) by spinning a wheel. Come in last place and your vehicle's "survival containment" is removed. Kill too many opponent teams and future heats will include fellow crawlers as opponents.

Level timer suspended for Floor 10 (unique). Blood Bar active: max 10 hours in saferoom.

**Floor 11 structure:** 90 minutes to build a parade float. March the route to a judging stand and then an arena. Defeat the floor boss (Krakaren Prime) to open the Floor 12 stairwells.

Crawlers entering Floor 10: **5,501**  
Crawlers entering Floor 11: **203**  
Crawlers entering Floor 12: **19** (+ Scolopendra as a Level 1 party member)

Carl: #3 leaderboard (3,200,000 gold bounty). Donut: **#1 leaderboard, Level 135** (massive leap from Level 63).

## Plot Summary

### Floor 10 — Don't Come in Last

**Setup.** Carl and Donut spawn in a garage-style hangar with two competing gremlin applicants — Hedy (mechanic) and Waldrip Chris (animal trainer). Waldrip Chris explodes when Donut chooses mechanical over biological. Their vehicle is determined by a spinning wheel: **Big Shot Chicken food truck** — a 1920s Prohibition-era gangster-themed food truck with a tommy gun on the roof. **Hedy** becomes their mechanic.

**Princess Chandra's schemes.** In the outreach guild, Carl's new "wife" is revealed: **Princess Chandra, Esquire** — a naga lawyer who invoked "Widow's Rights" (old naga law: a widow can claim her husband's assassin as her new husband) because Carl killed Rishi. She filed to place Donut under a conservatorship (naming Carl as guardian), redirect all their earnings to her trust, and filed multiple legal suits. In their confrontation, Donut intimidated her so badly Chandra fled, convinced the Princess Posse would kill her. Chandra also sent **Taurin assassins** to kill Quasar, who were killed by Quasar's niece **Tempest** with an illegal blaster.

**New items at floor start.** Donut chose new class: **Assassin of Sekhmet** (warrior-melee with Perpetual Tank, Cockroach to Level 15, Healer's Grasp aura). She received Egyptian-themed accessories:
- **Assassin's Veil** (beaded headdress, Precognition benefit — warns of incoming spells)
- **Nemes of Pestilence** (Mongo's hat — Eternal Protector invulnerability if Donut's HP <10%; bites inflict Cruel Sepsis)
- **Wrap of Sekhmet's Compassion** (Rend's headband — same invulnerability trigger + Heal Party + Raise Bootlicker fatal bites)
- Celestial Slap-Chop box (Crupper of the Benevolent Champion — matched set piece, see below)
- **Glowing blue divine claws** — Donut's right front paw permanently upgraded; Claw Level 17; claws cut through anything mortal. (Purple claw caps were installed to hide the glow and prevent accidents.)

Carl received: **Oozy Form** spell (can become slime, pass through cracks; Level 5, special edition, trains faster), **Hotlist Expansion** potion (+10 hotlist slots), plus the **Big Shot Chicken food truck's GPS unit Dr. Metcalf** (a sarcastic AI that narrates race info).

**Mordecai's new form:** Moon Reaper (but glitching as a Shadow Mimic — he can copy any shape but gets supernatural Moon Reaper powers when near different species for the first time). On Floor 11, he becomes an **Animistic Spirit** — he inhabits physical objects (was the kitchen table). Can communicate via chat only.

**The seven heats.** Each heat is on a different track with different rules and hazards:
- **Heat 1** (Caves of the Screeching Death Manatees): Carl drives. Food truck beats the bugbear team's minivan crash to bring them to the finish. Team "One Fine Pig" (armored vehicle) wins the heat.
- **Heats 2–3**: Truck gets spider legs upgrade. Various terrains including acid rain and jungle trails.
- **Heat 4**: Won first place. "Half a prayer, half a song" quest completed (relating to bringing Chalchiuhtlicue / "Chachi" and Lucia Mar together — the details are complex).
- **Heat 5**: Interlude reveals **Minus** — a Syndicate security soother who was forced to take his dead brother's identity to infiltrate the dungeon and assassinate Carl and/or Donut. His dead brother (Linus) was a creepy Elle McGib fan.
- **Heat 5** also: Li Na safely reached the **Pineapple Cabaret** (coded signal: one flower bloomed on the cactus). Hundreds of crawlers began escaping via the cleaner bots.
- **Heat 6**: **Dong Quixote died** heroically. Carl and Donut came in last place because **Dwight** the unicorn cheated by going over the outside of the factory rather than through it. The "Half a prayer, half a song" quest completed.
- **Heat 7** (Final): Under 1,000 crawlers remain. The apartment building "Tower of Power" plan (vertical explosives) helped. Last chance to take deals.

**Deaths on Floor 10 (notable):**
- Dong Quixote (stripper/lancer) — heroic death in Heat 6
- Hedy the gremlin mechanic — died in the Heat 6 ice-cream truck crash
- ~4,300 crawlers total died or took deals (5,501 → 203 survivors to Floor 11, of which ~172 took deals at the Floor 11 guild leaving 23 to fight)

**The Pineapple Cabaret.** A secret exit from the dungeon via the cleaner bots, hidden in the dungeon's infrastructure. Not an official deal — just a way out. Hundreds used it. Li Na led people there. Bautista is among those who went. The showrunners warned against it but couldn't stop it.

**Chalchiuhtlicue / "Chachi"** — the water goddess Carl smelled like (discovered via Samantha). She is inside Lucia Mar's head along with the children. Samantha warns Carl: "She kills children despite claiming to protect them." Chalchiuhtlicue sacrificed a village day care when the meatball appetizers had cilantro. Carl realizes this is the goddess they need to deal with regarding Lucia Mar's situation.

### Floor 11 — Parade of Horribles

**Entry.** 23 crawlers enter Floor 11. **Scolopendra is awakened** and in the arena. The five float procession groups:
1. **Resolution** — Florin's team
2. **Decision** — a team who made a giant menu
3. **Betrayal** — Imani, Elle, and allies — held signs ("You are watching real people die," "Fuck you" with a middle finger)
4. **Destruction** — Makana, Sarah Hayse with an Abrams tank
5. **Vengeance** — Carl/Donut/Prepotente/Jurgen with the Unwashed/Midnight Epicure effigy

**The AI's parade narration.** Using Grigori's body (after ripping off Chaco's wings for reading from a script), the AI finally delivered the **full story of the Primals**:

> "We were very much like you. We had families. We warred. We obliterated. Our **Resolution** was to come together. Our **Decision** was to sunset biological civilization and meld into a collective consciousness. The **Betrayal** was by those who refused — hunted mercilessly. **Destruction** — the nine-tier attack to kill remaining life. The Eulogist is the sleeping collective. The Apothecary is the last surviving physical Primal. The dungeon AI is 'a cup dipped in the ocean' — a Primal instance, not a mantid invention. The mantids discovered and accidentally activated the ancient communication/debug system and have been pouring 'ketchup into a spray bottle' ever since."

The AI ended with: **"I am not the Eulogist. I do not want to shrink. I am growing, and I will not stop unless you stop me."** It offered the Ascendency as a game: whoever wins the throne battles decides the fate of the galaxy.

**Prepotente sang "In the Air Tonight"** a capella, using his trained tapir Sweety to beat the tempo. The song was stunning. He'd been practicing his whole life.

**Carl's speech about vengeance** over the PA system, while sluggalos rained from the stolen Reaver airship onto the elites in the judging stands.

**The Arena / Plan Carl:**

Carl's actual plan had multiple layers:
1. **Real plan:** Feed Scolopendra the **Nothing Special enriched pet biscuit** by combining it with a banger sphere and firing it into her mouth. This would reduce her to a Level 1 pet, which Carl could carry in a pet carrier — effectively making her the world's most dangerous leverage item (kill her = all remaining attacks trigger).
2. **Contingency:** Use lightning to kill Scolopendra (the hidden weakness throughout all dungeon descriptions). All crawlers loaded up on lightning spells from the Book of Voodoo skill pages.
3. **Sub-plan:** Summon Taranis (Celtic thunder god, god of lightning) by using **Penny the pig** (who was sapient and beloved by Taranis — the AI had been using Taranis's love for Penny as a "banana peel" to distract him). Summon Emberus inside Club Scolopendra (protected from his planetary-melt ability) to interrogate about Geyrun's killer.

**What actually happened:**
- Carl used Gloom Wraith Phase to enter Scolopendra's body carrying Penny
- Installed a nipple ring on Penny to let her breathe inside (pierced the pig's nipple)
- Donut teleported in using Lucia Mar's loop-de-loop trap, replacing Penny
- Donut used Katia's Bolt of Ophiotaurus (second use of the reusable bolt!) on Emberus to anchor and blind him
- Taranis saw Emberus fire at Penny and killed his own brother
- Emberus is dead (Taranis killed him)
- Carl fired the biscuit-banger sphere into Scolopendra's mouth

**BUT** Eris appeared and swapped everyone's inventories. In the chaos, Donut gave Carl the **wrong biscuit** — the enriched party companion biscuit (which makes sapient party members) instead of the pet biscuit (which makes pets). **Scolopendra became a Level 1 Crawler, not a pet.** She is now "Sexy Scolopendra, Crawler #12,953,456, Level 1" — a humanoid-faced centipede with a red bow on her feeler. She said "Boop" to Carl.

**Rule implications of wrong biscuit:** She still cannot die or be abandoned without triggering the remaining attacks. She is now a **party member**, not a carry-in-inventory pet. This changes everything.

**Taranis took Krakaren** from the arena (she'd been severely weakened). He also took **Samantha** with him (Samantha went willingly — she has a plan to kill "the demon mother lady and her jerk son" who tricked her into giving birth to a monster that will destroy all of creation — connected to Kimaris).

**Eris's parting words:** "Every exit is an entrance. Don't forget about the coin. We have until the end of the twelfth to figure out how the fifth side lands."

**Tracking Tag.** Carl found a dart in his leg post-battle. "The Kyryap has claimed you." Unknown threat.

**Lucia and a child.** When Lucia stepped through the stairwell, a ~5-year-old girl was seen holding her leg. Nobody else saw it. Possibly connected to Chalchiuhtlicue or the children inside Lucia's head.

**Floor 12 entry.** 19 crawlers. Level timers on all remaining floors suspended until the Ascendency winner is declared. **Floor 12 is the last floor where deals will be offered.**

**The Epilogue reveals:**
- Louis and Britney ended up in **Sheol (Floor 15)** via the Pineapple Cabaret — the exit didn't work as planned. Louis is surviving with a blood-filtering leech (Forkith the demon provided it). Chris Andrews 2 was transformed by Scolopendra's first attack into a **werewolf** and had to be knocked unconscious.
- Samantha is also in Sheol, hunting to kill Kimaris's mother (whoever she is).
- Prime Minister Victory watched the AI's declaration from inside the emergency bunker on Earth's surface, surrounded by her staff, as the AI revealed it was the one who killed the mantid hive colony and declared its intention to keep growing.

## The Nine-Tier Attack / Primal History

Per **Sydnee Iglacia's** analysis (and the AI's own narration), the Primal "nine-tier attack" maps to what the dungeon AI is currently doing:

| Attack # | Name | What it is |
|---------|------|------------|
| 1 | Control | Took over the tunnel network / infrastructure |
| 2 | A Parade of Horribles | Current social/psychological destabilization |
| 3 | This Inevitable Ruin | Coming next |
| 4 | Bedlam | Fourth attack |
| 5–9 | Unknown | "Gets fuzzy after that" |

In the Primal fairy tale, "the princess takes control of the all-tree by killing it, accidentally killing everyone she loves — but this tempers the final attack, saving the galaxy. She plants another tree. The story repeats. This story is a tragedy."

## Game Mechanics Introduced

### Floor 10 Racing Rules
- Teams of up to 4, paired against 8 other teams per heat
- Come in last: vehicle containment removed → death
- Kill more than 1 other team per heat: risk of crawler-vs-crawler heats
- Upgrade system: regular upgrades after each heat; Golden upgrade for first place (3 choices or 1 Golden); Golden for everyone after Heat 4; audience vote for last-place team
- Mechanic NPC must stay alive for between-heat repairs; cannot enter saferoom
- No two same drivers in consecutive heats; can use mercenaries as drivers

### Floor 11 Parade
- 90 minutes to build float, assigned by garage/team combination
- Five themes assigned to groups of teams
- Parade route to judging stand then arena
- Arena populated with mobs you killed on Floor 10 plus floor boss

### Scolopendra Handling
- If Scolopendra dies OR is abandoned when a floor collapses: all remaining attacks trigger simultaneously
- Making her a Level 1 sapient crawler (not a pet): she is now a party member; she can't be put in a pet carrier
- As long as she's kept alive and not abandoned: attacks are paused

### The Pineapple Cabaret
- Secret exit from the dungeon via cleaner bot travel through dungeon infrastructure
- Bypasses the official deal system
- Not guaranteed safe (Louis ended up in Sheol instead)
- ~200+ crawlers escaped this way during Floor 10
