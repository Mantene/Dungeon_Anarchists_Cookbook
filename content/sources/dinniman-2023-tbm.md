# The Butcher's Masquerade

**The Butcher's Masquerade** is the fifth installment in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, published in 2023. It follows [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] as they navigate the sixth floor of the World Dungeon: The Hunting Grounds.

## Key Focus
- **Floor 6 (The Hunting Grounds)**: A wilderness floor where crawlers are hunted by wealthy intergalactic hunters (sportsmen) who pay to enter, while crawlers try to turn the tables on their hunters.
- **The Butcher's Masquerade**: A central, high-stakes gala event and faction clash featuring major crawler alliances, god interventions, and Carl's growing rebellion.
- **Interstellar Politics**: Expansion of the conflict between Syndicate corporate forces, the AI's erratic evolution, and the growing crawler rebellion.
