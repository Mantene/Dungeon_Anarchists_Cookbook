# Book 3: The Dungeon Anarchists Cookbook

**Author:** Matt Dinniman  
**Full Title:** *The Dungeon Anarchists Cookbook*  
**Raw Directory:** `raw/Book 3 - The Dungeon Anarchists Cookbook - Matt Dinniman/`  
**Format:** 34 chapters + Endgame Map + Epilogue  
**Note from Author:** The fourth floor is an intentionally confusing puzzle. The map near the end of the book explains the endgame layout.

---

## One-Line Summary

On Floor 4 (The Iron Tangle), Carl, Donut, and Katia must navigate a galaxy-spanning subway system, survive Hekla's betrayal, receive The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook (a legendary secret item), use Carl's Doomsday Scenario bomb to kill a Level 90 city boss, and discover that Prince Maestro — thought dead — is alive and sponsoring War God Grull.

---

## Floor Covered

**Floor page:** [Floor 4: The Iron Tangle](../floors/floor-4.md)

**Floor 4 — The Iron Tangle**  
- **Timer:** 10 days  
- **Structure:** A massive subway/train network with hundreds of colored lines, themed around real-world subways (Moscow Metro, Tokyo Metro, etc.). The entire floor is a miniaturized map of the galaxy's wormhole network. Trains run one-way on each line; stairwells are located only at stations with numbers 12, 24, 36, 48, 72, and 433. Crawlers start above station 80 and cannot descend early (unique rule — stairwells only open 6 hours before collapse).
- **Key Mechanics:** Day/night cycle replaced by constant train schedules. Monsters are addicted to "Rev-Up Vitamin Shots" (race-specific sedatives supplied by Krakaren bosses at every 5th station), which force them to loop between stops. Without their fix, they transform into something worse.
- **Transfer stations:** All prime-numbered stations. Desperado Club appears at stations ending in 1, Club Vanquisher at stations ending in 9.

**Floor 5 — Preview only (epilogue)**
- Theme: "Air Quadrant" (voted on by the viewing public)  
- Carl's party assigned to the Air Quadrant  
- Opponents: Dirigible Gnomes  
- Objective: Storm the Wasteland Fortress (a castle in the sky)

## Plot Summary

### Arrival and Race/Class Selection (Ch. 1–2)

Carl, Donut, Katia, and Mongo materialize in a moving Moscow subway car. Timer: 10 days.

**Donut's Floor 4 Character Actor specialty:** Football Hooligan  
- Gained: Mascot skill (when Mongo kills something, party gets +DEX and +CON bonus for hours), CON +10, Guinness skill (double strength when drunk)  
- Did NOT receive: Group Chant or Moving Riot  
- Lost from Floor 3 specialty: +5 DEX, 15% sales bonus  
- Retained: Negotiation skill (stayed at Level 4 from leveling it)

**Carl distributed 24 stat points:** STR 41 (base), INT 15, CON 34, DEX 23, CHA 25

**Mordecai:** Now a Grulke Infantry (toad creature) on Floor 4.

### New Items and Personal Space Setup (Ch. 2–3)

Carl and Donut each received **Fan Boxes** (Platinum tier — fans paid to vote):  
- **Carl's fan box:** *The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook* — 24th edition, unique legendary item. Initially appears as a joke recipe book, but Carl's Escape Plan skill reveals hidden text: a dungeon secret-sharing compendium passed between seasons. Contains explosive recipes, trap techniques, monster weaknesses, shortcut maps, and notes from past crawlers going back to the 15th season. Showrunners cannot read it. Must be kept secret. Each holder adds notes to the 25th edition. Key lore: **It confirms the sapper's table can be used to soak smoke curtains in healing potions to kill undead.**
- **Donut's fan box:** Enchanted Mongoliensis Saddle — tassel-covered, magically affixes to Mongo; rider gets +15% offensive spell damage; Mongo gets +20% CON and +20% melee damage while occupied.

**Personal Space upgrades (conjoined, now level 5):**  
- Ultra-Stabilized Bed: only 2 hours sleep needed
- Enhanced shower (+10% stats, reduced detectability)
- Enhanced Crafting Studio (used by Mordecai)
- Training Room (1 hr/day skill training)
- **Mordecai's room** now accessible (required level 5 communal space)
- **New purchases:** 2x Ultra-Stabilized bed, crafting tables (sapper, armorer, engineering), alchemy table level 3 (Mordecai's upgrade coupons), Bolt-Thrower's Workshop, metalworking table, repair bench

**New Carl items:** Bang Bro spell (fire+electrical damage to equipped item, 5 mana), Necklace of the Haute Bourgeoisie (+5 DEX, Talon Strike L5, 432 gold tax/10 days from medium skyfowl settlement), Scroll of Upgrade at Desperado casino → upgraded toe ring to +10 STR, Powerful Strike to L5.

**New Donut items:** Tiara of Mana Genita (+3 INT, mob detection; removed Sepsis Whore tiara destroyed on Floor 3), _Hole_ spell (temporary hole in any surface; trained to Level 3 by end of floor).

### The Prize Carousel Incident (Ch. 7–8)

Carl's Platinum fan box triggered the **Prize Carousel** show, hosted by **Chaco** (Pterolykos Song Bard, Level 66). The moment Mordecai saw Chaco, he flung a chair at him. Safe-room rules froze Mordecai and gave him a **7-day timeout** (out for most of the floor).

Chaco's connection: He did something to Mordecai "hundreds of cycles ago" related to Odette, saying "I had to do it or she would have died." Mordecai holds a profound grudge. Chaco expressed genuine remorse: "Not a day goes by where I don't regret it."

Carl's prize choice: He picked prize 6, *The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook*, over far more obviously useful items (two skill potions, 500 bombs, 2,000 earth books, his father's actual Harley Davidson, trap manual, enchanted chaps, a stat potion, and a Craftsman tool chest).

### Exploring The Iron Tangle (Ch. 4–12)

The party grinds their way through train lines, learning the system:
- Vernon the conductor (yellow line): revealed the trains don't fully loop; employees lose time/memory at station 435 via portal; the train car itself is in a partial loop
- **Gore-Gore the ManTauR** (Level 40 Neighborhood Boss, ochre line train engineer): killed via electrocution on the third rail after a train derailment. Carl obtained the Ochre Line Engineer's Key. Gore-Gore was an adherent of the war god Grull — Carl's gauntlet accumulated a 10.5% running chance to summon Grull during the fight.
- **Dismember the War Mage** on the Nightmare Express: decapitated via Donut's _Hole_ spell — she cast a hole through the metal barrier, Carl grabbed him by the hair, Donut snapped the hole shut.
- Carl took control of the Nightmare Express; cleared it using Protective Shell; hit Level 32.

**Katia's "Ugly-Ass Backpack" rig:** Katia built a backpack out of 15 tons of transformation mass (metal poles, balls, etc.). She can load it and transform into a giant battle-form. Used this to attach herself to the front of trains as a living cowcatcher (the "plow"), clearing the tracks of monsters. This made her level rocket.

### Mrs. McGibbons and Imani at the Desperado Club (Ch. 7–9)

**Elle McGibbons:** Now tiny blue-skinned Frost Maiden/Blizzardmancer Level 17. Brash, funny, foul-mouthed. Shoots icicles from her hands.

**Imani C.:** Level 24, Obsidian Butterfly race/Fire Spiritualist class. Twelve-foot ethereal butterfly wings that buff everyone they pass through. Still acts as healer/support.

**Brandon An is confirmed dead:** He died on Floor 3 holding the line against shade gremlins so the elderly residents could escape. He sent a posthumous message to Carl revealing he and Chris had a falling out over strategy; Chris left the party. Brandon's last request: Tell Chris "I love him."

**Chris Andrews 2:** Chose Igneous rock race (personality changed, became aggressive). Fighting solo. Has Carl blocked from chat.

### Hekla's Betrayal and Death (Ch. 22–25)

Hekla's plan (revealed post-mortem): She intended to kill Carl and Katia, leaving Donut alone and forcing her to join Brynhild's Daughters. This would give Hekla access to Mordecai's manager expertise. She shot two invisible crossbow bolts into Katia during a battle while claiming to be helping. Carl noticed and confronted her.

Katia used her Rush ability to try to escape — accidentally decapitated Hekla. **Hekla is dead.**

Katia's level: **Level 37** after this (mostly from killing Hekla who was Level 28).  
Carl: Level 34 → Donut: Level 32

**Katia inherits Hekla's enchanted automatic repeating crossbow** — heavy, legendary weapon.

**Brynhild's Daughters scattered** after Hekla's death. A subset (including Eva and Silfa's group) joins Carl's growing alliance.

Katia also received:
- Desperado Pass tattoo (accidentally killed Ji-Hoon at the club, now member)
- Find Crawler skill Level 3 (from gold savage box for player-killing)
- Championship Wrestler's Belt (strength bonus item from sponsor box)

### The Krakaren Endgame (Ch. 26–34)

The Krakaren collective mind — which had been supplying the monster "drugs" — broke free of Borant control. Thousands of **Krakaren Juvenile Clonelings** flooded the floor from below. The Iron Tangle announced self-destruct.

Carl organized a massive collaborative effort to save crawlers:
1. Negotiated with the Valtay Neural Enhancer implant (from his sponsor) to locate multiple crawlers
2. Organized "rapid-response carts" (battery-powered train carts) to distribute souvenir hats that worked as portal keys
3. Used a train bomb (soul crystal + explosives) to blow the ghoul generators at key stations
4. Built a crane/hoist in the stairwell chamber at station 36 to deploy a Krakaren-killing weapon

**Key new allies:**
- **Li Jun, Zhang, and Li Na** (Chinese crawlers): Zhang (Earth Mover, Level 28) used earth magic to bring the cart through impossible hallways; Li Na (Changbi/Slave Driver, Level 30, chains fighter) joined the final push
- **Tserendolgor** ("the dog soldier woman," Level 27 Dog Soldier/Crisper): German Shepherd-race crawler with a flamethrower, from the station 36 defense group
- **Bautista**: Reached the abyss, eventually escaped with Carl's coordination

### Carl's Doomsday Scenario and Prince Maestro (Ch. 33)

The **Mimic Rex** (Level 90 City Boss) appeared — a monster shaped like a station building that had killed 23,000+ crawlers. Simultaneously, Carl's gauntlet finally triggered a full **War God Grull summoning**.

The Grull sponsorship reveal: **Prince Maestro of the Skull Empire** — thought dead from the Valtay yacht attack in Book 2 — appears as Grull's sponsor and commentator. He is alive.

Carl used **Carl's Doomsday Scenario** (the god-tier unstable explosive from his inventory) to somehow dispatch the Mimic Rex. (Details: Carl threw the bomb — which explodes on removal from inventory — at the right moment in the fight with Grull vs. Mimic Rex.) The Mimic Rex was killed. Grull was temporarily summoned, but left when the timer ran out.

**Bautista reached Level 41** after the final battles.

Carl in the epilogue/final stages: Level 35+

### Sponsors and Political Developments

- **Carl's sponsor:** Valtay Corporation — sent a Bronze Benefactor Box containing a **Valtay Neural Enhancer #544** (a permanent brain implant; allows Carl to "see through portals" and has other hidden benefits)
- **Donut's sponsor:** Princess D'nadia of the Prism (the tentacle-faced Saccathian from the talk show) — sent loot including signed items
- **Katia's sponsor:** Princess Formidable of the Skull Empire (King Rust's only surviving daughter, now heir after Stalwart's presumed death)

The Valtay and Princess Formidable sponsoring Carl/Katia creates a dangerous political triangle with Borant.

## Game Mechanics Introduced

- **Character Actor class carry-over:** If Donut levels a class-given skill at least once before losing the class at the next floor, she keeps the extra levels she gained. The base class-granted levels go away, but the player-leveled ones remain. Useful strategy: use rare spells immediately to gain the kept levels.
- **Equippable mass for Doppelgangers:** Katia can load transformation mass into an equippable container (backpack) and access up to 15+ tons of additional body mass, enabling giant war-forms. The metal must be worn/equipped as a backpack.
- **Gods/Deities:** Not truly divine beings — they are rich aliens wearing "Soul Armor" (biological suit), similar to Valtay worm-people. Invulnerable on floors below 12. Can be temporarily summoned via equipment (Carl's gauntlet has a Grull-summon chance). Can be un-summoned after (Level + Deity Level) seconds. NOTE: "Take That Shit Off" type spells can remove aliens from the deity suits.
- **Sheol Glass Reaper Cases:** Used to contain explosives that are too unstable to store otherwise. The same type holds Carl's Doomsday Scenario.
- **Sheol Bricks/Rocks:** Demonic rocks that burn long and hot. Fire Brandy (the demon stoker on the Nightmare Express) gives them off as byproducts.
- **Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook:** Unique legendary item. 24th edition. Contains hidden text only visible via Escape Plan skill. Invisible to showrunners. Passed between seasons when holder dies/retires. Cannot be duplicated.
