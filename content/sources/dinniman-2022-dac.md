# The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook

**The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook** is the third installment in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, published in 2022. It follows [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] as they traverse the fourth floor of the World Dungeon, a massive train-based puzzle level known as "The Iron Tangle."

## Key Focus
- **Floor 4 (The Iron Tangle)**: Introduces complex transit-based mechanics, including color-coded subways, named express loops, and high-altitude portals.
- **The Cookbook**: Carl obtains *The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook*, a unique system-exploiting guide written by previous crawlers that enables him to construct custom explosives and navigate hidden system boundaries.
- **Faction and Corporate Politics**: Features the escalating proxy war between the Valtay Corporation and the Skull Empire, alongside Borant's corporate instability.
