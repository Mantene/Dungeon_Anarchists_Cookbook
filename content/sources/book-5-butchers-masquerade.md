# Book 5: The Butcher's Masquerade

**Author:** Matt Dinniman  
**Full Title:** *The Butcher's Masquerade*  
**Raw Directory:** `raw/Book 5 - The Butchers Masquerade - Matt Dinniman/`  
**Format:** 4 Parts, 75 chapters + Epilogue  
**Parts:** I. The Hunt | II. The Vengeance of the Daughter | III. The Unhinged Child | IV. The Butchering

---

## One-Line Summary

On Floor 6 (The Hunting Grounds), Carl and Donut fight hunters (especially the deadly mantis Vrah), complete the *Vengeance of the Daughter* storyline, survive the Butcher's Masquerade where Imogen/Ifechi is the country boss, kill Circe Took (stripped of her god-armor by _Laundry Day_), form the Princess Posse faction war team — and in the final twist, the Crown of the Sepsis Whore passes to Katia's head.

## Plot Summary

### Class Selection and Arrival (Ch. 1–5)

Carl is pulled into the Syndicate liaison Orren's office with Katia. The Gate of the Feral Gods is on the table — Orren wants Carl to voluntarily surrender it. Carl demands a lawyer.

**Quasar** appears (a Null/gray alien attorney in a tan suit with a gyrating hula girl tie). He negotiates a deal: Carl temporarily surrenders the gate and gets it back at the ninth floor, in exchange for four mercenaries with special spells: Bomo (_Teleport to Stairwell_, 25-day cooldown), The Sledge (_Zerzura_ spell — sends a town/settlement to a random floor, killing everything it lands on, which resurrects as "Children of Inpewt" revenants), Clay-ton, and Very Sullen.

**Carl's subclass: Agent Provocateur** (chose explosives focus; ignored Mordecai's advice to pick melee Revolutionary)  
Benefits: +1 INT per level-up, +1 Sapper's Table, +2 Explosives Handling, Advanced Bomb Maker's Workshop, faster explosives training.

**Donut's Floor 6 specialty: Legendary Diva** (bard class)  
- Spells cast through singing. Donut is a terrible singer.  
- Spells: _Standing Ovation_ (party +DEX + spell power), _Entourage_ (illusionary party members), _Encore_ (heal party + remove debuffs), a psionic attack, an ice attack  
- Also received: _Laundry Day_ spell (strips armor from opponents; at L15 can target gods wearing "soul armor")  
- Problem: Needs a "Golden Throat" enchantment (autotune) to make spells effective  
- **Mordecai is now a Pocket Kuma** — tiny, adorably cute fuzzy bear creature on Floor 6. Carl laughs himself to tears. Mordecai is furious.

**Carl attacked Zockau before hunters were released** — set up a missile battery in the park around the stairwell. Killed 35 hunters. Vrah (the mantis hunter queen/mayor of Zockau) survived. Got Donut's first **player-killer skull** (she accidentally hit a fleeing hunter with a magic missile).

**Leaderboard after the attack:**

10. Quan Ch (now Sergeant at Arms) — Level 48 — 100,000 (x3)

### Part I: The Hunt (Ch. 1–15)

Carl established a defensive settlement (Point Mongo) near a bridge in the jungle. Killed multiple hunters. Key events:  
- **Vrah** (mantis hunter): Is the main floor antagonist. She's a hive queen aspirant whose daughter Diwata can turn into a god (the pain god Algos's vessel). Carl shot her with an arrow during an escape and infected her.  
- **Miriam Dom** and **Prepotente**: They join up with Carl's group. Miriam is now a Vampire/Shepherd who drinks blood from Prepotente's kills. Bianca (her hellspawn goat-dragon familiar) is growing rapidly.  
- **Court injunction** (Quasar's work): Hunters are no longer allowed to communicate with people outside the dungeon.
- **Beatrice appears on Odette's show** — she was saved from bounty hunters by Lexis (Odette's agent). Now a Syndicate citizen at an undisclosed off-world location. She appeared drugged and disoriented on camera.

### Part II: The Vengeance of the Daughter (Ch. 16–29)

Carl and Donut ally with **Tsarina Signet** again on the jungle floor. Her story continues: she needs to confront her half-sister Queen Imogen of the High Elves.  
- **Samantha** (the mysterious entity in Carl's custody): a naiad/related creature who claimed to be Signet's third cousin. She helped bring Signet a tactical advantage at the Masquerade.  
- **Were-castors** (Clint and Holger): Signet's loyal crew; immune to paralysis, vicious fighters.  
- **Queen Imogen's familiar: Ferdinand** — yes, the orange tom cat. Ferdinand is a Level 100 Province Boss now ("familiar of Queen Imogen"). He attacked Imogen at the Masquerade, hitting her in the head and buying Carl a critical second.

### Part III: The Unhinged Child (Ch. 30–55)

Focus on the velociraptor pack quest and Lucia Mar:  
- **Kiwi** (Level 60 Mongoliensis Pack Leader): A female velociraptor who becomes Mongo's mate. She and her pack of 36 velociraptors join forces at the Masquerade.  
- **Lucia Mar**: The "Unhinged Child." She killed Ifechi using her Rubber reflect spell at the end of Floor 5. She set up a teleport ahead of the Masquerade and vanished before the boss fight. She is still alive.  
- **Stairwell city raid**: Carl, Elle, Bautista et al. cleared a hunter-held city near the stairwells.  
- **Beatrice reveal**: Odette says Beatrice was being planned as Queen Imogen (the country boss) before she was removed from Earth. A different person became Imogen instead.  
- **Zerzura spell use**: The Sledge cast _Zerzura_ to teleport Donut's captured changeling village (including Juice Box, Skarn, young changelings) to the ninth floor, where the revenants would emerge. The changelings get to survive and escape, but will face "Children of Inpewt" revenants.  
- **Chris Andrews 2**: Now free of Maggie (she was apparently separated by the Floor 5 flood). He's Level 35, Igneous rock, with bodyguard Clay-ton. He and Katia's "guy" Bautista became friendly.

### Part IV: The Butchering — The Butcher's Masquerade (Ch. 56–75)

The top 50 crawlers + remaining hunters + the Masquerade. Held at the High Elf castle. Four "teams":  
1. Crawlers  
2. Remaining 75 hunters  
3. Queen Imogen + high elf army  
4. Diwata (the pain god, using Circe Took as a vessel, Level 250)

**Queen Imogen revealed: Ifechi** — Florin's dead girlfriend, resurrected as a country boss. Level 145 Country Boss.

**Signet's act**: Signet allowed herself to be pulled into "the Nothing" with Imogen, removing Imogen's divine protection and enabling the castle to teleport. Signet may be dead (or may have survived in the Nothing).

**The fight:**
- Mongo and Kiwi tore Vrah apart (all limbs removed). **Vrah is dead.**
- Donut used _Laundry Day_ at Level 15 (used Pawna's Tears potion to raise it) to strip Diwata of her god-armor, revealing Circe Took, who was killed by her own nymph children. **Diwata banished. Circe Took dead.**
- Florin killed Queen Imogen/Ifechi with the killing blow. He immediately went down the stairs, devastated.
- **Dmitri and Maxim Popov** died but were reborn as baby nodling yearlings → declared INELIGIBLE (children not allowed) → **they ESCAPED the dungeon** and were transferred to surface "kinder facility" as free Syndicate citizens. Their game guide had planned this from the start.
- **Ferdinand** slammed into Imogen's head, saving Carl for one critical second. He was launched away; still in Carl's party (alive).

**Deaths at the Masquerade:**
- Firas M (Level 35) — killed by Britney's accidental lightning redirect (butterfly jacket)
- Gwendolyn Duet (Gwen) — killed in the battle
- Horton (the "_Wonderwall_ guy," musician) — dead
- Miriam Dom — died protecting Prepotente
- Many unnamed crawlers

**Survivors:** Carl, Donut, Katia, Prepotente, Elle, Imani, Bautista, Li Jun, Li Na, Tran (lost legs), Britney (burned face), Louis (alive but grieving Firas)

### The Princess Posse (Ch. 74)

After the battle, Carl and Donut become **Co-Warlords** of a new faction war team for Floor 9:  
- Name: **The Princess Posse**  
- Sponsored by: The Society for the Eradication of Cocker Spaniels (TSECS), NFC (= OIPAN, the anti-crawl pacifist organization)  
- Inherited spots from: the Dreadnoughts (abandoned) and the Burrowers (mantis group, abandoned after Vrah/Circe's deaths)  
- They have 0 mercenaries but a valid faction spot  
- Carl is pushing a vote to "Remove Safety Protections for Faction Sponsors" — if passed, it would force all faction leaders to actually risk their lives at Floor 9

### The Crown Twist (Epilogue)

**Eva Sigrid is killed** by Katia (her second player-killer skull). But Eva's final act: she pulled the **Crown of the Sepsis Whore** off Imogen's body and placed it on Katia's head as she died.

The Crown of the Sepsis Whore is now on Katia. This means on Floor 9, **only one** of Donut or Katia can descend to Floor 10 without first killing the entire Blood Sultanate royal family. A devastating complication that sets up the Floor 9 crisis.

### Prepotente Breaks Floor 7

Prepotente cracked the entire Floor 7 ("The Great Race") glass maze using:  
- His dead mother Miriam's Boots of Kiznet's Final Flight (which contained the tale of this floor)  
- A potion from their shared sponsors  
- The Enchanted Stick of Cascadia's Screams  

All crawlers were dumped directly onto Floor 8 (bypassing Floor 7 entirely). The announcement system ("Cascadia") screamed. Floor 7 never happened.

## Key Items and Mechanics (Book 5)

**Carl new items:**
- _Book of Lore – Commonwealth of the Bahamas_ (bestiary of Bahamas creatures/monsters for Floor 8)
- Ring of Divine Suffering worn and used (marking hunters → +1 stat on kill, stacking)
- _Tripper_ spell (creates/triggers traps; once every 5 hours; used to kill Zabit)
- Advanced Bomber's Studio (virtual test room + precision tuning workbench)

**Donut new items:**
- _Laundry Day_ spell raised to Level 15 (using Pawna's Tears potion; now can remove god-armor from deity vessels)
- _Standing Ovation_, _Entourage_, _Encore_ from Legendary Diva class
- Temporary charisma boost during Masquerade triggered Love Vampire skill (multiple elves fell under its effect)

**Katia:**
- Crown of the Sepsis Whore (unwillingly placed on her head by dying Eva)
- 2 player-killer skulls (killed Hekla Book 3, killed Eva Book 5)

**Faction Wars:**
- The Princess Posse: Carl and Donut are Co-Warlords
- 9 factions compete; Princess Posse starts with 0 troops but has spots for inherited armies
- One pending vote: Remove Safety Protections for Faction Sponsors

**Mechanics:**
- _Zerzura_ spell (Sledge): sends a town to a random floor; creates Children of Inpewt (revenants from whatever the town kills). Used to teleport the changeling settlement.
- _Laundry Day_ at Level 15: can strip god-armor from deity vessels (not the god itself, but the person wearing it as a suit).
- Nodling death mechanic: two-headed nodlings split into two baby nodlings when they die. If the babies are declared ineligible (children not allowed in the dungeon), they escape to the surface as free citizens.
