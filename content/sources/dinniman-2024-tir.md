# This Inevitable Ruin

**This Inevitable Ruin** is the seventh installment in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, published in 2024. It follows [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] as they enter the ninth floor of the World Dungeon: Faction Wars.

## Key Focus
- **Floor 9 (Faction Wars)**: A team-based territorial war simulation where crawlers, faction sponsors, private armies, and NPCs participate in active combat.
- **Removing Safety Protections**: Following Carl's Emergency Action Item, safety protections for Faction sponsors have been removed, making corporate representatives vulnerable in the dungeon.
- **Tenth Team (NPC Home Team)**: The introduction of a tenth team based in Larracos, co-warlorded by Juice Box and Ferdinand.
- **Cosmic Factions**: The conflict between the Valtay, the Skull Empire, the Apothecary, and ancient rebel entities over the rogue AI and the fate of the seeded planets.
