# The Eye of the Bedlam Bride

**The Eye of the Bedlam Bride** is the sixth installment in Matt Dinniman's *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, published in 2023. It follows [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] as they skip Floor 7 and navigate the eighth floor of the World Dungeon: The Ghosts of Earth.

## Key Focus
- **Floor 8 (The Ghosts of Earth)**: A precise replica of Earth's surface in the weeks before the collapse (specifically December 10th). Crawlers explore local regions where human inhabitants are intangible ghosts, but physical structures and objects maintain mass and momentum.
- **T'Ghee Card Deck Combat**: A card-based deck system where squad leaders capture local folklore monsters into T'Ghee totems (cards) to construct a deck of six totems alongside utility, mystic, and snare cards. Squad leaders lose access to normal spells and inventory during card battles.
- **Orisha Gods & Shrines**: The presence of Cuban/Yoruban deities (Orishas) such as Ogun, Asojano, and Yemaya, whose shrines and re-ascendency play a central role in the floor's mechanics and progression.
- **Historical Revelations**: The backstory of Odette's former party (Mordecai, Uzzi, Chaco, Hold Steady) and the tragic sacrifice of Uzzi to kill the goddess Dodola, alongside the revelation that the interviewer Rosetta was once a crawler herself.
