## One-Line Summary

After Earth is strip-mined by the alien Borant Corporation under Syndicate law, human survivors are forced into an 18-floor "World Dungeon" to claw back their planet's matter — while being broadcast as live entertainment to trillions of aliens. Carl, an ex-Coast Guard marine tech, and his ex-girlfriend's show cat Princess Donut crawl Floors 1 and 2.

## Plot Summary

### The Transformation (Ch. 1)
At 2:23 AM PST January 3rd, Earth's buildings are crushed by Borant Corporation's mining. Anyone indoors dies instantly. Carl, outside retrieving his ex-girlfriend Beatrice's Persian show cat Princess Donut from a tree, survives. A Syndicate AI announces the 18-level World Dungeon: reclaim your planet's matter or die trying.

### Tutorial Guild & Mordecai (Ch. 2–4)
Carl and Donut enter the dungeon into a dark hall. Carl fights and kills goblin mobs with his bare hands, then enters a tutorial guild. He meets **Mordecai** — a Rat Hooligan guildmaster, formerly a Skyfowl Changeling who crawled to Floor 11. Mordecai explains stats, skills, inventory, the rating system, and how loot boxes work.

Carl's starting loot boxes yield: Nightgaunt Cloak of Stoutness (+4 Con, poison/ice resist, anti-pierce), Trollskin Shirt of Pummeling (+7 Regen), Toe Ring of the Splatter Skunk (+3 Str, +3 Powerful Strike), Goblin Pass tattoo (goblin pass but +20% fairy damage).

### Donut's Transformation (Ch. 5–7)
Donut eats the Enhanced Pet Biscuit and transforms from a passive pet into a talking crawler. New stats: STR 11, INT 11, CON 2, DEX 8, CHA 25. Her Legendary Pet Box yields the **Enchanted Crown of the Sepsis Whore** — which she immediately equips. It places her in the royal line of succession for the Blood Sultanate on Floor 9, trapping her there until the entire family is killed. Donut names the party "The Royal Court of Princess Donut." Carl becomes "Royal Bodyguard."

### Floor 1 Crawl (Ch. 8–27)
Carl and Donut fight their way east, developing teamwork:
- Carl focuses on bare-knuckle fighting, explosives, foot-based attacks
- Donut: Magic Missile (primary), Torch (navigation), Second Chance (necromancy, raises dead mobs as 1-minute minions)

**Key Floor 1 bosses killed:**
- The Hoarder (Level 7, Neighborhood Boss): human woman fused with garbage. Carl strangled her with a metal chain.
- The Juicer (Level 9, Neighborhood Boss): human bodybuilder fused with a troglodyte. Donut bit through a vein in his neck.
- The Goblin War Chieftain (Neighborhood Boss): blown up by cart + black powder + hobgoblin pus. Also killed goblin babies (→ Desperado Pass tattoos for both).
- The Ball of Swine (Level 15, Borough Boss): 30+ Tuskling knights in a rolling ball formation. Defeated by a portable modular fortress ("The Speedbump") built with Brandon's team + ninja star spike strips.

**Key encounters:**
- Goblins Rory and Lorelai (shamankas): Donut charmed them; they provided the Goblin Copper Chopper with sidecar. Carl traded meth for it. Carl and Donut also started a meth war between goblins and llamas.
- Frank Q (Level 8, ICE agent, 3 skulls) and Maggie My (Level 9, 5 skulls): try to ambush Carl and Donut in a safe room. Frozen by the safe-zone rules. Carl booby-traps a rat corpse with lit dynamite for them.
- Brandon An's group (Team Meadow Lark): 4 able-bodied CNAs + 38 elderly nursing home residents from Wenatchee. Helped down to Floor 2 via the stairwell behind the Ball of Swine.

**Carl invents Carl's Jug O' Boom** (Ch. 32): moonshine jug + goblin oil + torch → incendiary tossable. Added to the Dungeon Codex. Carl earns 1 gold/kill when others use the recipe.

### Odette Interview (Ch. 28–29)
Carl and Donut are transported to a production boat on Earth's surface for *Dungeon Crawler After Hours with Odette*. Key revelations:
- Odette: legless human, former crawler (reached Floor 11 via deal), was Mordecai's trainer. Her "crab-taur" stage persona is elaborate cosplay over a floating wheelchair.
- Key advice: Do NOT change Donut's race (viewers love her as a cat). Listen to Mordecai's subtle clues for class/race selection. Loot boxes are NOT random — they're influenced by viewer engagement and sponsor interests.
- Odette's motivation: money. "The longer you survive, the more I make."
- Asks Carl to tell Mordecai to "seek her out" when his indentureship ends.

### Floor 2 Crawl (Ch. 30–47)
Views explode after Odette's show: 212 Billion views, 4.4 Billion followers, 793 Million favorites.

**Key Floor 2 bosses and mobs:**
- Krakaren Clone (Level 10, Neighborhood Boss): anti-vax octopus "mom" mob that left her room. Carl makes the Rev-Up Immunity Smoothie (1/3 moonshine + blended laminak fairy corpse) to fight her taint/measles debuffs. Defeats her via zombie clurichaun carrying a hobgoblin-pus bomb. Carl nearly dies; Donut drags him to safety.
- Brindle Grubs → Cow-Tailed Grubs → Pupae → Brindled Vespas: Floor 2's waste-disposal mob system. Pupae hatch into acid-spitting hornets. Carl and Donut carpet-bomb their way to the stairwell.
- Unvaccinated Clurichauns: level 3, MLM moonshine salesforce, inflict "The Taint" debuff (no healing while active).
- Laminak Fairies (Level 6): upper management of the Rev-Up enterprise. Key alchemy ingredient.
- Danger Dingoes (Level 5) + Kobolds: dog and rider mobs from Floor 2's kobold neighborhoods.
- Mind Horrors (Level 4): floating brains with psionic attacks, weak physically.

**The Rage Elemental incident (Ch. 38–40):**
Resident Jack urinates in the dungeon, summoning a Level 93 Rage Elemental. It kills Yolanda Martinez and Randall, then traps the group in a safe room. Carl builds the "Bomb Chicken" (MOAB) — a bicycle trailer bomb launcher. Using the alchemy table exploit (it's indestructible, used as a shield), Carl and Donut lure the elemental to the stairwell chamber. Carl uses his BigBoi Boxers' **Protective Shell** spell and an oil slick to slide the elemental down the stairwell. It dissolves when it hits the void — they get no XP but achieve a new record. Views hit 212 Billion.

**Zev encounter (Ch. 35–36):** Borant PR Agent Zev (a 2-foot kua-tin in a retro deep-sea suit) is assigned as their liaison. She turns out to be a non-Party member who fears for her family. She and Donut bond over *Gossip Girl*. Key intel from Mordecai (whispered): The Bloom (ultra-nationalist kua-tin "Party," like Nazi Germany) now controls the kua-tin government. Borant is bankrupt; the Party is rushing the dungeon to end quickly and collect its payout.

**Maestro subplot (Ch. 42–43):** During a forced interview on a Skull Empire show hosted by "The Maestro" (a Crown Prince orcish aristocrat), Carl antagonizes him live on galactic TV. A fan-made "Pork Boy Snick" (deepfake-style video) goes viral against the Maestro. Carl and Donut become equal in appearance fees to Lucia Mar.

**Agatha's mystery (Ch. 47):** Agatha reappears at the Floor 2 stairwell. She's still Level 4 (unchanged since the borough boss) despite having survived alone for days. She was edited out of the recap show. She carries what appears to be a special inventory potion Carl hasn't seen. She gives Carl a sharp warning look when he starts asking questions. Implication: she may be a plant, an AI agent, or otherwise connected to the dungeon's management. She descends to Floor 3.

**Pet Reward Room (Ch. 46):** Donut chooses a Mongoliensis (baby velociraptor) over the Tummy Acher or Brindle Grub. Named Mongo. Vicious and hostile at first; bonds with Donut (via Imani's accidental rescue from a Brindled Vespa) during the last hours of Floor 2. His dot turns orange, then he joins the party as "Royal Steed."

**Odette Epilogue Interview:** Reveals Mongoliensis = velociraptor. Pink-feathered, beaked, very aggressive. Odette advises Carl: get a pathfinder skill or mapping ability for lower floors. Make sure Donut selects her race first in Floor 3; Carl tailors his class to complement hers.

## Game Mechanics Introduced

See `wiki/mechanics/` for full pages.

- **Core stats:** STR, INT, CON, DEX, CHA. Base 3–5 for humans. No distribution until race/class chosen (Floor 3).
- **Skills:** Level through use. Hundreds of skills including Pugilism, Explosives Handling, IED, Second Chance, Magic Missile.
- **Inventory:** Unlimited dimensional storage. Time-locked (food never spoils). No living creatures. Must physically lift item for 4 seconds to store.
- **Party system:** Shared XP by participation %. Party leader = highest combined stats. Group chat.
- **Rating system:** Views → Followers → Favorites → Patrons. Patrons pay for loot boxes. NOT random — influenced by engagement and sponsor interests. Patronage spots auctioned starting Floor 4.
- **Boss types:** Neighborhood → Borough → City → Province → Country → Floor. Each tier drops one tier higher loot box. Persistent loot (maps, field guides) drops for all crawlers on the kill.
- **Safe rooms:** No combat. Safe room doors close 1 hour before floor collapse. Bopca Protector NPCs serve food and provide rooms.
- **Race/class selection:** Occurs on Floor 3. Race first, then class. Stat points distributed automatically to race/class minimums; remainder freely assignable. New "earth-specific" classes available each season.
- **Punishment system:** Urinating in dungeon summons Level 93 Rage Elemental (Floors 1–2). Removed Floor 3+.
- **Desperado Pass tattoo:** Granted for killing 20+ non-combatants in one attack. Grants access to the Desperado Club.
- **Goblin Pass tattoo:** Free passage through goblin territory. +20% damage from fairy-class mobs.
