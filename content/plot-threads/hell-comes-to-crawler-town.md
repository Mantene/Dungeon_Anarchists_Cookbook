# Hell Comes to Crawler Town

**Hell Comes to Crawler Town** was a floor-wide event/quest triggered on Floor 8 (The Ghosts of Earth) due to Sheol overflow and environmental failures.

## Quest Parameters
- **Objective**: Survive the sudden overflow of Sheol demons possessing undead shells in Cuba during the stairwell phase.
- **Status**: `resolved`

## Progression
- **[[277-book-6-chapter-54-evicted-demons-and-word-weaver-teleport]]**: The quest is initiated after the failure of the *Chowder War* quest, triggered by the collision of zombie turkeys and crab sperm in the global ocean.
- **[[278-book-6-chapter-55-survival-royale-and-madre-de-aguas]]**: Carl's teleport clusterfuck brings the Level 125 Madre de Aguas snake boss and thousands of zombie monk seals and crabs directly to the Cuba stairwell, freeing Level 140 demons.
- **[[285-book-6-chapter-62-sidewalk-ends-and-amayon-quest]]**: The quest merges with "Where the Sidewalk Ends" and eventually "The Missing Piece," where Carl frees Amayon and uses the Sheol fire/portal mechanics to globally evacuate all remaining crawlers. The event is resolved when the portal opens, sucking the Cuba region and remaining crawlers into the Floor 9 stairwells.
