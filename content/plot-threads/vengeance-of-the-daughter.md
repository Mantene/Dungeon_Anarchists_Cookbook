# Vengeance of the Daughter

**Vengeance of the Daughter** is a major questline and secondary program narrative on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds), focused on [[tsarina-signet]]'s rebellion against the Naiad Confederacy and her revenge against her half-sister, Queen [[imogen]] of the High Elves.

## Progression
- **[[160-book-5-chapter-15-hunting-map]]** (Chapter 15): Signet recruits Carl and Donut for her rebellion, triggering **Part One** of the quest. Her goal is to assassinate the leaders of the Naiad Confederacy holed up in Fort Freedom.
- **[[170-book-5-chapter-25-future-huntress]]** (Chapter 25): Carl executes the orc hunter [[future-huntress]] as a blood sacrifice to enable Signet's summoning spell. Carl launches [[samantha]] into Fort Freedom's water system as a distraction.
- **[[172-book-5-chapter-27-claude-sludgington]]** (Chapter 27): The assault on Fort Freedom succeeds, but the team is ambushed by the borough boss [[claude-sludgington-the-fourth]], who vaporizes [[nadine]]. Carl teleports Claude onto the shore, where he, Donut, and Mongo kill the boss.
- **[[173-book-5-chapter-28-big-tina]]** (Chapter 28): Part One of the quest completes. Langley's team leaves the southern shore unprotected, allowing [[big-tina]] to slaughter the Ursine guards and kill the were-castor [[clint-smashgrab]].
- **Triggering Part Two**: **Part Two of Two** is triggered, requiring Carl and Donut to help Signet assassinate Queen Imogen at the annual High Elf party ([[the-butchers-masquerade]]). 
- **[[219-book-5-chapter-74-the-final-cage-match]]**: During the final battle, Queen Imogen and her familiar [[ferdinand]] are defeated by the combined forces of the crawlers and the summoned deity [[diwata]]. The quest *Vengeance of the Daughter* is successfully completed.
