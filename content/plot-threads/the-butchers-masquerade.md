# The Butcher's Masquerade

**The Butcher's Masquerade** is the central plot thread and endgame event of Floor 6 (The Hunting Grounds), taking the form of an elite gala party.

## Current Status
- **Status**: `completed`
- **Goal**: Crawlers must qualify to receive an invitation by staying in the top 50 ranks or earning an invite through high ratings.

## Mechanics & Features
- **Attendance**: Restricted to the top 50 crawlers (plus one guest each), intergalactic celebrities, sponsors, and hunters.
- **Competitions**: Includes a talent show, costume contest, and a **Pet Beauty Contest** (which [[princess-donut]] is determined to enter [[mongo]] into).
- **The Prize Counter**: A central feature of the Masquerade where crawlers can exchange **Hunting Trophies** (physical severed hands/claws of the hunters they have permanently killed) for powerful, game-changing prizes.
- **Crawler Insurgency**: The event encourages crawlers to actively hunt down and kill their hunters (sponsors and tourists) rather than just fleeing, creating a high-stakes arena of violence.
