# Plot Thread: The Iron Tangle Escape Plan

**The Iron Tangle Escape Plan** tracks the crawlers' efforts to navigate the fourth floor of the World Dungeon—a transit-themed puzzle level—and reach the stairwells to progress to the next floor.

## Background and Floor Rules
The fourth floor consists of a complex transit network called the Iron Tangle. The rules and constraints include:
- **Upward-Only Subways**: Color-coded lines (like the Fulvous, Purple, or Mauve lines) only run upward. Crawlers who board them cannot travel back down.
- **Prime-Numbered Stations**: Stairwells to the fifth floor are only located at prime-numbered stations (12, 24, 36, 48, 72).
- **Backtrack Obstacle**: Crawlers begin the floor at stations higher than 80, meaning they must find specific backtracking routes or exit loops to reach the earlier prime stations.
- **Withdrawal DTs**: Mobs are kept in check on the tracks via Pooka-administered sedatives. When trains stop and the supply is cut, mobs go through withdrawal (the DTs), mutating into hyper-violent Stage 2 threats or Festering Ghouls, which enables them to breach transit stations.

## The Five Escape Paths
Through exploration, crowdsourcing, and the Cookbook, Carl identifies five possible routes to reach the lower stations:
1. **The Conductor Hat**: Wearing the souvenir conductor hat given at the start of the floor and riding a train to the end of the line. This teleports the crawler to the staging trainyard, but leaves them outside the train swarmed by thousands of zombies.
2. **The Engineer Key**: Killing a train engineer to loot their key. This teleports the crawler to the same trainyard, but safely inside the locomotive cab.
3. **The Employee Portal**: Taking the employee portal at Station 435. This route is guarded by the Kravyad boss and causes memory/time loss.
4. **Operating a Named Loop Train**: Named trains (like the *Nightmare Express* or the *Dismemberment Limited*) run in loops. If controlled, crawlers can switch tracks at the switching stations to access repair portals or Homeward Bound lines.
5. **The Robot Room Tunnel**: A secret tunnel behind deactivated automatons in the robot rooms. The tunnel opens when the robots wake up, which is triggered when the track mobs start their DT withdrawal.
