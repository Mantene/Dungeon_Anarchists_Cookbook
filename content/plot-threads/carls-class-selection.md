# Carl's Class Selection

This plot thread tracks [[carl]]'s build development, stat gains, and his preparation for selecting a race and class upon reaching Floor 3.

## Current Status
- **Status**: `open`
- **Class/Race options**: To be selected on Floor 3.
- **Current Level**: 13 (As of the end of Floor 2)

## Progression
- **[[002-book-1-chapter-2-welcome-to-first-floor]]**: Carl is assigned the race of Human, and his initial stat points are auto-distributed based on his physical and mental profile.
- **[[004-book-1-chapter-4-intergalactic-game-show]]**: Carl examines his stats (Strength: 6, Intelligence: 3, Constitution: 5, Dexterity: 5, Charisma: 4) and is told he cannot distribute new level-up points until he chooses a class on Floor 3. [[mordecai]] recommends a strength-based class.
- **[[005-book-1-chapter-5-loot-boxes]]**: Carl reaches Level 2. He gains the Pugilism skill.
- **[[006-book-1-chapter-6-magic-gear]]**: Carl equips the Enchanted Trollskin Shirt, Nightgaunt Cloak, and Toe Ring. His Strength rises to 9 and Constitution rises to 9. Mordecai suggests he consider a monk-based class due to his high Unarmed Combat and Powerful Strike skills.
- **[[008-book-1-chapter-8-bad-llama]]**: Carl fights the Bad Llama with bare hands, leveling his Unarmed Combat. He reaches Level 3 and gains the Foot Soldier and Smush skills, which heavily reward bare-foot attacks.
- **[[012-book-1-chapter-12-kneepads-and-commentary]]**: Carl gains the Iron Punch skill (Level 3). He notes the need to find war gauntlets or lumpers to protect his knuckles from breaking.
- **[[019-book-1-chapter-19-desperado-pass]]**: Carl reaches Level 8. He gains the *IED* skill (Level 3), which reduces IED accident rates and boosts damage yield. He drinks a Skill Potion that grants him the *Determine Value* skill (Level 1), enabling him to sort his inventory by item value.
- **[[021-book-1-chapter-21-spiral-maze]]**: Carl drives the Copper Chopper, raising his *Chopper Pilot* skill to Level 5 and unlocking the speed throttle control.
- **[[023-book-1-chapter-23-troglodyte-grind]]**: Carl notices that Donut's Strength is 18—double his own—due to her feline stat multipliers. He encourages her to practice using her claws, which she initially rejects as "inelegant."
- **[[024-book-1-chapter-24-the-juicer]]**: Carl fights the Juicer boss by lifting a lopsided 250-pound barbell and a 100-pound plate, illustrating how his high Strength stat can be used to weaponize the environment. Donut pounces with her claws and teeth, showing high damage potential but leaving her with near-fatal injuries due to her low Constitution.
- **[[025-book-1-chapter-25-war-gauntlet]]**: Carl reaches Level 9. He obtains the **Enchanted War Gauntlet of the Exalted Grull (Right-Handed)**, which transforms from a bracer to a spiked steel gauntlet when he forms a fist, granting +3 Strength, +1 Dexterity, +2 Iron Punch, and +1 Powerful Strike without negating his bare-knuckle bonus. He also gets the **Enchanted Pedicure Kit of the Sylph**, which grants a +15% barefoot damage bonus, +3 to Smush, unbreakable foot bones, and a trap-alarm system. Mordecai advises Carl to stick to bare-knuckle combat due to his high passive bonuses.
- **[[026-book-1-chapter-26-ball-of-swine]]**: Carl utilizes the spiked gauntlet and stomps to defeat Tuskling knights and courtesans.
- **[[027-book-1-chapter-27-second-floor]]**: Carl and Donut reach Level 10.
- **[[029-book-1-chapter-29-odettes-advice]]**: Odette warns Donut to remain a cat on Floor 3 because changing her race will cause her ratings and sponsors to plummet.
- **[[030-book-1-chapter-30-dingoes-and-grubs]]**: Carl drinks a Cheat Code Potion, boosting his *Pugilism* skill to Level 10. He equips the Pedicure Kit of the Sylph's barefoot buffs. Donut learns the *Second Chance* necromancy spell.
- **[[032-book-1-chapter-32-taint-and-moonshine]]**: Carl invents **Carl's Jug O' Boom**, raising his *Incendiary Device Handling* to Level 5 and expanding his ranged/explosive specialization.
- **[[035-book-1-chapter-35-saved-by-pedicure]]**: Carl equips the **Enchanted BigBoi Boxers**, which grant +2 Constitution (bringing it to 14) and the Level 15 *Protective Shell* item-based spell.
- **[[041-book-1-chapter-41-goodbyes-at-stairs]]**: Carl gains a **Sapper's Table** and 10 **Proximity Triggers**, cementing a secondary trapmaking specialty. Donut receives the *Enchanted Fur Brush of the Ecclesiastic*, granting her +2 Constitution.
- **[[044-book-1-chapter-44-accounts-tab]]**: Carl opens the *Accounts* tab, enabling him to track codex royalties for the Jug O' Boom.
- **[[046-book-1-chapter-46-menagerie-key]]**: Donut obtains a pet dinosaur, **[[mongo]]** (Mongoliensis), who bonds with her as a **Royal Steed** in [[047-book-1-chapter-47-royal-steed]], introducing companion management to her build. Both reach Level 13.
- **[[048-book-1-epilogue-epilogue]]**: Odette advises Carl to let Donut choose her class first so Carl can match hers, choose a pathfinder/mapping class, and look for a hidden class for Donut that will "make someone very angry." She reveals Mongo is a velociraptor.
