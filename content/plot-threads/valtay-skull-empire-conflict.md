# Valtay-Skull Empire Conflict

This plot thread tracks the intergalactic diplomatic and military conflict between the **Valtay Corporation** and the **Skull Empire**, sparked by actions related to [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]].

## Escalation on Floor 3
*   **The Metaphor**: During his interview on *Danger Zone* (Chapter 19, [[068-book-2-chapter-19-danger-zone]]), Carl delivers a speech using a "boiled frog" metaphor, openly encouraging the citizens of the Skull Empire to rebel against King Rust and his sons.
*   **The Assassination**: prince Stalwart of the Skull Empire retaliates by ordering a pulse weapon strike from his yacht in Earth orbit to execute Carl and Donut. However, because Carl swapped trailers with the pop singer [[manasa]] (a Valtay-controlled artist), the strike vaporizes Manasa instead.
*   **Retaliation**: The Valtay Corporation immediately destroys Prince Stalwart's yacht, killing Queen Consort Ugloo and the [[the-maestro|Maestro]].
*   **Political Fallout**: The conflict creates a major rift between the two former allies. The Saccathian Prism Kingdom condemns the Skull Empire's actions. The conflict is expected to distract the Valtay from their bankruptcy claims against the Borant Corporation, indirectly benefiting Borant.
*   **Deity Sponsorship**: Odette warns Carl that King Rust has spent a massive amount of money to sponsor a deity—likely the war god **Grull**—to target Carl on Floor 4 in revenge for his wife and son's deaths.
*   **Decoy Strategy & Planetary Failsafe**: In the Epilogue of Book 6 ([[296-book-6-epilogue-epilogue]]), Princess Formidable (daughter of King Rust) lobbies the Syndicate Council to trigger the planetary failsafe to blow up the star, which would vaporize the system and depose her father and brothers. The council mutes her. The Valtay representative reveals that they have bypassed the AI's quarantine using a loophole, preparing to deploy a private force of 150,000 mercenaries to Floor 9 to wipe out the crawlers.
