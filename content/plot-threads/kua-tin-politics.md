# Kua-tin Politics and the Bloom Party

This plot thread tracks the systemic corruption, financial desperation, and political rivalries of the Kua-tin administration (the Borant Corporation) and the ruling Bloom party.

## Current Status
- **Status**: `open`
- **Key Factions**: The Bloom Party (Kua-tin), Borant Corporation, Valtay Corporation (debt collectors), Skull Empire.

## Progression
- **[[031-book-1-chapter-31-follows-and-favorites]]**: The system announces the acceleration of the Floor 2 timer to six days and overhauls the patronage system to implement a bid-matching auction, front-loading credit acquisition.
- **[[036-book-1-chapter-36-meeting-zev]]**: [[mordecai]] reveals that the Kua-tin system's ruling party is **The Bloom** (an ultra-nationalist, fascist-like party). The Bloom has bankrupt the Kua-tin system. They are forcing the Borant Corporation to accelerate the dungeon timeline to cash out advertising, tourism, and sponsorship revenues early. Rules are being tweaked to kill crawlers faster.
- **[[044-book-1-chapter-44-accounts-tab]]**: [[zev]] reveals that Borant and the Bloom party are pleased with Carl and Donut because the viral "Pork Boy Snick" video humiliated their political rivals, the Skull Empire.
- **[[047-book-1-chapter-47-royal-steed]]**: Carl discovers illegal **Valtay Corporation** communications and combat technology hidden in [[agatha]]'s shopping cart, which causes a temporary feed outage.
- **[[048-book-1-epilogue-epilogue]]**: [[odette]] explains that the Valtay Corporation is a parasitic, technologically advanced corporate government. They have a fleet orbiting the Borant system ready to seize Borant's assets for debt collection. Borant forestalled the seizure by starting the Earth crawl early. The Valtay are scheduled to take over and run the next season of the crawl.
