# Survival of the Collapse

This plot thread tracks the overarching goal of [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] to survive the immediate aftermath of the Earth's collapse and successfully navigate the 18-Level World Dungeon.

## Current Status
- **Status**: `open`
- **Floor**: Floor 3
- **Remaining Crawlers**: 990,303 (as of the end of Floor 2)

## Progression
- **[[001-book-1-chapter-1-the-transformation]]**: Earth is collapsed. Carl and Donut survive because they are outdoors. The Syndicate AI announces the opening of the 18-Level World Dungeon.
- **[[002-book-1-chapter-2-welcome-to-first-floor]]**: Carl and Donut enter the dungeon. They receive multiple beginner achievements and basic loot boxes.
- **[[003-book-1-chapter-3-tutorial-guild]]**: Carl defeats his first goblins and accesses the safety of the tutorial guild.
- **[[009-book-1-chapter-9-mcdonalds-safe-room]]**: They discover a safe room designed like a Polish fast-food restaurant, establishing a temporary base of operations to sleep and prepare.
- **[[014-book-1-chapter-14-the-premiere]]**: The premiere of the show airs, and the staircases to Floor 2 begin to populate. Carl and Donut prepare to cover ground to locate a staircase before the floor collapses in 3.5 days.
- **[[016-book-1-chapter-16-goblin-chopper]]**: Carl and Donut acquire a steam-powered copper chopper to move rapidly between quadrants.
- **[[019-book-1-chapter-19-desperado-pass]]**: The global crawler count drops to 3,594,517. They rest in a safe room wait-station.
- **[[021-book-1-chapter-21-spiral-maze]]**: They find signs pointing to a stairwell to Floor 2, but the path is blocked by a giant rolling Borough Boss.
- **[[022-book-1-chapter-22-meadow-lark]]**: They meet the Meadow Lark caretakers and 38 elderly patients camped near the boss gate. Carl decides to help them fight the boss rather than look for another staircase.
- **[[026-book-1-chapter-26-ball-of-swine]]**: The party successfully defeats the Level 15 Borough Boss, popping the Ball of Swine and dropping the forcefield blocking the stairs.
- **[[027-book-1-chapter-27-second-floor]]**: The global crawler count is down to 2,552,085. Carl and Donut descend to Floor 2.
- **[[030-book-1-chapter-30-dingoes-and-grubs]]**: They arrive on Floor 2, and start grinding Danger Dingoes and Mind Horrors. They discover that they must destroy all dead monster corpses to prevent Brindle Grubs from pupating into more dangerous forms. They spot the Stairwell to Floor 3 in the northeast.
- **[[031-book-1-chapter-31-follows-and-favorites]]**: The Floor 1 collapse timer expires, and the crawler count drops to **1,292,526** as over 700,000 crawlers die. The Floor 2 timer is set to a short six-day limit, and bathroom offenses are penalized with a Level 93 Rage Elemental.
- **[[038-book-1-chapter-38-rage-elemental]]**: A crawler's bathroom violation summons the Level 93 Rage Elemental. It kills Yolanda Martinez and Randall, reducing the Meadow Lark survivors. Carl uses *Protective Shell* to survive a fatal claw attack.
- **[[039-book-1-chapter-39-platinum-scratchers]]**: The system announces a new rule: all safe rooms will close exactly one hour prior to floor collapse, preventing crawlers from hiding.
- **[[040-book-1-chapter-40-bomb-chicken]]**: Carl uses the "Bomb Chicken" launcher and oil to slide the Level 93 Rage Elemental down the stairs, exploiting the rule that descending monsters dissolve.
- **[[044-book-1-chapter-44-accounts-tab]]**: The crawler count drops below one million, hitting **990,303**.
- **[[045-book-1-chapter-45-frenzied-gerbil]]**: Carl and Donut defeat the Level 11 Neighborhood Boss, Ralph the Frenzied Gerbil, by using neutral Danger Dingoes and squishing it barefoot.
- **[[047-book-1-chapter-47-royal-steed]]**: Carl and Donut descend the stairs to the third floor with six hours remaining.
