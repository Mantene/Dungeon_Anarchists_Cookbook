# Donut vs Lucia Mar Feud

This plot thread tracks the media-driven conflict and impending confrontation between [[princess-donut]] and the front-runner crawler [[lucia-mar]] (along with her pets, [[cici]] and [[gustavo-3]]).

## Inception (Floor 3)
*   **Media Setup**: In Chapter 15 ([[064-book-2-chapter-15-desperado-club]]), Donut gets drunk on a Shirley Temple and mutters a few slurs about Rottweilers ("bitch-ass rottweilers"). The talk show media edit this into a dramatic challenge between Donut and Lucia Mar's Rottweilers.
*   **Lucia's Response**: In Chapter 21 ([[070-book-2-chapter-21-katia-grim]]), the news airs a segment featuring Lucia Mar and her dogs. Lucia, in her Lajabless form, rejects the television arena show but states she will find Donut in the dungeon and have Cici and Gus "tear this *puta* apart," promising to rip Carl to pieces and take his items.
*   **Leaderboard Context**: At the end of Floor 3, Lucia Mar is the #1 crawler on the leaderboard (Level 29), while Donut is #7 (Level 26) and Carl is #6 (Level 27). Mordecai expects that the system will push them to fight eventually, likely on or after Floor 6.
*   **Floor 8 Threat**: In Chapter 63 ([[286-book-6-chapter-63-personal-space-upgrade-and-doggie-door]]), Li Jun tells Carl that before Lucia Mar went down early to Floor 9, she declared she was saving something special for herself to settle the score with Donut. She plans to kill both Donut and Mongo during the Faction Wars.
