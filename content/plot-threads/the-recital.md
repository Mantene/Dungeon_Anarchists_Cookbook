# The Recital

**The Recital** is a local quest and narrative thread on the sixth floor (The Hunting Grounds) involving the dinosaur population and their attacks on [[point-mongo]].

## Goal & Mechanics
- **Trigger**: In [[156-book-5-chapter-11-the-recital]] (Chapter 11), Ursine Light Cleric [[tam]] complains about the growing dinosaur attacks on Point Mongo, triggering the quest.
- **Quest Parameters**: Exterminate the threat or find out what the dinosaurs want and give it to them.
- **Reward**: Gold Quest Box and a free town upgrade.

## Progression
- **[[156-book-5-chapter-11-the-recital]]** (Chapter 11): The quest is triggered. [[mongo]] is lured into the jungle by a dinosaur call, where Carl and Donut discover he has run off to mate with the pack leader [[kiwi]]. Kiwi and her pack become friendly to Donut.
- **[[173-book-5-chapter-28-big-tina]]** (Chapter 28): The quest is updated after Carl and Donut discover that the Ursine guards on the southern shore were slaughtered and eaten by a young, giant dinosaur named [[big-tina]] (who wears a pink feather boa). Kiwi's pack is searching for her to bring her back. Carl must decide whether to kill Big Tina or resolve her issues.
- **[[213-book-5-chapter-68-the-anaconda-recital]]**: Donut performs Nicki Minaj's "Anaconda" with backing tracks, and Mongo, Kiwi, and a pacified [[big-tina]] perform a choreographed dance. 
- **[[219-book-5-chapter-74-the-final-cage-match]]**: The recital successfully resolves the dinosaurs' needs, pacifies the Level 80 boss Big Tina, and completes the quest, awarding the crawlers their rewards.
