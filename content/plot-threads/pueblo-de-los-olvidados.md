# Pueblo de los Olvidados

**Pueblo de los Olvidados** is a major quest and storyline taking place in Havana, Cuba, on Floor 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth").

## Narrative Arc

### The Corrupted Settlement
Pueblo de los Olvidados is a cemetery settlement populated by Ghommids (other-realm spirit creatures). The presence of the Orisha deity [[asojano]] (the Lord of Smallpox), who visited the town and became trapped, corrupted the local veil. The rot turned the peaceful Ghommids (such as [[kipper]]) into ravenous, insane undead monsters. Crawlers who entered the settlement were locked in by a barrier, initiating a compulsory quest to find the source of the rot and cure the town to escape.

### The saferoom Intruder
During the quest, Asojano bypassed the dungeon's restrictions and entered the crawlers' saferoom (earning Carl the *Interloper* achievement). Asojano sat and interacted with Carl and Team Sister Ines while his health slowly drained.

### The Celestial Thorn Room Choice
In Chapter 15, the crawlers accessed the *Celestial Thorn Room* containing seven shrines and one *Sun of Reawakening*, forcing them to make a choice of which shrine to repair:
1. **Ogun**: Prevent a smite but receive no bonus.
2. **Legba**: 50% chance to avoid all future smites, Legba ascends.
3. **Shango**: Learn lightning spell, Shango ascends.
4. **Oshun**: Protected from next 5 fatal blows, Oshun ascends.
5. **Asojano**: Instantly win the quest and cure the town, Asojano does not ascend.
6. **Inle**: Gain healing immunity/constitution boost, Inle returns to Nothing.
7. **Yemaya**: Yemaya ascends, gifts a special card, and her friend Eileithyia grants a boon.

Following a secret warning from [[katia-grim]] that resurrecting Yemaya was crucial to saving both Katia and Donut from the *Crown of the Sepsis Whore*, Carl chose **Shrine Seven: Yemaya**.

### Climax and Resolution
Resurrecting Yemaya triggered a boss battle against Asojano, who broke the protection chamber and summoned waves of Ghommids to attack. Carl and his allies defeated Asojano, which cured the surviving Ghommids, completed the quest, and opened the exit to the settlement. However, this choice also angered the ascended god [[ogun]], who manifested physically in the region.
