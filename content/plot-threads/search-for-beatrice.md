# Search for Beatrice

This plot thread tracks [[princess-donut]]'s belief that her former owner, [[beatrice]], is alive in the dungeon and her quest to reunite with her.

## Current Status
- **Status**: `open`
- **Location of Beatrice**: Unknown (presumed deceased by [[carl]] and [[mordecai]])

## Progression
- **[[001-book-1-chapter-1-the-transformation]]**: Beatrice is in the Bahamas during the collapse. Carl breaks up with her beforehand.
- **[[008-book-1-chapter-8-bad-llama]]**: Donut mentions she was aware that Carl was looking at apartments that allowed cats before the collapse, meaning Carl secretly wanted to keep her.
- **[[012-book-1-chapter-12-kneepads-and-commentary]]**: Donut says that once they find Beatrice, Beatrice (who is her agent) will get her onto the televised specials. Donut expects to find Beatrice on the 5th or 6th floor, assuming the dungeon will shrink enough by then. Carl and Mordecai silently agree not to crush Donut's hopes, though they suspect Beatrice is dead.
- **[[019-book-1-chapter-19-desperado-pass]]**: Donut worries that Beatrice will be shocked and displeased by the ugly, blood-dripping dagger Desperado Pass tattoo branded onto Donut's shoulder blade.
- **[[024-book-1-chapter-24-the-juicer]]**: While acting out her dramatic near-death scene after the Juicer fight, Donut tells Carl that Beatrice will be very displeased with him if Donut dies.
- **[[028-book-1-chapter-28-studio-interview]]**: Before the *Dungeon Crawler After Hours* interview, Donut mentions Beatrice, reinforcing her belief that Beatrice is her professional agent and manager.
