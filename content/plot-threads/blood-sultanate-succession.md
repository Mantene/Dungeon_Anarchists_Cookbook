# Blood Sultanate Succession

This plot thread tracks [[princess-donut]]'s status as a royal member of the "Blood Sultanate," a faction on Floor 9 of the World Dungeon. This status imposes a mandatory condition for descent past Floor 9: she must slay the Sultan and all other members of the royal family.

## Current Status
- **Status**: `open`
- **Implication**: Donut cannot descend past Floor 9 until the Sultan and all other royal members of the Blood Sultanate are dead.

## Progression
- **[[007-book-1-chapter-7-princess-donut]]**: Donut receives and equips the *Crown of the Sepsis Whore*. A system warning informs her that placing the crown upon her head permanently enters her into the royal line of succession for the Blood Sultanate on Floor 9, requiring her to kill the Sultan and all other royal members to progress to Floor 10.
- **[[221-book-5-epilogue-welcome-to-the-great-race]]**: As [[eva-sigrid]] dies, she places the [[crown-of-the-sepsis-whore]] on [[katia-grim]]'s head. The crown melds into Katia's form, placing her also in the royal line of succession. This introduces a major restriction: only one of Donut or Katia will be allowed to use the stairs from Floor 9 to Floor 10.
