# Threat of Frank Q

This plot thread tracks the threat posed by [[frank-q]] and [[maggie-my]], a hostile player-killer duo murdering other crawlers.

## Current Status
- **Status**: `open`
- **Current Threat Level**: Extremely High (Frank and Maggie possess a Legendary tracking potion that allows them to pinpoint Carl and Donut anywhere).

## Progression
- **[[013-book-1-chapter-13-rebecca-w]]**: [[carl]] and [[princess-donut]] discover the naked, shot corpse of Rebecca W. The system tooltip identifies her killer as crawler Frank Q. Carl, angered by this act of crawler-on-crawler murder, vows to hunt Frank Q down, while Donut expresses fear and advises avoiding him.
- **[[020-book-1-chapter-20-naughty-list]]**: Carl confronts Frank Q in a safe room. Frank claims to be a federal agent who killed Rebecca in self-defense. Frank and his stealth partner, [[maggie-my]], ambush Carl and Donut, but because they fire weapons in a safe room, they are frozen. Carl and Donut escape, leaving a dynamite trap inside a dead rat's inventory.
- **[[043-book-1-chapter-43-pork-boy-incident]]**: During a live interview, the true backstory of the duo is exposed: they traveled with Maggie's teenage daughter, Yvette. Frank lost his hand and Yvette was severely injured when Frank looted Carl's dynamite trap. Maggie choked her own daughter Yvette to death to claim her experience points. [[the-maestro]] gives them a **Legendary Skill Potion** that upgrades Frank's *Find Crawler* skill to Level 15, allowing them to track Carl and Donut by name anywhere. Maggie vows to find Carl and Donut and watch them die.
- **[[044-book-1-chapter-44-accounts-tab]]**: Carl decides to actively target the local quadrant bosses to secure maps and prepare for Frank and Maggie's inevitable tracking hunt.
