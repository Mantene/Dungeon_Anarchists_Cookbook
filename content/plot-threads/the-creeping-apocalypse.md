# The Creeping Apocalypse

**Status**: `closed`

The Creeping Apocalypse was a world quest triggered by Miriam Dom's death, tasking crawlers and hunters with killing 50 Odious Creepers. The quest failed because two creepers north of Zockau were ignored, initiating the Gehenna Bramble collapse.
