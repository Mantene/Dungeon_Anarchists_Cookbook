# Escorting Meadow Lark Residents

This plot thread tracks the efforts of [[carl]], [[princess-donut]], and the Meadow Lark night staff to protect, escort, and successfully navigate elderly retirement home patients through the dungeon floors.

## Current Status
- **Status**: `resolved` (Floor 2 transit complete)
- **Remaining Survivors**: 36 residents (plus caretakers [[brandon-an]], [[chris-andrews-2]], and [[imani-c]], and eccentric crawler [[agatha]])
- **Casualties**: Nurse [[yolanda-martinez-13]], resident Randall, and resident Jack (killed on Floor 2).

## Progression
- **[[022-book-1-chapter-22-meadow-lark]]**: Carl and Donut meet the caretakers and residents camped out at the entrance of the spiral maze on Floor 1. Carl decides to help them fight the Borough Boss blocking the stairs. He proposes a plan to build a modular barricade (the "Speedbump") to trap the boss.
- **[[026-book-1-chapter-26-ball-of-swine]]**: The Meadow Lark caretakers and Carl successfully construct the Speedbump redoubt, wedging and popping the Level 15 Borough Boss (the Ball of Swine). The forcefield surrounding the Floor 2 stairs is dropped.
- **[[027-book-1-chapter-27-second-floor]]**: Carl and Donut decide to descend to Floor 2 first to scout the area and clear a safe path to a safe room for the elderly. They establish a group chat to coordinate. Agatha slips down the ramp ahead of them.
- **[[030-book-1-chapter-30-dingoes-and-grubs]]**: Carl and Donut locate a large safe room (a storm shelter) on Floor 2 and grind nearby quadrants to clear a path, preparing for the descent of the Meadow Lark residents.
- **[[031-book-1-chapter-31-follows-and-favorites]]**: The Floor 1 collapse reveals that 24 of the Meadow Lark residents chose to stay behind in the Waffle House safe room to die on their own terms, while 6 joined Brandon's group. Brandon's crew decides to drop the remaining residents to Floor 3 early to avoid the danger of Floor 2.
- **[[037-book-1-chapter-37-parade-float]]**: The caretakers construct a 10-car wheelchair-train platforms connected by *Yog's Special Chain* (300 feet, light and unbreakable) to pull the residents. Zev warns Carl that focus groups view the elderly as "dead weight." Jack steps off the train and urinates on the wall, violating the bathroom ban.
- **[[038-book-1-chapter-38-rage-elemental]]**: The violation summons a Level 93 Rage Elemental. Yolanda Martinez shoots Jack to stop him, but the monster coalesces, instantly disintegrating Yolanda and resident Randall. Carl uses *Protective Shell* to halt the monster's charge and rescues [[elle-mcgibbons]] from the ceiling under a *Reverse Gravity* spell. They escape into the Big Shot Chicken safe room.
- **[[041-book-1-chapter-41-goodbyes-at-stairs]]**: After Carl defeats the elemental, the remaining 36 residents and 3 caretakers (Brandon, Chris, Imani) descend the stairs to the third floor. Elle McGibbons mistakes Carl for Barry. Agatha pushes her cart down the stairs, resolving the Floor 2 escort mission.
