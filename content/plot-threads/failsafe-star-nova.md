# The Star Failsafe Trigger

The **Star Failsafe Trigger** is a narrative arc involving the illegal neural interface failsafe installed in Carl's brain by the Residual Goff, which has the power to destroy the system's central star.

## Narrative Arc

- **Installation**: In the Prologue of Book 7, the Residual [[paulie]] (Goff) utilizes a time-stop allowed by the system's AI to instruct Carl to drink a coffee containing a neural upgrade. This upgrade installs the failsafe trigger directly onto Carl's natural interface.
- **The Failsafe Mechanics**: The trigger bypasses standard AI containment. If five or six specific criteria are met ("tripping the fuse"), the central star of the system is sent into a nova reaction, destroying the system, the AI, and everyone within it.
- **The AI's Intent**: Goff reveals that the mantid-made AI is in severe pain, wants to die, and actively permitted the installation of the failsafe trigger.
- **The Compassion Clause**: Goff warns Carl that taking over hosts causes "cross-contamination." Because of his host Paulie's human compassion, Goff urges Carl to seek other avenues to disrupt the crawl rather than executing the nova, which would result in millions of innocent casualties.
