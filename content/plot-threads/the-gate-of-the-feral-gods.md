# The Gate of the Feral Gods

This plot thread tracks the discovery, assembly, and utilization of the **Gate of the Feral Gods**—a powerful artifact consisting of two *Mysterious Watches* and a *Winding Box*—which allows the crawlers to bypass the bubble constraints on Floor 5 (The Bubbles) and eventually flood Floor 9 (Larracos).

## Current Status
- **Status**: `resolved` (Floor 5 is completed, and the Gate is used to flood the Floor 9 faction market).

## Progression
- **[[125-book-4-chapter-15-death-trap-flight-to-the-suburbs]]**: The search for the three pieces of the watch-and-box artifact is initiated to find a way to escape or bypass bubble walls.
- **[[126-book-4-chapter-16-disposal-of-the-goose]]**: Carl retrieves the second piece (*Mysterious Watch*) from Kane's corpse. He learns Svern has the first piece and Ghazi the Mage has the third.
- **[[127-book-4-chapter-17-melodrama-and-merchandise]]**: Carl uses the *Mysterious Watch* to locate the principal Henrik, who is trapped in the flooded Necropolis.
- **[[130-book-4-chapter-20-the-lightning-portal]]**: The crawlers risk a dangerous route through a lightning portal to corner Ghazi the Mage and claim the final piece.
- **[[131-book-4-chapter-21-the-mad-dune-mages-waifu]]**: Carl defeats Ghazi and loots the *Mysterious Winding Box*.
- **[[132-book-4-chapter-22-flushed-into-the-ocean]]**: The winding box is secured, and Katia loots the instruction scroll detailing how to assemble and wind the watches to open the gate.
- **[[133-book-4-chapter-23-the-mechanics-of-the-gate]]**: Carl and the team study the gate instructions, learning that the gate can transport them to Floor 9 (Larracos) but requires a massive amount of energy and triggers a feral god summoning.
- **[[135-book-4-chapter-25-gelato-and-gills]]**: The team dives to the submarine *Akula* to disable its pump, attempting to drain the quadrant water and clear the path to the final watch piece.
- **[[136-book-4-chapter-26-mouthbrooding-mayhem]]**: Carl retrieves the final *Mysterious Watch* from Henrik's body on the bridge of the *Akula* just as the Level 82 City Boss, [[lusca]], swallows the sub.
- **[[137-book-4-chapter-27-confronting-lusca]]**: Cornered inside Lusca's mouth, Carl activates the gate. It teleports them to the dry land of Floor 5's lacuna, but summons the feral god [[emberus]], who immediately attacks Lusca.
- **[[138-book-4-chapter-28-extractions-and-sun-shrines]]**: The summoning of the feral fire god Emberus prompts intervention from other deities, cascading into faction conflict.
- **[[139-book-4-chapter-29-where-the-red-god-glows]]**: Clearing the Subterranean Quadrant pops Bubble 543, lifting bubble constraints and opening the way to the floor boss.
- **[[140-book-4-chapter-30-world-quest-party]]**: The crawlers manage the fallout of Emberus's presence while navigating the external lacuna.
- **[[141-book-4-chapter-31-a-deposit-on-quan]]**: Carl completes the celestial quest, rescuing the puppy [[orthrus]] and becoming a cleric-adherent of Emberus.
- **[[142-book-4-chapter-32-divine-intervention-benefits]]**: The crawlers turn the Gate into a repeatable extraction tool, extracting crawlers from other bubbles before they can be wiped out.
- **[[143-book-4-chapter-33-breaking-up-the-band]]**: The extractions are successfully completed, and the Gate is retrieved for the floor's endgame.
- **[[144-book-4-chapter-34-a-clash-of-demons]]**: Carl opens the Gate of the Feral Gods to Floor 9, flooding the Larracos faction market with the Water Quadrant's ocean, depth charges, and sharks. This completes Floor 5 and collapses the faction economy on Floor 9.
