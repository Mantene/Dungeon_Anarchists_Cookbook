# The Chowder War

**The Chowder War** is a regional conflict and associated quest introduced on Floor 8 ("The Ghosts of Earth") in Havana, Cuba.

## Context and Factions

The conflict is waged between two local folklore factions:
1. **The Monk Seals**: Level 70 aquatic melee tank sentries (such as [[yago]] and [[geraldo]]) trained in Caribbean Kung Fu. They view the ocean as sacred.
2. **The Red *Maníseros* Land Crabs**: A faction of forest-dwelling crabs who master the deadly martial art *Juego de maní*.

### The Cause of Conflict
Every year, the land crabs must migrate to the ocean to mate and reproduce. The Monk Seals consider this biological event a sacrilegious defilement of their holy waters, as the massive volume of crab reproductive fluids ("splooge") kills local fish, depletes resources, and suffocates young seals. Conversely, the land crabs fight out of evolutionary necessity, using *Juego de maní* to protect themselves from the aggressive kung-fu seals as they attempt to reach the water.
