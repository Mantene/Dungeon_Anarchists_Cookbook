# Find Out Who Killed My Son

**Find Out Who Killed My Son** is a quest unlocked by [[carl]] at the conclusion of Floor 6 (The Hunting Grounds).

## Quest Parameters
- **Objective**: Retrieve the item memorial-crystal-apito and speak to the high priest of Emberus at the Emberus Shrine in Club Vanquisher to investigate the murder of his son, Geyrun.
- **Status**: `open`

## Progression
- **[[220-book-5-chapter-75-co-warlord-of-the-princess-posse]]**: The quest is added to Carl's logs after the boss battle during the Butcher's Masquerade.
- **[[261-book-6-chapter-38-monroe-county-and-fathers-death]]**: Carl receives an update from Emberus instructing him to locate a temple of Nekhebit (who holds a grudge against Apito) and question the priestess.
