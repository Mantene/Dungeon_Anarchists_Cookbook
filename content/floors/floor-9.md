# Floor 9 — Faction Wars (Larracos, City of Dreams)

## Overview

- **Floor number:** 9
- **Environment:** The faction-war city of **Larracos** ("City of Dreams") and the surrounding settlement of Shanty Town. The same city Carl flooded with sharks from [Floor 5](floor-5.md).
- **Timer:** 30 days (or 90 hours after a winner is declared, whichever comes first).
- **Population:** 32,429 crawlers at entry, plus ~150,000 enemy mercenaries and 50,000 former crawlers (Homecoming Queen).
- **When entered:** Book 7, Ch. 1. Carl enters at #1 (6,000,000 gold bounty).

A full-scale war floor. The **Princess Posse** (co-warlorded by Carl and Donut) must outlast nine opposing faction teams. Resolves the Crown of the Sepsis Whore plot and breaks open **the Nothing**.

## Rules & Mechanics

- **Four phases:**
  1. **The Ceasefire** (60 hrs) — build army, recruit, prepare defenses.
  2. **The Ramp-Up** (180 hrs) — no offensive magic; enter enemy territory only via designated kilometer-wide corridors; brutal artillery/hand-to-hand combat.
  3. **Open Hostilities** — full magic; "Operation Ruin" begins.
  4. **The Peeling of Larracos** — triggered at 4 remaining teams; NPCs win by holding the castle a week, attackers by holding the throne room 6 hours.
- **Stairwells** appear only after Faction Wars ends or 30 hours before collapse.
- **Removed safety protections:** Faction sponsors can now be killed (the rule passed on [Floor 8](floor-8.md)).
- **The Containment Interface:** A secret menu installed in Carl's brain by Paulie, granting access to the AI's failsafe trigger. Carl secretly used it to **deactivate** the failsafe before the climax.

## Mob Roster

- **Zarogoths** — shadow monsters generated by the War Mage Rebellion's soul-crystal generator.
- **Gondii worm mercenaries** — Valtay worm-types that occupy dead soldiers' bodies as spies.
- **Rot Sticker Behemoths** — weaponized pets deployed by the Reavers as biological seeking missiles.
- **The nine faction armies** — Bone Clan (orcs), Operatic Collective, Blood Sultanate (nagas), Prism Kingdom, Dream, Madness, Reavers, plus the eliminated Sortion teams.

## Key Events

- **The 50,000 former crawlers arrive:** The Homecoming Queen deploys its army, led by cookbook authors [Tipid](../characters/tipid.md), [Rosetta](../characters/rosetta.md), and [Justice Light](../characters/justice-light.md).
- **Samantha's sacrifice:** [Samantha](../characters/samantha.md) gives up her only chance at a body (the Princess Lunette flesh golem) to save Louis's life after Tagg threatens his heart. Louis now has gills.
- **Katia's choice:** With 65 minutes left, [Katia](../characters/katia-grim.md) eats the Orchid of Eileithyia's Grace and chooses **pregnancy** — ejecting her from the dungeon to Earth's surface and finally freeing her from the Crown. Bautista pre-built her cybernetic limbs.
- **Zerzura:** The Sledge teleports all of Larracos + Shanty Town (and its NPCs) to the **12th floor**.
- **Princess Posse wins:** Team Retribution forfeits when [Juice Box](../characters/juice-box.md) and Ferdinand jump into the Nothing per Justice Light's plan.
- **Justice Light breaks the Nothing:** His real trap (not the throne-room decoy) drops a bijou-fairy trap and a demon trap into a portal to the Nothing — folding four planes together. **"The Nothing has been broken."** It drains into the Halls of the Ascendency, Sheol, and Scolopendra's Lair. **Scolopendra awakens.**
