# Floor 2 — The Cinderblock Warren

## Overview

- **Floor number:** 2
- **Environment:** Cinderblock walls coated in orange lichen. A waste-disposal ecosystem of grubs, hornets, and unvaccinated clurichauns.
- **Timer:** 6 days (shortened from the expected 10)
- **When entered:** Book 1, Ch. 30. Floor collapse aligns with the Epilogue.

The second tutorial-tier floor, where Carl and [Princess Donut](../characters/princess-donut.md) refine their combat synergy and survive the Rage Elemental incident. Views explode after the [Odette](../characters/odette.md) interview (212 billion views by floor's end).

## Rules & Mechanics

- **Rage Elemental punishment:** Urinating in the dungeon summons a Level 93 Rage Elemental — the floor's deadliest hazard (see Key Events). Removed from Floor 3 onward.
- **Alchemy exploit:** The alchemy table is indestructible and can be used as a mobile shield — Carl exploits this against the Rage Elemental.
- **Pet Reward Room:** Donut earns a pet choice; selects a Mongoliensis (baby velociraptor), [Mongo](../characters/mongo.md).
- Boss tiers, safe rooms, and loot mechanics carry over from **Floor 1**.

## Mob Roster

- **Brindle Grubs → Cow-Tailed Grubs → Pupae → Brindled Vespas** — the floor's waste-disposal lifecycle; pupae hatch into acid-spitting hornets.
- **Unvaccinated Clurichauns** (Level 3) — an MLM moonshine salesforce that inflicts "The Taint" debuff (blocks healing).
- **Laminak Fairies** (Level 6) — upper management of the clurichaun enterprise; key alchemy ingredient for the Rev-Up Immunity Smoothie.
- **Danger Dingoes** (Level 5) + **Kobolds** — dog-and-rider mobs.
- **Mind Horrors** (Level 4) — floating brains with psionic attacks; physically weak.

## Key Events

- **The Rage Elemental incident (Ch. 38–40):** Resident Jack urinates in the dungeon, summoning a Level 93 Rage Elemental that kills **Yolanda Martinez** and Randall and traps the group in a safe room. Carl builds the "Bomb Chicken" (a bicycle-trailer bomb launcher) and ultimately disposes of the elemental down the stairwell.
- **Zev becomes liaison (Ch. 35–36):** Borant PR agent [Zev](../characters/zev.md) is assigned to Carl and Donut. Mordecai whispers key intel: **The Bloom** (ultra-nationalist kua-tin party) controls the kua-tin government, and Borant is bankrupt — the Party is rushing the dungeon to collect its payout.
- **The Maestro feud (Ch. 42–43):** On a Skull Empire show, Carl antagonizes Crown Prince "The Maestro" live on galactic TV; a viral "Pork Boy Snick" deepfake humiliates the Maestro.
- **Agatha's mystery (Ch. 47):** [Agatha](../characters/agatha.md) reappears still Level 4 despite surviving alone for days, carrying an unfamiliar potion. She gives Carl a warning look and descends to Floor 3.
- **Mongo joins:** Donut's pet velociraptor [Mongo](../characters/mongo.md) bonds with the party (Royal Steed) in the floor's final hours.

## Survivors / Party State
