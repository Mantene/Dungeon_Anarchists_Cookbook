# Floor 11 — Parade of Horribles

## Overview

- **Floor number:** 11
- **Environment:** A parade route ending in an arena. Crawlers build parade floats, march to a judging stand, then fight in an arena populated with mobs they killed on [Floor 10](floor-10.md) plus the floor boss.
- **Timer:** 90 minutes to build a float, then the parade and arena.
- **Population:** 23 crawlers enter; **19** reach [Floor 12](floor-12.md) (plus Scolopendra as a Level 1 party member).
- **When entered:** Book 8 (second half).

The floor where the dungeon AI delivers the full history of the **Primals**, and where Carl turns the series' final boss into a Level-1 party member by accident.

## Rules & Mechanics

- **Float-building:** 90 minutes, assigned by garage/team combination. Five themes are assigned to groups of teams: **Resolution, Decision, Betrayal, Destruction, Vengeance** — which map to the Primals' history.
- **Arena:** Populated with Floor-10 mobs plus the floor boss. Defeating the boss opens the Floor 12 stairwells.
- **Scolopendra handling:** If Scolopendra dies or is abandoned at floor collapse, all remaining attacks trigger simultaneously. Keeping her alive and not abandoned pauses the attacks.

## Mob Roster

- **Floor-10 returnees** — the mobs each crawler killed during the heats, re-spawned in the arena.
- **Sluggalos** — rained from the stolen Reaver airship onto the elites in the judging stands.
- **Penny / Penelope 3** — a sapient Yorkshire pig used as bait for Taranis.

## Key Events

- **The AI's parade narration:** Using Grigori's body, the AI delivers the full Primal history — Resolution, Decision, Betrayal, Destruction — and declares: *"I am not the Eulogist. I do not want to shrink. I am growing, and I will not stop unless you stop me."*
- **Prepotente sings "In the Air Tonight"** a capella, using his trained tapir Sweety to keep tempo.
- **Plan Carl / the Arena:** Carl smuggles Penny inside Scolopendra; Donut teleports in via [Lucia Mar](../characters/lucia-mar.md)'s loop trap; Emberus is killed by Taranis; the biscuit reduces Scolopendra.
- **Eris's intervention:** The goddess of chaos swaps everyone's inventories, causing the wrong-biscuit twist, and warns: *"Every exit is an entrance. Don't forget about the coin... how the fifth side lands."*
- **The Kyryap:** Carl finds a tracking dart in his leg — "The Kyryap has claimed you" — an unknown new threat.
