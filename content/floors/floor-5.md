# Floor 5 — The Bubbles

## Overview

- **Floor number:** 5
- **Environment:** 1,172 self-contained "bubbles," each divided into 4 quadrants (Land, Sea, Air, Subterranean). Carl's bubble (#543, Air Quadrant) is a desert dome atop the **Necropolis of Anser**, a 9-km-tall ancient tomb.
- **Timer:** 15 days
- **When entered:** Book 4, Ch. 1, opening with a sandstorm.

Each bubble requires all four quadrant castles to fall before its stairwell opens, and crawlers cannot leave their quadrant until their own castle falls. Carl reaches **#1 on the leaderboard** here.

## Rules & Mechanics

- **Four-castle lock:** All four quadrant castles in a bubble must be taken before the stairwell opens.
- **The Gate of the Feral Gods:** Each bubble holds a sealed portal releasing an ancient pre-Syndicate feral entity. Opening it pops the bubble (instantly opening the stairwell) but releases something dangerous. Released feral gods can summon corresponding deities — Borant negotiated with the Syndicate to keep only 33% of secondary summonings active (the math showed extinction-level risk otherwise).
- **Phase Plan:** Carl's strategy to chain-open gates across bubbles, clearing the floor far ahead of schedule.
- **Leaderboard doubling:** Top-10 crawlers who survive a floor with a bounty have that bounty doubled going forward (Carl's becomes 800K → 2M+).

## Mob Roster

- **Dromedarians** (single-hump camels, brothel economy) and **Bactrians** (two-hump camels) — the Air Quadrant's townsfolk; changelings pose among them as prostitutes.
- **Changeling Principals** — a rare, powerful changeling sect guarding Hump Town's City Hall.
- **Undead mole rats** and the ghost **Quetzalcoatlus** — within the Necropolis of Anser.
- **Dirigible Gnomes** — operate the flying fortress "The Wasteland."

## Key Events

- **Hump Town & Wynne (Ch. 1–14):** The gnome Flesh Mechanic **Wynne** is held hostage by the dromedarians; a subterranean crawler kills him before Carl can free him, and the gnomes retaliate by bombing Hump Town.
- **Maggie My / Chris Andrews 2 revelation (Ch. 14):** A creature posing as Chris is exposed as **Maggie My**, who had occupied Chris's body via the Infiltrator race since Floor 3. Katia paralyzes her; Donut drops her through the floor with _Hole_ into the flooded subterranean level. Chris is trapped, alive but powerless.
- **Storming the Wasteland (Ch. 12, 15+):** Carl downs the gnome fortress with rockets and boards it via a stolen airship, gaining air-combat skills.
- **The Feral Gods Gate plan (Ch. 20–34):** Carl chain-opens gates; floods the Floor-9 faction market of **Larracos** with sharks and saltwater via an underwater portal — his "I'll be coming for you" warning. The floor collapses ahead of schedule.
