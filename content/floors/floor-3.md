# Floor 3 — The Over City

## Overview

- **Floor number:** 3
- **Environment:** A fake volcanic setting with a day/night cycle. Medieval-style villages guarded by Level-75 swordsmen (active only by day); open ruins outside the villages hold more dangerous nocturnal mobs.
- **Timer:** 8 days (shortened from the expected 10)
- **Size:** ~300,000 sq km (Arizona-sized)
- **When entered:** Book 2, Ch. 1. Carl and [Princess Donut](../characters/princess-donut.md) choose race and class here.

The floor where the full game opens up: race/class selection, the quest system, elites, the **Desperado Club**, and the leaderboard's first appearance.

## Rules & Mechanics

- **Race/Class Selection:** Occurs here. Race first, then class. Donut picks Cat → **Former Child Actor** (the Manager Benefit permanently binds [Mordecai](../characters/mordecai.md)). Carl picks **Primal** (extinct cosmic progenitor race) → **Compensated Anarchist** (requires 500B+ views + Explosives Handling L5).
- **Day/Night Cycle:** Villages are safe by day; guards abandon posts at night; alleys become deadly.
- **Quest system:** Silver / Gold / Platinum tiers; "Hadji" achievement upgrades the quest box. Elaborate quests signal a shortened floor timer.
- **Elites:** Powerful unique NPCs embedded in producer-written storylines with early "plot armor." Rule of thumb: don't murder-hobo an elite in Act 1.
- **Dungeon Locator Signs:** Hidden at intersections; reveal exits/shops/bosses. Require Carl's **Escape Plan** skill to read.
- **Production trailers & assassination risk:** Public booking schedules mean no privacy — leads to the trailer-swap that kills the pop star Manasa.
- **Precursor Burst:** A pre-explosion magical discharge that disables all equipped magical items in an area (items are safe in inventory). Central to the Doomsday Scenario's creation.

## Mob Roster

- **Level-75 Swordsman Guards** — village daytime defenders; disabled by precursor bursts.
- **Circus lemurs** — killed several crawlers (Grace, Nica, Lea), whose gear Carl loots.
- **Street Urchins** — nighttime alley mobs; Mongo defends a sleeping Donut from them.
- **Krasue** — floating undead ghost-head monsters; killed GumGum's mother.
- **City elves of the 201st Security Group** — a delusional militia treating skyfowl as angels.

## Key Events

- **Race/Class selection (Ch. 1–2):** Donut tricks Mordecai and binds him as her permanent manager; Carl becomes a Primal/Compensated Anarchist.
- **The Grimaldi's Circus quest (Ch. 3–12):** [Signet](../characters/signet.md), a Level 60 Half-Naiad/High-Elf summoner, kidnaps Carl to free her transformed father. Resolved via reconciliation; Carl jumps from Level 13 to 18. Begins the *Vengeance of the Daughter* storyline.
- **The Desperado Club (Ch. 12–13):** Carl visits the members' club via Mordecai and wins a non-transferable 100,000 Gold Casino Chip.
- **Danger Zone assassination attempt (Ch. 19):** Crown Prince Stalwart fires a pulse at Carl's trailer; a last-minute trailer swap kills the pop star Manasa instead, shattering the Valtay/Skull Empire alliance.
- **Carl's Doomsday Scenario created (Ch. 13–25):** The serial-killer/magistrate quest ends with Carl caging Remex's god-tier soul explosion in his inventory. [Katia Grim](../characters/katia-grim.md) joins the party during the escape (her **Rush** ability smashes through a wall to clear an exit).
