# Floor 12 — The Court of the Ascendency

> [!note] Current frontier of the series.
> As of the end of Book 8, Floor 12 has only just been entered. This page covers what is known at entry; it will expand if the series continues.

## Overview

- **Floor number:** 12
- **Environment:** The **Court of the Ascendency** (the "Halls of the Ascendency"). The entire city of Larracos + Shanty Town and its NPC population were teleported here via Zerzura at the end of [Floor 9](floor-9.md).
- **Timer:** Suspended. Level timers on all remaining floors are paused until the Ascendency winner is declared.
- **Population:** **19 crawlers** enter (plus Scolopendra as a Level 1 party member).
- **When entered:** End of Book 8. The last floor where deals will be offered.

## Rules & Mechanics

- **The Ascendency game:** The AI has framed Floor 12 onward as a contest — whoever wins the throne battles "decides the fate of the galaxy" and becomes "a true god."
- **Timers suspended:** No floor-collapse pressure until an Ascendency winner emerges.
- **Last deal floor:** Floor 12 is the final floor on which crawlers may take exit deals.
- **Scolopendra constraint:** Scolopendra (now a Level 1 party member) cannot die or be abandoned without triggering the dungeon's remaining attacks — a constant party liability and leverage item.
- **The Nothing's drainage:** The Nothing (broken on Floor 9) drains into the Halls of the Ascendency, meaning Floor 12 is directly affected by that catastrophe.
