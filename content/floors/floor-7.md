# Floor 7 — The Great Race (Destroyed)

> [!warning] This floor was never crawled.
> Floor 7 was annihilated by [Prepotente](../characters/prepotente.md) at the end of Book 5, before any crawler ran it. All crawlers were dumped directly from [Floor 6](floor-6.md) onto [Floor 8](floor-8.md).

## Overview

- **Floor number:** 7
- **Theme:** "The Great Race" — a glass-maze racing floor.
- **Timer:** N/A — destroyed before it opened.
- **When entered:** Never. Bypassed entirely at the end of Book 5.

Floor 7 is the dungeon's great anomaly: a floor that was scheduled but never actually run. Its destruction is one of the most consequential single acts in the series, costing Borant an entire floor's worth of broadcast revenue and demonstrating that a sufficiently clever crawler can break the dungeon's structure outright.

## Rules & Mechanics

- Intended to be a glass-maze "Great Race." The full ruleset never activated because the floor was destroyed pre-launch.
- **Bypass mechanic:** When Floor 7 was broken, the dungeon routed all surviving crawlers from Floor 6 straight to Floor 8.

## Mob Roster

- None encountered — the floor was never populated by crawlers.

## Bosses

- None fought. The "Great Race" boss/finish-line structure was destroyed before any heat began.

## Key Events

- **Prepotente breaks Floor 7 (end of Book 5):** [Prepotente](../characters/prepotente.md) cracked the entire Floor 7 glass maze using three items in combination:
  1. His dead mother Miriam's **Boots of Kiznet's Final Flight** (which contained the tale of this floor)
  2. A potion from their shared sponsors
  3. The **Enchanted Stick of Cascadia's Screams**
- The announcement system ("Cascadia") screamed, and all crawlers were dumped directly onto [Floor 8](floor-8.md). **Floor 7 never happened.**
- Confirmed again in Book 6: "Floor 7 never happened (Prepotente destroyed it at the end of Book 5)."

## Survivors / Party State

- Not applicable — no party state for a floor that was never crawled. All Floor 6 survivors proceeded straight to Floor 8.
