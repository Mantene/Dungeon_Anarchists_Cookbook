# Floor 8 — The Ghosts of Earth

## Overview

- **Floor number:** 8
- **Environment:** An Earth memory simulation (dated December 10). Carl and [Princess Donut](../characters/princess-donut.md) start in **Havana, Cuba** (Donut chose the wrong Caribbean island); Phase 2 takes place in the **Florida Keys**.
- **Timer:** 21 days total, split into three phases.
- **When entered:** Book 6, Ch. 1. (Reached directly from [Floor 6](floor-6.md) — [Floor 7](floor-7.md) was destroyed.)

The floor that introduces the **T'Ghee card battle system** and the **Faction Wars** rule changes that reshape [Floor 9](floor-9.md). Carl acquires the permanent **Eye of the Bedlam Bride** tattoo.

## Rules & Mechanics

- **Three-phase structure:**
  1. **Phase 1** (14 days): Explore the starting region and build a 6-totem T'Ghee card squad.
  2. **Phase 2** (48 hours, Dec 24–26): Travel to a new location and defeat 10 card-game opponents to collect tokens and obtain an exit key.
  3. **Phase 3** (remaining time): Return to the exit stairwell; keyless squads block keyholders' paths.
- **T'Ghee Card System:** 5 card types (Totem, Utility, Snare, Mystic, Special). A Squad Leader controls the deck; bodyguards can't cast during card battles. Rarities run Common → Mythic → Unique. A squad carries up to 6 active totems (8 with champion backups); one card may be kept when descending.
- **Crawlers as cards:** A dying crawler can be turned into a T'Ghee card by another crawler (as [Sister Ines](../characters/sister-ines.md) did to [Paz Lo](../characters/paz-lo.md)). Cards can be ripped to free them.
- **Demon Eviction:** When too many souls die in a region, Sheol demons are ejected upward to possess zombie shells; teleporting a possessed shell frees the demon at full power.
- **Primal Engine / AI emergence:** The dungeon AI "goes primal," seizing control from the showrunners and creating its own bosses.

## Mob Roster

- **Card totems** — summonable monsters (Raul the land crab, Jola the Yule Cat, Geraldo the seal, HeyZoos/Uzi Jesus, etc.).
- **Krasue, Ghommids, and Orisha-corrupted residents** of the Colón Cemetery.
- **Monk Seals & land crabs** — combatants in Sister Ines's "Chowder War."
- **Evicted Sheol demons** — released in the demon-eviction event.

## Key Events

- **The Thorn Room (Ch. 15–16):** Carl repairs Yemaya's shrine to give [Katia](../characters/katia-grim.md) a path out of the Crown obligation; the god Ogun kills Anton and dooms [Paz Lo](../characters/paz-lo.md) for failing to repair his shrine.
- **The Keymaster — Carl's family (Ch. 38–51):** The Phase-2 final boss is a hydra of people from Carl's life, revealing his half-brother **Asher** and that Beatrice hid knowledge of Asher from Carl.
- **The Residuals (Ch. 50–51):** Carl meets **Paulie/Goff**, a Residual alien, at a Homestead, Florida shelter — nearly learning how to shut off the dungeon before Orren pulls him out.
- **Faction Wars rules rewritten (Ch. 72):** A 30-minute audience vote passes **Remove Safety Protections for Faction Sponsors (98%)** and creates the **NPC "Home Team" (52%)**, co-warlorded by [Juice Box](../characters/juice-box.md) and Ferdinand.
- **The Eye of the Bedlam Bride:** Shi Maria briefly seizes Carl's body and tattoos herself onto his chest using toraline-root ink + Li Jun's eye blood.
- **The Homecoming Queen revealed:** [Tipid](../characters/tipid.md) and [Rosetta](../characters/rosetta.md) are hiding on a converted freighter with **50,000 former crawlers**, approved to enter Floor 9 as Princess Posse mercenaries.
- **Agatha's reveal (Epilogue):** [Agatha](../characters/agatha.md) is an ancient Residual alien (Gondii) from the faction opposing the Apothecary.
