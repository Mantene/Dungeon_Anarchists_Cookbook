# Floor 4 — The Iron Tangle

## Overview

- **Floor number:** 4
- **Environment:** A massive subway/train network with hundreds of colored lines themed after real-world metros (Moscow, Tokyo, etc.). The whole floor is a miniaturized map of the galaxy's wormhole network.
- **Timer:** 10 days
- **When entered:** Book 3, Ch. 1. Carl, [Princess Donut](../characters/princess-donut.md), [Katia Grim](../characters/katia-grim.md), and [Mongo](../characters/mongo.md) materialize in a moving Moscow subway car.

A deliberately confusing puzzle-floor (the author included an endgame map). Carl receives the legendary **Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook**, and uses Carl's Doomsday Scenario to kill a Level 90 city boss.

## Rules & Mechanics

- **One-way lines / restricted descent:** Trains run one-way per line. Stairwells exist only at stations numbered 12, 24, 36, 48, 72, and 433, and open only 6 hours before collapse. Crawlers start above station 80 and cannot descend early.
- **Monster sedation loop:** Mobs are addicted to race-specific "Rev-Up Vitamin Shots" supplied by Krakaren bosses at every 5th station, forcing them to loop between stops. Without their fix, they transform into something worse.
- **Transfer stations:** All prime-numbered stations. The Desperado Club appears at stations ending in 1; Club Vanquisher at stations ending in 9.
- **Character Actor carry-over rule:** If Donut levels a class-granted skill at least once before losing the class at the next floor, she keeps the player-earned levels.
- **Equippable mass for Doppelgangers:** [Katia](../characters/katia-grim.md) can load 15+ tons of transformation mass into a worn backpack to access giant battle-forms (her "Ugly-Ass Backpack" cowcatcher rig).
- **Sheol Glass Reaper Cases:** Containers for explosives too unstable to store — the same type holding Carl's Doomsday Scenario.

## Mob Roster

- **Krakaren Juvenile Clonelings** — thousands flood the floor when the Krakaren collective mind breaks free of Borant control, triggering self-destruct.
- **Ghoul generators** — station-based spawners blown up by Carl's train bombs.
- **Train mobs** — sedated commuter monsters looping the lines.

## Key Events

- **The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook (Ch. 2–8):** Carl's Platinum fan box is the 24th edition of the cookbook — a unique legendary item with hidden text only his Escape Plan skill can read, invisible to showrunners, passed between seasons.
- **The Prize Carousel incident (Ch. 7–8):** Mordecai throws a chair at the host **Chaco** and earns a 7-day safe-room timeout, sidelining him for most of the floor. Chaco's past wrong against Mordecai (tied to [Odette](../characters/odette.md)) is hinted at.
- **Hekla's betrayal and death (Ch. 22–25):** [Hekla](../characters/hekla.md) shoots invisible crossbow bolts into Katia; Carl catches her; Katia's Rush ability accidentally decapitates Hekla. Katia inherits Hekla's legendary repeating crossbow and jumps to Level 37–41.
- **The Krakaren endgame (Ch. 26–34):** Carl organizes a mass rescue using rapid-response carts, portal-key souvenir hats, and a hoist at station 36.
- **Prince Maestro lives (Ch. 33):** Thought dead since Book 2, **Prince Maestro** reappears as War God Grull's sponsor/commentator during the Mimic Rex fight.
