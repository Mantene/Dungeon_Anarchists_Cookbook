# Floor 10 — Don't Come in Last

## Overview

- **Floor number:** 10
- **Environment:** A death-race floor of seven heats, each on a different track (the Caves of the Screeching Death Manatees, acid-rain stretches, jungle trails, a factory, etc.).
- **Timer:** Suspended for Floor 10 (unique). A "Blood Bar" caps safe-room time at 10 hours.
- **Population:** 5,501 crawlers enter; 203 reach [Floor 11](floor-11.md).
- **When entered:** Book 8, Ch. 1. Carl is #3 (3.2M bounty); Donut is **#1, Level 135**.

The first of the two floors covered in Book 8. Crawlers race assigned vehicles through seven heats; coming in last removes a vehicle's "survival containment."

## Rules & Mechanics

- **Racing rules:** Teams of up to 4 (or solo), 9 teams per heat. Come in last → vehicle containment removed → death.
- **Vehicle assignment:** Determined by spinning a wheel — biological or mechanical. Carl and Donut get the **Big Shot Chicken** food truck (a 1920s gangster-themed truck with a roof tommy gun).
- **Mechanic NPC:** Must stay alive for between-heat repairs and cannot enter a safe room (Carl and Donut's is **Hedy** the gremlin).
- **Crawler-vs-crawler escalation:** Killing more than one opponent team per heat risks future heats pitting crawlers against each other.
- **Upgrade system:** Regular upgrades after each heat; Golden upgrades for first place (and for everyone after Heat 4); audience-voted upgrade for the last-place team.
- **The Pineapple Cabaret:** A secret, unofficial exit via the dungeon's cleaner-bot infrastructure. Not guaranteed safe — Louis ends up in Sheol instead.

## Mob Roster

- **Screeching Death Manatees** — Heat 1 cave hazard.
- **Opponent racing teams** — bugbears (Team Free Love: Jasha and Radoslav), "One Fine Pig" (armored vehicle), Dwight the cheating unicorn, etc.
- **Floor-10 mobs** — later repopulated into the Floor 11 arena.

## Key Events

- **Princess Chandra's schemes:** Carl's "wife" via naga Widow's Rights — **Princess Chandra, Esquire** — files to place Donut under conservatorship and seize their earnings; Donut intimidates her into fleeing.
- **Heat 5 — Minus:** A Syndicate security assassin, **Minus**, infiltrates the dungeon under his dead brother's identity to kill Carl and/or Donut.
- **The Pineapple Cabaret escape:** Li Na leads hundreds of crawlers (including Bautista) out via the cleaner-bot exit.
- **Heat 6:** Dong Quixote and Hedy die in the ice-cream-truck crash; Carl and Donut come in last because Dwight the unicorn cheated — surviving on the audience vote.
- **Chalchiuhtlicue ("Chachi"):** Samantha reveals the water goddess is inside [Lucia Mar](../characters/lucia-mar.md)'s head along with "the children."
