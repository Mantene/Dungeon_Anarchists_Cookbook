# Floor 1 — Underground Seattle

## Overview

- **Floor number:** 1 (the first dungeon floor; the World Dungeon has 18 levels)
- **Environment:** Underground stone-walled corridors beneath the ruins of Seattle. Dark halls, goblin warrens, llama territory, garbage-strewn neighborhoods.
- **Timer:** 5 days
- **Size / entrances:** ~150,000 dungeon entrances at the start
- **When entered:** Book 1, Ch. 1–2, immediately after **the Transformation**. Carl and [Princess Donut](../characters/princess-donut.md) enter into a dark hall.

The introductory floor. Carl kills his first goblin mobs bare-handed and reaches the tutorial guild, where [Mordecai](../characters/mordecai.md) explains the game's core systems.

## Rules & Mechanics

- **Tutorial Guild:** A safe instructional space staffed by Mordecai (then a Rat Hooligan guildmaster). Explains stats, skills, inventory, the rating system, and loot boxes.
- **Loot boxes:** NOT random — influenced by viewer engagement and sponsor interest. Tiered by rarity (Common → Legendary). Carl's starting boxes yield the Nightgaunt Cloak, Trollskin Shirt, Toe Ring of the Splatter Skunk, and a Goblin Pass tattoo.
- **Punishment system:** Urinating in the dungeon summons a Level 93 **Rage Elemental** (removed Floor 3+). This triggers on Floor 2 but the rule is established here.
- **Boss tiers:** Neighborhood → Borough → City → Province → Country → Floor. Each tier drops one tier higher of loot box; persistent loot (maps, field guides) drops for all crawlers present at the kill.
- **Safe rooms:** No combat allowed. Bopca Protector NPCs serve food and rent rooms. Doors seal 1 hour before floor collapse.
- **Desperado Pass tattoo:** Granted for killing 20+ non-combatants in a single attack (Carl and Donut earned theirs killing goblin babies). Grants access to the **Desperado Club**.
- **Goblin Pass tattoo:** Free passage through goblin territory; +20% damage taken from fairy-class mobs.

See [Game Systems](../mechanics/game-systems.md) for full stat/skill/loot rules.

## Mob Roster

- **Goblins** — basic melee mobs. Shamankas **Rory and Lorelai** were charmed by Donut and traded the Goblin Copper Chopper (steam bike with sidecar) for meth.
- **Hobgoblins** — source of hobgoblin pus (a key bomb ingredient) and dynamite.
- **Tusklings** — knight-class boars; 30+ formed the Ball of Swine in a rolling formation.
- **Llamas** — rival faction to the goblins; Carl and Donut started a meth war between the two.

## Key Events

- **The Transformation (Ch. 1):** Borant Corporation's mining crushes Earth's buildings at 2:23 AM PST, January 3rd. Carl, outside retrieving Beatrice's show cat Donut, survives.
- **Donut's transformation (Ch. 5–7):** Donut eats the Enhanced Pet Biscuit and becomes a talking crawler (CHA 25). Her Legendary Pet Box yields the **Enchanted Crown of the Sepsis Whore**, locking her into the Blood Sultanate royal succession on [Floor 9](floor-9.md) until that family is killed. She names the party "The Royal Court of Princess Donut."
- **Carl invents Carl's Jug O' Boom** — moonshine + goblin oil + torch; added to the Dungeon Codex, earning him 1 gold per kill when others use the recipe.
- **Frank Q / Maggie My ambush:** [Frank Q.](../characters/frank-q.md) (ICE agent) and Maggie My try to ambush Carl in a safe room; frozen by safe-zone rules. Carl booby-traps a rat corpse with dynamite for them.
- **The Meadow Lark group:** [Brandon An](../characters/brandon-an.md)'s team — 4 CNAs plus 38 elderly nursing-home residents from Wenatchee, including [Mrs. McGibbons](../characters/mrs-mcgibbons.md) — is helped down to Floor 2.

## Survivors / Party State

- **Carl** — alive, leveling fast, bonded with Donut as Royal Bodyguard.
- **Princess Donut** — alive; now a talking crawler; saddled with the Crown's Floor 9 obligation.
- **Brandon An's Meadow Lark group** — descends to Floor 2 ([Imani C.](../characters/imani-c.md), [Mrs. McGibbons](../characters/mrs-mcgibbons.md), Chris Andrews 2, and the elderly residents).
- **Agatha** — first appears here; her odd Level-4 stasis becomes a recurring mystery.
