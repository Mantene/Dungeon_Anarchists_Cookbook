# Floor 6 — The Hunting Grounds (The Selva)

## Overview

- **Floor number:** 6
- **Environment:** A jungle world reusing the **Floor 3** map. The hunter headquarters city of **Zockau**, dryad settlements throughout the jungle, and a high elf castle in the southeast.
- **Timer:** 17 days
- **Population:** 85,223 crawlers; 360+ hunters (each Level 50, given a +20 bump), held for a 30-hour grace period before release.
- **When entered:** Book 5, Ch. 1.

The "hunting" floor, where wealthy aliens pay to hunt crawlers — especially dangerous for top-10 crawlers like Carl (#1 at entry). Culminates in the **Butcher's Masquerade**.

## Rules & Mechanics

- **Hunters:** Paid alien hunters released after a 30-hour grace period, specifically targeting high-ranked crawlers.
- **Guild system activates:** Allows party coordination without forcing party membership.
- **Gate of the Feral Gods deal:** Carl temporarily surrenders the Gate to the Syndicate (to be returned on [Floor 9](floor-9.md)) in exchange for four mercenaries with special spells — negotiated by [Quasar](../characters/quasar.md): Bomo (_Teleport to Stairwell_), The Sledge (_Zerzura_), Clay-ton, and Very Sullen.
- **_Laundry Day_ (Donut):** Strips armor from opponents; at Level 15, can strip "soul armor" from gods (deity vessels).
- **_Zerzura_ (The Sledge):** Sends a town/settlement to a random floor, killing everything it lands on, which resurrects as "Children of Inpewt" revenants.

## Mob Roster

- **Hunters** — 360+ alien mercenaries; the mantis **Vrah** is their queen/mayor of Zockau.
- **Were-castors** (Clint and Holger) — Signet's paralysis-immune crew.
- **Kiwi's velociraptor pack** — 36 Mongoliensis led by **Kiwi** (Level 60), who becomes [Mongo](../characters/mongo.md)'s mate.
- **High elf army** — Queen Imogen's forces at the Masquerade.

## Key Events

- **Pre-emptive strike (Ch. 1):** Carl sets up a missile battery and kills 35 hunters in Zockau before the grace period ends; Donut earns her first player-killer skull.
- **Vengeance of the Daughter (Ch. 16–29):** The [Signet](../characters/signet.md) storyline resolves at the Masquerade; **Ferdinand** (the orange tom cat) appears as Queen Imogen's Level-100 familiar and buys Carl a critical second.
- **The Butcher's Masquerade (Ch. 56–75):** A four-way battle (crawlers / hunters / high elves / the pain god). The Popov twins die and are reborn as ineligible baby nodlings — **escaping the dungeon** as free citizens.
- **The Princess Posse forms (Ch. 74):** Carl and Donut become Co-Warlords of a Floor-9 faction-war team sponsored by OIPAN, inheriting abandoned faction spots.
- **The Crown twist (Epilogue):** A dying Eva Sigrid pulls the **Crown of the Sepsis Whore** off Imogen's body and places it on [Katia](../characters/katia-grim.md)'s head — meaning only one of Donut or Katia can leave Floor 9 without killing the Blood Sultanate.
- **Prepotente breaks Floor 7** (see [Floor 7](floor-7.md)), dumping all crawlers directly onto Floor 8.
